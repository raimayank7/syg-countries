Romania

Romania is a country in Southeast Europe. In the language of the place it is called România. The capital is Bucharest.

Land and position

Romania covers about 237,500 square kilometres. By area it ranks 83rd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Ghana and a little larger than Laos. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Bulgaria, Hungary, Moldova, Serbia, Ukraine. Its centre lies at about 46° north, 25° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 19 million people live in Romania (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 68th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.23 percent of the world's people. On average there are 80 people to each square kilometre. About 52 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 76 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Romanian, Hungarian, Romany. A person from Romania is called Romanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Romanian leu (code RON, symbol lei). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 429 billion (2025), which is about US$ 22,538 for each person (2025).

Time

Romania uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Europe/Bucharest). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
