Argentina

Land and position

Argentina covers about 2,766,890 square kilometres. By area it ranks 9th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than India and a little larger than Kazakhstan. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay, Uruguay. Its centre lies at about 34° south, 64° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 45.9 million people live in Argentina (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 35th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.56 percent of the world's people. On average there are 17 people to each square kilometre. About 92 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 78 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, English, Italian, German, French, Guarani. A person from Argentina is called Argentine.

Money and economy

The currency is the Argentine peso (code ARS, symbol $). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 683 billion (2025), which is about US$ 14,898 for each person (2025).

Time

Argentina spans 12 time zone areas, from UTC-3:00 to UTC-3:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
