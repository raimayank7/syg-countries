Lithuania

Lithuania is a country in Northern Europe. Its official name is Republic of Lithuania. In the language of the place it is called Lietuva. The capital is Vilnius.

Land and position

Lithuania covers about 65,200 square kilometres. By area it ranks 125th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Sri Lanka and a little larger than Latvia. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Belarus, Latvia, Poland, Russia. Its centre lies at about 56° north, 24° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 2.89 million people live in Lithuania (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 140th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 44 people to each square kilometre. About 69 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 77 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Lithuanian, Russian, Polish. A person from Lithuania is called Lithuanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 95.2 billion (2025), which is about US$ 32,959 for each person (2025).

Time

Lithuania uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Europe/Vilnius). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
