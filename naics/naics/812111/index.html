NAICS 812111: Barber Shops

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

812111 is the NAICS national industry Barber Shops in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 812 Personal and Laundry Services › 8121 Personal Care Services. In the code order it is number 954 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 81211 Hair, Nail, and Skin Care Services, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as barber shops or men's hair stylist shops primarily engaged in cutting, trimming, and styling men's and boys' hair; and/or shaving and trimming men's beards.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is cutting, trimming, and styling men's and boys' hair.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 812111 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 812111 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 812 is the subsector Personal and Laundry Services
  • 8121 is the industry group Personal Care Services
  • 81211 is the five-digit industry Hair, Nail, and Skin Care Services
  • 812111 is the national industry Barber Shops

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 3 terms to 812111, from "Barber shops" to "Hair stylist shops, men's". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Barber shops; Hair stylist services, men's; Hair stylist shops, men's.

Neighbouring national industries

Barber Shops sits in the NAICS industry 81211 Hair, Nail, and Skin Care Services, which has 3 national industries in all. The others are: 812112 Beauty Salons, 812113 Nail Salons.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 812111 the main activity here is cutting, trimming, and styling men's and boys' hair. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 812112 Beauty Salons: main activity is one or more of the following: (1) cutting, trimming, shampooing, coloring, waving, or styling hair
  • 812113 Nail Salons: main activity is providing nail care services, such as manicures, pedicures, and nail extensions

The NAICS industry around it

One level up is the NAICS industry 81211 Hair, Nail, and Skin Care Services. The Census describes it this way: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) providing hair care services; (2) providing nail care services; and (3) providing facials or applying makeup (except permanent makeup).
