NAICS 722310: Food Service Contractors

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

722310 is the NAICS national industry Food Service Contractors in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 72 Accommodation and Food Services › 722 Food Services and Drinking Places › 7223 Special Food Services. In the code order it is number 932 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 72231 Food Service Contractors, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing food services at institutional, governmental, commercial, or industrial locations of others based on contractual arrangements with these types of organizations for a specified period of time. The establishments of this industry provide food services for the convenience of the contracting organization or the contracting organization's customers. The contractual arrangement of these establishments with contracting organizations may vary by type of facility operated (e.g., cafeteria, restaurant, fast-food eating place), revenue sharing, cost structure, and personnel provided. Management staff is always provided by food service contractors.

Illustrative Examples:

Airline food service contractors Food concession contractors (e.g., at sporting, entertainment, convention facilities) Cafeteria food service contractors (e.g., at schools, hospitals, government offices)

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is providing food services at institutional, governmental, commercial, or industrial locations of others based on contractual arrangements with these types of organizations for a specified period of time.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 722310 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 722310 from the left:

  • 72 is the sector Accommodation and Food Services
  • 722 is the subsector Food Services and Drinking Places
  • 7223 is the industry group Special Food Services
  • 72231 is the five-digit industry Food Service Contractors
  • 722310 is the national industry Food Service Contractors

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 9 terms to 722310, from "Airline food services contractors" to "Industrial caterers (i.e., providing food services on a contractual arrangement (except single-event basis))". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Airline food services contractors; Cafeteria food services contractors (e.g., government office cafeterias, hospital cafeterias, school cafeterias); Food concession contractors (e.g., convention facilities, entertainment facilities, sporting facilities); Food service contractors, airline; Food service contractors, cafeteria; Food service contractors, concession operators (e.g., convention facilities, entertainment facilities, sporting facilities); Food service contractors, industrial; Food service contractors, institutional; Industrial caterers (i.e., providing food services on a contractual arrangement (except single-event basis)).

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Industrial caterers (i.e., providing food services on a contractual arrangement (except single-event basis)).
