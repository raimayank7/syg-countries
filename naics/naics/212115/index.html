NAICS 212115: Underground Coal Mining

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

212115 is the NAICS national industry Underground Coal Mining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 212 Mining (except Oil and Gas) › 2121 Coal Mining. In the code order it is number 68 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 21211 Coal Mining, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) underground mining of bituminous and anthracite coal; (2) developing bituminous and anthracite coal underground mine sites; and (3) underground mining and beneficiating (e.g., cleaning, washing, screening, and sizing) of bituminous and anthracite coal.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is one or more of the following: (1) underground mining of bituminous and anthracite coal.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 212115 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 212115 from the left:

  • 21 is the sector Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction
  • 212 is the subsector Mining (except Oil and Gas)
  • 2121 is the industry group Coal Mining
  • 21211 is the five-digit industry Coal Mining
  • 212115 is the national industry Underground Coal Mining

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 7 terms to 212115, from "Bituminous coal or anthracite underground mine site development for own account" to "Subbituminous coal underground mining". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Bituminous coal or anthracite underground mine site development for own account; Bituminous coal or anthracite underground mining; Coal, bituminous or anthracite, underground mining; Hard coal (i.e., anthracite) underground mining; Semianthracite underground mining; Semibituminous coal underground mining; Subbituminous coal underground mining.

Neighbouring national industries

Underground Coal Mining sits in the NAICS industry 21211 Coal Mining, which has 2 national industries in all. The others are: 212114 Surface Coal Mining.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 212115?

NAICS 212115 is the national industry "Underground Coal Mining" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 2121 Coal Mining.

What does NAICS 212115 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is one or more of the following: (1) underground mining of bituminous and anthracite coal.

Which sector is 212115 in?

212115 belongs to NAICS sector 21, Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction.

What kinds of business are indexed under 212115?

The Census index file lists 7 terms for it, for example Bituminous coal or anthracite underground mine site development for own account; Bituminous coal or anthracite underground mining; Coal, bituminous or anthracite, underground mining; Hard coal (i.e., anthracite) underground mining.

How is 212115 different from 212114?

212115 is Underground Coal Mining, while 212114 is Surface Coal Mining. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
