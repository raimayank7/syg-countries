NAICS 333248: All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333248 is the NAICS national industry All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3332 Industrial Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 358 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33324 Industrial Machinery Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing industrial machinery (except agricultural and farm-type; construction and mining machinery; food manufacturing-type machinery; semiconductor making machinery; and sawmill, woodworking, and paper making machinery).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing industrial machinery (except agricultural and farm-type.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333248 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 333248 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 333 is the subsector Machinery Manufacturing
  • 3332 is the industry group Industrial Machinery Manufacturing
  • 33324 is the five-digit industry Industrial Machinery Manufacturing
  • 333248 is the national industry All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing

Common questions

What is NAICS code 333248?

NAICS 333248 is the national industry "All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 3332 Industrial Machinery Manufacturing.

What does NAICS 333248 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is manufacturing industrial machinery (except agricultural and farm-type.

Which sector is 333248 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 333248?

How is 333248 different from 333241?

333248 is All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing, while 333241 is Food Product Machinery Manufacturing. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
