NAICS 2389: Other Specialty Trade Contractors

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

2389 is the NAICS industry group Other Specialty Trade Contractors in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 23 Construction › 238 Specialty Trade Contractors.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in site preparation activities and in specialized trades (except foundation, structure, and building exterior contractors; building equipment contractors; and building finishing contractors). The specialty trade work performed includes new work, additions, alterations, maintenance, and repairs.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is site preparation activities and in specialized trades (except foundation, structure, and building exterior contractors.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 2389 the main activity here is site preparation activities and in specialized trades (except foundation, structure, and building exterior contractors. The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 2383 Building Finishing Contractors: main activity is the specialty trades needed to finish buildings
  • 2382 Building Equipment Contractors: main activity is installing or servicing equipment that forms part of a building mechanical system (e.g., electricity, water, heating, and cooling)
  • 2381 Foundation, Structure, and Building Exterior Contractors: main activity is the specialty trades needed to complete the basic structure (i.e., foundation, frame, and shell) of buildings

What is NAICS code 2389?

Which sector is 2389 in?

How many national industries does 2389 cover?

How is 2389 different from 2381?

2389 is Other Specialty Trade Contractors, while 2381 is Foundation, Structure, and Building Exterior Contractors. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
