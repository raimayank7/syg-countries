NAICS 327120: Clay Building Material and Refractories Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

327120 is the NAICS national industry Clay Building Material and Refractories Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 327 Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing › 3271 Clay Product and Refractory Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 279 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32712 Clay Building Material and Refractories Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in shaping, molding, baking, burning, or hardening clay refractories, nonclay refractories, ceramic tile, structural clay tile, brick, and other structural clay building materials. A refractory is a material that will retain its shape and chemical identity when subjected to high temperatures and is used in applications that require extreme resistance to heat, such as furnace linings.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is shaping, molding, baking, burning, or hardening clay refractories, nonclay refractories, ceramic tile, structural clay tile, brick, and other structural clay building materials.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 327120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
