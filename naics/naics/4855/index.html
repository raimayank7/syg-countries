NAICS 4855: Charter Bus Industry

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4855 is the NAICS industry group Charter Bus Industry in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 485 Transit and Ground Passenger Transportation.

What it contains

Charter Bus Industry has 1 national industry under it, with a page of its own.

  • 485510 Charter Bus Industry: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing buses for charter.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 1 national industry under Charter Bus Industry.

The industry in full: 485510 Charter Bus Industry

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing buses for charter. These establishments provide bus services to meet customers' road transportation needs and generally do not operate over fixed routes and on regular schedules.

The Census index lists these terms for it: Bus charter services (except scenic, sightseeing); Charter bus services (except scenic, sightseeing).
