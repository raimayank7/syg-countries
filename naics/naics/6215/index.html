NAICS 6215: Medical and Diagnostic Laboratories

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

6215 is the NAICS industry group Medical and Diagnostic Laboratories in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services.

The industry in full: 621511 Medical Laboratories

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as medical laboratories primarily engaged in providing analytic or diagnostic services, including body fluid analysis, generally to the medical profession or to the patient on referral from a health practitioner.

How is 6215 different from 6211?

6215 is Medical and Diagnostic Laboratories, while 6211 is Offices of Physicians. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
