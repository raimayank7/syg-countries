NAICS 323120: Support Activities for Printing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

323120 is the NAICS national industry Support Activities for Printing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 323 Printing and Related Support Activities › 3231 Printing and Related Support Activities. In the code order it is number 226 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32312 Support Activities for Printing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing prepress and postpress services in support of printing activities. Prepress services may include such things as platemaking, typesetting, trade binding, and sample mounting. Postpress services include such things as book or paper bronzing, die cutting, edging, embossing, folding, gilding, gluing, and indexing.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is performing prepress and postpress services in support of printing activities.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 323120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 323120 from the left:

Is this the code to put on a form?
