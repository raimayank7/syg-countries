NAICS 711410: Agents and Managers for Artists, Athletes, Entertainers, and Other Public Figures

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

711410 is the NAICS national industry Agents and Managers for Artists, Athletes, Entertainers, and Other Public Figures in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 71 Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation › 711 Performing Arts, Spectator Sports, and Related Industries › 7114 Agents and Managers for Artists, Athletes, Entertainers, and Other Public Figures. In the code order it is number 909 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 71141 Agents and Managers for Artists, Athletes, Entertainers, and Other Public Figures, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments of agents and managers primarily engaged in representing and/or managing creative and performing artists, sports figures, entertainers, and other public figures. The representation and management includes activities, such as representing clients in contract negotiations; managing or organizing clients' financial affairs; and generally promoting the careers of their clients.

Illustrative Examples:

Celebrities' agents or managers Sports figures' agents or managers Literary agents Talent agents Modeling agents

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is representing and/or managing creative and performing artists, sports figures, entertainers, and other public figures.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 711410 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 711410 from the left:
