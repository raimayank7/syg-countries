NAICS 525910: Open-End Investment Funds

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

525910 is the NAICS national industry Open-End Investment Funds in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 525 Funds, Trusts, and Other Financial Vehicles › 5259 Other Investment Pools and Funds. In the code order it is number 721 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 52591 Open-End Investment Funds, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises legal entities (i.e., open-end investment funds) organized to pool assets that consist of securities or other financial instruments. Shares in these pools are offered to the public in an initial offering with additional shares offered continuously and perpetually and redeemed at a specific price determined by the net asset value.

Illustrative Examples:

Investment funds, open-ended Money market mutual funds, open-ended

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.
