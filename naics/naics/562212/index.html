NAICS 562212: Solid Waste Landfill

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

562212 is the NAICS national industry Solid Waste Landfill in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 562 Waste Management and Remediation Services › 5622 Waste Treatment and Disposal. In the code order it is number 837 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 56221 Waste Treatment and Disposal, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) operating landfills for the disposal of nonhazardous solid waste or (2) the combined activity of collecting and/or hauling nonhazardous waste materials within a local area and operating landfills for the disposal of nonhazardous solid waste. These establishments may produce byproducts, such as methane.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is (1) operating landfills for the disposal of nonhazardous solid waste or (2) the combined activity of collecting and/or hauling nonhazardous waste materials within a local area and operating landfills for the disposal of nonhazardous solid waste.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 562212 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 562212 from the left:

  • 562 is the subsector Waste Management and Remediation Services
  • 5622 is the industry group Waste Treatment and Disposal

The Census index file points 13 terms to 562212, from "Dumps, nonhazardous solid waste (e.g., trash)" to "Waste disposal landfills, nonhazardous solid". That equals the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. What is NAICS code 562212?

What does NAICS 562212 include?

How is 562212 different from 562211?

562212 is Solid Waste Landfill, while 562211 is Hazardous Waste Treatment and Disposal. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
