NAICS 3352: Household Appliance Manufacturing

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3352 is the NAICS industry group Household Appliance Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 335 Electrical Equipment, Appliance, and Component Manufacturing.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing small electric appliances, electric housewares, and major household appliances.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing small electric appliances, electric housewares, and major household appliances.
