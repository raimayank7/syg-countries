NAICS 339930: Doll, Toy, and Game Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339930 is the NAICS national industry Doll, Toy, and Game Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3399 Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 468 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33993 Doll, Toy, and Game Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing complete dolls, doll parts, doll clothes, action figures, toys, games (including electronic, such as gaming consoles and devices and handheld video games), hobby kits, and children's vehicles (except metal bicycles and tricycles).

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing complete dolls, doll parts, doll clothes, action figures, toys, games (including electronic, such as gaming consoles and devices and handheld video games), hobby kits, and children's vehicles (except metal bicycles and tricycles).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339930 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 339930 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 339 is the subsector Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 3399 is the industry group Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 33993 is the five-digit industry Doll, Toy, and Game Manufacturing
  • 339930 is the national industry Doll, Toy, and Game Manufacturing
