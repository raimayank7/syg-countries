NAICS 335314: Relay and Industrial Control Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

335314 is the NAICS national industry Relay and Industrial Control Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 335 Electrical Equipment, Appliance, and Component Manufacturing › 3353 Electrical Equipment Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 416 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33531 Electrical Equipment Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing relays, motor starters and controllers, and other industrial controls and control accessories.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing relays, motor starters and controllers, and other industrial controls and control accessories.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 335314 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 335314 from the left:

  • 3353 is the industry group Electrical Equipment Manufacturing
  • 33531 is the five-digit industry Electrical Equipment Manufacturing
