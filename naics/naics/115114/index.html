NAICS 115114: Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

115114 is the NAICS national industry Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry › 1151 Support Activities for Crop Production. In the code order it is number 60 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 6 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 11511 Support Activities for Crop Production, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing services on crops, subsequent to their harvest, with the intent of preparing them for market or further processing. These establishments provide postharvest activities, such as crop cleaning, sun drying, shelling, fumigating, curing, sorting, grading, packing, and cooling.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is performing services on crops, subsequent to their harvest, with the intent of preparing them for market or further processing.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 115114 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 115114 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 115 is the subsector Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry
  • 1151 is the industry group Support Activities for Crop Production
  • 11511 is the five-digit industry Support Activities for Crop Production
  • 115114 is the national industry Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning)

Neighbouring national industries

Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning) sits in the NAICS industry 11511 Support Activities for Crop Production, which has 6 national industries in all. The others are: 115111 Cotton Ginning, 115112 Soil Preparation, Planting, and Cultivating, 115113 Crop Harvesting, Primarily by Machine, 115115 Farm Labor Contractors and Crew Leaders, 115116 Farm Management Services.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 115114 the main activity here is performing services on crops, subsequent to their harvest, with the intent of preparing them for market or further processing. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 115113 Crop Harvesting, Primarily by Machine: main activity is mechanical harvesting, picking, and combining of crops, and related activities
  • 115115 Farm Labor Contractors and Crew Leaders: main activity is supplying labor for agricultural production or harvesting
  • 115112 Soil Preparation, Planting, and Cultivating: main activity is performing a soil preparation activity or crop production service, such as plowing, fertilizing, seed bed preparation, planting, cultivating, and crop protecting services

Codes that are easy to confuse with it

What does NAICS 115114 include?

Which sector is 115114 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 115114?

How is 115114 different from 115111?

115114 is Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning), while 115111 is Cotton Ginning. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
