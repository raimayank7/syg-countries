NAICS 4243: Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4243 is the NAICS industry group Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of piece goods, notions, and other dry goods; footwear; and clothing and clothing accessories.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of piece goods, notions, and other dry goods.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 4243 from the left:

  • 42 is the sector Wholesale Trade
  • 424 is the subsector Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods

Common questions

What is NAICS code 4243?

NAICS 4243 is the industry group "Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods.

What does NAICS 4243 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is the merchant wholesale distribution of piece goods, notions, and other dry goods.

Which sector is 4243 in?

4243 belongs to NAICS sector 42, Wholesale Trade.

How many national industries does 4243 cover?

4243 covers 3 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 4243 different from 4241?

4243 is Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers, while 4241 is Paper and Paper Product Merchant Wholesalers. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
