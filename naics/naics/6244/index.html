NAICS 6244: Child Care Services

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

6244 is the NAICS industry group Child Care Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 624 Social Assistance.

What it contains

Child Care Services has 1 national industry under it, with a page of its own.

  • 624410 Child Care Services: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing care and early learning opportunities for infants and children.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 1 national industry under Child Care Services.

The industry in full: 624410 Child Care Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing care and early learning opportunities for infants and children. These establishments generally care for children from birth through school age and may also offer pre-kindergarten, kindergarten, and/or before- or after-school educational programs. The care and early learning provided by these establishments may include opportunities for development in health, social and emotional learning, and family engagement.
