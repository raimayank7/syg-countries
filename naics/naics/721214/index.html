NAICS 721214: Recreational and Vacation Camps (except Campgrounds)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

721214 is the NAICS national industry Recreational and Vacation Camps (except Campgrounds) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 72 Accommodation and Food Services › 721 Accommodation › 7212 RV (Recreational Vehicle) Parks and Recreational Camps. In the code order it is number 930 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 72121 RV (Recreational Vehicle) Parks and Recreational Camps, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating overnight recreational camps, such as children's camps, family vacation camps, hunting and fishing camps, and outdoor adventure retreats, that offer trail riding, white water rafting, hiking, and similar activities. These establishments provide accommodation facilities, such as cabins and fixed campsites, and other amenities, such as food services, recreational facilities and equipment, and organized recreational activities.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is operating overnight recreational camps, such as children's camps, family vacation camps, hunting and fishing camps, and outdoor adventure retreats, that offer trail riding, white water rafting, hiking, and similar activities.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 721214 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
