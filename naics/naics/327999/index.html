NAICS 327999: All Other Miscellaneous Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

327999 is the NAICS national industry All Other Miscellaneous Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 327 Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing › 3279 Other Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 295 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32799 All Other Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing nonmetallic mineral products (except pottery, ceramics, and plumbing fixtures; clay building materials and refractories; glass and glass products; cement; ready-mix concrete; concrete products; lime; gypsum products; abrasive products; cut stone and stone products; ground and treated minerals and earth; and mineral wool).

Illustrative Examples:

Dry mix concrete manufacturing Mica products manufacturing Manmade and engineered proppants (e.g., resin-coated sand, ceramic materials) manufacturing Stucco and stucco products manufacturing Synthetic stones, for gem stones and industrial use, manufacturing

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing nonmetallic mineral products (except pottery, ceramics, and plumbing fixtures.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 327999 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
