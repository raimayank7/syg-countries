NAICS 115210: Support Activities for Animal Production

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

115210 is the NAICS national industry Support Activities for Animal Production in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry › 1152 Support Activities for Animal Production. In the code order it is number 63 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 11521 Support Activities for Animal Production, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing support activities related to raising livestock (e.g., cattle, goats, hogs, horses, poultry, sheep). These establishments may perform one or more of the following: (1) breeding services for animals, including companion animals (e.g., cats, dogs, pet birds); (2) pedigree record services; (3) boarding horses; (4) dairy herd improvement activities; (5) livestock spraying; and (6) sheep dipping and shearing.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is performing support activities related to raising livestock (e.g., cattle, goats, hogs, horses, poultry, sheep).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 115210 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 115210 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 115 is the subsector Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry
  • 1152 is the industry group Support Activities for Animal Production
  • 11521 is the five-digit industry Support Activities for Animal Production
  • 115210 is the national industry Support Activities for Animal Production

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 30 terms to 115210, from "Animal semen banks" to "Vaccinating livestock (except by veterinarians)". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Animal semen banks; Artificial insemination services for livestock; Artificial insemination services for pets; Boarding horses (except racehorses); Branding; Breeding, animal, services; Bull testing stations; Cattle spraying; Cleaning poultry houses; Corralling, drovers; Dairy herd improvement associations; Equine boarding; Farriers; Hoof trimming; Horses (except racehorses), boarding; Horses, training (except racehorses); Horseshoeing; Livestock breeding services (except consulting); Livestock spraying; Milk testing for butterfat and milk solids; Pedigree (i.e., livestock, pets, poultry) record services; Poultry catching services; Reproductive flushing services for animals; Semen collection; Sexing services for poultry (e.g., separating males/females); Sheep dipping and shearing; Showing of cattle, hogs, sheep, goats, and poultry; Stud services; Training horses (except racehorses); Vaccinating livestock (except by veterinarians).

6 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Boarding horses (except racehorses); Horses (except racehorses), boarding; Horses, training (except racehorses); Livestock breeding services (except consulting); Training horses (except racehorses); Vaccinating livestock (except by veterinarians).
