NAICS 621112: Offices of Physicians, Mental Health Specialists

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

621112 is the NAICS national industry Offices of Physicians, Mental Health Specialists in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services › 6211 Offices of Physicians. In the code order it is number 862 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 62111 Offices of Physicians, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments of health practitioners having the degree of M.D. (Doctor of Medicine) or D.O. (Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine) primarily engaged in the independent practice of psychiatry or psychoanalysis. These practitioners operate private or group practices in their own offices (e.g., centers, clinics) or in the facilities of others, such as hospitals or HMO medical centers.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the independent practice of psychiatry or psychoanalysis.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 621112 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
