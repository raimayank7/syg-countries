NAICS 1151: Support Activities for Crop Production

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

1151 is the NAICS industry group Support Activities for Crop Production in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry.

What it contains

Support Activities for Crop Production has 6 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 115111 Cotton Ginning: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in ginning cotton.
  • 115112 Soil Preparation, Planting, and Cultivating: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing a soil preparation activity or crop production service, such as plowing, fertilizing, seed bed preparation, planting,...
  • 115113 Crop Harvesting, Primarily by Machine: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in mechanical harvesting, picking, and combining of crops, and related activities.
  • 115114 Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning): This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing services on crops, subsequent to their harvest, with the intent of preparing them for market or further processing.
  • 115115 Farm Labor Contractors and Crew Leaders: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in supplying labor for agricultural production or harvesting.
  • 115116 Farm Management Services: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing farm management services on a contract or fee basis usually to citrus groves, orchards, or vineyards.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 6 national industries under Support Activities for Crop Production.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 1151 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 115 is the subsector Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry
  • 1151 is the industry group Support Activities for Crop Production

Neighbouring industry groups

Support Activities for Crop Production sits in the subsector 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry, which has 3 industry groups in all. The others are: 1152 Support Activities for Animal Production, 1153 Support Activities for Forestry.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry subsector provide support services that are an essential part of agricultural and forestry production. These support activities may be performed by the agriculture or forestry producing establishment or conducted independently as an alternative source of inputs required for the production process for a given crop, animal, or forestry industry. Common questions

What is NAICS code 1151?

NAICS 1151 is the industry group "Support Activities for Crop Production" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry.

What does NAICS 1151 include?

Support Activities for Crop Production

Which sector is 1151 in?

1151 belongs to NAICS sector 11, Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

How many national industries does 1151 cover?

1151 covers 6 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 1151 different from 1152?

1151 is Support Activities for Crop Production, while 1152 is Support Activities for Animal Production. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
