NAICS 459510: Used Merchandise Retailers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

459510 is the NAICS national industry Used Merchandise Retailers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 44-45 Retail Trade › 459 Sporting Goods, Hobby, Musical Instrument, Book, and Miscellaneous Retailers › 4595 Used Merchandise Retailers. In the code order it is number 597 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 45951 Used Merchandise Retailers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing used merchandise, antiques, and secondhand goods (except motor vehicles, such as automobiles, RVs, motorcycles, and boats; motor vehicle parts; tires; and mobile homes). This industry includes establishments retailing used merchandise on an auction basis.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is retailing used merchandise, antiques, and secondhand goods (except motor vehicles, such as automobiles, RVs, motorcycles, and boats.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 459510 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 459510 from the left:

  • 44-45 is the sector Retail Trade
  • 459 is the subsector Sporting Goods, Hobby, Musical Instrument, Book, and Miscellaneous Retailers
  • 4595 is the industry group Used Merchandise Retailers
  • 45951 is the five-digit industry Used Merchandise Retailers
  • 459510 is the national industry Used Merchandise Retailers

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 30 terms to 459510, from "Antique dealers (except motor vehicles)" to "Used rare collectors' items (e.g., autograph, coin, card, stamps), mail-order houses". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any.
