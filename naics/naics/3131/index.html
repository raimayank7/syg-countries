NAICS 3131: Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3131 is the NAICS industry group Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 313 Textile Mills.

What it contains

Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills has 1 national industry under it, with a page of its own.

  • 313110 Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 1 national industry under Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills.

The industry in full: 313110 Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) spinning yarn; (2) manufacturing thread of any fiber; (3) texturizing, throwing, twisting, and winding purchased yarn or manmade fibers and filaments; and (4) producing hemp yarn and further processing into rope or bags.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 313 Textile Mills. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Textile Mills subsector group establishments that transform a basic fiber (natural or synthetic) into a product, such as yarn or fabric that is further manufactured into usable items, such as apparel, sheets, towels, and textile bags for individual or industrial consumption. The further manufacturing may be performed in the same establishment and classified in this subsector, or it may be performed at a separate establishment and be classified elsewhere in the Manufacturing sector.
