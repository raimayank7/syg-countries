NAICS 541370: Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

541370 is the NAICS national industry Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 54 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 541 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 5413 Architectural, Engineering, and Related Services. In the code order it is number 762 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 54137 Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing surveying and mapping services of the surface of the earth, including the sea floor. These services may include surveying and mapping of areas above or below the surface of the earth, such as the creation of view easements or segregating rights in parcels of land by creating underground utility easements.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is performing surveying and mapping services of the surface of the earth, including the sea floor.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 541370 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 541370 from the left:

  • 54 is the sector Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services
  • 541 is the subsector Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services
  • 5413 is the industry group Architectural, Engineering, and Related Services
  • 54137 is the five-digit industry Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services
  • 541370 is the national industry Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 15 terms to 541370, from "Aerial surveying (except geophysical) services" to "Topographic surveying services". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Aerial surveying (except geophysical) services; Cadastral surveying services; Cartographic surveying services; Construction surveying services; Geodetic surveying services; Geographic information system (GIS) base mapping services; Geospatial mapping services; Hydrographic mapping services; Hydrographic surveying services; Land surveying services; Mapping (except geophysical) services; Photogrammetric mapping services; Surveying and mapping services (except geophysical); Topographic mapping services; Topographic surveying services.

3 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Aerial surveying (except geophysical) services; Mapping (except geophysical) services; Surveying and mapping services (except geophysical).
