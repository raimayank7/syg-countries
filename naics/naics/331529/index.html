NAICS 331529: Other Nonferrous Metal Foundries (except Die-Casting)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

331529 is the NAICS national industry Other Nonferrous Metal Foundries (except Die-Casting) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 331 Primary Metal Manufacturing › 3315 Foundries. In the code order it is number 313 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33152 Nonferrous Metal Foundries, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in pouring molten nonferrous metals (except aluminum) into molds to manufacture nonferrous castings (except nonferrous die-castings and aluminum castings). Establishments in this industry purchase nonferrous metals, such as copper, nickel, lead, and zinc, made in other establishments.

Cross-references. Establishments primarily engaged in--

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is pouring molten nonferrous metals (except aluminum) into molds to manufacture nonferrous castings (except nonferrous die-castings and aluminum castings).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.
