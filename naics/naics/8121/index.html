NAICS 8121: Personal Care Services

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

8121 is the NAICS industry group Personal Care Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 812 Personal and Laundry Services.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments, such as barber and beauty shops, that provide appearance care services to individual consumers.

What it contains

Personal Care Services has 5 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 812111 Barber Shops: This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as barber shops or men's hair stylist shops primarily engaged in cutting, trimming, and styling men's and boys' hair
  • 812112 Beauty Salons: This U.S. industry comprises establishments (except those known as barber shops or men's hair stylist shops) primarily engaged in one or more of the following
  • 812113 Nail Salons: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing nail care services, such as manicures, pedicures, and nail extensions.
  • 812191 Diet and Weight Reducing Centers: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing non-medical services to assist clients in attaining or maintaining a desired weight.
  • 812199 Other Personal Care Services: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing personal care services (except hair, nail, facial, nonpermanent makeup, or non-medical diet and weight reducing services).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 5 national industries under Personal Care Services.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 8121 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 812 is the subsector Personal and Laundry Services
  • 8121 is the industry group Personal Care Services

How many national industries does 8121 cover?

How is 8121 different from 8122?

8121 is Personal Care Services, while 8122 is Death Care Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
