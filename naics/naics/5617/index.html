NAICS 5617: Services to Buildings and Dwellings

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

5617 is the NAICS industry group Services to Buildings and Dwellings in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 561 Administrative and Support Services.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following: (1) exterminating and pest control services; (2) janitorial services; (3) landscaping services; (4) carpet and upholstery cleaning services; or (5) other services to buildings and dwellings.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is one of the following: (1) exterminating and pest control services.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 5617 from the left:

  • 56 is the sector Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services
  • 561 is the subsector Administrative and Support Services
  • 5617 is the industry group Services to Buildings and Dwellings

Common questions

What is NAICS code 5617?

NAICS 5617 is the industry group "Services to Buildings and Dwellings" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 561 Administrative and Support Services.

What does NAICS 5617 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is one of the following: (1) exterminating and pest control services.

Which sector is 5617 in?

5617 belongs to NAICS sector 56, Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services.

How many national industries does 5617 cover?

5617 covers 5 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 5617 different from 5611?

5617 is Services to Buildings and Dwellings, while 5611 is Office Administrative Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
