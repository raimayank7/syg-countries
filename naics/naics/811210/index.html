NAICS 811210: Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

811210 is the NAICS national industry Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 811 Repair and Maintenance › 8112 Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance. In the code order it is number 947 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 81121 Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repairing and maintaining one or more of the following: (1) consumer electronic equipment; (2) computers; (3) office machines; (4) communication equipment; and (5) other electronic and precision equipment and instruments, without retailing these products as new. Establishments in this industry repair items, such as microscopes, radar and sonar equipment, televisions, stereos, video recorders, computers, fax machines, photocopying machines, two-way radios, cellular telephones, and other communications equipment, scientific instruments, and medical equipment.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is repairing and maintaining one or more of the following: (1) consumer electronic equipment.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 811210 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 811210 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 811 is the subsector Repair and Maintenance
  • 8112 is the industry group Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance
  • 81121 is the five-digit industry Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance
  • 811210 is the national industry Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance
