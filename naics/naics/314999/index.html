NAICS 314999: All Other Miscellaneous Textile Product Mills

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

314999 is the NAICS national industry All Other Miscellaneous Textile Product Mills in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 314 Textile Product Mills › 3149 Other Textile Product Mills. In the code order it is number 192 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 31499 All Other Textile Product Mills, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing textile products (except carpets and rugs; curtains and linens; textile bags and canvas products; rope, cordage, and twine; and tire cords and tire fabrics) from purchased materials. These establishments may further embellish the textile products they manufacture with decorative stitching. Establishments primarily engaged in adding decorative stitching such as embroidery or other art needlework on textile products, including apparel, on a contract or fee basis for the trade, are included in this industry.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing textile products (except carpets and rugs.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 314999 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
