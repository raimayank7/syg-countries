NAICS 212230: Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

212230 is the NAICS national industry Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 212 Mining (except Oil and Gas) › 2122 Metal Ore Mining. In the code order it is number 71 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 21223 Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in developing the mine site, mining, and/or beneficiating (i.e., preparing) ores valued chiefly for their copper, nickel, lead, or zinc content. Beneficiating includes the transformation of ores into concentrates. Establishments primarily engaged in recovering copper concentrates by the precipitation, leaching, or electrowinning of copper ore are included in this industry.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is developing the mine site, mining, and/or beneficiating (i.e., preparing) ores valued chiefly for their copper, nickel, lead, or zinc content.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 212230 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 212230 from the left:

  • 21 is the sector Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction
  • 212 is the subsector Mining (except Oil and Gas)
  • 2122 is the industry group Metal Ore Mining
  • 21223 is the five-digit industry Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining
  • 212230 is the national industry Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining

The industry group around it

Common questions

What is NAICS code 212230?

NAICS 212230 is the national industry "Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 2122 Metal Ore Mining.

What does NAICS 212230 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is developing the mine site, mining, and/or beneficiating (i.e., preparing) ores valued chiefly for their copper, nickel, lead, or zinc content.

Which sector is 212230 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 212230?

How is 212230 different from 212210?

212230 is Copper, Nickel, Lead, and Zinc Mining, while 212210 is Iron Ore Mining. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
