NAICS 522292: Real Estate Credit

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

522292 is the NAICS national industry Real Estate Credit in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 522 Credit Intermediation and Related Activities › 5222 Nondepository Credit Intermediation. In the code order it is number 696 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 52229 Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in lending funds with real estate as collateral.

Illustrative Examples:

Home equity credit lending Mortgage companies Reverse mortgage lending Mortgage banking (i.e., nondepository mortgage lending)

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is lending funds with real estate as collateral.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 522292 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 522292 from the left:

  • 52 is the sector Finance and Insurance
  • 522 is the subsector Credit Intermediation and Related Activities
  • 5222 is the industry group Nondepository Credit Intermediation
  • 52229 is the five-digit industry Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation
  • 522292 is the national industry Real Estate Credit

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 9 terms to 522292, from "Construction lending" to "Reverse mortgage lending". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Construction lending; Farm mortgage lending; Federal Land Banks; Home equity credit lending; Loan correspondents (i.e., lending funds with real estate as collateral); Mortgage banking (i.e., nondepository mortgage lending); Mortgage companies; Real estate credit lending; Reverse mortgage lending.

Neighbouring national industries

Real Estate Credit sits in the NAICS industry 52229 Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation, which has 3 national industries in all. The others are: 522291 Consumer Lending, 522299 International, Secondary Market, and All Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation.
