NAICS 325120: Industrial Gas Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

325120 is the NAICS national industry Industrial Gas Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 325 Chemical Manufacturing › 3251 Basic Chemical Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 233 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32512 Industrial Gas Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing industrial organic and inorganic gases in compressed, liquid, and solid forms.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing industrial organic and inorganic gases in compressed, liquid, and solid forms.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 325120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 325120 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 325 is the subsector Chemical Manufacturing
  • 3251 is the industry group Basic Chemical Manufacturing
  • 32512 is the five-digit industry Industrial Gas Manufacturing
  • 325120 is the national industry Industrial Gas Manufacturing
