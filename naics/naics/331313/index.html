NAICS 331313: Alumina Refining and Primary Aluminum Production

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

331313 is the NAICS national industry Alumina Refining and Primary Aluminum Production in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 331 Primary Metal Manufacturing › 3313 Alumina and Aluminum Production and Processing. In the code order it is number 300 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33131 Alumina and Aluminum Production and Processing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) refining alumina (i.e., aluminum oxide) generally from bauxite; (2) making aluminum from alumina; and/or (3) making aluminum from alumina and rolling, drawing, extruding, or casting the aluminum they make into primary forms. Establishments in this industry may make primary aluminum or aluminum-based alloys from alumina.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is one or more of the following: (1) refining alumina (i.e., aluminum oxide) generally from bauxite.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.
