NAICS 321114: Wood Preservation

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

321114 is the NAICS national industry Wood Preservation in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 321 Wood Product Manufacturing › 3211 Sawmills and Wood Preservation. In the code order it is number 201 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32111 Sawmills and Wood Preservation, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) treating wood sawed, planed, or shaped in other establishments with creosote or other preservatives, such as alkaline copper quat, copper azole, and sodium borates, to prevent decay and to protect against fire and insects and/or (2) sawing round wood poles, pilings, and posts and treating them with preservatives.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is (1) treating wood sawed, planed, or shaped in other establishments with creosote or other preservatives, such as alkaline copper quat, copper azole, and sodium borates, to prevent decay and to protect against fire and insects and/or (2) sawing round wood poles, pilings, and posts and treating them with preservatives.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 321114 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
