NAICS 339910: Jewelry and Silverware Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339910 is the NAICS national industry Jewelry and Silverware Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3399 Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 466 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33991 Jewelry and Silverware Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) manufacturing, engraving, chasing, or etching fine and costume jewelry; (2) manufacturing, engraving, chasing, or etching metal personal goods (i.e., small articles carried on or about the person, such as compacts or cigarette cases); (3) manufacturing, engraving, chasing, or etching precious metal solid, precious metal clad, or pewter flatware and other hollowware; (4) stamping coins; (5) manufacturing unassembled jewelry parts and stock shop products, such as sheet, wire, and tubing; (6) cutting, slabbing, tumbling, carving, engraving, polishing, or faceting precious or semiprecious stones and gems; (7) recutting, repolishing, and setting gem stones; and (8) drilling, sawing, and peeling cultured and costume pearls. This industry includes establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing precious solid, precious clad, and precious plated jewelry and personal goods.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is one or more of the following: (1) manufacturing, engraving, chasing, or etching fine and costume jewelry.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339910 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 339910 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 339 is the subsector Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 3399 is the industry group Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 33991 is the five-digit industry Jewelry and Silverware Manufacturing
  • 339910 is the national industry Jewelry and Silverware Manufacturing
