NAICS 4235: Metal and Mineral (except Petroleum) Merchant Wholesalers

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4235 is the NAICS industry group Metal and Mineral (except Petroleum) Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of products of the primary metals industries (including metal service centers) and coal, coke, metal ores, and/or nonmetallic minerals (except precious and semiprecious stones and minerals used in construction).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is the merchant wholesale distribution of products of the primary metals industries (including metal service centers) and coal, coke, metal ores, and/or nonmetallic minerals (except precious and semiprecious stones and minerals used in construction).
