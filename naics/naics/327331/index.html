NAICS 327331: Concrete Block and Brick Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

327331 is the NAICS national industry Concrete Block and Brick Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 327 Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing › 3273 Cement and Concrete Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 286 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32733 Concrete Pipe, Brick, and Block Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing concrete block and brick.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing concrete block and brick.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 327331 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 327 is the subsector Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing
  • 3273 is the industry group Cement and Concrete Product Manufacturing
  • 32733 is the five-digit industry Concrete Pipe, Brick, and Block Manufacturing
