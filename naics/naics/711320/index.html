NAICS 711320: Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events without Facilities

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

711320 is the NAICS national industry Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events without Facilities in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 71 Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation › 711 Performing Arts, Spectator Sports, and Related Industries › 7113 Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events. In the code order it is number 908 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 71132 Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events without Facilities, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises promoters primarily engaged in organizing, promoting, and/or managing live performing arts productions, sports events, and similar events, such as state fairs, county fairs, agricultural fairs, concerts, and festivals, in facilities that are managed and operated by others. Theatrical (except motion picture) booking agencies are included in this industry.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is organizing, promoting, and/or managing live performing arts productions, sports events, and similar events, such as state fairs, county fairs, agricultural fairs, concerts, and festivals, in facilities that are managed and operated by others.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 711320 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
