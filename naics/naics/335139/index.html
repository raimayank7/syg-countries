NAICS 335139: Electric Lamp Bulb and Other Lighting Equipment Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

335139 is the NAICS national industry Electric Lamp Bulb and Other Lighting Equipment Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 335 Electrical Equipment, Appliance, and Component Manufacturing › 3351 Electric Lighting Equipment Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 410 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33513 Electric Lighting Equipment Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing electric light bulbs, tubes, and parts (except glass blanks for electric light bulbs and light emitting diodes (LEDs)), electric lighting fixtures (except residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and vehicular electric lighting fixtures), and nonelectric lighting equipment.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing electric light bulbs, tubes, and parts (except glass blanks for electric light bulbs and light emitting diodes (LEDs)), electric lighting fixtures (except residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and vehicular electric lighting fixtures), and nonelectric lighting equipment.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 335139 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
