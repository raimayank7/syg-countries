NAICS 334510: Electromedical and Electrotherapeutic Apparatus Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

334510 is the NAICS national industry Electromedical and Electrotherapeutic Apparatus Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing › 3345 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 398 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 9 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33451 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing electromedical and electrotherapeutic apparatus, such as magnetic resonance imaging equipment, medical ultrasound equipment, pacemakers, hearing aids, electrocardiographs, and electromedical endoscopic equipment.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing electromedical and electrotherapeutic apparatus, such as magnetic resonance imaging equipment, medical ultrasound equipment, pacemakers, hearing aids, electrocardiographs, and electromedical endoscopic equipment.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 334510 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
