NAICS 424120: Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

424120 is the NAICS national industry Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods › 4241 Paper and Paper Product Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 515 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42412 Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of stationery, office supplies, and/or gift wrap.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of stationery, office supplies, and/or gift wrap.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 424120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
