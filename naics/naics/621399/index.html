NAICS 621399: Offices of All Other Miscellaneous Health Practitioners

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

621399 is the NAICS national industry Offices of All Other Miscellaneous Health Practitioners in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services › 6213 Offices of Other Health Practitioners. In the code order it is number 869 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 62139 Offices of All Other Health Practitioners, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments of independent health practitioners (except physicians; dentists; chiropractors; optometrists; mental health specialists; physical, occupational, and speech therapists; audiologists; and podiatrists). These practitioners operate private or group practices in their own offices (e.g., centers, clinics) or in the facilities of others, such as hospitals or HMO medical centers.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 621399 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
