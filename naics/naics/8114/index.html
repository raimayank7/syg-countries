NAICS 8114: Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

8114 is the NAICS industry group Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 811 Repair and Maintenance.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in home and garden equipment and appliance repair and maintenance; reupholstery and furniture repair; footwear and leather goods repair; and other personal and household goods repair and maintenance.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is home and garden equipment and appliance repair and maintenance.

How the code is read

Common questions

What is NAICS code 8114?

NAICS 8114 is the industry group "Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 811 Repair and Maintenance.

What does NAICS 8114 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is home and garden equipment and appliance repair and maintenance.

Which sector is 8114 in?

8114 belongs to NAICS sector 81, Other Services (except Public Administration).

How many national industries does 8114 cover?

8114 covers 5 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 8114 different from 8111?

8114 is Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance, while 8111 is Automotive Repair and Maintenance. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
