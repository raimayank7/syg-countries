NAICS 423840: Industrial Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

423840 is the NAICS national industry Industrial Supplies Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods › 4238 Machinery, Equipment, and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 506 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42384 Industrial Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of supplies for machinery and equipment generally used in manufacturing, oil well, and warehousing activities.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is the merchant wholesale distribution of supplies for machinery and equipment generally used in manufacturing, oil well, and warehousing activities.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 423840 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
