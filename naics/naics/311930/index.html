NAICS 311930: Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

311930 is the NAICS national industry Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 311 Food Manufacturing › 3119 Other Food Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 169 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31193 Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing flavoring syrup drink concentrates and related products for soda fountain use or for the manufacture of soft drinks.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing flavoring syrup drink concentrates and related products for soda fountain use or for the manufacture of soft drinks.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 311930 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 311930 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 311 is the subsector Food Manufacturing
  • 3119 is the industry group Other Food Manufacturing
  • 31193 is the five-digit industry Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing
  • 311930 is the national industry Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 12 terms to 311930, from "Beverage bases manufacturing" to "Syrup, flavoring (except coffee based), manufacturing". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Beverage bases manufacturing; Beverage flavorings (except coffee based) manufacturing; Beverage syrups (except coffee based) manufacturing; Concentrates, drink (except frozen fruit juice), manufacturing; Concentrates, flavoring (except coffee based), manufacturing; Flavoring concentrates (except coffee based) manufacturing; Flavoring pastes, powders, and syrups for soft drink manufacturing; Fruit syrups, flavoring, manufacturing; Soda fountain syrups manufacturing; Soft drink concentrates (i.e., syrup) manufacturing; Syrup, beverage, manufacturing; Syrup, flavoring (except coffee based), manufacturing.

6 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Beverage flavorings (except coffee based) manufacturing; Beverage syrups (except coffee based) manufacturing; Concentrates, drink (except frozen fruit juice), manufacturing; Concentrates, flavoring (except coffee based), manufacturing; Flavoring concentrates (except coffee based) manufacturing; Syrup, flavoring (except coffee based), manufacturing.
