NAICS 213112: Support Activities for Oil and Gas Operations

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

213112 is the NAICS national industry Support Activities for Oil and Gas Operations in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 213 Support Activities for Mining › 2131 Support Activities for Mining. In the code order it is number 82 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 21311 Support Activities for Mining, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing support activities, on a contract or fee basis, for oil and gas operations (except geophysical surveying and mapping, site preparation, construction, and transportation activities). Services included are exploration; excavating slush pits and cellars, well surveying; running, cutting, and pulling casings, tubes, and rods; cementing wells, shooting wells; perforating well casings; acidizing and chemically treating wells; and cleaning out, bailing, and swabbing wells.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is performing support activities, on a contract or fee basis, for oil and gas operations (except geophysical surveying and mapping, site preparation, construction, and transportation activities).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 213112 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 213112?

NAICS 213112 is the national industry "Support Activities for Oil and Gas Operations" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 2131 Support Activities for Mining.

What does NAICS 213112 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is performing support activities, on a contract or fee basis, for oil and gas operations (except geophysical surveying and mapping, site preparation, construction, and transportation activities).

Which sector is 213112 in?

213112 belongs to NAICS sector 21, Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction.

What kinds of business are indexed under 213112?

The Census index file lists 70 terms for it, for example Acidizing oil and gas field wells on a contract basis; Building oil and gas well foundations on a contract basis; Building, erecting, repairing, and dismantling oil and gas field rigs and derricks on a contract basis; Cementing oil and gas well casings on a contract basis.

How is 213112 different from 213111?

213112 is Support Activities for Oil and Gas Operations, while 213111 is Drilling Oil and Gas Wells. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
