NAICS 333514: Special Die and Tool, Die Set, Jig, and Fixture Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333514 is the NAICS national industry Special Die and Tool, Die Set, Jig, and Fixture Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3335 Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 364 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33351 Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments, known as tool and die shops, primarily engaged in manufacturing special tools and fixtures, such as cutting dies and jigs.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing special tools and fixtures, such as cutting dies and jigs.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333514 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 333514 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 333 is the subsector Machinery Manufacturing
  • 3335 is the industry group Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing
  • 33351 is the five-digit industry Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing
  • 333514 is the national industry Special Die and Tool, Die Set, Jig, and Fixture Manufacturing

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 14 terms to 333514, from "Cutting dies, metalworking, manufacturing" to "Wire drawing and straightening dies manufacturing". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Cutting dies, metalworking, manufacturing; Diamond dies, metalworking, manufacturing; Die sets for metal stamping presses manufacturing; Dies and die holders for metal cutting and forming (except threading) manufacturing; Dies, metalworking (except threading), manufacturing; Dies, plastics forming, manufacturing; Dies, steel rule, metal cutting, manufacturing; Extrusion dies for use with all materials manufacturing; Jigs (e.g., checking, gauging, inspection) manufacturing; Jigs and fixtures for use with machine tools manufacturing; Punches for use with machine tools manufacturing; Subpresses, machine tool, manufacturing; Welding positioners (i.e., jigs) manufacturing; Wire drawing and straightening dies manufacturing.

2 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Dies and die holders for metal cutting and forming (except threading) manufacturing; Dies, metalworking (except threading), manufacturing.

Neighbouring national industries

Special Die and Tool, Die Set, Jig, and Fixture Manufacturing sits in the NAICS industry 33351 Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing, which has 5 national industries in all. The others are: 333511 Industrial Mold Manufacturing, 333515 Cutting Tool and Machine Tool Accessory Manufacturing, 333517 Machine Tool Manufacturing, 333519 Rolling Mill and Other Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 333514 the main activity here is manufacturing special tools and fixtures, such as cutting dies and jigs. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 333515 Cutting Tool and Machine Tool Accessory Manufacturing: main activity is manufacturing accessories and attachments for metal cutting and metal forming machine tools
  • 333511 Industrial Mold Manufacturing: main activity is manufacturing industrial molds for casting metals or forming other materials, such as plastics, glass, or rubber
  • 333517 Machine Tool Manufacturing: main activity is (1) manufacturing metal cutting machine tools (except handtools) and/or (2) manufacturing metal forming machine tools (except handtools), such as punching, sheering, bending, forming, pressing, forging and die-casting machines

The NAICS industry around it

One level up is the NAICS industry 33351 Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing. The Census describes it this way: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing metalworking machinery, such as metal cutting and metal forming machine tools; cutting tools; accessories for metalworking machinery; special dies, tools, jigs, and fixtures; industrial molds; rolling mill machinery; assembly machinery; coil handling, conversion, or straightening equipment; and wire drawing and fabricating
