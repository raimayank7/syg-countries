NAICS 561439: Other Business Service Centers (including Copy Shops)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

561439 is the NAICS national industry Other Business Service Centers (including Copy Shops) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 561 Administrative and Support Services › 5614 Business Support Services. In the code order it is number 810 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 56143 Business Service Centers, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises (1) establishments generally known as copy centers or shops primarily engaged in providing photocopying, duplicating, blueprinting, and other document copying services, without also providing printing services (e.g., offset printing, quick printing, digital printing, prepress services) and (2) establishments (except private mail centers) engaged in providing a range of office support services (except printing services), such as document copying services, facsimile services, word processing services, on-site PC rental services, and office product sales.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 561439 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 561439 from the left:

  • 561 is the subsector Administrative and Support Services
  • 5614 is the industry group Business Support Services
  • 56143 is the five-digit industry Business Service Centers

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 10 terms to 561439, from "Blueprinting services" to "Reprographic services". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Blueprinting services; Business service centers (except private mail centers); Business service centers (except private mail centers) providing range of office support services (except printing); Copy centers (except combined with printing services); Copy shops (except combined with printing services); Document copying services (except combined with printing services); Document duplicating services (except combined with printing services); Internet cafes (i.e., not serving food and beverages); Photocopying services (except combined with printing services); Reprographic services.

7 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Business service centers (except private mail centers); Business service centers (except private mail centers) providing range of office support services (except printing); Copy centers (except combined with printing services); Copy shops (except combined with printing services); Document copying services (except combined with printing services); Document duplicating services (except combined with printing services).
