NAICS 561990: All Other Support Services

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

561990 is the NAICS national industry All Other Support Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 561 Administrative and Support Services › 5619 Other Support Services. In the code order it is number 832 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 56199 All Other Support Services, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing day-to-day business and other organizational support services (except office administrative services, facilities support services, employment services, business support services, travel arrangement and reservation services, security and investigation services, services to buildings and other structures, packaging and labeling services, and convention and trade show organizing services).

Illustrative Examples:

Bartering services Flagging (i.e., traffic control) services Bottle exchanges Float decorating services Inventory taking services Contract meter reading services Lumber grading services Diving services on a contract or fee basis

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 561990 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 561990 from the left:

  • 561 is the subsector Administrative and Support Services
  • 5619 is the industry group Other Support Services
