NAICS 333310: Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333310 is the NAICS national industry Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3333 Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 359 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33331 Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing commercial and service industry machinery, such as optical instruments and lenses (except ophthalmic), photographic and photocopying equipment, automatic vending machinery, commercial laundry and drycleaning machinery, office machinery, automotive maintenance equipment (except mechanics' handtools), and commercial-type cooking equipment.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333310 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 333310 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 333 is the subsector Machinery Manufacturing
  • 3333 is the industry group Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing
  • 33331 is the five-digit industry Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing
  • 333310 is the national industry Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing
