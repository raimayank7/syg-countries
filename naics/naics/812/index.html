NAICS 812: Personal and Laundry Services

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

812 is the NAICS subsector Personal and Laundry Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration).

What the Census says

Industries in the Personal and Laundry Services subsector group establishments that provide personal and laundry services to individuals, households, and businesses. Services performed include: personal care services; death care services; laundry and drycleaning services; and a wide range of other personal services, such as pet care (except veterinary) services, photofinishing services, temporary parking services, and dating services.

The Personal and Laundry Services subsector is by no means all-inclusive of the services that could be termed personal services (i.e., those provided to individuals rather than businesses). There are many other subsectors, as well as sectors, that provide services to persons. Establishments providing legal, accounting, tax preparation, architectural, portrait photography, and similar professional services are classified in Sector 54, Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services; those providing job placement, travel arrangement, home security, interior and exterior house cleaning, exterminating, lawn and garden care, and similar support services are classified in Sector 56, Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services; those providing health and social services are classified in Sector 62, Health Care and Social Assistance; those providing amusement and recreation services are classified in Sector 71, Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation; those providing educational instruction are classified in Sector 61, Educational Services; those providing repair services are classified in Subsector 811, Repair and Maintenance; and those providing spiritual, civic, and advocacy services are classified in Subsector 813, Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations.

What it contains

Personal and Laundry Services has 4 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 8121 Personal Care Services (5 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments, such as barber and beauty shops, that provide appearance care services to individual consumers.
  • 8122 Death Care Services (2 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in preparing the dead for burial or interment, conducting funerals, operating sites or structures reserved for the interment of human or...
  • 8123 Drycleaning and Laundry Services (4 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating coin- or card-operated or similar self-service laundries and drycleaners
  • 8129 Other Personal Services (5 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, or drycleaning and laundry services).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 16 national industries under Personal and Laundry Services.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 812 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 812 is the subsector Personal and Laundry Services

Neighbouring subsectors

Personal and Laundry Services sits in the sector 81 Other Services (except Public Administration), which has 4 subsectors in all. The others are: 811 Repair and Maintenance, 813 Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations, 814 Private Households.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 812 the Industries in the Personal and Laundry Services subsector group establishments that provide personal and laundry services to individuals, households, and businesses.. The nearest codes in the same sector say:

  • 811 Repair and Maintenance: Industries in the Repair and Maintenance subsector restore machinery, equipment, and other products to working order.
  • 813 Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations: Industries in the Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations subsector group establishments that organize and promote religious activities
  • 814 Private Households: The Private Households subsector includes private households that engage in employing workers on or about the premises in activities primarily concerned with the operation of the household.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 812?

NAICS 812 is the subsector "Personal and Laundry Services" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration).

What does NAICS 812 include?

Industries in the Personal and Laundry Services subsector group establishments that provide personal and laundry services to individuals, households, and businesses.

Which sector is 812 in?

812 belongs to NAICS sector 81, Other Services (except Public Administration).

How many national industries does 812 cover?

812 covers 16 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 812 different from 811?

812 is Personal and Laundry Services, while 811 is Repair and Maintenance. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
