NAICS 3132: Fabric Mills

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3132 is the NAICS industry group Fabric Mills in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 313 Textile Mills.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following: (1) weaving broadwoven fabrics and felts (except tire fabrics and rugs); (2) weaving or braiding narrow fabrics; (3) making fabric-covered elastic yarn and thread; (4) manufacturing Schiffli machine embroideries; (5) manufacturing nonwoven fabrics and felts; (6) knitting weft (i.e., circular) and warp (i.e., flat) fabric; (7) knitting and finishing weft and warp fabric; (8) manufacturing lace; or (9) manufacturing, dyeing, and finishing lace and lace goods.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is one of the following: (1) weaving broadwoven fabrics and felts (except tire fabrics and rugs).

What it contains

Fabric Mills has 4 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 313210 Broadwoven Fabric Mills: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in weaving broadwoven fabrics and felts (except tire fabrics and rugs).
  • 313220 Narrow Fabric Mills and Schiffli Machine Embroidery: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following
  • 313230 Nonwoven Fabric Mills: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing nonwoven fabrics and felts.
  • 313240 Knit Fabric Mills: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 4 national industries under Fabric Mills.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 3132 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 313 is the subsector Textile Mills
  • 3132 is the industry group Fabric Mills

Neighbouring industry groups

Fabric Mills sits in the subsector 313 Textile Mills, which has 3 industry groups in all. The others are: 3131 Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills, 3133 Textile and Fabric Finishing and Fabric Coating Mills.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 3132 the main activity here is one of the following: (1) weaving broadwoven fabrics and felts (except tire fabrics and rugs). The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 3133 Textile and Fabric Finishing and Fabric Coating Mills: main activity is one of the following: (1) finishing textiles, fabrics, and apparel

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 313 Textile Mills. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Textile Mills subsector group establishments that transform a basic fiber (natural or synthetic) into a product, such as yarn or fabric that is further manufactured into usable items, such as apparel, sheets, towels, and textile bags for individual or industrial consumption. The further manufacturing may be performed in the same establishment and classified in this subsector, or it may be performed at a separate establishment and be classified elsewhere in the Manufacturing sector. Common questions

What is NAICS code 3132?

NAICS 3132 is the industry group "Fabric Mills" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 313 Textile Mills.

What does NAICS 3132 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is one of the following: (1) weaving broadwoven fabrics and felts (except tire fabrics and rugs).

Which sector is 3132 in?

3132 belongs to NAICS sector 31-33, Manufacturing.

How many national industries does 3132 cover?

3132 covers 4 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 3132 different from 3131?

3132 is Fabric Mills, while 3131 is Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
