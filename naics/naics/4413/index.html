NAICS 4413: Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4413 is the NAICS industry group Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 44-45 Retail Trade › 441 Motor Vehicle and Parts Dealers.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, including tires and tubes. Included in this industry group are establishments primarily engaged in retailing automotive parts and accessories in combination with automotive repair services.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, including tires and tubes.

What it contains

Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers has 2 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 441330 Automotive Parts and Accessories Retailers: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, with or without repairing automobiles
  • 441340 Tire Dealers: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new and/or used tires and tubes or retailing new tires in combination with automotive repair services.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 2 national industries under Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers.

The industry in full: 441330 Automotive Parts and Accessories Retailers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, with or without repairing automobiles; and/or establishments primarily engaged in retailing and installing automotive accessories.

The industry in full: 441340 Tire Dealers

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 4413 from the left:

  • 44-45 is the sector Retail Trade
  • 441 is the subsector Motor Vehicle and Parts Dealers
  • 4413 is the industry group Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers

Neighbouring industry groups

Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers sits in the subsector 441 Motor Vehicle and Parts Dealers, which has 3 industry groups in all. The others are: 4411 Automobile Dealers, 4412 Other Motor Vehicle Dealers.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 4413 the main activity here is retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, including tires and tubes. The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 4412 Other Motor Vehicle Dealers: main activity is retailing new and used vehicles (except automobiles, light trucks, such as sport utility vehicles, and passenger and cargo vans)
  • 4411 Automobile Dealers: main activity is retailing new and used automobiles and light trucks, such as sport utility vehicles, and passenger and cargo vans

The subsector around it

Common questions

What is NAICS code 4413?

NAICS 4413 is the industry group "Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 441 Motor Vehicle and Parts Dealers.

What does NAICS 4413 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is retailing new, used, and/or rebuilt automotive parts and accessories, including tires and tubes.

Which sector is 4413 in?

4413 belongs to NAICS sector 44-45, Retail Trade.

How many national industries does 4413 cover?

4413 covers 2 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 4413 different from 4411?

4413 is Automotive Parts, Accessories, and Tire Retailers, while 4411 is Automobile Dealers. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
