NAICS 423910: Sporting and Recreational Goods and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

423910 is the NAICS national industry Sporting and Recreational Goods and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods › 4239 Miscellaneous Durable Goods Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 509 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42391 Sporting and Recreational Goods and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of sporting goods and accessories; billiard and pool supplies; sporting firearms and ammunition; and/or marine pleasure craft, equipment, and supplies.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of sporting goods and accessories.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 423910 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
