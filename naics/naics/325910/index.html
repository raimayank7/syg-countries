NAICS 325910: Printing Ink Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

325910 is the NAICS national industry Printing Ink Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 325 Chemical Manufacturing › 3259 Other Chemical Product and Preparation Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 257 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32591 Printing Ink Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing printing and inkjet inks and inkjet cartridges.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing printing and inkjet inks and inkjet cartridges.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 325910 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 325910 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 325 is the subsector Chemical Manufacturing
  • 3259 is the industry group Other Chemical Product and Preparation Manufacturing
  • 32591 is the five-digit industry Printing Ink Manufacturing
  • 325910 is the national industry Printing Ink Manufacturing

The Census index file points 15 terms to 325910, from "Bronze printing inks manufacturing" to "Textile printing inks manufacturing". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any.
