NAICS 333120: Construction Machinery Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333120 is the NAICS national industry Construction Machinery Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3331 Agriculture, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 352 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33312 Construction Machinery Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing construction machinery, surface mining machinery, and logging equipment.

Illustrative Examples:

Backhoes manufacturing Bulldozers manufacturing Construction and surface mining-type rock drill bits manufacturing Construction-type tractors and attachments manufacturing Off-highway trucks manufacturing Pile-driving equipment manufacturing Portable crushing, pulverizing, and screening machinery manufacturing Powered post hole diggers manufacturing Road graders manufacturing Surface mining machinery (except drilling) manufacturing

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing construction machinery, surface mining machinery, and logging equipment.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 333120 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing

  • 3331 is the industry group Agriculture, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing
  • 33312 is the five-digit industry Construction Machinery Manufacturing

What is NAICS code 333120?

How is 333120 different from 333111?

333120 is Construction Machinery Manufacturing, while 333111 is Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
