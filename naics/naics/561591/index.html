NAICS 561591: Convention and Visitors Bureaus

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

561591 is the NAICS national industry Convention and Visitors Bureaus in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 561 Administrative and Support Services › 5615 Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services. In the code order it is number 818 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 56159 Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in marketing and promoting communities and facilities to businesses and leisure travelers through a range of activities, such as assisting organizations in locating meeting and convention sites; providing travel information on area attractions, lodging accommodations, restaurants; providing maps; and organizing group tours of local historical, recreational, and cultural attractions.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is marketing and promoting communities and facilities to businesses and leisure travelers through a range of activities, such as assisting organizations in locating meeting and convention sites.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 561591 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 561591 from the left:

  • 56 is the sector Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services
  • 561 is the subsector Administrative and Support Services
  • 5615 is the industry group Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services
  • 56159 is the five-digit industry Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services
  • 561591 is the national industry Convention and Visitors Bureaus
