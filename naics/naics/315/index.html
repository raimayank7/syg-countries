NAICS 315: Apparel Manufacturing

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

315 is the NAICS subsector Apparel Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing.

What the Census says

Industries in the Apparel Manufacturing subsector group establishments with two distinct manufacturing processes: (1) cut and sew (i.e., purchasing fabric and cutting and sewing to make a garment) and (2) the manufacture of garments in establishments that first knit fabric and then cut and sew the fabric into a garment. The Apparel Manufacturing subsector includes a diverse range of establishments manufacturing full lines of ready-to-wear apparel and custom apparel: apparel contractors, performing cutting or sewing operations on materials owned by others; jobbers, performing entrepreneurial functions involved in apparel manufacturing; and tailors, manufacturing custom garments for individual clients. Knitting fabric, when done alone, is classified in the Textile Mills subsector, but when knitting is combined with the production of complete garments, the activity is classified in the Apparel Manufacturing subsector.

What it contains

Apparel Manufacturing has 3 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 3151 Apparel Knitting Mills (1 national industry)
  • 3152 Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (2 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing cut and sew apparel from woven fabric or purchased knit fabric.
  • 3159 Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing (1 national industry)

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 4 national industries under Apparel Manufacturing.

How many national industries does 315 cover?

How is 315 different from 311?

315 is Apparel Manufacturing, while 311 is Food Manufacturing. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
