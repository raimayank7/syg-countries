NAICS 333612: Speed Changer, Industrial High-Speed Drive, and Gear Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333612 is the NAICS national industry Speed Changer, Industrial High-Speed Drive, and Gear Manufacturing. It is part of the industry group 3336 Engine, Turbine, and Power Transmission Equipment Manufacturing.

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing gears, speed changers, and industrial high-speed drives (except hydrostatic).
