NAICS 331110: Iron and Steel Mills and Ferroalloy Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

331110 is the NAICS national industry Iron and Steel Mills and Ferroalloy Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 331 Primary Metal Manufacturing › 3311 Iron and Steel Mills and Ferroalloy Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 296 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33111 Iron and Steel Mills and Ferroalloy Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) direct reduction of iron ore; (2) manufacturing pig iron in molten or solid form; (3) converting pig iron into steel; (4) making steel; (5) making steel and manufacturing shapes (e.g., bar, plate, rod, sheet, strip, wire); (6) making steel and forming pipe and tube; and (7) manufacturing electrometallurgical ferroalloys. Ferroalloys add critical elements, such as silicon and manganese for carbon steel and chromium, vanadium, tungsten, titanium, and molybdenum for low- and high-alloy metals. Ferroalloys include iron-rich alloys and more pure forms of elements added during the steel manufacturing process that alter or improve the characteristics of the metal.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is one or more of the following: (1) direct reduction of iron ore.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 331110 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
