NAICS 424690: Other Chemical and Allied Products Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

424690 is the NAICS national industry Other Chemical and Allied Products Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods › 4246 Chemical and Allied Products Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 534 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42469 Other Chemical and Allied Products Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of chemicals and allied products (except agricultural and medicinal chemicals, paints and varnishes, fireworks, and plastics materials and basic forms and shapes).

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of chemicals and allied products (except agricultural and medicinal chemicals, paints and varnishes, fireworks, and plastics materials and basic forms and shapes).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 424690 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
