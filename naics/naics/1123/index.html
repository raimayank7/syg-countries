NAICS 1123: Poultry and Egg Production

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

1123 is the NAICS industry group Poultry and Egg Production in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in breeding, hatching, and raising poultry for meat or egg production.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is breeding, hatching, and raising poultry for meat or egg production.

What it contains

Poultry and Egg Production has 5 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 112310 Chicken Egg Production: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in raising chickens for egg production.
  • 112320 Broilers and Other Meat Type Chicken Production: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in raising broilers, fryers, roasters, and other meat type chickens.
  • 112330 Turkey Production: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in raising turkeys for meat or egg production.
  • 112340 Poultry Hatcheries: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in hatching poultry of any kind.
  • 112390 Other Poultry Production: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in raising poultry (except chickens for meat or egg production and turkeys).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 5 national industries under Poultry and Egg Production.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 1123 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 112 is the subsector Animal Production and Aquaculture
  • 1123 is the industry group Poultry and Egg Production

Neighbouring industry groups

Poultry and Egg Production sits in the subsector 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture, which has 6 industry groups in all. The others are: 1121 Cattle Ranching and Farming, 1122 Hog and Pig Farming, 1124 Sheep and Goat Farming, 1125 Aquaculture, 1129 Other Animal Production.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 1123 the main activity here is breeding, hatching, and raising poultry for meat or egg production. The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 1124 Sheep and Goat Farming: main activity is raising sheep, lambs, and goats, or feeding lambs for fattening
  • 1121 Cattle Ranching and Farming: main activity is raising cattle, milking dairy cattle, or feeding cattle for fattening

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Animal Production and Aquaculture subsector raise or fatten animals for the sale of animals or animal products and/or raise aquatic plants and animals in controlled or selected aquatic environments for the sale of aquatic plants, animals, or their products. The subsector includes establishments, such as ranches, farms, and feedlots, primarily engaged in keeping, grazing, breeding, or feeding animals. Common questions

What is NAICS code 1123?

NAICS 1123 is the industry group "Poultry and Egg Production" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture.

What does NAICS 1123 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is breeding, hatching, and raising poultry for meat or egg production.

Which sector is 1123 in?

1123 belongs to NAICS sector 11, Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

How many national industries does 1123 cover?

1123 covers 5 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 1123 different from 1121?

1123 is Poultry and Egg Production, while 1121 is Cattle Ranching and Farming. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
