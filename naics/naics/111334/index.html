NAICS 111334: Berry (except Strawberry) Farming

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

111334 is the NAICS national industry Berry (except Strawberry) Farming in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 111 Crop Production › 1113 Fruit and Tree Nut Farming. In the code order it is number 16 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 7 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 11133 Noncitrus Fruit and Tree Nut Farming, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing berries.

Illustrative Examples:

Berry (except strawberries) farming Cranberry farming Blackberry farming Currant farming Blueberry farming Raspberry farming

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is growing berries.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 111334 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 111334 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 111 is the subsector Crop Production
  • 1113 is the industry group Fruit and Tree Nut Farming
  • 11133 is the five-digit industry Noncitrus Fruit and Tree Nut Farming
  • 111334 is the national industry Berry (except Strawberry) Farming

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 12 terms to 111334, from "Berry (except strawberry) farming" to "Youngberry farming". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Berry (except strawberry) farming; Blackberry farming; Blueberry farming; Boysenberry farming; Cranberry farming; Currant farming; Dewberry farming; Gooseberry farming; Huckleberry farming; Loganberry farming; Raspberry farming; Youngberry farming.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Berry (except strawberry) farming.

Neighbouring national industries

Berry (except Strawberry) Farming sits in the NAICS industry 11133 Noncitrus Fruit and Tree Nut Farming, which has 7 national industries in all. The others are: 111331 Apple Orchards, 111332 Grape Vineyards, 111333 Strawberry Farming, 111335 Tree Nut Farming, 111336 Fruit and Tree Nut Combination Farming, 111339 Other Noncitrus Fruit Farming.
