NAICS 334514: Totalizing Fluid Meter and Counting Device Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

334514 is the NAICS national industry Totalizing Fluid Meter and Counting Device Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing › 3345 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 402 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 9 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33451 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing totalizing (i.e., registering) fluid meters and counting devices. Examples of products made by these establishments are gas consumption meters, water consumption meters, parking meters, taxi meters, motor vehicle gauges, and fare collection equipment.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing totalizing (i.e., registering) fluid meters and counting devices.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 334514 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
