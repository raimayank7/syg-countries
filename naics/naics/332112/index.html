NAICS 332112: Nonferrous Forging

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

332112 is the NAICS national industry Nonferrous Forging in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing › 3321 Forging and Stamping. In the code order it is number 315 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33211 Forging and Stamping, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing nonferrous forgings from purchased nonferrous metals by hammering mill shapes. Establishments making nonferrous forgings and further manufacturing (e.g., machining, assembling) a specific manufactured product are classified in the industry of the finished product. Nonferrous forging establishments may perform surface finishing operations, such as cleaning and deburring, on the forgings they manufacture.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing nonferrous forgings from purchased nonferrous metals by hammering mill shapes.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 332112 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 332112 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 332 is the subsector Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing
  • 3321 is the industry group Forging and Stamping
  • 33211 is the five-digit industry Forging and Stamping
  • 332112 is the national industry Nonferrous Forging

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 8 terms to 332112, from "Aluminum forgings made from purchased metals, unfinished" to "Upset forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Aluminum forgings made from purchased metals, unfinished; Cold forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished; Copper forgings made from purchased metals, unfinished; Hammer forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished; Hot forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished; Press forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished; Titanium forgings made from purchased metals, unfinished; Upset forgings made from purchased nonferrous metals, unfinished.

Neighbouring national industries

Nonferrous Forging sits in the NAICS industry 33211 Forging and Stamping, which has 5 national industries in all. The others are: 332111 Iron and Steel Forging, 332114 Custom Roll Forming, 332117 Powder Metallurgy Part Manufacturing, 332119 Metal Crown, Closure, and Other Metal Stamping (except Automotive).

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 332112 the main activity here is manufacturing nonferrous forgings from purchased nonferrous metals by hammering mill shapes. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 332111 Iron and Steel Forging: main activity is manufacturing iron and steel forgings from purchased iron and steel by hammering mill shapes
  • 332114 Custom Roll Forming: main activity is custom roll forming metal products by use of rotary motion of rolls with various contours to bend or shape the products
  • 332117 Powder Metallurgy Part Manufacturing: main activity is manufacturing powder metallurgy products using any of the various powder metallurgy processing techniques, such as pressing and sintering or metal injection molding

What is NAICS code 332112?

What does NAICS 332112 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is manufacturing nonferrous forgings from purchased nonferrous metals by hammering mill shapes.

Which sector is 332112 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 332112?

How is 332112 different from 332111?

332112 is Nonferrous Forging, while 332111 is Iron and Steel Forging. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
