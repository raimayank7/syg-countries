NAICS 8129: Other Personal Services

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

8129 is the NAICS industry group Other Personal Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 812 Personal and Laundry Services.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, or drycleaning and laundry services).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, or drycleaning and laundry services).

Neighbouring industry groups

Other Personal Services sits in the subsector 812 Personal and Laundry Services, which has 4 industry groups in all. The others are: 8121 Personal Care Services, 8122 Death Care Services, 8123 Drycleaning and Laundry Services.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 8129 the main activity here is providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, or drycleaning and laundry services). The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 8123 Drycleaning and Laundry Services: main activity is operating coin- or card-operated or similar self-service laundries and drycleaners

Common questions

What is NAICS code 8129?

NAICS 8129 is the industry group "Other Personal Services" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 812 Personal and Laundry Services.

What does NAICS 8129 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, or drycleaning and laundry services).

Which sector is 8129 in?

How many national industries does 8129 cover?

How is 8129 different from 8121?

8129 is Other Personal Services, while 8121 is Personal Care Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
