NAICS 4234: Professional and Commercial Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4234 is the NAICS industry group Professional and Commercial Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of photographic equipment and supplies; office, computer, and computer peripheral equipment; and medical, dental, hospital, ophthalmic, and other commercial and professional equipment and supplies.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of photographic equipment and supplies.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods. What is NAICS code 4234?

NAICS 4234 is the industry group "Professional and Commercial Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods.

What does NAICS 4234 include?

Which sector is 4234 in?

How many national industries does 4234 cover?

How is 4234 different from 4231?

4234 is Professional and Commercial Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, while 4231 is Motor Vehicle and Motor Vehicle Parts and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
