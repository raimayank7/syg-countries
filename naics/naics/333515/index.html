NAICS 333515: Cutting Tool and Machine Tool Accessory Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333515 is the NAICS national industry Cutting Tool and Machine Tool Accessory Manufacturing. It is part of the industry group 3335 Metalworking Machinery Manufacturing.

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing accessories and attachments for metal cutting and metal forming machine tools.

Illustrative Examples:

Knives and bits for metalworking lathes, planers, and shapers manufacturing Measuring attachments (e.g., sine bars) for machine tool manufacturing Metalworking drill bits manufacturing Taps and dies (i.e., machine tool accessories) manufacturing
