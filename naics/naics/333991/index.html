NAICS 333991: Power-Driven Handtool Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333991 is the NAICS national industry Power-Driven Handtool Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3339 Other General Purpose Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 378 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 7 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33399 All Other General Purpose Machinery Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing power-driven (e.g., battery, corded, pneumatic) handtools, such as drills, screwguns, circular saws, chain saws, staplers, and nailers.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing power-driven (e.g., battery, corded, pneumatic) handtools, such as drills, screwguns, circular saws, chain saws, staplers, and nailers.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333991 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
