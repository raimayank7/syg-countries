NAICS 339999: All Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339999 is the NAICS national industry All Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3399 Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 476 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 6 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33999 All Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in miscellaneous manufacturing (except medical equipment and supplies, jewelry and silverware, sporting and athletic goods, dolls, toys, games, office supplies, signs, gaskets, packing, and sealing devices, musical instruments, fasteners, buttons, needles, pins, brooms, brushes, mops, and burial caskets).

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is miscellaneous manufacturing (except medical equipment and supplies, jewelry and silverware, sporting and athletic goods, dolls, toys, games, office supplies, signs, gaskets, packing, and sealing devices, musical instruments, fasteners, buttons, needles, pins, brooms, brushes, mops, and burial caskets).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339999 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
