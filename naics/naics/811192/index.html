NAICS 811192: Car Washes

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

811192 is the NAICS national industry Car Washes in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 811 Repair and Maintenance › 8111 Automotive Repair and Maintenance. In the code order it is number 945 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 81119 Other Automotive Repair and Maintenance, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in cleaning, washing, and/or waxing automotive vehicles, such as passenger cars, trucks, and vans, and trailers.

Illustrative Examples:

Automotive detail shops Mobile car and truck washes Car washes Self-service car washes

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is cleaning, washing, and/or waxing automotive vehicles, such as passenger cars, trucks, and vans, and trailers.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 811192 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 811 is the subsector Repair and Maintenance
  • 8111 is the industry group Automotive Repair and Maintenance
  • 81119 is the five-digit industry Other Automotive Repair and Maintenance
  • 811192 is the national industry Car Washes

The Census index file points 9 terms to 811192, from "Automotive detail shops" to "Truck and bus washes". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Automotive detail shops; Automotive detailing services (i.e., cleaning, polishing); Automotive washing and polishing; Car detailers; Car washes; Detailing services (i.e., cleaning and polishing), automotive; Mobile car and truck washes; Self-service car washes; Truck and bus washes.
