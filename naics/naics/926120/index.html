NAICS 926120: Regulation and Administration of Transportation Programs

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

926120 is the NAICS national industry Regulation and Administration of Transportation Programs in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 92 Public Administration › 926 Administration of Economic Programs › 9261 Administration of Economic Programs. In the code order it is number 1006 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 92612 Regulation and Administration of Transportation Programs, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises government establishments primarily engaged in the administration, regulation, licensing, planning, inspection, and investigation of transportation services and facilities. Included in this industry are government establishments responsible for motor vehicle and operator licensing, the Coast Guard (except the Coast Guard Academy), and parking authorities.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is the administration, regulation, licensing, planning, inspection, and investigation of transportation services and facilities.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 926120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 926120 from the left:

  • 92 is the sector Public Administration
  • 926 is the subsector Administration of Economic Programs
  • 9261 is the industry group Administration of Economic Programs
  • 92612 is the five-digit industry Regulation and Administration of Transportation Programs
  • 926120 is the national industry Regulation and Administration of Transportation Programs

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 15 terms to 926120, from "Aircraft inspection, government" to "Transportation safety programs, government". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Aircraft inspection, government; Coast Guard (except academy); Federal Aviation Administration (except air traffic control); Licensing of transportation equipment, facilities, and services; Merchant Marine (except academy); Motor carrier licensing and inspection offices; Motor vehicle licensing offices, government; National Transportation Safety Board; Port authorities and districts, nonoperating; Public transportation commissions, nonoperating; Railroad and warehouse commissions, nonoperating; Transit systems and authorities, nonoperating; Transportation departments, nonoperating; Transportation regulatory agencies; Transportation safety programs, government.

3 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Coast Guard (except academy); Federal Aviation Administration (except air traffic control); Merchant Marine (except academy).
