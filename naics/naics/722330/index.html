NAICS 722330: Mobile Food Services

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

722330 is the NAICS national industry Mobile Food Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 72 Accommodation and Food Services › 722 Food Services and Drinking Places › 7223 Special Food Services. In the code order it is number 934 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 72233 Mobile Food Services, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in preparing and serving meals and snacks for immediate consumption from motorized vehicles or nonmotorized carts. The establishment is the central location from which the caterer route is serviced, not each vehicle or cart. Included in this industry are establishments primarily engaged in providing food services from vehicles, such as hot dog carts and ice cream trucks.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is preparing and serving meals and snacks for immediate consumption from motorized vehicles or nonmotorized carts.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 722330 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 722330 from the left:

  • 72 is the sector Accommodation and Food Services
  • 722 is the subsector Food Services and Drinking Places
  • 7223 is the industry group Special Food Services
  • 72233 is the five-digit industry Mobile Food Services
  • 722330 is the national industry Mobile Food Services

Neighbouring national industries

Mobile Food Services sits in the industry group 7223 Special Food Services, which has 3 national industries in all. The others are: 722310 Food Service Contractors, 722320 Caterers.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 722330 the main activity here is preparing and serving meals and snacks for immediate consumption from motorized vehicles or nonmotorized carts. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:

  • 722320 Caterers: main activity is providing single event-based food services
  • 722310 Food Service Contractors: main activity is providing food services at institutional, governmental, commercial, or industrial locations of others based on contractual arrangements with these types of organizations for a specified period of time

The industry group around it

One level up is the industry group 7223 Special Food Services. The Census describes it this way: This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing food services at one or more of the following locations: (1) the customer's location; (2) a location designated by the customer; or (3) from motorized vehicles or nonmotorized
