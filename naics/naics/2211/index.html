NAICS 2211: Electric Power Generation, Transmission and Distribution

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

2211 is the NAICS industry group Electric Power Generation, Transmission and Distribution in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 22 Utilities › 221 Utilities.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in generating, transmitting, and/or distributing electric power. Establishments in this industry group may perform one or more of the following activities: (1) operate generation facilities that produce electric energy; (2) operate transmission systems that convey the electricity from the generation facility to the distribution system; and (3) operate distribution systems that convey electric power received from the generation facility or the transmission system to the final consumer.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is generating, transmitting, and/or distributing electric power.

What is NAICS code 2211?

What does NAICS 2211 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is generating, transmitting, and/or distributing electric power.

Which sector is 2211 in?

How many national industries does 2211 cover?

How is 2211 different from 2212?

2211 is Electric Power Generation, Transmission and Distribution, while 2212 is Natural Gas Distribution. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
