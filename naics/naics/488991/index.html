NAICS 488991: Packing and Crating

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

488991 is the NAICS national industry Packing and Crating in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 488 Support Activities for Transportation › 4889 Other Support Activities for Transportation. In the code order it is number 651 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 48899 Other Support Activities for Transportation, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in packing, crating, and otherwise preparing goods for transportation.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is packing, crating, and otherwise preparing goods for transportation.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 488991 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 3 terms to 488991, from "Crating goods for shipping" to "Preparing goods for transportation (i.e., crating, packing)". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Crating goods for shipping; Packing and preparing goods for shipping; Preparing goods for transportation (i.e., crating, packing).
