NAICS 335931: Current-Carrying Wiring Device Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

335931 is the NAICS national industry Current-Carrying Wiring Device Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 335 Electrical Equipment, Appliance, and Component Manufacturing › 3359 Other Electrical Equipment and Component Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 420 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33593 Wiring Device Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing current-carrying wiring devices.

Illustrative Examples:

Bus bars, electrical conductors (except switchgear-type), manufacturing GFCI (ground fault circuit interrupters) manufacturing Lamp holders manufacturing Lightning arrestors and coils manufacturing Receptacles (i.e., outlets), electrical, manufacturing Switches for electrical wiring (e.g., pressure, pushbutton, snap, tumbler) manufacturing

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing current-carrying wiring devices.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 335931 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 335931 from the left:
