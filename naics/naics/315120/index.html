NAICS 315120: Apparel Knitting Mills

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

315120 is the NAICS national industry Apparel Knitting Mills in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 315 Apparel Manufacturing › 3151 Apparel Knitting Mills. In the code order it is number 193 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31512 Apparel Knitting Mills, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in knitting apparel or knitting fabric and then manufacturing apparel. Jobbers, performing entrepreneurial functions involved in knitting apparel and accessories, are included.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is knitting apparel or knitting fabric and then manufacturing apparel.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 315120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 315120 from the left:
