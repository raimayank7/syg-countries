NAICS 6232: Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability, Mental Health, and Substance Abuse Facilities

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

6232 is the NAICS industry group Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability, Mental Health, and Substance Abuse Facilities in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing residential care (but not licensed hospital care) to people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, mental illness, or substance abuse problems.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is providing residential care (but not licensed hospital care) to people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, mental illness, or substance abuse problems.

The industry in full: 623210 Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability Facilities

This industry comprises establishments (e.g., group homes, hospitals, intermediate care facilities) primarily engaged in providing residential care services for persons with intellectual and developmental disabilities. These facilities may provide some health care, though the focus is room, board, protective supervision, and counseling.

The Census index lists these terms for it: Group homes, intellectual and developmental disability; Homes with or without health care, intellectual and developmental disability; Hospitals, intellectual and developmental disability; Intellectual and developmental disability facilities (e.g., homes, hospitals, intermediate care facilities), residential; Intellectual and developmental disability homes; Intellectual and developmental disability hospitals; Intellectual and developmental disability intermediate care facilities; Intermediate care facilities, intellectual and developmental disability.

The industry in full: 623220 Residential Mental Health and Substance Abuse Facilities

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing residential care and treatment for patients with mental health and substance abuse illnesses. These establishments provide room, board, supervision, and counseling services. Although medical services may be available at these establishments, they are incidental to the counseling, mental rehabilitation, and support services offered. These establishments generally provide a wide range of social services in addition to counseling.

Neighbouring industry groups

Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability, Mental Health, and Substance Abuse Facilities sits in the subsector 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities, which has 4 industry groups in all. The others are: 6231 Nursing Care Facilities (Skilled Nursing Facilities), 6233 Continuing Care Retirement Communities and Assisted Living Facilities for the Elderly, 6239 Other Residential Care Facilities.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Nursing and Residential Care Facilities subsector provide residential care combined with either nursing, supervisory, or other types of care as required by the residents. In this subsector, the facilities are a significant part of the production process, and the care provided is a mix of health and social services with the health services being largely some level of nursing

Common questions

What is NAICS code 6232?

NAICS 6232 is the industry group "Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability, Mental Health, and Substance Abuse Facilities" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities.

What does NAICS 6232 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is providing residential care (but not licensed hospital care) to people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, mental illness, or substance abuse problems.

Which sector is 6232 in?

6232 belongs to NAICS sector 62, Health Care and Social Assistance.

How many national industries does 6232 cover?

6232 covers 2 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 6232 different from 6231?

6232 is Residential Intellectual and Developmental Disability, Mental Health, and Substance Abuse Facilities, while 6231 is Nursing Care Facilities (Skilled Nursing Facilities). Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
