NAICS 115310: Support Activities for Forestry

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

115310 is the NAICS national industry Support Activities for Forestry in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry › 1153 Support Activities for Forestry. In the code order it is number 64 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 11531 Support Activities for Forestry, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing particular support activities related to timber production, wood technology, forestry economics and marketing, and forest protection. These establishments may provide support activities for forestry, such as estimating timber, forest firefighting, forest pest control, treating burned forests from the air for reforestation or on an emergency basis, and consulting on wood attributes and reforestation.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is performing particular support activities related to timber production, wood technology, forestry economics and marketing, and forest protection.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 115310 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 115310 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 115 is the subsector Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry
  • 1153 is the industry group Support Activities for Forestry
  • 11531 is the five-digit industry Support Activities for Forestry
  • 115310 is the national industry Support Activities for Forestry

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 13 terms to 115310, from "Aerial forest firefighting" to "Timber valuation". That equals the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Aerial forest firefighting; Aerial forest mulching or seeding; Aerial wildland firefighting; Cruising timber; Estimating timber; Fire prevention, forest; Firefighting, forest; Forest management plans preparation; Forest thinning; Forestry services; Pest control services, forestry; Reforestation (except forest nurseries); Timber valuation.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Reforestation (except forest nurseries).
