NAICS 331410: Nonferrous Metal (except Aluminum) Smelting and Refining

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

331410 is the NAICS national industry Nonferrous Metal (except Aluminum) Smelting and Refining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 331 Primary Metal Manufacturing › 3314 Nonferrous Metal (except Aluminum) Production and Processing. In the code order it is number 304 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33141 Nonferrous Metal (except Aluminum) Smelting and Refining, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) smelting ores into nonferrous metals and/or (2) the primary refining of nonferrous metals (except aluminum) by electrolytic methods or other processes.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is (1) smelting ores into nonferrous metals and/or (2) the primary refining of nonferrous metals (except aluminum) by electrolytic methods or other processes.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 331410 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
