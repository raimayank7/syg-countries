NAICS 5242: Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

5242 is the NAICS industry group Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 524 Insurance Carriers and Related Activities.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies or (2) providing other employee benefits and insurance related services, such as claims adjustment and third party administration.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is (1) acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies or (2) providing other employee benefits and insurance related services, such as claims adjustment and third party administration.
