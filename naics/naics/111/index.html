NAICS 111: Crop Production

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

111 is the NAICS subsector Crop Production in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

What the Census says

Industries in the Crop Production subsector grow crops mainly for food and fiber. The subsector comprises establishments, such as farms, orchards, groves, greenhouses, and nurseries, primarily engaged in growing crops, plants, vines, or trees and their seeds.

The industries in this subsector are grouped by similarity of production activity, including biological and physiological characteristics and economic requirements, the length of growing season, degree of crop rotation, extent of input specialization, labor requirements, and capital demands. The production process is typically completed when the raw product or commodity grown reaches the "farm gate" for market, that is, at the point of first sale or price determination.

Establishments are classified in the Crop Production subsector when crop production (i.e., value of crops for market) accounts for one-half or more of the establishment's total agricultural production. Within the subsector, establishments are classified in a specific industry when a product or industry family of products (i.e., oilseed and grain farming, vegetable and melon farming, fruit and tree nut farming) account for one-half or more of the establishment's agricultural production. Establishments with one-half or more crop production with no one product or family of products of an industry accounting for one-half of the establishment's agricultural production are treated as general combination crop farming and are classified in Industry 11199, All Other Crop Farming.

Industries in the Crop Production subsector include establishments that own, operate, and manage and those that operate and manage. Those that manage only are classified in Subsector 115, Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry. Establishments that raise aquatic plants in controlled or selected aquatic environments are classified in Subsector 112, Animal Production and Aquaculture.

What it contains

Crop Production has 5 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 1111 Oilseed and Grain Farming (8 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) growing oilseed and/or grain crops and/or (2) producing oilseed and grain seeds.
  • 1112 Vegetable and Melon Farming (2 national industries)
  • 1113 Fruit and Tree Nut Farming (9 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing fruit and/or tree nut crops.
  • 1114 Greenhouse, Nursery, and Floriculture Production (4 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing crops of any kind under cover and/or growing nursery stock and flowers.
  • 1119 Other Crop Farming (7 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) growing crops (except oilseed and/or grain

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 30 national industries under Crop Production.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 111 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 111 is the subsector Crop Production

Neighbouring subsectors

Crop Production sits in the sector 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting, which has 5 subsectors in all. The others are: 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture, 113 Forestry and Logging, 114 Fishing, Hunting and Trapping, 115 Support Activities for Agriculture and Forestry.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 111 the main activity here is growing crops, plants, vines, or trees and their seeds. The nearest codes in the same sector say:

  • 112 Animal Production and Aquaculture: main activity is keeping, grazing, breeding, or feeding animals
  • 113 Forestry and Logging: Industries in the Forestry and Logging subsector grow and harvest timber on a long production cycle (i.e., of 10 years or more).
  • 114 Fishing, Hunting and Trapping: Industries in the Fishing, Hunting and Trapping subsector harvest fish and other wild animals from their natural habitats and are dependent upon a continued supply of the natural resource.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 111?

NAICS 111 is the subsector "Crop Production" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

What does NAICS 111 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is growing crops, plants, vines, or trees and their seeds.

Which sector is 111 in?

111 belongs to NAICS sector 11, Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

How many national industries does 111 cover?

111 covers 30 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 111 different from 112?

111 is Crop Production, while 112 is Animal Production and Aquaculture. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
