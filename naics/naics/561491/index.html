NAICS 561491: Repossession Services

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

561491 is the NAICS national industry Repossession Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 56 Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services › 561 Administrative and Support Services › 5614 Business Support Services. In the code order it is number 813 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 56149 Other Business Support Services, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repossessing tangible assets (e.g., automobiles, boats, equipment, planes, furniture, appliances) for the creditor as a result of delinquent debts.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is repossessing tangible assets (e.g., automobiles, boats, equipment, planes, furniture, appliances) for the creditor as a result of delinquent debts.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 561491 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 561491 from the left:

  • 56 is the sector Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services
  • 561 is the subsector Administrative and Support Services
  • 5614 is the industry group Business Support Services
  • 56149 is the five-digit industry Other Business Support Services
  • 561491 is the national industry Repossession Services
