NAICS 813910: Business Associations

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

813910 is the NAICS national industry Business Associations in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 813 Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations › 8139 Business, Professional, Labor, Political, and Similar Organizations. In the code order it is number 978 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 81391 Business Associations, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in promoting the business interests of their members. These establishments may conduct research on new products and services; develop market statistics; sponsor quality and certification standards; lobby public officials; or publish newsletters, books, or periodicals for distribution to their members.

Illustrative Examples:

Agricultural organizations (except youth farming organizations, farm granges) Real estate boards Chambers of commerce Trade associations Manufacturers' associations

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is promoting the business interests of their members.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 813910 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 813910 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 813 is the subsector Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations
  • 8139 is the industry group Business, Professional, Labor, Political, and Similar Organizations
  • 81391 is the five-digit industry Business Associations
  • 813910 is the national industry Business Associations

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 813910 the main activity here is promoting the business interests of their members. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:

  • 813920 Professional Organizations: main activity is promoting the professional interests of their members and the profession as a whole
  • 813930 Labor Unions and Similar Labor Organizations: main activity is promoting the interests of organized labor and union employees
  • 813940 Political Organizations: main activity is promoting the interests of national, state, or local political parties or candidates
