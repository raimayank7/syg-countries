NAICS 423730: Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

423730 is the NAICS national industry Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods › 4237 Hardware, and Plumbing and Heating Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 501 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42373 Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of warm air heating and air-conditioning equipment and supplies.

Illustrative Examples:

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of warm air heating and air-conditioning equipment and supplies.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 423730 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 423730?

What does NAICS 423730 include?

Which sector is 423730 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 423730?

How is 423730 different from 423710?

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
