NAICS 321920: Wood Container and Pallet Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

321920 is the NAICS national industry Wood Container and Pallet Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 321 Wood Product Manufacturing › 3219 Other Wood Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 209 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32192 Wood Container and Pallet Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing wood pallets, wood box shook, wood boxes, other wood containers, and wood parts for pallets and containers.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing wood pallets, wood box shook, wood boxes, other wood containers, and wood parts for pallets and containers.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 321920 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 321920 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 321 is the subsector Wood Product Manufacturing
  • 3219 is the industry group Other Wood Product Manufacturing
  • 32192 is the five-digit industry Wood Container and Pallet Manufacturing
  • 321920 is the national industry Wood Container and Pallet Manufacturing
