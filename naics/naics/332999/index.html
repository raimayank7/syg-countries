NAICS 332999: All Other Miscellaneous Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

332999 is the NAICS national industry All Other Miscellaneous Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing › 3329 Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 349 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 6 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33299 All Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing fabricated metal products (except forgings and stampings, cutlery and handtools, architectural and structural metals, boilers, tanks, shipping containers, hardware, spring and wire products, machine shop products, turned products, screws, nuts and bolts, metal valves, ball and roller bearings, ammunition, small arms and other ordnances and accessories, and fabricated pipes and pipe fittings).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 332999 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
