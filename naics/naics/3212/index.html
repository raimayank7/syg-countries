NAICS 3212: Veneer, Plywood, and Engineered Wood Product Manufacturing

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3212 is the NAICS industry group Veneer, Plywood, and Engineered Wood Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 321 Wood Product Manufacturing.

What it contains

Veneer, Plywood, and Engineered Wood Product Manufacturing has 4 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 321211 Hardwood Veneer and Plywood Manufacturing: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing hardwood veneer and/or hardwood plywood.
  • 321212 Softwood Veneer and Plywood Manufacturing: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing softwood veneer and/or softwood plywood.
  • 321215 Engineered Wood Member Manufacturing: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing fabricated or laminated wood arches, wood roof and floor trusses, and/or other fabricated or laminated wood structural...
  • 321219 Reconstituted Wood Product Manufacturing: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing reconstituted wood sheets and boards.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 3212 from the left:
