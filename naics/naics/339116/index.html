NAICS 339116: Dental Laboratories

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339116 is the NAICS national industry Dental Laboratories in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3391 Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 465 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33911 Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing dentures, crowns, bridges, and orthodontic appliances customized for individual application.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing dentures, crowns, bridges, and orthodontic appliances customized for individual application.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339116 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 339116 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 339 is the subsector Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 3391 is the industry group Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing
  • 33911 is the five-digit industry Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing
  • 339116 is the national industry Dental Laboratories

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 6 terms to 339116, from "Bridges, custom made in dental laboratories" to "Teeth, custom made in dental laboratories". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Bridges, custom made in dental laboratories; Dental laboratories; Dentures, custom made in dental laboratories; Mouth guards and protectors, custom made in dental laboratories; Orthodontic appliance, custom made in dental laboratories; Teeth, custom made in dental laboratories.

Neighbouring national industries

Dental Laboratories sits in the NAICS industry 33911 Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing, which has 5 national industries in all. The others are: 339112 Surgical and Medical Instrument Manufacturing, 339113 Surgical Appliance and Supplies Manufacturing, 339114 Dental Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing, 339115 Ophthalmic Goods Manufacturing.
