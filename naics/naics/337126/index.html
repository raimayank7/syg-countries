NAICS 337126: Household Furniture (except Wood and Upholstered) Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

337126 is the NAICS national industry Household Furniture (except Wood and Upholstered) Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 337 Furniture and Related Product Manufacturing › 3371 Household and Institutional Furniture and Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 453 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33712 Household and Institutional Furniture Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing nonupholstered household-type furniture of materials other than wood, such as metal, plastics, reed, rattan, wicker, and fiberglass. The furniture may be partially upholstered (e.g., chairs with upholstered seats or backs), made on a stock or custom basis, and may be assembled or unassembled (i.e., knockdown).

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing nonupholstered household-type furniture of materials other than wood, such as metal, plastics, reed, rattan, wicker, and fiberglass.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 337126 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
