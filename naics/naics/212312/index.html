NAICS 212312: Crushed and Broken Limestone Mining and Quarrying

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

212312 is the NAICS national industry Crushed and Broken Limestone Mining and Quarrying in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 212 Mining (except Oil and Gas) › 2123 Nonmetallic Mineral Mining and Quarrying. In the code order it is number 74 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 21231 Stone Mining and Quarrying, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises (1) establishments primarily engaged in developing the mine site, mining or quarrying crushed and broken limestone (including related rocks, such as dolomite, cement rock, marl, travertine, and calcareous tufa) and (2) preparation plants primarily engaged in beneficiating limestone (e.g., grinding or pulverizing).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is developing the mine site, mining or quarrying crushed and broken limestone (including related rocks, such as dolomite, cement rock, marl, travertine, and calcareous tufa) and (2) preparation plants primarily engaged in beneficiating limestone (e.g., grinding or pulverizing).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 212312 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read
