NAICS 624: Social Assistance

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

624 is the NAICS subsector Social Assistance in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance.

What the Census says

Industries in the Social Assistance subsector provide a wide variety of social assistance services directly to their clients. These services do not include residential or accommodation services, except on a short-stay basis.

What it contains

Social Assistance has 4 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 6241 Individual and Family Services (3 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing nonresidential social assistance to children and youth, the elderly, persons with disabilities, and all other individuals...
  • 6242 Community Food and Housing, and Emergency and Other Relief Services (4 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following
  • 6243 Vocational Rehabilitation Services (1 national industry)
  • 6244 Child Care Services (1 national industry)

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 9 national industries under Social Assistance.

How the code is read

Neighbouring subsectors

Social Assistance sits in the sector 62 Health Care and Social Assistance, which has 4 subsectors in all. The others are: 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services, 622 Hospitals, 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities.

What is NAICS code 624?

How is 624 different from 621?

624 is Social Assistance, while 621 is Ambulatory Health Care Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
