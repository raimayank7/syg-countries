NAICS 4842: Specialized Freight Trucking

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4842 is the NAICS industry group Specialized Freight Trucking in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 484 Truck Transportation.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local or long-distance specialized freight trucking. The establishments of this industry are primarily engaged in the transportation of freight which, because of size, weight, shape, or other inherent characteristics, requires specialized equipment, such as flatbeds, tankers, or refrigerated trailers. This industry includes the transportation of used household, institutional, and commercial furniture and equipment.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is providing local or long-distance specialized freight trucking.

What it contains

Specialized Freight Trucking has 3 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 484210 Used Household and Office Goods Moving: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local or long-distance trucking of used household, used institutional, or used commercial furniture and equipment.
  • 484220 Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Local: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local, specialized trucking.
  • 484230 Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Long-Distance: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing long-distance specialized trucking.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 3 national industries under Specialized Freight Trucking.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 4842 from the left:

Is this the code to put on a form?
