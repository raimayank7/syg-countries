NAICS 325412: Pharmaceutical Preparation Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

325412 is the NAICS national industry Pharmaceutical Preparation Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 325 Chemical Manufacturing › 3254 Pharmaceutical and Medicine Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 248 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32541 Pharmaceutical and Medicine Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing in-vivo diagnostic substances and pharmaceutical preparations (except biological) intended for internal and external consumption in dose forms, such as ampoules, tablets, capsules, vials, ointments, powders, solutions, and suspensions.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing in-vivo diagnostic substances and pharmaceutical preparations (except biological) intended for internal and external consumption in dose forms, such as ampoules, tablets, capsules, vials, ointments, powders, solutions, and suspensions.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 325412 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
