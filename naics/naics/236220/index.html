NAICS 236220: Commercial and Institutional Building Construction

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

236220 is the NAICS national industry Commercial and Institutional Building Construction in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 23 Construction › 236 Construction of Buildings › 2362 Nonresidential Building Construction. In the code order it is number 105 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 23622 Commercial and Institutional Building Construction, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily responsible for the construction (including new work, additions, alterations, maintenance, and repairs) of commercial and institutional buildings and related structures, such as stadiums, grain elevators, and indoor swimming facilities. This industry includes establishments responsible for the on-site assembly of modular or prefabricated commercial and institutional buildings. Included in this industry are commercial and institutional building general contractors, commercial and institutional building for-sale builders, commercial and institutional building design-build firms, and commercial and institutional building project construction management firms.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 236220 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 236220 from the left:

The industry group around it
