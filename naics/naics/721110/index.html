NAICS 721110: Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

721110 is the NAICS national industry Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 72 Accommodation and Food Services › 721 Accommodation › 7211 Traveler Accommodation. In the code order it is number 925 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 72111 Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing short-term lodging in facilities known as hotels, motor hotels, resort hotels, and motels. The establishments in this industry may offer food and beverage services, recreational services, conference rooms, convention services, laundry services, parking, and other services.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is providing short-term lodging in facilities known as hotels, motor hotels, resort hotels, and motels.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 721110 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 721110 from the left:

  • 72 is the sector Accommodation and Food Services
  • 721 is the subsector Accommodation
  • 7211 is the industry group Traveler Accommodation
  • 72111 is the five-digit industry Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels
  • 721110 is the national industry Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 21 terms to 721110, from "Alpine skiing facilities with accommodations (i.e., ski resorts)" to "Tourist lodges". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Alpine skiing facilities with accommodations (i.e., ski resorts); Auto courts, lodging; Automobile courts, lodging; Health spas (i.e., physical fitness facilities) with accommodations; Hotel management services (i.e., providing management and operating staff to run hotel); Hotels (except casino hotels); Hotels (except casino hotels) with golf courses, tennis courts, and/or other health spa facilities (i.e., resorts); Hotels, membership; Hotels, resort, without casinos; Hotels, seasonal, without casinos; Membership hotels; Motels; Motor courts; Motor hotels without casinos; Motor inns; Motor lodges; Resort hotels without casinos; Seasonal hotels without casinos; Ski lodges and resorts with accommodations; Summer resort hotels without casinos; Tourist lodges.

2 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Hotels (except casino hotels); Hotels (except casino hotels) with golf courses, tennis courts, and/or other health spa facilities (i.e., resorts).

Neighbouring national industries

Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels sits in the industry group 7211 Traveler Accommodation, which has 4 national industries in all. The others are: 721120 Casino Hotels, 721191 Bed-and-Breakfast Inns, 721199 All Other Traveler Accommodation.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 721110 the main activity here is providing short-term lodging in facilities known as hotels, motor hotels, resort hotels, and motels. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:

  • 721120 Casino Hotels: main activity is providing short-term lodging in hotel facilities with a casino on the premises
  • 721191 Bed-and-Breakfast Inns: main activity is providing short-term lodging in facilities known as bed-and-breakfast inns
  • 721199 All Other Traveler Accommodation: main activity is providing short-term lodging (except hotels, motels, casino hotels, and bed-and-breakfast inns)

What does NAICS 721110 include?

Which sector is 721110 in?

How is 721110 different from 721120?

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
