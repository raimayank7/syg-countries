NAICS 325992: Photographic Film, Paper, Plate, Chemical, and Copy Toner Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

325992 is the NAICS national industry Photographic Film, Paper, Plate, Chemical, and Copy Toner Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 325 Chemical Manufacturing › 3259 Other Chemical Product and Preparation Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 260 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32599 All Other Chemical Product and Preparation Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing sensitized film, sensitized paper, sensitized cloth, sensitized plates, toners (i.e., for photocopiers, laser printers, and similar electrostatic printing devices), toner cartridges, and photographic chemicals.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing sensitized film, sensitized paper, sensitized cloth, sensitized plates, toners (i.e., for photocopiers, laser printers, and similar electrostatic printing devices), toner cartridges, and photographic chemicals.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 325992 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
