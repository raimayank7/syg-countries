NAICS 713290: Other Gambling Industries

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

713290 is the NAICS national industry Other Gambling Industries in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 71 Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation › 713 Amusement, Gambling, and Recreation Industries › 7132 Gambling Industries. In the code order it is number 918 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 71329 Other Gambling Industries, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating gambling facilities (except casinos or casino hotels) or providing gambling services.

Illustrative Examples:

Bingo, off-track betting, or slot machine parlors Coin- or card-operated gambling device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities) Bookmakers Lottery ticket sales agents (except retail stores) Card rooms (e.g., poker rooms)

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is operating gambling facilities (except casinos or casino hotels) or providing gambling services.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 713290 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 713290 from the left:

  • 71 is the sector Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation
  • 713 is the subsector Amusement, Gambling, and Recreation Industries
  • 7132 is the industry group Gambling Industries
  • 71329 is the five-digit industry Other Gambling Industries
  • 713290 is the national industry Other Gambling Industries

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 21 terms to 713290, from "Betting information services" to "Video gaming device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities)". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Betting information services; Bingo halls; Bingo parlors; Bookies; Bookmakers; Card rooms (e.g., poker rooms); Coin- or card-operated gambling device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities); Gambling control boards, operating gambling activities; Gambling device arcades or parlors, coin- or card-operated; Gambling device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities), coin- or card-operated; Lottery control boards (i.e., operating lotteries); Lottery corporations; Lottery ticket sales agents (except retail stores); Lottery ticket vendors (except retail stores); Off-track betting parlors; Online gambling sites; Online sports betting sites; Slot machine concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities); Slot machine parlors; Video gambling device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities); Video gaming device concession operators (i.e., supplying and servicing in others' facilities).

2 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Lottery ticket sales agents (except retail stores); Lottery ticket vendors (except retail stores).

The industry group around it

One level up is the industry group 7132 Gambling Industries. The Census describes it this way: This industry group comprises establishments (except casino hotels) primarily engaged in operating gambling facilities, such as casinos, bingo halls, and video gaming terminals, or in the provision of gambling services, such as lotteries and off-track betting. Casino hotels are classified in Industry 72112, Casino
