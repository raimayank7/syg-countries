NAICS 311520: Ice Cream and Frozen Dessert Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

311520 is the NAICS national industry Ice Cream and Frozen Dessert Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 311 Food Manufacturing › 3115 Dairy Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 154 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31152 Ice Cream and Frozen Dessert Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing ice cream, frozen yogurts, frozen ices, sherbets, frozen tofu, and other frozen desserts (except bakery products).

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing ice cream, frozen yogurts, frozen ices, sherbets, frozen tofu, and other frozen desserts (except bakery products).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 311520 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 311520 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 311 is the subsector Food Manufacturing
  • 3115 is the industry group Dairy Product Manufacturing
  • 31152 is the five-digit industry Ice Cream and Frozen Dessert Manufacturing
  • 311520 is the national industry Ice Cream and Frozen Dessert Manufacturing
