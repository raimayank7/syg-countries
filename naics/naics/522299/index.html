NAICS 522299: International, Secondary Market, and All Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

522299 is the NAICS national industry International, Secondary Market, and All Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 522 Credit Intermediation and Related Activities › 5222 Nondepository Credit Intermediation. In the code order it is number 697 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 52229 Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises (1) establishments primarily engaged in providing working capital funds to U.S. exporters, lending funds to foreign buyers of U.S. goods, and/or lending funds to domestic buyers of imported goods; (2) establishments primarily engaged in buying, pooling, and repackaging loans for sale to others on the secondary market; and (3) establishments primarily providing other nondepository credit (except credit card issuing, sales financing, consumer lending, and real estate credit). Examples of types of lending in this industry are short-term inventory credit, agricultural lending (except real estate and sales financing), and consumer cash lending secured by personal property.

Illustrative Examples:

Commodity Credit Corporation Factoring accounts receivable Industrial banks (i.e., known as), nondepository International trade financing Morris Plans (i.e., known as), nondepository Pawnshops Secondary market financing

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is providing working capital funds to U.S.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 522299 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
