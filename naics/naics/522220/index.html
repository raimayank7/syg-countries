NAICS 522220: Sales Financing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

522220 is the NAICS national industry Sales Financing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 522 Credit Intermediation and Related Activities › 5222 Nondepository Credit Intermediation. In the code order it is number 694 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 52222 Sales Financing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in sales financing or sales financing in combination with leasing. Sales financing establishments are primarily engaged in lending money for the purpose of providing collateralized goods through a contractual installment sales agreement, either directly from or through arrangements with dealers.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is sales financing or sales financing in combination with leasing.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 522220 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 522220 from the left:

  • 52 is the sector Finance and Insurance
  • 522 is the subsector Credit Intermediation and Related Activities
  • 5222 is the industry group Nondepository Credit Intermediation
  • 52222 is the five-digit industry Sales Financing
  • 522220 is the national industry Sales Financing

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 11 terms to 522220, from "Automobile finance leasing companies" to "Truck finance leasing". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Automobile finance leasing companies; Automobile financing; Commercial and inventory financing (except international trade financing); Equipment finance leasing; Financing, sales; Installment sales financing; Insurance premium financing; Leasing in combination with sales financing; Machinery finance leasing; Sales financing; Truck finance leasing.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Commercial and inventory financing (except international trade financing).

Neighbouring national industries

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 522220 the main activity here is sales financing or sales financing in combination with leasing. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:
