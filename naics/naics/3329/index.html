NAICS 3329: Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3329 is the NAICS industry group Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing fabricated metal products (except forgings and stampings, cutlery and handtools, architectural and structural metals, boilers, tanks, shipping containers, hardware, spring and wire products, machine shop products, turned products, screws, and nuts and bolts).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing fabricated metal products (except forgings and stampings, cutlery and handtools, architectural and structural metals, boilers, tanks, shipping containers, hardware, spring and wire products, machine shop products, turned products, screws, and nuts and bolts).

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing subsector transform metal into intermediate or end products, other than machinery, computers and electronics, and metal furniture, or treat metals and metal formed products fabricated elsewhere. Common questions

What is NAICS code 3329?

What does NAICS 3329 include?

Which sector is 3329 in?

How is 3329 different from 3321?

3329 is Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing, while 3321 is Forging and Stamping. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
