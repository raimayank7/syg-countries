NAICS 424340: Footwear Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

424340 is the NAICS national industry Footwear Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods › 4243 Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 519 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42434 Footwear Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of footwear of leather, rubber, and other materials, including athletic footwear (except specialty athletic footwear).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is the merchant wholesale distribution of footwear of leather, rubber, and other materials, including athletic footwear (except specialty athletic footwear).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 424340 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 424340 from the left:

  • 42 is the sector Wholesale Trade
  • 424 is the subsector Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods
  • 4243 is the industry group Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers
  • 42434 is the five-digit industry Footwear Merchant Wholesalers
  • 424340 is the national industry Footwear Merchant Wholesalers

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 7 terms to 424340, from "Athletic footwear (except specialty athletic footwear) merchant wholesalers" to "Slippers merchant wholesalers". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Athletic footwear (except specialty athletic footwear) merchant wholesalers; Boots (e.g., hiking, western, work) merchant wholesalers; Footwear merchant wholesalers; Leather cut stock for shoe and boot merchant wholesalers; Shoe accessories merchant wholesalers; Shoes merchant wholesalers; Slippers merchant wholesalers.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Athletic footwear (except specialty athletic footwear) merchant wholesalers.
