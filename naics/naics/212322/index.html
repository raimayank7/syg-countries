NAICS 212322: Industrial Sand Mining

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

212322 is the NAICS national industry Industrial Sand Mining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 212 Mining (except Oil and Gas) › 2123 Nonmetallic Mineral Mining and Quarrying. In the code order it is number 78 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 21232 Sand, Gravel, Clay, and Ceramic and Refractory Minerals Mining and Quarrying, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) operating industrial grade sand pits; (2) dredging for industrial grade sand; and (3) washing, screening, or otherwise preparing industrial grade sand.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is one or more of the following: (1) operating industrial grade sand pits.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 212322 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 212322 from the left:

  • 21 is the sector Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction
  • 212 is the subsector Mining (except Oil and Gas)
  • 2123 is the industry group Nonmetallic Mineral Mining and Quarrying
  • 21232 is the five-digit industry Sand, Gravel, Clay, and Ceramic and Refractory Minerals Mining and Quarrying
  • 212322 is the national industry Industrial Sand Mining

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 14 terms to 212322, from "Abrasive sand quarrying and/or beneficiating" to "Silica sand quarrying and/or beneficiating". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Abrasive sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Blast sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Enamel sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Filtration sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Foundry sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Glass sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Grinding sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Industrial sand beneficiating (e.g., screening, washing); Industrial sand sandpits and dredging; Molding sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Sand, blast, quarrying and/or beneficiating; Sand, industrial (e.g., engine, filtration, glass grinding, proppant), quarrying and/or beneficiating; Silica mining and/or beneficiating; Silica sand quarrying and/or beneficiating.

Neighbouring national industries

Industrial Sand Mining sits in the NAICS industry 21232 Sand, Gravel, Clay, and Ceramic and Refractory Minerals Mining and Quarrying, which has 3 national industries in all. The others are: 212321 Construction Sand and Gravel Mining, 212323 Kaolin, Clay, and Ceramic and Refractory Minerals Mining.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 212322 the main activity here is one or more of the following: (1) operating industrial grade sand pits. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 212321 Construction Sand and Gravel Mining: main activity is one or more of the following: (1) operating commercial grade (i.e., construction) sand and gravel pits
  • 212323 Kaolin, Clay, and Ceramic and Refractory Minerals Mining: main activity is developing the mine site and/or mining clay (e.g., china clay, paper clay and slip clay) or ceramic and refractory minerals and (2) establishments primarily engaged in beneficiating (i.e., preparing) clay or ceramic and refractory minerals

Common questions

What is NAICS code 212322?

NAICS 212322 is the national industry "Industrial Sand Mining" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 2123 Nonmetallic Mineral Mining and Quarrying.

What does NAICS 212322 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is one or more of the following: (1) operating industrial grade sand pits.

Which sector is 212322 in?

212322 belongs to NAICS sector 21, Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction.

What kinds of business are indexed under 212322?

The Census index file lists 14 terms for it, for example Abrasive sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Blast sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Enamel sand quarrying and/or beneficiating; Filtration sand quarrying and/or beneficiating.

How is 212322 different from 212321?

212322 is Industrial Sand Mining, while 212321 is Construction Sand and Gravel Mining. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
