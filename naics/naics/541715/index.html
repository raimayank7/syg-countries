NAICS 541715: Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences (except Nanotechnology and Biotechnology)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

541715 is the NAICS national industry Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences (except Nanotechnology and Biotechnology) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 54 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 541 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 5417 Scientific Research and Development Services. In the code order it is number 781 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 54171 Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in conducting research and experimental development (except nanotechnology and biotechnology research and experimental development) in the physical, engineering, and life sciences, such as agriculture, electronics, environmental, biology, botany, computers, chemistry, food, fisheries, forests, geology, health, mathematics, medicine, oceanography, pharmacy, physics, veterinary, and other allied subjects.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 541715 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
