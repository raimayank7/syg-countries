NAICS 541940: Veterinary Services

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

541940 is the NAICS national industry Veterinary Services in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 54 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 541 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 5419 Other Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services. In the code order it is number 795 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 54194 Veterinary Services, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments of licensed veterinary practitioners primarily engaged in the practice of veterinary medicine, dentistry, or surgery for animals; and establishments primarily engaged in providing testing services for licensed veterinary practitioners.

Illustrative Examples:

Animal hospitals Veterinary clinics Veterinarians' offices Veterinary testing laboratories

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is the practice of veterinary medicine, dentistry, or surgery for animals.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 541940 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 541940 from the left:

  • 54 is the sector Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services
  • 541 is the subsector Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services
  • 5419 is the industry group Other Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services
  • 54194 is the five-digit industry Veterinary Services
  • 541940 is the national industry Veterinary Services
