NAICS 325212: Synthetic Rubber Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

325212 is the NAICS national industry Synthetic Rubber Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 325 Chemical Manufacturing › 3252 Resin, Synthetic Rubber, and Artificial and Synthetic Fibers and Filaments Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 240 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 32521 Resin and Synthetic Rubber Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing synthetic rubber.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing synthetic rubber.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 325212 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How is 325212 different from 325211?

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
