NAICS 2131: Support Activities for Mining

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

2131 is the NAICS industry group Support Activities for Mining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 213 Support Activities for Mining.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 5 national industries under Support Activities for Mining.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 2131 from the left:

  • 21 is the sector Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction
  • 213 is the subsector Support Activities for Mining
  • 2131 is the industry group Support Activities for Mining
