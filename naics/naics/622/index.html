NAICS 622: Hospitals

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

622 is the NAICS subsector Hospitals in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance.

What the Census says

Industries in the Hospitals subsector provide medical, diagnostic, and treatment services that include physician, nursing, and other health services to inpatients and the specialized accommodation services required by inpatients. Hospitals may also provide outpatient services as a secondary activity. Establishments in the Hospitals subsector provide inpatient health services, many of which can only be provided using the specialized facilities and equipment that form a significant and integral part of the production process.

What it contains

Hospitals has 3 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 6221 General Medical and Surgical Hospitals (1 national industry)
  • 6222 Psychiatric and Substance Abuse Hospitals (1 national industry)
  • 6223 Specialty (except Psychiatric and Substance Abuse) Hospitals (1 national industry)

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 3 national industries under Hospitals.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 622 from the left:

  • 62 is the sector Health Care and Social Assistance
  • 622 is the subsector Hospitals

Neighbouring subsectors

Hospitals sits in the sector 62 Health Care and Social Assistance, which has 4 subsectors in all. The others are: 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services, 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities, 624 Social Assistance.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 622 the Industries in the Hospitals subsector provide medical, diagnostic, and treatment services that include physician, nursing, and other health services to inpatients and the specialized accommodation services required by inpatients.. The nearest codes in the same sector say:

  • 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services: Industries in the Ambulatory Health Care Services subsector provide health care services directly or indirectly to ambulatory patients and do not usually provide inpatient services.
  • 623 Nursing and Residential Care Facilities: Industries in the Nursing and Residential Care Facilities subsector provide residential care combined with either nursing, supervisory, or other types of care as required by the residents.
  • 624 Social Assistance: Industries in the Social Assistance subsector provide a wide variety of social assistance services directly to their clients.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 622?

What does NAICS 622 include?

Which sector is 622 in?

How many national industries does 622 cover?

How is 622 different from 621?

622 is Hospitals, while 621 is Ambulatory Health Care Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
