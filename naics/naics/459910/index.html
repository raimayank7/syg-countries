NAICS 459910: Pet and Pet Supplies Retailers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

459910 is the NAICS national industry Pet and Pet Supplies Retailers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 44-45 Retail Trade › 459 Sporting Goods, Hobby, Musical Instrument, Book, and Miscellaneous Retailers › 4599 Other Miscellaneous Retailers. In the code order it is number 598 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 45991 Pet and Pet Supplies Retailers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing pets, pet foods, and pet supplies.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is retailing pets, pet foods, and pet supplies.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 459910 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 459910 from the left:

  • 44-45 is the sector Retail Trade
  • 459 is the subsector Sporting Goods, Hobby, Musical Instrument, Book, and Miscellaneous Retailers
  • 4599 is the industry group Other Miscellaneous Retailers
  • 45991 is the five-digit industry Pet and Pet Supplies Retailers
  • 459910 is the national industry Pet and Pet Supplies Retailers

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 3 terms to 459910, from "Feed stores, pet" to "Pet supply stores". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Feed stores, pet; Pet shops; Pet supply stores.
