NAICS 315250: Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (except Contractors)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

315250 is the NAICS national industry Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (except Contractors) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 315 Apparel Manufacturing › 3152 Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 195 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31525 Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (except Contractors), holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing cut and sew apparel from purchased fabric. Clothing jobbers, who perform entrepreneurial functions involved in apparel manufacture, including buying raw materials, designing and preparing samples, arranging for apparel to be made from their materials, and marketing finished apparel, are included.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing cut and sew apparel from purchased fabric.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 315250 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
