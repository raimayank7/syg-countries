NAICS 333111: Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

333111 is the NAICS national industry Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 333 Machinery Manufacturing › 3331 Agriculture, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 350 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33311 Agricultural Implement Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing agricultural and farm machinery and equipment, and other turf and grounds care equipment, including planting, harvesting, and grass mowing equipment (except lawn and garden-type).

Illustrative Examples:

Combines (i.e., harvester-threshers) manufacturing Cotton ginning machinery manufacturing Feed processing equipment, farm-type, manufacturing Fertilizing machinery, farm-type, manufacturing Grass mowing equipment (except lawn and garden) manufacturing Haying machines manufacturing Milking machines manufacturing Planting machines, farm-type, manufacturing Plows, farm-type, manufacturing Poultry brooders, feeders, and waterers manufacturing Tractors and attachments, farm-type, manufacturing

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing agricultural and farm machinery and equipment, and other turf and grounds care equipment, including planting, harvesting, and grass mowing equipment (except lawn and garden-type).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 333111 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 333111 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 333 is the subsector Machinery Manufacturing
  • 3331 is the industry group Agriculture, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing
  • 33311 is the five-digit industry Agricultural Implement Manufacturing
  • 333111 is the national industry Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing

Common questions

What is NAICS code 333111?

NAICS 333111 is the national industry "Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 3331 Agriculture, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing.

What does NAICS 333111 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is manufacturing agricultural and farm machinery and equipment, and other turf and grounds care equipment, including planting, harvesting, and grass mowing equipment (except lawn and garden-type).

Which sector is 333111 in?

333111 belongs to NAICS sector 31-33, Manufacturing.

What kinds of business are indexed under 333111?

The Census index file lists 66 terms for it, for example Bale throwers manufacturing; Balers, farm-type (e.g., cotton, hay, straw), manufacturing; Berry harvesting machines manufacturing; Blowers, forage, manufacturing.

How is 333111 different from 333112?

333111 is Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing, while 333112 is Lawn and Garden Tractor and Home Lawn and Garden Equipment Manufacturing. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
