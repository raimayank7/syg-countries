NAICS 2382: Building Equipment Contractors

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

2382 is the NAICS industry group Building Equipment Contractors in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 23 Construction › 238 Specialty Trade Contractors.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in installing or servicing equipment that forms part of a building mechanical system (e.g., electricity, water, heating, and cooling). The work performed may include new work, additions, alterations, maintenance, and repairs. Contractors installing specialized building equipment, such as elevators, escalators, service station equipment, and central vacuum cleaning systems, are also included.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is installing or servicing equipment that forms part of a building mechanical system (e.g., electricity, water, heating, and cooling).

What it contains

Building Equipment Contractors has 3 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 238210 Electrical Contractors and Other Wiring Installation Contractors: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in installing and servicing electrical wiring and equipment.
  • 238220 Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in installing and servicing plumbing, heating, and air-conditioning equipment.
  • 238290 Other Building Equipment Contractors: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in installing or servicing building equipment (except electrical, plumbing, heating, cooling, or ventilation equipment).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 3 national industries under Building Equipment Contractors.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 2382?

What does NAICS 2382 include?

Which sector is 2382 in?

How many national industries does 2382 cover?

How is 2382 different from 2381?

2382 is Building Equipment Contractors, while 2381 is Foundation, Structure, and Building Exterior Contractors. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
