NAICS 424130: Industrial and Personal Service Paper Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

424130 is the NAICS national industry Industrial and Personal Service Paper Merchant Wholesalers. It is part of the industry group 4241 Paper and Paper Product Merchant Wholesalers.

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of kraft wrapping and other coarse paper, paperboard, converted paper (except stationery and office supplies), and/or related disposable plastics products.
