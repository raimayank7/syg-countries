NAICS 512199: Other Motion Picture and Video Industries

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

512199 is the NAICS national industry Other Motion Picture and Video Industries in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 51 Information › 512 Motion Picture and Sound Recording Industries › 5121 Motion Picture and Video Industries. In the code order it is number 665 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 51219 Postproduction Services and Other Motion Picture and Video Industries, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing motion picture and video services (except motion picture and video production, distribution, exhibition, and teleproduction and other postproduction services).

Illustrative Examples:

Motion picture film laboratories Stock footage film libraries Film preservation services

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is providing motion picture and video services (except motion picture and video production, distribution, exhibition, and teleproduction and other postproduction services).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 512199 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 512199 from the left:

  • 51 is the sector Information
  • 512 is the subsector Motion Picture and Sound Recording Industries
  • 5121 is the industry group Motion Picture and Video Industries
  • 51219 is the five-digit industry Postproduction Services and Other Motion Picture and Video Industries
  • 512199 is the national industry Other Motion Picture and Video Industries

The NAICS industry around it

One level up is the NAICS industry 51219 Postproduction Services and Other Motion Picture and Video Industries. The Census describes it this way: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing postproduction services and other services to the motion picture industry, including specialized motion picture or video postproduction services, such as editing, film/tape transfers, titling, subtitling, credits, closed captioning, and computer-produced graphics, animation and special effects, as well as developing and processing motion picture film. Common questions

What is NAICS code 512199?

NAICS 512199 is the national industry "Other Motion Picture and Video Industries" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 5121 Motion Picture and Video Industries.

What does NAICS 512199 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is providing motion picture and video services (except motion picture and video production, distribution, exhibition, and teleproduction and other postproduction services).

Which sector is 512199 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 512199?

How is 512199 different from 512191?

512199 is Other Motion Picture and Video Industries, while 512191 is Teleproduction and Other Postproduction Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
