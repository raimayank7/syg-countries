NAICS 339940: Office Supplies (except Paper) Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339940 is the NAICS national industry Office Supplies (except Paper) Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3399 Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 469 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33994 Office Supplies (except Paper) Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing office supplies. Examples of products made by these establishments are pens, pencils, felt tip markers, crayons, chalk, pencil sharpeners, staplers, modeling clay, hand operated stamps, stamp pads, stencils, carbon paper, and inked ribbons.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing office supplies.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339940 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 339940 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 339 is the subsector Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 3399 is the industry group Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing
  • 33994 is the five-digit industry Office Supplies (except Paper) Manufacturing
  • 339940 is the national industry Office Supplies (except Paper) Manufacturing
