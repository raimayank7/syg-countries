NAICS 482111: Line-Haul Railroads

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

482111 is the NAICS national industry Line-Haul Railroads in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 482 Rail Transportation › 4821 Rail Transportation. In the code order it is number 608 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 48211 Rail Transportation, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as line-haul railroads primarily engaged in operating railroads for the transport of passengers and/or cargo over a long distance within a rail network. These establishments provide for the intercity movement of trains between the terminals and stations on main and branch lines of a line-haul rail network (except for local switching services).

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is operating railroads for the transport of passengers and/or cargo over a long distance within a rail network.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 482111 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 482111 from the left:

  • 48-49 is the sector Transportation and Warehousing
  • 482 is the subsector Rail Transportation
  • 4821 is the industry group Rail Transportation
  • 48211 is the five-digit industry Rail Transportation
  • 482111 is the national industry Line-Haul Railroads

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 5 terms to 482111, from "Freight railways, line-haul" to "Railway transportation, line-haul". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Freight railways, line-haul; Passenger railways, line-haul; Railroad transportation, line-haul; Railroads, line-haul; Railway transportation, line-haul.

Neighbouring national industries

Line-Haul Railroads sits in the NAICS industry 48211 Rail Transportation, which has 2 national industries in all. The others are: 482112 Short Line Railroads.
