NAICS 6213: Offices of Other Health Practitioners

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

6213 is the NAICS industry group Offices of Other Health Practitioners in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments of independent health practitioners (except physicians and dentists).

How is 6213 different from 6211?

6213 is Offices of Other Health Practitioners, while 6211 is Offices of Physicians. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
