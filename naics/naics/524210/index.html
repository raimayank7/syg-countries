NAICS 524210: Insurance Agencies and Brokerages

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

524210 is the NAICS national industry Insurance Agencies and Brokerages in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 52 Finance and Insurance › 524 Insurance Carriers and Related Activities › 5242 Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities. In the code order it is number 714 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 52421 Insurance Agencies and Brokerages, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 524210 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 524210 from the left:

  • 52 is the sector Finance and Insurance
  • 524 is the subsector Insurance Carriers and Related Activities
  • 5242 is the industry group Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities
  • 52421 is the five-digit industry Insurance Agencies and Brokerages
  • 524210 is the national industry Insurance Agencies and Brokerages

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 6 terms to 524210, from "Agencies, insurance" to "Life insurance agencies". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Agencies, insurance; Brokerages, insurance; Brokers' offices, insurance; Insurance agencies; Insurance brokerages; Life insurance agencies.

Neighbouring national industries

Insurance Agencies and Brokerages sits in the industry group 5242 Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities, which has 4 national industries in all. The others are: 524291 Claims Adjusting, 524292 Pharmacy Benefit Management and Other Third Party Administration of Insurance and Pension Funds, 524298 All Other Insurance Related Activities.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 524210 the main activity here is acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:

  • 524291 Claims Adjusting: main activity is investigating, appraising, and settling insurance claims
  • 524292 Pharmacy Benefit Management and Other Third Party Administration of Insurance and Pension Funds: main activity is providing pharmacy benefit management (PBM) services and other third party administration services of insurance and pension funds, such as claims processing and other administrative services to insurance carriers, employee benefit plans,...
  • 524298 All Other Insurance Related Activities: main activity is providing insurance services on a contract or fee basis (except insurance agencies and brokerages, claims adjusting, and third party administration)

Common questions

What is NAICS code 524210?

NAICS 524210 is the national industry "Insurance Agencies and Brokerages" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 5242 Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities.

What does NAICS 524210 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies.

Which sector is 524210 in?

524210 belongs to NAICS sector 52, Finance and Insurance.

What kinds of business are indexed under 524210?

The Census index file lists 6 terms for it, for example Agencies, insurance; Brokerages, insurance; Brokers' offices, insurance; Insurance agencies.

How is 524210 different from 524291?

524210 is Insurance Agencies and Brokerages, while 524291 is Claims Adjusting. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
