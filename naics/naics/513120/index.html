NAICS 513120: Periodical Publishers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

513120 is the NAICS national industry Periodical Publishers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 51 Information › 513 Publishing Industries › 5131 Newspaper, Periodical, Book, and Directory Publishers. In the code order it is number 671 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 51312 Periodical Publishers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments known either as magazine publishers or periodical publishers. These establishments carry out the operations necessary for producing and distributing magazines and other periodicals, such as gathering, writing, and editing articles, and selling and preparing advertisements. These establishments may publish magazines and other periodicals in print or electronic form, including exclusively on the Internet.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 513120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

What is NAICS code 513120?

What does NAICS 513120 include?

Which sector is 513120 in?

What kinds of business are indexed under 513120?

How is 513120 different from 513110?

513120 is Periodical Publishers, while 513110 is Newspaper Publishers. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
