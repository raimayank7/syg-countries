NAICS 541720: Research and Development in the Social Sciences and Humanities

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

541720 is the NAICS national industry Research and Development in the Social Sciences and Humanities in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 54 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 541 Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services › 5417 Scientific Research and Development Services. In the code order it is number 782 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 54172 Research and Development in the Social Sciences and Humanities, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in conducting research and analyses in cognitive development, sociology, psychology, language, behavior, economic, and other social science and humanities research.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is conducting research and analyses in cognitive development, sociology, psychology, language, behavior, economic, and other social science and humanities research.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 541720 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
