NAICS 513199: All Other Publishers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

513199 is the NAICS national industry All Other Publishers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 51 Information › 513 Publishing Industries › 5131 Newspaper, Periodical, Book, and Directory Publishers. In the code order it is number 675 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 51319 Other Publishers, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments generally known as publishers (except newspaper, magazine, book, directory, mailing list, music, software, and greeting card publishers). These establishments may publish works in print or electronic form, including exclusively on the Internet.

Illustrative Examples:

Art publishers Calendar publishers

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 513199 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 513199 from the left:

  • 51 is the sector Information
  • 513 is the subsector Publishing Industries
  • 5131 is the industry group Newspaper, Periodical, Book, and Directory Publishers
  • 51319 is the five-digit industry Other Publishers
  • 513199 is the national industry All Other Publishers

How is 513199 different from 513191?

513199 is All Other Publishers, while 513191 is Greeting Card Publishers. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
