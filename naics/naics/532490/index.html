NAICS 532490: Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

532490 is the NAICS national industry Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 53 Real Estate and Rental and Leasing › 532 Rental and Leasing Services › 5324 Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing. In the code order it is number 746 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 53249 Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting or leasing nonconsumer-type machinery and equipment (except heavy construction, transportation, mining, and forestry machinery and equipment without operators; and office machinery and equipment). Establishments in this industry rent or lease products, such as manufacturing equipment; metalworking, telecommunications, motion picture, theatrical machinery and equipment, or service industry machinery; institutional (i.e., public building) furniture, such as furniture for schools, theaters, or buildings; or agricultural equipment without operators.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is renting or leasing nonconsumer-type machinery and equipment (except heavy construction, transportation, mining, and forestry machinery and equipment without operators.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 532490 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 532490 from the left:

  • 53 is the sector Real Estate and Rental and Leasing

  • 5324 is the industry group Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing
  • 53249 is the five-digit industry Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing

The industry group around it

One level up is the industry group 5324 Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing. The Census describes it this way: This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting or leasing commercial-type and industrial-type machinery and equipment. Establishments included in this industry group are generally involved in providing capital or investment-type equipment that clients use in their business operations. These establishments typically cater to a business clientele and do not generally operate a retail-like or storefront
