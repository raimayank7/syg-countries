NAICS 9221: Justice, Public Order, and Safety Activities

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

9221 is the NAICS industry group Justice, Public Order, and Safety Activities in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 92 Public Administration › 922 Justice, Public Order, and Safety Activities.

What it contains

Justice, Public Order, and Safety Activities has 7 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 9221 from the left:
