NAICS 4883: Support Activities for Water Transportation

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4883 is the NAICS industry group Support Activities for Water Transportation in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 488 Support Activities for Transportation.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following: (1) operating ports, harbors (including docking and pier facilities), or canals; (2) providing stevedoring and other marine cargo handling services (except warehousing); (3) providing navigational services to shipping; or (4) providing other services to water transportation.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is one of the following: (1) operating ports, harbors (including docking and pier facilities), or canals.

Support Activities for Water Transportation has 4 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 488330 Navigational Services to Shipping: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing navigational services to shipping.
  • 488390 Other Support Activities for Water Transportation: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing services to water transportation (except port and harbor operations

Common questions

What is NAICS code 4883?

What does NAICS 4883 include?

Which sector is 4883 in?

How many national industries does 4883 cover?

How is 4883 different from 4881?

4883 is Support Activities for Water Transportation, while 4881 is Support Activities for Air Transportation. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
