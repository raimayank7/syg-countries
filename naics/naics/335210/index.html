NAICS 335210: Small Electrical Appliance Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

335210 is the NAICS national industry Small Electrical Appliance Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 335 Electrical Equipment, Appliance, and Component Manufacturing › 3352 Household Appliance Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 411 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 33521 Small Electrical Appliance Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing small electric appliances and electric housewares, household-type fans (except attic fans), household-type vacuum cleaners, and other electric household-type floor care machines.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing small electric appliances and electric housewares, household-type fans (except attic fans), household-type vacuum cleaners, and other electric household-type floor care machines.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 335210 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
