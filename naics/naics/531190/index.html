NAICS 531190: Lessors of Other Real Estate Property

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

531190 is the NAICS national industry Lessors of Other Real Estate Property in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 53 Real Estate and Rental and Leasing › 531 Real Estate › 5311 Lessors of Real Estate. In the code order it is number 727 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 53119 Lessors of Other Real Estate Property, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of real estate (except buildings), such as manufactured home (i.e., mobile home) sites, vacant lots, and grazing land.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is acting as lessors of real estate (except buildings), such as manufactured home (i.e., mobile home) sites, vacant lots, and grazing land.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 531190 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 531190 from the left:

  • 53 is the sector Real Estate and Rental and Leasing
  • 531 is the subsector Real Estate
  • 5311 is the industry group Lessors of Real Estate
  • 53119 is the five-digit industry Lessors of Other Real Estate Property
  • 531190 is the national industry Lessors of Other Real Estate Property

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 19 terms to 531190, from "Agricultural property rental or leasing" to "Vacation and recreation land rental or leasing". That is above the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Agricultural property rental or leasing; Airport leasing, not operating airport, rental or leasing; Equity real estate investment trusts (REITs), primarily leasing real estate (except residential buildings and dwellings, nonresidential buildings, miniwarehouses, and self-storage units); Farmland rental or leasing; Flea market space (except under roof) rental or leasing; Forest land rental or leasing; Grazing land rental or leasing; Land rental or leasing; Manufactured (mobile) home parks; Manufactured (mobile) home sites rental or leasing; Mining property leasing; Mobile (manufactured) home parks; Mobile (manufactured) home sites rental or leasing; Railroad right of way leasing; Real estate (except building) rental or leasing; Residential trailer parks; Trailer park or court, residential; Vacant lot rental or leasing; Vacation and recreation land rental or leasing.

3 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Equity real estate investment trusts (REITs), primarily leasing real estate (except residential buildings and dwellings, nonresidential buildings, miniwarehouses, and self-storage units); Flea market space (except under roof) rental or leasing; Real estate (except building) rental or leasing.

Neighbouring national industries

Lessors of Other Real Estate Property sits in the industry group 5311 Lessors of Real Estate, which has 4 national industries in all. The others are: 531110 Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings, 531120 Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings (except Miniwarehouses), 531130 Lessors of Miniwarehouses and Self-Storage Units.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 531190 the main activity here is acting as lessors of real estate (except buildings), such as manufactured home (i.e., mobile home) sites, vacant lots, and grazing land. The nearest codes in the same industry group say:

  • 531130 Lessors of Miniwarehouses and Self-Storage Units: main activity is renting or leasing space for self-storage
  • 531120 Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings (except Miniwarehouses): main activity is acting as lessors of buildings (except miniwarehouses and self-storage units) that are not used as residences or dwellings
  • 531110 Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings: main activity is acting as lessors of buildings used as residences or dwellings, such as single-family homes, apartment buildings, and town homes
