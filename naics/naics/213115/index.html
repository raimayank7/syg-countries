NAICS 213115: Support Activities for Nonmetallic Minerals (except Fuels) Mining

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

213115 is the NAICS national industry Support Activities for Nonmetallic Minerals (except Fuels) Mining in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 21 Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction › 213 Support Activities for Mining › 2131 Support Activities for Mining. In the code order it is number 85 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 21311 Support Activities for Mining, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing support activities, on a contract or fee basis, for the mining and quarrying of nonmetallic minerals (except fuel) and for the extraction of nonmetallic minerals (except geophysical surveying and mapping, site preparation, construction, and transportation activities). Exploration for these minerals is included in this industry. Exploration services include traditional prospecting methods, such as taking core samples and making geological observations at prospective sites.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is providing support activities, on a contract or fee basis, for the mining and quarrying of nonmetallic minerals (except fuel) and for the extraction of nonmetallic minerals (except geophysical surveying and mapping, site preparation, construction, and transportation activities).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 213115 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

What is NAICS code 213115?

NAICS 213115 is the national industry "Support Activities for Nonmetallic Minerals (except Fuels) Mining" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 2131 Support Activities for Mining.

What does NAICS 213115 include?

How is 213115 different from 213111?

213115 is Support Activities for Nonmetallic Minerals (except Fuels) Mining, while 213111 is Drilling Oil and Gas Wells. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
