NAICS 531: Real Estate

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

531 is the NAICS subsector Real Estate in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 53 Real Estate and Rental and Leasing.

What the Census says

Industries in the Real Estate subsector group establishments primarily engaged in renting or leasing real estate to others; managing real estate for others; selling, buying, or renting real estate for others; and providing other real estate related services, such as appraisal services.

This subsector includes equity real estate investment trusts (REITs) primarily engaged in leasing buildings, dwellings, or other real estate property to others. Mortgage REITs are classified in Subsector 525, Funds, Trusts, and Other Financial Vehicles.

Establishments primarily engaged in subdividing and developing unimproved real estate and constructing buildings for sale are classified in Subsector 236, Construction of Buildings. Establishments primarily engaged in subdividing and improving raw land for subsequent sale to builders are classified in Subsector 237, Heavy and Civil Engineering Construction.

What it contains

Real Estate has 3 industry groups under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 5311 Lessors of Real Estate (4 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of (1) residential buildings and dwellings
  • 5312 Offices of Real Estate Agents and Brokers (1 national industry)
  • 5313 Activities Related to Real Estate (4 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing real estate services (except lessors of real estate and offices of real estate agents and brokers).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 9 national industries under Real Estate.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 531 from the left:

  • 53 is the sector Real Estate and Rental and Leasing
  • 531 is the subsector Real Estate

What the index file lists

The Census index file has 155 entries that point to industries under Real Estate. A spread of them, in alphabetical order: Agencies, real estate; Appraisal services, real estate; Building, residential, rental or leasing; Condominium managers' offices, residential; Equity real estate investment trusts (REITs), primarily leasing miniwarehouses and self-storage units; Flea market space (except under roof) rental or leasing; Insurance building rental or leasing; Managers' offices, nonresidential real estate; Medical building rental or leasing; Office building rental or leasing; Real estate agencies; Real estate listing services; Residential real estate agencies; Selling time-share condominiums for others (i.e., agents, brokers). Neighbouring subsectors

Real Estate sits in the sector 53 Real Estate and Rental and Leasing, which has 3 subsectors in all. The others are: 532 Rental and Leasing Services, 533 Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (except Copyrighted Works).

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 531 the main activity here is renting or leasing real estate to others. The nearest codes in the same sector say:

  • 532 Rental and Leasing Services: main activity is leasing in combination with providing loans are classified in Sector 52, Finance and Insurance
  • 533 Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (except Copyrighted Works): main activity is assigning rights to assets, such as patents, trademarks, brand names, and/or franchise agreements, for which a royalty payment or licensing fee is paid to the asset holder

Common questions

What is NAICS code 531?

NAICS 531 is the subsector "Real Estate" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 53 Real Estate and Rental and Leasing.

What does NAICS 531 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is renting or leasing real estate to others.

Which sector is 531 in?

531 belongs to NAICS sector 53, Real Estate and Rental and Leasing.

How many national industries does 531 cover?

531 covers 9 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 531 different from 532?

531 is Real Estate, while 532 is Rental and Leasing Services. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
