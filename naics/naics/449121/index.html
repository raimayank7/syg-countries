NAICS 449121: Floor Covering Retailers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

449121 is the NAICS national industry Floor Covering Retailers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 44-45 Retail Trade › 449 Furniture, Home Furnishings, Electronics, and Appliance Retailers › 4491 Furniture and Home Furnishings Retailers. In the code order it is number 570 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 44912 Home Furnishings Retailers, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new floor coverings, such as rugs and carpets, laminate and vinyl floor coverings, linoleum flooring, and floor tile (except ceramic tile or hardwood floor coverings only); or retailing new floor coverings in combination with installation and repair services.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is retailing new floor coverings, such as rugs and carpets, laminate and vinyl floor coverings, linoleum flooring, and floor tile (except ceramic tile or hardwood floor coverings only).

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 449121 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 449121 from the left:

  • 44-45 is the sector Retail Trade
  • 449 is the subsector Furniture, Home Furnishings, Electronics, and Appliance Retailers
  • 4491 is the industry group Furniture and Home Furnishings Retailers
  • 44912 is the five-digit industry Home Furnishings Retailers
  • 449121 is the national industry Floor Covering Retailers

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 3 terms to 449121, from "Carpet stores" to "Rug stores". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Carpet stores; Floor covering stores (except hardwood or ceramic tile only); Rug stores.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Floor covering stores (except hardwood or ceramic tile only).

Neighbouring national industries

Floor Covering Retailers sits in the NAICS industry 44912 Home Furnishings Retailers, which has 3 national industries in all. The others are: 449122 Window Treatment Retailers, 449129 All Other Home Furnishings Retailers.
