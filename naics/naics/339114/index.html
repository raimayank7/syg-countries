NAICS 339114: Dental Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

339114 is the NAICS national industry Dental Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 339 Miscellaneous Manufacturing › 3391 Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 463 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 5 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33911 Medical Equipment and Supplies Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing dental equipment and supplies used by dental laboratories and offices of dentists, such as dental chairs, dental instrument delivery systems, dental hand instruments, dental impression material, and dental cements.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing dental equipment and supplies used by dental laboratories and offices of dentists, such as dental chairs, dental instrument delivery systems, dental hand instruments, dental impression material, and dental cements.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 339114 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
