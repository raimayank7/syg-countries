NAICS 332618: Other Fabricated Wire Product Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

332618 is the NAICS national industry Other Fabricated Wire Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing › 3326 Spring and Wire Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 333 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33261 Spring and Wire Product Manufacturing, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing fabricated wire products (except springs) made from purchased wire.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing fabricated wire products (except springs) made from purchased wire.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 332618 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
