NAICS 111219: Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

111219 is the NAICS national industry Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 111 Crop Production › 1112 Vegetable and Melon Farming. In the code order it is number 10 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 11121 Vegetable and Melon Farming, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) growing melons and/or vegetables (except potatoes; dry peas; dry beans; field, silage, or seed corn; and sugar beets); (2) producing vegetable and/or melon seeds; and (3) growing vegetable and/or melon bedding plants.

Illustrative Examples:

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is one or more of the following: (1) growing melons and/or vegetables (except potatoes.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 111219 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 111219 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 111 is the subsector Crop Production
  • 1112 is the industry group Vegetable and Melon Farming
  • 11121 is the five-digit industry Vegetable and Melon Farming
  • 111219 is the national industry Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming

How is 111219 different from 111211?

111219 is Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming, while 111211 is Potato Farming. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
