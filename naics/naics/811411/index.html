NAICS 811411: Home and Garden Equipment Repair and Maintenance

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

811411 is the NAICS national industry Home and Garden Equipment Repair and Maintenance in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 811 Repair and Maintenance › 8114 Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance. In the code order it is number 949 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 2 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 81141 Home and Garden Equipment and Appliance Repair and Maintenance, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repairing and servicing home and garden equipment without retailing new home and garden equipment, such as lawnmowers, handheld power tools, edgers, snowblowers, leaf blowers, and trimmers.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is repairing and servicing home and garden equipment without retailing new home and garden equipment, such as lawnmowers, handheld power tools, edgers, snowblowers, leaf blowers, and trimmers.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 811411 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 811411 from the left:

  • 81 is the sector Other Services (except Public Administration)
  • 811 is the subsector Repair and Maintenance
  • 8114 is the industry group Personal and Household Goods Repair and Maintenance
  • 81141 is the five-digit industry Home and Garden Equipment and Appliance Repair and Maintenance
  • 811411 is the national industry Home and Garden Equipment Repair and Maintenance

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 10 terms to 811411, from "Engine repair, small" to "Tractor, lawn and garden, repair and maintenance services without retailing new lawn and garden tractors". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Engine repair, small; Garden equipment repair and maintenance services without retailing new garden equipment; Garden tool sharpening and repair services; Handtools, power-driven, repair and maintenance services without retailing new power-driven handtools; Lawn and garden equipment repair and maintenance services without retailing new lawn and garden equipment; Lawnmower repair and maintenance shops without retailing new lawnmowers; Saw repair and maintenance (except sawmills) without retailing new saws; Small engine repair and maintenance shops; Tool, home and garden, sharpening and repair services without retailing new home and garden tools; Tractor, lawn and garden, repair and maintenance services without retailing new lawn and garden tractors.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Saw repair and maintenance (except sawmills) without retailing new saws.
