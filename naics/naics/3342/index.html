NAICS 3342: Communications Equipment Manufacturing

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3342 is the NAICS industry group Communications Equipment Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing wire telephone and data communications equipment, radio and television broadcast and wireless communications equipment, and all other communications equipment.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing wire telephone and data communications equipment, radio and television broadcast and wireless communications equipment, and all other communications equipment.

What it contains

Communications Equipment Manufacturing has 3 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 3342 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 334 is the subsector Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing
  • 3342 is the industry group Communications Equipment Manufacturing

Neighbouring industry groups

Communications Equipment Manufacturing sits in the subsector 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing, which has 6 industry groups in all. The others are: 3341 Computer and Peripheral Equipment Manufacturing, 3343 Audio and Video Equipment Manufacturing, 3344 Semiconductor and Other Electronic Component Manufacturing, 3345 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing, 3346 Manufacturing and Reproducing Magnetic and Optical Media.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing subsector group establishments that manufacture computers, computer peripherals, communications equipment, and similar electronic products, and establishments that manufacture components for such products. The Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing industries are combined in the hierarchy of NAICS because of their economic significance to the economies of all three North American countries. Common questions

What is NAICS code 3342?

NAICS 3342 is the industry group "Communications Equipment Manufacturing" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 334 Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing.

What does NAICS 3342 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is manufacturing wire telephone and data communications equipment, radio and television broadcast and wireless communications equipment, and all other communications equipment.

Which sector is 3342 in?

3342 belongs to NAICS sector 31-33, Manufacturing.

How many national industries does 3342 cover?

3342 covers 3 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 3342 different from 3341?

3342 is Communications Equipment Manufacturing, while 3341 is Computer and Peripheral Equipment Manufacturing. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
