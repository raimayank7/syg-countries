NAICS 8134: Civic and Social Organizations

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

8134 is the NAICS industry group Civic and Social Organizations in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 81 Other Services (except Public Administration) › 813 Religious, Grantmaking, Civic, Professional, and Similar Organizations.

What it contains

Civic and Social Organizations has 1 national industry under it, with a page of its own.

  • 813410 Civic and Social Organizations: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in promoting the civic and social interests of their members.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 1 national industry under Civic and Social Organizations.

The industry in full: 813410 Civic and Social Organizations

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in promoting the civic and social interests of their members. Establishments in this industry may operate bars and restaurants for their members.

How is 8134 different from 8131?

8134 is Civic and Social Organizations, while 8131 is Religious Organizations. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
