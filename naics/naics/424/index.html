NAICS 424: Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods

Subsector, 2022 NAICS.

424 is the NAICS subsector Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade.

What the Census says

Industries in the Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods subsector sell nondurable goods to other businesses. Nondurable goods are items generally with a normal life expectancy of less than three years. Nondurable goods merchant wholesale trade establishments are engaged in wholesaling products, such as paper and paper products, chemicals and chemical products, drugs, textiles and textile products, apparel, footwear, groceries, farm products, petroleum and petroleum products, alcoholic beverages, books, magazines, newspapers, flowers and nursery stock, and tobacco products.

The detailed industries within the subsector are organized in the classification structure based on the products sold.

Agents and brokers primarily engaged in wholesaling nondurable goods, generally on a commission or fee basis, are classified in Subsector 425, Wholesale Trade Agents and Brokers.

  • 4242 Drugs and Druggists' Sundries Merchant Wholesalers (1 national industry)

  • 4244 Grocery and Related Product Merchant Wholesalers (9 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of (1) a general line of groceries
  • 4245 Farm Product Raw Material Merchant Wholesalers (3 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of agricultural products (except raw milk, live poultry, and fresh fruits and vegetables), such...
  • 4246 Chemical and Allied Products Merchant Wholesalers (2 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of chemicals, plastics materials and basic forms and shapes, and allied products.
  • 4247 Petroleum and Petroleum Products Merchant Wholesalers (2 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of petroleum and petroleum products, including liquefied petroleum gas.
  • 4248 Beer, Wine, and Distilled Alcoholic Beverage Merchant Wholesalers (2 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of beer, ale, wine, and/or distilled alcoholic beverages.
  • 4249 Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers (6 national industries): This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of nondurable goods, such as farm supplies
