NAICS 315990: Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

315990 is the NAICS national industry Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 315 Apparel Manufacturing › 3159 Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 196 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31599 Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing apparel and accessories (except apparel knitting mills, cut and sew apparel contractors, and cut and sew apparel manufacturing (except contractors)). Jobbers, who perform entrepreneurial functions involved in apparel accessories manufacture, including buying raw materials, designing and preparing samples, arranging for apparel accessories to be made from their materials, and marketing finished apparel accessories, are included. Examples of products made by these establishments are belts, caps, gloves (except medical, safety, sporting), hats, and neckties.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is manufacturing apparel and accessories (except apparel knitting mills, cut and sew apparel contractors, and cut and sew apparel manufacturing (except contractors)).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 315990 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
