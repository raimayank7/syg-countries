NAICS 4889: Other Support Activities for Transportation

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4889 is the NAICS industry group Other Support Activities for Transportation in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 48-49 Transportation and Warehousing › 488 Support Activities for Transportation.

Other Support Activities for Transportation has 2 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 2 national industries under Other Support Activities for Transportation.

The industry in full: 488991 Packing and Crating

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in packing, crating, and otherwise preparing goods for transportation.

The Census index lists these terms for it: Crating goods for shipping; Packing and preparing goods for shipping; Preparing goods for transportation (i.e., crating, packing).

The industry in full: 488999 All Other Support Activities for Transportation

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing support activities to transportation (except for air transportation; rail transportation; water transportation; road transportation; freight transportation arrangement; and packing and crating).

Common questions

What is NAICS code 4889?

What does NAICS 4889 include?

Other Support Activities for Transportation

Which sector is 4889 in?

How many national industries does 4889 cover?

How is 4889 different from 4881?

4889 is Other Support Activities for Transportation, while 4881 is Support Activities for Air Transportation. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
