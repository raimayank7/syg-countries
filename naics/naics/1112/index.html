NAICS 1112: Vegetable and Melon Farming

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

1112 is the NAICS industry group Vegetable and Melon Farming in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 111 Crop Production.

What it contains

Vegetable and Melon Farming has 2 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 111211 Potato Farming: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing potatoes and/or producing seed potatoes.
  • 111219 Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 2 national industries under Vegetable and Melon Farming.

The industry in full: 111211 Potato Farming

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing potatoes and/or producing seed potatoes.

The Census index lists these terms for it: Potato farming, field and seed potato production; Sweet potato farming, field and seed potato production; Vegetable and melon farming, potato dominant crop, field and seed production; Vegetable and potato farming, potato dominant crop, field and seed potato production; Yam farming, field and seed production.

The industry in full: 111219 Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) growing melons and/or vegetables (except potatoes; dry peas; dry beans; field, silage, or seed corn; and sugar beets); (2) producing vegetable and/or melon seeds; and (3) growing vegetable and/or melon bedding plants.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 1112 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 111 is the subsector Crop Production
  • 1112 is the industry group Vegetable and Melon Farming

Common questions

What is NAICS code 1112?

NAICS 1112 is the industry group "Vegetable and Melon Farming" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 111 Crop Production.

What does NAICS 1112 include?

Vegetable and Melon Farming

Which sector is 1112 in?

1112 belongs to NAICS sector 11, Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

How many national industries does 1112 cover?

1112 covers 2 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 1112 different from 1111?

1112 is Vegetable and Melon Farming, while 1111 is Oilseed and Grain Farming. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
