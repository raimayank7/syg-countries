NAICS 4453: Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

4453 is the NAICS industry group Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 44-45 Retail Trade › 445 Food and Beverage Retailers.

What it contains

Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers has 1 national industry under it, with a page of its own.

  • 445320 Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing packaged alcoholic beverages, such as ale, beer, wine, and liquor.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 1 national industry under Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers.

The industry in full: 445320 Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing packaged alcoholic beverages, such as ale, beer, wine, and liquor.

The Census index lists these terms for it: Beer stores, packaged; Duty free liquor shops; Liquor stores, package; Package stores (i.e., liquor); Wine shops, packaged.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 4453 from the left:

  • 44-45 is the sector Retail Trade
  • 445 is the subsector Food and Beverage Retailers
  • 4453 is the industry group Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers

What the index file lists

Neighbouring industry groups

Beer, Wine, and Liquor Retailers sits in the subsector 445 Food and Beverage Retailers, which has 3 industry groups in all. The others are: 4451 Grocery and Convenience Retailers, 4452 Specialty Food Retailers.

The subsector around it

One level up is the subsector 445 Food and Beverage Retailers. The Census describes it this way: Industries in the Food and Beverage Retailers subsector primarily retail general or specialized lines of food and beverage products. Establishments in this subsector with fixed point-of-sale locations have special equipment (e.g., freezers, refrigerated display cases, refrigerators) for displaying food and beverage products and have staff trained in the processing of food products to guarantee the proper storage and sanitary conditions required by regulatory authority.
