NAICS 3152: Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

3152 is the NAICS industry group Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 315 Apparel Manufacturing.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing cut and sew apparel from woven fabric or purchased knit fabric. Included in this industry group is a diverse range of establishments manufacturing full lines of ready-to-wear apparel and custom apparel: apparel contractors, performing cutting or sewing operations on materials owned by others; jobbers, performing entrepreneurial functions involved in apparel manufacturing; and tailors, manufacturing custom garments for individual clients. Establishments weaving or knitting fabric, without manufacturing apparel, are classified in Subsector 313, Textile Mills.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is manufacturing cut and sew apparel from woven fabric or purchased knit fabric.

Neighbouring industry groups

Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing sits in the subsector 315 Apparel Manufacturing, which has 3 industry groups in all. The others are: 3151 Apparel Knitting Mills, 3159 Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing.

Common questions

What is NAICS code 3152?

NAICS 3152 is the industry group "Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 315 Apparel Manufacturing.

What does NAICS 3152 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is manufacturing cut and sew apparel from woven fabric or purchased knit fabric.

Which sector is 3152 in?

3152 belongs to NAICS sector 31-33, Manufacturing.

How many national industries does 3152 cover?

3152 covers 2 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 3152 different from 3151?

3152 is Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing, while 3151 is Apparel Knitting Mills. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
