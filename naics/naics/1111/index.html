NAICS 1111: Oilseed and Grain Farming

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

1111 is the NAICS industry group Oilseed and Grain Farming in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 11 Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting › 111 Crop Production.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) growing oilseed and/or grain crops and/or (2) producing oilseed and grain seeds. These crops have an annual life cycle and are typically grown in open fields.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is (1) growing oilseed and/or grain crops and/or (2) producing oilseed and grain seeds.

What it contains

Oilseed and Grain Farming has 8 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 111110 Soybean Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing soybeans and/or producing soybean seeds.
  • 111120 Oilseed (except Soybean) Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing fibrous oilseed producing plants and/or producing oilseed seeds, such as sunflower, safflower, flax, rape, canola, and sesame.
  • 111130 Dry Pea and Bean Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing dry peas, beans, and/or lentils.
  • 111140 Wheat Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing wheat and/or producing wheat seeds.
  • 111150 Corn Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing corn (except sweet corn) and/or producing corn seeds.
  • 111160 Rice Farming: This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing rice (except wild rice) and/or producing rice seeds.
  • 111191 Oilseed and Grain Combination Farming: This U.S. industry comprises establishments engaged in growing a combination of oilseed(s) and grain(s) with no one oilseed (or family of oilseeds) or grain (or family of grains) accounting for...
  • 111199 All Other Grain Farming: This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing grains and/or producing grain(s) seeds (except wheat, corn, rice, and oilseed(s) and grain(s) combinations).

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 8 national industries under Oilseed and Grain Farming.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 1111 from the left:

  • 11 is the sector Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting
  • 111 is the subsector Crop Production
  • 1111 is the industry group Oilseed and Grain Farming

Neighbouring industry groups

Oilseed and Grain Farming sits in the subsector 111 Crop Production, which has 5 industry groups in all. The others are: 1112 Vegetable and Melon Farming, 1113 Fruit and Tree Nut Farming, 1114 Greenhouse, Nursery, and Floriculture Production, 1119 Other Crop Farming.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 1111 the main activity here is (1) growing oilseed and/or grain crops and/or (2) producing oilseed and grain seeds. The nearest codes in the same subsector say:

  • 1113 Fruit and Tree Nut Farming: main activity is growing fruit and/or tree nut crops
  • 1114 Greenhouse, Nursery, and Floriculture Production: main activity is growing crops of any kind under cover and/or growing nursery stock and flowers

The subsector around it

Common questions

What is NAICS code 1111?

NAICS 1111 is the industry group "Oilseed and Grain Farming" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 111 Crop Production.

What does NAICS 1111 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is (1) growing oilseed and/or grain crops and/or (2) producing oilseed and grain seeds.

Which sector is 1111 in?

1111 belongs to NAICS sector 11, Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting.

How many national industries does 1111 cover?

1111 covers 8 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 1111 different from 1112?

1111 is Oilseed and Grain Farming, while 1112 is Vegetable and Melon Farming. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
