NAICS 424990: Other Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

424990 is the NAICS national industry Other Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 424 Merchant Wholesalers, Nondurable Goods › 4249 Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 544 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42499 Other Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of nondurable goods (except printing and writing paper; stationery and office supplies; industrial and personal service paper; drugs and druggists' sundries; apparel, piece goods, and notions; grocery and related products; farm product raw materials; chemical and allied products; petroleum and petroleum products; beer, wine, and distilled alcoholic beverages; farm supplies; books, periodicals, and newspapers; flowers, nursery stock, and florists' supplies; tobacco and tobacco products; and paint, varnishes, wallpaper, and supplies).

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is the merchant wholesale distribution of nondurable goods (except printing and writing paper.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 424990 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
