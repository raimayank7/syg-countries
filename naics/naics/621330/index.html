NAICS 621330: Offices of Mental Health Practitioners (except Physicians)

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

621330 is the NAICS national industry Offices of Mental Health Practitioners (except Physicians) in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 62 Health Care and Social Assistance › 621 Ambulatory Health Care Services › 6213 Offices of Other Health Practitioners. In the code order it is number 866 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 62133 Offices of Mental Health Practitioners (except Physicians), holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments of independent mental health practitioners (except physicians) primarily engaged in (1) the diagnosis and treatment of mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders and/or (2) the diagnosis and treatment of individual or group social dysfunction brought about by such causes as mental illness, alcohol and substance abuse, physical and emotional trauma, or stress. These practitioners operate private or group practices in their own offices (e.g., centers, clinics) or in the facilities of others, such as hospitals or HMO medical centers.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is (1) the diagnosis and treatment of mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders and/or (2) the diagnosis and treatment of individual or group social dysfunction brought about by such causes as mental illness, alcohol and substance abuse, physical and emotional trauma, or stress.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 621330 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 6 terms to 621330, from "Clinical psychologists' offices (e.g., centers, clinics)" to "Social workers', mental health, offices (e.g., centers, clinics)". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Clinical psychologists' offices (e.g., centers, clinics); Doctors of psychology offices (e.g., centers, clinics); Psychoanalysts' (except MDs or DOs) offices (e.g., centers, clinics); Psychologists' offices (e.g., centers, clinics), clinical; Psychotherapists' (except MDs or DOs) offices (e.g., centers, clinics); Social workers', mental health, offices (e.g., centers, clinics).

2 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Psychoanalysts' (except MDs or DOs) offices (e.g., centers, clinics); Psychotherapists' (except MDs or DOs) offices (e.g., centers, clinics).
