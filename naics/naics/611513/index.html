NAICS 611513: Apprenticeship Training

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

611513 is the NAICS national industry Apprenticeship Training in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 61 Educational Services › 611 Educational Services › 6115 Technical and Trade Schools. In the code order it is number 852 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 4 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 61151 Technical and Trade Schools, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in offering apprenticeship training programs. These programs involve applied training as well as course work.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is offering apprenticeship training programs.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 611513 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 611513 from the left:

  • 61 is the sector Educational Services
  • 611 is the subsector Educational Services
  • 6115 is the industry group Technical and Trade Schools
  • 61151 is the five-digit industry Technical and Trade Schools
  • 611513 is the national industry Apprenticeship Training

Businesses and products indexed here

The Census index file points 10 terms to 611513, from "Apprenticeship training programs" to "Vocational apprenticeship training". That is below the median of 13 terms for a six-digit industry that has any. The entries are listed below as the Census writes them, so they show what the code is used for in practice: Apprenticeship training programs; Carpenters' apprenticeship training; Craft union apprenticeship training programs; Electricians' apprenticeship training; Mechanic's apprenticeship training; Plumbers' apprenticeship training; Sheet metal workers' apprenticeship training; Steam fitters' apprenticeship training; Trade union apprenticeship training programs; Vocational apprenticeship training.

Neighbouring national industries

Apprenticeship Training sits in the NAICS industry 61151 Technical and Trade Schools, which has 4 national industries in all. The others are: 611511 Cosmetology and Barber Schools, 611512 Flight Training, 611519 Other Technical and Trade Schools.

How it differs from the codes next to it

If two neighbouring codes seem to fit, compare their descriptions. For 611513 the main activity here is offering apprenticeship training programs. The nearest codes in the same NAICS industry say:

  • 611512 Flight Training: main activity is offering aviation and flight training
  • 611511 Cosmetology and Barber Schools: main activity is offering training in barbering, hair styling, or the cosmetic arts, such as makeup or skin care
  • 611519 Other Technical and Trade Schools: main activity is offering job or career vocational or technical courses (except cosmetology and barber training, aviation and flight training, and apprenticeship training)

Codes that are easy to confuse with it

These codes are in other parts of the classification but use some of the same words or index terms, so a search can land on them instead. Check which activity each one is for before choosing:

  • 611420 Computer Training shares the word "training" in its title
  • 611430 Professional and Management Development Training shares the word "training" in its title

Common questions

What is NAICS code 611513?

NAICS 611513 is the national industry "Apprenticeship Training" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 6115 Technical and Trade Schools.

What does NAICS 611513 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is offering apprenticeship training programs.

Which sector is 611513 in?

611513 belongs to NAICS sector 61, Educational Services.

What kinds of business are indexed under 611513?

How is 611513 different from 611511?

611513 is Apprenticeship Training, while 611511 is Cosmetology and Barber Schools. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
