NAICS 334515: Instrument Manufacturing for Measuring and Testing Electricity and Electrical Signals

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

334515 is the NAICS national industry Instrument Manufacturing for Measuring and Testing Electricity and Electrical Signals. It is part of the industry group 3345 Navigational, Measuring, Electromedical, and Control Instruments Manufacturing.

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing instruments for measuring and testing the characteristics of electricity and electrical signals. Examples of products made by these establishments are circuit and continuity testers, voltmeters, ohm meters, wattmeters, multimeters, and semiconductor test equipment.
