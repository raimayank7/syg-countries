NAICS 423110: Automobile and Other Motor Vehicle Merchant Wholesalers

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

423110 is the NAICS national industry Automobile and Other Motor Vehicle Merchant Wholesalers in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 42 Wholesale Trade › 423 Merchant Wholesalers, Durable Goods › 4231 Motor Vehicle and Motor Vehicle Parts and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers. In the code order it is number 477 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 42311 Automobile and Other Motor Vehicle Merchant Wholesalers, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of new and used passenger automobiles, trucks, trailers, and other motor vehicles, such as motorcycles, motor homes, and snowmobiles.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is the merchant wholesale distribution of new and used passenger automobiles, trucks, trailers, and other motor vehicles, such as motorcycles, motor homes, and snowmobiles.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.
