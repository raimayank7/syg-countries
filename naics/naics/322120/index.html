NAICS 322120: Paper Mills

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

322120 is the NAICS national industry Paper Mills in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 322 Paper Manufacturing › 3221 Pulp, Paper, and Paperboard Mills. In the code order it is number 214 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 32212 Paper Mills, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing paper from pulp. These establishments may manufacture or purchase pulp. In addition, the establishments may convert the paper they make. The activity of making paper classifies an establishment into this industry regardless of the output.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing paper from pulp.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 322120 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How it differs from the codes next to it
