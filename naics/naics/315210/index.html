NAICS 315210: Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

315210 is the NAICS national industry Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 315 Apparel Manufacturing › 3152 Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 194 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31521 Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments commonly referred to as contractors primarily engaged in (1) cutting materials owned by others for apparel and accessories and/or (2) sewing materials owned by others for apparel and accessories.

In plain words: a business belongs here when its main activity is (1) cutting materials owned by others for apparel and accessories and/or (2) sewing materials owned by others for apparel and accessories.

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 315210 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 315210 from the left:

  • 31-33 is the sector Manufacturing
  • 315 is the subsector Apparel Manufacturing
  • 3152 is the industry group Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing
  • 31521 is the five-digit industry Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors
  • 315210 is the national industry Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors
