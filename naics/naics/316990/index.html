NAICS 316990: Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

316990 is the NAICS national industry Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 316 Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing › 3169 Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing. In the code order it is number 199 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

Its five-digit parent, 31699 Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing, holds only this one industry, so the two codes carry the same title and the same meaning.

What the Census says

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing leather products (except footwear and apparel) from purchased leather or leather substitutes (e.g., fabric, plastics).

Illustrative Examples:

Billfolds, all materials, manufacturing Boot and shoe cut stock and findings, leather, manufacturing Dog furnishings (e.g., collars, harnesses, leashes, muzzles), manufacturing Luggage, all materials, manufacturing Shoe soles, leather, manufacturing Purses, women's, all materials (except metal), manufacturing Toilet kits and cases (except metal) manufacturing Watch bands (except metal) manufacturing Welders' jackets, leggings, and sleeves, leather, manufacturing

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is manufacturing leather products (except footwear and apparel) from purchased leather or leather substitutes (e.g., fabric, plastics).

The description opens with "This industry", the wording the Census uses for a class whose definition is shared by the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 316990 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.
