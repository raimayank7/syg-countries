NAICS 332813: Electroplating, Plating, Polishing, Anodizing, and Coloring

National industry, 2022 NAICS.

332813 is the NAICS national industry Electroplating, Plating, Polishing, Anodizing, and Coloring in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 31-33 Manufacturing › 332 Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing › 3328 Coating, Engraving, Heat Treating, and Allied Activities. In the code order it is number 339 of the 1,012 six-digit industries.

It is one of 3 national industries inside the five-digit NAICS industry 33281 Coating, Engraving, Heat Treating, and Allied Activities, and the last digit tells them apart from each other.

What the Census says

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in electroplating, plating, anodizing, coloring, buffing, polishing, cleaning, and sandblasting metals and metal products for the trade. Included in this industry are establishments that perform these processes on other materials, such as plastics, in addition to metals.

To read that description in everyday terms, the main activity this code covers is electroplating, plating, anodizing, coloring, buffing, polishing, cleaning, and sandblasting metals and metal products for the trade.

The description opens with "This U.S. industry", which is the wording the Census uses for a class that is defined for the United States specifically, as opposed to "This industry", used when the class is shared by the three countries.

The Census entry also carries a Cross-References note that sends some related activities to other codes. The workbook keeps only its heading, so read the full entry for 332813 on census.gov for the exact exclusions.

1 of the entries carry an "except" qualifier, which marks a boundary with another code: Coloring metals and metal products (except coating) for the trade.
