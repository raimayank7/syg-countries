NAICS 7111: Performing Arts Companies

Industry group, 2022 NAICS.

7111 is the NAICS industry group Performing Arts Companies in the 2022 edition of the classification. It is filed under 71 Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation › 711 Performing Arts, Spectator Sports, and Related Industries.

What the Census says

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in producing live presentations involving the performances of actors and actresses, singers, dancers, musical groups and artists, and other performing artists.

Put simply, the class is meant for establishments whose main work is producing live presentations involving the performances of actors and actresses, singers, dancers, musical groups and artists, and other performing artists.

Performing Arts Companies has 4 national industries under it, each with a page of its own.

  • 711110 Theater Companies and Dinner Theaters: This industry comprises (1) companies, groups, or theaters primarily engaged in producing the following live theatrical presentations
  • 711120 Dance Companies: This industry comprises companies, groups, or theaters primarily engaged in producing all types of live theatrical dance (e.g., ballet, contemporary dance, folk dance) presentations.
  • 711130 Musical Groups and Artists: This industry comprises (1) groups primarily engaged in producing live musical entertainment (except theatrical musical or opera productions) and (2) independent (i.e., freelance) artists primarily...
  • 711190 Other Performing Arts Companies: This industry comprises companies or groups (except theater companies, dance companies, and musical groups and artists) primarily engaged in producing live theatrical presentations.

Counting down to the six-digit level, there are 4 national industries under Performing Arts Companies.

How the code is read

A NAICS code builds up one level at a time, and each extra digit narrows the activity. Reading 7111 from the left:

  • 71 is the sector Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation
  • 711 is the subsector Performing Arts, Spectator Sports, and Related Industries
  • 7111 is the industry group Performing Arts Companies

Common questions

What is NAICS code 7111?

NAICS 7111 is the industry group "Performing Arts Companies" in the 2022 North American Industry Classification System, filed under 711 Performing Arts, Spectator Sports, and Related Industries.

What does NAICS 7111 include?

The Census description covers establishments whose main activity is producing live presentations involving the performances of actors and actresses, singers, dancers, musical groups and artists, and other performing artists.

Which sector is 7111 in?

7111 belongs to NAICS sector 71, Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation.

How many national industries does 7111 cover?

7111 covers 4 six-digit national industries in the 2022 list.

How is 7111 different from 7112?

7111 is Performing Arts Companies, while 7112 is Spectator Sports. Compare their descriptions: they are separate codes because the Census treats the two activities as different.

Is this the code to put on a form?

Not by itself. A form asks for the code of the main activity, in the NAICS year it names. Read the description above and the codes beside it, and follow the instructions of the agency that asks.
