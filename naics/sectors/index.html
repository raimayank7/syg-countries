NAICS sectors

20 sectors, 1012 national industries.

NAICS sorts all businesses into sectors, then subsectors, industry groups and industries; the number of digits of the code shows the level: 2 digits for a sector, 3 for a subsector, 4 for an industry group and 6 for a national industry. Three sectors share a combined code because they are made of several two-digit parts: Manufacturing (31-33), Retail Trade (44-45) and Transportation and Warehousing (48-49).
