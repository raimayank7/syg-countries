Guernsey

Guernsey is in Northern Europe. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. Its official name is Bailiwick of Guernsey. In the languages of the place it is called Guernesey, Dgèrnésiais. The capital is St. Peter Port.

Land and position

Guernsey covers about 78 square kilometres. By area it ranks 232nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Montserrat and a little larger than San Marino. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 49° north, 3° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 65,228 people live in Guernsey (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 207th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 836 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: English, Jèrriais. A person from Guernsey is called Channel Islander.

Money and economy

The currency is the British pound (code GBP, symbol £). It is also the main currency of Isle of Man, Jersey, United Kingdom. Also listed as used here: Guernsey pound (GGP).

Time

Guernsey uses one time zone, UTC (Europe/Guernsey). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
