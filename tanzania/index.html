Tanzania

Tanzania is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is United Republic of Tanzania. The capital is Dodoma.

Land and position

Tanzania covers about 945,087 square kilometres. By area it ranks 32nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Egypt and a little larger than Nigeria. It shares a land border with 8 neighbours: Burundi, DR Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Rwanda, Uganda, Zambia. Its centre lies at about 6° south, 35° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 70.5 million people live in Tanzania (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 21st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.86 percent of the world's people. On average there are 75 people to each square kilometre. About 37 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 67 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Swahili (macrolanguage), English, Arabic. A person from Tanzania is called Tanzanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Tanzanian shilling (code TZS, symbol Sh). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 90.1 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,319 for each person (2025).

Time

Tanzania uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Africa/Dar es Salaam). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
