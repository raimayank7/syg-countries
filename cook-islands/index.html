Cook Islands

The Cook Islands is in Polynesia. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. In the language of the place it is called Kūki 'Āirani. The capital is Avarua.

Land and position

The Cook Islands covers about 240 square kilometres. By area it ranks 222nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Saint Pierre and Miquelon and a little larger than American Samoa. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 21° south, 160° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 21,388 people live in the Cook Islands (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 224th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 89 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: English, Maori. A person from the Cook Islands is called Cook Islander.

Money and economy

The currency is the New Zealand dollar (code NZD, symbol $). It is also the main currency of New Zealand, Niue, Pitcairn Islands, Tokelau. Also listed as used here: Cook Islands dollar (CKD).

Time

The Cook Islands uses one time zone, UTC-10:00 (Pacific/Rarotonga). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
