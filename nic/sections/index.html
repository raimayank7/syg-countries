NIC-2008 sections and divisions

21 sections, 88 divisions, 238 groups, 419 classes, 1,300 sub-classes.

NIC-2008 sorts all economic activities into sections (letters A to U), divisions (two digits), groups (three), classes (four) and sub-classes (five). The first four digits follow the international ISIC Rev.4; the fifth digit was added for national needs. Pick a division to see its groups and classes.
