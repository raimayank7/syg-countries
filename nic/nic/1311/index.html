NIC 1311: Preparation and spinning of textile fibres

Class, NIC-2008.

1311 is the NIC-2008 class Preparation and spinning of textile fibres. It belongs to group 131 Spinning, weaving and finishing of textiles, in division 13 Manufacture of textiles, section C Manufacturing.

What the classification says about it

This class excludes: - preparatory operations carried out in combination with agriculture or farming, see 01 - retting of plants bearing vegetable textile fibres (jute, flax, coir etc.), see 0116 - cotton ginning, see 0163 - manufacture of synthetic or artificial fibres and tows, manufacture of single yarns (including high-tenacity yarn and yarn for carpets) of synthetic or artificial fibres, see 2030 - manufacture of glass fibres, see 2310

Five-digit sub-classes

NIC-2008 divides this class into 5 sub-classes, the level of detail added for Indian needs: 13111 Preparation and spinning of cotton fiber including blended* cotton; 13112 Preparation and spinning of silk fiber including blended* silk; 13113 Preparation and spinning of wool, including other animal hair and blended* wool including other animal hair; 13114 Preparation and spinning of man-made fiber including blended* man-made fiber; 13119 Preparation and spinning of jute, mesta and other natural fibers including blended natural fibers n.e.c. *Blended yarn/fabrics means, yarn/fabrics containing more than 50% of one fiber..

Link to NIC-2004

The concordance table of the publication links this class to NIC-2004 as 1711(p)+1713(p). "(p)" means part of that class and "+" means that parts of several classes were joined.

Neighbouring classes

Other classes of the same group: 1312 Weaving of textiles, 1313 Finishing of textiles.
