NIC 2432: Casting of non-ferrous metals

Class, NIC-2008.

2432 is the NIC-2008 class Casting of non-ferrous metals. It belongs to group 243 Casting of metals, in division 24 Manufacture of basic metals, section C Manufacturing.

What the classification says about it

This class includes: - casting of semi-finished products of aluminium, magnesium, titanium, zinc etc. - casting of light metal castings - casting of heavy metal castings - casting of precious metal castings - die-casting of non-ferrous metal castings

Five-digit sub-classes

NIC-2008 divides this class into 1 sub-class, the level of detail added for Indian needs: 24320 Casting of non-ferrous metals.

Link to NIC-2004

The concordance table of the publication links this class to NIC-2004 as 2732. "(p)" means part of that class and "+" means that parts of several classes were joined.

Neighbouring classes
