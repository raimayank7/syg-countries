NIC 2392: Manufacture of clay building materials

Class, NIC-2008.

2392 is the NIC-2008 class Manufacture of clay building materials. It belongs to group 239 Manufacture of non-metallic mineral products n.e.c., in division 23 Manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products, section C Manufacturing.

What the classification says about it

This class excludes: - manufacture of artificial stone (e.g. cultured marble), see 2220 - manufacture of refractory ceramic products, see 2391

Five-digit sub-classes

NIC-2008 divides this class into 4 sub-classes, the level of detail added for Indian needs: 23921 Manufacture of bricks; 23922 Manufacture of non-refractory ceramic sanitary wares: sinks, baths, water- closet pans, flushing cistern etc.; 23923 Manufacture of non-refractory ceramic pipes, conduits, guttering and pipe fittings; 23929 Manufacture of other clay building materials.

Link to NIC-2004

The concordance table of the publication links this class to NIC-2004 as 2691(p)+2693. "(p)" means part of that class and "+" means that parts of several classes were joined.

Neighbouring classes

Other classes of the same group: 2391 Manufacture of refractory products, 2393 Manufacture of other porcelain and ceramic products, 2394 Manufacture of cement, lime and plaster, 2395 Manufacture of articles of concrete, cement and plaster, 2396 Cutting, shaping and finishing of stone, 2399 Manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products n.e.c..

Where this class sits

Class 2392 is number 2 of the 7 classes in group 239 and number 3 of the 8 classes in division 23; across all of NIC-2008 it is number 113 of 419 classes in code order. It has 4 sub-classes, below the average of 5.1 for classes of this division. The code is built from the top down: section C is the letter level, 23 is the two-digit division, 239 the three-digit group and 2392 this four-digit class, which NIC-2008 divides further into five-digit sub-classes. On a form, give the most detailed level the form accepts, and if it offers a choice of sub-classes pick the one whose title matches the main activity of the business.
