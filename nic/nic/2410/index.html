NIC 2410: Manufacture of basic iron and steel

Class, NIC-2008.

2410 is the NIC-2008 class Manufacture of basic iron and steel. It belongs to group 241 Manufacture of basic iron and steel, in division 24 Manufacture of basic metals, section C Manufacturing.

What the classification says about it

This class excludes: - manufacture of tubes, pipes and hollow profiles and of tube or pipe fittings of cast-iron, see 2431 - manufacture of seamless tubes and pipes of steel by centrifugal casting, see 2431 - manufacture of tube or pipe fittings of cast-steel, see 2431

Five-digit sub-classes

NIC-2008 divides this class into 9 sub-classes, the level of detail added for Indian needs: 24101 Manufacture of pig iron and spiegeleisen in pigs, blocks or other primary forms; 24102 Manufacture of direct reduction of iron (sponge iron) and other spongy ferrous products; 24103 Manufacture of steel in ingots or other primary forms, and other semi- finished products of steel; 24104 Manufacture of ferro-alloys; 24105 Manufacture of hot-rolled and cold-rolled products of steel; 24106 Manufacture of tube and tube fittings of basic iron and steel; 24107 Manufacture of railway track materials (unassembled rails) of steel; 24108 Manufacture of wire of steel by cold drawing or stretching; 24109 Manufacture of other basic iron and steel n.e.c.

Link to NIC-2004

The concordance table of the publication links this class to NIC-2004 as 2711+2712+2713+2714+2715+2716+2717+2718+2719. "(p)" means part of that class and "+" means that parts of several classes were joined.

Where this class sits

Class 2410 is number 1 of the 1 classes in group 241 and number 1 of the 4 classes in division 24; across all of NIC-2008 it is number 119 of 419 classes in code order. It has 9 sub-classes, above the average of 4.5 for classes of this division. The code is built from the top down: section C is the letter level, 24 is the two-digit division, 241 the three-digit group and 2410 this four-digit class, which NIC-2008 divides further into five-digit sub-classes. On a form, give the most detailed level the form accepts, and if it offers a choice of sub-classes pick the one whose title matches the main activity of the business.
