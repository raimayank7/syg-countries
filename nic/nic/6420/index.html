NIC 6420: Activities of holding companies

Class, NIC-2008.

6420 is the NIC-2008 class Activities of holding companies. It belongs to group 642 Activities of holding companies, in division 64 Financial service activities, except insurance and pension funding, section K Financial and insurance activities.

What the classification says about it

This class includes the activities of holding companies, i.e. units that hold the assets (owning controlling-levels of equity) of a group of subsidiary corporations and whose principal activity is owning the group. The holding companies in this class do not provide any other service to the businesses in which the equity is held, i.e. they do not administer or manage other units.

Five-digit sub-classes

NIC-2008 divides this class into 1 sub-class, the level of detail added for Indian needs: 64200 Activities of holding companies.

Link to NIC-2004

The concordance table of the publication links this class to NIC-2004 as 6599(p). "(p)" means part of that class and "+" means that parts of several classes were joined.
