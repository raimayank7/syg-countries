Earthquakes by country and area

The 106 areas with the most earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above since 1900.

USGS gives every earthquake a place name such as "Northern Honshu, Japan" or "Mid-Atlantic Ridge". This list groups the earthquakes by the last part of that name: a country, a state, an island group or an ocean ridge. It is the way the catalog itself names places, so a country with an offshore trench may be listed under its own name and under the name of the sea region too. Only areas with at least 60 listed earthquakes get a page. The areas near the Pacific Ocean (Indonesia, Japan, Papua New Guinea, the Philippines, Russia's Kuril and Kamchatka coasts, Tonga, Alaska, Vanuatu and Chile) lead the list, which fits the way most large earthquakes happen where tectonic plates meet.
