Earthquakes of 1937: the strongest and how many

234 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 234 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1937: 136 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 89 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 7 January 1937, listed as "Southern Qinghai, China".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (27), Indonesia (21), Russia (20), Papua New Guinea (11), Philippines (10), Mid-Atlantic Ridge (10). The busiest month was August with 25 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1937, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1937 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 19; February 24; March 23; April 15; May 13; June 12; July 23; August 25; September 22; October 14; November 19; December 25. Compared with 1936, which has 263 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 29 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 7 January 1937 M7.8 Southern Qinghai, China; 25 January 1937 M7.2 76 km E of Kirakira, Solomon Islands; 21 February 1937 M7.4 170 km ESE of Kuril’sk, Russia; 16 April 1937 M7.3 227 km WNW of Haveluloto, Tonga; 21 June 1937 M7.1 55 km SW of Santiago de Cao, Peru; 22 July 1937 M7.1 Central Alaska; 11 August 1937 M7.1 205 km NNE of Gili Air, Indonesia; 20 August 1937 M7.6 6 km E of Tignoan, Philippines; 23 December 1937 M7.4 3 km W of Huixtepec, Mexico.
