Earthquakes of 1972: the strongest and how many

874 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 874 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1972: 742 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 117 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 2 December 1972, listed as "1972 Mindanao, Philippines Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (81), Japan (56), Philippines (51), China (49), Papua New Guinea (48), Vanuatu (44). The busiest month was September with 106 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1972, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 57; February 57; March 69; April 75; May 70; June 74; July 68; August 88; September 106; October 53; November 75; December 82. Compared with 1971, which has 899 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 25 fewer.
