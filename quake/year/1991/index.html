Earthquakes of 1991: the strongest and how many

1,578 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,578 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1991: 1,441 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 120 of 6 to 6.9, 17 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 22 April 1991, listed as "34 km S of Limón, Costa Rica".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (238), Papua New Guinea (106), Russia (106), Philippines (94), Fiji (88), Tonga (82). The busiest month was December with 175 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1991, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 172; February 125; March 131; April 137; May 126; June 142; July 115; August 135; September 96; October 136; November 88; December 175. Compared with 1990, which has 1,757 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 179 fewer.
