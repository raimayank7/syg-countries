Earthquakes of 1918: the strongest and how many

133 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 133 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1918: 39 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 82 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 3 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 15 August 1918, listed as "1918 Mindanao, Philippines Earthquake". It was one of 3 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Papua New Guinea (12), Greece (8), Philippines (8), Turkey (7), Indonesia (7), Puerto Rico (7). The busiest month was June with 16 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1918, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1918 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 8; February 9; March 5; April 7; May 9; June 16; July 14; August 16; September 14; October 11; November 14; December 10. Compared with 1917, which has 151 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 18 fewer.
