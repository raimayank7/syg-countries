Earthquakes of 1950: the strongest and how many

230 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 230 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1950: 107 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 110 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.6 on 15 August 1950, listed as "1950 Assam-Tibet Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were India (40), Vanuatu (15), China (12), Japan (12), Indonesia (11), Mid-Atlantic Ridge (7). The busiest month was August with 45 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1950, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1950 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 8; February 14; March 12; April 11; May 14; June 11; July 19; August 45; September 29; October 25; November 24; December 18. Compared with 1949, which has 146 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 84 more.
