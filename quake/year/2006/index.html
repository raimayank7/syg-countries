Earthquakes of 2006: the strongest and how many

1,879 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,879 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2006: 1,726 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 142 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 15 November 2006, listed as "2006 Kuril Islands Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (304), Tonga (134), Kuril Islands (130), Papua New Guinea (119), Russia (70), Fiji (67). The busiest month was November with 250 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 87; February 104; March 136; April 194; May 192; June 127; July 231; August 146; September 114; October 166; November 250; December 132. Compared with 2005, which has 1,844 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 35 more.
