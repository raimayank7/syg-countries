Earthquakes of 1957: the strongest and how many

346 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 346 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1957: 171 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 156 of 6 to 6.9, 17 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.6 on 9 March 1957, listed as "1957 Atka, Alaska Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Alaska (97), Russia (22), Indonesia (21), Greece (17), Turkey (14), Philippines (12). The busiest month was March with 56 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1957, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1957 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 22; February 20; March 56; April 40; May 27; June 31; July 30; August 23; September 21; October 31; November 23; December 22. Compared with 1956, which has 243 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 103 more.
