Earthquakes of 1984: the strongest and how many

1,652 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,652 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1984: 1,519 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 119 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 7 February 1984, listed as "85 km SE of Honiara, Solomon Islands".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (211), Japan (124), Papua New Guinea (111), Russia (90), Philippines (84), Fiji (81). The busiest month was September with 156 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1984, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 131; February 115; March 147; April 128; May 133; June 138; July 148; August 134; September 156; October 142; November 146; December 134. Compared with 1983, which has 1,787 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 135 fewer.
