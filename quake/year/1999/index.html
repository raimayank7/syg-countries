Earthquakes of 1999: the strongest and how many

1,192 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,192 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1999: 1,057 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 117 of 6 to 6.9, 18 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 20 September 1999, listed as "21 km S of Puli, Taiwan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (147), Papua New Guinea (105), Russia (71), Japan (70), Philippines (56), Tonga (51). The busiest month was December with 143 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 96; February 93; March 99; April 79; May 100; June 79; July 84; August 102; September 105; October 91; November 121; December 143. Compared with 1998, which has 1,077 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 115 more.
