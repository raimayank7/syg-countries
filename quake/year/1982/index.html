Earthquakes of 1982: the strongest and how many

1,488 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,488 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1982: 1,401 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 79 of 6 to 6.9, 8 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.3 on 19 June 1982, listed as "18 km SSW of La Libertad, El Salvador".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (157), Tonga (128), Japan (119), Papua New Guinea (85), Fiji (68), Philippines (63). The busiest month was December with 181 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1982, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 129; February 95; March 136; April 106; May 121; June 109; July 116; August 132; September 138; October 105; November 120; December 181. Compared with 1981, which has 1,259 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 229 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 11 January 1982 M7.1 4 km SW of Gigmoto, Philippines; 7 June 1982 M7.0 5 km NNW of San Juan Bautista Lo de Soto, Mexico; 19 June 1982 M7.3 18 km SSW of La Libertad, El Salvador; 4 July 1982 M7.1 west of the Bonin Islands, Japan; 7 July 1982 M7.0 north of Macquarie Island; 23 July 1982 M7.1 93 km ENE of Hasaki, Japan; 5 August 1982 M7.0 207 km S of Lata, Solomon Islands; 19 December 1982 M7.2 south of Tonga.
