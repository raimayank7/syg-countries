Earthquakes of 1905: the strongest and how many

75 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 75 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1905: 19 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 35 of 6 to 6.9, 20 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 23 July 1905, listed as "The 1905 Bulnay (Bolnai), Mongolia Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (5), Papua New Guinea (4), Greece (4), Alaska (4), Turkey (4), Russia (4). The busiest month was July with 10 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1905, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1905 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 6; February 5; March 4; April 9; May 7; June 7; July 10; August 4; September 6; October 6; November 5; December 6. Compared with 1904, which has 26 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 49 more.
