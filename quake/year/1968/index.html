Earthquakes of 1968: the strongest and how many

697 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 697 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1968: 538 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 139 of 6 to 6.9, 19 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 16 May 1968, listed as "1968 Tokachi-Oki Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (129), Indonesia (51), Peru (40), Russia (34), Iran (33), Philippines (30). The busiest month was May with 89 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1968, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 51; February 45; March 34; April 33; May 89; June 86; July 70; August 76; September 83; October 48; November 43; December 39. Compared with 1967, which has 565 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 132 more.
