Earthquakes of 2023: the strongest and how many

1,780 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,780 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2023: 1,633 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 128 of 6 to 6.9, 19 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 6 February 2023, listed as "Pazarcik earthquake, Kahramanmaras earthquake sequence".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (178), Philippines (167), Japan (137), Papua New Guinea (102), Tonga (96), South Sandwich Islands (76). The busiest month was December with 281 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 137; February 156; March 120; April 130; May 174; June 131; July 108; August 106; September 117; October 179; November 141; December 281. Compared with 2022, which has 1,725 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 55 more.
