Earthquakes of 1913: the strongest and how many

85 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 85 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1913: 12 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 62 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 14 March 1913, listed as "47 km ESE of Kalbay, Philippines".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (15), Philippines (11), Papua New Guinea (7), Indonesia (6), Alaska (4), Taiwan (3). The busiest month was April with 18 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1913, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1913 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 9; February 5; March 10; April 18; May 6; June 6; July 7; August 4; September 3; October 10; November 4; December 3. Compared with 1912, which has 103 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 18 fewer.
