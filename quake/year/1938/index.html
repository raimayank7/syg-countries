Earthquakes of 1938: the strongest and how many

309 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 309 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1938: 160 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 126 of 6 to 6.9, 21 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.5 on 1 February 1938, listed as "1938 Banda Sea Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (72), Indonesia (19), Papua New Guinea (13), Philippines (13), Russia (12), Alaska (11). The busiest month was November with 56 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1938, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1938 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 19; February 19; March 17; April 18; May 30; June 22; July 29; August 19; September 22; October 22; November 56; December 36. Compared with 1937, which has 234 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 75 more.
