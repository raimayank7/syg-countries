Earthquakes of 2005: the strongest and how many

1,844 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,844 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2005: 1,694 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 139 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.6 on 28 March 2005, listed as "78 km WSW of Singkil, Indonesia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (535), Japan (103), India (85), Papua New Guinea (82), Tonga (57), Fiji (49). The busiest month was January with 331 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 331; February 149; March 200; April 162; May 117; June 118; July 134; August 114; September 135; October 157; November 107; December 120. Compared with 2004, which has 1,670 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 174 more.
