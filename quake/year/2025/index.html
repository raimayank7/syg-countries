Earthquakes of 2025: the strongest and how many

2,129 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,129 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2025: 1,984 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 129 of 6 to 6.9, 15 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.8 on 29 July 2025, listed as "2025 Kamchatka Peninsula, Russia Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (508), Indonesia (144), Japan (134), Papua New Guinea (123), Philippines (91), South Sandwich Islands (73). The busiest month was July with 413 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 152; February 129; March 115; April 156; May 143; June 118; July 413; August 219; September 195; October 178; November 153; December 158. Compared with 2024, which has 1,507 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 622 more.
