Earthquakes of 1998: the strongest and how many

1,077 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,077 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1998: 956 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 109 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 25 March 1998, listed as "1998 Balleny Islands Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (125), Japan (75), Papua New Guinea (66), Philippines (49), Tonga (46), Alaska (43). The busiest month was May with 114 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 100; February 69; March 99; April 69; May 114; June 73; July 88; August 93; September 86; October 104; November 96; December 86. Compared with 1997, which has 1,235 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 158 fewer.
