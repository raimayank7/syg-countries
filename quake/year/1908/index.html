Earthquakes of 1908: the strongest and how many

38 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 38 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1908: 8 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 19 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.5 on 26 March 1908, listed as "6 km S of Copala, Mexico".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Greece (4), Afghanistan (3), Mexico (3), Russia (3), China (2), Alaska (2). The busiest month was March with 5 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1908, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1908 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 3; February 2; March 5; April 3; May 4; June 3; July 2; August 4; September 1; October 5; November 3; December 3. Compared with 1907, which has 85 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 47 fewer.
