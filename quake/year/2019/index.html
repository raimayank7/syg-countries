Earthquakes of 2019: the strongest and how many

1,645 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,645 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2019: 1,500 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 135 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 26 May 2019, listed as "78 km NE of Navarro, Peru".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (284), Tonga (112), Philippines (109), Papua New Guinea (103), Japan (72), Kermadec Islands (56). The busiest month was January with 162 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 162; February 104; March 139; April 141; May 116; June 159; July 154; August 104; September 140; October 135; November 158; December 133. Compared with 2018, which has 1,768 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 123 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 22 February 2019 M7.5 115 km ESE of Palora, Ecuador; 1 March 2019 M7.0 22 km NNE of Azángaro, Peru; 6 May 2019 M7.1 32 km NW of Bulolo, Papua New Guinea; 14 May 2019 M7.6 48 km NE of Kokopo, Papua New Guinea; 26 May 2019 M8.0 78 km NE of Navarro, Peru; 15 June 2019 M7.3 Kermadec Islands, New Zealand; 24 June 2019 M7.3 Banda Sea; 6 July 2019 M7.1 Ridgecrest Earthquake Sequence; 14 July 2019 M7.2 155 km SSE of Sofifi, Indonesia; 14 November 2019 M7.1 141 km NW of Ternate, Indonesia.
