Earthquakes of 2021: the strongest and how many

2,227 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,227 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2021: 2,070 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 138 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 3 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 29 July 2021, listed as "2021 Chignik, Alaska Earthquake". It was one of 3 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were South Sandwich Islands (313), Kermadec Islands (217), Indonesia (167), Loyalty Islands (139), New Zealand (106), Japan (104). The busiest month was March with 364 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 149; February 274; March 364; April 144; May 143; June 107; July 149; August 335; September 142; October 113; November 134; December 173. Compared with 2020, which has 1,450 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 777 more.
