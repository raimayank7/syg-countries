Earthquakes of 1946: the strongest and how many

148 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 148 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1946: 54 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 82 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.6 on 1 April 1946, listed as "The 1946 Unimak Island, Alaska Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Alaska (12), Russia (9), Indonesia (8), California (8), Dominican Republic (8), Japan (6). The busiest month was March with 18 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1946, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1946 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 12; February 8; March 18; April 17; May 12; June 12; July 12; August 14; September 10; October 8; November 15; December 10. Compared with 1945, which has 123 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 25 more.
