Earthquakes of 1949: the strongest and how many

146 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 146 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1949: 63 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 71 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 22 August 1949, listed as "The 1949 Haida Gwaii, Canada Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (14), Tajikistan (10), China (9), Papua New Guinea (9), Turkey (9), Greece (7). The busiest month was August with 21 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1949, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1949 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 8; February 15; March 14; April 7; May 11; June 8; July 20; August 21; September 12; October 11; November 10; December 9. Compared with 1948, which has 179 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 33 fewer.
