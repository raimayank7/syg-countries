Earthquakes of 1942: the strongest and how many

155 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 155 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1942: 56 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 86 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 24 August 1942, listed as "21 km NNE of Minas de Marcona, Peru". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Ecuador (14), Japan (13), Philippines (12), Greece (9), Turkey (9), Papua New Guinea (8). The busiest month was December with 20 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1942, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1942 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 7; February 7; March 8; April 7; May 16; June 17; July 12; August 19; September 15; October 10; November 17; December 20. Compared with 1941, which has 195 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 40 fewer.
