Earthquakes of 2009: the strongest and how many

2,072 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,072 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2009: 1,912 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 143 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 29 September 2009, listed as "2009 Samoa Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (280), Tonga (188), Philippines (157), Vanuatu (106), Papua New Guinea (89), Solomon Islands (71). The busiest month was October with 273 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 205; February 183; March 167; April 147; May 140; June 141; July 188; August 159; September 163; October 273; November 150; December 156. Compared with 2008, which has 1,965 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 107 more.
