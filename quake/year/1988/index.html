Earthquakes of 1988: the strongest and how many

1,574 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,574 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1988: 1,452 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 111 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 6 March 1988, listed as "Gulf of Alaska".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (169), Papua New Guinea (99), Fiji (89), Philippines (84), Tonga (81), Japan (74). The busiest month was February with 161 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1988, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 137; February 161; March 140; April 121; May 121; June 100; July 145; August 147; September 119; October 106; November 146; December 131. Compared with 1987, which has 1,546 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 28 more.
