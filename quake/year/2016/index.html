Earthquakes of 2016: the strongest and how many

1,706 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,706 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2016: 1,559 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 131 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 17 December 2016, listed as "140 km E of Kokopo, Papua New Guinea".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (139), Papua New Guinea (131), Japan (130), New Zealand (97), Solomon Islands (94), Vanuatu (75). The busiest month was December with 247 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 132; February 104; March 105; April 159; May 94; June 126; July 128; August 154; September 178; October 107; November 172; December 247. Compared with 2015, which has 1,571 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 135 more.
