Earthquakes of 1922: the strongest and how many

99 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 99 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1922: 18 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 75 of 6 to 6.9, 5 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.5 on 11 November 1922, listed as "94 km ENE of Vallenar, Chile".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (11), Taiwan (9), Greece (8), Chile (7), Philippines (6), California (5). The busiest month was April with 13 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1922, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1922 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 8; February 5; March 6; April 13; May 12; June 7; July 4; August 12; September 9; October 6; November 7; December 10. Compared with 1921, which has 81 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 18 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 17 January 1922 M7.9 143 km ESE of San Antonio del Estrecho, Peru; 31 January 1922 M7.4 123 km W of Big Lagoon, California; 12 June 1922 M7.0 111 km SSW of Altata, Mexico; 1 September 1922 M7.7 50 km SSE of Yilan, Taiwan; 11 October 1922 M7.2 35 km N of El Cardo, Peru; 11 November 1922 M8.5 94 km ENE of Vallenar, Chile.
