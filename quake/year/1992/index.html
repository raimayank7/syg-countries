Earthquakes of 1992: the strongest and how many

1,669 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,669 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1992: 1,491 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 165 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 12 December 1992, listed as "37 km WNW of Maumere, Indonesia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (256), Japan (134), Papua New Guinea (130), Tonga (81), Fiji (77), Russia (63). The busiest month was December with 176 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1992, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 105; February 109; March 134; April 112; May 147; June 149; July 150; August 135; September 157; October 161; November 134; December 176. Compared with 1991, which has 1,578 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 91 more.
