Earthquakes of 1958: the strongest and how many

352 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 352 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1958: 233 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 112 of 6 to 6.9, 6 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 6 November 1958, listed as "1958 Kuril Islands Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (43), Alaska (37), Japan (28), Vanuatu (19), Papua New Guinea (19), Greece (14). The busiest month was April with 42 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1958, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1958 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 31; February 27; March 17; April 42; May 27; June 33; July 35; August 35; September 26; October 18; November 37; December 24. Compared with 1957, which has 346 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 6 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 15 January 1958 M7.0 21 km SW of Santa Rita de Siguas, Peru; 19 January 1958 M7.6 near the coast of Ecuador; 11 March 1958 M7.2 68 km NNE of Ishigaki, Japan; 10 July 1958 M7.8 The 1958 Lituya Bay, Alaska Earthquake and Tsunami; 26 July 1958 M7.5 81 km SSE of Puerto Rosario de Laberinto, Peru; 6 November 1958 M8.3 1958 Kuril Islands Earthquake; 12 November 1958 M7.0 132 km SSE of Kuril’sk, Russia.
