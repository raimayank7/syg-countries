Earthquakes of 1947: the strongest and how many

141 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 141 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1947: 58 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 68 of 6 to 6.9, 15 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 25 March 1947, listed as "46 km E of Gisborne, New Zealand".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (13), Greece (12), Russia (9), Philippines (7), Indonesia (7), China (5). The busiest month was August with 18 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1947, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1947 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 7; February 7; March 7; April 15; May 14; June 15; July 12; August 18; September 9; October 13; November 15; December 9. Compared with 1946, which has 148 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 7 fewer.
