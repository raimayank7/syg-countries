Earthquakes of 2004: the strongest and how many

1,670 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,670 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2004: 1,513 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 141 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 9.1 on 26 December 2004, listed as "2004 Sumatra - Andaman Islands Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (285), India (135), Japan (123), Tonga (82), Kermadec Islands (59), Vanuatu (57). The busiest month was December with 346 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 143; February 112; March 120; April 120; May 122; June 88; July 106; August 94; September 121; October 123; November 175; December 346. Compared with 2003, which has 1,366 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 304 more.
