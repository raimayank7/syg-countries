Earthquakes of 1985: the strongest and how many

1,790 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,790 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1985: 1,630 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 145 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 3 March 1985, listed as "25 km WSW of Valparaíso, Chile". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (252), Japan (190), Papua New Guinea (137), Chile (122), Philippines (82), Vanuatu (69). The busiest month was March with 213 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1985, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 158; February 117; March 213; April 142; May 115; June 137; July 139; August 161; September 180; October 143; November 148; December 137. Compared with 1984, which has 1,652 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 138 more.
