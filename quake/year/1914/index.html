Earthquakes of 1914: the strongest and how many

71 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 71 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1914: 17 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 38 of 6 to 6.9, 15 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 26 May 1914, listed as "1914 Biak Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (7), Indonesia (7), Japan (6), Turkey (4), Pakistan (3), India (3). The busiest month was October with 13 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1914, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1914 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 4; February 6; March 8; April 4; May 8; June 4; July 8; August 5; September 2; October 13; November 7; December 2. Compared with 1913, which has 85 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 14 fewer.
