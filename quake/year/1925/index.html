Earthquakes of 1925: the strongest and how many

159 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 159 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1925: 53 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 96 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.3 on 10 November 1925, listed as "60 km WNW of Sorong, Indonesia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (15), Indonesia (14), Philippines (12), Russia (10), Mid-Atlantic Ridge (7), South Indian Ocean (7). The busiest month was June with 21 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1925, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1925 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 9; February 13; March 10; April 10; May 19; June 21; July 14; August 14; September 12; October 10; November 13; December 14. Compared with 1924, which has 127 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 32 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 18 January 1925 M7.2 Kuril Islands; 22 March 1925 M7.2 113 km SSE of Port-Vila, Vanuatu; 11 April 1925 M7.0 South Indian Ocean; 16 April 1925 M7.1 43 km ESE of Hengchun, Taiwan; 3 May 1925 M7.1 129 km WNW of Ternate, Indonesia; 9 June 1925 M7.0 61 km W of Angoram, Papua New Guinea; 19 August 1925 M7.0 Komandorskiye Ostrova, Russia region; 13 October 1925 M7.1 northern Mid-Atlantic Ridge; 10 November 1925 M7.3 60 km WNW of Sorong, Indonesia; 13 November 1925 M7.2 23 km NNW of Bugko, Philippines.
