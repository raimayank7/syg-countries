Earthquakes of 1944: the strongest and how many

124 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 124 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1944: 41 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 71 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 7 December 1944, listed as "1944 Toankai Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (12), Indonesia (11), Turkey (9), China (9), Greece (7), Papua New Guinea (7). The busiest month was December with 19 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1944, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1944 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 5; February 13; March 9; April 6; May 8; June 11; July 8; August 10; September 12; October 14; November 9; December 19. Compared with 1943, which has 158 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 34 fewer.
