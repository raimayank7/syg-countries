Earthquakes of 1917: the strongest and how many

151 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 151 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1917: 64 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 72 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 1 May 1917, listed as "1917 Kermadec Islands, New Zealand Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (11), Alaska (11), Philippines (9), Greece (9), Indonesia (8), China (6). The busiest month was July with 27 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1917, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1917 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 13; February 9; March 6; April 12; May 20; June 22; July 27; August 10; September 5; October 8; November 10; December 9. Compared with 1916, which has 86 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 65 more.
