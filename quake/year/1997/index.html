Earthquakes of 1997: the strongest and how many

1,235 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,235 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1997: 1,099 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 120 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 14 October 1997, listed as "192 km WSW of Haveluloto, Tonga".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (115), Russia (95), Japan (91), Vanuatu (61), Tonga (61), Papua New Guinea (58). The busiest month was December with 163 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 97; February 64; March 114; April 137; May 102; June 85; July 102; August 80; September 80; October 108; November 103; December 163. Compared with 1996, which has 1,370 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 135 fewer.
