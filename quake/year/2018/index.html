Earthquakes of 2018: the strongest and how many

1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2018: 1,634 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 117 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 19 August 2018, listed as "2018 Fiji Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (223), Papua New Guinea (191), Fiji (93), Japan (91), New Caledonia (91), Russia (68). The busiest month was August with 196 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 131; February 155; March 143; April 98; May 139; June 99; July 117; August 196; September 187; October 191; November 121; December 191. Compared with 2017, which has 1,566 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 202 more.
