Earthquakes of 1964: the strongest and how many

583 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 583 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1964: 439 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 137 of 6 to 6.9, 6 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 9.2 on 28 March 1964, listed as "The 1964 Prince William Sound, Alaska Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Alaska (144), Russia (45), Indonesia (39), Japan (35), Papua New Guinea (28), Iran (24). The busiest month was March with 86 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1964, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 29; February 25; March 86; April 79; May 52; June 51; July 60; August 40; September 39; October 47; November 45; December 30. Compared with 1963, which has 330 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 253 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 28 March 1964 M9.2 The 1964 Prince William Sound, Alaska Earthquake; 7 May 1964 M7.0 83 km WSW of Fukaura, Japan; 26 May 1964 M7.8 South Sandwich Islands region; 16 June 1964 M7.6 27 km NW of Murakami, Japan; 6 July 1964 M7.3 10 km SW of Villa Nicolás Bravo, Mexico; 9 July 1964 M7.2 54 km E of Luganville, Vanuatu; 12 September 1964 M7.0 Auckland Islands, New Zealand region.
