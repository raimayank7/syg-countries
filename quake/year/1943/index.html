Earthquakes of 1943: the strongest and how many

158 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 158 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1943: 45 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 96 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 6 April 1943, listed as "45 km NNW of Illapel, Chile".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (18), Indonesia (10), Russia (9), Greece (8), South Sandwich Islands (8), Philippines (8). The busiest month was November with 22 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1943, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1943 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 6; February 7; March 21; April 14; May 8; June 16; July 13; August 8; September 15; October 11; November 22; December 17. Compared with 1942, which has 155 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 3 more.
