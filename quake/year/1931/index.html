Earthquakes of 1931: the strongest and how many

294 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 294 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1931: 170 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 107 of 6 to 6.9, 17 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 2 November 1931, listed as "58 km ESE of Takanabe, Japan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (36), Indonesia (17), Pakistan (17), Mexico (16), China (16), Russia (15). The busiest month was August with 40 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1931, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1931 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 27; February 21; March 29; April 27; May 30; June 23; July 23; August 40; September 24; October 25; November 11; December 14. Compared with 1930, which has 281 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 13 more.
