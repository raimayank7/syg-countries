Earthquakes of 1941: the strongest and how many

195 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 195 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1941: 93 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 91 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 18 November 1941, listed as "1941 Hyuganada Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (21), India (16), Japan (14), California (11), Turkey (9), Greece (9). The busiest month was July with 24 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1941, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1941 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 13; February 11; March 17; April 14; May 14; June 19; July 24; August 16; September 22; October 15; November 18; December 12. Compared with 1940, which has 216 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 21 fewer.
