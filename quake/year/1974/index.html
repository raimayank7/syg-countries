Earthquakes of 1974: the strongest and how many

1,410 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,410 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1974: 1,313 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 86 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 3 October 1974, listed as "77 km WSW of Callao, Peru".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (158), Papua New Guinea (119), Japan (94), Vanuatu (78), Tonga (54), Fiji (53). The busiest month was August with 143 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1974, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 134; February 136; March 102; April 97; May 125; June 129; July 127; August 143; September 101; October 110; November 102; December 104. Compared with 1973, which has 1,435 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 25 fewer.
