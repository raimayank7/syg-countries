Earthquakes of 1959: the strongest and how many

351 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 351 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1959: 243 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 102 of 6 to 6.9, 6 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 4 May 1959, listed as "76 km ENE of Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (25), Indonesia (22), Japan (21), Alaska (17), Greece (15), Papua New Guinea (13). The busiest month was August with 51 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1959, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1959 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 35; February 17; March 16; April 32; May 32; June 27; July 24; August 51; September 43; October 17; November 30; December 27. Compared with 1958, which has 352 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 1 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 4 May 1959 M7.9 76 km ENE of Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia; 14 June 1959 M7.1 136 km E of Iquique, Chile; 15 August 1959 M7.2 38 km ENE of Hengchun, Taiwan; 17 August 1959 M7.0 67 km WNW of Gizo, Solomon Islands; 18 August 1959 M7.2 The 1959 Hebgen Lake, Montana Earthquake; 14 September 1959 M7.3 Kermadec Islands region.
