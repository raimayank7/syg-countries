Earthquakes of 1901: the strongest and how many

18 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 18 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1901: 1 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 2 of 6 to 6.9, 15 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 9 August 1901, listed as "Southeast of the Loyalty Islands".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (4), Near the coast of Ecuador (1), Canada (1), California (1), Kuril Islands (1), Solomon Islands (1). The busiest month was December with 4 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1901, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1901 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 2; February 0; March 1; April 1; May 1; June 2; July 1; August 3; September 0; October 1; November 2; December 4. Compared with 1900, which has 13 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 5 more.
