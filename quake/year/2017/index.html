Earthquakes of 2017: the strongest and how many

1,566 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,566 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2017: 1,456 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 103 of 6 to 6.9, 6 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 8 September 2017, listed as "2017 Tehuantepec, Mexico Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (163), Japan (80), Papua New Guinea (78), New Caledonia (72), Tonga (69), Chile (67). The busiest month was November with 166 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 152; February 111; March 136; April 136; May 151; June 100; July 111; August 122; September 146; October 123; November 166; December 112. Compared with 2016, which has 1,706 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 140 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 10 January 2017 M7.3 189 km SSE of Tabiauan, Philippines; 22 January 2017 M7.9 35 km WNW of Panguna, Papua New Guinea; 17 July 2017 M7.7 Komandorskiye Ostrova, Russia region; 8 September 2017 M8.2 2017 Tehuantepec, Mexico Earthquake; 19 September 2017 M7.1 1 km S of Matzaco, Mexico; 12 November 2017 M7.3 29 km S of (name unreadable in source), Iraq; 19 November 2017 M7.0 85 km ENE of Tadine, New Caledonia.
