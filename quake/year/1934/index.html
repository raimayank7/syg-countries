Earthquakes of 1934: the strongest and how many

253 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 253 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1934: 136 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 105 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 15 January 1934, listed as "1934 Bihar-Nepal Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (29), Indonesia (20), Papua New Guinea (17), Philippines (16), Mexico (12), China (11). The busiest month was July with 31 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1934, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1934 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 20; February 27; March 23; April 24; May 21; June 23; July 31; August 20; September 21; October 9; November 18; December 16. Compared with 1933, which has 269 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 16 fewer.
