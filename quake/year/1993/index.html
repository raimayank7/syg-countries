Earthquakes of 1993: the strongest and how many

1,565 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,565 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1993: 1,417 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 136 of 6 to 6.9, 12 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 8 August 1993, listed as "32 km S of Inarajan Village, Guam".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (229), Japan (115), Papua New Guinea (104), Tonga (82), Fiji (70), Philippines (66). The busiest month was September with 162 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1993, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 115; February 112; March 152; April 118; May 144; June 123; July 138; August 125; September 162; October 125; November 109; December 142. Compared with 1992, which has 1,669 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 104 fewer.
