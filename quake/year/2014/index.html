Earthquakes of 2014: the strongest and how many

1,749 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,749 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2014: 1,594 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 143 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 1 April 2014, listed as "93 km NW of Iquique, Chile".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (173), Papua New Guinea (169), Chile (151), Japan (94), Philippines (88), Tonga (79). The busiest month was April with 245 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 113; February 109; March 161; April 245; May 146; June 154; July 125; August 121; September 131; October 135; November 153; December 156. Compared with 2013, which has 1,610 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 139 more.
