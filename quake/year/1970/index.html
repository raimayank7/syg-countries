Earthquakes of 1970: the strongest and how many

650 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 650 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1970: 498 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 135 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 31 July 1970, listed as "95 km N of San Antonio del Estrecho, Peru".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (53), Peru (45), Philippines (43), Indonesia (38), China (33), Japan (32). The busiest month was April with 77 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1970, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 44; February 60; March 62; April 77; May 49; June 63; July 44; August 50; September 37; October 46; November 47; December 71. Compared with 1969, which has 627 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 23 more.
