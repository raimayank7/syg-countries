Earthquakes of 1923: the strongest and how many

129 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 129 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1923: 36 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 78 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.4 on 3 February 1923, listed as "1923 Kamchatka, Russia Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (23), Russia (12), Indonesia (8), Greece (7), Alaska (7), Mid-Atlantic Ridge (6). The busiest month was September with 20 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1923, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1923 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 4; February 13; March 8; April 7; May 12; June 8; July 17; August 12; September 20; October 7; November 14; December 7. Compared with 1922, which has 99 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 30 more.
