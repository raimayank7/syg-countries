Earthquakes of 1971: the strongest and how many

899 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 899 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1971: 756 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 132 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 26 July 1971, listed as "1971 Bougainville Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Papua New Guinea (86), Indonesia (69), Japan (65), Turkey (58), Russia (52), Alaska (48). The busiest month was July with 127 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1971, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 61; February 59; March 70; April 79; May 87; June 74; July 127; August 82; September 82; October 72; November 51; December 55. Compared with 1970, which has 650 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 249 more.
