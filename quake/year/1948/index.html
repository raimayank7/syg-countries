Earthquakes of 1948: the strongest and how many

179 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 179 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1948: 65 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 102 of 6 to 6.9, 12 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 24 January 1948, listed as "3 km W of Buga, Philippines".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (18), Greece (17), Russia (15), Alaska (9), Philippines (9), Indonesia (9). The busiest month was March with 21 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1948, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1948 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 15; February 12; March 21; April 9; May 19; June 15; July 16; August 18; September 16; October 15; November 11; December 12. Compared with 1947, which has 141 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 38 more.
