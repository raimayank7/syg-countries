Earthquakes of 2013: the strongest and how many

1,610 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,610 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2013: 1,468 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 123 of 6 to 6.9, 17 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 24 May 2013, listed as "2013 Sea of Okhotsk Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Solomon Islands (233), Indonesia (129), Japan (120), Papua New Guinea (97), Russia (94), Tonga (62). The busiest month was February with 288 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 106; February 288; March 109; April 138; May 135; June 93; July 117; August 112; September 118; October 136; November 144; December 114. Compared with 2012, which has 1,698 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 88 fewer.
