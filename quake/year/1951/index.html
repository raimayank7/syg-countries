Earthquakes of 1951: the strongest and how many

260 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 260 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1951: 125 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 127 of 6 to 6.9, 8 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 24 November 1951, listed as "Taiwan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Taiwan (32), Russia (18), Indonesia (16), China (16), Greece (13), Japan (12). The busiest month was October with 45 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1951, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1951 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 25; February 9; March 12; April 19; May 20; June 13; July 19; August 25; September 18; October 45; November 34; December 21. Compared with 1950, which has 230 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 30 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 11 July 1951 M7.1 Bonin Islands, Japan region; 21 October 1951 M7.5 58 km SSW of Hualien City, Taiwan; 22 October 1951 M7.2 27 km WSW of Hualien City, Taiwan; 6 November 1951 M7.0 Kuril Islands; 18 November 1951 M7.7 89 km WSW of Nagqu, China; 24 November 1951 M7.3 81 km E of Yujing, Taiwan; 24 November 1951 M7.8 Taiwan; 8 December 1951 M7.5 South Indian Ocean.
