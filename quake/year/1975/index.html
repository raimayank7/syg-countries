Earthquakes of 1975: the strongest and how many

1,552 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,552 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1975: 1,446 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 93 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 26 May 1975, listed as "Azores-Cape St. Vincent Ridge".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (173), Papua New Guinea (155), Russia (142), Philippines (124), Japan (105), Tonga (70). The busiest month was June with 197 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1975, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 105; February 103; March 111; April 157; May 114; June 197; July 138; August 119; September 117; October 123; November 141; December 127. Compared with 1974, which has 1,410 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 142 more.
