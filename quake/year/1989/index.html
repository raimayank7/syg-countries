Earthquakes of 1989: the strongest and how many

1,545 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,545 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1989: 1,408 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 129 of 6 to 6.9, 7 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 23 May 1989, listed as "1989 Macquarie Ridge Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (217), Japan (142), Philippines (111), Papua New Guinea (86), Fiji (81), Tonga (67). The busiest month was May with 168 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1989, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 128; February 144; March 114; April 127; May 168; June 117; July 79; August 128; September 104; October 138; November 145; December 153. Compared with 1988, which has 1,574 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 29 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 10 February 1989 M7.1 152 km WNW of Tobelo, Indonesia; 5 May 1989 M7.1 69 km W of Tarauacá, Brazil; 14 May 1989 M7.0 Kermadec Islands, New Zealand; 23 May 1989 M8.0 1989 Macquarie Ridge Earthquake; 4 September 1989 M7.0 164 km SE of Chignik, Alaska; 1 November 1989 M7.4 73 km ENE of Miyako, Japan; 7 December 1989 M7.1 59 km ESE of Kainantu, Papua New Guinea; 15 December 1989 M7.6 37 km ENE of Barcelona, Philippines.
