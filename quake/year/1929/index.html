Earthquakes of 1929: the strongest and how many

305 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 305 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1929: 180 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 111 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 27 June 1929, listed as "1929 South Sandwich Islands Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Philippines (27), Japan (25), Alaska (25), Indonesia (23), Russia (11), Taiwan (10). The busiest month was July with 42 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1929, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1929 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 25; February 20; March 30; April 24; May 31; June 39; July 42; August 27; September 18; October 18; November 13; December 18. Compared with 1928, which has 300 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 5 more.
