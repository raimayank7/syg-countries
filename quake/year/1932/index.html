Earthquakes of 1932: the strongest and how many

217 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 217 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1932: 126 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 85 of 6 to 6.9, 5 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 3 June 1932, listed as "1932 Jalisco, Mexico Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (19), Greece (18), Mexico (16), Indonesia (14), Philippines (12), Alaska (10). The busiest month was June with 35 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1932, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1932 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 16; February 13; March 26; April 13; May 22; June 35; July 15; August 15; September 17; October 13; November 10; December 22. Compared with 1931, which has 294 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 77 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 14 May 1932 M7.7 148 km SE of Tondano, Indonesia; 21 May 1932 M7.1 12 km S of Conchagua, El Salvador; 3 June 1932 M8.1 1932 Jalisco, Mexico Earthquake; 18 June 1932 M7.8 7 km E of Los Tepames, Mexico; 22 June 1932 M7.7 12 km NNE of La Central, Mexico; 25 December 1932 M7.8 108 km WSW of Laojunmiao, China.
