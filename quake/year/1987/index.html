Earthquakes of 1987: the strongest and how many

1,546 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,546 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1987: 1,380 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 153 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 30 November 1987, listed as "200 km WSW of Yakutat, Alaska".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (178), Papua New Guinea (132), Japan (97), Alaska (79), Philippines (78), Tonga (77). The busiest month was March with 156 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1987, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 132; February 151; March 156; April 120; May 118; June 134; July 136; August 115; September 114; October 146; November 117; December 107. Compared with 1986, which has 1,756 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 210 fewer.
