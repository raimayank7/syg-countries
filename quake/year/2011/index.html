Earthquakes of 2011: the strongest and how many

2,701 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,701 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2011: 2,494 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 187 of 6 to 6.9, 19 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 9.1 on 11 March 2011, listed as "2011 Great Tohoku Earthquake, Japan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (873), Indonesia (190), Vanuatu (145), Tonga (115), Papua New Guinea (112), Kermadec Islands (109). The busiest month was March with 701 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 201; February 157; March 701; April 190; May 212; June 193; July 250; August 182; September 156; October 151; November 163; December 145. Compared with 2010, which has 2,397 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 304 more.
