Earthquakes of 1983: the strongest and how many

1,787 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,787 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1983: 1,651 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 122 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 18 March 1983, listed as "157 km ESE of Kokopo, Papua New Guinea".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (279), Japan (156), Papua New Guinea (114), Tonga (88), Russia (83), Philippines (81). The busiest month was July with 192 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1983, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 168; February 125; March 130; April 145; May 150; June 128; July 192; August 141; September 143; October 131; November 155; December 179. Compared with 1982, which has 1,488 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 299 more.
