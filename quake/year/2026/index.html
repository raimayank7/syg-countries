Earthquakes of 2026: the strongest and how many

1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2026 (so far: the catalog is read to 12 November 2026): 1,641 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 114 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 7 June 2026, listed as "25 km SW of Kablalan, Philippines".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (232), Philippines (140), Japan (112), Vanuatu (107), Russia (96), Tonga (83). The busiest month was July with 228 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 179; February 113; March 159; April 169; May 118; June 180; July 228; August 190; September 164; October 223; November 45; December 0. Compared with 2025, which has 2,129 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 361 fewer.
