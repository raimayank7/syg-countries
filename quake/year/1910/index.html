Earthquakes of 1910: the strongest and how many

74 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 74 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1910: 17 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 40 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.1 on 12 April 1910, listed as "1910 Taiwan Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Vanuatu (9), California (7), Nevada (5), Japan (4), Taiwan (3), Philippines (3). The busiest month was November with 13 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1910, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1910 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 5; February 4; March 4; April 4; May 9; June 12; July 3; August 4; September 8; October 1; November 13; December 7. Compared with 1909, which has 61 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 13 more.
