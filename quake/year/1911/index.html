Earthquakes of 1911: the strongest and how many

50 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 50 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1911: 8 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 28 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 3 January 1911, listed as "The 1911 Kemin Earthquake, Kyrgyzstan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Italy (4), Japan (4), Alaska (4), Afghanistan (3), Russia (3), Mexico (3). The busiest month was April with 8 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1911, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1911 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 3; February 4; March 3; April 8; May 1; June 2; July 7; August 3; September 8; October 5; November 4; December 2. Compared with 1910, which has 74 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 24 fewer.
