Earthquakes of 2007: the strongest and how many

2,286 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,286 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2007: 2,090 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 178 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 4 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.4 on 12 September 2007, listed as "122 km SW of Bengkulu, Indonesia". It was one of 4 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (394), Papua New Guinea (167), Solomon Islands (101), Chile (98), Japan (88), Tonga (87). The busiest month was September with 270 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 206; February 158; March 166; April 264; May 138; June 127; July 166; August 215; September 270; October 179; November 209; December 188. Compared with 2006, which has 1,879 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 407 more.
