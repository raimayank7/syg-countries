Earthquakes of 1980: the strongest and how many

1,403 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,403 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1980: 1,308 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 89 of 6 to 6.9, 6 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 17 July 1980, listed as "199 km S of Lata, Solomon Islands".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (154), Philippines (107), Japan (91), Tonga (82), Fiji (80), Papua New Guinea (73). The busiest month was June with 147 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1980, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 130; February 127; March 120; April 107; May 99; June 147; July 135; August 100; September 91; October 123; November 114; December 110. Compared with 1979, which has 1,451 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 48 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 23 February 1980 M7.0 30 km S of Shikotan, Russia; 8 July 1980 M7.5 196 km SSE of Lata, Solomon Islands; 17 July 1980 M7.9 199 km S of Lata, Solomon Islands; 10 October 1980 M7.3 3 km NNE of Chlef, Algeria; 25 October 1980 M7.2 207 km E of Tadine, New Caledonia; 8 November 1980 M7.2 The 1980 Eureka, California Earthquake.
