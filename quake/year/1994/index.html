Earthquakes of 1994: the strongest and how many

1,689 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,689 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1994: 1,531 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 145 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 4 October 1994, listed as "48 km E of Shikotan, Russia". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (315), Russia (232), Japan (114), Vanuatu (108), Tonga (75), Papua New Guinea (69). The busiest month was October with 255 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1994, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 111; February 107; March 86; April 114; May 146; June 182; July 133; August 151; September 126; October 255; November 133; December 145. Compared with 1993, which has 1,565 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 124 more.
