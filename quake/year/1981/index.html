Earthquakes of 1981: the strongest and how many

1,259 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,259 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1981: 1,176 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 73 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 1 September 1981, listed as "133 km NE of Hihifo, Tonga".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (119), Japan (99), Papua New Guinea (90), Tonga (78), Philippines (71), Russia (63). The busiest month was January with 137 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1981, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 137; February 111; March 120; April 88; May 102; June 95; July 109; August 79; September 108; October 99; November 106; December 105. Compared with 1980, which has 1,403 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 144 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 30 January 1981 M7.0 243 km ESE of Attu Station, Alaska; 25 May 1981 M7.6 off the west coast of the South Island of New Zealand; 6 July 1981 M7.0 southeast of the Loyalty Islands; 15 July 1981 M7.0 92 km NW of Port-Vila, Vanuatu; 28 July 1981 M7.1 75 km ESE of Kerman, Iran; 1 September 1981 M7.7 133 km NE of Hihifo, Tonga; 16 October 1981 M7.2 135 km W of Valparaíso, Chile; 25 October 1981 M7.3 7 km NNE of Petacalco, Mexico; 19 December 1981 M7.2 61 km W of Eresós, Greece; 26 December 1981 M7.1 Kermadec Islands, New Zealand.
