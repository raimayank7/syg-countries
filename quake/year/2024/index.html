Earthquakes of 2024: the strongest and how many

1,507 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,507 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2024: 1,408 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 89 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.5 on 1 January 2024, listed as "2024 Noto Peninsula, Japan Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (133), Japan (99), Tonga (93), Papua New Guinea (85), Philippines (76), Taiwan (67). The busiest month was April with 181 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 133; February 104; March 122; April 181; May 129; June 109; July 114; August 105; September 125; October 121; November 112; December 152. Compared with 2023, which has 1,780 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 273 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 1 January 2024 M7.5 2024 Noto Peninsula, Japan Earthquake; 22 January 2024 M7.0 128 km WNW of Aykol, China; 2 April 2024 M7.4 15 km S of Hualien City, Taiwan; 28 June 2024 M7.2 10 km WSW of Atiquipa, Peru; 11 July 2024 M7.1 106 km WSW of Sangay, Philippines; 19 July 2024 M7.4 41 km ESE of San Pedro de Atacama, Chile; 8 August 2024 M7.1 2024 Hyuganada Sea, Japan Earthquake; 17 August 2024 M7.0 102 km E of Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia; 5 December 2024 M7.0 2024 Offshore Cape Mendocino, California Earthquake; 17 December 2024 M7.3 24 km WNW of Port-Vila, Vanuatu.
