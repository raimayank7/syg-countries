Earthquakes of 2008: the strongest and how many

1,965 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,965 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2008: 1,786 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 167 of 6 to 6.9, 12 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 12 May 2008, listed as "58 km W of Tianpeng, China".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (224), China (114), Vanuatu (113), Japan (98), Philippines (85), Papua New Guinea (80). The busiest month was May with 188 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 127; February 169; March 164; April 185; May 188; June 182; July 141; August 174; September 151; October 156; November 149; December 179. Compared with 2007, which has 2,286 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 321 fewer.
