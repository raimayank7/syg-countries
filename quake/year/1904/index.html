Earthquakes of 1904: the strongest and how many

26 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 26 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1904: 5 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 8 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 25 June 1904, listed as "115 km ESE of Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Russia (6), Panama (2), Chile (2), Bulgaria (2), Hawaii (2), Japan (2). The busiest month was June with 7 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1904, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1904 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 1; February 0; March 3; April 3; May 1; June 7; July 1; August 5; September 0; October 2; November 0; December 3. Compared with 1903, which has 14 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 12 more.
