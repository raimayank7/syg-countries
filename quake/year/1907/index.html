Earthquakes of 1907: the strongest and how many

85 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 85 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1907: 20 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 47 of 6 to 6.9, 17 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 4 January 1907, listed as "The 1907 Sumatra Earthquake and Tsunami".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Philippines (9), Indonesia (8), Japan (6), Papua New Guinea (5), Russia (4), Tajikistan (4). The busiest month was August with 13 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1907, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1907 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 7; February 2; March 4; April 6; May 10; June 11; July 6; August 13; September 6; October 8; November 5; December 7. Compared with 1906, which has 94 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 9 fewer.
