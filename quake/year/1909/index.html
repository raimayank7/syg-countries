Earthquakes of 1909: the strongest and how many

61 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 61 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1909: 17 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 28 of 6 to 6.9, 16 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 7 July 1909, listed as "52 km ESE of Farkhār, Afghanistan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (10), Italy (3), Turkey (3), California (3), Indonesia (3), Mexico (3). The busiest month was April with 8 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1909, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1909 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 4; February 4; March 7; April 8; May 4; June 7; July 6; August 6; September 4; October 7; November 2; December 2. Compared with 1908, which has 38 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 23 more.
