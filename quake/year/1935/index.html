Earthquakes of 1935: the strongest and how many

248 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 248 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1935: 137 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 98 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 20 September 1935, listed as "New Guinea, Papua New Guinea".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (27), Philippines (19), Indonesia (17), Papua New Guinea (15), China (12), Iran (9). The busiest month was October with 32 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1935, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1935 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 19; February 12; March 11; April 23; May 20; June 17; July 21; August 22; September 27; October 32; November 21; December 23. Compared with 1934, which has 253 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 5 fewer.
