Earthquakes of 1977: the strongest and how many

1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,768 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1977: 1,683 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 74 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.3 on 19 August 1977, listed as "1977 Sumba, Indonesia Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (335), Philippines (120), Tonga (102), Papua New Guinea (96), Solomon Islands (91), Fiji (87). The busiest month was August with 190 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1977, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 139; February 103; March 142; April 165; May 129; June 119; July 121; August 190; September 147; October 187; November 165; December 161. Compared with 1976, which has 1,762 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 6 more.
