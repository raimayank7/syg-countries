Earthquakes of 2002: the strongest and how many

1,309 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,309 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2002: 1,170 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 126 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.9 on 3 November 2002, listed as "2002 Denali Fault, Alaska Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (178), Papua New Guinea (107), Japan (74), Fiji (63), Vanuatu (63), Tonga (58). The busiest month was October with 140 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 123; February 89; March 106; April 93; May 92; June 113; July 79; August 107; September 131; October 140; November 134; December 102. Compared with 2001, which has 1,349 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 40 fewer.
