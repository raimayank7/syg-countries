Earthquakes of 2020: the strongest and how many

1,450 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,450 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2020: 1,329 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 112 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 22 July 2020, listed as "2020 Perryville, Alaska Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (126), Japan (91), Papua New Guinea (86), Alaska (79), Tonga (69), Philippines (63). The busiest month was January with 160 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 160; February 109; March 121; April 106; May 119; June 130; July 105; August 137; September 119; October 142; November 89; December 113. Compared with 2019, which has 1,645 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 195 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 28 January 2020 M7.7 123 km NNW of Lucea, Jamaica; 13 February 2020 M7.0 95 km ENE of Kuril’sk, Russia; 25 March 2020 M7.5 221 km SSE of Severo-Kuril’sk, Russia; 18 June 2020 M7.4 south of the Kermadec Islands; 23 June 2020 M7.4 9 km SE of Santa María Xadani, Mexico; 17 July 2020 M7.0 114 km NNW of Popondetta, Papua New Guinea; 22 July 2020 M7.8 2020 Perryville, Alaska Earthquake; 19 October 2020 M7.6 2020 Sand Point, Alaska Earthquake; 30 October 2020 M7.0 13 km NNE of Néon Karlovásion, Greece.
