Earthquakes of 1915: the strongest and how many

90 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 90 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1915: 19 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 61 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 1 May 1915, listed as "266 km SSW of Severo-Kuril’sk, Russia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (14), Russia (10), Greece (10), Indonesia (6), California (5), Philippines (5). The busiest month was May with 14 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1915, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1915 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 7; February 2; March 4; April 9; May 14; June 11; July 6; August 12; September 5; October 6; November 6; December 8. Compared with 1914, which has 71 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 19 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 5 January 1915 M7.3 44 km SSW of Sola, Vanuatu; 5 January 1915 M7.3 92 km NNW of Yonakuni, Japan; 25 February 1915 M7.1 Fiji region; 28 February 1915 M7.2 98 km SSW of Ishigaki, Japan; 1 May 1915 M7.8 266 km SSW of Severo-Kuril’sk, Russia; 6 June 1915 M7.3 136 km WSW of Challapata, Bolivia; 31 July 1915 M7.3 233 km SE of Atlasovo, Russia; 7 September 1915 M7.7 12 km NW of Chalatenango, El Salvador; 8 October 1915 M7.0 139 km S of Ōyama, Japan; 1 November 1915 M7.6 119 km SE of Kamaishi, Japan.
