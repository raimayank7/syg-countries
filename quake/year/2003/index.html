Earthquakes of 2003: the strongest and how many

1,366 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,366 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2003: 1,212 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 139 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.2 on 25 September 2003, listed as "2003 Tokachi-Oki Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (171), Japan (125), Papua New Guinea (85), Russia (69), Philippines (63), Tonga (60). The busiest month was September with 148 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 106; February 86; March 102; April 84; May 108; June 87; July 126; August 130; September 148; October 125; November 131; December 133. Compared with 2002, which has 1,309 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 57 more.
