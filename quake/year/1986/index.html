Earthquakes of 1986: the strongest and how many

1,756 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,756 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1986: 1,618 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 127 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 7 May 1986, listed as "1986 Atka, Alaska Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (230), Alaska (155), Japan (145), Kermadec Islands (122), Fiji (93), Tonga (80). The busiest month was May with 220 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1986, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 128; February 90; March 161; April 117; May 220; June 158; July 147; August 161; September 112; October 192; November 137; December 133. Compared with 1985, which has 1,790 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 34 fewer.
