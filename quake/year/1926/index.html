Earthquakes of 1926: the strongest and how many

273 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 273 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1926: 151 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 113 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 26 October 1926, listed as "158 km WSW of Abepura, Indonesia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (31), Indonesia (24), Papua New Guinea (13), Chile (12), Turkey (11), Solomon Islands (11). The busiest month was October with 41 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1926, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1926 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 12; February 14; March 27; April 15; May 21; June 24; July 21; August 39; September 27; October 41; November 16; December 16. Compared with 1925, which has 159 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 114 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 25 January 1926 M7.5 62 km SW of Malango, Solomon Islands; 8 February 1926 M7.1 113 km SSW of Puerto El Triunfo, El Salvador; 27 March 1926 M7.1 110 km SE of Gizo, Solomon Islands; 12 April 1926 M7.3 34 km WNW of Kirakira, Solomon Islands; 25 August 1926 M7.0 southeast of the Loyalty Islands; 10 September 1926 M7.1 138 km SSE of Pundong, Indonesia; 16 September 1926 M7.1 33 km WNW of Malango, Solomon Islands; 3 October 1926 M7.5 north of Macquarie Island; 26 October 1926 M7.6 158 km WSW of Abepura, Indonesia.
