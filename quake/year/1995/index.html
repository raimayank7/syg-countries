Earthquakes of 1995: the strongest and how many

1,495 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,495 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1995: 1,295 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 180 of 6 to 6.9, 18 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 30 July 1995, listed as "36 km NNE of Antofagasta, Chile". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (172), Russia (164), Japan (114), Philippines (92), Papua New Guinea (89), Tonga (66). The busiest month was December with 176 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1995, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 153; February 136; March 92; April 137; May 170; June 100; July 108; August 123; September 90; October 119; November 91; December 176. Compared with 1994, which has 1,689 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 194 fewer.
