Earthquakes of 1921: the strongest and how many

81 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 81 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1921: 20 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 53 of 6 to 6.9, 8 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 15 November 1921, listed as "71 km SSW of Jurm, Afghanistan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Japan (11), Indonesia (9), Russia (7), Greece (5), China (4), Mexico (3). The busiest month was September with 11 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1921, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded. Magnitudes of 1921 are estimates made from old instrument records and later re-evaluated; treat them as approximate.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 6; February 7; March 10; April 10; May 10; June 5; July 2; August 6; September 11; October 6; November 5; December 3. Compared with 1920, which has 99 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 18 fewer. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 4 February 1921 M7.1 32 km NE of Chajul, Guatemala; 27 February 1921 M7.4 103 km ESE of Neiafu, Tonga; 28 March 1921 M7.2 7 km SW of El Viejo, Nicaragua; 11 September 1921 M7.6 233 km S of Pundong, Indonesia; 15 October 1921 M7.0 143 km NNW of Sola, Vanuatu; 11 November 1921 M7.4 62 km E of Baculin, Philippines; 15 November 1921 M7.8 71 km SSW of Jurm, Afghanistan; 18 December 1921 M7.3 130 km W of Puerto Nariño, Colombia.
