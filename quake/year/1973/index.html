Earthquakes of 1973: the strongest and how many

1,435 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,435 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1973: 1,342 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 84 of 6 to 6.9, 9 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.7 on 17 June 1973, listed as "19 km ESE of Nemuro, Japan".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (148), Japan (113), Philippines (93), Russia (93), Papua New Guinea (89), Vanuatu (70). The busiest month was July with 166 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1973, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 109; February 102; March 117; April 97; May 84; June 164; July 166; August 115; September 102; October 108; November 135; December 136. Compared with 1972, which has 874 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 561 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 30 January 1973 M7.5 35 km SW of Aguililla, Mexico; 6 February 1973 M7.4 203 km NW of Kangding, China; 28 February 1973 M7.2 38 km ESE of Severo-Kuril’sk, Russia; 17 March 1973 M7.0 13 km ENE of San Andres, Philippines; 17 June 1973 M7.7 19 km ESE of Nemuro, Japan; 24 June 1973 M7.1 58 km SSW of Shikotan, Russia; 6 October 1973 M7.0 east of the South Sandwich Islands; 28 December 1973 M7.5 81 km NW of Port-Olry, Vanuatu; 29 December 1973 M7.2 20 km WSW of Port-Olry, Vanuatu.
