Earthquakes of 2000: the strongest and how many

1,494 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,494 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2000: 1,334 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 145 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 16 November 2000, listed as "2000 New Ireland Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Papua New Guinea (255), Indonesia (211), Japan (162), Tonga (61), Philippines (57), Alaska (53). The busiest month was November with 235 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 106; February 95; March 94; April 87; May 91; June 159; July 150; August 138; September 90; October 123; November 235; December 126. Compared with 1999, which has 1,192 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 302 more.
