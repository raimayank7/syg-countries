Earthquakes of 1965: the strongest and how many

628 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 628 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1965: 497 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 116 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.7 on 4 February 1965, listed as "1965 Western Aleutian Islands (Hawadax/Rat Islands) Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Alaska (132), Indonesia (48), Japan (39), Russia (38), Papua New Guinea (29), Vanuatu (24). The busiest month was February with 92 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1965, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 33; February 92; March 68; April 54; May 48; June 63; July 38; August 58; September 49; October 36; November 51; December 38. Compared with 1964, which has 583 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 45 more.
