Earthquakes of 2010: the strongest and how many

2,397 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 2,397 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2010: 2,223 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 150 of 6 to 6.9, 23 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.8 on 27 February 2010, listed as "2010 Maule, Chile Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Chile (404), Indonesia (204), Vanuatu (148), Japan (140), Papua New Guinea (131), Tonga (115). The busiest month was February with 303 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 204; February 303; March 287; April 150; May 141; June 139; July 190; August 191; September 173; October 169; November 172; December 278. Compared with 2009, which has 2,072 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 325 more.
