Earthquakes of 2012: the strongest and how many

1,698 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,698 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2012: 1,565 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 117 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 2 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.6 on 11 April 2012, listed as "2012 Wharton Basin Earthquake". It was one of 2 earthquakes of magnitude 8 or more that year.

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (194), Japan (153), Philippines (101), Tonga (92), Papua New Guinea (88), Vanuatu (72). The busiest month was February with 194 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 173; February 194; March 129; April 185; May 107; June 111; July 118; August 137; September 145; October 140; November 137; December 122. Compared with 2011, which has 2,701 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 1,003 fewer.
