Earthquakes of 1967: the strongest and how many

565 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 565 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1967: 452 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 103 of 6 to 6.9, 10 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.3 on 21 December 1967, listed as "34 km NE of Tocopilla, Chile".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (50), Alaska (36), Papua New Guinea (33), Japan (31), Russia (27), Chile (24). The busiest month was January with 72 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1967, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 72; February 39; March 41; April 52; May 43; June 44; July 44; August 47; September 47; October 45; November 41; December 50. Compared with 1966, which has 554 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 11 more. The earthquakes of magnitude 7 or more, in date order: 5 January 1967 M7.0 77 km SSW of Bulgan, Mongolia; 9 February 1967 M7.0 41 km SE of Baraya, Colombia; 13 February 1967 M7.0 Reykjanes Ridge; 15 February 1967 M7.0 124 km SSW of Tarauacá, Brazil; 22 July 1967 M7.3 9 km E of Akyazı, Turkey; 3 September 1967 M7.0 179 km WSW of Huarmey, Peru; 9 October 1967 M7.2 Fiji region; 15 October 1967 M7.1 9 km E of Granada, Nicaragua; 21 December 1967 M7.3 34 km NE of Tocopilla, Chile; 25 December 1967 M7.3 192 km ESE of Kokopo, Papua New Guinea.
