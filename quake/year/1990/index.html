Earthquakes of 1990: the strongest and how many

1,757 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,757 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1990: 1,604 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 135 of 6 to 6.9, 18 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.8 on 18 April 1990, listed as "75 km NNW of Gorontalo, Indonesia".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (251), Philippines (140), Papua New Guinea (101), Fiji (90), Tonga (87), Japan (86). The busiest month was April with 187 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1990, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 132; February 127; March 145; April 187; May 131; June 138; July 175; August 141; September 134; October 129; November 137; December 181. Compared with 1989, which has 1,545 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 212 more.
