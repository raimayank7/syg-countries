Earthquakes of 2001: the strongest and how many

1,349 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,349 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2001: 1,212 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 122 of 6 to 6.9, 14 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.4 on 23 June 2001, listed as "6 km SSW of Atico, Peru".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (192), Peru (100), Papua New Guinea (95), Japan (87), Russia (52), Tonga (52). The busiest month was June with 154 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 137; February 120; March 96; April 103; May 93; June 154; July 113; August 124; September 86; October 122; November 112; December 89. Compared with 2000, which has 1,494 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 145 fewer.
