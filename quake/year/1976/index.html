Earthquakes of 1976: the strongest and how many

1,762 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,762 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 1976: 1,653 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 95 of 6 to 6.9, 13 of 7 to 7.9 and 1 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 8.0 on 14 January 1976, listed as "1976 Kermadec Islands, New Zealand Earthquake".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (172), Philippines (148), Russia (129), Papua New Guinea (126), Japan (97), Kermadec Islands (88). The busiest month was January with 248 earthquakes. For comparison, the catalog counts about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year since 1996. Fewer were recorded in 1976, but not because the Earth was quieter: instruments were scarce, so smaller earthquakes went unrecorded.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 248; February 135; March 120; April 101; May 135; June 141; July 160; August 157; September 167; October 129; November 130; December 139. Compared with 1975, which has 1,552 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 210 more.
