Earthquakes of 2022: the strongest and how many

1,725 earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above in the USGS catalog.

The USGS catalog lists 1,725 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more in 2022: 1,598 of magnitude 5 to 5.9, 116 of 6 to 6.9, 11 of 7 to 7.9 and 0 of 8 or more. The strongest was magnitude 7.6 on 10 September 2022, listed as "70 km E of Kainantu, Papua New Guinea".

The most affected areas by number of listed earthquakes were Indonesia (164), South Sandwich Islands (128), Japan (128), Philippines (90), Papua New Guinea (83), Kermadec Islands (79). The busiest month was March with 194 earthquakes. For comparison, in the last 30 years the catalog averages about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more a year and about 148 of magnitude 6 or more.

Month by month the catalog lists: January 165; February 140; March 194; April 136; May 144; June 113; July 134; August 122; September 157; October 119; November 165; December 136. Compared with 2021, which has 2,227 listed earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, that is 502 fewer.
