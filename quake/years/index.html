Earthquakes by year since 1900

How many earthquakes of magnitude 5 and above the USGS catalog lists for each year.

Between 1900 and 12 November 2026 the catalog holds 107,113 earthquakes of magnitude 5.0 or more, 14,485 of magnitude 6 or more, 1,620 of magnitude 7 or more and 100 of magnitude 8 or more. Over the last 30 full years, an average year has about 1,711 earthquakes of magnitude 5 or more, 148 of magnitude 6 or more, 15 of magnitude 7 or more and 1 of magnitude 8 or more.

The counts of the first half of the century look lower, but that is the instruments, not the planet: far fewer earthquakes were recorded then, and magnitudes were estimated differently. Great earthquakes, those of magnitude 8 or more, are rare and were noticed everywhere, so their counts are the most comparable across the century.
