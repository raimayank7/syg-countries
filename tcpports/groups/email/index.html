Email ports: 7 well-known port numbers

These are the best-known ports for the group "Email". For each, the table gives the name the IANA registry uses and a plain description of what the service does. Open a port for its registrant, how to check it and what to watch. Ports that carry passwords or private data in plain text have an encrypted twin in most cases, mentioned in the notes. The notes are general good practice, not an assessment of any system. In general: run only the services you need, keep them up to date, keep databases, file sharing and remote administration off the public internet, and prefer the encrypted version of a service when there is one.

PortNameUsed for
25smtpThe port on which mail servers pass email to each other with SMTP. Mail programs do not normally use it to send; they use 587 or 465.
110pop3POP3, the Post Office Protocol: a mail program downloads messages from the server, usually removing them from the server afterwards.
143imapIMAP, the Internet Message Access Protocol: a mail program reads and manages messages that stay on the server, so several devices see the same mailbox.
465urd, submissions, igmpv3liteMessage submission over TLS: a mail program connects here, encrypted from the start, to hand over outgoing mail with a login. IANA lists it as "submissions".
587submissionMessage submission: mail programs send outgoing email to their server here, normally after logging in and switching to encrypted mode with STARTTLS.
993imapsIMAP over TLS: the encrypted version of IMAP, where the connection is encrypted from the start.
995pop3sPOP3 over TLS: the encrypted version of POP3.

Data: IANA Service Name and Transport Protocol Port Number Registry, read November 2026. Notes on well-known ports are our own. Independent site, not connected with IANA.
