Ports 5000 to 5999: registered (user) ports

433 numbers of this range have an entry in the IANA registry.

This range holds the registered (user) ports. Of the 1,000 port numbers from 5000 to 5999, 433 have at least one entry in the registry; the lowest assigned is 5000 and the highest is 5999. The registrants with the most entries here are [IESG] (9); [International_Civil_Aviation_Organisation] (8); [Eric_Soderberg_2]; [Edward_Yim] (6). Registration dates in the data run from 2002 to 2026.

Ports 1024 to 49151 are the "registered" or user ports. Anyone can ask IANA to register a port for a service; many applications and vendors have done so. Ports 49152 to 65535 are not registered at all: they are for temporary and private use. Search the table with the box below. A number with no row is unassigned or reserved: programs may still use it by agreement, but the registry does not name a service for it.
