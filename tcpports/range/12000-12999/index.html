Ports 12000 to 12999: registered (user) ports

25 numbers of this range have an entry in the IANA registry.

This range holds the registered (user) ports. Of the 1,000 port numbers from 12000 to 12999, 25 have at least one entry in the registry; the lowest assigned is 12000 and the highest is 12865. The registrants with the most entries here are [Eugene_Cox] (5); [Tim_Young] (3); [Alexander_V_Ivanov] (2). Registration dates in the data run from 2002 to 2022.

Ports 1024 to 49151 are the "registered" or user ports. Anyone can ask IANA to register a port for a service; many applications and vendors have done so. Ports 49152 to 65535 are not registered at all: they are for temporary and private use. Search the table with the box below. A number with no row is unassigned or reserved: programs may still use it by agreement, but the registry does not name a service for it.
