Ports 47000 to 47999: registered (user) ports

8 numbers of this range have an entry in the IANA registry.

This range holds the registered (user) ports. Of the 1,000 port numbers from 47000 to 47999, 8 have at least one entry in the registry; the lowest assigned is 47000 and the highest is 47809. The registrants with the most entries here are [Dirk_Kutscher] (1); [Ryan_Mack] (1); [JVL_Industri_Elektronik] (1). Registration dates in the data run from 2009 to 2013.

Ports 1024 to 49151 are the "registered" or user ports. Anyone can ask IANA to register a port for a service; many applications and vendors have done so. Ports 49152 to 65535 are not registered at all: they are for temporary and private use. Search the table with the box below. A number with no row is unassigned or reserved: programs may still use it by agreement, but the registry does not name a service for it.
