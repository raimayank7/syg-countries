Ports 0 to 1023: well-known (system) ports

697 numbers of this range have an entry in the IANA registry.

This range holds the well-known (system) ports. Of the 1,024 port numbers from 0 to 1023, 697 have at least one entry in the registry; the lowest assigned is 1 and the highest is 1022. The registrants with the most entries here are [IESG] (29); [Jon_Postel] (21); [Randall_Stewart] (7). Registration dates in the data run from 2007 to 2025.

Ports 0 to 1023 are the "system" or well-known ports: historically only privileged programs could listen on them, and they carry the main internet services such as web, mail and remote login. Search the table with the box below. A number with no row is unassigned or reserved: programs may still use it by agreement, but the registry does not name a service for it.
