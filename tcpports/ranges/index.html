All port number ranges

Port numbers run from 0 to 65535. The registry divides them into three ranges (RFC 6335): 0 to 1023 well-known, 1024 to 49151 registered, and 49152 to 65535 dynamic or private. The table lists the ranges in which the registry has entries and how many port numbers have one. Of all 65,536 numbers, 6,125 have an entry.

How to use this page

Read the table from top to bottom: entries are dense in the first ten thousand numbers and thin out quickly after that, because the early numbers were handed out first and in blocks, while most later applications ask for only one or two ports. A number with no entry is not necessarily unused: it is only unregistered, and a program may still listen on it, which is why the checks in the port guides look at what is actually running. Open a range to see the ports that have entries, and use the home page to look up a single number.
