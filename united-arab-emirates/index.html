United Arab Emirates

The United Arab Emirates is a country in Western Asia. In the language of the place it is called الإمارات. The capital is Abu Dhabi.

Land and position

The United Arab Emirates covers about 82,880 square kilometres. By area it ranks 118th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Austria and a little larger than Czechia. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Oman, Saudi Arabia. Its centre lies at about 24° north, 54° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 11.5 million people live in the United Arab Emirates (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 86th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.14 percent of the world's people. On average there are 139 people to each square kilometre. About 86 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 83 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, Persian, English, Hindi, Urdu. A person from the United Arab Emirates is called Emirati.

Money and economy

The currency is the United Arab Emirates dirham (code AED, symbol د.إ). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 552 billion (2024), which is about US$ 50,274 for each person (2024).

Time

The United Arab Emirates uses one time zone, UTC+4:00 (Asia/Dubai). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
