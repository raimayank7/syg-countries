Electricity prices in Italy

0.3142 euro per kWh, household, 2026 (first half)

For a household using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh a year, the average electricity price in Italy was 0.3142 euro per kWh including taxes in 2026 (first half) (Eurostat). In 2026 (first half), the latest half-year with data for most countries, it was 0.3142 euro, 35th of 39 countries from the lowest and 10.9% above the EU average of 0.2832 euro.

How much of the price is taxes and levies

Without taxes and levies the same price was 0.2356 euro per kWh in 2026 (first half), so taxes, levies and other charges that Eurostat counts made up about 25.0% of the price with taxes.

Common questions

What is the electricity price in Italy?

Eurostat gives 0.3142 euro per kWh including all taxes for a household using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh a year in 2026 (first half).

Why is the price in euro for countries that do not use the euro?

Eurostat converts national-currency prices to euro so countries can be compared; the exchange rate used affects the figure.

Is this my bill price?

No. It is a national average for a defined household band. Your contract, region and fixed charges decide your bill.
