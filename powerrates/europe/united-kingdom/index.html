Electricity prices in United Kingdom

0.2203 euro per kWh, household, 2020 (first half)

For a household using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh a year, the average electricity price in United Kingdom was 0.2203 euro per kWh including taxes in 2020 (first half) (Eurostat).

How much of the price is taxes and levies

Without taxes and levies the same price was 0.1532 euro per kWh in 2020 (first half), so taxes, levies and other charges that Eurostat counts made up about 30.5% of the price with taxes.

Price by size of household use

Eurostat publishes household prices in five bands of yearly use. Smaller users often pay more per kWh because fixed charges are spread over fewer units.

Common questions

What is the electricity price in United Kingdom?

Eurostat gives 0.2203 euro per kWh including all taxes for a household using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh a year in 2020 (first half).

Why is the price in euro for countries that do not use the euro?

Eurostat converts national-currency prices to euro so countries can be compared; the exchange rate used affects the figure.

Is this my bill price?

No. It is a national average for a defined household band. Your contract, region and fixed charges decide your bill.
