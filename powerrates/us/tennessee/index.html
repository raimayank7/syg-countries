Electricity prices in Tennessee

13.22 cents per kWh, residential, 2025

The average residential electricity price in Tennessee was 13.22 cents per kWh in 2025 (EIA annual average). That is 23.3% below the US average of 17.23 cents, and puts Tennessee 11th of 51 states and DC from the lowest price (1 is the cheapest). The latest monthly average is 13.42 cents (August 2026). Five years earlier (2020) it was 10.76 cents, a change of 22.9% in nominal terms. Ten years earlier (2015): 10.30 cents, 28.3% change.

Common questions

What is the average electricity price in Tennessee?

EIA's annual residential average for 2025 was 13.22 cents per kWh. The US average was 17.23 cents.

Is this what my bill will show?

Not exactly. A state average mixes all suppliers and plans, and bills add fixed charges and taxes that vary. Your own contract rate is on your bill.

Are these prices adjusted for inflation?

No. They are the prices recorded in each year.
