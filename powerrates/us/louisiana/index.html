Electricity prices in Louisiana

12.52 cents per kWh, residential, 2025

The average residential electricity price in Louisiana was 12.52 cents per kWh in 2025 (EIA annual average). That is 27.3% below the US average of 17.23 cents, and puts Louisiana 4th of 51 states and DC from the lowest price (1 is the cheapest). The latest monthly average is 12.87 cents (August 2026). Five years earlier (2020) it was 9.67 cents, a change of 29.5% in nominal terms. Ten years earlier (2015): 9.33 cents, 34.2% change.

Common questions

What is the average electricity price in Louisiana?

EIA's annual residential average for 2025 was 12.52 cents per kWh. The US average was 17.23 cents.

Is this what my bill will show?

Not exactly. A state average mixes all suppliers and plans, and bills add fixed charges and taxes that vary. Your own contract rate is on your bill.

Are these prices adjusted for inflation?

No. They are the prices recorded in each year.
