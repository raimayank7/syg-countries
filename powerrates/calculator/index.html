Electricity prices

Electricity cost calculator

Pick a place, then enter the electricity you use a month, or the watts and hours of an appliance. The price used is the average residential price from this site, not your own tariff: for your real cost use the price per kWh on your bill.

Energy cost only: real bills add fixed charges, network charges and taxes that the average may or may not include. The calculation runs in your browser.

Data: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA, file updated 2026-10-22) and Eurostat (table nrg_pc_204, updated 2026-10-19). Averages are not tariffs and are not adjusted for inflation. Independent site, not connected with EIA, Eurostat or any energy supplier.
