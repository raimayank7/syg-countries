Storm event reports: Martin, Florida

178 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 178 reports for Martin, Florida from 1996 to 2025, of 10 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 60 reports (33.7%). Reports in the database total 3 deaths (2 direct and 1 indirect) and 13 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $19.6 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2020 (12) and the year with the fewest is 2025 (1).

Martin makes up 0.52% of the 34,087 reports recorded for Florida as a whole.

Common questions

How many storm event reports are there for Martin, Florida?

The NOAA Storm Events Database has 178 reports for this county or county-equivalent from 1996 to 2025.

Is this the number of storms?

No. Each row of the database is one event for one county or forecast zone, so one storm can be several rows, and populated places get more reports because people see and report events.

Does this show how safe Martin is?

No. The counts depend on reporting and population and are not a risk rating. For flood and hazard risk use official maps from FEMA and the National Weather Service.
