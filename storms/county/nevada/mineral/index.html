Common questions

How many storm event reports are there for Mineral, Nevada?

The NOAA Storm Events Database has 38 reports for this county or county-equivalent from 1996 to 2025.

Is this the number of storms?

No. Each row of the database is one event for one county or forecast zone, so one storm can be several rows, and populated places get more reports because people see and report events.

Does this show how safe Mineral is?

No. The counts depend on reporting and population and are not a risk rating. For flood and hazard risk use official maps from FEMA and the National Weather Service.
