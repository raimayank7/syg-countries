Storm event reports: Richland, South Carolina

1,233 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 1,233 reports for Richland, South Carolina from 1996 to 2025, of 8 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 703 reports (57.0%). Reports in the database total 11 deaths (11 direct and 0 indirect) and 58 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $41.2 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2015 (99) and the year with the fewest is 2002 (11).

Richland makes up 4.34% of the 28,414 reports recorded for South Carolina as a whole.

Common questions

How many storm event reports are there for Richland, South Carolina?

The NOAA Storm Events Database has 1,233 reports for this county or county-equivalent from 1996 to 2025.

Is this the number of storms?

No. Each row of the database is one event for one county or forecast zone, so one storm can be several rows, and populated places get more reports because people see and report events.

Does this show how safe Richland is?

No. The counts depend on reporting and population and are not a risk rating. For flood and hazard risk use official maps from FEMA and the National Weather Service.
