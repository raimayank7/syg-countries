Storm event reports: Baltimore, Maryland

1,221 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 1,221 reports for Baltimore, Maryland from 1996 to 2025, of 9 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 560 reports (45.9%). Reports in the database total 4 deaths (4 direct and 0 indirect) and 43 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $40.8 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (101) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (4).

Baltimore makes up 5.25% of the 23,256 reports recorded for Maryland as a whole.

Common questions

How many storm event reports are there for Baltimore, Maryland?

The NOAA Storm Events Database has 1,221 reports for this county or county-equivalent from 1996 to 2025.

Is this the number of storms?

No. Each row of the database is one event for one county or forecast zone, so one storm can be several rows, and populated places get more reports because people see and report events.

Does this show how safe Baltimore is?

No. The counts depend on reporting and population and are not a risk rating. For flood and hazard risk use official maps from FEMA and the National Weather Service.
