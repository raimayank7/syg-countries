How to use this page

The table lists the states and the number of storm reports in each for the years of the database. States with more people, more spotters and more land report more events, so a high count does not by itself mean a more dangerous climate. The columns for deaths, injuries and damage show the recorded effects of those reports. Open a state for the event types that dominate it, the worst years and its counties, and use the guide on damage estimates before you compare the money figures between years.
