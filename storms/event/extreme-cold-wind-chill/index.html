Extreme Cold/Wind Chill: reports in the US, 2000 to 2025

18,104 reports

The database holds 18,104 reports of the event type "Extreme Cold/Wind Chill" for US states between 2000 and 2025 (the type is first recorded in 2000), with 268 deaths, 59 injuries and damage estimated at $361.1 million (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.
