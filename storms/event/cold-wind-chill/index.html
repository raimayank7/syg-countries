Cold/Wind Chill: reports in the US, 1996 to 2025

18,010 reports

The database holds 18,010 reports of the event type "Cold/Wind Chill" for US states between 1996 and 2025, with 739 deaths, 341 injuries and damage estimated at $2.08 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.
