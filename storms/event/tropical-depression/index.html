Tropical Depression: reports in the US, 2007 to 2025

538 reports

The database holds 538 reports of the event type "Tropical Depression" for US states between 2007 and 2025 (the type is first recorded in 2007), with 8 deaths, 5 injuries and damage estimated at $73.5 million (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.
