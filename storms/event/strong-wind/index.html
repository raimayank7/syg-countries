Strong Wind: reports in the US, 1996 to 2025

27,990 reports

The database holds 27,990 reports of the event type "Strong Wind" for US states between 1996 and 2025, with 377 deaths, 701 injuries and damage estimated at $933.5 million (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.
