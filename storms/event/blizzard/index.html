Blizzard: reports in the US, 1996 to 2025

16,977 reports

The database holds 16,977 reports of the event type "Blizzard" for US states between 1996 and 2025, with 247 deaths, 653 injuries and damage estimated at $1.36 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.

How this type fits into the whole database

Reports of "blizzard" are 1.0% of all 1,721,830 reports in the database for these years, 1.1% of the recorded deaths (direct and indirect together) and 0.2% of the estimated damage. The year with the most reports is 1996 (1,431) and the year with the most deaths is 2022 (56). Averaged over its first 5 recorded years the type has about 693 reports a year, and over the last 5 about 488. The three states with the most reports hold 43% of them. A rise or fall between years can come from the weather, but also from changes in how reports are collected and coded: event definitions and reporting practice can change over time, and some types appear in the file only from a later year. For damage, the figures are estimates not adjusted for inflation, and they are rounded or missing for many reports.

Data: NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, 1996 to 2025, read 14 November 2026. Counts are of reports, not storms; damage figures are estimates and are not adjusted for inflation. Independent site, not connected with NOAA or the National Weather Service.
