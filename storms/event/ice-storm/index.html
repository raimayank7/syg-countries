Ice Storm: reports in the US, 1996 to 2025

12,494 reports

The database holds 12,494 reports of the event type "Ice Storm" for US states between 1996 and 2025, with 193 deaths, 1,879 injuries and damage estimated at $6.07 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The table shows the 15 states with the most reports; reports follow population and reporting practice, so the order says little about the weather alone. How the type is defined is in the National Weather Service's Storm Data documentation.
