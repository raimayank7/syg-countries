How to read storm damage estimates

Damage figures in the database are entered by National Weather Service staff after an event, from local reports and surveys, in rounded form (for example 3K for three thousand dollars, 1.5M for one and a half million, 2B for two billion). Property and crop damage are separate fields; this site adds them together and says so.

Cautions

  • They are estimates at the time of entry; they are not the sums paid by insurers or government.
  • They are not adjusted for inflation, so a figure from 1998 and one from 2024 are not in the same money.
  • Large events can be entered in parts, and some damage never reaches the database.

When you compare years, compare reports and the pattern, not the dollars, or adjust the dollars with an official price index.
