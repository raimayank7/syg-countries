All states · Event types · What the database is

The database holds 1,721,830 reports for the US states, with 23,133 deaths and 101,430 injuries recorded and property and crop damage estimated at $604.11 billion (not adjusted for inflation). Every county and state has its own page of counts by event type and year.

Data: NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, 1996 to 2025, read 14 November 2026. Counts are of reports, not storms; damage figures are estimates and are not adjusted for inflation. Independent site, not connected with NOAA or the National Weather Service.
