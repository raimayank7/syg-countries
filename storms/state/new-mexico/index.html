Storm event reports in New Mexico

22,417 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 22,417 reports for New Mexico from 1996 to 2025, of 28 different event types. The most common type is Drought with 6,205 reports (27.7%). Reports in the database total 185 deaths (118 direct and 67 indirect) and 607 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $3.22 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2013 (1,387) and the year with the fewest is 2001 (204).
