Storm event reports in Nebraska

53,940 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 53,940 reports for Nebraska from 1996 to 2025, of 28 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 20,799 reports (38.6%). Reports in the database total 128 deaths (63 direct and 65 indirect) and 754 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $7.12 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (2,479) and the year with the fewest is 2002 (1,298).
