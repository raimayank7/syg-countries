Storm event reports in Alaska

9,524 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 9,524 reports for Alaska from 1996 to 2025, of 34 different event types. The most common type is High Wind with 3,775 reports (39.6%). Reports in the database total 100 deaths (94 direct and 6 indirect) and 133 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $482.3 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 1999 (581) and the year with the fewest is 2018 (121).
