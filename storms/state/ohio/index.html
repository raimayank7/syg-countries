Storm event reports in Ohio

44,029 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 44,029 reports for Ohio from 1996 to 2025, of 33 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 17,021 reports (38.7%). Reports in the database total 262 deaths (231 direct and 31 indirect) and 1,475 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $6.94 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2019 (2,279) and the year with the fewest is 2009 (926).
