Storm event reports in Michigan

30,526 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 30,526 reports for Michigan from 1996 to 2025, of 32 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 9,794 reports (32.1%). Reports in the database total 228 deaths (174 direct and 54 indirect) and 1,930 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $6.30 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (1,675) and the year with the fewest is 2017 (628).
