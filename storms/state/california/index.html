Storm event reports in California

40,437 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 40,437 reports for California from 1996 to 2025, of 40 different event types. The most common type is High Wind with 7,057 reports (17.5%). Reports in the database total 1,334 deaths (1,130 direct and 204 indirect) and 4,873 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $36.25 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (3,148) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (396).
