Storm event reports in Kentucky

50,390 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 50,390 reports for Kentucky from 1996 to 2025, of 27 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 16,583 reports (32.9%). Reports in the database total 474 deaths (392 direct and 82 indirect) and 2,293 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $3.36 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2010 (2,997) and the year with the fewest is 1999 (746).
