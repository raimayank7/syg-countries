Storm event reports in North Carolina

48,362 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 48,362 reports for North Carolina from 1996 to 2025, of 37 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 16,815 reports (34.8%). Reports in the database total 634 deaths (561 direct and 73 indirect) and 1,944 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $16.48 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (2,539) and the year with the fewest is 2021 (904).
