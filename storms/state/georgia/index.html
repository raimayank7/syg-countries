Storm event reports in Georgia

48,663 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 48,663 reports for Georgia from 1996 to 2025, of 35 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 19,511 reports (40.1%). Reports in the database total 341 deaths (290 direct and 51 indirect) and 2,494 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $12.11 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2002 (2,752) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (795).
