Storm event reports in Wisconsin

40,012 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 40,012 reports for Wisconsin from 1996 to 2025, of 32 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 9,666 reports (24.2%). Reports in the database total 455 deaths (266 direct and 189 indirect) and 1,908 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $4.35 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (2,024) and the year with the fewest is 2003 (759).
