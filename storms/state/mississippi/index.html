Storm event reports in Mississippi

34,408 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 34,408 reports for Mississippi from 1996 to 2025, of 31 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 14,217 reports (41.3%). Reports in the database total 516 deaths (470 direct and 46 indirect) and 2,435 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $32.92 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (1,942) and the year with the fewest is 1999 (652).
