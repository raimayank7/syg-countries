Storm event reports in Hawaii

15,331 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 15,331 reports for Hawaii from 1996 to 2025, of 27 different event types. The most common type is High Surf with 9,295 reports (60.6%). Reports in the database total 178 deaths (164 direct and 14 indirect) and 183 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $5.89 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2018 (840) and the year with the fewest is 2025 (126).
