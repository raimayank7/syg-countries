Storm event reports in New York

50,675 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 50,675 reports for New York from 1996 to 2025, of 36 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 15,459 reports (30.5%). Reports in the database total 605 deaths (513 direct and 92 indirect) and 1,595 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $5.44 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2019 (2,498) and the year with the fewest is 2001 (939).
