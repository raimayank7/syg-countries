Storm event reports in District of Columbia

930 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 930 reports for District of Columbia from 1996 to 2025, of 25 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 294 reports (31.6%). Reports in the database total 16 deaths (16 direct and 0 indirect) and 380 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $186.9 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2018 (80) and the year with the fewest is 2009 (9).
