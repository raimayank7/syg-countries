Storm event reports in Oklahoma

62,023 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 62,023 reports for Oklahoma from 1996 to 2025, of 28 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 20,406 reports (32.9%). Reports in the database total 477 deaths (402 direct and 75 indirect) and 5,059 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $9.61 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (3,764) and the year with the fewest is 2010 (1,229).
