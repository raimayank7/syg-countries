Storm event reports in West Virginia

27,179 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 27,179 reports for West Virginia from 1996 to 2025, of 29 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 7,039 reports (25.9%). Reports in the database total 147 deaths (127 direct and 20 indirect) and 208 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $1.35 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2025 (2,187) and the year with the fewest is 2005 (422).
