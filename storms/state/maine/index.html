Storm event reports in Maine

14,977 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 14,977 reports for Maine from 1996 to 2025, of 34 different event types. The most common type is Heavy Snow with 2,938 reports (19.6%). Reports in the database total 40 deaths (36 direct and 4 indirect) and 210 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $746.1 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 1999 (825) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (211).
