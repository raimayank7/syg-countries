Storm event reports in North Dakota

28,848 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 28,848 reports for North Dakota from 1996 to 2025, of 26 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 8,282 reports (28.7%). Reports in the database total 77 deaths (61 direct and 16 indirect) and 368 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $6.02 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (1,486) and the year with the fewest is 1998 (485).
