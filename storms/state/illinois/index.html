Storm event reports in Illinois

57,649 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 57,649 reports for Illinois from 1996 to 2025, of 34 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 17,286 reports (30.0%). Reports in the database total 975 deaths (852 direct and 123 indirect) and 2,497 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $7.22 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (2,891) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (1,262).
