Storm event reports in Iowa

61,067 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 61,067 reports for Iowa from 1996 to 2025, of 26 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 13,739 reports (22.5%). Reports in the database total 151 deaths (106 direct and 45 indirect) and 1,537 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $12.46 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (3,457) and the year with the fewest is 2002 (1,374).
