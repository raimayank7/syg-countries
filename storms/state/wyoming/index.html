Storm event reports in Wyoming

23,980 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 23,980 reports for Wyoming from 1996 to 2025, of 26 different event types. The most common type is High Wind with 8,457 reports (35.3%). Reports in the database total 112 deaths (92 direct and 20 indirect) and 498 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $288.5 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2017 (1,641) and the year with the fewest is 2004 (214).
