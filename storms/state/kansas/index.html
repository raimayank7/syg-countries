Storm event reports in Kansas

77,995 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 77,995 reports for Kansas from 1996 to 2025, of 27 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 32,461 reports (41.6%). Reports in the database total 265 deaths (167 direct and 98 indirect) and 1,683 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $3.99 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (3,664) and the year with the fewest is 2020 (1,782).
