Storm event reports in Missouri

62,687 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 62,687 reports for Missouri from 1996 to 2025, of 28 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 16,397 reports (26.2%). Reports in the database total 847 deaths (774 direct and 73 indirect) and 10,459 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $11.10 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (3,194) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (1,116).
