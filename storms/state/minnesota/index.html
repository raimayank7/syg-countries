Storm event reports in Minnesota

49,512 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 49,512 reports for Minnesota from 1996 to 2025, of 32 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 13,403 reports (27.1%). Reports in the database total 208 deaths (131 direct and 77 indirect) and 1,076 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $5.28 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (2,728) and the year with the fewest is 2000 (1,017).
