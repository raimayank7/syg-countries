Storm event reports in Virginia

53,941 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 53,941 reports for Virginia from 1996 to 2025, of 35 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 18,211 reports (33.8%). Reports in the database total 218 deaths (185 direct and 33 indirect) and 1,248 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $3.31 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2018 (3,085) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (706).
