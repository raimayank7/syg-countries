Storm event reports in Louisiana

24,689 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 24,689 reports for Louisiana from 1996 to 2025, of 34 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 8,413 reports (34.1%). Reports in the database total 1,179 deaths (1,087 direct and 92 indirect) and 1,335 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $96.83 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (2,199) and the year with the fewest is 2007 (463).
