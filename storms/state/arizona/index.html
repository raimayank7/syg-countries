Storm event reports in Arizona

16,583 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 16,583 reports for Arizona from 1996 to 2025, of 32 different event types. The most common type is Flash Flood with 3,480 reports (21.0%). Reports in the database total 3,072 deaths (2,130 direct and 942 indirect) and 1,305 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $4.42 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (1,419) and the year with the fewest is 2002 (213).
