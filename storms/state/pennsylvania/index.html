Storm event reports in Pennsylvania

47,823 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 47,823 reports for Pennsylvania from 1996 to 2025, of 35 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 18,963 reports (39.7%). Reports in the database total 807 deaths (673 direct and 134 indirect) and 2,586 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $4.19 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2019 (2,395) and the year with the fewest is 2016 (898).
