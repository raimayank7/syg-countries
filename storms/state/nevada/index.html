Storm event reports in Nevada

8,327 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 8,327 reports for Nevada from 1996 to 2025, of 29 different event types. The most common type is High Wind with 2,851 reports (34.2%). Reports in the database total 985 deaths (596 direct and 389 indirect) and 1,107 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $1.09 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (698) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (127).
