Storm event reports in Massachusetts

15,318 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 15,318 reports for Massachusetts from 1996 to 2025, of 30 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 3,901 reports (25.5%). Reports in the database total 59 deaths (47 direct and 12 indirect) and 662 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $904.2 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2016 (889) and the year with the fewest is 2002 (209).
