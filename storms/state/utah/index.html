Storm event reports in Utah

11,239 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 11,239 reports for Utah from 1996 to 2025, of 29 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 2,002 reports (17.8%). Reports in the database total 253 deaths (234 direct and 19 indirect) and 1,857 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $1.07 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (689) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (186).
