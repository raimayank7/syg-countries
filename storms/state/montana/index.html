Storm event reports in Montana

33,265 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 33,265 reports for Montana from 1996 to 2025, of 31 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 7,306 reports (22.0%). Reports in the database total 158 deaths (89 direct and 69 indirect) and 367 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $373.7 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (1,901) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (601).
