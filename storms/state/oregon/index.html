Storm event reports in Oregon

11,108 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 11,108 reports for Oregon from 1996 to 2025, of 38 different event types. The most common type is High Wind with 3,039 reports (27.4%). Reports in the database total 406 deaths (327 direct and 79 indirect) and 361 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $4.98 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2006 (745) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (176).
