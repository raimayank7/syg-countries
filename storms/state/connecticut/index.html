Storm event reports in Connecticut

6,529 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 6,529 reports for Connecticut from 1996 to 2025, of 28 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 2,073 reports (31.8%). Reports in the database total 61 deaths (54 direct and 7 indirect) and 231 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $259.6 million (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2018 (335) and the year with the fewest is 2004 (97).
