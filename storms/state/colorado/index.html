Storm event reports in Colorado

45,959 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 45,959 reports for Colorado from 1996 to 2025, of 29 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 12,791 reports (27.8%). Reports in the database total 318 deaths (288 direct and 30 indirect) and 1,658 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $10.83 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (2,102) and the year with the fewest is 2001 (1,011).
