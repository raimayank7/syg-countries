Storm event reports in South Dakota

49,523 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 49,523 reports for South Dakota from 1996 to 2025, of 27 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 14,637 reports (29.6%). Reports in the database total 129 deaths (71 direct and 58 indirect) and 703 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $2.62 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2022 (2,741) and the year with the fewest is 1998 (647).
