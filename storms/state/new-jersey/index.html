Storm event reports in New Jersey

25,285 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 25,285 reports for New Jersey from 1996 to 2025, of 35 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 4,997 reports (19.8%). Reports in the database total 337 deaths (258 direct and 79 indirect) and 2,681 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $28.07 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2002 (1,050) and the year with the fewest is 2022 (549).
