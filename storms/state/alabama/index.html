Storm event reports in Alabama

37,005 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 37,005 reports for Alabama from 1996 to 2025, of 35 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 16,027 reports (43.3%). Reports in the database total 693 deaths (650 direct and 43 indirect) and 4,988 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $11.30 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (2,468) and the year with the fewest is 1999 (714).
