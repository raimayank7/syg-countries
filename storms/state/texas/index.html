Storm event reports in Texas

131,060 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 131,060 reports for Texas from 1996 to 2025, of 40 different event types. The most common type is Hail with 39,767 reports (30.3%). Reports in the database total 1,967 deaths (1,621 direct and 346 indirect) and 17,340 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $119.74 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2023 (7,689) and the year with the fewest is 2010 (2,950).
