Storm event reports in Indiana

36,319 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 36,319 reports for Indiana from 1996 to 2025, of 33 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 12,845 reports (35.4%). Reports in the database total 338 deaths (239 direct and 99 indirect) and 1,407 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $3.22 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2008 (1,933) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (694).
