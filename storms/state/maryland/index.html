Storm event reports in Maryland

23,256 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 23,256 reports for Maryland from 1996 to 2025, of 34 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 7,249 reports (31.2%). Reports in the database total 234 deaths (219 direct and 15 indirect) and 1,474 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $1.51 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2018 (1,220) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (377).
