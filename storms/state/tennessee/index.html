Storm event reports in Tennessee

41,329 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 41,329 reports for Tennessee from 1996 to 2025, of 30 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 17,275 reports (41.8%). Reports in the database total 594 deaths (530 direct and 64 indirect) and 3,186 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $8.87 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2025 (2,343) and the year with the fewest is 1999 (916).
