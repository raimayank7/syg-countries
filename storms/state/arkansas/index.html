Storm event reports in Arkansas

39,604 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 39,604 reports for Arkansas from 1996 to 2025, of 32 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 11,995 reports (30.3%). Reports in the database total 378 deaths (334 direct and 44 indirect) and 3,055 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $5.65 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (2,811) and the year with the fewest is 1997 (742).
