Storm event reports in South Carolina

28,414 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 28,414 reports for South Carolina from 1996 to 2025, of 36 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 13,837 reports (48.7%). Reports in the database total 283 deaths (251 direct and 32 indirect) and 952 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $2.42 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2011 (1,723) and the year with the fewest is 2021 (465).
