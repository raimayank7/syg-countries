Storm event reports in Florida

34,087 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 34,087 reports for Florida from 1996 to 2025, of 36 different event types. The most common type is Thunderstorm Wind with 11,767 reports (34.5%). Reports in the database total 1,304 deaths (1,078 direct and 226 indirect) and 3,875 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $84.61 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 1998 (1,701) and the year with the fewest is 2006 (699).
