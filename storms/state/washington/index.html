Storm event reports in Washington

9,802 reports, 1996 to 2025

The NOAA Storm Events Database holds 9,802 reports for Washington from 1996 to 2025, of 38 different event types. The most common type is Heavy Snow with 3,188 reports (32.5%). Reports in the database total 323 deaths (250 direct and 73 indirect) and 957 injuries, with property and crop damage estimated at $5.07 billion (not adjusted for inflation). The year with the most reports is 2017 (546) and the year with the fewest is 1996 (139).
