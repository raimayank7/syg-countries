How to use this page

The four guides explain the Legal Entity Identifier from different sides. One explains what an LEI is and what a record contains. One takes the 20 characters apart, including the two check digits, with a worked check you can follow by hand. One describes the general steps for obtaining an LEI from an accredited issuer, and one explains the statuses a record can have, such as a lapsed registration that has not been renewed. Use the lookup on the home page to read a real record, and the guides to understand the answer it gives.

  • What is an LEI?

    What a Legal Entity Identifier is, who issues it, what a record contains and why companies in financial markets have one.

  • How an LEI is built

    The 20 characters of an LEI explained: the issuer prefix, the entity part and the two check digits, with a worked example of the MOD 97-10 check.

  • How to get an LEI

    The general steps to obtain a Legal Entity Identifier through an accredited issuer, what information is asked, and how renewal works.

  • LEI statuses explained

    What the status of an LEI record means, why an entity may have a lapsed LEI, and what to do when you see one.
