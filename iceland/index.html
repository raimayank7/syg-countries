Iceland

Iceland is a country in Northern Europe. In the language of the place it is called Ísland. The capital is Reykjavik.

Land and position

Iceland covers about 103,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 109th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Guatemala and a little larger than South Korea. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 65° north, 18° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 392,404 people live in Iceland (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 180th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 3.8 people to each square kilometre. About 94 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 83 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Icelandic, English, German, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian. A person from Iceland is called Icelander.

Money and economy

The currency is the Icelandic króna (code ISK, symbol kr). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 38.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 98,323 for each person (2025).

Time

Iceland uses one time zone, UTC (Atlantic/Reykjavik). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
