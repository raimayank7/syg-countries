Türkiye

Türkiye is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Republic of Türkiye. The capital is Ankara.

Land and position

Türkiye covers about 780,580 square kilometres. By area it ranks 38th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Mozambique and a little larger than Chile. It shares a land border with 8 neighbours: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Georgia, Greece, Iraq, Iran, Syria. Its centre lies at about 39° north, 35° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 85.9 million people live in Türkiye (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 18th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1 percent of the world's people. On average there are 110 people to each square kilometre. About 89 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 77 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Turkish, Kurdish, Dimli (individual language), Azerbaijani, Avaric. A person from Türkiye is called Turkish.

Money and economy

The currency is the Turkish lira (code TRY, symbol ₺). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.6 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 18,599 for each person (2025).

Time

Türkiye uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Europe/Istanbul). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
