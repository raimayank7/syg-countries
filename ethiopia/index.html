Ethiopia

Ethiopia is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia. In the language of the place it is called ኢትዮጵያ. The capital is Addis Ababa.

Land and position

Ethiopia covers about 1,127,127 square kilometres. By area it ranks 28th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Colombia and a little larger than Bolivia. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Djibouti, Eritrea, Kenya, Sudan, Somalia, South Sudan. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 8° north, 38° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 135 million people live in Ethiopia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 10th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1.6 percent of the world's people. On average there are 120 people to each square kilometre. About 24 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Amharic, English, Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, Sidamo. A person from Ethiopia is called Ethiopian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Ethiopian birr (code ETB, symbol Br). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 126 billion (2025), which is about US$ 933 for each person (2025).

Time

Ethiopia uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Africa/Addis Ababa). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
