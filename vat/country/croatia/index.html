VAT rates in Croatia

25% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Croatia is 25% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 23rd lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

The database lists 23 entries for Croatia besides the standard rate: 23 reduced rates. The reduced and special rates in use are 5%, 13%.

What is the standard VAT rate in Croatia?

Does Croatia have reduced VAT rates?

Yes: the database lists 23 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.

Is this tax advice?

No. Which rate applies depends on the exact goods or service and the rules of the country. Check with the national tax authority.
