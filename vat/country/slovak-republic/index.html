VAT rates in Slovak Republic

23% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Slovak Republic is 23% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 20th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

The database lists 14 entries for Slovak Republic besides the standard rate: 14 reduced rates. The reduced and special rates in use are 5%, 19%.
