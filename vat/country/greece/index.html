VAT rates in Greece

24% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Greece is 24% (situation on 2026/01/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 22nd lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 64 entries for Greece besides the standard rate: 59 reduced rates, 1 super-reduced rate, 3 parking rates, 1 exempted. The reduced and special rates in use are 4%, 6%, 13%, 17%.
