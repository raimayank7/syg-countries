VAT rates in Spain

21% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Spain is 21% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 14th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%. The database lists more than one standard rate (7%, 21%): the extra one is a regional rate, see the note below.

Common questions

What is the standard VAT rate in Spain?

The Commission's database shows 21% (situation on 2026/07/01).

Does Spain have reduced VAT rates?

Yes: the database lists 26 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.

Is this tax advice?

No. Which rate applies depends on the exact goods or service and the rules of the country. Check with the national tax authority.
