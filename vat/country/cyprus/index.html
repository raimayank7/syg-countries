VAT rates in Cyprus

19% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Cyprus is 19% (situation on 2026/01/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 3rd lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 32 entries for Cyprus besides the standard rate: 21 reduced rates, 7 super-reduced rates, 1 exempted, 3 not applicables. The reduced and special rates in use are 3%, 5%, 9%.
