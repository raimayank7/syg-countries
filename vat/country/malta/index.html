VAT rates in Malta

18% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Malta is 18% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 2nd lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 41 entries for Malta besides the standard rate: 25 reduced rates, 4 parking rates, 12 exempteds. The reduced and special rates in use are 5%, 7%, 12%.

Yes: the database lists 41 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.
