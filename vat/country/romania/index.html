VAT rates in Romania

21% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Romania is 21% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 13th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 34 entries for Romania besides the standard rate: 33 reduced rates, 1 exempted. The reduced and special rates in use are 11%.
