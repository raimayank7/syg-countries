VAT rates in Belgium

21% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Belgium is 21% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 8th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Note from the database: Article 1, paragraph 1, of the Royal Decree n° 20 of 20 July 1970
