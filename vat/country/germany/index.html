VAT rates in Germany

19% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Germany is 19% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 4th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

The database lists 42 entries for Germany besides the standard rate: 38 reduced rates, 2 exempteds, 2 out of scopes. The reduced and special rates in use are 7%.

Common questions

What is the standard VAT rate in Germany?

The Commission's database shows 19% (situation on 2026/07/01).

Does Germany have reduced VAT rates?

Yes: the database lists 42 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.

Is this tax advice?

No. Which rate applies depends on the exact goods or service and the rules of the country. Check with the national tax authority.
