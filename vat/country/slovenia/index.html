VAT rates in Slovenia

22% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Slovenia is 22% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 16th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.
