VAT rates in Finland

25.5% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Finland is 25.5% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 26th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 18 entries for Finland besides the standard rate: 18 reduced rates. The reduced and special rates in use are 10%, 13.5%.

Common questions

What is the standard VAT rate in Finland?

The Commission's database shows 25.5% (situation on 2026/07/01).

Does Finland have reduced VAT rates?

Yes: the database lists 18 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.

Is this tax advice?

No. Which rate applies depends on the exact goods or service and the rules of the country. Check with the national tax authority.
