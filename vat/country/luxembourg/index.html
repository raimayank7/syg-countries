VAT rates in Luxembourg

17% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Luxembourg is 17% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 1st lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%.

Note from the database: Art. 39(3) of VAT law

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 90 entries for Luxembourg besides the standard rate: 29 reduced rates, 46 super-reduced rates, 14 parking rates, 1 exempted. The reduced and special rates in use are 3%, 8%, 14%.
