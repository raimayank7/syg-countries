VAT rates in Latvia

21% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Latvia is 21% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 10th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Note from the database: Section 41, Paragraph 1, Clause 1 of the Law .
