VAT rates in Poland

23% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Poland is 23% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 18th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 43 entries for Poland besides the standard rate: 42 reduced rates, 1 super-reduced rate. The reduced and special rates in use are 5%, 8%.
