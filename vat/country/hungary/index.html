VAT rates in Hungary

27% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Hungary is 27% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 27th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 68 entries for Hungary besides the standard rate: 67 reduced rates, 1 exempted. The reduced and special rates in use are 5%, 18%.
