VAT rates in Ireland

23% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Ireland is 23% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 17th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Note from the database: The Standard Rate of VAT was temporarily reduced from 23% to 21% from 1 September 2020 to 28 February 2021. It has been restored to 23% from 1 March 2021.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 33 entries for Ireland besides the standard rate: 23 reduced rates, 7 exempteds, 3 not applicables. The reduced and special rates in use are 9%, 13.5%.

Does Ireland have reduced VAT rates?

Yes: the database lists 33 reduced, super-reduced, parking, zero or exempt entries by category.

Is this tax advice?

No. Which rate applies depends on the exact goods or service and the rules of the country. Check with the national tax authority.
