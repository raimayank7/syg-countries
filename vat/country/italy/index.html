VAT rates in Italy

22% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Italy is 22% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 15th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 166 entries for Italy besides the standard rate: 139 reduced rates, 27 super-reduced rates. The reduced and special rates in use are 4%, 5%, 10%.
