VAT rates in Bulgaria

20% standard rate

The standard VAT rate in Bulgaria is 20% (situation on 2026/07/01 in the European Commission's database). Among the 27 EU member states it is the 6th lowest (1 is the lowest); the average standard rate is 21.91%. The highest is Hungary at 27% and the lowest Luxembourg at 17%.

Reduced, super-reduced and other rates

The database lists 5 entries for Bulgaria besides the standard rate: 5 reduced rates. The reduced and special rates in use are 9%.
