Pharmaceutical products of a kind normally used for health care, prevention of ...: VAT rates in Europe

The European Commission's database lists 64 entries from 26 countries for this category. The reduced and special rates used are 2.1% (1), 3% (3), 5% (11), 5.5% (1), 6% (12), 7% (2), 8% (1), 9% (13), 9.5% (1), 10% (6), 11% (1), 12% (2), 13% (2), 13.5% (2).

The category, as the database words it: Pharmaceutical products of a kind normally used for health care, prevention of illness and as treatment for medical and veterinary purposes, including products used for contraception and sanitary protection

How the countries treat this category

Of the 29 countries and territories in the database, 26 (90%) list at least one entry for this category; the other 3 list none, which means the category has no special rate there and the standard rate applies, or that the national rules describe it in other words. The entries are of these types: reduced rate (54), exempted (6), super-reduced rate (4). The lowest rate listed is 0% (Austria) and the highest 13.5% (Ireland); the middle value is 6%. Where a reduced, super-reduced or parking rate is listed, it is on average 34% of that country's standard rate. Zero-rated or exempt treatment is listed by Austria, Belgium, Greece, Ireland, Malta. The difference between a zero rate and an exemption matters to businesses: with a zero rate the seller can usually still recover input VAT, with an exemption it generally cannot. Check the national rules for the exact conditions, because a country may apply the rate to only part of the category. The entries are shown as the Commission database lists them, and the rules of each country should be checked before you issue an invoice.
