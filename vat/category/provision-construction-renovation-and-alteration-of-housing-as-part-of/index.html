Provision, construction, renovation and alteration of housing, as part of a ...: VAT rates in Europe

The European Commission's database lists 19 entries from 13 countries for this category. The reduced and special rates used are 5% (6), 5.5% (1), 6% (2), 8% (1), 9.5% (1), 10% (4), 11% (1), 12% (2), 13.5% (1).

The category, as the database words it: Provision, construction, renovation and alteration of housing, as part of a social policy

How the countries treat this category

Of the 29 countries and territories in the database, 13 (45%) list at least one entry for this category; the other 16 list none, which means the category has no special rate there and the standard rate applies, or that the national rules describe it in other words. The entries are of these types: reduced rate (19). The lowest rate listed is 5% (Cyprus) and the highest 13.5% (Ireland); the middle value is 8%. Where a reduced, super-reduced or parking rate is listed, it is on average 37% of that country's standard rate. The entries are shown as the Commission database lists them, and the rules of each country should be checked before you issue an invoice.
