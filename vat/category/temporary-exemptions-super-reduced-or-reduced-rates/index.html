Temporary Exemptions, super-reduced or reduced rates: VAT rates in Europe

The European Commission's database lists 4 entries from 4 countries for this category. The reduced and special rates used are 4% (1), 6% (1), 8% (2).

The category, as the database words it: Temporary Exemptions, super-reduced or reduced rates (Article 105a(2) of the VAT Directive)

How the countries treat this category

Of the 29 countries and territories in the database, 4 (14%) list at least one entry for this category; the other 25 list none, which means the category has no special rate there and the standard rate applies, or that the national rules describe it in other words. The entries are of these types: super-reduced rate (4). The lowest rate listed is 4% (Italy) and the highest 8% (Poland); the middle value is 8%. Where a reduced, super-reduced or parking rate is listed, it is on average 32% of that country's standard rate. The entries are shown as the Commission database lists them, and the rules of each country should be checked before you issue an invoice.
