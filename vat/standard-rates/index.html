Standard VAT rates in Europe

The EU member states' standard rates run from 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary); the average of the 27 member states is 21.91%. Northern Ireland follows the EU rules for goods; the United Kingdom is listed with data only up to 2020 and is not part of the average. Spain's 21% is shown; its 7% Canary Islands rate is a regional rate described on its page.
