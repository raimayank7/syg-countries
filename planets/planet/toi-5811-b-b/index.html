TOI-5811 B b is a confirmed planet that orbits the star TOI-5811 B. In the archive it is a gas giant, about 22.0 times the radius of Earth (279,937 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), or 1.96 of Jupiter's radius, with a mass of about 257 Earth masses (0.81 times Jupiter's). It was discovered in 2026 by the transit method.

How big is TOI-5811 B b?

Its radius is 22.0 Earth radii, so it is much larger than Earth. That is 196% of Jupiter's radius. With a mass of 257 Earth masses it packs 257 times Earth's mass into about 10,604 times its volume. The density comes out at 0.134 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That points to a world with a thick envelope of gas or water, not a bare rock.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 6.26 days, so its year is 0.0171 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25. Its average distance from the star is 0.0731 AU, where 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun; Mercury is at 0.39 AU and Jupiter at 5.2 AU. It is closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun.

Temperature and starlight

The archive gives an equilibrium temperature of 1,530 K (1,257 degrees Celsius). That is the temperature the planet would have if starlight were its only source of heat; it is not the temperature of a surface, and an atmosphere can change it a lot. This is hotter than the melting point of many rocks. It receives 1,320 times the starlight that Earth gets from the Sun.

The star

The star is TOI-5811 B, with spectral type K2 IV+, a surface temperature of 4,840 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 3.73 times the Sun's, a mass of 1.08 times the Sun's. The system has 1 known planet.

How far away is it?

The system is about 555 light years (170 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 555 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 9,785,204 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 8.85: too faint for the naked eye, but within reach of binoculars or a small telescope.

Common questions

How was TOI-5811 B b discovered?

TOI-5811 B b was discovered in 2026 by the transit method using Multiple Observatories.

How long is a year on TOI-5811 B b?

It orbits its star every 6.26 days.

How far is TOI-5811 B b from Earth?

About 555 light years.

Could life exist on TOI-5811 B b?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
