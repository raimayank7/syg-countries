Super-Earth orbiting Kepler-793.

Kepler-793 b is a confirmed planet that orbits the star Kepler-793. In the archive it is a super-Earth, about 1.37 times the radius of Earth (17,457 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), with a mass of about 2.45 Earth masses. It was discovered in 2016 by the transit method, with Kepler.

How big is Kepler-793 b?

Its radius is 1.37 Earth radii, so it is larger than Earth. With a mass of 2.45 Earth masses it packs 2.45 times Earth's mass into about 2.57 times its volume. The density comes out at 5.24 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That is dense enough for a rocky or metal-rich world.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 4.24 days, so its year is 0.0116 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25. Its average distance from the star is 0.0491 AU, where 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun; Mercury is at 0.39 AU and Jupiter at 5.2 AU. It is closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun.

Temperature and starlight

The archive gives an equilibrium temperature of 1,177 K (904 degrees Celsius). That is the temperature the planet would have if starlight were its only source of heat; it is not the temperature of a surface, and an atmosphere can change it a lot. This is hotter than boiling water. It receives 617 times the starlight that Earth gets from the Sun.

The star

The star is Kepler-793, with a surface temperature of 5,745 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 0.97 times the Sun's, a mass of 0.98 times the Sun's. The system has 1 known planet.

How far away is it?

The system is about 3,215 light years (986 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 3,215 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 56,691,836 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 14.7: far too faint to see without a large telescope.

Common questions

How was Kepler-793 b discovered?

Kepler-793 b was discovered in 2016 by the transit method using Kepler.

How long is a year on Kepler-793 b?

It orbits its star every 4.24 days.

How far is Kepler-793 b from Earth?

About 3,215 light years.

Could life exist on Kepler-793 b?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
