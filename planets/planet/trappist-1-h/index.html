TRAPPIST-1 h: exoplanet

Earth-sized planet orbiting TRAPPIST-1.

TRAPPIST-1 h is a confirmed planet that orbits the star TRAPPIST-1. In the archive it is an Earth-sized planet, about 0.755 times the radius of Earth (9,620 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), with a mass of about 0.326 Earth masses. It was discovered in 2017 by the transit method.

How big is TRAPPIST-1 h?

Its radius is 0.755 Earth radii, so it is smaller than Earth. With a mass of 0.326 Earth masses it packs 0.326 times Earth's mass into about 0.43 times its volume. The density comes out at 4.16 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That is dense enough for a rocky or metal-rich world.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 18.8 days, so its year is 0.0514 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25. Its average distance from the star is 0.0619 AU, where 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun; Mercury is at 0.39 AU and Jupiter at 5.2 AU. It is closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun.

Temperature and starlight

The archive gives an equilibrium temperature of 172 K (-101 degrees Celsius). That is the temperature the planet would have if starlight were its only source of heat; it is not the temperature of a surface, and an atmosphere can change it a lot. This is below the freezing point of water. It receives 0.144 times the starlight that Earth gets from the Sun.

The star

The star is TRAPPIST-1, with spectral type M8.0 V, a surface temperature of 2,566 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 0.119 times the Sun's, a mass of 0.0898 times the Sun's. The system has 7 known planets. The planets of this star: TRAPPIST-1 b, TRAPPIST-1 c, TRAPPIST-1 d, TRAPPIST-1 e, TRAPPIST-1 f, TRAPPIST-1 g, TRAPPIST-1 h.

How far away is it?

The system is about 40.5 light years (12.4 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 40.5 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 714,897 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 17.0: far too faint to see without a large telescope.

Common questions

How was TRAPPIST-1 h discovered?

TRAPPIST-1 h was discovered in 2017 by the transit method using Multiple Observatories.

How long is a year on TRAPPIST-1 h?

It orbits its star every 18.8 days.

How far is TRAPPIST-1 h from Earth?

About 40.5 light years.

Could life exist on TRAPPIST-1 h?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
