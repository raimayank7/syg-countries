Kepler-1052 c: exoplanet

Neptune-like planet orbiting Kepler-1052.

Kepler-1052 c is a confirmed planet that orbits the star Kepler-1052. In the archive it is a Neptune-like planet, about 2.30 times the radius of Earth (29,307 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), with a mass of about 5.90 Earth masses. It was discovered in 2023 by the transit method, with Kepler.

How big is Kepler-1052 c?

Its radius is 2.30 Earth radii, so it is larger than Earth. With a mass of 5.90 Earth masses it packs 5.90 times Earth's mass into about 12.2 times its volume. The density comes out at 2.66 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That is in between, like a mix of rock and water or gas.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 181 days, so its year is 0.495 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25. Its average distance from the star is 0.614 AU, where 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun; Mercury is at 0.39 AU and Jupiter at 5.2 AU. It is farther out than Mercury but inside Earth's orbit.

Temperature and starlight

The archive gives an equilibrium temperature of 302 K (29 degrees Celsius). That is the temperature the planet would have if starlight were its only source of heat; it is not the temperature of a surface, and an atmosphere can change it a lot. This is within the range where water can be liquid, if the pressure is right. It receives 1.96 times the starlight that Earth gets from the Sun.

The star

The star is Kepler-1052, with a surface temperature of 5,818 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 0.848 times the Sun's, a mass of 0.937 times the Sun's. The system has 2 known planets. The planets of this star: Kepler-1052 b, Kepler-1052 c.

How far away is it?

The system is about 3,077 light years (943 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 3,077 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 54,261,568 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 14.9: far too faint to see without a large telescope.

Common questions

How was Kepler-1052 c discovered?

Kepler-1052 c was discovered in 2023 by the transit method using Kepler.

How long is a year on Kepler-1052 c?

It orbits its star every 181 days.

How far is Kepler-1052 c from Earth?

About 3,077 light years.

Could life exist on Kepler-1052 c?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
