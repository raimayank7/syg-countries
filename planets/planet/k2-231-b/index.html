K2-231 b: exoplanet

Neptune-like planet orbiting K2-231.

K2-231 b is a confirmed planet that orbits the star K2-231. In the archive it is a Neptune-like planet, about 2.50 times the radius of Earth (31,855 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), with a mass of about 6.80 Earth masses. It was discovered in 2018 by the transit method, with K2.

How big is K2-231 b?

Its radius is 2.50 Earth radii, so it is larger than Earth. With a mass of 6.80 Earth masses it packs 6.80 times Earth's mass into about 15.6 times its volume. The density comes out at 2.39 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That points to a world with a thick envelope of gas or water, not a bare rock.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 13.8 days, so its year is 0.0379 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25.

The star

The star is K2-231, with a surface temperature of 5,695 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 0.95 times the Sun's, a mass of 1.01 times the Sun's. The system has 1 known planet.

How far away is it?

The system is about 1,048 light years (321 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 1,048 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 18,474,472 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 12.8: far too faint to see without a large telescope.

Common questions

How was K2-231 b discovered?

K2-231 b was discovered in 2018 by the transit method using K2.

How long is a year on K2-231 b?

It orbits its star every 13.8 days.

How far is K2-231 b from Earth?

About 1,048 light years.

Could life exist on K2-231 b?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
