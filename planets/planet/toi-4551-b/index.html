TOI-4551 b is a confirmed planet that orbits the star TOI-4551. In the archive it is a gas giant, about 11.9 times the radius of Earth (151,109 km across at its widest, against Earth's 12,742 km), or 1.06 of Jupiter's radius, with a mass of about 474 Earth masses (1.49 times Jupiter's). It was discovered in 2023 by the transit method, with Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS).

How big is TOI-4551 b?

Its radius is 11.9 Earth radii, so it is much larger than Earth. That is 106% of Jupiter's radius. With a mass of 474 Earth masses it packs 474 times Earth's mass into about 1,668 times its volume. The density comes out at 1.74 g/cm3 (Earth: 5.51; water: 1.00). That points to a world with a thick envelope of gas or water, not a bare rock.

Orbit and year

It goes once around its star in 9.96 days, so its year is 0.0273 Earth years long. Mercury takes 88 days; Earth takes 365.25. Its average distance from the star is 0.0994 AU, where 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun; Mercury is at 0.39 AU and Jupiter at 5.2 AU. It is closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun. The orbit is slightly oval (eccentricity 0.101; a circle is 0).

Temperature and starlight

The archive gives an equilibrium temperature of 1,655 K (1,382 degrees Celsius). That is the temperature the planet would have if starlight were its only source of heat; it is not the temperature of a surface, and an atmosphere can change it a lot. This is hotter than the melting point of many rocks. It receives 1,248 times the starlight that Earth gets from the Sun.

The star

The star is TOI-4551, with a surface temperature of 4,896 K (the Sun: 5,772 K), a radius of 3.55 times the Sun's, a mass of 1.31 times the Sun's. The system has 1 known planet.

How far away is it?

The system is about 704 light years (216 parsecs) from us. Light from it left 704 years ago. A spacecraft travelling like Voyager 1, at about 17 km per second, would need roughly 12,422,294 years. The star has an apparent magnitude of 9.97: too faint for the naked eye, but within reach of binoculars or a small telescope.

Common questions

How was TOI-4551 b discovered?

TOI-4551 b was discovered in 2023 by the transit method using Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS).

How long is a year on TOI-4551 b?

It orbits its star every 9.96 days.

How far is TOI-4551 b from Earth?

About 704 light years.

Could life exist on TOI-4551 b?

This site does not say. Size, temperature and starlight are only part of what matters; nothing is known about its atmosphere or surface.
