Planets discovered by HATNet

The NASA Exoplanet Archive credits HATNet with the discovery of 67 confirmed planets, found between 2006 and 2021. The most used method was transit (67 planets). The table lists up to 40 of them, those with the most measured values first.

What the discovered planets are like

Of the 67 planets, 67 have a measured radius, 67 a measured mass and 67 an orbital period in the archive. The median radius is 14.3 Earth radii (smallest 4.36, largest 24.0). The median orbit takes 3.38 days. The median distance is 1,023 light years and the farthest is 3,051. By the simple size classes used on this site they are: 66 gas giants, 1 Neptune-like planets. They orbit 67 different stars. The size classes are a rough sort by radius or mass; the archive records only what was measured, so planets without a measured size are listed as plain planets.

The busiest year for this facility was 2010, with 12 planets credited. Its planets were found with transit (67). A facility is credited in the archive for the discovery paper, which can name a telescope, a spacecraft or a survey team, so counts of one facility are not directly comparable with those of another kind of facility.
