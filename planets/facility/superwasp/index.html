Planets discovered by SuperWASP

The NASA Exoplanet Archive credits SuperWASP with the discovery of 122 confirmed planets, found between 2007 and 2025. The most used method was transit (120 planets). The table lists up to 40 of them, those with the most measured values first.

What the discovered planets are like

Of the 122 planets, 122 have a measured radius, 122 a measured mass and 122 an orbital period in the archive. The median radius is 13.9 Earth radii (smallest 6.15, largest 22.0). The median orbit takes 3.41 days. The median distance is 878 light years and the farthest is 2,460. By the simple size classes used on this site they are: 122 gas giants. They orbit 121 different stars; 1 of those stars have more than one planet in this list. The size classes are a rough sort by radius or mass; the archive records only what was measured, so planets without a measured size are listed as plain planets.

The busiest year for this facility was 2012, with 23 planets credited. Its planets were found with transit (120), radial velocity (2). A facility is credited in the archive for the discovery paper, which can name a telescope, a spacecraft or a survey team, so counts of one facility are not directly comparable with those of another kind of facility.
