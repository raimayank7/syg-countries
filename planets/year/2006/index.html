Exoplanets discovered in 2006

32 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 32 confirmed planets as discovered in 2006, which brought the total to 204. Most were found by the radial velocity method (21 of them). Others used transit (5), imaging (5), microlensing (1). The facilities credited with most of them were W. M. Keck Observatory (8), La Silla Observatory (4), Anglo-Australian Telescope (4), Hubble Space Telescope (3).

The nearest of them is GJ 849 b, 28.7 light years away; the most massive is HN Peg b with about 6,992 Earth masses.
