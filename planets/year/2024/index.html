Exoplanets discovered in 2024

260 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 260 confirmed planets as discovered in 2024, which brought the total to 5,844. Most were found by the transit method (187 of them). Others used radial velocity (29), microlensing (28), imaging (11). The facilities credited with most of them were Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (178), Multiple Observatories (25), KMTNet (16), OGLE (9).

The nearest of them is Barnard b, 5.96 light years away; the most massive is 2MASS J11011926-7732383 b with about 8,899 Earth masses.

What the newly found planets are like

Of the 260 planets, 259 have a measured radius, 260 a measured mass and 222 an orbital period in the archive. The median radius is 3.68 Earth radii (smallest 0.4, largest 21.6). The median orbit takes 6.38 days. The median distance is 397 light years and the farthest is 26,190. By the simple size classes used on this site they are: 114 gas giants, 100 Neptune-like planets, 25 super-Earths, 21 Earth-sized planets. They orbit 232 different stars; 22 of those stars have more than one planet in this list. The size classes are a rough sort by radius or mass; the archive records only what was measured, so planets without a measured size are listed as plain planets.

That is 4.0% of all 6,470 confirmed planets in the archive. The year before, 2023, had 323. Discovery counts rise and fall with the telescopes in operation and with the years in which large batches of candidates were confirmed together, so a single year says little about the pace of the field.
