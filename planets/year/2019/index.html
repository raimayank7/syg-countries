Exoplanets discovered in 2019

194 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 194 confirmed planets as discovered in 2019, which brought the total to 4,096. Most were found by the transit method (107 of them). Others used radial velocity (65), microlensing (14), imaging (4). The facilities credited with most of them were K2 (38), Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (32), La Silla Observatory (31), Las Campanas Observatory (14).

The nearest of them is eps Ind A b, 11.9 light years away; the most massive is HD 125390 b with about 8,646 Earth masses.
