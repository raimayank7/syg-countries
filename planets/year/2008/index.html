Exoplanets discovered in 2008

62 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 62 confirmed planets as discovered in 2008, which brought the total to 318. Most were found by the radial velocity method (36 of them). Others used transit (15), imaging (7), microlensing (4). The facilities credited with most of them were La Silla Observatory (15), W. M. Keck Observatory (8), SuperWASP (6), CoRoT (4).

The nearest of them is GJ 832 b, 16.2 light years away; the most massive is CoRoT-3 b with about 6,884 Earth masses.
