Exoplanets discovered in 2013

128 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 128 confirmed planets as discovered in 2013, which brought the total to 911. Most were found by the transit method (79 of them). Others used radial velocity (34), imaging (7), microlensing (5). The facilities credited with most of them were Kepler (73), La Silla Observatory (17), Haute-Provence Observatory (6), Paranal Observatory (3).

The nearest of them is GJ 667 C c, 23.6 light years away; the most massive is DENIS-P J082303.1-491201 b with about 9,058 Earth masses.
