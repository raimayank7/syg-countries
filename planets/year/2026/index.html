Exoplanets discovered in 2026

380 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 380 confirmed planets as discovered in 2026, which brought the total to 6,470. Most were found by the transit method (292 of them). Others used radial velocity (46), microlensing (31), imaging (7). The facilities credited with most of them were Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (287), KMTNet (27), Multiple Observatories (21), Multiple Facilities (11).

The nearest of them is GJ 887 d, 10.7 light years away; the most massive is TYC 4998-437-1 b with about 9,157 Earth masses.
