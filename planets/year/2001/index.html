Exoplanets discovered in 2001

12 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 12 confirmed planets as discovered in 2001, which brought the total to 58. Most were found by the radial velocity method (12 of them). The facilities credited with most of them were W. M. Keck Observatory (4), Anglo-Australian Telescope (3), La Silla Observatory (2), Multiple Observatories (2).

The nearest of them is 47 UMa c, 45.0 light years away; the most massive is HD 39091 b with about 4,481 Earth masses.
