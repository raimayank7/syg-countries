Exoplanets discovered in 2018

308 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 308 confirmed planets as discovered in 2018, which brought the total to 3,902. Most were found by the transit method (244 of them). Others used radial velocity (38), microlensing (21), imaging (3). The facilities credited with most of them were K2 (191), HATSouth (21), SuperWASP-South (16), W. M. Keck Observatory (14).

The nearest of them is GJ 15 A c, 11.6 light years away; the most massive is 2MASS J22501512+2325342 b with about 9,535 Earth masses.
