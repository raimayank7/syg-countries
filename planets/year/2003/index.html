Exoplanets discovered in 2003

22 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 22 confirmed planets as discovered in 2003, which brought the total to 109. Most were found by the radial velocity method (21 of them). Others used pulsar timing (1). The facilities credited with most of them were La Silla Observatory (13), Haute-Provence Observatory (4), Multiple Observatories (2), Hubble Space Telescope (1).

The nearest of them is HD 3651 b, 36.3 light years away; the most massive is HD 41004 B b with about 5,838 Earth masses.
