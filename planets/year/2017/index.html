Exoplanets discovered in 2017

152 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 152 confirmed planets as discovered in 2017, which brought the total to 3,594. Most were found by the transit method (87 of them). Others used radial velocity (48), microlensing (10), imaging (3). The facilities credited with most of them were K2 (44), Multiple Observatories (28), La Silla Observatory (18), Kepler (9).

The nearest of them is Ross 128 b, 11.0 light years away; the most massive is Luhman 16 b with about 9,344 Earth masses.
