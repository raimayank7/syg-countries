Exoplanets discovered in 2000

16 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 16 confirmed planets as discovered in 2000, which brought the total to 46. Most were found by the radial velocity method (16 of them). The facilities credited with most of them were Multiple Observatories (6), W. M. Keck Observatory (5), Anglo-Australian Telescope (3), La Silla Observatory (2).

The nearest of them is eps Eri b, 10.4 light years away; the most massive is HD 168443 c with about 5,500 Earth masses.
