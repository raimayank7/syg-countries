Exoplanets discovered in 2021

564 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 564 confirmed planets as discovered in 2021, which brought the total to 4,894. Most were found by the transit method (457 of them). Others used radial velocity (77), microlensing (21), imaging (5). The facilities credited with most of them were Kepler (311), Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (77), K2 (50), Multiple Observatories (30).

The nearest of them is HD 95735 c, 18.5 light years away; the most massive is HD 26161 b with about 9,045 Earth masses.
