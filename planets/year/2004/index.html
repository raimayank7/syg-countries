Exoplanets discovered in 2004

27 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 27 confirmed planets as discovered in 2004, which brought the total to 136. Most were found by the radial velocity method (18 of them). Others used transit (5), imaging (3), microlensing (1). The facilities credited with most of them were W. M. Keck Observatory (7), OGLE (5), Anglo-Australian Telescope (4), La Silla Observatory (4).

The nearest of them is GJ 436 b, 31.8 light years away; the most massive is GQ Lup b with about 6,356 Earth masses.
