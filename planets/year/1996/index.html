Exoplanets discovered in 1996

6 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 6 confirmed planets as discovered in 1996, which brought the total to 10. Most were found by the radial velocity method (6 of them). The facilities credited with most of them were Lick Observatory (5), Multiple Observatories (1).

The nearest of them is 55 Cnc b, 41.0 light years away; the most massive is 70 Vir b with about 2,380 Earth masses.
