Exoplanets discovered in 2022

367 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 367 confirmed planets as discovered in 2022, which brought the total to 5,261. Most were found by the transit method (192 of them). Others used radial velocity (115), microlensing (51), imaging (4). The facilities credited with most of them were Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (112), Multiple Observatories (94), K2 (65), KMTNet (34).

The nearest of them is GJ 1002 b, 15.8 light years away; the most massive is HD 188641 b with about 9,333 Earth masses.
