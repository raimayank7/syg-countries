Exoplanets discovered in 2015

155 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 155 confirmed planets as discovered in 2015, which brought the total to 1,938. Most were found by the transit method (99 of them). Others used radial velocity (46), imaging (5), microlensing (4). The facilities credited with most of them were Kepler (46), K2 (28), Multiple Observatories (13), La Silla Observatory (11).

The nearest of them is Wolf 1061 c, 14.0 light years away; the most massive is VHS J125601.92-125723.9 b with about 5,085 Earth masses.
