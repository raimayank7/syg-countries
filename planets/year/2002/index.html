Exoplanets discovered in 2002

29 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 29 confirmed planets as discovered in 2002, which brought the total to 87. Most were found by the radial velocity method (28 of them). Others used transit (1). The facilities credited with most of them were W. M. Keck Observatory (8), Anglo-Australian Telescope (7), La Silla Observatory (5), Lick Observatory (4).

The nearest of them is 55 Cnc d, 41.0 light years away; the most massive is iot Dra b with about 3,757 Earth masses.
