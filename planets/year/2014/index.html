Exoplanets discovered in 2014

872 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 872 confirmed planets as discovered in 2014, which brought the total to 1,783. Most were found by the transit method (810 of them). Others used radial velocity (48), microlensing (6), orbital brightness modulation (3). The facilities credited with most of them were Kepler (784), Multiple Observatories (20), SuperWASP (20), W. M. Keck Observatory (13).

The nearest of them is GJ 15 A b, 11.6 light years away; the most massive is KOI-319 c with about 8,804 Earth masses.
