Exoplanets discovered in 2010

93 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 93 confirmed planets as discovered in 2010, which brought the total to 498. Most were found by the transit method (46 of them). Others used radial velocity (37), imaging (6), microlensing (2). The facilities credited with most of them were La Silla Observatory (19), SuperWASP (14), Kepler (12), HATNet (12).

The nearest of them is GJ 876 e, 15.2 light years away; the most massive is HIP 78530 b with about 7,300 Earth masses.
