Exoplanets discovered in 2025

246 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 246 confirmed planets as discovered in 2025, which brought the total to 6,090. Most were found by the transit method (144 of them). Others used radial velocity (62), microlensing (23), imaging (8). The facilities credited with most of them were Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) (129), La Silla Observatory (19), KMTNet (18), Multiple Observatories (17).

The nearest of them is Proxima Cen d, 4.24 light years away; the most massive is KMT-2024-BLG-0404L b with about 8,658 Earth masses.
