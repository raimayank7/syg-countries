Exoplanets discovered in 1999

13 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 13 confirmed planets as discovered in 1999, which brought the total to 30. Most were found by the radial velocity method (13 of them). The facilities credited with most of them were W. M. Keck Observatory (5), La Silla Observatory (5), Multiple Observatories (2), Fred Lawrence Whipple Observatory (1).

The nearest of them is GJ 86 b, 35.2 light years away; the most massive is ups And c with about 4,443 Earth masses.
