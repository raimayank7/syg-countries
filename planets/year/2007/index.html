Exoplanets discovered in 2007

52 new confirmed planets.

The archive lists 52 confirmed planets as discovered in 2007, which brought the total to 256. Most were found by the radial velocity method (34 of them). Others used transit (16), pulsation timing variations (1), imaging (1). The facilities credited with most of them were La Silla Observatory (11), W. M. Keck Observatory (8), HATNet (5), SuperWASP (4).

The nearest of them is GJ 674 b, 14.8 light years away; the most massive is CT Cha b with about 5,403 Earth masses.
