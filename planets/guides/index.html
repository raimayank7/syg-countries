Exoplanet guides

How to use this page

The three guides explain the ideas behind the exoplanet pages. One describes the main ways astronomers find planets around other stars and how many each method has found. One explains what an exoplanet is and how the planets are sorted by size on this site. One explains the habitable zone, why a planet in that zone is not necessarily habitable, and why the lists here use the starlight a planet receives. They are written for readers without a background in astronomy and use the counts in the NASA Exoplanet Archive as examples.

  • How exoplanets are found

    The main ways astronomers find planets around other stars and how many each has found, in plain words.

  • What is an exoplanet?

    What an exoplanet is, how astronomers group them by size, and how many confirmed planets are known today.

  • The habitable zone explained

    What the habitable zone is, why a planet in it is not necessarily habitable, and how the lists on this site use starlight instead.
