Exoplanets found by imaging: 98 planets

How the method works

Direct imaging blocks the glare of a star and photographs the planet itself. It works best for young, hot planets that are large and far from their stars.

What it has found

The archive lists 98 confirmed planets discovered by imaging, the first in 2004. The planets found this way that have a measured radius have a median radius of 12.4 Earth radii. The table shows up to 40 of them.
