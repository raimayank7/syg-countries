Exoplanets found by eclipse timing variations: 17 planets

How the method works

In a pair of stars that eclipse each other, the timing of eclipses can drift if a planet orbits them and pulls on them.

What it has found

The archive lists 17 confirmed planets discovered by eclipse timing variations, the first in 2009. The planets found this way that have a measured radius have a median radius of 12.9 Earth radii. The table shows up to 40 of them.
