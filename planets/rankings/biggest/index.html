The biggest exoplanets by radius

The largest confirmed planets measured by radius.

Planets cannot grow larger than Jupiter by much: beyond about 1.2 Jupiter radii, extra mass squeezes a gas planet rather than enlarging it, so the largest planets are mostly hot ones puffed up by heat from their stars.

50 planets are listed. The values are the archive's preferred values, and planets flagged as controversial are left out. Open a planet for the full story.
