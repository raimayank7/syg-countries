The nearest exoplanets

The confirmed exoplanets closest to us, nearest first.

The nearest known planets orbit small, dim stars within a few light years of the Sun. Being near does not make them easy to study: planets are found when they pass in front of their star or tug at it, and both signals are weak.

50 planets are listed. The values are the archive's preferred values, and planets flagged as controversial are left out. Open a planet for the full story.

How to read this list

6,372 of the 6,396 planets with preferred values in the archive have a measured distance, and only they can appear here. Across all of them the lowest value is 4.24, the middle value 1,143 and the highest 27,723. The 50 planets in this list were found by radial velocity (50), in the years 1998 to 2026 (the middle year is 2019). A list like this shows what current methods can measure: the methods favour planets that are large, close to their stars or close to us, so the extremes depend on how planets are found as well as on what exists. Values are the archive's preferred ones and change as measurements improve.
