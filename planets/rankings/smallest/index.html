The smallest exoplanets by radius

The smallest confirmed planets measured by radius.

Small planets are hard to find because they block very little starlight. The smallest listed here are about the size of Mars or smaller.

50 planets are listed. The values are the archive's preferred values, and planets flagged as controversial are left out. Open a planet for the full story.

How to read this list

6,358 of the 6,396 planets with preferred values in the archive have a measured radius, and only they can appear here. Across all of them the lowest value is 0.31, the middle value 2.86 and the highest 87.2. The 50 planets in this list were found by transit (45), radial velocity (4), pulsar timing (1), in the years 1994 to 2025 (the middle year is 2016). A list like this shows what current methods can measure: the methods favour planets that are large, close to their stars or close to us, so the extremes depend on how planets are found as well as on what exists. Values are the archive's preferred ones and change as measurements improve.
