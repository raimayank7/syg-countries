The hottest exoplanets

The confirmed planets with the highest equilibrium temperature.

The hottest planets orbit extremely close to bright, hot stars. Their equilibrium temperature, from starlight alone, exceeds the temperature at which many metals melt.

50 planets are listed. The values are the archive's preferred values, and planets flagged as controversial are left out. Open a planet for the full story.

How to read this list

5,883 of the 6,396 planets with preferred values in the archive have a measured equilibrium temperature, and only they can appear here. Across all of them the lowest value is 1.48, the middle value 760 and the highest 3,921. The 50 planets in this list were found by transit (39), imaging (11), in the years 2004 to 2026 (the middle year is 2017). A list like this shows what current methods can measure: the methods favour planets that are large, close to their stars or close to us, so the extremes depend on how planets are found as well as on what exists. Values are the archive's preferred ones and change as measurements improve.
