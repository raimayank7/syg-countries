Small planets that get Earth-like starlight

Planets under twice the radius of Earth that receive between a quarter and one and a half times the starlight Earth gets.

This list uses a simple rule and nothing more: a radius under twice Earth's and between 0.25 and 1.5 times Earth's starlight. It says nothing about air, water or life, and the starlight values depend on each star's measured properties. They are places worth studying, not promises.

45 planets are listed. The values are the archive's preferred values, and planets flagged as controversial are left out. Open a planet for the full story.
