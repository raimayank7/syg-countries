Bolivia

Bolivia is a country in South America. Its official name is Plurinational State of Bolivia. In the languages of the place it is called Wuliwya, Volívia, Buliwya. The capital is Sucre.

Land and position

Bolivia covers about 1,098,580 square kilometres. By area it ranks 29th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Ethiopia and a little larger than Mauritania. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Peru, Paraguay. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 17° south, 65° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 12.6 million people live in Bolivia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 79th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.15 percent of the world's people. On average there are 11 people to each square kilometre. About 72 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 69 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, Quechua, Aymara. A person from Bolivia is called Bolivian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Bolivian boliviano (code BOB, symbol Bs.). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 64.8 billion (2025), which is about US$ 5,148 for each person (2025).

Time

Bolivia uses one time zone, UTC-4:00 (America/La Paz). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
