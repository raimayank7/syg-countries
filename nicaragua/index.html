Nicaragua

Nicaragua is a country in Central America. Its official name is Republic of Nicaragua. The capital is Managua.

Land and position

Nicaragua covers about 129,494 square kilometres. By area it ranks 99th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Greece and a little larger than Eritrea. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Costa Rica, Honduras. Its centre lies at about 13° north, 85° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 7.01 million people live in Nicaragua (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 109th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 54 people to each square kilometre. About 59 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 75 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, English. A person from Nicaragua is called Nicaraguan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Nicaraguan córdoba (code NIO, symbol C$). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 22.2 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,173 for each person (2025).

Time

Nicaragua uses one time zone, UTC-6:00 (America/Managua). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
