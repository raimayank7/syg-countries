The files of the five regional registries list 262,651 IPv4 address blocks and 71,130 IPv6 prefixes allocated or assigned to organisations in 239 countries and regions. This site turns them into one page per country with the ranges in CIDR form. Please read why the country of an address is not the place where someone is before you use any of it.

Data: delegated-extended statistics files of APNIC, ARIN, RIPE NCC, LACNIC and AFRINIC, downloaded 14 November 2026. Shows where blocks were first allocated, not where they are used. Independent site, not connected with the registries.
