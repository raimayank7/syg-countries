LACNIC: allocations by country

181,930,752

IPv4 addresses allocated or assigned, as listed in its file.

LACNIC is one of the five regional internet registries. Its service region is generally described as Latin America and parts of the Caribbean; the website is https://www.lacnic.net/. Its file lists 181,930,752 IPv4 addresses (181.9 million) allocated or assigned in 20,463 records, and 13,607 IPv6 records, to organisations in 30 countries and regions. In the same file, 256 IPv4 addresses are marked available (not yet allocated) at the time of reading. Note that a registry also lists some blocks to countries outside its usual region, for example where an organisation moved or where an older allocation was transferred.
