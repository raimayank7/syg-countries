APNIC: allocations by country

888,875,200

IPv4 addresses allocated or assigned, as listed in its file.

APNIC is one of the five regional internet registries. Its service region is generally described as the Asia Pacific region; the website is https://www.apnic.net/. Its file lists 888,875,200 IPv4 addresses (888.9 million) allocated or assigned in 58,392 records, and 17,332 IPv6 records, to organisations in 80 countries and regions. In the same file, 2,899,520 IPv4 addresses are marked available (not yet allocated) at the time of reading. Note that a registry also lists some blocks to countries outside its usual region, for example where an organisation moved or where an older allocation was transferred.
