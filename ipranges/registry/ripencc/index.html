RIPE NCC: allocations by country

856,137,120

IPv4 addresses allocated or assigned, as listed in its file.

RIPE NCC is one of the five regional internet registries. Its service region is generally described as Europe, the Middle East and parts of Central Asia; the website is https://www.ripe.net/. Its file lists 856,137,120 IPv4 addresses (856.1 million) allocated or assigned in 100,895 records, and 26,893 IPv6 records, to organisations in 127 countries and regions. In the same file, 4,096 IPv4 addresses are marked available (not yet allocated) at the time of reading. Note that a registry also lists some blocks to countries outside its usual region, for example where an organisation moved or where an older allocation was transferred.
