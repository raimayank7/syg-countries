AFRINIC: allocations by country

116,155,392

IPv4 addresses allocated or assigned, as listed in its file.

AFRINIC is one of the five regional internet registries. Its service region is generally described as Africa; the website is https://afrinic.net/. Its file lists 116,155,392 IPv4 addresses (116.2 million) allocated or assigned in 5,545 records, and 1,693 IPv6 records, to organisations in 56 countries and regions. In the same file, 574,208 IPv4 addresses are marked available (not yet allocated) at the time of reading. Note that a registry also lists some blocks to countries outside its usual region, for example where an organisation moved or where an older allocation was transferred.
