ARIN: allocations by country

1,644,502,272

IPv4 addresses allocated or assigned, as listed in its file.

ARIN is one of the five regional internet registries. Its service region is generally described as North America and parts of the Caribbean; the website is https://www.arin.net/. Its file lists 1,644,502,272 IPv4 addresses (1.64 billion) allocated or assigned in 77,356 records, and 11,605 IPv6 records, to organisations in 60 countries and regions. In the same file, 0 IPv4 addresses are marked available (not yet allocated) at the time of reading. Note that a registry also lists some blocks to countries outside its usual region, for example where an organisation moved or where an older allocation was transferred.
