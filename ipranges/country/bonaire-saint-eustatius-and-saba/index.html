With a population of about 18,012 (GeoNames) that is about 143.55 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Common questions

How many IP addresses does Bonaire, Saint Eustatius and Saba have?

The registries list 25,856 IPv4 addresses in 13 allocation records for Bonaire, Saint Eustatius and Saba and 6 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for Bonaire, Saint Eustatius and Saba ?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
