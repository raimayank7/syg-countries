IP address ranges in Norway

15,535,376

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in Norway.

The five regional registries list 15,535,376 IPv4 addresses (15.5 million) in 1,330 allocation records for Norway, which is 0.42% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 0.36% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 30th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 457 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 5,610,870 (GeoNames) that is about 276.88 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

RIPE NCC listed 15,462,928 addresses (99.5%). ARIN listed 66,816 addresses (0.4%). APNIC listed 5,632 addresses (0.0%).

The largest single block

The largest block is 139.105.0.0/16, 139.106.0.0/15, 139.108.0.0/14 with 1,048,576 addresses, first allocated on 1990-02-21 by RIPE NCC.

Common questions

How many IP addresses does Norway have?

The registries list 15,535,376 IPv4 addresses in 1,330 allocation records for Norway and 457 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for Norway?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
