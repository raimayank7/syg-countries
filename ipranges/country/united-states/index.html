IP address ranges in United States

1,606,587,040

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in United States.

The five regional registries list 1,606,587,040 IPv4 addresses (1.61 billion) in 70,572 allocation records for United States, which is 43.57% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 37.41% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 1st of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 10,913 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 341,784,857 (GeoNames) that is about 470.06 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

ARIN listed 1,574,308,096 addresses (98.0%). RIPE NCC listed 29,031,072 addresses (1.8%). APNIC listed 3,245,824 addresses (0.2%). LACNIC listed 2,048 addresses (0.0%).

The largest single block

The largest block is 6.0.0.0/8 with 16,777,216 addresses, first allocated on 1994-02-01 by ARIN.

Common questions

How many IP addresses does United States have?

The registries list 1,606,587,040 IPv4 addresses in 70,572 allocation records for United States and 10,913 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for United States?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
