IP address ranges in United Kingdom

138,750,208

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in United Kingdom.

The five regional registries list 138,750,208 IPv4 addresses (138.8 million) in 9,129 allocation records for United Kingdom, which is 3.76% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 3.23% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 4th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 2,032 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 69,487,000 (GeoNames) that is about 199.68 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

RIPE NCC listed 138,636,800 addresses (99.9%). ARIN listed 89,088 addresses (0.1%). APNIC listed 24,320 addresses (0.0%).

The largest single block

The largest block is 25.0.0.0/8 with 16,777,216 addresses, first allocated on 1995-01-01 by RIPE NCC.

Common questions

How many IP addresses does United Kingdom have?

The registries list 138,750,208 IPv4 addresses in 9,129 allocation records for United Kingdom and 2,032 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for United Kingdom?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
