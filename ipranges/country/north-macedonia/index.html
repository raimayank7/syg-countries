IP address ranges in North Macedonia

685,568

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in North Macedonia.

The five regional registries list 685,568 IPv4 addresses (686 thousand) in 147 allocation records for North Macedonia, which is 0.02% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 0.02% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 100th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 42 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 1,820,909 (GeoNames) that is about 37.65 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

RIPE NCC listed 685,568 addresses (100.0%).

Common questions

How many IP addresses does North Macedonia have?

The registries list 685,568 IPv4 addresses in 147 allocation records for North Macedonia and 42 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for North Macedonia?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
