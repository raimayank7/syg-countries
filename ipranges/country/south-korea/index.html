IP address ranges in South Korea

112,498,496

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in South Korea.

The five regional registries list 112,498,496 IPv4 addresses (112.5 million) in 2,431 allocation records for South Korea, which is 3.05% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 2.62% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 6th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 183 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 51,684,564 (GeoNames) that is about 217.66 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

APNIC listed 112,474,944 addresses (100.0%). ARIN listed 17,920 addresses (0.0%). RIPE NCC listed 5,632 addresses (0.0%).

Common questions

How many IP addresses does South Korea have?

The registries list 112,498,496 IPv4 addresses in 2,431 allocation records for South Korea and 183 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for South Korea?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
