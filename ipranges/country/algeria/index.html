IP address ranges in Algeria

4,768,256

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in Algeria.

The five regional registries list 4,768,256 IPv4 addresses (4.8 million) in 37 allocation records for Algeria, which is 0.13% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 0.11% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 57th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 9 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 47,435,312 (GeoNames) that is about 10.05 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

AFRINIC listed 4,768,256 addresses (100.0%).

Common questions

How many IP addresses does Algeria have?

The registries list 4,768,256 IPv4 addresses in 37 allocation records for Algeria and 9 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for Algeria?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
