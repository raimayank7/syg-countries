IP address ranges in British Virgin Islands

910,848

IPv4 addresses first allocated to organisations in British Virgin Islands.

The five regional registries list 910,848 IPv4 addresses (911 thousand) in 200 allocation records for British Virgin Islands, which is 0.02% of all allocated IPv4 addresses and 0.02% of the 4,294,967,296 IPv4 addresses that exist in total, which puts it 94th of 239 countries and regions by IPv4 addresses. It also holds 48 IPv6 allocation records.

With a population of about 39,732 (GeoNames) that is about 2292.48 IPv4 addresses for every 100 people in the country’s allocations. This is a measure of allocation, not of use: addresses are shared by many people through routers and mobile networks, and a large share of any country’s addresses sits with companies that serve users elsewhere.

Which registry listed them

RIPE NCC listed 870,656 addresses (95.6%). ARIN listed 36,608 addresses (4.0%). APNIC listed 3,584 addresses (0.4%).

The largest single block

The largest block is 177.28.0.0/15 with 131,072 addresses, first allocated on 2011-01-05 by RIPE NCC.

Common questions

How many IP addresses does British Virgin Islands have?

The registries list 910,848 IPv4 addresses in 200 allocation records for British Virgin Islands and 48 IPv6 records.

Does this show where an address is used?

No. It shows the country of the organisation that first received the block. Blocks are rented, sold and used across borders.

Where can I get the list of ranges for British Virgin Islands?

Each page links a plain text file of the IPv4 ranges in CIDR form and one of IPv6 prefixes. Check the date of the file before you rely on it for a firewall rule: allocations change and the country of allocation is not the country of use.
