Brazil

Brazil is a country in South America. Its official name is Federative Republic of Brazil. In the language of the place it is called Brasil. The capital is Brasília.

Land and position

Brazil covers about 8,511,965 square kilometres. By area it ranks 6th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than China and a little larger than Australia. It shares a land border with 10 neighbours: Argentina, Bolivia, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Peru, Paraguay, Suriname, Uruguay, Venezuela. Its centre lies at about 10° south, 55° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 213 million people live in Brazil (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 7th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 2.6 percent of the world's people. On average there are 25 people to each square kilometre. About 88 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 76 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Portuguese, Spanish, English, French. A person from Brazil is called Brazilian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Brazilian real (code BRL, symbol R$). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 2.28 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 10,713 for each person (2025).

Time

Brazil spans 16 time zone areas, from UTC-5:00 to UTC-2:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
