Qatar

Qatar is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is State of Qatar. In the language of the place it is called قطر. The capital is Doha.

Land and position

Qatar covers about 11,437 square kilometres. By area it ranks 166th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Falkland Islands and a little larger than Gambia. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: Saudi Arabia. Its centre lies at about 26° north, 51° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 2.97 million people live in Qatar (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 139th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 260 people to each square kilometre. About 99 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 83 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, Spanish. A person from Qatar is called Qatari.

Money and economy

The currency is the Qatari riyal (code QAR, symbol ر.ق). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 216 billion (2025), which is about US$ 72,525 for each person (2025).

Time

Qatar uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Asia/Qatar). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
