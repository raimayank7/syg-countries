Pakistan

Pakistan is a country in Southern Asia. Its official name is Islamic Republic of Pakistan. In the language of the place it is called پاكستان. The capital is Islamabad.

Land and position

Pakistan covers about 803,940 square kilometres. By area it ranks 36th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Namibia and a little larger than Mozambique. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Afghanistan, China, India, Iran. Its centre lies at about 30° north, 70° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 255 million people live in Pakistan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 5th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 3.1 percent of the world's people. On average there are 317 people to each square kilometre. About 40 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Urdu, English, Panjabi, Sindhi, Pushto, Brahui. A person from Pakistan is called Pakistani.

Money and economy

The currency is the Pakistani rupee (code PKR, symbol ₨). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 407 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,596 for each person (2025).

Time

Pakistan uses one time zone, UTC+5:00 (Asia/Karachi). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
