Czechia

The Czechia is a country in Central Europe. Its official name is Czech Republic. In the language of the place it is called Česko. The capital is Prague.

Land and position

The Czechia covers about 78,866 square kilometres. By area it ranks 119th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than United Arab Emirates and a little larger than Panama. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Austria, Germany, Poland, Slovakia. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 50° north, 16° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 10.9 million people live in the Czechia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 89th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.13 percent of the world's people. On average there are 138 people to each square kilometre. About 73 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 80 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Czech, Slovak.

Money and economy

The currency is the Czech koruna (code CZK, symbol Kč). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 391 billion (2025), which is about US$ 35,917 for each person (2025).

Time

The Czechia uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Europe/Prague). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
