Mauritania

Mauritania is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Islamic Republic of Mauritania. In the language of the place it is called موريتانيا. The capital is Nouakchott.

Land and position

Mauritania covers about 1,030,700 square kilometres. By area it ranks 30th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Bolivia and a little larger than Egypt. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Algeria, Western Sahara, Mali, Senegal. Its centre lies at about 20° north, 12° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 5.32 million people live in Mauritania (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 126th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 5.2 people to each square kilometre. About 61 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 69 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, Pulaar, Soninke, French, Hassaniyya, Wolof. A person from Mauritania is called Mauritanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Mauritanian ouguiya (code MRU, symbol UM). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 11.7 billion (2025), which is about US$ 2,198 for each person (2025).

Time

Mauritania uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Nouakchott). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
