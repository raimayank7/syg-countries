Land and position

Antarctica covers about 14,000,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 2nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Russia and a little larger than Canada. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 90° south, 0° east, in the southern hemisphere.
