Uganda

Uganda is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Uganda. The capital is Kampala.

Land and position

Uganda covers about 236,040 square kilometres. By area it ranks 85th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Laos and a little larger than Guyana. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: DR Congo, Kenya, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 1° north, 32° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 51.4 million people live in Uganda (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 31st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.63 percent of the world's people. On average there are 218 people to each square kilometre. About 32 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Ganda, Swahili (macrolanguage), Arabic. A person from Uganda is called Ugandan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Ugandan shilling (code UGX, symbol Sh). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 62 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,206 for each person (2025).

Time

Uganda uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Africa/Kampala). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
