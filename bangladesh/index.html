Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a country in Southern Asia. Its official name is People's Republic of Bangladesh. In the language of the place it is called বাংলাদেশ. The capital is Dhaka.

Land and position

Bangladesh covers about 144,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 95th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Suriname and a little larger than Tajikistan. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: India, Myanmar. Its centre lies at about 24° north, 90° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 176 million people live in Bangladesh (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 8th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 2.1 percent of the world's people. On average there are 1,220 people to each square kilometre. About 33 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 75 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Bengali, English. A person from Bangladesh is called Bangladeshi.

Money and economy

The currency is the Bangladeshi taka (code BDT, symbol ৳). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 456 billion (2025), which is about US$ 2,597 for each person (2025).

Time

Bangladesh uses one time zone, UTC+6:00 (Asia/Dhaka). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
