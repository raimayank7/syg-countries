Generic domain extensions (gTLDs)

1265 listed.

Generic extensions are not tied to a country. They include the old ones (.com, .org, .net, .info), the sponsored ones for a community such as .museum or .aero, and the ones added since 2012 for words, places, professions and brands. Many of the newer ones are brand extensions that a company runs for itself and that no outsider can register. The list below is long; use the filter on the full table to find one.
