.sd is a country-code top-level domain (ccTLD). It stands for Sudan, and its two letters come from the international country-code list (ISO 3166). It is run by Sudan Internet Society, based in Sudan.

Who runs .sd

The registry (the manager that IANA lists for it) is Sudan Internet Society. It also runs .سودان. Its website is www.isoc.sd; that is the place to read the rules for who may register a name, which documents are needed and what the extension costs through each registrar.

How to look up a .sd domain

Registration data for a domain under .sd is published by its registry. The WHOIS server is whois.nic.sd; from a command line, whois -h whois.nic.sd example.sd asks it about a name. The RDAP service (the newer, structured replacement for WHOIS) has the base address https://rdap.nic.sd; add domain/example.sd to it to ask about one name. Many registries hide the personal details of the registrant of a name, so a lookup may show only the registrar, the dates and the status. See WHOIS and RDAP for how to read the answer.

Name servers and DNSSEC

IANA lists 7 name servers for .sd, 3 of them reachable over IPv6 as well as IPv4. There is no DS record for it in the root zone, so DNSSEC validation does not reach names under this extension. See the guide to DNSSEC and DS records.

Should you use .sd?

A country-code extension is a strong signal of the country it belongs to. Google says that country-code extensions are tied to a specific country and so tell search engines that a site is meant for it, while it treats a few vanity ones (such as .tv and .me) as generic. Registries differ in who may register: some are open to anyone, others require a local presence, a local company or documents. Read the rules on the registry's website (linked above) before you choose a name you cannot use.

Official records

IANA lists these documents for the extension: IANA Report on Redelegation of the .SD Top-Level Domain (2002-12-20).

Common questions

Who runs .sd?

.sd is run by Sudan Internet Society, based in Sudan.

Is .sd a country-code extension?

Yes. .sd is the country-code extension of Sudan.

How can I look up who a .sd domain is registered to?

Ask the WHOIS server whois.nic.sd or the RDAP service https://rdap.nic.sd. Personal details are often hidden.

Does .sd support DNSSEC?

IANA lists no DS record for .sd, so it is not signed at the root.
