Amazon Registry Services, Inc.: 50 domain extensions

Amazon Registry Services, Inc. is the registry that IANA lists as manager of 50 active top-level domains: .amazon, .audible, .author, .book, .bot, .call, .coupon, .deal and more. The business address on record is in the United States of America. A registry keeps the master list of every name under an extension and publishes the data that makes the names work. It does not usually sell to the public; people buy through a registrar, which pays the registry a wholesale fee.

The extensions were delegated between 2015 and 2020.

Its website: www.nic.amazon.

How it is run

Of its 50 extensions, 50 are signed with DNSSEC and 50 have name servers reachable over IPv6. WHOIS lookups go to whois.nic.coupon, whois.nic.song, whois.nic.xn--1ck2e1b, whois.nic.xn--bck1b9a5dre4c. and others. Its RDAP service is at rdap.identitydigital.services, rdap.nominet.uk. Each extension has its own page with the full details.
