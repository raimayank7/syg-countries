Deutsche Vermögensberatung Aktiengesellschaft DVAG: 4 domain extensions

Deutsche Vermögensberatung Aktiengesellschaft DVAG is the registry that IANA lists as manager of 4 active top-level domains: .dvag, .pohl, .vermögensberater, .vermögensberatung. The business address on record is in Germany. A registry keeps the master list of every name under an extension and publishes the data that makes the names work. It does not usually sell to the public; people buy through a registrar, which pays the registry a wholesale fee.

The extensions were delegated between 2014 and 2014.

Its website: www.dvag-registry.de.

How it is run

Of its 4 extensions, 4 are signed with DNSSEC and 4 have name servers reachable over IPv6. WHOIS lookups go to whois.nic.dvag, whois.nic.pohl, whois.nic.xn--vermgensberater-ctb, whois.nic.xn--vermgensberatung-pwb. Its RDAP service is at rdap.centralnic.com. Each extension has its own page with the full details.
