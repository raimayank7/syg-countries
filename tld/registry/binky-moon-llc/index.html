Binky Moon, LLC: 196 domain extensions

Binky Moon, LLC is the registry that IANA lists as manager of 196 active top-level domains: .academy, .accountants, .agency, .apartments, .associates, .bargains, .bike, .bingo and more. The business address on record is in the United States of America. A registry keeps the master list of every name under an extension and publishes the data that makes the names work. It does not usually sell to the public; people buy through a registrar, which pays the registry a wholesale fee.

The extensions were delegated between 2013 and 2016.

Its website: www.identity.digital.

How it is run

Of its 196 extensions, 196 are signed with DNSSEC and 196 have name servers reachable over IPv6. IANA lists no WHOIS server for it. Its RDAP service is at rdap.identitydigital.services. Each extension has its own page with the full details.
