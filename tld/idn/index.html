Domain extensions in other scripts: 151 internationalised TLDs

Domain names were first limited to the letters A to Z, digits and hyphens. Since 2010 the root zone also holds extensions written in other scripts, so that an address can be typed completely in Hindi, Arabic, Chinese or Cyrillic. IANA lists 151 active ones: Han 59, Arabic 32, Cyrillic 17, Katakana 8, Devanagari 6, Hangul 4. Behind every one is an ASCII form that starts with "xn--"; that is what the DNS actually uses, and browsers show you the native form.

This page groups them by script. Open any to see who runs it. If you want to understand the "xn--" form, read the guide to IDN and punycode.
