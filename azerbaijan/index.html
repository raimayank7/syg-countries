Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Republic of Azerbaijan. In the languages of the place it is called Azərbaycan, Азербайджан. The capital is Baku.

Land and position

Azerbaijan covers about 86,600 square kilometres. By area it ranks 116th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Serbia and a little larger than Austria. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Armenia, Georgia, Iran, Russia, Türkiye. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 40° north, 48° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 10.2 million people live in Azerbaijan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 95th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.12 percent of the world's people. On average there are 118 people to each square kilometre. About 59 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 75 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Azerbaijani, Russian, Armenian. A person from Azerbaijan is called Azerbaijani.

Money and economy

The currency is the Azerbaijani manat (code AZN, symbol ₼). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 75.9 billion (2025), which is about US$ 7,411 for each person (2025).

Time

Azerbaijan uses one time zone, UTC+4:00 (Asia/Baku). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
