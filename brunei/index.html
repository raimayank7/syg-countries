Brunei

Brunei is a country in South-Eastern Asia. Its official name is Nation of Brunei, Abode of Peace. In the language of the place it is called Negara Brunei Darussalam. The capital is Bandar Seri Begawan.

Land and position

Brunei covers about 5,770 square kilometres. By area it ranks 175th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Palestine and a little larger than Trinidad and Tobago. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: Malaysia. Its centre lies at about 4° north, 115° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 466,330 people live in Brunei (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 175th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 81 people to each square kilometre. About 75 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 76 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Malay (macrolanguage), English. A person from Brunei is called Bruneian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Brunei dollar (code BND, symbol $). Also listed as used here: Singapore dollar (SGD). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 15 billion (2025), which is about US$ 32,235 for each person (2025).

Time

Brunei uses one time zone, UTC+8:00 (Asia/Brunei). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
