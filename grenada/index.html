Land and position

Grenada covers about 344 square kilometres. By area it ranks 213th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than United States Virgin Islands and a little larger than Caribbean Netherlands. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 12° north, 62° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 117,303 people live in Grenada (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 195th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 341 people to each square kilometre. About 37 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 75 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English. A person from Grenada is called Grenadian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Eastern Caribbean dollar (code XCD, symbol $). It is also the main currency of Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Dominica, Montserrat, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.42 billion (2025), which is about US$ 12,107 for each person (2025).

Time

Grenada uses one time zone, UTC-4:00 (America/Grenada). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
