Estonia VAT number (KMKR) check: format and check digit

Type a number to check its shape and check digit. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Estonia VAT number (KMKR): the check on this page tests whether a number has the right shape and a correct check digit. Estonia's VAT number (KMKR).

What the number looks like

The letters EE, then 9 digits starting with 10 (the two letters in front are the country code used in EU VAT numbers; the number is often written without them within its own country).

How the check digit works

The first eight digits are multiplied by 3, 7, 1, 3, 7, 1, 3, 7 and added. The check digit is the amount that brings the sum up to the next multiple of 10.

Worked example

Take the example number EE108906747. The digits used and their weights:

Digit10890674
Weight37137137
Product30827062128

Sum = 93; the next multiple of 10 is 100, so the check digit is 7; the last digit is 7.

Examples that pass the check

These numbers were constructed from random digits and pass the check; they are not real businesses. Tap one to test it.

Check that it really exists

A number that passes the check can still be unregistered, cancelled or belong to someone else. To confirm that a business holds this number, use VIES, the European Commission's VAT number check: https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/. Ask the business for the number and confirm the name and address that the register shows.

Common mistakes

  • Typing letters O or I for zeros and ones.
  • Adding or dropping the country code.
  • Copying spaces and dots from an invoice (this tool removes them).

Common questions

Is a number that passes the check a real Estonia VAT number (KMKR)?

Not necessarily. The check shows that the number is possible. About one in ten random numbers of the right length pass a typical check digit. Use VIES, the European Commission's VAT number check to confirm a registration.

Is my number sent anywhere when I check it?

No. The check runs in your browser; nothing is sent to this site or anyone else.

Why does a number I know is valid fail?

Most often a digit was mistyped or the number belongs to a variant this check does not cover. See the notes on this page, and use the official service.

Checks the shape and check digit only. It does not show that a number is registered: use the official service. Independent site, not connected with any tax authority.
