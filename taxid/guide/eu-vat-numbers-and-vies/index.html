EU VAT numbers and VIES

A business registered for VAT in an EU country has a VAT number: a two-letter country code (Greece uses EL) followed by a national number. Each country builds the national number its own way; this site covers 21 EU countries plus Norway, Switzerland and the UK.

VIES

VIES (VAT Information Exchange System) is the European Commission's service for asking whether a VAT number is currently valid for cross-border trade within the EU. It queries the national databases and answers valid or not valid, and for some countries the business name and address. It is the check to use when you sell goods to a business in another EU country and want to charge no VAT: keep a record of the answer. Link: ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies.

How this site fits

This site only checks the number's shape and check digit, instantly and in your browser. Use it first to catch typing mistakes; then confirm with VIES. A number can pass here and fail VIES (not registered, deregistered, or not registered for cross-border trade).

Norway, Switzerland and (since 2021) most UK numbers are not in VIES: use each country's own register, linked on its page. Which rules apply to your sale or purchase is a matter for a tax adviser or your tax authority.
