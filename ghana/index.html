Ghana

Ghana is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Ghana. The capital is Accra.

Land and position

Ghana covers about 239,460 square kilometres. By area it ranks 82nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than United Kingdom and a little larger than Romania. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Togo. Its centre lies at about 8° north, 2° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 35.1 million people live in Ghana (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 47th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.43 percent of the world's people. On average there are 146 people to each square kilometre. About 59 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 66 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Akan, Ewe, Twi. A person from Ghana is called Ghanaian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Ghanaian cedi (code GHS, symbol ₵). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 114 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,257 for each person (2025).

Time

Ghana uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Accra). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
