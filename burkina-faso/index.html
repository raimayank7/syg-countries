Land and position

Burkina Faso covers about 274,200 square kilometres. By area it ranks 75th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Philippines and a little larger than New Zealand. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Benin, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Mali, Niger, Togo. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 13° north, 2° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 24.1 million people live in Burkina Faso (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 59th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.29 percent of the world's people. On average there are 88 people to each square kilometre. About 29 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 61 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Mossi. A person from Burkina Faso is called Burkinabe.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Mali, Niger, Senegal, Togo. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 27.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,148 for each person (2025).

Time

Burkina Faso uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Ouagadougou). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
