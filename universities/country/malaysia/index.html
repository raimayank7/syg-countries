Universities in Malaysia

Wikidata lists 106 universities in Malaysia. For 94 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1816 (Penang Free School) and the latest 2015 (Asia School of Business). 4 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is International Islamic University Malaysia (29,501 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Kuala Lumpur (2), Tanjung Malim (1), Kota Kinabalu (1), Durian Tunggal (1), Kampar District (1), Kota Samarahan (1).
