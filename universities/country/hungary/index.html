Universities in Hungary

Wikidata lists 29 universities in Hungary. For 29 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1531 (University of Tokaj) and the latest 2012 (National University of Public Service). 3 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of Pécs (20,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Budapest (3), Kecskemét (1), Székesfehérvár (1), Budapest District VIII (1), Dunaújváros (1), Miskolc (1), Pécs (1).
