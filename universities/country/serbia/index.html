Universities in Serbia

Wikidata lists 37 universities in Serbia. For 31 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1937 (University of Belgrade Faculty of Economics) and the latest 2019 (Academy of Vocational Studies Belgrade). 3 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of Priština (North Mitrovica) (9,740 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Belgrade (5), Novi Sad (2), Novi Pazar (2), Savski Venac City Municipality (1), Niš (1), Mitrovica (1).
