Universities in Uzbekistan

Wikidata lists 68 universities in Uzbekistan. For 46 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1918 (National University of Uzbekistan named after Mirzo Ulugbek) and the latest 2025 (Al-Khwarizmi University). 9 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Uzbek State World Language University (30,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Urgench (2), Alisher Navoiy university building (1), Tashkent (1), Tashkent University of Information Technologies building (1), Bukhara (1).
