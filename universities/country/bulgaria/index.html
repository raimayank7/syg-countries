Universities in Bulgaria

Wikidata lists 34 universities in Bulgaria. For 32 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1881 (Nikola Vaptsarov Naval Academy) and the latest 2010 (European Polytechnical University). 9 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Plovdiv University (18,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Sofia (4), Blagoevgrad (1), Burgas (1), Svishtov (1), Varna (1), Plovdiv (1), Stara Zagora (1), Veliko Tarnovo (1).
