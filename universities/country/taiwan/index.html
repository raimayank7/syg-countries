Universities in Taiwan

Wikidata lists 89 universities in Taiwan. For 88 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1879 (St. John's University) and the latest 2018 (National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology). 76 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology (21,982 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: New Taipei City (3), Kaohsiung (2), Changhua City (2), Luzhu District (2), Guishan District (1), Taichung (1), Tainan (1), Taishan District (1).
