Universities in Thailand

Wikidata lists 131 universities in Thailand. For 79 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1887 (Mahachulalongkornrajavidyalaya University) and the latest 2017 (CMKL University). 3 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Kasetsart University (68,099 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Bangkok (4), Nakhon Pathom (2), Hiran Ruchi (1), Chatuchak (1), Mueang Chiang Mai (1), Chiang Rai (1), Wat Kanlaya (1), Lak Si (1).
