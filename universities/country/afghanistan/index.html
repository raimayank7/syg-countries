Universities in Afghanistan

Wikidata lists 67 universities in Afghanistan. For 47 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1931 (Kabul University) and the latest 2022 (Afghan International Islamic University). 1 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Kabul University (22,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Kabul (5), Mazar-i-Sharif (1), Herat (1), Jalalabad (1), Gardez (1), Taloqan (1).

Founding years by century

20th century: 15. 21st century: 32.
