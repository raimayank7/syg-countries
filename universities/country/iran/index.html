Universities in Iran

Wikidata lists 369 universities in Iran. For 228 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1024 (Isfahan University of Medical Sciences) and the latest 2013 (Pars University of Art and Architecture). 6 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Islamic Azad University of Semnan (10,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Tehran (7), Isfahan (3), Zanjan (2), Ahvaz (2), Jiroft (2), Zahedan (2), Abhar (1), Ardabil (1).

Founding years by century

11th century: 1. 14th century: 3. 19th century: 2. 20th century: 187. 21st century: 35.
