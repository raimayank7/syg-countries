Universities in Czechia

Wikidata lists 29 universities in Czechia. For 29 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1348 (Charles University) and the latest 2013 (Czech College). 6 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Charles University (49,508 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Prague (13), Brno (4), Ostrava (2), Ústí nad Labem (1), Opava (1), Liberec (1), Zlín (1), Hradec Králové (1).
