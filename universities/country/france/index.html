Universities in France

Wikidata lists 56 universities in France. For 49 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1452 (University of Valence) and the latest 2023 (City University of Paris). 6 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Institut national des langues et civilisations orientales (8,082 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Paris (2), Saumur (1), Six-Fours-les-Plages (1), Die (1), Hôtel de Massa (1), Nîmes (1), Hérouville-Saint-Clair (1), Jouy-en-Josas (1).
