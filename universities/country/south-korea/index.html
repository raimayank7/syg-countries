Universities in South Korea

Wikidata lists 123 universities in South Korea. For 108 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1910 (Seoul National University of Science and Technology) and the latest 2022 (Korea Institute of Energy Technology). 1 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Korea National University of Cultural Heritage (850 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Muan County (1), Daejeon (1), Sangju (1).
