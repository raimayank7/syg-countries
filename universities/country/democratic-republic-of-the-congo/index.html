Universities in Democratic Republic of the Congo

Wikidata lists 71 universities in Democratic Republic of the Congo. For 58 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1954 (University of Kinshasa) and the latest 2022 (Université Notre Dame de Lomami). 4 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Université de Lubumbashi (33,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Kinshasa (3), Lubumbashi (1).

Founding years by century

20th century: 32. 21st century: 26.
