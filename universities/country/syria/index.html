Universities in Syria

Wikidata lists 35 universities in Syria. For 32 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1919 (Faculty of Law Damascus University) and the latest 2019 (Faculty of Theology). 1 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Homs University (141,233 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Damascus (2), Aleppo (2), Azaz (1).

20th century: 5. 21st century: 27.
