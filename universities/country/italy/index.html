Universities in Italy

Wikidata lists 122 universities in Italy. For 105 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1175 (University of Modena and Reggio Emilia) and the latest 2017 (Istituto Italiano di Criminologia). 18 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of Padua (75,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Milan (2), Rome (2), Verona (1), Fonteghetto della Farina (1), Turin (1), University of Palermo (1), Bolzano (1), Trieste (1).
