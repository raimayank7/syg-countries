Universities in Peru

Wikidata lists 93 universities in Peru. For 76 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1822 (National University of Education Enrique Guzmán y Valle) and the latest 2013 (National University of Barranca). 5 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is National University of Trujillo (19,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Trujillo (4), Juliaca (2), Iquitos (2), Pucallpa (2), Puerto Maldonado (1), Huanta (1), Yurimaguas (1), Chota (1).
