Universities in Brazil

Wikidata lists 263 universities in Brazil. For 175 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1818 (Museu Nacional) and the latest 2009 (Federal University of Fronteira Sul). 10 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of São Paulo (97,964 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Fortaleza (2), Chapecó (2), Belo Horizonte (2), Manaus (2), Curitiba (2), Valinhos (1), São Paulo (1), Santana (1).
