Universities in Switzerland

Wikidata lists 50 universities in Switzerland. For 37 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1833 (University of Zurich) and the latest 2019 (Schaffhausen Institute of Technology). 8 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of Zurich (25,732 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Geneva (3), Zurich (2), St. Gallen (1), Lausanne (1), Brig-Glis (1), Chemin du Pavillon (1), Lucerne (1), Crans-Montana (1).
