Universities in Ethiopia

Wikidata lists 63 universities in Ethiopia. For 57 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1939 (Ambo University) and the latest 2017 (Adwa Pan-African University).

Towns with the most listed universities: Hawaasa (2), Arba Minch (1), Bahir Dar (1), Addis Ababa (1), Mekele (1).

Founding years by century

20th century: 22. 21st century: 35.
