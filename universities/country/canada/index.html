Universities in Canada

Wikidata lists 134 universities in Canada. For 115 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1789 (University of King's College) and the latest 2024 (University of Niagara Falls Canada). 5 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Mount Allison University (72,100 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Wolfville (1), Kelowna (1), Ottawa (1), Winnipeg (1), British Columbia (1).
