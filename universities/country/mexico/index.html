Universities in Mexico

Wikidata lists 302 universities in Mexico. For 208 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1732 (Universidad de Guanajuato) and the latest 2017 (Center for Research and Gender Studies). 2 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is National Autonomous University of Mexico (360,883 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Mexico City (2), Monterrey (1), Coyoacán (1), Rectorate of the Autonomous University of Mexico State (1), Corredor Tres Centurias (1).
