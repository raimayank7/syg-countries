Universities in Uganda

Wikidata lists 64 universities in Uganda. For 57 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1948 (Bugema University) and the latest 2019 (Equator University of Science and Technology). 2 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Bugema University (7,500 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Mbarara City (1), Uganda (1), Kampala (1).

20th century: 12. 21st century: 45.
