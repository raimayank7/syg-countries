Universities in Philippines

Wikidata lists 445 universities in Philippines. For 408 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1725 (Saint Mary's College of Quezon City) and the latest 2024 (University of Santo Tomas Santa Rosa). 3 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Southern Leyte State University (12,219 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Manila (3), Malolos (1), Tuguegarao (1), Muñoz (1), Maramag (1), Puerto Princesa (1), Marikina (1), General Santos (1).
