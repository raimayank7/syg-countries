Universities in Saudi Arabia

Wikidata lists 58 universities in Saudi Arabia. For 53 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1872 (Dar Al-Uloom Colleges) and the latest 2026 (FAiRER University). 3 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is King Khalid University (61,708 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Jeddah (2), Ha'il (1), Jizan (1), Riyadh (1), Mecca (1).
