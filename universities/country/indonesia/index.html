Universities in Indonesia

Wikidata lists 872 universities in Indonesia. For 839 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1849 (University of Indonesia) and the latest 2025 (Universitas Muhammadiyah Kolaka Utara). 18 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Universitas Terbuka (1,045,665 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Yogyakarta (3), Bogor (2), Bandung (2), Mulyorejo (1), South Tangerang (1), Banjarmasin (1), Tanjungpinang (1), West Bandung (1).
