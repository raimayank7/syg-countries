Universities in Poland

Wikidata lists 43 universities in Poland. For 42 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1364 (Jagiellonian University) and the latest 2012 (Akademia Lospuma Training Institute). 17 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of Warmia and Mazury in Olsztyn (43,655 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Kraków (3), Bydgoszcz (2), Warsaw (2), Poznań (1), Kielce (1), Toruń (1), Oliva Campus (1), Katowice (1).
