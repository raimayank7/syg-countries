Universities in China

Wikidata lists 631 universities in China. For 563 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 976 (Hunan University) and the latest 2025 (Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo). 24 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Sichuan University (70,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Shanghai (4), Harbin (4), Beijing (3), Dalian (3), Tianjin (3), Shijiazhuang (2), Changchun (2), Nanjing (2).
