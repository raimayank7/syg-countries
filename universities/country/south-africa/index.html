Universities in South Africa

Wikidata lists 55 universities in South Africa. For 52 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1829 (University of Cape Town) and the latest 2019 (Sol-Tech). 21 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of South Africa (420,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Cape Town (2), Bloemfontein (1), Gqeberha (1), Potchefstroom (1), Makhanda (1), Stellenbosch (1), Alice (1), Pretoria (1).
