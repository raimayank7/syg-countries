Universities in Nigeria

Wikidata lists 397 universities in Nigeria. For 322 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1895 (Hope Waddell Training Institution) and the latest 2026 (Abdulfattah Durojaiye University). 95 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is National Open University of Nigeria, Lafia (500,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Ilorin (35), Oyo State (3), Maiduguri (2), Katsina State (2), Owerri (2), Gusau (2), Delta State (2), Minna (2).
