Universities in Russia

Wikidata lists 314 universities in Russia. For 309 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1758 (I.M. Sechenov First Moscow State Medical University) and the latest 2022 (Ufa University of Science and Technology). 96 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Russian University of Transport (90,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Moscow (17), Saint Petersburg (6), Ufa (4), Barnaul (3), Yekaterinburg (3), Volgograd (3), Astrakhan (2), Ivanovo (2).
