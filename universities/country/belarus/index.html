Universities in Belarus

Wikidata lists 40 universities in Belarus. For 39 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1812 (Jesuit Academy of Polotsk) and the latest 2018 (Nil Hilevich University). 14 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Belarusian National Technical University (28,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Minsk (3), Brest (2), Vitebsk (2), Baranavichy (1), Gomel (1), Main building of the Belarusian-Russian University (1), Mahilioŭ State University main building (1), Building of Peasant Land Bank, Viciebsk (1).
