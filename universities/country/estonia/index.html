Universities in Estonia

Wikidata lists 6 universities in Estonia. For 6 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1632 (University of Tartu) and the latest 2003 (International University Audentes). 2 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Tallinn University of Technology (14,151 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Tallinn (4), Tartu (2).
