Universities in Georgia

Wikidata lists 37 universities in Georgia. For 30 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1918 (Tbilisi State Medical University) and the latest 2017 (Tbilisi International University). 5 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Ilia State University (16,601 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Tbilisi (4), Sokhumi (1), Kutaisi (1), Batumi (1).
