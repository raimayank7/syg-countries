Universities in United Kingdom

Wikidata lists 187 universities in United Kingdom. For 153 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1546 (Trinity College) and the latest 2026 (London and South East University Group). 33 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is University of London (45,500 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: London (9), Cardiff (2), Glasgow (2), Manchester (2), Dundee (2), Coventry (1), Cranfield (1), Fife (1).
