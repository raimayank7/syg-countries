Universities in Ecuador

Wikidata lists 51 universities in Ecuador. For 42 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1620 (Central University of Ecuador) and the latest 2025 (Universidad Estatal de Milagro). 2 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Universidad Internacional del Ecuador (6,094 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Puyo (1), Guayaquil (1), Samborondón Canton (1), Quito (1), Loja (1).
