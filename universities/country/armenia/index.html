Universities in Armenia

Wikidata lists 28 universities in Armenia. For 24 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1874 (Gevorkian Theological Seminary) and the latest 2001 (European University of Armenia). 6 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Yerevan State Medical University (8,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Yerevan (8), Kotayk Province (1).
