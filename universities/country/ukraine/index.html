Universities in Ukraine

Wikidata lists 200 universities in Ukraine. For 191 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1784 (Danylo Halytsky Lviv National Medical University) and the latest 2021 (Presidential University). 70 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Kyiv National Economic University named after Vadym Hetman (36,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Kyiv (6), Chernivtsi (1), Pekarska Street, Lviv (1), Dnipro (1), Kramatorsk (1), Lviv Oblast (1), Ivano-Frankivsk (1), Kamianets-Podilskyi (1).
