Universities in Colombia

Wikidata lists 138 universities in Colombia. For 89 of them it records a founding year: the earliest year is 1708 (Universidad de San Buenaventura) and the latest 2024 (Corporación Unificada Nacional de Educación Superior). 2 have a recorded student number; the largest recorded is Pontifical Bolivarian University (26,000 students).

Towns with the most listed universities: Bogotá (6), Bucaramanga (2), Manizales (1), Tunja (1), Medellín (1), Cartagena (1), Cali (1).
