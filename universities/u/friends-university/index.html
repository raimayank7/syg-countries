Friends University

United States

Friends University is a university in United States. Wikidata gives its year of founding as 1898. The number of students recorded is 1,671 (the year of this count is not shown here and may be old). Its website is friends.edu.

How it compares inside United States

Of the 1,559 universities in United States with a recorded founding year, this one is the 415th oldest by that year (a plain sort of the years, not a claim about which is the oldest). The median founding year among them is 1945. Among the 880 universities in United States with a recorded student number, it is 626th by that number (the counts are of different years and definitions, so treat the order as rough). The median recorded number is 3,225.

Before you rely on this page

These facts come from Wikidata, which volunteers edit; a founding year can refer to an older predecessor, and a student count can be years old. A listing here is not a statement that the university is recognised or accredited. If you plan to study here, check admission rules, fees and recognition with the university itself and with the official education authority of United States.
