IBAN format of United Arab Emirates: 23 characters, starts with AE

An IBAN of the United Arab Emirates is 23 characters long and begins with AE. Here is what each part means, a made-up example, and how to check one.

The parts with a name here are: Bank code, account number. The bank decides the bank code and the account number; you never have to work them out yourself. The layout code AE2!n3!n16!n is how the registry writes this: a number, then n for digits, a for capital letters or c for letters or digits.

How to check an IBAN of the United Arab Emirates

  1. Count the characters without spaces. There must be exactly 23.
  2. The first two must be AE, and the next two must be digits.
  3. After those four, the rest must follow the layout: 3 digits, then 16 digits.
  4. Run the check-digit test. The IBAN checker does all four steps for you and shows which one fails.

Writing it down

On paper, an IBAN is written in groups of four characters with spaces, as in the example above, so that it is easier to read and compare. In online banking and in files it is entered without spaces. Both forms are the same IBAN. Letters are always capitals.

How it compares

At 23 characters, it sits between the shortest IBAN in the registry (Norway, 15 characters) and the longest (Russia, 33 characters). The same length is used by Gibraltar, Iraq, Israel, Oman, Somalia, Timor-Leste, each with its own layout. After the country code it has digits only.

Common questions

How many characters does an IBAN of the United Arab Emirates have?

An IBAN of the United Arab Emirates always has 23 characters: the letters AE, two check digits and 19 more characters for the bank and the account.

What does an IBAN of the United Arab Emirates start with?

It starts with AE, the country code, followed by two check digits.

Is United Arab Emirates in SEPA?

No. The United Arab Emirates uses IBAN but is not part of the Single Euro Payments Area.
