Namibia

Namibia is a country in Southern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Namibia. In the language of the place it is called Namibië. The capital is Windhoek.

Land and position

Namibia covers about 825,418 square kilometres. By area it ranks 35th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Venezuela and a little larger than Pakistan. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Angola, Botswana, South Africa, Zambia. Its centre lies at about 22° south, 17° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 3.09 million people live in Namibia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 137th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 3.7 people to each square kilometre. About 51 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Afrikaans, German, Herero, Khoekhoe. A person from Namibia is called Namibian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Namibian dollar (code NAD, symbol $). Also listed as used here: South African rand (ZAR). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 15.1 billion (2025), which is about US$ 4,876 for each person (2025).

Time

Namibia uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Windhoek). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
