Volcanoes by country

95 countries and territories, 1,609 volcanoes in all.

The countries are those named in the NOAA location database, so some are territories or island groups and a few volcanoes lie on borders or under the sea and are filed under one country. The number of volcanoes in a country depends on its geology and on how thoroughly NOAA has catalogued it: a country with many entries is not necessarily more dangerous than one with few.
