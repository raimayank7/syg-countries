Volcanoes of Italy

15 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 15 volcanoes in Italy: 4 stratovolcano, 3 caldera, 2 complex volcano, 1 submarine volcanoes, 1 submarine, 1 explosion crater.

The highest is Etna at 3,357 m (11,014 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 62 events for volcanoes of Italy, with an estimated 23,072 deaths in all.
