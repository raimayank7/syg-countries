Volcanoes of Philippines

49 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 49 volcanoes in Philippines: 38 stratovolcano, 5 compound volcano, 1 pyroclastic cone, 1 tuff cone, 1 complex volcano, 1 fumarole field.

The highest is Apo at 2,938 m (9,639 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 50 events for volcanoes of Philippines, with an estimated 7,930 deaths in all.
