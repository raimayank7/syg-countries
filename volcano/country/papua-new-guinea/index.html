Volcanoes of Papua New Guinea

54 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 54 volcanoes in Papua New Guinea: 23 stratovolcano, 5 complex volcano, 4 caldera, 4 volcanic field, 3 submarine volcano, 3 pyroclastic shield.

The highest is Doma Peaks at 3,568 m (11,706 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 43 events for volcanoes of Papua New Guinea, with an estimated 5,534 deaths in all.
