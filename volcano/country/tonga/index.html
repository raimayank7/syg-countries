Volcanoes of Tonga

22 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 22 volcanoes in Tonga: 13 submarine volcano, 3 stratovolcano, 2 caldera, 2 submarine, 1 not volcanic, 1 shield volcano.

The highest is Kao at 1,009 m (3,310 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 5 events for volcanoes of Tonga, with an estimated 31 deaths in all.

Tonga in the database as a whole

With 22 volcanoes Tonga is number 18 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 1.4% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from -24.8° to -15.1°, so they lie in the southern hemisphere. Of the 22 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is -25 m; 21 are below 1,000 m, 1 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 0 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 16 Historical, 3 not stated, 2 Holocene, 1 Fumarolic.

4 of the volcanoes of Tonga (18%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database, and the ones with most events are Niuafo'ou (2), Unnamed (no. 40301) (1), Hunga Tonga-Hunga Ha'apai (1). The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.

The events of this country run from 1853 to 2022, and the one with the most recorded deaths is that of 24 June 1853 (Niuafo'ou, an estimated 25 deaths).
