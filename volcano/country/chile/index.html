Volcanoes of Chile

88 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 88 volcanoes in Chile: 57 stratovolcano, 6 cinder cone, 5 caldera, 4 pyroclastic cone, 3 lava dome, 2 shield volcano.

The highest is San Pedro-San Pablo at 6,142 m (20,151 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 20 events for volcanoes of Chile, with an estimated 106 deaths in all.
