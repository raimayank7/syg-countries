Volcanoes of Mexico

41 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 41 volcanoes in Mexico: 15 stratovolcano, 8 cinder cone, 7 shield volcano, 3 volcanic field, 3 tuff cone, 2 lava dome.

The highest is Orizaba, Pico de at 5,564 m (18,255 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 13 events for volcanoes of Mexico, with an estimated 2,035 deaths in all.

Mexico in the database as a whole

With 41 volcanoes Mexico is number 10 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 2.5% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from 15.1° to 32.4°, so they lie in the northern hemisphere. Of the 41 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is 2,280 m; 11 are below 1,000 m, 12 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 18 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 20 Holocene, 11 Historical, 8 Radiocarbon, 1 Fumarolic.

5 of the volcanoes of Mexico (12%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database, and the ones with most events are Colima (4), Chichon, El (4), Popocatepetl (3). The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.

The events of this country run from 930 to 2022, and the one with the most recorded deaths is that of 29 March 1982 (Chichon, El, an estimated 1,879 deaths).
