Volcanoes of Colombia

14 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 14 volcanoes in Colombia: 11 stratovolcano, 1 complex volcano, 1 shield volcano, 1 lava dome.

The highest is Huila, Nevado del at 5,364 m (17,598 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 11 events for volcanoes of Colombia, with an estimated 25,475 deaths in all.

Colombia in the database as a whole

With 14 volcanoes Colombia is number 26 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 0.9% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from 0.9° to 5.2°, so they lie in the northern hemisphere. Of the 14 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is 4,338 m; 0 are below 1,000 m, 1 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 13 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 7 Historical, 5 Radiocarbon, 2 Holocene.

5 of the volcanoes of Colombia (36%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database, and the ones with most events are Ruiz, Nevado del (3), Huila, Nevado del (3), Purace (2). The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.

The events of this country run from 1595 to 2008, and the one with the most recorded deaths is that of 13 November 1985 (Ruiz, Nevado del, an estimated 23,080 deaths).
