Volcanoes of Iceland

44 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 44 volcanoes in Iceland: 19 stratovolcano, 8 crater rows, 4 submarine volcano, 3 fissure vent, 3 subglacial volcano, 3 caldera.

The highest is Oraefajokull at 2,010 m (6,594 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 54 events for volcanoes of Iceland, with an estimated 10,188 deaths in all.
