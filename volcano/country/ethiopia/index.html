Volcanoes of Ethiopia

58 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 58 volcanoes in Ethiopia: 20 stratovolcano, 11 shield volcano, 8 fissure vent, 4 caldera, 2 pumice cone, 2 cinder cone.

The highest is Tepi at 2,728 m (8,950 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 5 events for volcanoes of Ethiopia, with an estimated 50 deaths in all.
