Volcanoes of Russia

170 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 170 volcanoes in Russia: 70 stratovolcano, 47 shield volcano, 11 caldera, 10 cinder cone, 6 submarine volcano, 5 somma volcano.

The highest is Elbrus at 5,642 m (18,510 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 22 events for volcanoes of Russia, with an estimated 20 deaths in all.
