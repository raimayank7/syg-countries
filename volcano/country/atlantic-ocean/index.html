Volcanoes of Atlantic Ocean

13 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 13 volcanoes in Atlantic Ocean: 9 submarine volcano, 3 stratovolcano, 1 submarine.

The highest is Tristan da Cunha at 2,060 m (6,759 ft).

Atlantic Ocean in the database as a whole

With 13 volcanoes Atlantic Ocean is number 29 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 0.8% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from -53.9° to 66.0°, so they lie in the northern and southern hemispheres. Of the 13 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is -1,415 m; 12 are below 1,000 m, 1 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 0 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 6 Uncertain, 4 Historical, 2 Holocene, 1 not stated.

0 of the volcanoes of Atlantic Ocean (0%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database. The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.
