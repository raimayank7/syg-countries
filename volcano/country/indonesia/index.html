Volcanoes of Indonesia

136 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 136 volcanoes in Indonesia: 89 stratovolcano, 15 complex volcano, 13 caldera, 7 unknown, 3 submarine volcano, 2 maar.

The highest is Kerinci at 3,800 m (12,467 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 209 events for volcanoes of Indonesia, with an estimated 143,504 deaths in all.

Indonesia in the database as a whole

With 136 volcanoes Indonesia is number 3 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 8.5% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from -8.9° to 5.4°, so they lie in the northern and southern hemispheres. Of the 136 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is 1,749 m; 26 are below 1,000 m, 96 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 14 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 72 Historical, 42 Holocene, 19 Fumarolic, 3 Uncertain.

45 of the volcanoes of Indonesia (33%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database, and the ones with most events are Merapi (28), Semeru (25), Kelud (18). The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.

The events of this country run from 416 to 2026, and the one with the most recorded deaths is that of 10 April 1815 (Tambora, an estimated 60,000 deaths).
