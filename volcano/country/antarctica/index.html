Volcanoes of Antarctica

37 volcanoes

NOAA's Volcano Location Database lists 37 volcanoes in Antarctica: 17 stratovolcano, 9 shield volcano, 4 submarine volcano, 2 pyroclastic cone, 2 cinder cone, 1 caldera.

The highest is Erebus at 3,794 m (12,448 ft).

The significant-eruption database has 1 events for volcanoes of Antarctica.

Antarctica in the database as a whole

With 37 volcanoes Antarctica is number 11 of 95 countries and territories in the NOAA location database, and holds 2.3% of the 1,609 volcanoes listed worldwide. Their latitudes run from -78.5° to -29.0°, so they lie in the southern hemisphere. Of the 37 volcanoes with a recorded height the median is 1,100 m; 17 are below 1,000 m, 12 are between 1,000 and 2,999 m and 8 are 3,000 m or higher. By status in the database: 13 Historical, 13 Holocene, 3 Uncertain, 3 Fumarolic.

1 of the volcanoes of Antarctica (3%) have at least one significant event in the NOAA eruption database, and the ones with most events are Deception Island (1). The database records only significant events, so the share says how many volcanoes have caused deaths or damage or were notably large, and says nothing about how many are active today.

The events of this country run from 1967 to 1967.
