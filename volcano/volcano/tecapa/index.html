Tecapa volcano

1,593 m

El Salvador · Stratovolcano

Tecapa is a stratovolcano in El Salvador (13.49°N, 88.50°W), 1,593 m (5,226 ft) above sea level. The database gives its status as Holocene and the code of its last known eruption period as U.

Of the 22 volcanoes listed for El Salvador with a recorded height, it is the 9th highest; the median height there is 1,369 m.

Among all 1,604 volcanoes with a recorded height it ranks 719th by height.

Eruptions

NOAA's significant-eruption database has no entry for this volcano: it did not cause deaths or major damage, or it is not recorded as a significant event. That is not a statement about whether it is active. For current activity see the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program and your national observatory.
