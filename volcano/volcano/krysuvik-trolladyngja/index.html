Krysuvik-Trolladyngja volcano

360 m

Iceland · Crater rows

Krysuvik-Trolladyngja is a crater rows in Iceland (63.92°N, 22.07°W), 360 m (1,181 ft) above sea level. The database gives its status as Historical and the code of its last known eruption period as D6.

Of the 44 volcanoes listed for Iceland with a recorded height, it is the 34th highest; the median height there is 1,072 m.
