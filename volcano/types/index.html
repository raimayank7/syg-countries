Types of volcano

42 types, by the number of volcanoes in the database.

The type is the shape or structure NOAA records for each volcano: the cone of a stratovolcano, the broad slopes of a shield volcano, the basin of a caldera, the many small vents of a volcanic field and so on. Each type page shows how many volcanoes of the type there are, where, how high they are and which of them have significant events. The counts describe the catalogue and can change when NOAA reclassifies a volcano.
