Pyroclastic cone: volcanoes of this type

36 volcanoes

NOAA classifies 36 volcanoes as "pyroclastic cone". The countries with most are Chile (4), Argentina (4), Kenya (3), China (3), Papua New Guinea (2), Canada (2). The highest is Ashikule Volcanic Field at 5,090 m.

3 significant events in NOAA's database belong to volcanoes of this type, with an estimated 2 deaths in all.
