Crater rows: volcanoes of this type

9 volcanoes

NOAA classifies 9 volcanoes as "crater rows". The countries with most are Iceland (8), Japan (1). The highest is Tenchozan at 1,046 m.

2 significant events in NOAA's database belong to volcanoes of this type, with an estimated 1 deaths in all.

This type in numbers

The 9 crater rows volcanoes make up 0.6% of the 1,609 in the database and are spread over 2 countries and territories; the six with most of them hold 100% of the type. 9 lie in the northern hemisphere and 0 in the southern. Of the 9 with a recorded height the lowest is 140 m, the median 550 m and the highest 1,046 m. By status in the database: 6 Historical, 1 Radiocarbon, 1 Tephrochronology, 1 not stated. Volcano types describe shape and structure, so one volcano can be recorded under only one of them even when its history includes more than one kind of activity.

Events of this type in the eruption database

2 significant events belong to volcanoes of this type, spread over 2 of them; the ones with the most events are Reykjanes (1), Krysuvik-Trolladyngja (1). The events run from 1151 to 2023, and the one with the most recorded deaths is that of 18 December 2023 at Reykjanes (an estimated 1 deaths). 1 of the events have a Volcanic Explosivity Index in the database; the highest is VEI 1. The numbers show where the catalogue has recorded significant events for this shape of volcano, and say nothing about which types are the most dangerous: that depends on where people live and on how well old events were recorded.
