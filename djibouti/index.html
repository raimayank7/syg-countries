Djibouti

Djibouti is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Djibouti. In the language of the place it is called جيبوتي. The capital is Djibouti.

Land and position

Djibouti covers about 23,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 152nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than North Macedonia and a little larger than Belize. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Eritrea, Ethiopia, Somalia. Its centre lies at about 12° north, 43° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 1.18 million people live in Djibouti (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 161st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 51 people to each square kilometre. About 73 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 66 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Arabic, Somali, Afar.

Money and economy

The currency is the Djiboutian franc (code DJF, symbol Fr). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 4.62 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,906 for each person (2025).

Time

Djibouti uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Africa/Djibouti). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
