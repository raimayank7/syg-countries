Saint Pierre and Miquelon

Saint Pierre and Miquelon is in North America. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. In the language of the place it is called Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon. The capital is Saint-Pierre.

Land and position

Saint Pierre and Miquelon covers about 242 square kilometres. By area it ranks 221st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Niue and a little larger than Cook Islands. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 47° north, 56° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 7,012 people live in Saint Pierre and Miquelon (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 234th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 29 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: French. A person from Saint Pierre and Miquelon is called Saint-Pierrais, Miquelonnais.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more.

Time

Saint Pierre and Miquelon uses one time zone, UTC-3:00 (America/Miquelon). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
