The 36th Congress (1859 to 1861)

322 people filed as members: 70 in the Senate and 252 in the House at some point during it.

The 36th Congress began in 1859. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
