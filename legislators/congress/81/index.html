The 81st Congress (1949 to 1951)

557 people filed as members: 109 in the Senate and 448 in the House at some point during it.

The 81st Congress began in 1949. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
