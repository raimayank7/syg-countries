The 42nd Congress (1871 to 1873)

342 people filed as members: 78 in the Senate and 264 in the House at some point during it.

The 42nd Congress began in 1871. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
