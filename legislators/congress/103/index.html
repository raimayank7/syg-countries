The 103rd Congress (1993 to 1995)

552 people filed as members: 104 in the Senate and 448 in the House at some point during it.

The 103rd Congress began in 1993. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
