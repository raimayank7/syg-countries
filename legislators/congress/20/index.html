The 20th Congress (1827 to 1829)

282 people filed as members: 53 in the Senate and 229 in the House at some point during it.

The 20th Congress began in 1827. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
