The 55th Congress (1897 to 1899)

468 people filed as members: 95 in the Senate and 373 in the House at some point during it.

The 55th Congress began in 1897. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
