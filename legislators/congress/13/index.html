The 13th Congress (1813 to 1815)

251 people filed as members: 47 in the Senate and 204 in the House at some point during it.

The 13th Congress began in 1813. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
