The 63rd Congress (1913 to 1915)

559 people filed as members: 101 in the Senate and 458 in the House at some point during it.

The 63rd Congress began in 1913. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
