The 53rd Congress (1893 to 1895)

475 people filed as members: 94 in the Senate and 381 in the House at some point during it.

The 53rd Congress began in 1893. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
