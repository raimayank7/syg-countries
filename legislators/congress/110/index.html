The 110th Congress (2007 to 2009)

555 people filed as members: 102 in the Senate and 453 in the House at some point during it.

The 110th Congress began in 2007. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
