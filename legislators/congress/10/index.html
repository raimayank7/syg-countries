The 10th Congress (1807 to 1809)

194 people filed as members: 39 in the Senate and 155 in the House at some point during it.

The 10th Congress began in 1807. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
