The 49th Congress (1885 to 1887)

425 people filed as members: 84 in the Senate and 341 in the House at some point during it.

The 49th Congress began in 1885. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
