The 19th Congress (1825 to 1827)

292 people filed as members: 60 in the Senate and 232 in the House at some point during it.

The 19th Congress began in 1825. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
