The 12th Congress (1811 to 1813)

190 people filed as members: 38 in the Senate and 152 in the House at some point during it.

The 12th Congress began in 1811. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
