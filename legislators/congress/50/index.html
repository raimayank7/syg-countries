The 50th Congress (1887 to 1889)

415 people filed as members: 77 in the Senate and 338 in the House at some point during it.

The 50th Congress began in 1887. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
