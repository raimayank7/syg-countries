The 5th Congress (1797 to 1799)

159 people filed as members: 42 in the Senate and 117 in the House at some point during it.

The 5th Congress began in 1797. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
