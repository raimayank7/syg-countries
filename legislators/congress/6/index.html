The 6th Congress (1799 to 1801)

153 people filed as members: 38 in the Senate and 115 in the House at some point during it.

The 6th Congress began in 1799. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
