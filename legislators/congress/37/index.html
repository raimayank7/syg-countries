The 37th Congress (1861 to 1863)

287 people filed as members: 76 in the Senate and 211 in the House at some point during it.

The 37th Congress began in 1861. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
