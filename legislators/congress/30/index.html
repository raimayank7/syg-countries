The 30th Congress (1847 to 1849)

313 people filed as members: 73 in the Senate and 240 in the House at some point during it.

The 30th Congress began in 1847. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
