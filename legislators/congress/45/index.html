The 45th Congress (1877 to 1879)

394 people filed as members: 83 in the Senate and 311 in the House at some point during it.

The 45th Congress began in 1877. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
