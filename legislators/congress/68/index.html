The 68th Congress (1923 to 1925)

563 people filed as members: 103 in the Senate and 460 in the House at some point during it.

The 68th Congress began in 1923. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
