The 56th Congress (1899 to 1901)

475 people filed as members: 92 in the Senate and 383 in the House at some point during it.

The 56th Congress began in 1899. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
