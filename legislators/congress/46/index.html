The 46th Congress (1879 to 1881)

393 people filed as members: 82 in the Senate and 311 in the House at some point during it.

The 46th Congress began in 1879. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
