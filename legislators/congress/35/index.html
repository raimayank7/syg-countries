The 35th Congress (1857 to 1859)

327 people filed as members: 72 in the Senate and 255 in the House at some point during it.

The 35th Congress began in 1857. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
