The 67th Congress (1921 to 1923)

565 people filed as members: 105 in the Senate and 460 in the House at some point during it.

The 67th Congress began in 1921. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
