The 39th Congress (1865 to 1867)

274 people filed as members: 60 in the Senate and 214 in the House at some point during it.

The 39th Congress began in 1865. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
