The 17th Congress (1821 to 1823)

264 people filed as members: 54 in the Senate and 210 in the House at some point during it.

The 17th Congress began in 1821. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
