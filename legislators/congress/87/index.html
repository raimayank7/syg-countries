The 87th Congress (1961 to 1963)

559 people filed as members: 109 in the Senate and 450 in the House at some point during it.

The 87th Congress began in 1961. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
