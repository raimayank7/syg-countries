The 66th Congress (1919 to 1921)

565 people filed as members: 99 in the Senate and 466 in the House at some point during it.

The 66th Congress began in 1919. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
