The 11th Congress (1809 to 1811)

203 people filed as members: 45 in the Senate and 158 in the House at some point during it.

The 11th Congress began in 1809. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
