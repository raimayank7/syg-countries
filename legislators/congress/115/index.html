The 115th Congress (2017 to 2019)

561 people filed as members: 105 in the Senate and 456 in the House at some point during it.

The 115th Congress began in 2017. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
