The 8th Congress (1803 to 1805)

193 people filed as members: 44 in the Senate and 149 in the House at some point during it.

The 8th Congress began in 1803. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
