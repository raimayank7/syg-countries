The 40th Congress (1867 to 1869)

316 people filed as members: 69 in the Senate and 247 in the House at some point during it.

The 40th Congress began in 1867. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
