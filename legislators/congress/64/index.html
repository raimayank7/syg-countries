The 64th Congress (1915 to 1917)

549 people filed as members: 100 in the Senate and 449 in the House at some point during it.

The 64th Congress began in 1915. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
