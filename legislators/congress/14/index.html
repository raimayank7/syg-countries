The 14th Congress (1815 to 1817)

258 people filed as members: 46 in the Senate and 212 in the House at some point during it.

The 14th Congress began in 1815. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
