The 28th Congress (1843 to 1845)

300 people filed as members: 60 in the Senate and 240 in the House at some point during it.

The 28th Congress began in 1843. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
