The 52nd Congress (1891 to 1893)

446 people filed as members: 94 in the Senate and 352 in the House at some point during it.

The 52nd Congress began in 1891. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
