The 23rd Congress (1833 to 1835)

318 people filed as members: 55 in the Senate and 263 in the House at some point during it.

The 23rd Congress began in 1833. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
