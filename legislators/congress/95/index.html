The 95th Congress (1977 to 1979)

554 people filed as members: 109 in the Senate and 445 in the House at some point during it.

The 95th Congress began in 1977. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
