The 78th Congress (1943 to 1945)

560 people filed as members: 104 in the Senate and 456 in the House at some point during it.

The 78th Congress began in 1943. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
