The 41st Congress (1869 to 1871)

348 people filed as members: 76 in the Senate and 272 in the House at some point during it.

The 41st Congress began in 1869. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
