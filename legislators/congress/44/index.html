The 44th Congress (1875 to 1877)

400 people filed as members: 81 in the Senate and 319 in the House at some point during it.

The 44th Congress began in 1875. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
