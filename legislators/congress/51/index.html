The 51st Congress (1889 to 1891)

453 people filed as members: 90 in the Senate and 363 in the House at some point during it.

The 51st Congress began in 1889. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
