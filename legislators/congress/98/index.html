The 98th Congress (1983 to 1985)

548 people filed as members: 101 in the Senate and 447 in the House at some point during it.

The 98th Congress began in 1983. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
