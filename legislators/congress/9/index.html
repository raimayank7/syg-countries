The 9th Congress (1805 to 1807)

194 people filed as members: 38 in the Senate and 156 in the House at some point during it.

The 9th Congress began in 1805. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
