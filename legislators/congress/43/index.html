The 43rd Congress (1873 to 1875)

396 people filed as members: 79 in the Senate and 317 in the House at some point during it.

The 43rd Congress began in 1873. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
