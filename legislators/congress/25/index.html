The 25th Congress (1837 to 1839)

322 people filed as members: 60 in the Senate and 262 in the House at some point during it.

The 25th Congress began in 1837. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
