The 34th Congress (1855 to 1857)

308 people filed as members: 62 in the Senate and 246 in the House at some point during it.

The 34th Congress began in 1855. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
