The 62nd Congress (1911 to 1913)

522 people filed as members: 106 in the Senate and 416 in the House at some point during it.

The 62nd Congress began in 1911. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
