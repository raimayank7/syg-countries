The 57th Congress (1901 to 1903)

470 people filed as members: 91 in the Senate and 379 in the House at some point during it.

The 57th Congress began in 1901. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
