The 92nd Congress (1971 to 1973)

550 people filed as members: 104 in the Senate and 446 in the House at some point during it.

The 92nd Congress began in 1971. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
