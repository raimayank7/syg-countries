The 22nd Congress (1831 to 1833)

280 people filed as members: 55 in the Senate and 225 in the House at some point during it.

The 22nd Congress began in 1831. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
