The 18th Congress (1823 to 1825)

278 people filed as members: 54 in the Senate and 224 in the House at some point during it.

The 18th Congress began in 1823. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
