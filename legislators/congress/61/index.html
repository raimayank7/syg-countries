The 61st Congress (1909 to 1911)

510 people filed as members: 101 in the Senate and 409 in the House at some point during it.

The 61st Congress began in 1909. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
