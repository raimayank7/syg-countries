The 73rd Congress (1933 to 1935)

553 people filed as members: 101 in the Senate and 452 in the House at some point during it.

The 73rd Congress began in 1933. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
