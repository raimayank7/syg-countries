The 32nd Congress (1851 to 1853)

312 people filed as members: 69 in the Senate and 243 in the House at some point during it.

The 32nd Congress began in 1851. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
