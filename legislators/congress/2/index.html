The 2nd Congress (1791 to 1793)

104 people filed as members: 32 in the Senate and 72 in the House at some point during it.

The 2nd Congress began in 1791. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
