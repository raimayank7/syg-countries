The 4th Congress (1795 to 1797)

158 people filed as members: 43 in the Senate and 115 in the House at some point during it.

The 4th Congress began in 1795. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
