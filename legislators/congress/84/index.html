The 84th Congress (1955 to 1957)

543 people filed as members: 101 in the Senate and 442 in the House at some point during it.

The 84th Congress began in 1955. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
