The 72nd Congress (1931 to 1933)

566 people filed as members: 104 in the Senate and 462 in the House at some point during it.

The 72nd Congress began in 1931. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
