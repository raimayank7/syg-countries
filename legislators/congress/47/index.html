The 47th Congress (1881 to 1883)

403 people filed as members: 85 in the Senate and 318 in the House at some point during it.

The 47th Congress began in 1881. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
