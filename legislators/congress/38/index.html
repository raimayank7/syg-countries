The 38th Congress (1863 to 1865)

260 people filed as members: 58 in the Senate and 202 in the House at some point during it.

The 38th Congress began in 1863. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
