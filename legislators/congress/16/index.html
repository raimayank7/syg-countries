The 16th Congress (1819 to 1821)

257 people filed as members: 52 in the Senate and 205 in the House at some point during it.

The 16th Congress began in 1819. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
