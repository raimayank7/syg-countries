The 79th Congress (1945 to 1947)

572 people filed as members: 117 in the Senate and 455 in the House at some point during it.

The 79th Congress began in 1945. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
