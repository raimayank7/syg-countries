The 33rd Congress (1853 to 1855)

322 people filed as members: 71 in the Senate and 251 in the House at some point during it.

The 33rd Congress began in 1853. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
