The 31st Congress (1849 to 1851)

319 people filed as members: 73 in the Senate and 246 in the House at some point during it.

The 31st Congress began in 1849. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
