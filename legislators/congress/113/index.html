The 113th Congress (2013 to 2015)

555 people filed as members: 105 in the Senate and 450 in the House at some point during it.

The 113th Congress began in 2013. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
