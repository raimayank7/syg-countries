The 58th Congress (1903 to 1905)

499 people filed as members: 93 in the Senate and 406 in the House at some point during it.

The 58th Congress began in 1903. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
