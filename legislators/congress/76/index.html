The 76th Congress (1939 to 1941)

567 people filed as members: 104 in the Senate and 463 in the House at some point during it.

The 76th Congress began in 1939. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
