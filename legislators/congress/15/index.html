The 15th Congress (1817 to 1819)

248 people filed as members: 49 in the Senate and 199 in the House at some point during it.

The 15th Congress began in 1817. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
