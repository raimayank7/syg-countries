The 1st Congress (1789 to 1791)

95 people filed as members: 29 in the Senate and 66 in the House at some point during it.

The 1st Congress began in 1789. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
