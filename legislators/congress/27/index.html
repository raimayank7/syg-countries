The 27th Congress (1841 to 1843)

321 people filed as members: 59 in the Senate and 262 in the House at some point during it.

The 27th Congress began in 1841. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
