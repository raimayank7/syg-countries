The 112th Congress (2011 to 2013)

553 people filed as members: 102 in the Senate and 451 in the House at some point during it.

The 112th Congress began in 2011. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
