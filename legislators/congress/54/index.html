The 54th Congress (1895 to 1897)

468 people filed as members: 91 in the Senate and 377 in the House at some point during it.

The 54th Congress began in 1895. A Congress lasts two years, so members who left or joined part-way are included: the totals are people who held a seat at some time in it, not the size of the chamber on one day.
