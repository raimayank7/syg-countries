Air quality in Choctaw, Oklahoma

37 median AQI, 2019

The EPA's annual summary for Choctaw, Oklahoma has data for 3 years between 2017 and 2019. In the latest year, 2019, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 252 days: 238 Good, 14 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 37 and the highest was 90; on 10% of days it was above 47 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2019: Ozone 252 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Choctaw, Oklahoma?

In 2019 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 37, with 238 Good days of 252 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 3 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
