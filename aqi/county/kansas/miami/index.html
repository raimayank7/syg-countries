Air quality in Miami, Kansas

41 median AQI, 1997

The EPA's annual summary for Miami, Kansas has data for 2 years between 1996 and 1997. In the latest year, 1997, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 125 days: 97 Good, 23 Moderate, 5 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 41 and the highest was 133; on 10% of days it was above 74 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 1997: Ozone 125 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Miami, Kansas?

In 1997 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 41, with 97 Good days of 125 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 2 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
