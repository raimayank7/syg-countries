Air quality in Park, Colorado

48 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Park, Colorado has data for 19 years between 2007 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 203 days: 128 Good, 73 Moderate, 2 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 48 and the highest was 105; on 10% of days it was above 77 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 63.1% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: Ozone 203 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Compared with ten years earlier

In 2015, 75.4% of the days with an index were Good; in 2025, 63.1%. The median AQI was 45 in 2015 and 48 in 2025. One year can differ from another because of weather and smoke events, so compare more than two points before drawing a conclusion.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Park, Colorado?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 48, with 128 Good days of 203 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 19 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
