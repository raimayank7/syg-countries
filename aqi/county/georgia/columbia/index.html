Air quality in Columbia, Georgia

36 median AQI, 2024

The EPA's annual summary for Columbia, Georgia has data for 20 years between 2005 and 2024. In the latest year, 2024, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 172 days: 162 Good, 10 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 36 and the highest was 90; on 10% of days it was above 48 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2024: Ozone 172 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Columbia, Georgia?

In 2024 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 36, with 162 Good days of 172 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 20 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
