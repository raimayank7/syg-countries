Air quality in Paulding, Georgia

28 median AQI, 2017

The EPA's annual summary for Paulding, Georgia has data for 32 years between 1980 and 2017. In the latest year, 2017, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 11 days: 10 Good, 1 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 28 and the highest was 58; on 10% of days it was above 49 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2017: PM2.5 11 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Paulding, Georgia?

In 2017 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 28, with 10 Good days of 11 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 32 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
