Air quality in Morgan, Indiana

35 median AQI, 2019

The EPA's annual summary for Morgan, Indiana has data for 33 years between 1984 and 2019. In the latest year, 2019, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 364 days: 344 Good, 20 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 35 and the highest was 87; on 10% of days it was above 48 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2019: Ozone 364 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Morgan, Indiana?

In 2019 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 35, with 344 Good days of 364 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 33 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
