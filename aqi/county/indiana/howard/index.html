Air quality in Howard, Indiana

43 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Howard, Indiana has data for 26 years between 1999 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 362 days: 241 Good, 118 Moderate, 3 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 43 and the highest was 122; on 10% of days it was above 61 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 66.6% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: Ozone 182 days, PM2.5 180 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Compared with ten years earlier

Common questions

What is the air quality in Howard, Indiana?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 43, with 241 Good days of 362 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 26 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
