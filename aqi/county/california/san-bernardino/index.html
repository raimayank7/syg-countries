Air quality in San Bernardino, California

77 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for San Bernardino, California has data for 46 years between 1980 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 365 days: 46 Good, 209 Moderate, 67 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 39 Unhealthy, 4 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 77 and the highest was 207; on 10% of days it was above 156 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 12.6% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: Ozone 225 days, PM2.5 125 days, PM10 11 days, Nitrogen dioxide 4 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Compared with ten years earlier

Common questions

What is the air quality in San Bernardino, California?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 77, with 46 Good days of 365 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 46 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
