Air quality in Franklin, Maine

27 median AQI, 2017

The EPA's annual summary for Franklin, Maine has data for 25 years between 1981 and 2017. In the latest year, 2017, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 242 days: 202 Good, 40 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 27 and the highest was 75; on 10% of days it was above 54 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2017: PM2.5 242 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Franklin, Maine?

In 2017 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 27, with 202 Good days of 242 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 25 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
