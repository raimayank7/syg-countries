Air quality in St. Charles, Louisiana

31 median AQI, 2014

The EPA's annual summary for St. Charles, Louisiana has data for 30 years between 1980 and 2014. In the latest year, 2014, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 365 days: 341 Good, 24 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 31 and the highest was 97; on 10% of days it was above 47 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2014: Ozone 365 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in St. Charles, Louisiana?

In 2014 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 31, with 341 Good days of 365 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 30 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
