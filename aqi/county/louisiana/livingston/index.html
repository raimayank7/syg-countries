Air quality in Livingston, Louisiana

45 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Livingston, Louisiana has data for 34 years between 1992 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 363 days: 225 Good, 138 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 45 and the highest was 90; on 10% of days it was above 59 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 62.0% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: PM2.5 237 days, Ozone 124 days, Nitrogen dioxide 2 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Livingston, Louisiana?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 45, with 225 Good days of 363 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 34 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
