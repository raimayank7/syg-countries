Air quality in Pope, Arkansas

46 median AQI, 2012

The EPA's annual summary for Pope, Arkansas has data for 23 years between 1989 and 2012. In the latest year, 2012, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 57 days: 35 Good, 22 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 46 and the highest was 70; on 10% of days it was above 61 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2012: PM2.5 57 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Pope, Arkansas?

In 2012 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 46, with 35 Good days of 57 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 23 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
