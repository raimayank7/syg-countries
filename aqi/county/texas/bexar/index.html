Air quality in Bexar, Texas

52 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Bexar, Texas has data for 46 years between 1980 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 365 days: 158 Good, 196 Moderate, 10 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 1 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 52 and the highest was 154; on 10% of days it was above 80 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 43.3% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: PM2.5 203 days, Ozone 152 days, PM10 6 days, Nitrogen dioxide 4 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Bexar, Texas?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 52, with 158 Good days of 365 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 46 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
