Air quality in Lexington, South Carolina

41 median AQI, 2023

The EPA's annual summary for Lexington, South Carolina has data for 39 years between 1980 and 2023. In the latest year, 2023, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 149 days: 96 Good, 53 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 41 and the highest was 77; on 10% of days it was above 58 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2023: PM2.5 140 days, PM10 9 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Lexington, South Carolina?

In 2023 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 41, with 96 Good days of 149 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 39 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
