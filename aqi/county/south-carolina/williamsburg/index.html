Air quality in Williamsburg, South Carolina

44 median AQI, 2007

The EPA's annual summary for Williamsburg, South Carolina has data for 17 years between 1991 and 2007. In the latest year, 2007, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 245 days: 177 Good, 64 Moderate, 4 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 44 and the highest was 122; on 10% of days it was above 71 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2007: Ozone 245 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Williamsburg, South Carolina?

In 2007 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 44, with 177 Good days of 245 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 17 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
