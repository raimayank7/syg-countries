Air quality in Manati, Puerto Rico

17 median AQI, 2005

The EPA's annual summary for Manati, Puerto Rico has data for 16 years between 1990 and 2005. In the latest year, 2005, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 19 days: 19 Good, 0 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 17 and the highest was 39; on 10% of days it was above 36 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2005: PM10 19 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Manati, Puerto Rico?

In 2005 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 17, with 19 Good days of 19 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 16 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
