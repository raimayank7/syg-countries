Air quality in Custer, Nebraska

3 median AQI, 1980

The EPA's annual summary for Custer, Nebraska has data for 1 years between 1980 and 1980. In the latest year, 1980, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 74 days: 74 Good, 0 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 3 and the highest was 32; on 10% of days it was above 29 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 1980: Carbon monoxide 39 days, Ozone 29 days, Nitrogen dioxide 6 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.
