Air quality in Kenai Peninsula , Alaska

9 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Kenai Peninsula , Alaska has data for 29 years between 1988 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 102 days: 102 Good, 0 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 9 and the highest was 28; on 10% of days it was above 16 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 100.0% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: PM2.5 102 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Kenai Peninsula , Alaska?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 9, with 102 Good days of 102 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 29 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
