Air quality in Grays Harbor, Washington

22 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Grays Harbor, Washington has data for 30 years between 1990 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 365 days: 355 Good, 10 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 22 and the highest was 76; on 10% of days it was above 41 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 97.3% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: PM2.5 365 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Compared with ten years earlier

In 2015, 88.3% of the days with an index were Good; in 2025, 97.3%. The median AQI was 29 in 2015 and 22 in 2025. One year can differ from another because of weather and smoke events, so compare more than two points before drawing a conclusion.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Grays Harbor, Washington?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 22, with 355 Good days of 365 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 30 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
