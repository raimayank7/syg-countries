Air quality in Somerset, New Jersey

35 median AQI, 1983

The EPA's annual summary for Somerset, New Jersey has data for 4 years between 1980 and 1983. In the latest year, 1983, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 185 days: 144 Good, 41 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 35 and the highest was 78; on 10% of days it was above 61 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 1983: Carbon monoxide 185 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Somerset, New Jersey?

In 1983 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 35, with 144 Good days of 185 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 4 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
