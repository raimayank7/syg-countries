Air quality in Burlington, New Jersey

5 median AQI, 2009

The EPA's annual summary for Burlington, New Jersey has data for 30 years between 1980 and 2009. In the latest year, 2009, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 354 days: 354 Good, 0 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 5 and the highest was 16; on 10% of days it was above 8 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2009: Carbon monoxide 354 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Burlington, New Jersey?

In 2009 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 5, with 354 Good days of 354 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 30 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
