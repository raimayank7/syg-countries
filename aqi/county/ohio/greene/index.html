Air quality in Greene, Ohio

40 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Greene, Ohio has data for 37 years between 1989 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 245 days: 209 Good, 34 Moderate, 2 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 40 and the highest was 105; on 10% of days it was above 58 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 85.3% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: Ozone 245 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Greene, Ohio?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 40, with 209 Good days of 245 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 37 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
