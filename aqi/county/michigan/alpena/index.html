Air quality in Alpena, Michigan

37 median AQI, 2002

The EPA's annual summary for Alpena, Michigan has data for 9 years between 1986 and 2002. In the latest year, 2002, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 107 days: 64 Good, 43 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 37 and the highest was 95; on 10% of days it was above 70 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2002: PM2.5 107 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Alpena, Michigan?

In 2002 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 37, with 64 Good days of 107 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 9 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
