Air quality in Union, South Dakota

44 median AQI, 2021

The EPA's annual summary for Union, South Dakota has data for 13 years between 2009 and 2021. In the latest year, 2021, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 273 days: 179 Good, 90 Moderate, 2 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 2 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 44 and the highest was 167; on 10% of days it was above 64 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2021: Ozone 155 days, PM2.5 117 days, PM10 1 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Union, South Dakota?

In 2021 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 44, with 179 Good days of 273 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 13 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
