Air quality in Vilas, Wisconsin

36 median AQI, 2025

The EPA's annual summary for Vilas, Wisconsin has data for 36 years between 1981 and 2025. In the latest year, 2025, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 199 days: 183 Good, 15 Moderate, 1 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 36 and the highest was 105; on 10% of days it was above 49 (the 90th percentile). Good days were 92.0% of the days with an index; across the 984 monitored counties in 2025 the median county had 78.1% good days.

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2025: Ozone 199 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Vilas, Wisconsin?

In 2025 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 36, with 183 Good days of 199 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 36 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
