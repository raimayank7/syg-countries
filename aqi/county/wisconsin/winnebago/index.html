Air quality in Winnebago, Wisconsin

45 median AQI, 2003

The EPA's annual summary for Winnebago, Wisconsin has data for 24 years between 1980 and 2003. In the latest year, 2003, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 201 days: 139 Good, 49 Moderate, 13 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 45 and the highest was 143; on 10% of days it was above 80 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2003: Ozone 184 days, PM2.5 17 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Winnebago, Wisconsin?

In 2003 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 45, with 139 Good days of 201 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 24 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
