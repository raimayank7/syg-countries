Air quality in Franklin, North Carolina

43 median AQI, 2015

The EPA's annual summary for Franklin, North Carolina has data for 23 years between 1993 and 2015. In the latest year, 2015, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 211 days: 171 Good, 40 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 43 and the highest was 93; on 10% of days it was above 58 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 2015: Ozone 211 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Franklin, North Carolina?

In 2015 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 43, with 171 Good days of 211 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 23 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
