Air quality in Campbell, Virginia

15 median AQI, 1994

The EPA's annual summary for Campbell, Virginia has data for 6 years between 1989 and 1994. In the latest year, 1994, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 30 days: 30 Good, 0 Moderate, 0 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 0 Unhealthy, 0 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 15 and the highest was 38; on 10% of days it was above 28 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 1994: PM10 30 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Campbell, Virginia?

In 1994 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 15, with 30 Good days of 30 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 6 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
