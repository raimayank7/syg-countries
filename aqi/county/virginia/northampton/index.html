Air quality in Northampton, Virginia

45 median AQI, 1990

The EPA's annual summary for Northampton, Virginia has data for 11 years between 1980 and 1990. In the latest year, 1990, the monitors gave an Air Quality Index for 214 days: 131 Good, 59 Moderate, 18 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 5 Unhealthy, 1 Very unhealthy and 0 Hazardous. The median daily AQI was 45 and the highest was 207; on 10% of days it was above 105 (the 90th percentile).

The pollutant that set the index for the day (the "main pollutant") in 1990: Ozone 214 days. Ozone is the gas that builds up in warm sunny weather; PM2.5 is fine particles, from smoke, vehicles and industry. See the guide.

Common questions

What is the air quality in Northampton, Virginia?

In 1990 the median daily AQI at its monitors was 45, with 131 Good days of 214 days with an index. See the table for other years.

Why does the data show only some years?

The county had enough monitoring data for an index in 11 years of the 1980 to 2025 period.

Is the air safe to breathe?

This site does not give health advice. The index categories are the EPA's; follow the EPA's AirNow guidance and your doctor's advice on days with a high index.
