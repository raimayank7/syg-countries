What the AQI categories mean

The US Air Quality Index (AQI) turns measurements of several pollutants into one number from 0 up. The EPA divides it into six categories:

CategoryIndex range
Good0 to 50
Moderate51 to 100
Unhealthy for sensitive groups101 to 150
Unhealthy151 to 200
Very unhealthy201 to 300
Hazardousabove 300

The index of a day is the highest of the indices of the pollutants measured that day: if ozone gives 60 and fine particles give 120, the day is 120. The EPA publishes the health messages for each category on its AirNow site; this site does not give health advice. (The category names and ranges are the EPA's published breakpoints, written here from general knowledge and not read from the data files.)

On this site "Days with AQI" counts the days in a year with enough data for an index, "Good" counts days in the first category, and "Unhealthy or worse" adds the Unhealthy, Very unhealthy and Hazardous days.
