Air quality in Alabama by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 31 counties in Alabama that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 15 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 30 to 52 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 133. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
