Air quality in Idaho by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 31 counties in Idaho that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 19 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 10 to 47 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 215. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
