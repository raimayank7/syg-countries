Air quality in South Carolina by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 28 counties in South Carolina that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 13 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 35 to 48 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 170. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
