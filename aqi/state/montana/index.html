Air quality in Montana by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 36 counties in Montana that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 23 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 11 to 45 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 197. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
