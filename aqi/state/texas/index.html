Air quality in Texas by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 63 counties in Texas that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 45 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 6 to 70 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 2122. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
