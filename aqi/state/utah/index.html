Air quality in Utah by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 19 counties in Utah that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 17 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 12 to 57 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 175. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
