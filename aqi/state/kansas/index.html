Air quality in Kansas by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 23 counties in Kansas that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 12 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 27 to 48 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 370. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
