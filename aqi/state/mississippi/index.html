Air quality in Mississippi by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 29 counties in Mississippi that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 10 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 34 to 52 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 143. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
