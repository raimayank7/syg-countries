Air quality in North Carolina by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 62 counties in North Carolina that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 34 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 24 to 51 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 121. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
