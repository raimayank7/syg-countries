Air quality in Oklahoma by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 45 counties in Oklahoma that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 20 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 31 to 51 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 508. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
