Air quality in Georgia by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 39 counties in Georgia that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 26 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 27 to 52 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 181. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
