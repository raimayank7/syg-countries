Air quality in Maine by county

The EPA's annual summaries have data for 17 counties in Maine that have, or had, an air monitor. In 2025, 11 of them have a summary; their median AQIs run from 32 to 39 and the highest daily AQI in any of them was 170. Only counties with a monitor appear; a county missing here has no monitor in the data, which says nothing about its air.
