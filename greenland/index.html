Greenland

Greenland is in North America. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. In the language of the place it is called Kalaallit Nunaat. The capital is Nuuk.

Land and position

Greenland covers about 2,166,086 square kilometres. By area it ranks 13th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than DR Congo and a little larger than Mexico. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 72° north, 40° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 56,831 people live in Greenland (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 209th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 0.0 people to each square kilometre. About 89 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 70 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Kalaallisut, Danish, English. A person from Greenland is called Greenlandic.

Money and economy

The currency is the krone (code DKK, symbol kr.). It is also the main currency of Denmark, Faroe Islands. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 3.33 billion (2023), which is about US$ 58,499 for each person (2023).
