Burundi

Burundi is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Burundi. In the language of the place it is called Uburundi. The capital is Gitega.

Land and position

Burundi covers about 27,830 square kilometres. By area it ranks 148th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Equatorial Guinea and a little larger than Haiti. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: DR Congo, Rwanda, Tanzania. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 4° south, 30° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 14.4 million people live in Burundi (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 78th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.18 percent of the world's people. On average there are 517 people to each square kilometre. About 26 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 64 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Rundi. A person from Burundi is called Burundian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Burundian franc (code BIF, symbol Fr). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 3.36 billion (2025), which is about US$ 234 for each person (2025).

Time

Burundi uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Bujumbura). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
