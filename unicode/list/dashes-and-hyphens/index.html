Dashes and hyphens to copy: - – — ― − ‑

There is more than one dash, and writers use them for different jobs. The hyphen-minus on your keyboard is U+002D. The en dash – (U+2013) joins ranges such as 10–20 or pages 4–9; the em dash — (U+2014) sets off a part of a sentence; the minus sign − (U+2212) is for mathematics and is as wide as a plus sign; the non-breaking hyphen ‑ (U+2011) stops a line from breaking at a hyphen, which is useful in phone numbers and codes.

All dashes in Unicode (category "dash punctuation") are listed. Copy the right one and your text will look and break lines as you mean it to.

This list has 28 characters from 15 Unicode blocks (Armenian, Basic Latin, CJK Compatibility Forms, CJK Symbols and Punctuation, Garay, General Punctuation and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 16.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
