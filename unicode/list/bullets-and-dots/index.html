Bullet points and dots to copy: • ‣ ◦ ▪ ● · ∙

Bullets mark list items in plain text: emails, messages, bios and spreadsheets. The standard bullet is U+2022; there are also the triangular bullet ‣, the hollow bullet ◦, small squares, large circles and the middle dot · (U+00B7) that is used in many languages as a separator.

When you paste a bullet into a text field you get a character, not a list: it will not indent the second line of a long item (the wrapped line goes back under the bullet). For lists that must look right, use the list tools of your editor. The characters below are the ones whose names are bullet, middle dot, or common solid and hollow dots and small squares.

This list has 36 characters from 9 Unicode blocks (Arrows, Dingbats, General Punctuation, Geometric Shapes, Katakana, Latin-1 Supplement and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 3.2. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
