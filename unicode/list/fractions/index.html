Fraction characters to copy: ½ ⅓ ¼ ¾ ⅛

Unicode has ready-made fraction characters such as ½, ¼ and ¾ (from Latin-1) and ⅓, ⅔, ⅕, ⅛ and others from the Number Forms block. They are single characters, so they stay together and keep the line height of normal text.

Only a limited set of fractions exists. For any other, write the numerator, the fraction slash (⁄, U+2044) and the denominator; fonts that support it join them into a neat fraction, and others show them side by side. Do not use the ordinary slash if you want the fraction to be read as one number by typographic software.

This list has 100 characters from 16 Unicode blocks (Common Indic Number Forms, Coptic, General Punctuation, Indic Siyaq Numbers, Kharoshthi, Latin-1 Supplement and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 18.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
