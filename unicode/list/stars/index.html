Star symbols to copy and paste: ★ ☆ ✪ ✯ ⭐

Stars show up in ratings, bullet lists, decorations and usernames. The classic pair is the black star (filled) and the white star (outline), which are used for rating displays: three filled and two outlined makes a three-out-of-five rating that works in plain text anywhere. Beyond those, Unicode has stars with different numbers of points, stars in circles, stars with tails, and the emoji stars.

Every character below has the word star in its official name, from the Dingbats, Miscellaneous Symbols, Geometric Shapes and emoji blocks. Tap Copy to put one on your clipboard. If you need many in a row for a rating, copy a filled one and an outline one and repeat them; no special formatting is needed, and screen readers will read the name of each character, so a rating written this way is better with a text alternative such as "4 out of 5".

This list has 60 characters from 8 Unicode blocks (Dingbats, Geometric Shapes Extended, Mathematical Operators, Miscellaneous Symbols, Miscellaneous Symbols and Arrows, Miscellaneous Symbols and Pictographs and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 15.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
