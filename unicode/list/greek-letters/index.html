Greek letters to copy: α β γ δ π Σ Ω µ

Greek letters are everywhere in science and mathematics: π for the ratio of a circle, Σ for a sum, Δ for a change, Ω for ohms, μ for micro. This list gives the Greek and Coptic block's Greek letters, capital and small, plus the accented forms used in modern Greek text.

One detail trips people up. Unicode has two characters that look like the Greek mu: the Greek small letter mu U+03BC and the micro sign U+00B5 in the Latin-1 block. They are different characters with identical shapes in most fonts. For text in Greek or maths use the letter; the micro sign exists for compatibility with old character sets and some units. A similar pair exists for Ω (ohm sign U+2126 and Greek capital omega U+03A9).

This list has 114 characters from 1 Unicode block (Greek and Coptic). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 7.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
