Heart symbols to copy and paste: ♥ ❤ ♡ ❣ 💖

The heart is the most used symbol in messages. Unicode keeps the heart suit of playing cards (black and white), the heavy heart ornaments of the Dingbats block, and a large family of colour emoji hearts: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, black, white, brown, sparkling, growing, broken and more.

The list below holds every character with "heart" in its official name. Two choices matter when you copy. The plain text hearts (the suit signs ♥ and ♡ and the heavy ❤) take the colour of the text around them, so they turn red when you colour the text red. The emoji hearts keep their own colours. The heavy black heart U+2764 can appear either way depending on the app, because it has both a text and a picture form.

This list has 50 characters from 8 Unicode blocks (CJK Radicals Supplement, Dingbats, Kangxi Radicals, Miscellaneous Symbols, Miscellaneous Symbols and Pictographs, Playing Cards and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 15.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
