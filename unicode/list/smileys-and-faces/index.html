Smiley and face emoji to copy: 😀 😂 😍 😎 🙂

This list has every Unicode character with "face" in its name from the emoticons and face groups: smiling, laughing, crying, angry, surprised, sleepy, masked, cat and monkey faces and many more. Most are emoji, drawn in colour by your phone or computer; the look differs between Apple, Google, Samsung and Microsoft, so the same emoji can read a little differently to the person who receives it.

Pasting works in almost every app. If a person with an older device sees an empty box, their system does not yet have the emoji, because new emoji are added every year. Skin-tone and hair variants are not single characters: they are built from a base character and an invisible modifier, so they are not on this list.

This list has 169 characters from 5 Unicode blocks (Emoticons, Miscellaneous Symbols, Miscellaneous Symbols and Pictographs, Supplemental Symbols and Pictographs, Symbols and Pictographs Extended-A). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 18.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
