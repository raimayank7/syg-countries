Spaces and invisible characters to copy: no-break space, thin space, zero width space

Not every space is the same. Besides the normal space there is the no-break space (U+00A0), which stops a line breaking between two words; the en and em spaces; the thin and hair spaces used in typesetting; the narrow no-break space used in French and Mongolian; and the ideographic space of Chinese, Japanese and Korean text. In the format-character group are invisible helpers like the zero width space, zero width joiner, word joiner and the byte order mark.

You cannot see any of these, so each button copies the character itself and the row shows its name and code. Be careful with the invisible ones: pasting a zero width character into a username, a file name or a password can make it look identical and still not match. See the invisible characters guide for what each does.

This list has 57 characters from 11 Unicode blocks (Arabic, Arabic Extended-A, Arabic Extended-B, Arabic Presentation Forms-B, Basic Latin, CJK Symbols and Punctuation and others). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 14.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
