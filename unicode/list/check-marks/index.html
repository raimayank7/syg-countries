Check marks and crosses to copy: ✓ ✔ ☑ ✗ ✘ ❌

Ticks and crosses are used in to-do lists, comparison tables and forms. Unicode has plain and heavy check marks, check marks inside ballot boxes, crosses, ballot crosses and the emoji versions in colour. Pick the pair that suits your page: for example ✓ and ✗ for a quiet look, or ✔ and ✘ for a bolder one.

These are text characters, so they can be coloured with ordinary styles (green for a tick, red for a cross). The emoji forms ✅ and ❌ keep their own colours and may look different on each phone. The list shows every character whose name has check mark, ballot box with a mark, or cross mark in it.

This list has 24 characters from 6 Unicode blocks (Dingbats, Miscellaneous Symbols, Miscellaneous Symbols and Arrows, Miscellaneous Symbols and Pictographs, Miscellaneous Technical, Symbols for Legacy Computing). They were added between Unicode 1.1 and 16.0. Tap a symbol to copy it.

Common questions

How do I copy a symbol from this page?

Tap or click the symbol. It is copied to your clipboard, and a short message confirms it. Then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V, or press and hold in a text field on a phone.

Why do some symbols look like empty boxes?

The font on your device does not have that character. The characters are valid Unicode, but each font covers only part of it. Try another app or font, or choose a more common symbol from the list.

Can I use these symbols in a username, a name or a file?

Many sites accept them, but some block symbols or turn them into question marks. Test on the site first. Avoid symbols in file names and passwords, where an unusual character can cause errors on another system.
