Mende Kikakui: Unicode block U+1E800 to U+1E8DF

213 characters you can look at, copy and paste. Data from Unicode 18.0.

The Mende Kikakui block covers the code points U+1E800 to U+1E8DF (224 positions) in the Supplementary Multilingual Plane (plane 1). Unicode has assigned 213 of them so far, which are 197 other letters, 9 other numbers, 7 non-spacing marks. All its characters were added in Unicode 7.0.

The characters belong to the script: Mende Kikakui.

What the block contains

The Unicode code charts divide this block into groups: Syllables in k-; Syllables in w-; Syllables in wv-; Syllables in m-; Syllables in b-; Vowels; Syllables in s-; Syllables in l-; Syllables in d-; Syllables in t-; Syllables in j-; Syllables in y-; Syllables in f-; Syllables in n- and more.

How many characters are in the Mende Kikakui block?

Unicode 18.0 has 213 assigned characters in the Mende Kikakui block, out of 224 code points reserved for it (U+1E800 to U+1E8DF).

Can I copy these characters?

Yes. Press Copy in the row of the character you want. Whether it appears correctly depends on the fonts of the app or device that shows it: a missing font draws an empty box.
