Egyptian Hieroglyphs Extended-A: Unicode block U+13460 to U+143FF

3,995 characters you can look at, copy and paste. Data from Unicode 18.0.

The Egyptian Hieroglyphs Extended-A block covers the code points U+13460 to U+143FF (4,000 positions) in the Supplementary Multilingual Plane (plane 1). Unicode has assigned 3,995 of them so far, which are 3,995 other letters. All its characters were added in Unicode 16.0.

The characters belong to the script: Egyptian Hieroglyphs.

What the block contains

The Unicode code charts divide this block into groups: A01. Man seated or kneeling empty handed; A02. Man standing empty handed; A04. Man adoring or bent; A05. Man on the ground or in water; A06. Man or god standing, holding a staff; A07. Man seated or kneeling, holding something; A08. Man standing, holding something; A09. Man seated, pouring water; A10. Man standing, pouring water, spreading powder; A11. Man hiding; A12. Man working; A13. Shepherd and porter; A14. Man carrying a bundle; A15. Man or god standing, composed with a hieroglyphic sign and more.

Each name below links to a page with the character's codes, how to type it and where it is used, where such a page exists. The copy button puts the character itself on your clipboard.

Unicode 18.0 has 3,995 assigned characters in the Egyptian Hieroglyphs Extended-A block, out of 4,000 code points reserved for it (U+13460 to U+143FF).

Can I copy these characters?

Yes. Press Copy in the row of the character you want. Whether it appears correctly depends on the fonts of the app or device that shows it: a missing font draws an empty box.
