How to use this page

The list covers the compounds that have their own page on this site, sorted by name. Each page gives the formula, the molar mass worked out from the standard atomic weights, the identifiers used in chemical databases and, where the source records them, the hazard statements of the labelling system. Use the filter to find a compound by name or formula. The pages are reference data and teaching aids; they do not replace the safety data sheet from the supplier of a chemical, which is the document to read before you handle a substance.
