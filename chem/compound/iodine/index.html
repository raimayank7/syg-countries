Iodine (I₂)

Molar mass 253.81 g/mol. PubChem CID 807.

Iodine has the molecular formula I₂ and a molar mass of 253.81 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 807. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is molecular iodine.

What Iodine is made of

One formula unit has 2 atoms of 1 element. By mass, iodine makes up the largest share: 100.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 2 x 126.904 = 253.81 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Iodine

One mole of Iodine weighs 253.81 g. So 10 g is 0.039 mol, and 100 g is 0.394 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 253.81 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
