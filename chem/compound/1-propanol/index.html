1-Propanol (C₃H₈O)

Molar mass 60.10 g/mol. PubChem CID 1031.

1-Propanol has the molecular formula C₃H₈O and a molar mass of 60.10 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 1031. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is propan-1-ol.

What 1-Propanol is made of

One formula unit has 12 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 60.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 3 x 12.011 + 15.999 + 8 x 1.008 = 60.10 g/mol.

Grams and moles of 1-Propanol

One mole of 1-Propanol weighs 60.10 g. So 10 g is 0.166 mol, and 100 g is 1.664 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 60.10 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
