Monosodium Glutamate (C₅H₁₀NNaO₅)

Molar mass 187.13 g/mol. PubChem CID 23689119.

Monosodium Glutamate has the molecular formula C₅H₁₀NNaO₅ and a molar mass of 187.13 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 23689119. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is sodium;(4S)-4-amino-5-hydroxy-5-oxopentanoate;hydrate.

What Monosodium Glutamate is made of

One formula unit has 22 atoms of 5 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 42.7 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 5 x 15.999 + 5 x 12.011 + 22.990 + 14.007 + 10 x 1.008 = 187.13 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Monosodium Glutamate

One mole of Monosodium Glutamate weighs 187.13 g. So 10 g is 0.053 mol, and 100 g is 0.534 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 187.13 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Monosodium Glutamate, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
