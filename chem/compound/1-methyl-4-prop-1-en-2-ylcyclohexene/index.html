Molar mass 136.23 g/mol. PubChem CID 22311.

1-Methyl-4-Prop-1-En-2-Ylcyclohexene has the molecular formula C₁₀H₁₆ and a molar mass of 136.23 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 22311.
