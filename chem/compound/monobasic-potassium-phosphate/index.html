Monobasic potassium phosphate (H₂KO₄P)

Molar mass 136.09 g/mol. PubChem CID 516951.

Monobasic potassium phosphate has the molecular formula H₂KO₄P and a molar mass of 136.09 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 516951. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is potassium dihydrogen phosphate.

What Monobasic potassium phosphate is made of

One formula unit has 8 atoms of 4 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 47.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 15.999 + 39.098 + 30.974 + 2 x 1.008 = 136.08 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Monobasic potassium phosphate

One mole of Monobasic potassium phosphate weighs 136.09 g. So 10 g is 0.073 mol, and 100 g is 0.735 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 136.09 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Monobasic potassium phosphate, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
