Sodium Chloride (ClNa)

Molar mass 58.44 g/mol. PubChem CID 5234.

Sodium Chloride has the molecular formula ClNa and a molar mass of 58.44 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 5234.

What Sodium Chloride is made of

One formula unit has 2 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, chlorine makes up the largest share: 60.7 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 35.450 + 22.990 = 58.44 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Sodium Chloride

One mole of Sodium Chloride weighs 58.44 g. So 10 g is 0.171 mol, and 100 g is 1.711 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 58.44 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling (EU harmonised classification)

Signal word: Danger. Pictograms: Corrosive.

  • H318: Causes serious eye damage
  • H412: Harmful to aquatic life with long lasting effects

Precautionary statement codes: P264+P265, P273, P280, P305+P354+P338, P317, and P501
