Ferrous Sulfate (FeO₄S)

Molar mass 151.91 g/mol. PubChem CID 24393.

Ferrous Sulfate has the molecular formula FeO₄S and a molar mass of 151.91 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 24393. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is iron(2+) sulfate.

What Ferrous Sulfate is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 42.1 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 15.999 + 55.840 + 32.070 = 151.91 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Ferrous Sulfate

One mole of Ferrous Sulfate weighs 151.91 g. So 10 g is 0.066 mol, and 100 g is 0.658 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 151.91 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
