Hydrogen Cyanide (CHN)

Molar mass 27.02 g/mol. PubChem CID 768.

Hydrogen Cyanide has the molecular formula CHN and a molar mass of 27.02 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 768. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is formonitrile.

What Hydrogen Cyanide is made of

One formula unit has 3 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, nitrogen makes up the largest share: 51.8 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 14.007 + 12.011 + 1.008 = 27.03 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Hydrogen Cyanide

One mole of Hydrogen Cyanide weighs 27.02 g. So 10 g is 0.370 mol, and 100 g is 3.700 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 27.02 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
