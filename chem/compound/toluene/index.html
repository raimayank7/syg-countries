Toluene (C₇H₈)

Molar mass 92.14 g/mol. PubChem CID 1140.

Toluene has the molecular formula C₇H₈ and a molar mass of 92.14 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 1140.

What Toluene is made of

One formula unit has 15 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 91.2 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 7 x 12.011 + 8 x 1.008 = 92.14 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Toluene

One mole of Toluene weighs 92.14 g. So 10 g is 0.109 mol, and 100 g is 1.085 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 92.14 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
