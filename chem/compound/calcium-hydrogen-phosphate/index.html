Calcium hydrogen phosphate (CaHO₄P)

Molar mass 136.06 g/mol. PubChem CID 24441.

Calcium hydrogen phosphate has the molecular formula CaHO₄P and a molar mass of 136.06 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 24441.

What Calcium hydrogen phosphate is made of

One formula unit has 7 atoms of 4 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 47.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 15.999 + 40.080 + 30.974 + 1.008 = 136.06 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Calcium hydrogen phosphate

One mole of Calcium hydrogen phosphate weighs 136.06 g. So 10 g is 0.073 mol, and 100 g is 0.735 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 136.06 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Calcium hydrogen phosphate, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
