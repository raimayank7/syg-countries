Ethanol (C₂H₆O)

Molar mass 46.07 g/mol. PubChem CID 702.

Ethanol has the molecular formula C₂H₆O and a molar mass of 46.07 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 702.

What Ethanol is made of

One formula unit has 9 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 52.1 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 2 x 12.011 + 15.999 + 6 x 1.008 = 46.07 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Ethanol

One mole of Ethanol weighs 46.07 g. So 10 g is 0.217 mol, and 100 g is 2.171 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 46.07 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
