Calcium hypochlorite (CaCl₂O₂)

Molar mass 142.98 g/mol. PubChem CID 24504.

Calcium hypochlorite has the molecular formula CaCl₂O₂ and a molar mass of 142.98 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 24504. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is calcium dihypochlorite.

What Calcium hypochlorite is made of

One formula unit has 5 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, chlorine makes up the largest share: 49.6 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 2 x 35.450 + 40.080 + 2 x 15.999 = 142.98 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Calcium hypochlorite

One mole of Calcium hypochlorite weighs 142.98 g. So 10 g is 0.070 mol, and 100 g is 0.699 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 142.98 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling (EU harmonised classification)

Signal word: Danger. Pictograms: Oxidizer, Corrosive, Irritant, Environmental Hazard.

  • H272: May intensify fire; oxidizer
  • H302: Harmful if swallowed
  • H314: Causes severe skin burns and eye damage
  • H400: Very toxic to aquatic life
