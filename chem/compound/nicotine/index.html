Nicotine (C₁₀H₁₄N₂)

Molar mass 162.23 g/mol. PubChem CID 89594.

Nicotine has the molecular formula C₁₀H₁₄N₂ and a molar mass of 162.23 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 89594. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is 3-[(2S)-1-methylpyrrolidin-2-yl]pyridine.

What Nicotine is made of

One formula unit has 26 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 74.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 10 x 12.011 + 2 x 14.007 + 14 x 1.008 = 162.24 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Nicotine

One mole of Nicotine weighs 162.23 g. So 10 g is 0.062 mol, and 100 g is 0.616 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 162.23 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
