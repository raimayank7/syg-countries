Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (C₁₂H₂₅NaO₄S)

Molar mass 288.38 g/mol. PubChem CID 3423265.

Sodium Lauryl Sulfate has the molecular formula C₁₂H₂₅NaO₄S and a molar mass of 288.38 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 3423265. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is sodium dodecyl sulfate.

What Sodium Lauryl Sulfate is made of

One formula unit has 43 atoms of 5 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 50.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 12 x 12.011 + 4 x 15.999 + 32.070 + 25 x 1.008 + 22.990 = 288.39 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Sodium Lauryl Sulfate

One mole of Sodium Lauryl Sulfate weighs 288.38 g. So 10 g is 0.035 mol, and 100 g is 0.347 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 288.38 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Sodium Lauryl Sulfate, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
