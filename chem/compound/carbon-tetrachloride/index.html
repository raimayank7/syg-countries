Carbon Tetrachloride (CCl₄)

Molar mass 153.80 g/mol. PubChem CID 5943.

Carbon Tetrachloride has the molecular formula CCl₄ and a molar mass of 153.80 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 5943. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is tetrachloromethane.

What Carbon Tetrachloride is made of

One formula unit has 5 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, chlorine makes up the largest share: 92.2 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 35.450 + 12.011 = 153.81 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Carbon Tetrachloride

One mole of Carbon Tetrachloride weighs 153.80 g. So 10 g is 0.065 mol, and 100 g is 0.650 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 153.80 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
