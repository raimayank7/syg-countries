Vinyl Chloride (C₂H₃Cl)

Molar mass 62.50 g/mol. PubChem CID 6338.

Vinyl Chloride has the molecular formula C₂H₃Cl and a molar mass of 62.50 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 6338. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is chloroethene.

What Vinyl Chloride is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, chlorine makes up the largest share: 56.7 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 35.450 + 2 x 12.011 + 3 x 1.008 = 62.50 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Vinyl Chloride

One mole of Vinyl Chloride weighs 62.50 g. So 10 g is 0.160 mol, and 100 g is 1.600 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 62.50 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
