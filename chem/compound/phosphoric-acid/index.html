Phosphoric Acid (H₃O₄P)

Molar mass 98.00 g/mol. PubChem CID 1004.

Phosphoric Acid has the molecular formula H₃O₄P and a molar mass of 98.00 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 1004.

What Phosphoric Acid is made of

One formula unit has 8 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 65.3 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 15.999 + 30.974 + 3 x 1.008 = 97.99 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Phosphoric Acid

One mole of Phosphoric Acid weighs 98.00 g. So 10 g is 0.102 mol, and 100 g is 1.020 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 98.00 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
