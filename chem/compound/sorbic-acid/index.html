Sorbic Acid (C₆H₈O₂)

Molar mass 112.13 g/mol. PubChem CID 643460.

Sorbic Acid has the molecular formula C₆H₈O₂ and a molar mass of 112.13 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 643460. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is (2E,4E)-hexa-2,4-dienoic acid.

What Sorbic Acid is made of

One formula unit has 16 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 64.3 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 6 x 12.011 + 2 x 15.999 + 8 x 1.008 = 112.13 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Sorbic Acid

One mole of Sorbic Acid weighs 112.13 g. So 10 g is 0.089 mol, and 100 g is 0.892 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 112.13 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Sorbic Acid, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
