Ammonium Chloride (ClH₄N)

Molar mass 53.49 g/mol. PubChem CID 25517.

Ammonium Chloride has the molecular formula ClH₄N and a molar mass of 53.49 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 25517. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is azanium chloride.

What Ammonium Chloride is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, chlorine makes up the largest share: 66.3 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 35.450 + 14.007 + 4 x 1.008 = 53.49 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Ammonium Chloride

One mole of Ammonium Chloride weighs 53.49 g. So 10 g is 0.187 mol, and 100 g is 1.870 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 53.49 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
