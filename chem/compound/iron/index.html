Iron (Fe)

Molar mass 55.84 g/mol. PubChem CID 23925.

Iron has the molecular formula Fe and a molar mass of 55.84 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 23925.

What Iron is made of

One formula unit has 1 atom of 1 element. By mass, iron makes up the largest share: 100.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 55.840 = 55.84 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Iron

One mole of Iron weighs 55.84 g. So 10 g is 0.179 mol, and 100 g is 1.791 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 55.84 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling

PubChem shows no harmonised EU classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) for Iron, so none is given here. That is not a statement that the substance is harmless. For handling and safety, use the safety data sheet of the product you have.
