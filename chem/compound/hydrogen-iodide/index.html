Hydrogen iodide (HI)

Molar mass 127.91 g/mol. PubChem CID 24841.

Hydrogen iodide has the molecular formula HI and a molar mass of 127.91 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 24841. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is iodane.

What Hydrogen iodide is made of

One formula unit has 2 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, iodine makes up the largest share: 99.2 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 126.904 + 1.008 = 127.91 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Hydrogen iodide

One mole of Hydrogen iodide weighs 127.91 g. So 10 g is 0.078 mol, and 100 g is 0.782 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 127.91 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Hazard labelling (EU harmonised classification)

Signal word: Danger. Pictograms: Compressed Gas, Corrosive.

  • H314: Causes severe skin burns and eye damage
