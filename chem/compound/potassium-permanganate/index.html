Potassium Permanganate (KMnO₄)

Molar mass 158.03 g/mol. PubChem CID 516875.

Potassium Permanganate has the molecular formula KMnO₄ and a molar mass of 158.03 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 516875.

What Potassium Permanganate is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, oxygen makes up the largest share: 40.5 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 4 x 15.999 + 54.938 + 39.098 = 158.03 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Potassium Permanganate

One mole of Potassium Permanganate weighs 158.03 g. So 10 g is 0.063 mol, and 100 g is 0.633 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 158.03 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
