Mercury (Hg)

Molar mass 200.59 g/mol. PubChem CID 23931.

Mercury has the molecular formula Hg and a molar mass of 200.59 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 23931.

What Mercury is made of

One formula unit has 1 atom of 1 element. By mass, mercury makes up the largest share: 100.0 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 200.590 = 200.59 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Mercury

One mole of Mercury weighs 200.59 g. So 10 g is 0.050 mol, and 100 g is 0.499 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 200.59 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
