Hydrazine (H₄N₂)

Molar mass 32.05 g/mol. PubChem CID 9321.

Hydrazine has the molecular formula H₄N₂ and a molar mass of 32.05 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 9321.

What Hydrazine is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, nitrogen makes up the largest share: 87.4 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 2 x 14.007 + 4 x 1.008 = 32.05 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Hydrazine

One mole of Hydrazine weighs 32.05 g. So 10 g is 0.312 mol, and 100 g is 3.121 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 32.05 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
