Sodium Hydroxide (HNaO)

Molar mass 40.00 g/mol. PubChem CID 14798.

Sodium Hydroxide has the molecular formula HNaO and a molar mass of 40.00 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 14798.

What Sodium Hydroxide is made of

One formula unit has 3 atoms of 3 elements. By mass, sodium makes up the largest share: 57.5 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 22.990 + 15.999 + 1.008 = 40.00 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Sodium Hydroxide

One mole of Sodium Hydroxide weighs 40.00 g. So 10 g is 0.250 mol, and 100 g is 2.500 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 40.00 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
