Ethylene (C₂H₄)

Molar mass 28.05 g/mol. PubChem CID 6325.

Ethylene has the molecular formula C₂H₄ and a molar mass of 28.05 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 6325. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is ethene.

What Ethylene is made of

One formula unit has 6 atoms of 2 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 85.6 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 2 x 12.011 + 4 x 1.008 = 28.05 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Ethylene

One mole of Ethylene weighs 28.05 g. So 10 g is 0.357 mol, and 100 g is 3.565 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 28.05 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.

Signal word: Danger. Pictograms: Flammable, Compressed Gas, Irritant.
