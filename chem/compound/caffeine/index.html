Caffeine (C₈H₁₀N₄O₂)

Molar mass 194.19 g/mol. PubChem CID 2519.

Caffeine has the molecular formula C₈H₁₀N₄O₂ and a molar mass of 194.19 g/mol. In PubChem it is compound number 2519. Its systematic (IUPAC) name is 1,3,7-trimethylpurine-2,6-dione.

What Caffeine is made of

One formula unit has 24 atoms of 4 elements. By mass, carbon makes up the largest share: 49.5 per cent. The molar mass is the sum of the atomic weights of all atoms: 8 x 12.011 + 4 x 14.007 + 2 x 15.999 + 10 x 1.008 = 194.19 g/mol.

Grams and moles of Caffeine

One mole of Caffeine weighs 194.19 g. So 10 g is 0.051 mol, and 100 g is 0.515 mol. A solution of 1 mol per litre contains 194.19 g of it in each litre. A mole is 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant). The converter works both ways.
