Molar mass calculator

Type a formula; get the molar mass and the share of each element.

How it works

The calculator reads the formula, counts the atoms of each element, multiplies by the standard atomic weights from PubChem's periodic table and adds them up. Brackets work (Ca(OH)2), and so does a dot for water of crystallisation (CuSO4.5H2O). Write element symbols with the right capital letters: Co is cobalt, but CO is carbon and oxygen. The result is the molar mass in grams per mole; see how to calculate molar mass for the method by hand. The calculation runs in your browser.

Atomic weights from PubChem, read November 2026. Charges and isotope labels are not handled.

Data: PubChem, National Center for Biotechnology Information, U.S. National Library of Medicine (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), read November 2026.
