Chlorine (Cl) has atomic number 17 and a standard atomic weight of 35.450. It is a halogen, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a gas. 32 substances in this list contain it, from Aluminum Chloride to Zinc Chloride. The heaviest of them is Barium chloride at 208.23 g/mol.

The table shows, for the substances that contain chlorine, what share of their mass is chlorine. The highest share is 100.0 per cent, in Chlorine. The atomic weight is the number used in every molar mass on this site: the molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic weights of its atoms.
