Hydrogen (H) has atomic number 1 and a standard atomic weight of 1.008. It is a nonmetal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a gas. 182 substances in this list contain it, from (-)-Asparagine to Water. The heaviest of them is Folic Acid at 441.40 g/mol.
