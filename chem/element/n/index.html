Nitrogen (N) has atomic number 7 and a standard atomic weight of 14.007. It is a nonmetal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a gas. 79 substances in this list contain it, from (-)-Asparagine to Zinc Nitrate. The heaviest of them is Folic Acid at 441.40 g/mol.
