Aluminum (Al) has atomic number 13 and a standard atomic weight of 26.982. It is a post-transition metal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a solid. 4 substances in this list contain it, from Alumina to Aluminum Sulfate. The heaviest of them is Aluminum Sulfate at 342.20 g/mol.

The table shows, for the substances that contain aluminum, what share of their mass is aluminum. The highest share is 100.0 per cent, in Aluminum. The atomic weight is the number used in every molar mass on this site: the molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic weights of its atoms.
