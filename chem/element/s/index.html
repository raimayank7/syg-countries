Sulfur (S) has atomic number 16 and a standard atomic weight of 32.070. It is a nonmetal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a solid. 23 substances in this list contain it, from Aluminum Sulfate to Zinc Sulfate. The heaviest of them is Aluminum Sulfate at 342.20 g/mol.
