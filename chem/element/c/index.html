Carbon (C) has atomic number 6 and a standard atomic weight of 12.011. It is a nonmetal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a solid. 167 substances in this list contain it, from (-)-Asparagine to Vitamin D3. The heaviest of them is Folic Acid at 441.40 g/mol.

The table shows, for the substances that contain carbon, what share of their mass is carbon. The highest share is 100.0 per cent, in Carbon. The atomic weight is the number used in every molar mass on this site: the molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic weights of its atoms.
