Lead (Pb) has atomic number 82 and a standard atomic weight of 207.000. It is a post-transition metal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a solid. 4 substances in this list contain it, from Lead to Lead sulfate. The heaviest of them is Lead nitrate at 331.00 g/mol.

The table shows, for the substances that contain lead, what share of their mass is lead. The highest share is 100.0 per cent, in Lead. The atomic weight is the number used in every molar mass on this site: the molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic weights of its atoms.
