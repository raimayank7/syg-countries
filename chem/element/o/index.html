Oxygen (O) has atomic number 8 and a standard atomic weight of 15.999. It is a nonmetal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a gas. 193 substances in this list contain it, from (-)-Asparagine to Zinc Sulfate. The heaviest of them is Folic Acid at 441.40 g/mol.
