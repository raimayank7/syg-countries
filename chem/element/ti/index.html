Titanium (Ti) has atomic number 22 and a standard atomic weight of 47.867. It is a transition metal, and at room temperature and standard pressure it is a solid. 2 substances in this list contain it, from Titanium to Titanium dioxide. The heaviest of them is Titanium dioxide at 79.87 g/mol.

The table shows, for the substances that contain titanium, what share of their mass is titanium. The highest share is 100.0 per cent, in Titanium. The atomic weight is the number used in every molar mass on this site: the molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic weights of its atoms.
