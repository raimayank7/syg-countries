Russia

Russia is a country in Eastern Europe. Its official name is Russian Federation. In the language of the place it is called Россия. The capital is Moscow.

Land and position

Russia covers about 17,100,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 1st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little larger than Antarctica. It shares a land border with 14 neighbours: Azerbaijan, Belarus, China, Estonia, Finland, Georgia, North Korea, Kazakhstan, Lithuania, Latvia, Mongolia, Norway, Poland, Ukraine. Its centre lies at about 60° north, 100° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 144 million people live in Russia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 9th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1.7 percent of the world's people. On average there are 8.4 people to each square kilometre. About 75 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 73 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Russian, Tatar, Kalmyk, Caucasian languages, Adyghe, Komi, Chechen, Tuvinian. A person from Russia is called Russian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Russian ruble (code RUB, symbol ₽). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 2.56 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 17,547 for each person (2025).

Time

Russia spans 26 time zone areas, from UTC+2:00 to UTC+12:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
