Egypt

Egypt is a country in Northern Africa. Its official name is Arab Republic of Egypt. In the language of the place it is called مصر. The capital is Cairo.

Land and position

Egypt covers about 1,001,450 square kilometres. By area it ranks 31st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Mauritania and a little larger than Tanzania. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Israel, Libya, Palestine, Sudan. Its centre lies at about 27° north, 30° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 118 million people live in Egypt (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 13th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1.4 percent of the world's people. On average there are 118 people to each square kilometre. About 43 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 72 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, English, French. A person from Egypt is called Egyptian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Egyptian pound (code EGP, symbol £). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 365 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,086 for each person (2025).

Time

Egypt uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Cairo). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
