Postal codes of Terengganu, Malaysia

157 codes, 20000 to 24300.

The GeoNames data lists 157 postal codes in Terengganu, Malaysia, covering 22 named places. In code order they run from 20000 to 24300.

The places with the most codes are Kuala Terengganu (116), Permaisuri (5), Dungun (4), Ajil (3), Jerteh (3), Kuala Besut (3). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.11 and 5.82 and longitude 102.54 and 103.47, around a centre at about 5.20, 103.11 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
