Postal codes of Melaka, Malaysia

116 codes, 75000 to 78309.

The GeoNames data lists 116 postal codes in Melaka, Malaysia, covering 18 named places. In code order they run from 75000 to 78309.

The places with the most codes are Melaka (92), Jasin (5), Masjid Tanah (3), Durian Tunggal (2), Tanjong Kling (2), Asahan (2). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.14 and 2.43 and longitude 102.10 and 102.51, around a centre at about 2.23, 102.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
