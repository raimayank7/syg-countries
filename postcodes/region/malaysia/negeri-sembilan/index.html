Postal codes of Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia

154 codes, 70000 to 73509.

The GeoNames data lists 154 postal codes in Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia, covering 25 named places. In code order they run from 70000 to 73509.

The places with the most codes are Seremban (71), Kuala Pilah (6), Port Dickson (5), Rantau (5), Mantin (5), Gemencheh (5). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.43 and 3.11 and longitude 101.77 and 102.58, around a centre at about 2.72, 102.07 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
