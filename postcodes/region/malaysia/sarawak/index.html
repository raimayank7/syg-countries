Postal codes of Sarawak, Malaysia

220 codes, 93000 to 98859.

The GeoNames data lists 220 postal codes in Sarawak, Malaysia, covering 47 named places. In code order they run from 93000 to 98859.

The places with the most codes are Kuching (116), Bintulu (11), Serian (5), Sri Aman (4), Sibu (4), Sarikei (4). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 1.02 and 4.82 and longitude 109.80 and 115.43, around a centre at about 1.96, 111.24 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
