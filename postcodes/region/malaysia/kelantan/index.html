Postal codes of Kelantan, Malaysia

131 codes, 15000 to 18500.

The GeoNames data lists 131 postal codes in Kelantan, Malaysia, covering 23 named places. In code order they run from 15000 to 18500.

The places with the most codes are Kota Bharu (87), Pasir Mas (10), Bachok (9), Tanah Merah (4), Tumpat (3), Wakaf Bharu (2). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.04 and 6.20 and longitude 101.92 and 102.44, around a centre at about 6.03, 102.23 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
