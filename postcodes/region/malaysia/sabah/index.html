Postal codes of Sabah, Malaysia

397 codes, 88000 to 91309.

The GeoNames data lists 397 postal codes in Sabah, Malaysia, covering 33 named places. In code order they run from 88000 to 91309.

The places with the most codes are Kota Kinabalu (206), Sandakan (45), Tawau (30), Lahad Datu (22), Putatan (5), Keningau (4). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.28 and 6.83 and longitude 115.38 and 118.62, around a centre at about 5.68, 116.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
