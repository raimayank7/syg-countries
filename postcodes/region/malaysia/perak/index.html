Postal codes of Perak, Malaysia

229 codes, 30000 to 39000.

The GeoNames data lists 229 postal codes in Perak, Malaysia, covering 74 named places. In code order they run from 30000 to 39000.

The places with the most codes are Ipoh (109), Teluk Intan (8), Kuala Kangsar (7), Taiping (7), Kampar (5), Tanjong Malim (4). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 3.69 and 5.47 and longitude 100.52 and 101.52, around a centre at about 4.56, 101.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
