Postal codes of Selangor, Malaysia

277 codes, 26060 to 68100.

The GeoNames data lists 277 postal codes in Selangor, Malaysia, covering 54 named places. In code order they run from 26060 to 68100.

The places with the most codes are Shah Alam (80), Petaling Jaya (78), Klang (27), Puchong (10), Subang Jaya (8), Rawang (6). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.77 and 3.78 and longitude 100.88 and 102.39, around a centre at about 3.11, 101.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
