Postal codes of Johor, Malaysia

154 codes, 79000 to 86900.

The GeoNames data lists 154 postal codes in Johor, Malaysia, covering 58 named places. In code order they run from 79000 to 86900.

The places with the most codes are Johor Bahru (60), Nusajaya (43), Sungai Mati (2), Tangkak (2), Muar (2), Kluang (2). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 1.42 and 6.14 and longitude 100.37 and 103.97, around a centre at about 1.66, 103.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
