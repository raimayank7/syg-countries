Postal codes of Kedah, Malaysia

145 codes, 05000 to 34950.

The GeoNames data lists 145 postal codes in Kedah, Malaysia, covering 36 named places. In code order they run from 05000 to 34950.

The places with the most codes are Alor Setar (75), Pendang (7), Alor Star (6), Sungai Petani (5), Kulim (5), Jitra (3). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.19 and 6.46 and longitude 99.85 and 100.78, around a centre at about 5.98, 100.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
