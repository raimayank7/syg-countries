Postal codes of Pulau Pinang, Malaysia

142 codes, 10000 to 14400.

The GeoNames data lists 142 postal codes in Pulau Pinang, Malaysia, covering 24 named places. In code order they run from 10000 to 14400.

The places with the most codes are Pulau Pinang (88), Butterworth (15), Simpang Ampat (5), Bayan Lepas (4), Bukit Mertajam (4), Balik Pulau (3). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.19 and 5.50 and longitude 100.22 and 100.50, around a centre at about 5.40, 100.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
