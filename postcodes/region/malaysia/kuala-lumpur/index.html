Postal codes of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

278 codes, 50000 to 68100.

The GeoNames data lists 278 postal codes in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, covering 4 named places. In code order they run from 50000 to 68100.

The places with the most codes are Kuala Lumpur (277), Cheras (2), Setapak (1), Batu Caves (1). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 3.08 and 3.23 and longitude 101.64 and 101.74, around a centre at about 3.14, 101.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
