Postal codes of Perlis, Malaysia

77 codes, 01000 to 02800.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Perlis, Malaysia, covering 6 named places. In code order they run from 01000 to 02800.

The places with the most codes are Kangar (67), Simpang Ampat (4), Arau (3), Kuala Perlis (1), Padang Besar (1), Kaki Bukit (1). Postal codes in Malaysia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 6.27 and 6.65 and longitude 100.13 and 100.31, around a centre at about 6.44, 100.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
