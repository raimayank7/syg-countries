Postal codes of Scotland, United Kingdom

455 codes, AB10 to ZE3.

The GeoNames data lists 455 postal codes in Scotland, United Kingdom, covering 5,459 named places and 37 sub-areas. In code order they run from AB10 to ZE3.

The places with the most codes are Glasgow (16), Milton (14), Kirkton (13), Newton (12), Edinburgh (11), Aberdeen (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Highland (1351), Aberdeenshire (729), Argyll and Bute (693), Dumfries and Galloway (420). Postal codes in United Kingdom are written one of 7 layouts ("@# #@@", "@## #@@", "@@# #@@", "@@## #@@", "@#@ #@@", "@@#@ #@@") and more.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.69 and 60.76 and longitude -7.49 and -0.80, around a centre at about 56.81, -3.96 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
