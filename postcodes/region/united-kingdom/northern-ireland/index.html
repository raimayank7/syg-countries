Postal codes of Northern Ireland, United Kingdom

81 codes, BT1 to BT94.

The GeoNames data lists 81 postal codes in Northern Ireland, United Kingdom, covering 367 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from BT1 to BT94.

The places with the most codes are Belfast (18), Craigavon (6), Enniskillen (4), Belfast West (3), Belfast South (3), Ballymena (3). The sub-areas with most codes are County Antrim (70), County Down (61), County Tyrone (57), Belfast Greater (55). Postal codes in United Kingdom are written one of 7 layouts ("@# #@@", "@## #@@", "@@# #@@", "@@## #@@", "@#@ #@@", "@@#@ #@@") and more.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.06 and 55.24 and longitude -8.08 and -5.44, around a centre at about 54.62, -6.41 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: County Antrim (70); County Down (61); County Tyrone (57); Belfast Greater (55); County Armagh (44); County Londonderry (43); County Fermanagh (16); Belfast (1).
