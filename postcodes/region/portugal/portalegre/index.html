Postal codes of Portalegre, Portugal

2,987 codes, 6040-011 to 7480-999.

The GeoNames data lists 2,987 postal codes in Portalegre, Portugal, covering 761 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6040-011 to 7480-999.

The places with the most codes are Portalegre (310), Elvas (309), Campo Maior (129), Castelo de Vide (122), Ponte de Sor (114), Alter do Chão (97). The sub-areas with most codes are Portalegre (580), Elvas (359), Ponte de Sor (341), Nisa (275). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.86 and 39.61 and longitude -8.24 and -7.03, around a centre at about 39.19, -7.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
