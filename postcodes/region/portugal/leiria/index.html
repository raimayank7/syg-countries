Postal codes of Leiria, Portugal

10,906 codes, 2400-000 to 3280-999.

The GeoNames data lists 10,906 postal codes in Leiria, Portugal, covering 2,247 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2400-000 to 3280-999.

The places with the most codes are Leiria (1182), Peniche (402), Caldas da Rainha (352), Nazaré (183), Pataias (181), Mira de Aire (171). The sub-areas with most codes are Leiria (2870), Alcobaça (1229), Peniche (1076), Pombal (1000). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.23 and 40.06 and longitude -9.38 and -8.13, around a centre at about 39.65, -8.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
