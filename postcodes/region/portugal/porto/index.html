Postal codes of Porto, Portugal

31,680 codes, 4000-000 to 5040-077.

The GeoNames data lists 31,680 postal codes in Porto, Portugal, covering 802 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4000-000 to 5040-077.

The places with the most codes are Porto (4297), Vila Nova de Gaia (1859), Maia (1837), Póvoa de Varzim (768), Matosinhos (600), Rio Tinto (479). The sub-areas with most codes are Porto (4297), Vila Nova de Gaia (3571), Gondomar (2634), Matosinhos (2272). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.03 and 41.45 and longitude -8.76 and -7.90, around a centre at about 41.22, -8.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
