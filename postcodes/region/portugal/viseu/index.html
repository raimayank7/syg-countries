Postal codes of Viseu, Portugal

9,051 codes, 3430-001 to 5130-999.

The GeoNames data lists 9,051 postal codes in Viseu, Portugal, covering 2,514 named places and 24 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3430-001 to 5130-999.

The places with the most codes are Viseu (1342), Mangualde (209), Lamego (197), Tondela (123), Nelas (110), São Pedro do Sul (98). The sub-areas with most codes are Viseu (2190), Cinfães (840), Lamego (559), Tondela (548). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.34 and 41.19 and longitude -8.35 and -7.32, around a centre at about 40.78, -7.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
