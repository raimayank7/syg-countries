Postal codes of Braga, Portugal

13,590 codes, 4615-801 to 4905-167.

The GeoNames data lists 13,590 postal codes in Braga, Portugal, covering 1,165 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4615-801 to 4905-167.

The places with the most codes are Braga (1462), Guimarães (947), Vila Nova de Famalicão (502), Barcelos (318), Fafe (318), Esposende (197). The sub-areas with most codes are Guimarães (2876), Braga (2424), Vila Nova de Famalicão (2084), Barcelos (1806). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.27 and 41.79 and longitude -8.79 and -7.82, around a centre at about 41.50, -8.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
