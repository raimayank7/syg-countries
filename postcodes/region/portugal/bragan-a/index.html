Postal codes of Bragança, Portugal

2,960 codes, 5140-000 to 5385-999.

The GeoNames data lists 2,960 postal codes in Bragança, Portugal, covering 672 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 5140-000 to 5385-999.

The places with the most codes are Bragança (476), Mirandela (361), Macedo de Cavaleiros (154), Vila Flor (125), Torre de Moncorvo (104), Mogadouro (97). The sub-areas with most codes are Bragança (664), Mirandela (510), Miranda do Douro (262), Mogadouro (236). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.06 and 41.97 and longitude -7.43 and -6.22, around a centre at about 41.50, -6.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
