Postal codes of Guarda, Portugal

3,942 codes, 3570-001 to 6440-999.

The GeoNames data lists 3,942 postal codes in Guarda, Portugal, covering 885 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3570-001 to 6440-999.

The places with the most codes are Guarda (414), Trancoso (202), Vila Nova de Foz Côa (197), Pinhel (180), Vilar Formoso (147), Seia (142). The sub-areas with most codes are Vila Nova de Foz Côa (847), Guarda (683), Seia (409), Trancoso (393). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.26 and 41.14 and longitude -7.82 and -6.82, around a centre at about 40.68, -7.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
