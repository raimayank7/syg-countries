Postal codes of Viana do Castelo, Portugal

5,992 codes, 4900-000 to 4990-999.

The GeoNames data lists 5,992 postal codes in Viana do Castelo, Portugal, covering 2,034 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4900-000 to 4990-999.

The places with the most codes are Viana do Castelo (1123), Ponte de Lima (232), Barroselas (217), Valença (183), Vila Praia de Âncora (167), Vila Nova de Anha (120). The sub-areas with most codes are Viana do Castelo (2304), Monção (862), Arcos de Valdevez (826), Ponte de Lima (792). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.63 and 42.15 and longitude -8.86 and -8.12, around a centre at about 41.85, -8.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

10 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Viana do Castelo (2304); Monção (862); Arcos de Valdevez (826); Ponte de Lima (792); Melgaço (686); Valença (580); Caminha (490); Ponte da Barca (430); Paredes de Coura (326); Vila Nova de Cerveira (194).
