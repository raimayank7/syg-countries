Postal codes of Coimbra, Portugal

9,133 codes, 3000-000 to 3420-999.

The GeoNames data lists 9,133 postal codes in Coimbra, Portugal, covering 2,009 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3000-000 to 3420-999.

The places with the most codes are Coimbra (1921), Figueira da Foz (217), Buarcos (158), Lousã (123), Oliveira do Hospital (114), Cantanhede (109). The sub-areas with most codes are Coimbra (3057), Oliveira do Hospital (1087), Figueira da Foz (935), Cantanhede (662). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.93 and 40.48 and longitude -8.90 and -7.75, around a centre at about 40.23, -8.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
