Postal codes of Aveiro, Portugal

13,668 codes, 3020-740 to 4550-999.

The GeoNames data lists 13,668 postal codes in Aveiro, Portugal, covering 1,648 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3020-740 to 4550-999.

The places with the most codes are Aveiro (971), Espinho (639), Ovar (398), São João da Madeira (340), Esmoriz (302), Argoncilhe (236). The sub-areas with most codes are Santa Maria da Feira (2767), Ovar (1565), Aveiro (1512), Oliveira de Azeméis (1041). Postal codes in Portugal are written four digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.29 and 41.08 and longitude -8.77 and -8.11, around a centre at about 40.81, -8.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
