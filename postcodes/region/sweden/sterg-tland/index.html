Postal codes of Östergötland, Sweden

789 codes, 570 60 to 618 93.

The GeoNames data lists 789 postal codes in Östergötland, Sweden, covering 51 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 570 60 to 618 93.

The places with the most codes are Linköping (205), Norrköping (175), Motala (53), Finspång (35), Mjölby (33), Åtvidaberg (28). The sub-areas with most codes are Linköping (228), Norrköping (214), Mjölby (73), Motala (62). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.82 and 58.83 and longitude 14.65 and 16.80, around a centre at about 58.47, 15.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
