Postal codes of Jämtland, Sweden

461 codes, 820 46 to 883 95.

The GeoNames data lists 461 postal codes in Jämtland, Sweden, covering 72 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 820 46 to 883 95.

The places with the most codes are Östersund (145), Frösön (40), Strömsund (30), Sveg (16), Krokom (12), Brunflo (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Östersund (137), Strömsund (51), Härjedalen (51), Åre (39). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.83 and 64.50 and longitude 12.10 and 16.80, around a centre at about 63.14, 14.65 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
