Postal codes of Halland, Sweden

490 codes, 266 95 to 519 93.

The GeoNames data lists 490 postal codes in Halland, Sweden, covering 71 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 266 95 to 519 93.

The places with the most codes are Halmstad (108), Varberg (53), Falkenberg (47), Kungsbacka (40), Onsala (20), Laholm (19). The sub-areas with most codes are Halmstad (141), Kungsbacka (100), Varberg (88), Falkenberg (66). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.25 and 57.57 and longitude 11.93 and 13.52, around a centre at about 56.96, 12.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
