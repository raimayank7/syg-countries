Postal codes of Gävleborg, Sweden

586 codes, 774 68 to 829 98.

The GeoNames data lists 586 postal codes in Gävleborg, Sweden, covering 72 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 774 68 to 829 98.

The places with the most codes are Gävle (146), Hudiksvall (46), Sandviken (40), Bollnäs (33), Söderhamn (30), Ljusdal (21). The sub-areas with most codes are Gävle (163), Hudiksvall (80), Sandviken (68), Bollnäs (62). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.28 and 62.13 and longitude 15.00 and 17.34, around a centre at about 61.12, 16.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
