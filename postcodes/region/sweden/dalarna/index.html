Postal codes of Dalarna, Sweden

522 codes, 770 10 to 797 92.

The GeoNames data lists 522 postal codes in Dalarna, Sweden, covering 76 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 770 10 to 797 92.

The places with the most codes are Borlänge (77), Falun (74), Ludvika (37), Mora (32), Avesta (28), Leksand (20). The sub-areas with most codes are Falun (79), Borlänge (76), Ludvika (51), Avesta (44). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.08 and 61.42 and longitude 13.07 and 16.42, around a centre at about 60.54, 15.23 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
