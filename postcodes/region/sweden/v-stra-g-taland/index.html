Postal codes of Västra Götaland, Sweden

3,324 codes, 330 27 to 668 95.

The GeoNames data lists 3,324 postal codes in Västra Götaland, Sweden, covering 316 named places and 49 sub-areas. In code order they run from 330 27 to 668 95.

The places with the most codes are Göteborg (758), Borås (256), Skövde (90), Västra Frölunda (76), Uddevalla (62), Trollhättan (62). The sub-areas with most codes are Göteborg (780), Borås (235), Skövde (103), Mölndal (86). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.00 and 59.05 and longitude 11.17 and 14.65, around a centre at about 57.97, 12.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
