Postal codes of Västmanland, Sweden

435 codes, 640 45 to 749 72.

The GeoNames data lists 435 postal codes in Västmanland, Sweden, covering 30 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 640 45 to 749 72.

The places with the most codes are Västerås (171), Köping (41), Fagersta (32), Arboga (29), Sala (27), Hallstahammar (23). The sub-areas with most codes are Västerås (163), Köping (47), Sala (43), Arboga (33). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.39 and 60.08 and longitude 15.55 and 16.95, around a centre at about 59.67, 16.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
