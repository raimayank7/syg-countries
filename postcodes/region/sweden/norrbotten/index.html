Postal codes of Norrbotten, Sweden

583 codes, 930 81 to 986 31.

The GeoNames data lists 583 postal codes in Norrbotten, Sweden, covering 96 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 930 81 to 986 31.

The places with the most codes are Luleå (126), Piteå (48), Boden (47), Kiruna (46), Kalix (26), Gällivare (23). The sub-areas with most codes are Luleå (140), Piteå (85), Boden (54), Kiruna (52). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 65.15 and 68.44 and longitude 17.89 and 24.14, around a centre at about 66.10, 21.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
