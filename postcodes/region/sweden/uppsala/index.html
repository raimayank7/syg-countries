Postal codes of Uppsala, Sweden

614 codes, 740 10 to 819 66.

The GeoNames data lists 614 postal codes in Uppsala, Sweden, covering 47 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 740 10 to 819 66.

The places with the most codes are Uppsala (277), Enköping (66), Bålsta (30), Knivsta (30), Tierp (26), Skutskär (16). The sub-areas with most codes are Uppsala (283), Enköping (50), Östhammar (49), Tierp (43). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.57 and 60.64 and longitude 16.82 and 18.48, around a centre at about 59.92, 17.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
