Postal codes of Gotland, Sweden

186 codes, 620 10 to 624 70.

The GeoNames data lists 186 postal codes in Gotland, Sweden, covering 15 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 620 10 to 624 70.

The places with the most codes are Visby (76), Hemse (15), Romakloster (14), Slite (11), Klintehamn (10), Lärbro (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Gotland (76). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.05 and 57.92 and longitude 18.20 and 19.13, around a centre at about 57.53, 18.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
