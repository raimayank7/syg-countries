Postal codes of Jönköping, Sweden

622 codes, 330 10 to 578 95.

The GeoNames data lists 622 postal codes in Jönköping, Sweden, covering 73 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 330 10 to 578 95.

The places with the most codes are Jönköping (126), Värnamo (44), Huskvarna (36), Nässjö (34), Tranås (34), Vetlanda (27). The sub-areas with most codes are Jönköping (196), Värnamo (61), Nässjö (57), Gislaved (52). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.03 and 58.13 and longitude 13.25 and 15.57, around a centre at about 57.62, 14.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
