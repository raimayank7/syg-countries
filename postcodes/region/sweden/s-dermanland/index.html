Postal codes of Södermanland, Sweden

459 codes, 610 53 to 649 91.

The GeoNames data lists 459 postal codes in Södermanland, Sweden, covering 41 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 610 53 to 649 91.

The places with the most codes are Eskilstuna (106), Nyköping (59), Katrineholm (38), Strängnäs (38), Flen (23), Oxelösund (21). The sub-areas with most codes are Eskilstuna (125), Nyköping (77), Strängnäs (68), Katrineholm (56). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.83 and 59.42 and longitude 13.80 and 17.55, around a centre at about 59.10, 16.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
