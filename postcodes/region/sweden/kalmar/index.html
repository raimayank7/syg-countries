Postal codes of Kalmar, Sweden

559 codes, 360 23 to 598 98.

The GeoNames data lists 559 postal codes in Kalmar, Sweden, covering 83 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 360 23 to 598 98.

The places with the most codes are Kalmar (84), Västervik (45), Oskarshamn (39), Nybro (30), Vimmerby (29), Hultsfred (22). The sub-areas with most codes are Kalmar (100), Västervik (72), Vimmerby (54), Nybro (51). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.35 and 58.14 and longitude 15.25 and 17.00, around a centre at about 57.09, 16.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
