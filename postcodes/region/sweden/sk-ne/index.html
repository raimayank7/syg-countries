Postal codes of Skåne, Sweden

2,482 codes, 200 01 to 298 96.

The GeoNames data lists 2,482 postal codes in Skåne, Sweden, covering 203 named places and 33 sub-areas. In code order they run from 200 01 to 298 96.

The places with the most codes are Malmö (621), Helsingborg (251), Lund (140), Kristianstad (68), Ängelholm (52), Ystad (47). The sub-areas with most codes are Helsingborg (209), Kristianstad (142), Hässleholm (96), Ängelholm (77). Postal codes in Sweden are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.33 and 56.47 and longitude 12.50 and 14.52, around a centre at about 55.81, 13.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
