176 codes, 6297 to 7999.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.05 and 43.83 and longitude 26.00 and 27.09, around a centre at about 43.18, 26.36 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
