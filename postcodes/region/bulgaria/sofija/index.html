Postal codes of София / Sofija, Bulgaria

203 codes, 2000 to 2782.

The GeoNames data lists 203 postal codes in София / Sofija, Bulgaria, covering 280 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2000 to 2782.

The places with the most codes are Елов дол / Elov dol (1), Храбърско / Khrabursko (1), Мала Раковица / Mala Rakovica (1), Делян / Deljan (1), Росоман / Rosoman (1), Златуша / Zlatusha (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Своге / Svoge (37), Драгоман / Dragoman (34), Ихтиман / Ikhtiman (28), Самоков / Samokov (28). Postal codes in Bulgaria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.87 and 43.14 and longitude 22.78 and 24.35, around a centre at about 42.76, 23.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
