Postal codes of Габрово / Gabrovo, Bulgaria

114 codes, 5050 to 9498.

The GeoNames data lists 114 postal codes in Габрово / Gabrovo, Bulgaria, covering 355 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 5050 to 9498.

The places with the most codes are Генчовци / Genchovci (2), Малчовци / Malchovci (2), Мечковица / Mechkovica (1), Геновци / Genovci (1), Ветрово / Vetrovo (1), Руйчовци / Rujchovci (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Габрово / Gabrovo (134), Трявна / Trjavna (107), Дряново / Drjanovo (63), Севлиево / Sevlievo (53). Postal codes in Bulgaria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.72 and 43.72 and longitude 24.88 and 27.22, around a centre at about 42.90, 25.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
