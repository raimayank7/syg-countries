Postal codes of Русе / Ruse, Bulgaria

82 codes, 7000 to 7958.

The GeoNames data lists 82 postal codes in Русе / Ruse, Bulgaria, covering 83 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7000 to 7958.

The places with the most codes are Обретеник / Obretenik (1), Екзарх Йосиф / Ekzarkh Josif (1), Батин / Batin (1), Горно Абланово / Gorno Ablanovo (1), Борово / Borovo (1), Волово / Volovo (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Иваново / Ivanovo (13), Русе / Ruse (13), Две могили / Dve mogili (12), Бяла / Bjala (11). Postal codes in Bulgaria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.03 and 43.98 and longitude 25.52 and 26.93, around a centre at about 43.65, 26.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
