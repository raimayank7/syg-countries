Postal codes of Klaipėda County, Lithuania

53 codes, 00001 to 99069.

The GeoNames data lists 53 postal codes in Klaipėda County, Lithuania, covering 950 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00001 to 99069.

The places with the most codes are Girininkų k. (5), Šilininkų k. (4), Klaipėda (3), Medsėdžių k. (3), Laugalių k. (3), Šakinių k. (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Klaipėdos r. sav. (338), Šilutės r. sav. (328), Kretingos r. sav. (194), Skuodo r. sav. (185). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.14 and 56.28 and longitude 21.06 and 22.94, around a centre at about 55.73, 21.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
