Postal codes of Kaunas County, Lithuania

102 codes, 44001 to 60070.

The GeoNames data lists 102 postal codes in Kaunas County, Lithuania, covering 2,398 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 44001 to 60070.

The places with the most codes are Kaunas (20), Pakalniškių k. (7), Būdų k. (6), Žemaitkiemio k. (5), Pagojo k. (5), Pagirių k. (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Kauno r. sav. (553), Raseinių r. sav. (542), Prienų r. sav. (414), Kėdainių r. sav. (410). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.53 and 55.53 and longitude 22.78 and 24.60, around a centre at about 55.07, 23.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
