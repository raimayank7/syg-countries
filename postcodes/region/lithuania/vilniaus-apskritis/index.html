Postal codes of Vilniaus apskritis, Lithuania

1,459 codes, 01001 to 27102.

The GeoNames data lists 1,459 postal codes in Vilniaus apskritis, Lithuania, covering 3,464 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01001 to 27102.

The places with the most codes are Vilnius (1360), Slabados k. (9), Naujakiemio k. (8), Gudelių k. (7), Bajorų k. (7), Miškinių k. (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Vilniaus m. sav. (1563), Vilniaus r. sav. (1167), Švenčionių r. sav. (541), Ukmergės r. sav. (508). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.17 and 55.40 and longitude 24.50 and 26.60, around a centre at about 54.79, 25.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
