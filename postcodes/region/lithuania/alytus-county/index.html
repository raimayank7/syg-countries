Postal codes of Alytus County, Lithuania

51 codes, 62001 to 67083.

The GeoNames data lists 51 postal codes in Alytus County, Lithuania, covering 996 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 62001 to 67083.

The places with the most codes are Kaniūkų k. (5), Būdos k. (5), Neravų k. (4), Žagarių k. (4), Barčių k. (4), Papiškių k. (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Alytaus r. sav. (430), Lazdijų r. sav. (315), Varėnos r. sav. (305), Druskininkų sav. (72). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.00 and 54.50 and longitude 23.43 and 24.72, around a centre at about 54.28, 24.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
