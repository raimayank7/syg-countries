Postal codes of Tauragė County, Lithuania

51 codes, 72001 to 99035.

The GeoNames data lists 51 postal codes in Tauragė County, Lithuania, covering 1,097 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 72001 to 99035.

The places with the most codes are Kalniškių k. (7), Dvarviečių k. (5), Naujininkų k. (4), Milaičių k. (3), Balandžių k. (3), Girininkų k. (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Šilalės r. sav. (424), Jurbarko r. sav. (369), Tauragės r. sav. (305), Pagėgių sav. (105). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.94 and 55.63 and longitude 21.83 and 23.42, around a centre at about 55.31, 22.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
