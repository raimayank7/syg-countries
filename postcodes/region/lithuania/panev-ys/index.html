Postal codes of Panevėžys, Lithuania

63 codes, 35001 to 42069.

The GeoNames data lists 63 postal codes in Panevėžys, Lithuania, covering 2,143 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 35001 to 42069.

The places with the most codes are Panevėžys (9), Naujikų k. (7), Bajoriškių k. (7), Girelės k. (6), Užubalių k. (5), Smilgių k. (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Panevėžio r. sav. (719), Rokiškio r. sav. (595), Biržų r. sav. (429), Kupiškio r. sav. (349). Postal codes in Lithuania are written the fixed text "LT", then a hyphen, then five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.48 and 56.40 and longitude 24.10 and 25.80, around a centre at about 55.89, 24.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
