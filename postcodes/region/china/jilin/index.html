Postal codes of Jilin, China

52 codes, 130000 to 138000.

The GeoNames data lists 52 postal codes in Jilin, China, covering 52 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 130000 to 138000.

The places with the most codes are Changchun (1), Nong'an County (1), Dehui (1), Yushu (1), Jiutai District (1), Shuangyang District (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Yanbian Chaoxianzu Zizhizhou (8), Changchun Shi (6), Jilin Shi (6), Siping Shi (5). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.13 and 45.62 and longitude 122.78 and 130.36, around a centre at about 43.71, 125.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
