Postal codes of Liaoning, China

59 codes, 110000 to 125300.

The GeoNames data lists 59 postal codes in Liaoning, China, covering 59 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 110000 to 125300.

The places with the most codes are Shenyang (1), Liaozhong District (1), Xinmin (1), Faku County (1), Kangping County (1), Dalian (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Dalian Shi (7), Tieling Shi (6), Chaoyang Shi (6), Shenyang Shi (5). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.91 and 42.54 and longitude 119.40 and 124.39, around a centre at about 41.14, 122.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
