Postal codes of Sichuan, China

164 codes, 610000 to 646500.

The GeoNames data lists 164 postal codes in Sichuan, China, covering 164 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 610000 to 646500.

The places with the most codes are Chengdu (1), Longquanyi District (1), Shuangliu District (1), Qingbaijiang District (1), Jintang County (1), Wenjiang District (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Garzê (18), Liangshan Yizu Zizhizhou (17), Chengdu Shi (15), Aba Zangzu Qiangzu Zizhizhou (13). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 28.76 and 32.53 and longitude 102.22 and 108.04, around a centre at about 30.78, 104.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
