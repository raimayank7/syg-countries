Postal codes of Guizhou, China

79 codes, 550000 to 565300.

The GeoNames data lists 79 postal codes in Guizhou, China, covering 79 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 550000 to 565300.

The places with the most codes are Guiyang (1), Xiuwen County (1), Kaiyang County (1), Xifeng County (1), Qingzhen (1), Liupanshui (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Qiandongnan Miao and Dong Autonomous Prefecture (15), Zunyi Shi (13), Qiannan Bouyeizu Miaozu Zizhizhou (12), Tongren Diqu (10). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 25.10 and 28.57 and longitude 104.32 and 108.54, around a centre at about 26.85, 106.33 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
