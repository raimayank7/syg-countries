Postal codes of Jiangsu, China

75 codes, 210000 to 226600.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in Jiangsu, China, covering 76 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 210000 to 226600.

The places with the most codes are Nanjing (1), Jiangning District (1), Lishui District (1), Gaochun District (1), Luhe District (1), Jiangpu County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Yancheng Shi (8), Xuzhou (7), Suzhou Shi (7), Nantong Shi (7). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 31.30 and 34.60 and longitude 117.28 and 121.17, around a centre at about 32.73, 119.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
