Postal codes of Hunan, China

100 codes, 410000 to 427200.

The GeoNames data lists 100 postal codes in Hunan, China, covering 99 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 410000 to 427200.

The places with the most codes are Lengshuijiang (2), Changsha (1), Changsha County (1), Wangcheng District (1), Liuyang (1), Ningxiang City (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Huaihua Prefecture (11), Hengyang Shi (9), Shaoyang County (9), Changde Shi (9). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 25.77 and 29.14 and longitude 110.00 and 113.63, around a centre at about 27.64, 111.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
