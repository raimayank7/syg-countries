Postal codes of Guangdong, China

99 codes, 510000 to 529800.

The GeoNames data lists 99 postal codes in Guangdong, China, covering 101 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 510000 to 529800.

The places with the most codes are Guangzhou (1), Huadu District (1), Conghua District (1), Zengcheng District (1), Panyu District and Nansha District (1), Xinfeng County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Shaoguan Shi (8), Qingyuan Shi (8), Zhaoqing Shi (7), Meizhou Shi (7). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 20.92 and 25.13 and longitude 110.08 and 116.62, around a centre at about 23.19, 113.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
