Postal codes of Heilongjiang, China

80 codes, 150000 to 165300.

The GeoNames data lists 80 postal codes in Heilongjiang, China, covering 80 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 150000 to 165300.

The places with the most codes are Harbin (1), Shuangcheng District (1), Wuchang (1), Acheng District (1), Bin County (1), Hulan District (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Harbin Shi (13), Qiqihar Shi (10), Suihua Shi (9), Jiamusi Shi (8). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.08 and 49.17 and longitude 123.96 and 134.30, around a centre at about 46.87, 127.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
