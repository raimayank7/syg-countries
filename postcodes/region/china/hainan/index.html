Postal codes of Hainan, China

21 codes, 570000 to 578000.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Hainan, China, covering 21 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 570000 to 578000.

The places with the most codes are Wuzhishan City (1), Ledong Li Autonomous County (1), Dongfang (1), Changjiang Li Autonomous County (1), Lingao County (1), Lingshui Li Autonomous County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Haikou Shi (2), Danzhou Shi (2), Wuzhishan City (1), Ledong Li Autonomous County (1). Postal codes in China are written six digits.
