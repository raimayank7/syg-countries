Postal codes of Henan, China

125 codes, 450000 to 477200.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Henan, China, covering 124 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 450000 to 477200.

The places with the most codes are Qi County (2), Jiyuan (1), Zhengzhou (1), Xingyang (1), Xinzheng (1), Gongyi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Nanyang Shi (12), Zhoukou Shi (11), Luoyang Shi (10), Xinyang Shi (9). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.08 and 36.10 and longitude 110.86 and 116.36, around a centre at about 34.30, 113.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
