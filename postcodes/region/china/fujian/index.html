Postal codes of Fujian, China

67 codes, 350000 to 366300.

The GeoNames data lists 67 postal codes in Fujian, China, covering 67 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 350000 to 366300.

The places with the most codes are Fuzhou (1), Minhou County (1), Changle (1), Fuqing (1), Pingtan County (1), Lianjiang County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Sanming Shi (11), Zhangzhou Shi (10), Nanping Shi (10), Fuzhou Shi (9). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 24.51 and 27.76 and longitude 117.02 and 120.21, around a centre at about 25.93, 118.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
