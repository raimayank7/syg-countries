Postal codes of Xinjiang, China

82 codes, 830000 to 848500.

The GeoNames data lists 82 postal codes in Xinjiang, China, covering 82 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 830000 to 848500.

The places with the most codes are Shihezi (1), Ürümqi (1), Karamay (1), Hami City (1), Barköl Kazakh Autonomous County (1), Yiwu County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kaxgar Diqu (12), Ili Kazak Zizhizhou (10), Bayingolin Mongol Zizhizhou (9), Aksu Diqu (9). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.42 and 46.75 and longitude 76.17 and 87.97, around a centre at about 41.78, 80.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
