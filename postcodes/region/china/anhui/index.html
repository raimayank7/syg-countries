Postal codes of Anhui, China

78 codes, 230000 to 247200.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in Anhui, China, covering 78 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 230000 to 247200.

The places with the most codes are Hefei (1), Changfeng County (1), Feixi County (1), Lujiang County (1), Feidong County (1), Chaohu (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Anqing Shi (10), Chuzhou Shi (7), Xuancheng Shi (7), Hefei Shi (6). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 29.71 and 33.97 and longitude 115.36 and 119.01, around a centre at about 31.73, 117.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
