Postal codes of Qinghai, China

41 codes, 624700 to 817500.

The GeoNames data lists 41 postal codes in Qinghai, China, covering 41 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 624700 to 817500.

The places with the most codes are Xining (1), Datong Hui and Tu Autonomous County (1), Ping'an District (1), Ledu District (1), Huangzhong County (1), Huangyuan County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Haixi (7), Xining Shi (6), Golog Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture (6), Yushu Zangzu Zizhizhou (6). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.32 and 44.70 and longitude 82.12 and 102.31, around a centre at about 37.75, 93.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
