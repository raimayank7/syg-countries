Postal codes of Inner Mongolia, China

77 codes, 010000 to 029300.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Inner Mongolia, China, covering 77 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 010000 to 029300.

The places with the most codes are Hohhot (1), Tumed Left Banner (1), Togtoh County (1), Horinger County (1), Qingshuihe County (1), Wuchuan County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Xilin Gol Meng (12), Wulanchabu Shi (11), Chifeng Shi (10), Hulunbuir Region (10). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.18 and 50.78 and longitude 106.82 and 122.27, around a centre at about 43.41, 114.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
