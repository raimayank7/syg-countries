Postal codes of Hubei, China

80 codes, 430000 to 448200.

The GeoNames data lists 80 postal codes in Hubei, China, covering 80 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 430000 to 448200.

The places with the most codes are Tianmen (1), Qianjiang (1), Xiantao (1), Shennongjia (1), Wuhan (1), Caidian District (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Yichangshi (9), Huanggang Shi (9), Xiangyang (8), Enshi Tujiazu Miaozu Zizhizhou (8). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 29.72 and 32.65 and longitude 110.67 and 115.55, around a centre at about 30.94, 112.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
