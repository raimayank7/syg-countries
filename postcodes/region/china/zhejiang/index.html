Postal codes of Zhejiang, China

74 codes, 202400 to 325800.

The GeoNames data lists 74 postal codes in Zhejiang, China, covering 74 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 202400 to 325800.

The places with the most codes are Hangzhou (1), Yuhang District (1), Xiaoshan District (1), Lin'an (1), Fuyang District (1), Tonglu County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Lishui Shi (10), Ningbo Shi (9), Wenzhou Shi (9), Hangzhou Shi (8). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 27.78 and 31.22 and longitude 118.62 and 122.20, around a centre at about 29.33, 120.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
