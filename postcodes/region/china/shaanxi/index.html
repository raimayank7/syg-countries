Postal codes of Shaanxi, China

98 codes, 710000 to 727500.

The GeoNames data lists 98 postal codes in Shaanxi, China, covering 98 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 710000 to 727500.

The places with the most codes are Xi'an (1), Chang'an District (1), Gaoling District (1), Hu County (1), Zhouzhi County (1), Lantian County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Yan’an Shi (13), Xianyang Shi (12), Weinan Shi (12), Yulin Shi (12). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.68 and 38.29 and longitude 107.02 and 109.93, around a centre at about 34.53, 108.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
