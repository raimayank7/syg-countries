Postal codes of Yunnan, China

113 codes, 650000 to 679300.

The GeoNames data lists 113 postal codes in Yunnan, China, covering 111 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 650000 to 679300.

The places with the most codes are Yiliang County (2), Yimen County (2), Jingdong Yi Autonomous County (1), Kunming (1), Xishan District (1), Guandu District (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kunming Shi (14), Honghe Hanizu Yizu Zizhizhou (13), Dali Baizu Zizhizhou (11), Yuxi Shi (10). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 23.36 and 27.32 and longitude 98.51 and 103.78, around a centre at about 25.19, 101.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
