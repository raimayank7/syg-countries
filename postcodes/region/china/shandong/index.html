Postal codes of Shandong, China

125 codes, 250000 to 277700.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Shandong, China, covering 124 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 250000 to 277700.

The places with the most codes are Huangdao District (2), Jinan (1), Licheng District (1), Zhangqiu (1), Changqing District (1), Pingyin County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Dezhou Shi (12), Yantai Shi (11), Weifang Shi (11), Jining Shi (11). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.86 and 37.73 and longitude 115.47 and 122.44, around a centre at about 36.50, 117.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
