Postal codes of Hebei, China

144 codes, 050000 to 253800.

The GeoNames data lists 144 postal codes in Hebei, China, covering 143 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 050000 to 253800.

The places with the most codes are Wei County (2), Shijiazhuang (1), Jingxing Mining District (1), Luquan District (1), Jingxing County (1), Pingshan County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Baoding Shi (22), Shijiazhuang Shi (19), Xingtai County (18), Zhangjiakou Shi (16). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.61 and 40.95 and longitude 114.20 and 119.59, around a centre at about 38.53, 115.91 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
