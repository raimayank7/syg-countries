Postal codes of Tibet, China

75 codes, 850000 to 860700.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in Tibet, China, covering 72 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 850000 to 860700.

The places with the most codes are Gamba County (2), Sa'gya County (2), Dinggyê County (2), Lhasa (1), Dagzê County (1), Maizhokunggar County (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Rikaze Diqu (21), Shannan Diqu (12), Qamdo Shi (11), Nagqu Shi (10). Postal codes in China are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 28.72 and 31.48 and longitude 91.10 and 97.07, around a centre at about 30.45, 91.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
