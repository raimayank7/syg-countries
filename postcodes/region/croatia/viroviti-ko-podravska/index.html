Postal codes of Virovitičko-Podravska, Croatia

5 codes, 33000 to 33520.

The GeoNames data lists 5 postal codes in Virovitičko-Podravska, Croatia, covering 190 named places. In code order they run from 33000 to 33520.

The places with the most codes are Brezik (2), Milanovac (2), Rit (1), Lukač (1), Kapela Dvor (1), Bušetina (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.50 and 45.97 and longitude 17.17 and 18.00, around a centre at about 45.73, 17.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
