Postal codes of Karlovačka, Croatia

6 codes, 47000 to 47300.

The GeoNames data lists 6 postal codes in Karlovačka, Croatia, covering 647 named places. In code order they run from 47000 to 47300.

The places with the most codes are Čatrnja (2), Zagorje (2), Sjeničak Lasinjski (1), Jarnevići (1), Jadrići (1), Jurovski Brod (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.94 and 45.72 and longitude 15.04 and 15.90, around a centre at about 45.36, 15.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
