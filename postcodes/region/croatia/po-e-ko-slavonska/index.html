Postal codes of Požeško-Slavonska, Croatia

5 codes, 34000 to 34551.

The GeoNames data lists 5 postal codes in Požeško-Slavonska, Croatia, covering 270 named places. In code order they run from 34000 to 34551.

The places with the most codes are Skenderovci (2), Antunovac (2), Klisa (2), Toranj (2), Bučje (2), Češljakovci (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.22 and 45.51 and longitude 16.96 and 18.10, around a centre at about 45.39, 17.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
