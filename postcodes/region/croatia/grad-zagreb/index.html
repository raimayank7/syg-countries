Postal codes of Grad Zagreb, Croatia

16 codes, 10000 to 10363.

The GeoNames data lists 16 postal codes in Grad Zagreb, Croatia, covering 110 named places. In code order they run from 10000 to 10363.

The places with the most codes are Zagreb-Črnomerec (1), Zagreb-Donji Grad (1), Zagreb-Podsljeme (1), Sljeme (Medvednica-Tomislavac) (1), Zagreb-Maksimir (1), Zagreb-Gornji Grad- Medveščak (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.63 and 45.94 and longitude 15.78 and 16.21, around a centre at about 45.82, 16.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
