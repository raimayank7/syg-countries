Postal codes of Ličko-Senjska, Croatia

10 codes, 53000 to 53291.

The GeoNames data lists 10 postal codes in Ličko-Senjska, Croatia, covering 262 named places. In code order they run from 53000 to 53291.

The places with the most codes are Doljani (2), Lički Ribnik (1), Trnovac (1), Debelo Brdo I (1), Žabica (1), Široka Kula (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.38 and 45.07 and longitude 14.75 and 16.10, around a centre at about 44.70, 15.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
