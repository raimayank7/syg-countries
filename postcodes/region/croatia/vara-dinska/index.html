Postal codes of Varaždinska, Croatia

10 codes, 42000 to 42250.

The GeoNames data lists 10 postal codes in Varaždinska, Croatia, covering 300 named places. In code order they run from 42000 to 42250.

The places with the most codes are Selnik (2), Gornji Kućan (1), Prekno (1), Jalkovec (1), Gojanec (1), Šijanec (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.03 and 46.38 and longitude 15.90 and 16.74, around a centre at about 46.25, 16.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
