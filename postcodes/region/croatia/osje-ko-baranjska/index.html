Postal codes of Osječko-Baranjska, Croatia

14 codes, 31000 to 34551.

The GeoNames data lists 14 postal codes in Osječko-Baranjska, Croatia, covering 260 named places. In code order they run from 31000 to 34551.

The places with the most codes are Nemetin (1), Osijek (1), Podravlje (1), Sarvaš (1), Tvrđavica (1), Divoš (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.23 and 45.89 and longitude 17.06 and 19.06, around a centre at about 45.55, 18.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
