Postal codes of Primorsko-Goranska, Croatia

27 codes, 51000 to 51562.

The GeoNames data lists 27 postal codes in Primorsko-Goranska, Croatia, covering 506 named places. In code order they run from 51000 to 51562.

The places with the most codes are Ilovik (2), Ponikve (2), Podstene (2), Sveti Anton (2), Kostrena (1), Rijeka (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.46 and 45.64 and longitude 14.16 and 15.22, around a centre at about 45.30, 14.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
