Postal codes of Vukovarsko-Srijemska, Croatia

10 codes, 32000 to 32284.

The GeoNames data lists 10 postal codes in Vukovarsko-Srijemska, Croatia, covering 87 named places. In code order they run from 32000 to 32284.

The places with the most codes are Vukovar (1), Lipovača (1), Ćelije (1), Pačetin (1), Bogdanovci (1), Petrovci (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.86 and 45.46 and longitude 18.54 and 19.38, around a centre at about 45.22, 18.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
