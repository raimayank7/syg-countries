Postal codes of Zagrebačka, Croatia

22 codes, 10255 to 10454.

The GeoNames data lists 22 postal codes in Zagrebačka, Croatia, covering 661 named places. In code order they run from 10255 to 10454.

The places with the most codes are Kraj Gornji (2), Luka (2), Kraljev Vrh (2), Goli Vrh (2), Celine (2), Strmec (2). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.48 and 46.39 and longitude 15.37 and 16.69, around a centre at about 45.79, 16.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
