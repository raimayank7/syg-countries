Postal codes of Krapinsko-Zagorska, Croatia

17 codes, 49000 to 49290.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Krapinsko-Zagorska, Croatia, covering 423 named places. In code order they run from 49000 to 49290.

The places with the most codes are Gregurovec (2), Sveti Križ (2), Jazvine (1), Pretkovec (1), Mala Pačetina (1), Pristava Krapinska (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.95 and 46.21 and longitude 15.62 and 16.23, around a centre at about 46.09, 15.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
