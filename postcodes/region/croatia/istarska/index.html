Postal codes of Istarska, Croatia

13 codes, 52000 to 52470.

The GeoNames data lists 13 postal codes in Istarska, Croatia, covering 636 named places. In code order they run from 52000 to 52470.

The places with the most codes are Brajkovići (2), Brčići (2), Žužići (2), Rajki (2), Orbanići (2), Prhati (2). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.80 and 45.50 and longitude 13.50 and 14.18, around a centre at about 45.22, 13.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
