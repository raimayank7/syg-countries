Postal codes of Zadarska, Croatia

34 codes, 23000 to 23450.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Zadarska, Croatia, covering 221 named places. In code order they run from 23000 to 23450.

The places with the most codes are Gorica (2), Lovinac (2), Murvica Gornja (1), Crno (1), Briševo (1), Kožino (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.86 and 44.49 and longitude 14.60 and 16.11, around a centre at about 44.14, 15.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
