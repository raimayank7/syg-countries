Postal codes of Brodsko-Posavska, Croatia

11 codes, 35000 to 35430.

The GeoNames data lists 11 postal codes in Brodsko-Posavska, Croatia, covering 185 named places. In code order they run from 35000 to 35430.

The places with the most codes are Brđani (2), Rastušje (1), Stupnički Kuti (1), Donja Vrba (1), Zbjeg (1), Kindrovo (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.05 and 45.37 and longitude 17.17 and 18.49, around a centre at about 45.21, 17.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
