Postal codes of Bjelovarsko-Bilogorska, Croatia

6 codes, 43000 to 43500.

The GeoNames data lists 6 postal codes in Bjelovarsko-Bilogorska, Croatia, covering 316 named places. In code order they run from 43000 to 43500.

The places with the most codes are Martinac (2), Babinac (2), Prokljuvani (2), Rastovac (2), Kobasičari (1), Breza (1). Postal codes in Croatia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.52 and 46.07 and longitude 16.52 and 17.45, around a centre at about 45.76, 16.96 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
