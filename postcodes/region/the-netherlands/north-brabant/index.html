Postal codes of North Brabant, The Netherlands

521 codes, 4251 to 9999.

The GeoNames data lists 521 postal codes in North Brabant, The Netherlands, covering 276 named places and 56 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4251 to 9999.

The places with the most codes are Eindhoven (34), Tilburg (25), Breda (22), 's-Hertogenbosch (16), Bergen op Zoom (12), Rosmalen (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Oss Municipality (38), Land van Cuijk (35), Eindhoven Municipality (34), Gemeente 's-Hertogenbosch (31). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.24 and 51.82 and longitude 4.22 and 6.03, around a centre at about 51.58, 5.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
