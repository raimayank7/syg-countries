Postal codes of Overijssel, The Netherlands

299 codes, 7146 to 9999.

The GeoNames data lists 299 postal codes in Overijssel, The Netherlands, covering 175 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7146 to 9999.

The places with the most codes are Zwolle (26), Enschede (23), Deventer (18), Almelo (10), Hengelo (9), Kampen (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Hardenberg (44), Gemeente Steenwijkerland (34), Gemeente Zwolle (26), Deventer Municipality (24). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.15 and 52.83 and longitude 5.84 and 7.04, around a centre at about 52.45, 6.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
