Postal codes of Drenthe, The Netherlands

263 codes, 7571 to 9766.

The GeoNames data lists 263 postal codes in Drenthe, The Netherlands, covering 217 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7571 to 9766.

The places with the most codes are Emmen (13), Hoogeveen (10), Assen (8), Meppel (6), Nieuweroord (3), Beilen (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Aa en Hunze (35), Gemeente Emmen (32), Coevorden Municipality (28), Borger-Odoorn Municipality (27). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.62 and 53.19 and longitude 6.16 and 7.04, around a centre at about 52.88, 6.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
