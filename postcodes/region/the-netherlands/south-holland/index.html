Postal codes of South Holland, The Netherlands

548 codes, 1428 to 9999.

The GeoNames data lists 548 postal codes in South Holland, The Netherlands, covering 184 named places and 49 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1428 to 9999.

The places with the most codes are Rotterdam (67), 's-Gravenhage (62), Zoetermeer (17), Leiden (16), Schiedam (14), Delft (13). The sub-areas with most codes are Rotterdam Municipality (79), Gemeente Den Haag (62), Hoeksche Waard (21), Molenlanden (20). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.68 and 52.30 and longitude 3.89 and 5.01, around a centre at about 51.98, 4.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
