Postal codes of Guelders, The Netherlands

500 codes, 1954 to 8611.

The GeoNames data lists 500 postal codes in Guelders, The Netherlands, covering 327 named places and 51 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1954 to 8611.

The places with the most codes are Arnhem (27), Nijmegen (23), Apeldoorn (22), Wageningen (10), Doetinchem (9), Harderwijk (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Apeldoorn (34), West Betuwe (28), Arnhem Municipality (27), Nijmegen Municipality (24). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.75 and 52.51 and longitude 5.01 and 6.80, around a centre at about 52.01, 5.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
