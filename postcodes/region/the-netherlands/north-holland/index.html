Postal codes of North Holland, The Netherlands

476 codes, 1011 to 3633.

The GeoNames data lists 476 postal codes in North Holland, The Netherlands, covering 234 named places and 44 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1011 to 3633.

The places with the most codes are Amsterdam (81), Haarlem (19), Alkmaar (14), Hilversum (11), Amstelveen (10), Zaandam (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Amsterdam (85), Gemeente Haarlemmermeer (33), Alkmaar Municipality (32), Gemeente Zaanstad (23). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.18 and 53.15 and longitude 4.53 and 5.28, around a centre at about 52.50, 4.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
