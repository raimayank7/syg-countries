Postal codes of Zeeland, The Netherlands

153 codes, 4301 to 4698.

The GeoNames data lists 153 postal codes in Zeeland, The Netherlands, covering 124 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4301 to 4698.

The places with the most codes are Middelburg (9), Terneuzen (8), Vlissingen (8), Goes (7), Zierikzee (3), 's-Heerenhoek (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Terneuzen (20), Schouwen-Duiveland Municipality (20), Borsele Municipality (16), Hulst Municipality (16). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.21 and 51.73 and longitude 3.38 and 4.22, around a centre at about 51.46, 3.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
