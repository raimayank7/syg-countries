Postal codes of Limburg, The Netherlands

282 codes, 1234 to 9999.

The GeoNames data lists 282 postal codes in Limburg, The Netherlands, covering 190 named places and 31 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1234 to 9999.

The places with the most codes are Maastricht (18), Venlo (14), Heerlen (11), Kerkrade (10), Roermond (7), Sittard (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Sittard-Geleen (25), Venlo Municipality (21), Horst aan de Maas Municipality (19), Maastricht Municipality (18). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.76 and 51.77 and longitude 5.65 and 6.19, around a centre at about 51.12, 5.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
