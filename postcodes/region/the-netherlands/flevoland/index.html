Postal codes of Flevoland, The Netherlands

97 codes, 1309 to 8323.

The GeoNames data lists 97 postal codes in Flevoland, The Netherlands, covering 19 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1309 to 8323.

The places with the most codes are Almere (43), Lelystad (19), Zeewolde (10), Emmeloord (5), Dronten (4), Urk (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Gemeente Almere (43), Gemeente Lelystad (19), Noordoostpolder Municipality (16), Gemeente Zeewolde (10). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.27 and 52.81 and longitude 5.13 and 5.90, around a centre at about 52.47, 5.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
