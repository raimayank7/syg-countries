Postal codes of Friesland, The Netherlands

475 codes, 8388 to 9873.

The GeoNames data lists 475 postal codes in Friesland, The Netherlands, covering 415 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8388 to 9873.

The places with the most codes are Leeuwarden (26), Heerenveen (10), Sneek (8), Drachten (7), Nes (3), Oudega (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Sûdwest Fryslân (99), Leeuwarden Municipality (62), Noardeast-Fryslân (55), De Fryske Marren Municipality (54). Postal codes in The Netherlands are written four digits, then a space, then two letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.81 and 53.48 and longitude 5.07 and 6.38, around a centre at about 53.11, 5.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
