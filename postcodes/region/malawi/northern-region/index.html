Postal codes of Northern Region, Malawi

78 codes, 101100 to 107101.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in Northern Region, Malawi, covering 78 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 101100 to 107101.

The places with the most codes are Chitipa BOMA (1), Kameme (1), Bulambya Songwe (1), Mwabulambya (1), Mwenemisuku (1), Lwangwa (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Mzuzu (17), Mzimba (15), Nkhata Bay (14), Rumphi (13). Postal codes in Malawi are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -12.60 and -9.57 and longitude 33.08 and 34.74, around a centre at about -11.22, 33.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Mzuzu (17); Mzimba (15); Nkhata Bay (14); Rumphi (13); Chitipa (10); Karonga (7); Likoma (2).
