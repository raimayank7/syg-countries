Postal codes of Southern Region, Malawi

223 codes, 301100 to 316111.

The GeoNames data lists 223 postal codes in Southern Region, Malawi, covering 218 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 301100 to 316111.

The places with the most codes are Lundu (2), Ngabu (2), Mpunga (2), Malemia (2), Chiwalo (2), Chikwawa BOMA (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Blantyre Urban (39), Zomba Urban (20), Machinga (19), Chikwawa (16). Postal codes in Malawi are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -17.12 and -13.71 and longitude 34.45 and 35.68, around a centre at about -15.50, 35.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
