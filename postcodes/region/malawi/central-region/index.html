Postal codes of Central Region, Malawi

190 codes, 201100 to 210111.

The GeoNames data lists 190 postal codes in Central Region, Malawi, covering 190 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 201100 to 210111.

The places with the most codes are Dedza Township (1), Tambala (1), Chauma (1), Kasumbu (1), Kachindamoto (1), Kamenya Gwaza (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Lilongwe Urban (58), Kasungu Rural (27), Lilongwe Rural (18), Mchinji (14). Postal codes in Malawi are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -14.85 and -12.30 and longitude 32.78 and 34.89, around a centre at about -13.71, 33.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
