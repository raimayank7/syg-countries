Postal codes of İstanbul, Turkey

277 codes, 34010 to 34990.

The GeoNames data lists 277 postal codes in İstanbul, Turkey, covering 261 named places and 39 sub-areas. In code order they run from 34010 to 34990.

The places with the most codes are Merkezköy (10), Mimarsinan (4), Bahçelievler (3), Cumhuriyet (3), Sanayi (3), Esentepe (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Fatih (17), Kadiköy (17), Üsküdar (16), Şişli (15). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.85 and 41.41 and longitude 28.08 and 29.86, around a centre at about 41.03, 28.97 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

39 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Fatih (17); Kadiköy (17); Üsküdar (16); Şişli (15); Sariyer (11); Ümraniye (10); Beşiktaş (10); Bahçelievler (9); Beyoğlu (9); Pendik (9); Maltepe (9); Ataşehir (9).

Other regions of Turkey
