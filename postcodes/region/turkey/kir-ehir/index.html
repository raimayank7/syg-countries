Postal codes of Kirşehir, Turkey

13 codes, 40000 to 40730.

The GeoNames data lists 13 postal codes in Kirşehir, Turkey, covering 260 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 40000 to 40730.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (2), Demirli (2), Ödemişli (1), Yetikli (1), Ömeruşaği (1), Polatli (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kirşehir (56), Kaman (52), Çiçekdaği (47), Mucur (45). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.85 and 39.77 and longitude 33.44 and 34.68, around a centre at about 39.34, 34.11 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kirşehir (56); Kaman (52); Çiçekdaği (47); Mucur (45); Akpinar (27); Akçakent (20); Boztepe (15).
