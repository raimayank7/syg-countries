Postal codes of Konya, Turkey

133 codes, 42005 to 42980.

The GeoNames data lists 133 postal codes in Konya, Turkey, covering 739 named places and 31 sub-areas. In code order they run from 42005 to 42980.

The places with the most codes are Yenice (4), Pinarbaşi (3), Orhaniye (3), Yeniköy (3), Taşağil (3), Yeşilyurt (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Ereğli (52), Ilgin (51), Bozkir (49), Beyşehir (46). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.84 and 39.91 and longitude 31.36 and 34.39, around a centre at about 37.94, 32.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

31 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ereğli (52); Ilgin (51); Bozkir (49); Beyşehir (46); Kadinhani (42); Seydişehir (41); Çumra (41); Yunak (37); Kulu (36); Meram (36); Cihanbeyli (35); Akşehir (35).
