Postal codes of Mardin, Turkey

25 codes, 47060 to 47880.

The GeoNames data lists 25 postal codes in Mardin, Turkey, covering 586 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 47060 to 47880.

The places with the most codes are Göllü (3), Gölbaşi (2), Aliçli (2), Taşlica (2), Pinarcik (2), Ovabaşi (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kiziltepe (162), Derik (73), Nusaybin (69), Artuklu (68). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.94 and 38.41 and longitude 39.92 and 41.88, around a centre at about 37.37, 40.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

10 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kiziltepe (162); Derik (73); Nusaybin (69); Artuklu (68); Midyat(Estel) (63); Mazidaği (52); Ömerli (44); Savur (39); Dargeçit (38); Yeşilli (10).
