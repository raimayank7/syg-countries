Postal codes of Manisa, Turkey

76 codes, 45000 to 45970.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Manisa, Turkey, covering 725 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 45000 to 45970.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (7), Hamidiye (4), Dereköy (4), Karaköy (4), Gölbaşi (3), Çanakçi (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Akhisar (96), Salihli (82), Demirci (80), Alaşehir (72). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.12 and 39.40 and longitude 27.18 and 29.02, around a centre at about 38.77, 28.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Akhisar (96); Salihli (82); Demirci (80); Alaşehir (72); Soma (59); Gördes (55); Kula (54); Selendi (47); Köprübaşi (45); Saruhanli (43); Turgutlu (40); Kirkağaç (33).
