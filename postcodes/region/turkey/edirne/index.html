Postal codes of Edirne, Turkey

27 codes, 22020 to 22970.

The GeoNames data lists 27 postal codes in Edirne, Turkey, covering 269 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 22020 to 22970.

The places with the most codes are Kadiköy (2), Danişment (2), Hasköy (2), Kavakli (2), Korucu (2), Küçükdoğanca (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Uzunköprü (57), Keşan (50), Edirne (41), Lalapaşa (28). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.62 and 41.96 and longitude 26.08 and 26.98, around a centre at about 41.26, 26.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Uzunköprü (57); Keşan (50); Edirne (41); Lalapaşa (28); Meriç (24); Havsa (23); İpsala (23); Enez (20); Süloğlu(Süleoğlu) (11).

Other regions of Turkey

Sivas; Erzurum; Samsun; Kastamonu; Balikesir; Şanliurfa; Konya; Manisa.
