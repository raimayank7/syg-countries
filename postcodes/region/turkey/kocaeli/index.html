Postal codes of Kocaeli, Turkey

34 codes, 41000 to 41950.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Kocaeli, Turkey, covering 194 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 41000 to 41950.

The places with the most codes are Merkezköy (5), Cumaköy (2), Durakli (2), Yağcilar (2), Elmacik (2), Denizli (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kandira (92), Gölcük (22), Karamürsel (20), Gebze (19). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.56 and 41.19 and longitude 29.39 and 30.31, around a centre at about 40.88, 29.93 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kandira (92); Gölcük (22); Karamürsel (20); Gebze (19); Körfez(Tütünçiftlik) (18); Kartepe (10); İzmit (8); Başiskele (7); Derince (2); Dilovasi (2); Çayirova (1); Darica (1).
