Postal codes of Balikesir, Turkey

65 codes, 10010 to 10975.

The GeoNames data lists 65 postal codes in Balikesir, Turkey, covering 764 named places and 20 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10010 to 10975.

The places with the most codes are Dereköy (5), Işiklar (5), Beyköy (4), Yeniköy (4), Akçapinar (3), Örenköy (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Dursunbey (102), Gönen (91), Bigadiç (71), Sindirgi (69). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.11 and 40.65 and longitude 26.65 and 28.97, around a centre at about 39.70, 27.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

20 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Dursunbey (102); Gönen (91); Bigadiç (71); Sindirgi (69); İvrindi (67); Kepsut (64); Susurluk (47); Savaştepe (46); Manyas (46); Balya (45); Bandirma (35); Burhaniye (27).
