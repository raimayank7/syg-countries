Postal codes of Sinop, Turkey

9 codes, 57000 to 57900.

The GeoNames data lists 9 postal codes in Sinop, Turkey, covering 409 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 57000 to 57900.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (4), Akçakese (3), Yeşilyurt (3), Boyali (2), Türkmen (2), Şeyhli (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Boyabat (107), Durağan (49), Erfelek (47), Ayancik (44). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.22 and 42.05 and longitude 34.25 and 35.39, around a centre at about 41.66, 34.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Boyabat (107); Durağan (49); Erfelek (47); Ayancik (44); Sinop (43); Gerze (42); Saraydüzü (33); Türkeli (33); Dikmen (31).
