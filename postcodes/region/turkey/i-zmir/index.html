Postal codes of İzmir, Turkey

128 codes, 35030 to 35990.

The GeoNames data lists 128 postal codes in İzmir, Turkey, covering 667 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 35030 to 35990.

The places with the most codes are Ovacik (7), Yeniköy (5), Bozköy (4), Yeşilköy (4), Eğridere (3), Dereköy (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Bergama (118), Ödemiş (109), Tire (66), Kiraz (53). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.87 and 39.34 and longitude 26.31 and 28.47, around a centre at about 38.48, 27.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

30 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Bergama (118); Ödemiş (109); Tire (66); Kiraz (53); Bayindir (40); Kemalpaşa (35); Kinik (32); Menderes(Cumaovasi) (30); Torbali (29); Menemen (28); Dikili (27); Beydağ (22).
