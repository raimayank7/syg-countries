Postal codes of Bartin, Turkey

12 codes, 74100 to 74610.

The GeoNames data lists 12 postal codes in Bartin, Turkey, covering 267 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 74100 to 74610.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (3), Hatipler (2), Hisarköy (2), Uluköy (2), Abdipaşa (2), Aşağidere (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Bartin (142), Ulus (73), Amasra (31), Kurucaşile (29). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.97 and 41.89 and longitude 31.54 and 33.72, around a centre at about 41.56, 32.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Bartin (142); Ulus (73); Amasra (31); Kurucaşile (29).
