Postal codes of Sakarya, Turkey

27 codes, 54050 to 54950.

The GeoNames data lists 27 postal codes in Sakarya, Turkey, covering 429 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 54050 to 54950.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (3), Ortaköy (3), Ahmediye (2), İhsaniye (2), Maksudiye (2), Taşoluk (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Hendek (72), Geyve (65), Akyazi (47), Kaynarca (42). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.30 and 41.14 and longitude 30.01 and 31.00, around a centre at about 40.76, 30.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Hendek (72); Geyve (65); Akyazi (47); Kaynarca (42); Karasu (34); Pamukova (31); Kocaali (31); Adapazari (28); Tarakli (23); Sapanca (19); Ferizli (18); Söğütlü (16).

Other regions of Turkey

Sivas; Erzurum; Samsun; Kastamonu; Balikesir; Şanliurfa; Konya; Manisa.
