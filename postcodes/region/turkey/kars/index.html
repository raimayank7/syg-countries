Postal codes of Kars, Turkey

9 codes, 36000 to 36900.

The GeoNames data lists 9 postal codes in Kars, Turkey, covering 352 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 36000 to 36900.

The places with the most codes are Karakale (4), Yaylacik (3), Başköy (3), Söğütlü (3), Akçakale (3), Karabağ (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kars (74), Kağizman (63), Selim (53), Arpaçay (48). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.89 and 41.30 and longitude 41.93 and 43.74, around a centre at about 40.48, 43.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kars (74); Kağizman (63); Selim (53); Arpaçay (48); Sarikamiş (42); Digor (39); Akyaka (28); Susuz (28).
