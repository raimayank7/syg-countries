Postal codes of Kilis, Turkey

4 codes, 79000 to 79700.

The GeoNames data lists 4 postal codes in Kilis, Turkey, covering 141 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 79000 to 79700.

The places with the most codes are Fericek (1), Belentepe (1), Yuvabaşi (1), Kurtaran (1), Koçcağiz (1), Yastica (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kilis (53), Musabeyli (47), Elbeyli (24), Polateli (17). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.65 and 37.15 and longitude 36.74 and 37.73, around a centre at about 36.82, 37.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kilis (53); Musabeyli (47); Elbeyli (24); Polateli (17).
