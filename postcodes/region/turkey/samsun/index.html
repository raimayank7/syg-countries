Postal codes of Samsun, Turkey

39 codes, 55000 to 55900.

The GeoNames data lists 39 postal codes in Samsun, Turkey, covering 884 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 55000 to 55900.

The places with the most codes are Yeşilköy (6), Yeniköy (5), Örencik (4), Başalan (4), Esentepe (4), Ortaköy (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Vezirköprü (139), Çarşamba (125), Bafra (120), Kavak (87). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.87 and 41.70 and longitude 34.95 and 37.12, around a centre at about 41.20, 36.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Vezirköprü (139); Çarşamba (125); Bafra (120); Kavak (87); Havza (82); Terme (68); Ladik (57); Alaçam (54); Canik (40); Tekkeköy (39); Atakum (36); Ayvacik (35).
