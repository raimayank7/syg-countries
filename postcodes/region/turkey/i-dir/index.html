Postal codes of Iğdir, Turkey

7 codes, 76000 to 76900.

The GeoNames data lists 7 postal codes in Iğdir, Turkey, covering 173 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 76000 to 76900.

The places with the most codes are Hasankent (1), Taşuçan (1), Aşağicivanli (1), Söğütlü (1), Kiznefer (1), Aliköse (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Tuzluca (85), Iğdir (44), Aralik (23), Karakoyunlu (21). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.63 and 40.78 and longitude 42.30 and 44.60, around a centre at about 39.95, 43.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tuzluca (85); Iğdir (44); Aralik (23); Karakoyunlu (21).
