Postal codes of Trabzon, Turkey

50 codes, 61000 to 61950.

The GeoNames data lists 50 postal codes in Trabzon, Turkey, covering 514 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 61000 to 61950.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (7), Ortaköy (5), Yeşilköy (4), Yeşilyurt (4), Birlik (3), Oğuz (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Akçaabat (65), Maçka (60), Of (60), Ortahisar (50). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.55 and 41.09 and longitude 39.15 and 40.45, around a centre at about 40.89, 39.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

18 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Akçaabat (65); Maçka (60); Of (60); Ortahisar (50); Arakli (46); Vakfikebir (36); Çaykara (32); Sürmene (29); Beşikdüzü (29); Arsin (25); Şalpazari (25); Yomra (21).
