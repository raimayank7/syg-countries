Postal codes of Hatay, Turkey

73 codes, 31001 to 31960.

The GeoNames data lists 73 postal codes in Hatay, Turkey, covering 307 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 31001 to 31960.

The places with the most codes are Merkezköy (5), Yeşilköy (2), Kaletepe (2), Gökgöl (1), Yoncadüzü (1), Yukariburnaz (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kirikhan (58), Altinözü (47), Samandağ (34), Hassa (32). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.90 and 37.00 and longitude 35.89 and 36.68, around a centre at about 36.41, 36.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kirikhan (58); Altinözü (47); Samandağ (34); Hassa (32); Reyhanli (32); İskenderun (26); Kumlu (14); Antakya (14); Belen (12); Erzin (11); Dörtyol (11); Defne (8).
