Postal codes of Düzce, Turkey

14 codes, 81010 to 81950.

The GeoNames data lists 14 postal codes in Düzce, Turkey, covering 305 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 81010 to 81950.

The places with the most codes are Tepeköy (2), Döngelli (2), Taşlik (2), Dereköy (2), Subaşi (2), Kirazli (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Düzce (125), Akçakoca (46), Yiğilca (39), Gölyaka (23). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.19 and 41.79 and longitude 29.90 and 33.09, around a centre at about 40.88, 31.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Düzce (125); Akçakoca (46); Yiğilca (39); Gölyaka (23); Cumayeri (22); Kaynaşli (22); Çilimli (21); Gümüşova (19).
