Postal codes of Erzincan, Turkey

27 codes, 24002 to 24880.

The GeoNames data lists 27 postal codes in Erzincan, Turkey, covering 438 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 24002 to 24880.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (3), Doluca (2), Kalecik (2), Elmali (2), Aktaş (2), Dallica (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tercan (79), Kemah (74), Erzincan (64), Kemaliye (63). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.04 and 40.02 and longitude 38.33 and 40.65, around a centre at about 39.64, 39.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tercan (79); Kemah (74); Erzincan (64); Kemaliye (63); Iliç(İliç) (59); Çayirli (49); Üzümlü (27); Refahiye (26); Otlukbeli (11).
