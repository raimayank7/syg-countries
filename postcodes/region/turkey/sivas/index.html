Postal codes of Sivas, Turkey

31 codes, 58000 to 58980.

The GeoNames data lists 31 postal codes in Sivas, Turkey, covering 1,152 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 58000 to 58980.

The places with the most codes are Karacaören (6), Ortaköy (5), Dereköy (4), Kümbet (4), Başören (4), Akpinar (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Sivas (163), Zara (131), Yildizeli (123), Kangal (111). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.60 and 40.49 and longitude 35.93 and 38.77, around a centre at about 39.65, 37.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Sivas (163); Zara (131); Yildizeli (123); Kangal (111); Divriği (107); İmranli (105); Şarkişla (97); Hafik (78); Suşehri (71); Gürün (63); Koyulhisar (45); Ulaş (40).
