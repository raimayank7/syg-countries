Postal codes of Muş, Turkey

21 codes, 49090 to 49800.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Muş, Turkey, covering 327 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 49090 to 49800.

The places with the most codes are Söğütlü (2), Çobandaği (2), Çayiryolu (2), Sağlicak (2), Kalecik (2), Seki (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Muş (107), Varto (102), Bulanik (63), Malazgirt (36). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.53 and 39.44 and longitude 41.16 and 42.66, around a centre at about 39.01, 41.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Muş (107); Varto (102); Bulanik (63); Malazgirt (36); Korkut (30); Hasköy (19).
