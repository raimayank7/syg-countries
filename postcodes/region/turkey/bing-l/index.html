Postal codes of Bingöl, Turkey

10 codes, 12000 to 12900.

The GeoNames data lists 10 postal codes in Bingöl, Turkey, covering 302 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 12000 to 12900.

The places with the most codes are Çayağzi (2), Elmali (2), Direkli (2), Çanakçi (2), Ortaköy (2), Yeniköy (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Bingöl (79), Genç (55), Karliova (48), Adakli (35). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.49 and 39.48 and longitude 39.95 and 41.19, around a centre at about 39.04, 40.57 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Bingöl (79); Genç (55); Karliova (48); Adakli (35); Kiği (29); Solhan (28); Yayladere (21); Yedisu (14).
