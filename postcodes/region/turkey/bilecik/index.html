Postal codes of Bilecik, Turkey

11 codes, 11000 to 11800.

The GeoNames data lists 11 postal codes in Bilecik, Turkey, covering 222 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 11000 to 11800.

The places with the most codes are Dereköy (2), Borcak (2), Çiftlik (2), Akçapinar (2), Hisarcik (2), Karaağaç (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Gölpazari (49), Bozüyük (48), Osmaneli (28), Pazaryeri (25). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.67 and 40.49 and longitude 29.75 and 30.63, around a centre at about 40.12, 30.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Gölpazari (49); Bozüyük (48); Osmaneli (28); Pazaryeri (25); Yenipazar (24); Söğüt (24); Bilecik (23); İnhisar (10).
