Postal codes of Artvin, Turkey

17 codes, 08000 to 08890.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Artvin, Turkey, covering 305 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 08000 to 08890.

The places with the most codes are Çimenli (3), Başköy (3), Dereiçi (3), Avcilar (3), Pinarli (2), Güneşli (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Şavşat (66), Yusufeli (63), Ardanuç (50), Borçka (37). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.60 and 41.52 and longitude 41.27 and 42.49, around a centre at about 41.20, 41.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Şavşat (66); Yusufeli (63); Ardanuç (50); Borçka (37); Artvin (37); Hopa (31); Arhavi (31); Murgul(Göktaş) (12).
