Postal codes of Ankara, Turkey

116 codes, 06010 to 06980.

The GeoNames data lists 116 postal codes in Ankara, Turkey, covering 717 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 06010 to 06980.

The places with the most codes are Karacaören (4), Kinik (3), Aktepe (3), Sarayköy (3), Yeşilöz (3), Avşar (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kizilcahamam (103), Nallihan (77), Haymana (73), Beypazari (68). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.12 and 40.67 and longitude 30.88 and 34.13, around a centre at about 39.94, 32.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

25 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kizilcahamam (103); Nallihan (77); Haymana (73); Beypazari (68); Polatli (54); Çubuk (50); Şereflikoçhisar (48); Çamlidere (42); Bala (39); Kalecik (38); Güdül (27); Çankaya (23).
