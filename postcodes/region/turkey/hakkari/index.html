Postal codes of Hakkari, Turkey

7 codes, 30000 to 30800.

The GeoNames data lists 7 postal codes in Hakkari, Turkey, covering 134 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30000 to 30800.

The places with the most codes are Aksu (2), Akçali (2), Kamişli (2), Kavakli (2), Kolbaşi (1), Köşkönü (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Yüksekova (60), Hakkari (39), Şemdinli (24), Çukurca (15). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.02 and 37.79 and longitude 33.87 and 44.76, around a centre at about 37.48, 43.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Yüksekova (60); Hakkari (39); Şemdinli (24); Çukurca (15).
