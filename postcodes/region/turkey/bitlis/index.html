Postal codes of Bitlis, Turkey

8 codes, 13000 to 13810.

The GeoNames data lists 8 postal codes in Bitlis, Turkey, covering 305 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 13000 to 13810.

The places with the most codes are Budakli (3), Gölbaşi (2), Balli (2), Ekinli (2), Küllüce (2), Çalidüzü (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tatvan (62), Mutki (61), Hizan (57), Bitlis (47). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.00 and 39.02 and longitude 41.56 and 43.10, around a centre at about 38.47, 42.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tatvan (62); Mutki (61); Hizan (57); Bitlis (47); Adilcevaz (30); Güroymak (29); Ahlat (28).
