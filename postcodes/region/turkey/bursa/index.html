Postal codes of Bursa, Turkey

74 codes, 16010 to 16990.

The GeoNames data lists 74 postal codes in Bursa, Turkey, covering 673 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 16010 to 16990.

The places with the most codes are Osmaniye (6), Orhaniye (6), Yeniköy (5), Hamidiye (4), Kurşunlu (4), Çeltikçi (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Mustafakemalpaşa (109), İnegöl (96), Karacabey (65), Yenişehir (63). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.61 and 40.60 and longitude 28.13 and 36.88, around a centre at about 40.12, 29.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Mustafakemalpaşa (109); İnegöl (96); Karacabey (65); Yenişehir (63); Orhaneli (56); Osmangazi (51); Mudanya (50); Nilüfer (42); İznik (40); Büyükorhan (40); Keles (37); Orhangazi (31).
