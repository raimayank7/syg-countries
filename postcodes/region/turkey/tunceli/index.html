Postal codes of Tunceli, Turkey

9 codes, 62000 to 62950.

The GeoNames data lists 9 postal codes in Tunceli, Turkey, covering 375 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 62000 to 62950.

The places with the most codes are Kozluca (2), Konaklar (2), Güleç (2), Çatköy (2), Akpinar (1), Taşitli (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Mazgirt (72), Ovacik (67), Pülümür (53), Tunceli (53). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.78 and 39.60 and longitude 38.78 and 40.24, around a centre at about 39.15, 39.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Mazgirt (72); Ovacik (67); Pülümür (53); Tunceli (53); Pertek (46); Çemişgezek (33); Hozat (30); Nazimiye (25).
