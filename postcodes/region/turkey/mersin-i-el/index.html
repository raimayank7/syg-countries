Postal codes of Mersin(İçel), Turkey

70 codes, 33010 to 33990.

The GeoNames data lists 70 postal codes in Mersin(İçel), Turkey, covering 565 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 33010 to 33990.

The places with the most codes are Sarikavak (3), Tepeköy (2), Köselerli (2), Üçtepe (2), Yenice (2), Karadiken (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tarsus (133), Mut (91), Silifke (77), Erdemli (61). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.06 and 37.99 and longitude 30.55 and 35.14, around a centre at about 36.70, 33.96 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tarsus (133); Mut (91); Silifke (77); Erdemli (61); Gülnar (Gülpinar) (52); Anamur (42); Toroslar (40); Akdeniz (26); Mezitli (21); Aydincik (20); Yenişehir (15); Çamliyayla (12).
