Postal codes of Çankiri, Turkey

15 codes, 18000 to 18900.

The GeoNames data lists 15 postal codes in Çankiri, Turkey, covering 380 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 18000 to 18900.

The places with the most codes are Saraycik (3), Yenice (3), Dikenli (2), Bayindir (2), Dodurga (2), Hacilar (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Ilgaz (75), Çerkeş (53), Çankiri (52), Yaprakli (42). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.54 and 41.83 and longitude 31.60 and 35.10, around a centre at about 40.74, 33.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ilgaz (75); Çerkeş (53); Çankiri (52); Yaprakli (42); Kurşunlu (29); Orta (28); Bayramören (28); Kizilirmak (27); Şabanözü (22); Eldivan (17); Korgun (13); Atkaracalar (12).
