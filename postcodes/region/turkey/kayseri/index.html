Postal codes of Kayseri, Turkey

41 codes, 38010 to 38940.

The GeoNames data lists 41 postal codes in Kayseri, Turkey, covering 462 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 38010 to 38940.

The places with the most codes are Kaleköy (4), Saraycik (3), Kizilören (3), İncesu (2), Kizik (2), Çukuryurt (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Pinarbaşi (113), Kocasinan (58), Develi (50), Tomarza (49). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.77 and 39.23 and longitude 34.92 and 36.90, around a centre at about 38.63, 35.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Pinarbaşi (113); Kocasinan (58); Develi (50); Tomarza (49); Sariz (37); Bünyan (33); Yahyali (30); Sarioğlan (24); Yeşilhisar (22); Talas (15); Felahiye (13); Melikgazi (11).
