Postal codes of Aydin, Turkey

55 codes, 09000 to 09990.

The GeoNames data lists 55 postal codes in Aydin, Turkey, covering 449 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 09000 to 09990.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (6), Dereköy (5), Yeşilköy (4), Işiklar (3), Esentepe (3), Ovacik (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Çine (74), Nazilli (58), Koçarli (47), Bozdoğan (46). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.20 and 38.08 and longitude 27.06 and 28.82, around a centre at about 37.76, 28.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Çine (74); Nazilli (58); Koçarli (47); Bozdoğan (46); Söke (42); Karacasu (33); Germencik (32); Kuyucak (29); Köşk (25); İncirliova (24); Karpuzlu (20); Yenipazar (15).
