Postal codes of Bayburt, Turkey

5 codes, 69000 to 69500.

The GeoNames data lists 5 postal codes in Bayburt, Turkey, covering 156 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 69000 to 69500.

The places with the most codes are Dikmetaş (2), Demirözü (1), Çimentepe (1), Otlukbeli (1), Petekli (1), Yazibaşi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bayburt (103), Demirözü (30), Aydintepe (24). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.98 and 40.55 and longitude 39.67 and 40.58, around a centre at about 40.28, 40.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
