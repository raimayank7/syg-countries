Postal codes of Isparta, Turkey

25 codes, 32000 to 32950.

The GeoNames data lists 25 postal codes in Isparta, Turkey, covering 221 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 32000 to 32950.

The places with the most codes are Yeşilyurt (2), Yeniköy (2), Çobanisa (2), Terziler (2), Kuyucak (2), Eyüpler (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Yalvaç (39), Sütçüler (31), Eğirdir (30), Şarkikaraağaç (29). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.17 and 38.45 and longitude 30.15 and 33.40, around a centre at about 37.91, 30.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Yalvaç (39); Sütçüler (31); Eğirdir (30); Şarkikaraağaç (29); Isparta (26); Keçiborlu (16); Aksu (14); Gelendost (14); Senirkent (9); Gönen (8); Atabey (6); Uluborlu (5).
