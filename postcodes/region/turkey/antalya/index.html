Postal codes of Antalya, Turkey

89 codes, 07010 to 07995.

The GeoNames data lists 89 postal codes in Antalya, Turkey, covering 606 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 07010 to 07995.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (5), Beldibi (5), Çiplakli (4), Çamlica (4), Dereköy (4), Üzümlü (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Alanya (107), Manavgat (87), Serik (81), Kaş (63). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.11 and 37.41 and longitude 29.32 and 32.58, around a centre at about 36.75, 31.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

19 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Alanya (107); Manavgat (87); Serik (81); Kaş (63); Korkuteli (54); Elmali (52); Akseki (48); Gazipaşa (42); Kumluca (29); Gündoğmuş (28); Döşemealti (20); Finike (19).

Other regions of Turkey

Sivas; Erzurum; Samsun; Kastamonu; Balikesir; Şanliurfa; Konya; Manisa.
