Postal codes of Denizli, Turkey

74 codes, 20010 to 20980.

The GeoNames data lists 74 postal codes in Denizli, Turkey, covering 407 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20010 to 20980.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (4), Tekkeköy (3), Sazak (3), Gölcük (2), Ovacik (2), Hisarköy (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Çivril (69), Acipayam (52), Tavas (50), Çal (32). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.93 and 39.21 and longitude 28.66 and 30.02, around a centre at about 37.78, 29.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

19 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Çivril (69); Acipayam (52); Tavas (50); Çal (32); Buldan (30); Çameli (27); Sarayköy (26); Kale (26); Honaz (18); Güney (18); Bozkurt (15); Bekilli (13).
