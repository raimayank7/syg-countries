Postal codes of Kktc, Turkey

57 codes, 99010 to 99970.

The GeoNames data lists 57 postal codes in Kktc, Turkey, covering 195 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 99010 to 99970.

The places with the most codes are Boğazköy (2), Lefke (2), Lefkoşa (1), Haspolat (1), Hamitköy (1), Değirmenlik (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Girne (47), Gazimağusa (46), İskele (45), Lefkoşa (30). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.28 and 41.96 and longitude 31.78 and 41.40, around a centre at about 39.68, 36.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Girne (47); Gazimağusa (46); İskele (45); Lefkoşa (30); Güzelyurt (29).
