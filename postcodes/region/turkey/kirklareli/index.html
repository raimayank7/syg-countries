Postal codes of Kirklareli, Turkey

16 codes, 39000 to 39790.

The GeoNames data lists 16 postal codes in Kirklareli, Turkey, covering 187 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 39000 to 39790.

The places with the most codes are Müsellim (3), Doğanca (2), Çavuşköy (2), Kadiköy (2), Pehlivanköy (1), Kumköy (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kirklareli (37), Lüleburgaz (36), Babaeski (34), Vize (26). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.24 and 42.07 and longitude 26.91 and 28.09, around a centre at about 41.63, 27.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kirklareli (37); Lüleburgaz (36); Babaeski (34); Vize (26); Kofçaz (17); Demirköy (17); Pinarhisar (16); Pehlivanköy (9).
