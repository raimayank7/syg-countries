Postal codes of Aksaray, Turkey

22 codes, 68000 to 68940.

The GeoNames data lists 22 postal codes in Aksaray, Turkey, covering 195 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 68000 to 68940.

The places with the most codes are Sariağil (2), Sofular (2), Pinarbaşi (2), Güneşli (1), Başaran (1), Bayramdüğün (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Aksaray (94), Ortaköy (32), Ağaçören (29), Gülağaç (14). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.02 and 39.01 and longitude 33.28 and 34.43, around a centre at about 38.53, 34.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Aksaray (94); Ortaköy (32); Ağaçören (29); Gülağaç (14); Güzelyurt (12); Eskil (10); Sariyahşi (7).
