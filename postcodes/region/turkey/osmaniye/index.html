Postal codes of Osmaniye, Turkey

18 codes, 80000 to 80990.

The GeoNames data lists 18 postal codes in Osmaniye, Turkey, covering 174 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80000 to 80990.

The places with the most codes are Bahçe (2), Yeniköy (2), Arslanpinari (1), Toprakkale (1), Sazlik (1), Sayhüyüğü (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kadirli (62), Osmaniye (39), Düziçi (29), Bahçe (16). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.35 and 38.16 and longitude 34.84 and 37.35, around a centre at about 37.29, 36.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kadirli (62); Osmaniye (39); Düziçi (29); Bahçe (16); Sumbas (16); Toprakkale (7); Hasanbeyli (7).
