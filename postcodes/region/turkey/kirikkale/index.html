Postal codes of Kirikkale, Turkey

16 codes, 71000 to 71900.

The GeoNames data lists 16 postal codes in Kirikkale, Turkey, covering 200 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 71000 to 71900.

The places with the most codes are Yeniyapan (2), Yahşihan (1), Hacibali (1), Bedesten (1), Hisarköy (1), Kiliçlar (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Keskin (56), Delice (42), Balişeyh (29), Sulakyurt (27). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.45 and 40.33 and longitude 33.35 and 34.20, around a centre at about 39.86, 33.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Keskin (56); Delice (42); Balişeyh (29); Sulakyurt (27); Kirikkale (15); Çelebi (14); Yahşihan (9); Bahşili (6); Karakeçili (3).
