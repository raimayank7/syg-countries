Postal codes of Adana, Turkey

64 codes, 01010 to 01960.

The GeoNames data lists 64 postal codes in Adana, Turkey, covering 518 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01010 to 01960.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (4), Boztahta (3), Çiçekli (2), Dikilitaş (2), Hamidiye (2), Akdam (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Ceyhan (86), Kozan (83), Karaisali (63), Sariçam (44). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.56 and 38.35 and longitude 34.81 and 36.58, around a centre at about 37.33, 35.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ceyhan (86); Kozan (83); Karaisali (63); Sariçam (44); Feke (40); Karataş (38); Tufanbeyli (30); Aladağ(Karsanti) (28); Saimbeyli (27); Seyhan (22); Yüreğir (22); İmamoğlu (20).

Other regions of Turkey
