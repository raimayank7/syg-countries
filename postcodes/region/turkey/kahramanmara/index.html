Postal codes of Kahramanmaraş, Turkey

71 codes, 46000 to 46840.

The GeoNames data lists 71 postal codes in Kahramanmaraş, Turkey, covering 397 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 46000 to 46840.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (3), Ağcaşar (2), Özbek (2), Çiçekköy (2), Karahüyük (2), Elmali (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Elbistan (78), Pazarcik (68), Göksun (60), Afşin (57). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.23 and 38.56 and longitude 36.21 and 37.65, around a centre at about 37.88, 36.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Elbistan (78); Pazarcik (68); Göksun (60); Afşin (57); Andirin (52); Türkoğlu (40); Ekinözü (18); Onikişubat (15); Çağliyancerit (12); Nurhak (7); Dulkadiroğlu (6).

Other regions of Turkey
