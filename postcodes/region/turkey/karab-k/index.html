Postal codes of Karabük, Turkey

11 codes, 78000 to 78700.

The GeoNames data lists 11 postal codes in Karabük, Turkey, covering 271 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 78000 to 78700.

The places with the most codes are Akören (3), Belen (3), Yaziköy (2), Kadiköy (2), Karahasanlar (2), Yeşilköy (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Eflani (55), Safranbolu (55), Eskipazar (50), Karabük (44). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.10 and 41.78 and longitude 29.65 and 34.10, around a centre at about 41.18, 32.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Eflani (55); Safranbolu (55); Eskipazar (50); Karabük (44); Ovacik (43); Yenice (35).
