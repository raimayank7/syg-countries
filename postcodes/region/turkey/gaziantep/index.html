Postal codes of Gaziantep, Turkey

46 codes, 27000 to 27970.

The GeoNames data lists 46 postal codes in Gaziantep, Turkey, covering 422 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 27000 to 27970.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (3), Karacaören (3), İkizkuyu (2), Büyükşahinbey (2), Kazikli (2), Şahinbey (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Nizip (83), Şahinbey (63), Şehitkamil (62), İslahiye (54). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.24 and 38.29 and longitude 30.69 and 38.99, around a centre at about 37.11, 37.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Nizip (83); Şahinbey (63); Şehitkamil (62); İslahiye (54); Araban (41); Yavuzeli (40); Nurdaği (36); Karkamiş (36); Oğuzeli (24).

Other regions of Turkey
