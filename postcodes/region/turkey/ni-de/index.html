Postal codes of Niğde, Turkey

24 codes, 51000 to 51900.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Niğde, Turkey, covering 160 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 51000 to 51900.

The places with the most codes are Tepeköy (3), Karacaören (2), Ulukişla (2), Özyurt (2), Ovacik (2), Elmali (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Niğde (55), Ulukişla (40), Bor (27), Çamardi (22). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.47 and 38.33 and longitude 34.02 and 35.15, around a centre at about 37.90, 34.68 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Niğde (55); Ulukişla (40); Bor (27); Çamardi (22); Çiftlik(Özyurt) (14); Altunhisar (9).
