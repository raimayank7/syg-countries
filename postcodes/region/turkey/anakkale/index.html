Postal codes of Çanakkale, Turkey

25 codes, 17000 to 17980.

The GeoNames data lists 25 postal codes in Çanakkale, Turkey, covering 487 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 17000 to 17980.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (4), Yeşilköy (3), Çavuşköy (3), Yenice (3), Kalafat (2), Şirinköy (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Biga (80), Yenice (78), Bayramiç (76), Ayvacik (67). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.48 and 40.70 and longitude 25.74 and 27.51, around a centre at about 39.99, 26.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Biga (80); Yenice (78); Bayramiç (76); Ayvacik (67); Ezine (50); Çan (44); Çanakkale (43); Lapseki (43); Gelibolu (28); Eceabat (13); Gökçeada(İmroz) (10); Bozcaada (1).
