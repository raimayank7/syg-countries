Postal codes of Karaman, Turkey

13 codes, 70000 to 70810.

The GeoNames data lists 13 postal codes in Karaman, Turkey, covering 172 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 70000 to 70810.

The places with the most codes are Kayaönü (2), Elmayurdu (2), Çukurbağ (2), Akpinar (2), Büyükkarapinar (1), Başyayla (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Karaman (98), Ermenek (30), Ayranci (23), Sariveliler (13). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.47 and 39.93 and longitude 32.56 and 36.88, around a centre at about 37.05, 33.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Karaman (98); Ermenek (30); Ayranci (23); Sariveliler (13); Kazimkarabekir (7); Başyayla (5).
