Postal codes of Yozgat, Turkey

19 codes, 66000 to 66900.

The GeoNames data lists 19 postal codes in Yozgat, Turkey, covering 580 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 66000 to 66900.

The places with the most codes are Hamzali (3), Başpinar (3), Gülpinar (3), Gökdere (2), Sarikaya (2), Çandir (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Yozgat (100), Akdağmadeni (93), Sorgun (89), Yerköy (60). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.06 and 40.23 and longitude 34.15 and 36.15, around a centre at about 39.73, 35.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

14 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Yozgat (100); Akdağmadeni (93); Sorgun (89); Yerköy (60); Çekerek (45); Şefaatli (45); Sarikaya (40); Boğazliyan (34); Kadişehri (29); Aydincik (25); Çayiralan (23); Saraykent (18).
