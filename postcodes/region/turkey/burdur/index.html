Postal codes of Burdur, Turkey

17 codes, 15000 to 15930.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Burdur, Turkey, covering 205 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 15000 to 15930.

The places with the most codes are Dereköy (3), Karacaören (2), Yuva (2), Karapinar (2), Kavacik (2), Yazir (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Burdur (55), Yeşilova (40), Bucak (39), Tefenni (15). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.88 and 37.79 and longitude 29.37 and 30.89, around a centre at about 37.44, 30.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Burdur (55); Yeşilova (40); Bucak (39); Tefenni (15); Gölhisar (14); Çavdir (13); Ağlasun (10); Karamanli (9); Kemer (8); Çeltikçi (7); Altinyayla(Dirmil) (6).
