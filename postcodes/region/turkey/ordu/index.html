Postal codes of Ordu, Turkey

31 codes, 52000 to 52960.

The GeoNames data lists 31 postal codes in Ordu, Turkey, covering 511 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 52000 to 52960.

The places with the most codes are Yeniköy (4), Yeşilköy (4), Kaleköy (3), Güzelyurt (3), Tepeköy (3), Şenyurt (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Altinordu (72), Ünye (68), Fatsa (67), Mesudiye (61). Postal codes in Turkey are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.33 and 41.13 and longitude 36.69 and 38.09, around a centre at about 40.86, 37.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

19 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Altinordu (72); Ünye (68); Fatsa (67); Mesudiye (61); Perşembe (46); Akkuş (41); Ulubey (36); Kumru (33); Gölköy (26); Korgan (21); Kabadüz(Karadüz) (13); Çatalpinar (13).
