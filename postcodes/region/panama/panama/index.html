Postal codes of Panama, Panama

214 codes, 07001 to 07229.

The GeoNames data lists 214 postal codes in Panama, Panama, covering 212 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 07001 to 07229.

The places with the most codes are Chilibre (2), El Bosque (2), San Miguel /Cabec) (1), La Ensenada (1), La Esmeralda (1), La Guinea (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Panama (109), La Chorrera (18), Tocumen (15), Capira (13). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 8.09 and 9.34 and longitude -81.23 and -78.40, around a centre at about 8.96, -79.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Panama (109); La Chorrera (18); Tocumen (15); Capira (13); Chame (11); San Miguelito (10); San Carlos (9); Arraijan (8); Chepo (7); Balboa (6); Chiman (5); Taboga (3).
