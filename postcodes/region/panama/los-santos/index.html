Postal codes of Los Santos, Panama

78 codes, 06001 to 06078.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in Los Santos, Panama, covering 78 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 06001 to 06078.

The places with the most codes are Guarare (1), El Espinal (1), El Macano (1), Guarare Arriba (1), El Enea (1), La Pasera (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Las Tablas (24), Los Santos (14), Macaracas (11), Guarare (10). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.29 and 7.94 and longitude -80.63 and -80.06, around a centre at about 7.70, -80.36 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Las Tablas (24); Los Santos (14); Macaracas (11); Guarare (10); Tonosi (9); Pedasi (5); Pocri (5).
