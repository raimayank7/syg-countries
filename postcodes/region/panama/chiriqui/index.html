Postal codes of Chiriqui, Panama

95 codes, 04001 to 04095.

The GeoNames data lists 95 postal codes in Chiriqui, Panama, covering 93 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 04001 to 04095.

The places with the most codes are Pedregal (2), Santa Cruz (2), Alanje (Cabec) (1), Divala (1), Guarumal (1), Palo Grande (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bugaba (13), David (10), Tole (9), Alanje (8). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 8.10 and 8.85 and longitude -82.87 and -81.58, around a centre at about 8.48, -82.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
