Postal codes of Comarca Ngobe Bugle, Panama

58 codes, 10001 to 10058.

The GeoNames data lists 58 postal codes in Comarca Ngobe Bugle, Panama, covering 58 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10001 to 10058.

The places with the most codes are Soloy (1), Boca De Balsa (1), Cerro Banco (1), Cerro De Patena (1), Camaron Arriba (1), Emplanada De Corcha (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Muna (12), Nurun (9), Kankintu (9), Besiko (8). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 8.16 and 9.18 and longitude -82.07 and -81.36, around a centre at about 8.51, -81.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
