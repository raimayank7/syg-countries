Postal codes of Cocle, Panama

43 codes, 02001 to 02043.

The GeoNames data lists 43 postal codes in Cocle, Panama, covering 42 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 02001 to 02043.

The places with the most codes are El Valle (2), Aguadulce (Cabec) (1), El Cristo (1), El Roble (1), Pocri (1), Barrios Unidos (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Anton (11), Penonome (10), La Pintada (6), Nata (6). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 8.17 and 8.97 and longitude -80.75 and -80.15, around a centre at about 8.50, -80.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Anton (11); Penonome (10); La Pintada (6); Nata (6); Aguadulce (5); Ola (5).
