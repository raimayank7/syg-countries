Postal codes of Veraguas, Panama

95 codes, 08001 to 08095.

The GeoNames data lists 95 postal codes in Veraguas, Panama, covering 92 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 08001 to 08095.

The places with the most codes are San Jose (3), San Martin De Porres (2), Atalaya (Cabec) (1), El Barrito (1), La Montaðuela (1), San Antonio (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Calobre (12), Las Palmas (12), Santiago (12), Sona (10). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.34 and 8.79 and longitude -81.60 and -80.73, around a centre at about 8.13, -81.15 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Calobre (12); Las Palmas (12); Santiago (12); Sona (10); Montijo (8); Cañazas (7); Santa Fe (7); La Mesa (6); San Francisco (6); Atalaya (5); Rio De Jesus (5); Mariato (5).
