Postal codes of Colon, Panama

46 codes, 03001 to 03050.

The GeoNames data lists 46 postal codes in Colon, Panama, covering 46 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 03001 to 03050.

The places with the most codes are Barrio Norte (1), Barrio Sur (1), Buena Vista (1), Cativa (1), Ciricito (1), Cristobal (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Colon (20), Santa Isabel (8), Chagres (7), Donoso (6). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.08 and 9.63 and longitude -80.57 and -79.20, around a centre at about 9.35, -79.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Colon (20); Santa Isabel (8); Chagres (7); Donoso (6); Portobelo (5).
