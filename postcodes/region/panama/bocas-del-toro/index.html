Postal codes of Bocas Del Toro, Panama

17 codes, 01001 to 01017.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Bocas Del Toro, Panama, covering 17 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01001 to 01017.

The places with the most codes are Bocas Del Toro (Cabecera) (1), Bastimentos (1), Punta Laurel (1), Cauchero (1), Tierra Oscura (1), Changuinola (Cabec) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Changuinola (7), Bocas Del Toro (5), Chiriqui Grande (5). Postal codes in Panama are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 8.93 and 9.54 and longitude -82.74 and -82.13, around a centre at about 9.24, -82.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
