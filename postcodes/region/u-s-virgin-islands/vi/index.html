Postal codes of Vi, U.S. Virgin Islands

16 codes, 00801 to 00851.

The GeoNames data lists 16 postal codes in Vi, U.S. Virgin Islands, covering 5 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00801 to 00851.

The places with the most codes are Christiansted (5), St Thomas (5), Frederiksted (2), Kingshill (2), St John (2). The sub-areas with most codes are St. Croix (9), St. Thomas (5), St. John (2). Postal codes in U.S. Virgin Islands are written five digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.71 and 18.35 and longitude -64.97 and -64.68, around a centre at about 17.99, -64.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

3 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: St. Croix (9); St. Thomas (5); St. John (2).
