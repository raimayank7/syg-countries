Postal codes of Roi Et, Thailand

15 codes, 45000 to 45250.

The GeoNames data lists 15 postal codes in Roi Et, Thailand, covering 20 named places. In code order they run from 45000 to 45250.

The places with the most codes are Muang Roi Et (1), Chang Han (1), Chiang Khaw (1), Sri Somdet (1), Phon Thong (1), Salaphum (1). Postal codes in Thailand are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 15.48 and 16.72 and longitude 102.73 and 103.99, around a centre at about 15.89, 103.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
