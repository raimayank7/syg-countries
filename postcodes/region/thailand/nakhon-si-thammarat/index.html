Postal codes of Nakhon Si Thammarat, Thailand

20 codes, 80000 to 84310.

The GeoNames data lists 20 postal codes in Nakhon Si Thammarat, Thailand, covering 24 named places. In code order they run from 80000 to 84310.

The places with the most codes are Phra Phrom (1), Muang Nakhon Si Thammarat (1), Thung Song (1), Sichon (1), Chula Phon (1), Ron Phibun (1). Postal codes in Thailand are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.97 and 14.65 and longitude 99.37 and 100.60, around a centre at about 8.93, 99.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
