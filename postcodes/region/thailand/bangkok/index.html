Postal codes of Bangkok, Thailand

26 codes, 10100 to 10800.

The GeoNames data lists 26 postal codes in Bangkok, Thailand, covering 44 named places. In code order they run from 10100 to 10800.

The places with the most codes are Pom Prap Sattru Phai (1), Samphanthawong (1), Wattana (1), Bang Kholame (1), Yannawa (1), Thung Khru (1). Postal codes in Thailand are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 13.67 and 14.33 and longitude 99.92 and 100.87, around a centre at about 13.79, 100.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
