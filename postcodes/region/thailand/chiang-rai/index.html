Postal codes of Chiang Rai, Thailand

15 codes, 57000 to 57340.

The GeoNames data lists 15 postal codes in Chiang Rai, Thailand, covering 18 named places. In code order they run from 57000 to 57340.

The places with the most codes are Muang Chiang Rai (1), Mae Chan (1), Doi Luang (1), Mae Pha Luang (1), Phan (1), Mae Sai (1). Postal codes in Thailand are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 19.37 and 20.43 and longitude 99.50 and 100.40, around a centre at about 19.92, 99.93 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
