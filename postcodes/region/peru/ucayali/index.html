Postal codes of Ucayali, Peru

28 codes, 25000 to 25701.

The GeoNames data lists 28 postal codes in Ucayali, Peru, covering 761 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 25000 to 25701.

The places with the most codes are Nuevo Progreso (7), Santa Rosa (6), Vista Alegre (6), Nuevo Paraiso (6), Los Angeles (5), Santa Clara (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Coronel Portillo (406), Atalaya (259), Padre Abad (191), Purus (53). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -11.38 and -7.69 and longitude -75.84 and -70.55, around a centre at about -9.24, -74.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Coronel Portillo (406); Atalaya (259); Padre Abad (191); Purus (53).
