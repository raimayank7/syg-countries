Postal codes of La Libertad, Peru

140 codes, 13000 to 13871.

The GeoNames data lists 140 postal codes in La Libertad, Peru, covering 2,861 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 13000 to 13871.

The places with the most codes are Santa Rosa (17), Buenos Aires (14), Bellavista (14), Miraflores (13), Santa Cruz (11), La Esperanza (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Pataz (704), Sanchez Carrión (672), Otuzco (454), Gran Chimu (315). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.96 and -6.97 and longitude -79.69 and -76.93, around a centre at about -7.86, -78.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Pataz (704); Sanchez Carrión (672); Otuzco (454); Gran Chimu (315); Bolivar (289); Santiago De Chuco (281); Ascope (192); Trujillo (191); Julcan (164); Viru (131); Chepen (114); Pacasmayo (105).
