Postal codes of Amazonas, Peru

105 codes, 01000 to 01841.

The GeoNames data lists 105 postal codes in Amazonas, Peru, covering 2,433 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01000 to 01841.

The places with the most codes are Santa Rosa (17), Buenos Aires (17), San Antonio (16), Vista Alegre (15), Miraflores (15), San Juan (14). The sub-areas with most codes are Chachapoyas (768), Luya (708), Utcubamba (465), Rodriguez De Mendoza (310). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -6.97 and -3.12 and longitude -78.69 and -77.30, around a centre at about -6.01, -77.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Chachapoyas (768); Luya (708); Utcubamba (465); Rodriguez De Mendoza (310); Bongara (291); Bagua (289); Condorcanqui (254).
