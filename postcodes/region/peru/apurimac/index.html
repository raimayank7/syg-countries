Postal codes of Apurimac, Peru

97 codes, 03000 to 03881.

The GeoNames data lists 97 postal codes in Apurimac, Peru, covering 3,475 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 03000 to 03881.

The places with the most codes are Patahuasi (13), Santa Rosa (10), Tambo (8), Lambraspata (8), Palcca (8), Yanama (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Andahuaylas (824), Aymaraes (815), Abancay (619), Cotabambas (573). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -14.75 and -13.31 and longitude -73.82 and -72.07, around a centre at about -13.97, -73.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Andahuaylas (824); Aymaraes (815); Abancay (619); Cotabambas (573); Antabamba (514); Grau (380); Chincheros (378).
