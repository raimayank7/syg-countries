Postal codes of Tumbes, Peru

22 codes, 24000 to 24560.

The GeoNames data lists 22 postal codes in Tumbes, Peru, covering 183 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 24000 to 24560.

The places with the most codes are Pueblo Nuevo (5), Tumbes (2), Cardalitos (2), Los Pozos (2), Nueva Esperanza (2), Quebrada Seca (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tumbes (82), Contralmirante Villar (75), Zarumilla (36). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.19 and -3.45 and longitude -81.03 and -80.19, around a centre at about -3.76, -80.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

3 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tumbes (82); Contralmirante Villar (75); Zarumilla (36).
