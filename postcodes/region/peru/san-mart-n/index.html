Postal codes of San Martín, Peru

112 codes, 22000 to 22866.

The GeoNames data lists 112 postal codes in San Martín, Peru, covering 1,919 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 22000 to 22866.

The places with the most codes are Santa Rosa (21), Nueva Esperanza (17), Vista Alegre (16), San Juan (14), Miraflores (12), Buenos Aires (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Picota (356), Lamas (338), Bellavista (325), San Martín (314). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.76 and -5.48 and longitude -77.76 and -75.55, around a centre at about -6.84, -76.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

10 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Picota (356); Lamas (338); Bellavista (325); San Martín (314); Mariscal Cáceres (300); Tocache (277); Moyobamba (247); Huallaga (219); Rioja (188); El Dorado (133).

Other regions of Peru

Cuzco; Puno; Ancash; Ayacucho; Huancavelica; Huanuco; Cajamarca; Lima.
