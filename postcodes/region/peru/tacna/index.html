Postal codes of Tacna, Peru

28 codes, 23000 to 23850.

The GeoNames data lists 28 postal codes in Tacna, Peru, covering 757 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 23000 to 23850.

The places with the most codes are Tacna (4), Pocollay (3), La Esperanza (3), Alfonso Ugarte (3), Huayllani (3), Caplina (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tacna (296), Tarata (233), Candarave (190), Jorge Basadre (83). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -18.33 and -16.79 and longitude -71.13 and -69.47, around a centre at about -17.51, -70.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tacna (296); Tarata (233); Candarave (190); Jorge Basadre (83).
