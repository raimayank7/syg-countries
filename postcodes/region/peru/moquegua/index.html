Postal codes of Moquegua, Peru

23 codes, 18000 to 18620.

The GeoNames data lists 23 postal codes in Moquegua, Peru, covering 1,246 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 18000 to 18620.

The places with the most codes are Pucara (6), San Antonio (4), San Pedro (3), Buena Vista (3), Quebrada Honda (3), Camata (3). The sub-areas with most codes are General Sanchez Cerro (673), Mariscal Nieto (608), Ilo (45). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -17.69 and -15.99 and longitude -71.46 and -70.04, around a centre at about -16.67, -70.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

3 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: General Sanchez Cerro (673); Mariscal Nieto (608); Ilo (45).
