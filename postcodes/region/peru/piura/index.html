Postal codes of Piura, Peru

118 codes, 20000 to 20851.

The GeoNames data lists 118 postal codes in Piura, Peru, covering 2,266 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20000 to 20851.

The places with the most codes are Nueva Esperanza (18), Santa Rosa (18), Pueblo Nuevo (11), Miraflores (10), Santa Cruz (9), Nuevo Progreso (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Ayabaca (814), Huancabamba (648), Morropon (412), Piura (391). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -5.96 and -4.11 and longitude -81.30 and -79.22, around a centre at about -5.01, -80.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ayabaca (814); Huancabamba (648); Morropon (412); Piura (391); Sullana (332); Sechura (92); Paita (55); Talara (46).
