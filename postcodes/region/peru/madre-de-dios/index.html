Postal codes of Madre De Dios, Peru

17 codes, 17000 to 17800.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Madre De Dios, Peru, covering 305 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 17000 to 17800.

The places with the most codes are Santa Rosa (3), Nueva Alianza (3), Diamante (2), Aguas Negras (2), Nuevo San Juan (2), Miraflores (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tambopata (187), Manu (97), Tahuamanu (37). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -13.19 and -10.95 and longitude -72.09 and -68.66, around a centre at about -12.47, -69.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

3 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tambopata (187); Manu (97); Tahuamanu (37).
