Postal codes of Cajamarca, Peru

195 codes, 06000 to 06861.

The GeoNames data lists 195 postal codes in Cajamarca, Peru, covering 4,286 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 06000 to 06861.

The places with the most codes are Vista Alegre (36), Santa Rosa (33), La Union (28), La Laguna (25), El Porvenir (24), Miraflores (23). The sub-areas with most codes are Jaen (840), Cajamarca (830), Chota (748), Cutervo (622). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -7.69 and -4.92 and longitude -79.40 and -77.85, around a centre at about -6.58, -78.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Jaen (840); Cajamarca (830); Chota (748); Cutervo (622); San Ignacio (539); Celendín (525); Contumaza (481); San Miguel (427); Cajabamba (344); Santa Cruz (327); San Marcos (282); Hualgayoc (231).
