Postal codes of Ica, Peru

56 codes, 11000 to 11850.

The GeoNames data lists 56 postal codes in Ica, Peru, covering 1,200 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 11000 to 11850.

The places with the most codes are San Antonio (10), San Pedro (9), Santa Rosa (8), Casa Blanca (7), San Isidro (7), Pueblo Nuevo (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Ica (490), Chincha (370), Pisco (206), Nazca (175). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -15.37 and -13.01 and longitude -76.32 and -74.68, around a centre at about -13.94, -75.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ica (490); Chincha (370); Pisco (206); Nazca (175); Palpa (171).

Cuzco; Puno; Ancash; Ayacucho; Huancavelica; Huanuco; Cajamarca; Lima.
