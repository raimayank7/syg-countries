Postal codes of Ayacucho, Peru

140 codes, 05000 to 05855.

The GeoNames data lists 140 postal codes in Ayacucho, Peru, covering 6,191 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 05000 to 05855.

The places with the most codes are Pallcca (24), Santa Rosa (20), Patahuasi (18), Pampahuasi (17), Cruz Pata (14), Ccollpa (13). The sub-areas with most codes are Lucanas (2301), Parinacochas (835), Víctor Fajardo (708), Huamanga (677). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -15.63 and -12.36 and longitude -75.11 and -73.12, around a centre at about -14.01, -74.15 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Lucanas (2301); Parinacochas (835); Víctor Fajardo (708); Huamanga (677); Cangallo (621); Huanca Sancos (569); La Mar (522); Huanta (505); Sucre (332); Vilcas Huamán (291); Paucar Del Sara Sara (247).
