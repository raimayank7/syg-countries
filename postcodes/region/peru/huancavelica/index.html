Postal codes of Huancavelica, Peru

116 codes, 09000 to 09890.

The GeoNames data lists 116 postal codes in Huancavelica, Peru, covering 5,149 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 09000 to 09890.

The places with the most codes are Vista Alegre (26), Santa Rosa (20), Putacca (17), Patahuasi (17), Patacancha (17), Pampahuasi (14). The sub-areas with most codes are Castrovirreyna (1609), Huaytara (1590), Huancavelica (1183), Tayacaja (918). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -14.07 and -12.03 and longitude -75.73 and -74.33, around a centre at about -13.00, -75.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Castrovirreyna (1609); Huaytara (1590); Huancavelica (1183); Tayacaja (918); Angaraes (598); Churcampa (500); Acobamba (404).
