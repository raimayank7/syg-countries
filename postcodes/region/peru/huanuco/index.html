Postal codes of Huanuco, Peru

120 codes, 10000 to 10891.

The GeoNames data lists 120 postal codes in Huanuco, Peru, covering 5,438 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10000 to 10891.

The places with the most codes are Vista Alegre (28), Mesapata (20), Buena Vista (17), Buenos Aires (16), Tranca (15), San Cristobal (15). The sub-areas with most codes are Lauricocha (1323), Huanuco (1008), Huamalies (897), Dos De Mayo (838). Postal codes in Peru are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -10.45 and -8.47 and longitude -77.29 and -74.54, around a centre at about -9.76, -76.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Lauricocha (1323); Huanuco (1008); Huamalies (897); Dos De Mayo (838); Ambo (724); Yarowilca (507); Pachitea (384); Leoncio Prado (324); Marañon (240); Puerto Inca (231); Huacaybamba (220).
