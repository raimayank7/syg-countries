Postal codes of Calabria, Italy

211 codes, 87010 to 89900.

The GeoNames data lists 211 postal codes in Calabria, Italy, covering 862 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 87010 to 89900.

The places with the most codes are Reggio Calabria (16), Sant'Angelo (2), Cirella (2), Longobardi (2), Altilia (2), Marinella (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Cosenza (314), Reggio Di Calabria (255), Catanzaro (160), Vibo Valentia (109). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.92 and 40.11 and longitude 15.64 and 17.13, around a centre at about 38.89, 16.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
