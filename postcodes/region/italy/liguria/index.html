Postal codes of Liguria, Italy

197 codes, 12071 to 19139.

The GeoNames data lists 197 postal codes in Liguria, Italy, covering 531 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 12071 to 19139.

The places with the most codes are Genova (48), Laspezia (15), Orero (2), Casanova (2), San Bernardo (2), Prela' (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Genova (240), La Spezia (129), Savona (122), Imperia (109). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.78 and 44.65 and longitude 7.51 and 10.05, around a centre at about 44.24, 8.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
