Postal codes of Umbria, Italy

98 codes, 05010 to 06135.

The GeoNames data lists 98 postal codes in Umbria, Italy, covering 412 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 05010 to 06135.

The places with the most codes are Perugia (16), Collesecco (2), Ponte Nuovo (2), Macchie (2), Lippiano (1), Monte Santa Maria Tiberina (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Perugia (306), Terni (124). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.40 and 43.55 and longitude 11.94 and 13.21, around a centre at about 42.93, 12.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
