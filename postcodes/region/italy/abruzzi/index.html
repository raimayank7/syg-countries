Postal codes of Abruzzi, Italy

155 codes, 64010 to 67100.

The GeoNames data lists 155 postal codes in Abruzzi, Italy, covering 658 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 64010 to 67100.

The places with the most codes are Pescara (11), San Giovanni (3), San Nicola (2), Paterno (2), Santo Stefano (2), Pagliare (2). The sub-areas with most codes are L'Aquila (249), Chieti (166), Teramo (166), Pescara (98). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.74 and 42.88 and longitude 13.04 and 14.77, around a centre at about 42.30, 13.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
