Postal codes of Campania, Italy

304 codes, 80010 to 84135.

The GeoNames data lists 304 postal codes in Campania, Italy, covering 1,155 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80010 to 84135.

The places with the most codes are Napoli (26), Salerno (16), San Felice (3), Serra (3), Casola (2), Pagliara (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Salerno (421), Napoli (240), Caserta (238), Avellino (205). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.00 and 41.49 and longitude 13.82 and 15.75, around a centre at about 40.84, 14.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
