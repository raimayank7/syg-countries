Postal codes of Emilia-Romagna, Italy

336 codes, 29010 to 48100.

The GeoNames data lists 336 postal codes in Emilia-Romagna, Italy, covering 1,428 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 29010 to 48100.

The places with the most codes are Bologna (20), Parma (8), Modena (7), San Martino (5), Villanova (5), Reggio Emilia (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Modena (294), Bologna (246), Parma (225), Reggio Nell'Emilia (161). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.78 and 45.11 and longitude 9.29 and 12.74, around a centre at about 44.51, 11.05 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
