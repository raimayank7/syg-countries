Postal codes of Valle D'Aosta, Italy

20 codes, 11010 to 11100.

The GeoNames data lists 20 postal codes in Valle D'Aosta, Italy, covering 135 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 11010 to 11100.

The places with the most codes are Roisan (2), Vieyes (1), Plan D'Introd (1), Allein (1), Valgrisenche (1), Montan (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Valle D'Aosta (136). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.59 and 45.93 and longitude 6.95 and 7.88, around a centre at about 45.74, 7.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
