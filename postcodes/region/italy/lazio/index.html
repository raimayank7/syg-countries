Postal codes of Lazio, Italy

262 codes, 00010 to 04100.

The GeoNames data lists 262 postal codes in Lazio, Italy, covering 827 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00010 to 04100.

The places with the most codes are Roma (74), Santa Lucia (3), San Bartolomeo (2), La Botte (2), Sgurgola (1), Patoni (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Roma (348), Frosinone (178), Rieti (168), Latina (108). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.90 and 42.82 and longitude 11.50 and 13.99, around a centre at about 41.93, 12.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
