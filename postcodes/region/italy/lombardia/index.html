Postal codes of Lombardia, Italy

754 codes, 20001 to 46100.

The GeoNames data lists 754 postal codes in Lombardia, Italy, covering 2,398 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20001 to 46100.

The places with the most codes are Milano (38), Brescia (17), Bergamo (10), Sant'Antonio (4), Santa Croce (3), Ospitaletto (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Brescia (385), Bergamo (347), Milano (279), Pavia (240). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.75 and 46.54 and longitude 8.53 and 11.35, around a centre at about 45.59, 9.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
