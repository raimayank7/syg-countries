Postal codes of Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Italy

125 codes, 33010 to 34170.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Italy, covering 525 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 33010 to 34170.

The places with the most codes are Trieste (32), Caneva (2), Villanova (2), Castello (2), San Leonardo (2), Rivarotta (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Udine (314), Pordenone (131), Gorizia (60), Trieste (58). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.59 and 46.59 and longitude 12.44 and 13.86, around a centre at about 46.03, 13.16 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
