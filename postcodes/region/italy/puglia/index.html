Postal codes of Puglia, Italy

184 codes, 70010 to 76125.

The GeoNames data lists 184 postal codes in Puglia, Italy, covering 448 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 70010 to 76125.

The places with the most codes are Bari (13), Taranto (4), Foggia (3), San Cataldo (2), San Paolo (2), San Marco (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Lecce (160), Bari (101), Foggia (99), Taranto (53). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.80 and 42.12 and longitude 14.98 and 18.49, around a centre at about 40.73, 17.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
