Postal codes of Toscana, Italy

312 codes, 50010 to 59100.

The GeoNames data lists 312 postal codes in Toscana, Italy, covering 1,366 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50010 to 59100.

The places with the most codes are Firenze (26), Livorno (9), Pisa (9), Mercatale (3), San Quirico (3), Montemagno (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Firenze (264), Lucca (191), Pisa (167), Grosseto (161). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.36 and 44.43 and longitude 9.76 and 12.35, around a centre at about 43.61, 10.97 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
