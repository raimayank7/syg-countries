Postal codes of Sardegna, Italy

160 codes, 07010 to 09170.

The GeoNames data lists 160 postal codes in Sardegna, Italy, covering 612 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 07010 to 09170.

The places with the most codes are Cagliari (13), La Maddalena (2), Santa Maria (2), Berchidda (2), San Giovanni (2), Santa Lucia (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Sassari (208), Nuoro (126), Oristano (118), Cagliari (101). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.01 and 41.24 and longitude 8.15 and 9.79, around a centre at about 40.09, 9.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
