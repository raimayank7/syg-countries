Postal codes of Veneto, Italy

342 codes, 30010 to 45100.

The GeoNames data lists 342 postal codes in Veneto, Italy, covering 1,413 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30010 to 45100.

The places with the most codes are Padova (24), Verona (23), Venezia (13), Mestre (5), Sant'Elena (4), Santo Stefano (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Verona (274), Treviso (261), Vicenza (241), Padova (235). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.87 and 46.60 and longitude 10.69 and 13.04, around a centre at about 45.59, 11.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
