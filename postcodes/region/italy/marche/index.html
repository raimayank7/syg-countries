Postal codes of Marche, Italy

172 codes, 60010 to 63900.

The GeoNames data lists 172 postal codes in Marche, Italy, covering 557 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60010 to 63900.

The places with the most codes are Ancona (11), Pesaro (3), Pianello (2), Montefortino (2), Padiglione (2), Piagge (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Ancona (156), Pesaro E Urbino (152), Macerata (116), Ascoli Piceno (80). Postal codes in Italy are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.74 and 43.96 and longitude 12.25 and 13.89, around a centre at about 43.37, 13.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
