Postal codes of Sabirabad, Azerbaijan

28 codes, AZ 5400 to AZ 5446.

The GeoNames data lists 28 postal codes in Sabirabad, Azerbaijan, covering 28 named places. In code order they run from AZ 5400 to AZ 5446.

The places with the most codes are Sabirabad (1), 1 Sayli (1), 3 Sayli (1), Axisxa (1), Axtaçi (1), Cavad (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.79 and 40.09 and longitude 48.34 and 48.88, around a centre at about 40.01, 48.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
