Postal codes of Qəbələ, Azerbaijan

26 codes, AZ 3600 to AZ 3638.

The GeoNames data lists 26 postal codes in Qəbələ, Azerbaijan, covering 26 named places. In code order they run from AZ 3600 to AZ 3638.

The places with the most codes are Qəbələ (1), 1 Sayli (1), 2 Sayli (1), Bum (1), Bunud (1), Çuxur Qəbələ (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.80 and 41.06 and longitude 47.68 and 47.99, around a centre at about 40.92, 47.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
