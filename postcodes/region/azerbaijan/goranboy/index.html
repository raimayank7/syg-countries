Postal codes of Goranboy, Azerbaijan

23 codes, AZ 2200 to AZ 2248.

The GeoNames data lists 23 postal codes in Goranboy, Azerbaijan, covering 23 named places. In code order they run from AZ 2200 to AZ 2248.

The places with the most codes are Goranboy (1), 1 Sayli (1), Duzqishlaq (1), Aşaği Ağcakənd (1), Borsunlu (1), Boluslu (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.42 and 40.75 and longitude 46.52 and 46.89, around a centre at about 40.60, 46.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
