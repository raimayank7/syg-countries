Postal codes of Göyçay, Azerbaijan

27 codes, AZ 2300 to AZ 2333.

The GeoNames data lists 27 postal codes in Göyçay, Azerbaijan, covering 27 named places. In code order they run from AZ 2300 to AZ 2333.

The places with the most codes are Göyçay (1), 1 Sayli (1), 2 Sayli (1), 3 Sayli (1), Alxasafa (1), Alpoud (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.53 and 40.65 and longitude 47.67 and 48.02, around a centre at about 40.59, 47.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
