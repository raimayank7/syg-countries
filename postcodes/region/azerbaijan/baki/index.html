Postal codes of Baki, Azerbaijan

142 codes, AZ 1000 to AZ 1170.

The GeoNames data lists 142 postal codes in Baki, Azerbaijan, covering 142 named places. In code order they run from AZ 1000 to AZ 1170.

The places with the most codes are Baki (1), Baki-1 (1), Baki-2 (1), Baki-3 (1), Baki-4 (1), Baki-5 (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.31 and 40.38 and longitude 48.55 and 49.89, around a centre at about 40.10, 49.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
