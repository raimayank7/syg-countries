Postal codes of Gədəbəy, Azerbaijan

22 codes, AZ 2100 to AZ 2144.

The GeoNames data lists 22 postal codes in Gədəbəy, Azerbaijan, covering 22 named places. In code order they run from AZ 2100 to AZ 2144.

The places with the most codes are Gədəbəy (1), Çanaqçi (1), Arabaçi (1), Böyük Qaramurad (1), Çobankənd (1), Dəyirmandağ (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.41 and 40.83 and longitude 45.42 and 46.13, around a centre at about 40.61, 45.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
