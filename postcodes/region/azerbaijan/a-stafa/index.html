Postal codes of Ağstafa, Azerbaijan

18 codes, AZ 0500 to AZ 0533.

The GeoNames data lists 18 postal codes in Ağstafa, Azerbaijan, covering 18 named places. In code order they run from AZ 0500 to AZ 0533.

The places with the most codes are Ağstafa (1), Ağstafa Vağzal (1), Aşaği Göycəli (1), Aşaği Kəsəmən (1), Böyük Kəsik (1), Dağkəsəmən (1). Postal codes in Azerbaijan are written the fixed text "AZ", then a space, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.92 and 41.39 and longitude 45.19 and 46.19, around a centre at about 41.12, 45.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
