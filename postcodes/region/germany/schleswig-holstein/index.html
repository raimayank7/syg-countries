Postal codes of Schleswig-Holstein, Germany

517 codes, 21039 to 47789.

The GeoNames data lists 517 postal codes in Schleswig-Holstein, Germany, covering 1,296 named places. In code order they run from 21039 to 47789.

The places with the most codes are Kiel (17), Lübeck (12), Norderstedt (5), Flensburg (5), Neumünster (4), Hohenfelde (3). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.37 and 55.02 and longitude 7.89 and 11.19, around a centre at about 54.11, 9.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
