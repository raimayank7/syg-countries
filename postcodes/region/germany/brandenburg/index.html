Postal codes of Brandenburg, Germany

264 codes, 01945 to 19357.

The GeoNames data lists 264 postal codes in Brandenburg, Germany, covering 1,254 named places. In code order they run from 01945 to 19357.

The places with the most codes are Cottbus (10), Potsdam (8), Schipkau (4), Brandenburg an der Havel (4), Frankfurt (Oder) (4), Kloster Lehnin (3). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.38 and 53.46 and longitude 11.29 and 14.75, around a centre at about 52.41, 13.57 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
