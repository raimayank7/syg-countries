Postal codes of Bremen, Germany

73 codes, 27554 to 28779.

The GeoNames data lists 73 postal codes in Bremen, Germany, covering 83 named places. In code order they run from 27554 to 28779.

The places with the most codes are Bremen (34), Bremerhaven (7), Radio Bremen (2), Agentur für Arbeit Bremen (2), Weser-Elbe Sparkasse (1), Zech Management GmbH (1). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.03 and 53.59 and longitude 8.57 and 8.97, around a centre at about 53.11, 8.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
