Postal codes of Nordrhein-Westfalen, Germany

1,423 codes, 03075 to 96039.

The GeoNames data lists 1,423 postal codes in Nordrhein-Westfalen, Germany, covering 2,062 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 03075 to 96039.

The places with the most codes are Köln (45), Düsseldorf (37), Amtsgericht (33), Essen (32), Dortmund (27), Stadtverwaltung (26). The sub-areas with most codes are Regierungsbezirk Köln (695), Düsseldorf District (622), Regierungsbezirk Arnsberg (621), Regierungsbezirk Detmold (416). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.39 and 52.44 and longitude 5.93 and 9.39, around a centre at about 51.41, 7.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
