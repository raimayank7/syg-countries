Postal codes of Rheinland-Pfalz, Germany

750 codes, 51598 to 76891.

The GeoNames data lists 750 postal codes in Rheinland-Pfalz, Germany, covering 2,531 named places. In code order they run from 51598 to 76891.

The places with the most codes are Amtsgericht (12), Mainz (11), Koblenz (7), Ludwigshafen am Rhein (7), Stadtverwaltung (7), Finanzamt (7). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.98 and 50.90 and longitude 6.13 and 8.49, around a centre at about 49.98, 7.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
