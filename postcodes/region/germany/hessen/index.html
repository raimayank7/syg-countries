Postal codes of Hessen, Germany

831 codes, 10898 to 97419.

The GeoNames data lists 831 postal codes in Hessen, Germany, covering 1,098 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10898 to 97419.

The places with the most codes are Frankfurt am Main (41), Amtsgericht (14), Wiesbaden (13), Stadtverwaltung (12), Kassel (11), Wetzlar (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Regierungsbezirk Darmstadt (779), Regierungsbezirk Kassel (297), Regierungsbezirk Gießen (220). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.41 and 51.64 and longitude 7.80 and 10.17, around a centre at about 50.39, 8.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
