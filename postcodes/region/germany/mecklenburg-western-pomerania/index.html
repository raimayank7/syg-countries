Postal codes of Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Germany

223 codes, 17030 to 23999.

The GeoNames data lists 223 postal codes in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Germany, covering 992 named places. In code order they run from 17030 to 23999.

The places with the most codes are Rostock (10), Schwerin (6), Neuenkirchen (4), Bülow (4), Neubrandenburg (3), Lindetal (3). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.12 and 54.67 and longitude 10.65 and 14.35, around a centre at about 53.77, 12.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
