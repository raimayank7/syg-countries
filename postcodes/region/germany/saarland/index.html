Postal codes of Saarland, Germany

91 codes, 50424 to 66839.

The GeoNames data lists 91 postal codes in Saarland, Germany, covering 91 named places. In code order they run from 50424 to 66839.

The places with the most codes are Saarbrücken (16), Neunkirchen/Saar (3), Agentur für Arbeit Saarbrücken (2), Heusweiler (1), Kleinblittersdorf (1), Sulzbach/Saar (1). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.15 and 49.61 and longitude 6.38 and 7.34, around a centre at about 49.30, 6.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
