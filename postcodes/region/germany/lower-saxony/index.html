Postal codes of Lower Saxony, Germany

1,011 codes, 19273 to 71029.

The GeoNames data lists 1,011 postal codes in Lower Saxony, Germany, covering 2,020 named places. In code order they run from 19273 to 71029.

The places with the most codes are Hannover (29), Braunschweig (15), Amtsgericht (12), Oldenburg (9), Osnabrück (9), Göttingen (7). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.35 and 53.86 and longitude 6.69 and 11.56, around a centre at about 52.74, 9.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
