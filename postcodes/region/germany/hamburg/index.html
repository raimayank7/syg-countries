Postal codes of Hamburg, Germany

229 codes, 20038 to 50423.

The GeoNames data lists 229 postal codes in Hamburg, Germany, covering 354 named places. In code order they run from 20038 to 50423.

The places with the most codes are Hamburg (100), Hamburg Eimsbüttel (9), Hamburg Bramfeld (8), Hamburg Sankt Pauli (7), Hamburg Bahrenfeld (7), Techniker Krankenkasse (7). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.42 and 53.92 and longitude 8.50 and 10.27, around a centre at about 53.57, 10.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
