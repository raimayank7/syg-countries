Postal codes of Thuringia, Germany

264 codes, 04600 to 99998.

The GeoNames data lists 264 postal codes in Thuringia, Germany, covering 1,057 named places. In code order they run from 04600 to 99998.

The places with the most codes are Erfurt (13), Gera (6), Jena (4), Harth-Pöllnitz (3), Suhl (3), Weimar (3). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.24 and 51.59 and longitude 9.93 and 12.63, around a centre at about 50.92, 11.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
