Postal codes of Saxony, Germany

493 codes, 01053 to 10875.

The GeoNames data lists 493 postal codes in Saxony, Germany, covering 687 named places. In code order they run from 01053 to 10875.

The places with the most codes are Leipzig (31), Dresden (29), Chemnitz (15), Zwickau (6), Plauen (4), Riesa (3). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.22 and 51.64 and longitude 11.93 and 14.98, around a centre at about 51.00, 13.24 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
