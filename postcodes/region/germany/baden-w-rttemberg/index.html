Postal codes of Baden-Württemberg, Germany

1,559 codes, 10875 to 97999.

The GeoNames data lists 1,559 postal codes in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, covering 2,117 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10875 to 97999.

The places with the most codes are Stuttgart (35), Stadtverwaltung (25), Finanzamt (14), Mannheim (14), Karlsruhe (14), Freiburg im Breisgau (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Regierungsbezirk Stuttgart (900), Karlsruhe Region (602), Freiburg Region (465), Tübingen Region (440). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.55 and 49.76 and longitude 7.56 and 10.43, around a centre at about 48.63, 8.96 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
