Postal codes of Saxony-Anhalt, Germany

271 codes, 06073 to 39649.

The GeoNames data lists 271 postal codes in Saxony-Anhalt, Germany, covering 1,342 named places. In code order they run from 06073 to 39649.

The places with the most codes are Magdeburg (15), Halle (13), Dessau (5), Schkopau (3), Sandersdorf (3), Peißen (2). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.96 and 52.99 and longitude 10.60 and 13.15, around a centre at about 51.91, 11.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
