Postal codes of Land Berlin, Germany

361 codes, 10026 to 96076.

The GeoNames data lists 361 postal codes in Land Berlin, Germany, covering 397 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10026 to 96076.

The places with the most codes are Berlin (181), Berlin Kreuzberg (7), Berlin Friedrichshain (4), Bundesdruckerei GmbH (3), Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin Preußischer Kulturbesitz (2), Deutsches Institut für Wirtschaftsforschung (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Schöneberg (193). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.39 and 52.64 and longitude 13.13 and 13.70, around a centre at about 52.50, 13.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
