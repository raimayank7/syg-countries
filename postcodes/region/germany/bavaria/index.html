Postal codes of Bavaria, Germany

2,487 codes, 20083 to 97909.

The GeoNames data lists 2,487 postal codes in Bavaria, Germany, covering 3,051 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20083 to 97909.

The places with the most codes are München (75), Nürnberg (28), Augsburg (14), Finanzamt (13), Stadtverwaltung (10), Würzburg (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Upper Bavaria (1094), Regierungsbezirk Mittelfranken (476), Swabia (468), Regierungsbezirk Unterfranken (424). Postal codes in Germany are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.41 and 50.52 and longitude 9.01 and 13.79, around a centre at about 48.88, 11.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
