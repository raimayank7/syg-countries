33 codes, 2820000 to 3270000.

The GeoNames data lists 33 postal codes in Región del Libertador General Bernardo O’Higgins, Chile, covering 33 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2820000 to 3270000.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.79 and -33.96 and longitude -71.91 and -70.32, around a centre at about -34.39, -71.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
