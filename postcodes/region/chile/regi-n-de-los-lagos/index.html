Postal codes of Región de los Lagos, Chile

30 codes, 5290000 to 5880000.

The GeoNames data lists 30 postal codes in Región de los Lagos, Chile, covering 30 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 5290000 to 5880000.

The places with the most codes are Puerto Montt (1), Puerto Varas (1), Cochamo (1), Calbuco (1), Maullín (1), Los Muermos (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Provincia de Chiloé (10), Provincia de Llanquihue (9), Provincia de Osorno (7), Provincia de Palena (4). Postal codes in Chile are written seven digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -43.68 and -40.43 and longitude -74.00 and -71.98, around a centre at about -41.83, -73.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
