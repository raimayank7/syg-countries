Postal codes of Región Metropolitana, Chile

52 codes, 7500000 to 9810000.

The GeoNames data lists 52 postal codes in Región Metropolitana, Chile, covering 52 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7500000 to 9810000.

The places with the most codes are Providencia (1), Las Condes (1), Vitacura (1), Lo Barnechea (1), Ñuñoa (1), Macul (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Provincia de Santiago (32), Provincia de Melipilla (5), Provincia de Talagante (5), Provincia de Maipo (4). Postal codes in Chile are written seven digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.04 and -33.06 and longitude -71.45 and -70.10, around a centre at about -33.52, -70.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
