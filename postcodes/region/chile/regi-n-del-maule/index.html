Postal codes of Región del Maule, Chile

30 codes, 3340000 to 3720000.

The GeoNames data lists 30 postal codes in Región del Maule, Chile, covering 30 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3340000 to 3720000.

The places with the most codes are Talca (1), San Rafael (1), Pelarco (1), Río Claro (1), San Clemente (1), Maule (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Provincia de Talca (10), Provincia de Curicó (9), Provincia de Linares (8), Provincia de Cauquenes (3). Postal codes in Chile are written seven digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -36.26 and -34.84 and longitude -72.61 and -70.71, around a centre at about -35.48, -71.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
