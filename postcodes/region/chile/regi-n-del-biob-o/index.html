Postal codes of Región del Biobío, Chile

54 codes, 3780000 to 4600000.

The GeoNames data lists 54 postal codes in Región del Biobío, Chile, covering 54 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3780000 to 4600000.

The places with the most codes are Concepción (1), Chiguayante (1), San Pedro De La Paz (1), Penco (1), Tomé (1), Florida (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Provincia de Ñuble (21), Provincia de Biobío (14), Provincia de Concepción (12), Provincia de Arauco (7). Postal codes in Chile are written seven digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -38.30 and -36.18 and longitude -73.59 and -71.29, around a centre at about -37.05, -72.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
