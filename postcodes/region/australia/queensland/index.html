Postal codes of Queensland, Australia

461 codes, 4000 to 9015.

The GeoNames data lists 461 postal codes in Queensland, Australia, covering 3,857 named places and 78 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4000 to 9015.

The places with the most codes are Brisbane (11), Spring Creek (3), Greenmount (3), Aurukun (2), Tarramba (2), Stonehenge (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Brisbane (339), Toowoomba (257), Mackay (137), Sunshine Coast (136). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -29.13 and -9.23 and longitude 138.15 and 153.53, around a centre at about -24.44, 150.16 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
