Postal codes of Victoria, Australia

749 codes, 3000 to 8785.

The GeoNames data lists 749 postal codes in Victoria, Australia, covering 3,342 named places and 81 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3000 to 8785.

The places with the most codes are Melbourne (16), Golden Point (3), Benalla (3), Sunshine West (3), Springfield (3), Happy Valley (3). The sub-areas with most codes are East Gippsland (154), Wellington (142), Northern Grampians (121), Baw Baw (93). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -39.03 and -34.08 and longitude 141.00 and 149.75, around a centre at about -37.32, 144.68 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
