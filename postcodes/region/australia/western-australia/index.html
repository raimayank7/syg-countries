Postal codes of Western Australia, Australia

503 codes, 0872 to 6997.

The GeoNames data lists 503 postal codes in Western Australia, Australia, covering 1,816 named places and 138 sub-areas. In code order they run from 0872 to 6997.

The places with the most codes are Perth (20), Tuart Hill (5), Leederville (4), Cannington (3), Nedlands (3), Northbridge (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Greater Geraldton (68), Albany (64), Stirling (52), Swan (47). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -35.09 and -14.29 and longitude 113.42 and 133.14, around a centre at about -31.06, 117.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
