Postal codes of Tasmania, Australia

120 codes, 7000 to 7470.

The GeoNames data lists 120 postal codes in Tasmania, Australia, covering 808 named places and 29 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7000 to 7470.

The places with the most codes are Ansons Bay (2), North Hobart (2), Hobart (2), Sandy Bay (2), Kingston (2), Mangana (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Launceston (51), Huon Valley (50), Central Highlands (Tas.) (46), Meander Valley (44). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -43.56 and -39.62 and longitude 143.85 and 148.32, around a centre at about -41.86, 146.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
