Postal codes of Northern Territory, Australia

51 codes, 0800 to 4825.

The GeoNames data lists 51 postal codes in Northern Territory, Australia, covering 387 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 0800 to 4825.

The places with the most codes are Winnellie (5), Alice Springs (3), Tennant Creek (3), Katherine (3), Virginia (3), Amoonguna (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Darwin (63), Palmerston (48), Central Desert (46), Roper Gulf (40). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -25.58 and -11.15 and longitude 129.08 and 137.85, around a centre at about -15.85, 132.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
