Postal codes of Australian Capital Territory, Australia

30 codes, 2540 to 2914.

The GeoNames data lists 30 postal codes in Australian Capital Territory, Australia, covering 171 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2540 to 2914.

The places with the most codes are Canberra (2), Belconnen (2), Top Naas (1), Kowen Forest (1), Jervis Bay (1), Wreck Bay (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Unincorporated ACT (164), Snowy Valleys (4), Shoalhaven (3), Queanbeyan-Palerang (2). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -35.64 and -35.00 and longitude 148.82 and 150.59, around a centre at about -35.30, 149.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
