Postal codes of South Australia, Australia

350 codes, 0872 to 5950.

The GeoNames data lists 350 postal codes in South Australia, Australia, covering 2,045 named places and 72 sub-areas. In code order they run from 0872 to 5950.

The places with the most codes are Adelaide (6), Port Augusta (3), Kuitpo (2), Kulpara (2), Melton (2), Ulyerra (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Unincorporated SA (185), Mid Murray (79), Yorke Peninsula (71), Goyder (63). Postal codes in Australia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -38.05 and -22.20 and longitude 129.15 and 141.00, around a centre at about -34.13, 138.36 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
