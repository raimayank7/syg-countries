Postal codes of Provincia de Cartago, Costa Rica

51 codes, 30101 to 30804.

The GeoNames data lists 51 postal codes in Provincia de Cartago, Costa Rica, covering 48 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30101 to 30804.

The places with the most codes are Dulce Nombre (2), San Rafael (2), Santa Rosa (2), Oriental (1), Occidental (1), Carmen (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Turrialba (12), Cartago (11), La Unión (8), Paraíso (5). Postal codes in Costa Rica are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.68 and 10.05 and longitude -84.05 and -83.48, around a centre at about 9.88, -83.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Turrialba (12); Cartago (11); La Unión (8); Paraíso (5); Oreamuno (5); El Guarco (4); Jiménez (3); Alvarado (3).
