Postal codes of Provincia de Guanacaste, Costa Rica

59 codes, 50101 to 51104.

The GeoNames data lists 59 postal codes in Provincia de Guanacaste, Costa Rica, covering 58 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50101 to 51104.

The places with the most codes are Palmira (2), Liberia (1), Cañas Dulces (1), Mayorga (1), Nacascolo (1), Curubandé (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Santa Cruz (9), Nicoya (7), Tilarán (7), Nandayure (6). Postal codes in Costa Rica are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.84 and 11.07 and longitude -85.81 and -84.84, around a centre at about 10.36, -85.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
