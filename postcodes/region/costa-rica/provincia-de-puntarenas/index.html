Postal codes of Provincia de Puntarenas, Costa Rica

57 codes, 60101 to 61102.

The GeoNames data lists 57 postal codes in Provincia de Puntarenas, Costa Rica, covering 57 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60101 to 61102.

The places with the most codes are Puntarenas (1), Pitahaya (1), Chomes (1), Lepanto (1), Paquera (1), Manzanillo (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Puntarenas (16), Buenos Aires (9), Esparza (5), Osa (5). Postal codes in Costa Rica are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.53 and 10.29 and longitude -87.06 and -82.87, around a centre at about 9.35, -84.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
