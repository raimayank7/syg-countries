Postal codes of Provincia de Alajuela, Costa Rica

111 codes, 20101 to 21504.

The GeoNames data lists 111 postal codes in Provincia de Alajuela, Costa Rica, covering 96 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20101 to 21504.

The places with the most codes are San José (4), San Isidro (4), San Rafael (4), San Juan (3), Santiago (2), Concepción (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Alajuela (14), San Ramón (13), San Carlos (13), Grecia (8). Postal codes in Costa Rica are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.87 and 10.96 and longitude -85.36 and -84.17, around a centre at about 10.22, -84.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
