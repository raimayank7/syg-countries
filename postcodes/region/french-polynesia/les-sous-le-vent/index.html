Postal codes of Îles Sous-le-Vent, French Polynesia

6 codes, 98730 to 98735.

The GeoNames data lists 6 postal codes in Îles Sous-le-Vent, French Polynesia, covering 38 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 98730 to 98735.

The places with the most codes are Avera (2), Nunue (1), Anau (1), Faanui (1), Vaitape (1), Tefarerii (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Tahaa (12), Huahine (10), Bora-Bora (4), Maupiti (4). Postal codes in French Polynesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -16.89 and -16.44 and longitude -154.65 and -150.97, around a centre at about -16.69, -151.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tahaa (12); Huahine (10); Bora-Bora (4); Maupiti (4); Taputapuatea (4); Tumaraa (4); Uturoa (1).
