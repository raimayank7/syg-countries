Postal codes of Îles du Vent, French Polynesia

25 codes, 98701 to 98729.

The GeoNames data lists 25 postal codes in Îles du Vent, French Polynesia, covering 42 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 98701 to 98729.

The places with the most codes are Paopao (2), Punaauia (2), Arue (1), Faaa (1), Hitiaa (1), Mahaena (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Moorea-Maiao (20), Taiarapu-Est (5), Hitiaa O Te Ra (4), Taiarapu-Ouest (3). Postal codes in French Polynesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -17.88 and -17.00 and longitude -149.87 and -148.07, around a centre at about -17.59, -149.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Moorea-Maiao (20); Taiarapu-Est (5); Hitiaa O Te Ra (4); Taiarapu-Ouest (3); Mahina (2); Punaauia (2); Teva I Uta (2); Arue (1); Faaa (1); Paea (1); Papara (1); Papeete (1).
