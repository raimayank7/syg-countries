Postal codes of Îles Tuamotu-Gambier, French Polynesia

34 codes, 98755 to 98793.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Îles Tuamotu-Gambier, French Polynesia, covering 73 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 98755 to 98793.

The places with the most codes are Hitianau (2), Tuuhora (1), Tepupahea (1), Rautini (1), Niutahi (1), Raitahiti (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Gambier (17), Hao (10), Makemo (10), Fakarava (6). Postal codes in French Polynesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -23.34 and -14.17 and longitude -148.67 and -134.48, around a centre at about -18.19, -141.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Gambier (17); Hao (10); Makemo (10); Fakarava (6); Hikueru (5); Rangiroa (5); Anaa (3); Arutua (3); Takaroa (3); Fangatau (2); Manihi (2); Nukutavake (2).
