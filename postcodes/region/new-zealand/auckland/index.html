Postal codes of Auckland, New Zealand

255 codes, 0600 to 2697.

The GeoNames data lists 255 postal codes in Auckland, New Zealand, covering 173 named places. In code order they run from 0600 to 2697.

The places with the most codes are Wiri (6), Ellerslie (4), East Tamaki (4), Mangere (4), Te Atatu South (3), Swanson (3). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -37.40 and -36.15 and longitude 174.40 and 175.49, around a centre at about -36.84, 174.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
