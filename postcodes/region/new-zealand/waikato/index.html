Postal codes of Waikato, New Zealand

220 codes, 2121 to 4376.

The GeoNames data lists 220 postal codes in Waikato, New Zealand, covering 135 named places. In code order they run from 2121 to 4376.

The places with the most codes are Claudelands (5), Hamilton East (4), Leamington (4), Waihi (4), Tuakau (3), Te Rapa (3). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -39.03 and -36.63 and longitude 174.62 and 176.37, around a centre at about -37.80, 175.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
