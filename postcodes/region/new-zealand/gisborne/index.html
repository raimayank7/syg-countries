Postal codes of Gisborne, New Zealand

32 codes, 4010 to 4094.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in Gisborne, New Zealand, covering 23 named places. In code order they run from 4010 to 4094.

The places with the most codes are Te Karaka (3), Ruatoria (3), Kaiti (2), Tiniroto (2), Tolaga Bay (2), Tokomaru (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -38.77 and -37.63 and longitude 177.53 and 178.45, around a centre at about -38.28, 178.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
