Postal codes of Northland, New Zealand

124 codes, 0110 to 0975.

The GeoNames data lists 124 postal codes in Northland, New Zealand, covering 98 named places. In code order they run from 0110 to 0975.

The places with the most codes are Woodhill (3), Waipu (3), Ruawai (3), Ruakaka (2), Riverside (2), Maungakaramea (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -36.22 and -34.65 and longitude 172.95 and 174.57, around a centre at about -35.61, 174.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
