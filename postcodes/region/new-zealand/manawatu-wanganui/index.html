Postal codes of Manawatu-Wanganui, New Zealand

169 codes, 3920 to 5581.

The GeoNames data lists 169 postal codes in Manawatu-Wanganui, New Zealand, covering 119 named places. In code order they run from 3920 to 5581.

The places with the most codes are Marton (4), Ashhurst (3), Sanson (3), Kakatahi (3), Raetihi (3), Taihape (3). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -40.72 and -38.62 and longitude 174.80 and 176.41, around a centre at about -40.04, 175.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
