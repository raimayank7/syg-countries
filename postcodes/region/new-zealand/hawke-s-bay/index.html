Postal codes of Hawke's Bay, New Zealand

83 codes, 3360 to 4295.

The GeoNames data lists 83 postal codes in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand, covering 55 named places. In code order they run from 3360 to 4295.

The places with the most codes are Porangahau (4), Parkvale (3), Blackburn (3), Waipukurau (3), Waipawa (3), Petane (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -40.28 and -38.82 and longitude 176.25 and 177.92, around a centre at about -39.61, 176.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
