Postal codes of Southland, New Zealand

75 codes, 9600 to 9893.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in Southland, New Zealand, covering 56 named places. In code order they run from 9600 to 9893.

The places with the most codes are Te Anau (3), Lumsden (3), Otautau (2), Tuatapere (2), Nightcaps (2), Ohai (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -46.90 and -45.42 and longitude 167.60 and 169.13, around a centre at about -46.09, 168.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
