Postal codes of Taranaki, New Zealand

76 codes, 4310 to 4685.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Taranaki, New Zealand, covering 59 named places. In code order they run from 4310 to 4685.

The places with the most codes are Oakura (3), Eltham (3), Okato (3), Fitzroy (2), Waitara (2), Inglewood (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -39.80 and -38.87 and longitude 173.82 and 174.80, around a centre at about -39.30, 174.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
