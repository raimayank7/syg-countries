Postal codes of Canterbury, New Zealand

222 codes, 7260 to 9498.

The GeoNames data lists 222 postal codes in Canterbury, New Zealand, covering 154 named places. In code order they run from 7260 to 9498.

The places with the most codes are Christchurch (6), Hanmer Springs (4), Waiau (3), Amberley (3), Waikari (3), Oxford (3). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -44.86 and -42.00 and longitude 169.97 and 174.01, around a centre at about -43.61, 172.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
