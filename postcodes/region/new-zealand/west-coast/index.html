Postal codes of West Coast, New Zealand

42 codes, 7802 to 7895.

The GeoNames data lists 42 postal codes in West Coast, New Zealand, covering 33 named places. In code order they run from 7802 to 7895.

The places with the most codes are Runanga (2), Hokitika (2), Ross (2), Hector (2), Carters Beach (2), Reefton (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -43.46 and -41.22 and longitude 170.02 and 172.18, around a centre at about -42.40, 171.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
