Postal codes of Bay of Plenty, New Zealand

85 codes, 3010 to 3642.

The GeoNames data lists 85 postal codes in Bay of Plenty, New Zealand, covering 59 named places. In code order they run from 3010 to 3642.

The places with the most codes are Tauranga (5), Omokoroa Beach (3), Te Puke (3), Kawerau (3), Waimana (3), Mamaku (2). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -38.61 and -37.40 and longitude 175.92 and 177.67, around a centre at about -37.94, 176.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
