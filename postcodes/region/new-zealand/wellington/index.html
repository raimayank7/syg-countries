Postal codes of Wellington, New Zealand

112 codes, 5010 to 6972.

The GeoNames data lists 112 postal codes in Wellington, New Zealand, covering 78 named places. In code order they run from 5010 to 6972.

The places with the most codes are Mount Victoria (6), Masterton (4), Kelburn (4), Petone (3), Otaki (3), Martinborough (3). Postal codes in New Zealand are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -41.41 and -40.76 and longitude 174.71 and 176.07, around a centre at about -41.13, 175.11 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
