Postal codes of Burgenland, Austria

166 codes, 2413 to 8385.

The GeoNames data lists 166 postal codes in Burgenland, Austria, covering 331 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2413 to 8385.

The places with the most codes are Zillingtal / Celindof (3), Welten (3), Apetlon (3), Oberschützen (3), Eisenstadt (2), Hornstein / Vorištan (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Oberwart (106), Politischer Bezirk Oberpullendorf (68), Politischer Bezirk Güssing (64), Politischer Bezirk Jennersdorf (41). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.84 and 48.10 and longitude 16.01 and 17.11, around a centre at about 47.40, 16.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
