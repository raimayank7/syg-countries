Postal codes of Wien, Austria

128 codes, 1000 to 2301.

The GeoNames data lists 128 postal codes in Wien, Austria, covering 27 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1000 to 2301.

The places with the most codes are Wien (79), Wien Postfach (23), Wien, Donaustadt (2), Wien, Innere Stadt (1), Wien, Leopoldstadt (1), Wien, Landstraße (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Wien (Stadt) (95). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.14 and 48.30 and longitude 16.17 and 16.61, around a centre at about 48.21, 16.36 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
