Postal codes of Tirol, Austria

291 codes, 6000 to 9992.

The GeoNames data lists 291 postal codes in Tirol, Austria, covering 612 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6000 to 9992.

The places with the most codes are Innsbruck (10), Sölden (4), Moos (4), See (3), Natters (3), Thaur (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Lienz (158), Politischer Bezirk Innsbruck Land (126), Politischer Bezirk Schwaz (88), Politischer Bezirk Kufstein (86). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.70 and 47.68 and longitude 10.18 and 12.93, around a centre at about 47.22, 11.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
