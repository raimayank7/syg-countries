Postal codes of Steiermark, Austria

387 codes, 2654 to 9463.

The GeoNames data lists 387 postal codes in Steiermark, Austria, covering 1,888 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2654 to 9463.

The places with the most codes are Graz (12), Greith (11), Thal (6), Berndorf (6), Oberdorf (6), Mitterberg (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Südoststeiermark (335), Politischer Bezirk Graz-Umgebung (317), Politischer Bezirk Hartberg-Fürstenfeld (287), Politischer Bezirk Leibnitz (273). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.62 and 47.81 and longitude 13.60 and 16.14, around a centre at about 47.13, 15.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
