Postal codes of Kärnten, Austria

218 codes, 8820 to 9873.

The GeoNames data lists 218 postal codes in Kärnten, Austria, covering 2,275 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8820 to 9873.

The places with the most codes are Aich (13), Bach (11), Dellach (10), Hart (9), Goritschach (8), St. Peter (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Sankt Veit an der Glan (588), Politischer Bezirk Spittal an der Drau (449), Politischer Bezirk Klagenfurt Land (383), Politischer Bezirk Völkermarkt (337). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.44 and 47.07 and longitude 12.72 and 15.02, around a centre at about 46.74, 14.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
