Postal codes of Oberösterreich, Austria

454 codes, 3334 to 8934.

The GeoNames data lists 454 postal codes in Oberösterreich, Austria, covering 4,905 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3334 to 8934.

The places with the most codes are Au (31), Edt (25), Moos (23), Dorf (23), Grub (23), Aigen (21). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Braunau am Inn (915), Politischer Bezirk Vöcklabruck (886), Politischer Bezirk Grieskirchen (869), Politischer Bezirk Schärding (558). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.55 and 48.74 and longitude 12.76 and 14.97, around a centre at about 48.23, 13.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
