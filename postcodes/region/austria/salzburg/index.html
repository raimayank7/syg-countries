Postal codes of Salzburg, Austria

149 codes, 4854 to 8974.

The GeoNames data lists 149 postal codes in Salzburg, Austria, covering 650 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4854 to 8974.

The places with the most codes are Salzburg (13), Au (7), Winkl (7), Sonnberg (5), Unterberg (4), Wald (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Salzburg-Umgebung (279), Politischer Bezirk Zell am See (231), Politischer Bezirk Sankt Johann im Pongau (125), Politischer Bezirk Hallein (88). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.05 and 48.03 and longitude 12.12 and 13.95, around a centre at about 47.57, 13.05 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
