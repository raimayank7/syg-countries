Postal codes of Vorarlberg, Austria

101 codes, 6700 to 6993.

The GeoNames data lists 101 postal codes in Vorarlberg, Austria, covering 160 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6700 to 6993.

The places with the most codes are Mittelberg (3), Bregenz (3), Litze (2), Türtsch (2), Dalaas (2), Lech (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Politischer Bezirk Bludenz (77), Politischer Bezirk Bregenz (59), Politischer Bezirk Feldkirch (31), Politischer Bezirk Dornbirn (4). Postal codes in Austria are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.97 and 47.58 and longitude 9.58 and 10.19, around a centre at about 47.29, 9.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
