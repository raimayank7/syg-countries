Postal codes of Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil

77 codes, 79000-000 to 79995-000.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil, covering 77 named places and 77 sub-areas. In code order they run from 79000-000 to 79995-000.

The places with the most codes are Água Clara (1), Alcinópolis (1), Amambaí (1), Anastácio (1), Anaurilândia (1), Angélica (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Água Clara (1), Alcinópolis (1), Amambai (1), Anastácio (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -23.93 and -17.67 and longitude -57.58 and -51.35, around a centre at about -21.25, -54.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
