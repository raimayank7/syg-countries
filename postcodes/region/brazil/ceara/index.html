Postal codes of Ceara, Brazil

184 codes, 60000-000 to 63970-000.

The GeoNames data lists 184 postal codes in Ceara, Brazil, covering 184 named places and 184 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60000-000 to 63970-000.

The places with the most codes are Abaiara (1), Acarapé (1), Acaraú (1), Acopiara (1), Aiuaba (1), Alcântaras (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abaiara (1), Acarape (1), Acaraú (1), Acopiara (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -7.80 and -2.87 and longitude -41.24 and -37.41, around a centre at about -4.95, -39.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
