Postal codes of Pernambuco , Brazil

185 codes, 50000-000 to 56980-000.

The GeoNames data lists 185 postal codes in Pernambuco , Brazil, covering 185 named places and 185 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50000-000 to 56980-000.

The places with the most codes are Abreu e Lima (1), Afogados da Ingazeira (1), Afrânio (1), Agrestina (1), Água Preta (1), Águas Belas (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abreu e Lima (1), Afogados da Ingazeira (1), Afrânio (1), Agrestina (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -9.22 and -3.85 and longitude -41.06 and -32.42, around a centre at about -8.22, -36.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
