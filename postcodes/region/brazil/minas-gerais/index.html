Postal codes of Minas Gerais, Brazil

853 codes, 30000-000 to 39998-000.

The GeoNames data lists 853 postal codes in Minas Gerais, Brazil, covering 853 named places and 853 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30000-000 to 39998-000.

The places with the most codes are Abadia dos Dourados (1), Abaeté (1), Abre Campo (1), Acaiaca (1), Açucena (1), Água Boa (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abadia dos Dourados (1), Abaeté (1), Abre Campo (1), Acaiaca (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -22.83 and -14.38 and longitude -50.81 and -40.04, around a centre at about -19.58, -44.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
