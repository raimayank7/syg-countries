Postal codes of Para, Brazil

143 codes, 66000-000 to 68890-000.

The GeoNames data lists 143 postal codes in Para, Brazil, covering 143 named places and 143 sub-areas. In code order they run from 66000-000 to 68890-000.

The places with the most codes are Abaetetuba (1), Abel Figueiredo (1), Acará (1), Afuá (1), Água Azul do Norte (1), Alenquer (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abaetetuba (1), Abel Figueiredo (1), Acará (1), Afuá (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -9.14 and 0.57 and longitude -57.77 and -46.32, around a centre at about -3.03, -49.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
