Postal codes of Paraiba, Brazil

223 codes, 58000-000 to 58995-000.

The GeoNames data lists 223 postal codes in Paraiba, Brazil, covering 223 named places and 219 sub-areas. In code order they run from 58000-000 to 58995-000.

The places with the most codes are Água Branca (1), Aguiar (1), Alagoa Grande (1), Alagoa Nova (1), Alagoinha (1), Alcantil (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Água Branca (1), Aguiar (1), Alagoa Grande (1), Alagoa Nova (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.16 and -6.16 and longitude -38.70 and -34.84, around a centre at about -7.06, -36.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
