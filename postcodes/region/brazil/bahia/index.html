Postal codes of Bahia, Brazil

415 codes, 40000-000 to 48990-000.

The GeoNames data lists 415 postal codes in Bahia, Brazil, covering 415 named places and 413 sub-areas. In code order they run from 40000-000 to 48990-000.

The places with the most codes are Abaíra (1), Abaré (1), Acajutiba (1), Adustina (1), Água Fria (1), Érico Cardoso (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abaíra (1), Abaré (1), Acajutiba (1), Adustina (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -18.01 and -8.81 and longitude -45.91 and -37.62, around a centre at about -12.92, -40.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
