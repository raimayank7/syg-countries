Postal codes of Rio Grande do Norte , Brazil

166 codes, 59000-000 to 59995-000.

The places with the most codes are Acari (1), Açu (1), Afonso Bezerra (1), Água Nova (1), Alexandria (1), Almino Afonso (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Acari (1), Açu (1), Afonso Bezerra (1), Água Nova (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -6.89 and -4.91 and longitude -38.54 and -35.05, around a centre at about -5.98, -36.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
