Postal codes of Mato Grosso , Brazil

126 codes, 78000-000 to 78890-000.

The GeoNames data lists 126 postal codes in Mato Grosso , Brazil, covering 126 named places and 126 sub-areas. In code order they run from 78000-000 to 78890-000.

The places with the most codes are Acorizal (1), Água Boa (1), Alta Floresta (1), Alto Araguaia (1), Alto Boa Vista (1), Alto Garças (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Acorizal (1), Água Boa (1), Alta Floresta (1), Alto Araguaia (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -17.77 and -9.04 and longitude -60.06 and -50.83, around a centre at about -13.65, -55.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
