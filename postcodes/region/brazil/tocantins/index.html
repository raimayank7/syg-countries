Postal codes of Tocantins, Brazil

139 codes, 77000-000 to 77995-000.

The GeoNames data lists 139 postal codes in Tocantins, Brazil, covering 139 named places and 138 sub-areas. In code order they run from 77000-000 to 77995-000.

The places with the most codes are Abreulândia (1), Aguiarnópolis (1), Aliança do Tocantins (1), Almas (1), Alvorada (1), Ananás (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abreulândia (1), Aguiarnópolis (1), Aliança do Tocantins (1), Almas (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -13.06 and -5.25 and longitude -50.05 and -46.39, around a centre at about -9.28, -48.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
