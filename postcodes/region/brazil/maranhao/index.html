Postal codes of Maranhao , Brazil

217 codes, 65000-000 to 65995-000.

The sub-areas with most codes are Açailândia (1), Afonso Cunha (1), Água Doce do Maranhão (1), Alcântara (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -9.53 and -1.18 and longitude -48.36 and -42.02, around a centre at about -4.25, -44.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
