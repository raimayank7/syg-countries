Postal codes of Parana, Brazil

399 codes, 80000-000 to 87990-000.

The GeoNames data lists 399 postal codes in Parana, Brazil, covering 399 named places and 399 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80000-000 to 87990-000.

The places with the most codes are Abatiá (1), Adrianópolis (1), Agudos do Sul (1), Almirante Tamandaré (1), Altamira do Paraná (1), Altônia (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abatiá (1), Adrianópolis (1), Agudos do Sul (1), Almirante Tamandaré (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.44 and -22.57 and longitude -54.46 and -48.36, around a centre at about -24.38, -51.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
