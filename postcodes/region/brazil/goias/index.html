Postal codes of Goias, Brazil

242 codes, 72800-000 to 76740-000.

The GeoNames data lists 242 postal codes in Goias, Brazil, covering 242 named places and 242 sub-areas. In code order they run from 72800-000 to 76740-000.

The places with the most codes are Abadia de Goiás (1), Abadiânia (1), Acreúna (1), Adelândia (1), Água Fria de Goiás (1), Água Limpa (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abadia de Goiás (1), Abadiânia (1), Acreúna (1), Adelândia (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -19.12 and -12.99 and longitude -53.08 and -46.06, around a centre at about -16.18, -49.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
