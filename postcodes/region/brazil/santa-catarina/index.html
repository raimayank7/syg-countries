Postal codes of Santa Catarina , Brazil

293 codes, 88000-000 to 89998-000.

The GeoNames data lists 293 postal codes in Santa Catarina , Brazil, covering 293 named places and 293 sub-areas. In code order they run from 88000-000 to 89998-000.

The places with the most codes are Abdon Batista (1), Abelardo Luz (1), Agrolândia (1), Agronômica (1), Água Doce (1), Águas de Chapecó (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Abdon Batista (1), Abelardo Luz (1), Agrolândia (1), Agronômica (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -29.29 and -26.05 and longitude -53.72 and -48.50, around a centre at about -27.26, -50.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
