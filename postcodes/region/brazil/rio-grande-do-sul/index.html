Postal codes of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil

467 codes, 90000-000 to 99990-000.

The GeoNames data lists 467 postal codes in Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, covering 467 named places and 467 sub-areas. In code order they run from 90000-000 to 99990-000.

The places with the most codes are Água Santa (1), Agudo (1), Ajuricaba (1), Alecrim (1), Alegrete (1), Alegria (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Água Santa (1), Agudo (1), Ajuricaba (1), Alecrim (1). Postal codes in Brazil are written five digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -33.66 and -27.18 and longitude -57.31 and -49.82, around a centre at about -28.95, -52.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
