Postal codes of Kanton Aargau, Switzerland

249 codes, 4303 to 8967.

The GeoNames data lists 249 postal codes in Kanton Aargau, Switzerland, covering 276 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4303 to 8967.

The places with the most codes are Baden (6), Zofingen PF (4), Aarau (2), Brugg AG (2), Zofingen (2), Zofingen PostFinance (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Baden (41), Bezirk Zofingen (36), Bezirk Bremgarten (29), Bezirk Brugg (28). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.15 and 47.61 and longitude 7.73 and 8.44, around a centre at about 47.41, 8.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
