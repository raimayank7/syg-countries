Postal codes of Kanton Thurgau, Switzerland

125 codes, 8252 to 9573.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Kanton Thurgau, Switzerland, covering 205 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8252 to 9573.

The places with the most codes are Frauenfeld (6), Amriswil (1), Hefenhofen (1), Hagenwil b. Amriswil (1), Sommeri (1), Biessenhofen (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Weinfelden (65), Bezirk Frauenfeld (45), Bezirk Kreuzlingen (37), Bezirk Münchwilen (30). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.40 and 47.69 and longitude 8.70 and 9.46, around a centre at about 47.56, 9.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
