Postal codes of Kanton Zürich, Switzerland

291 codes, 6340 to 8955.

The GeoNames data lists 291 postal codes in Kanton Zürich, Switzerland, covering 297 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6340 to 8955.

The places with the most codes are Zürich (50), Winterthur (8), Wetzikon ZH (2), Urdorf (2), Sihlbrugg (1), Uetliberg (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Zürich (61), Bezirk Winterthur (51), Bezirk Andelfingen (33), Bezirk Bülach (28). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.18 and 47.69 and longitude 8.38 and 8.94, around a centre at about 47.42, 8.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
