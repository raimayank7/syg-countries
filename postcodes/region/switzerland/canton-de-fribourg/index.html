Postal codes of Canton de Fribourg, Switzerland

177 codes, 1410 to 3286.

The GeoNames data lists 177 postal codes in Canton de Fribourg, Switzerland, covering 284 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1410 to 3286.

The places with the most codes are Fribourg (3), Prévondavaux (1), Cheyres (1), Seiry (1), Lully FR (1), Estavayer-le-Lac (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Sarine District (64), Broye District (48), Gruyère District (47), Glâne District (46). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.48 and 47.00 and longitude 6.76 and 7.32, around a centre at about 46.75, 7.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
