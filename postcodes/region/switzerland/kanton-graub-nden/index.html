Postal codes of Kanton Graubünden, Switzerland

274 codes, 6534 to 7748.

The GeoNames data lists 274 postal codes in Kanton Graubünden, Switzerland, covering 313 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6534 to 7748.

The places with the most codes are Chur (5), Valbella (1), Lenzerheide/Lai (1), Vaz/Obervaz (1), Lantsch/Lenz (1), Brienz/Brinzauls GR (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Region Surselva (66), Region Viamala (51), Region Prättigau / Davos (38), Region Maloja (30). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.21 and 47.03 and longitude 8.74 and 10.46, around a centre at about 46.68, 9.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
