Postal codes of Kanton Basel-Landschaft, Switzerland

89 codes, 2814 to 4497.

The GeoNames data lists 89 postal codes in Kanton Basel-Landschaft, Switzerland, covering 97 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2814 to 4497.

The places with the most codes are Basel (1), Basel PF Operations Center (1), Basel PF OC (1), Bruderholz (1), Binningen (1), Binningen 1 (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Sissach (29), Bezirk Arlesheim (24), Bezirk Liestal (15), Bezirk Waldenburg (15). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.35 and 47.55 and longitude 7.34 and 7.94, around a centre at about 47.46, 7.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
