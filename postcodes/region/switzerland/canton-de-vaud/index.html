Postal codes of Canton de Vaud, Switzerland

338 codes, 1000 to 1892.

The GeoNames data lists 338 postal codes in Canton de Vaud, Switzerland, covering 473 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1000 to 1892.

The places with the most codes are Lausanne (13), Yverdon-les-Bains (2), Vevey (2), Villeneuve VD (1), Noville (1), Chessel (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Jura-Nord vaudois District (101), Morges District (72), Broye-Vully District (57), Gros-de-Vaud District (55). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.21 and 46.95 and longitude 6.08 and 7.21, around a centre at about 46.59, 6.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
