Postal codes of Canton de Neuchâtel, Switzerland

67 codes, 2000 to 2525.

The GeoNames data lists 67 postal codes in Canton de Neuchâtel, Switzerland, covering 84 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2000 to 2525.

The places with the most codes are Auvernier (1), Colombier NE (1), Bôle (1), Areuse (1), Cortaillod (1), Boudry (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Boudry District (19), Val-de-Ruz District (19), Neuchâtel District (16), Val-de-Travers District (13). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.87 and 47.12 and longitude 6.48 and 7.07, around a centre at about 46.99, 6.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
