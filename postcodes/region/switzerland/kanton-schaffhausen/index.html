Postal codes of Kanton Schaffhausen, Switzerland

36 codes, 8200 to 8455.

The GeoNames data lists 36 postal codes in Kanton Schaffhausen, Switzerland, covering 37 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8200 to 8455.

The places with the most codes are Schaffhausen (5), Neunkirch (1), Gächlingen (1), Löhningen (1), Stetten SH (1), Lohn SH (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Schaffhausen (14), Bezirk Reiat (10), Bezirk Stein (6), Bezirk Unterklettgau (5). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.57 and 47.79 and longitude 8.43 and 8.86, around a centre at about 47.71, 8.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
