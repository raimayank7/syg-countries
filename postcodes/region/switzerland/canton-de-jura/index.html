Postal codes of Canton de Jura, Switzerland

80 codes, 2336 to 2954.

The GeoNames data lists 80 postal codes in Canton de Jura, Switzerland, covering 93 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2336 to 2954.

The places with the most codes are Delémont (1), Develier (1), Bourrignon (1), Soyhières (1), Mettembert (1), Pleigne (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Porrentruy District (41), Delémont District (30), Franches-Montagnes District (21), Moutier (1). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.18 and 47.49 and longitude 6.90 and 7.51, around a centre at about 47.36, 7.16 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
