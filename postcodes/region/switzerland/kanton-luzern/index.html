Postal codes of Kanton Luzern, Switzerland

138 codes, 4806 to 6404.

The GeoNames data lists 138 postal codes in Kanton Luzern, Switzerland, covering 152 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4806 to 6404.

The places with the most codes are Luzern (10), Kriens (2), Ebikon (2), Hochdorf (2), Entlebuch (2), Luzern 16 (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Willisau District (40), Sursee District (33), Hochdorf District (29), Lucerne-Land District (28). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.82 and 47.27 and longitude 7.84 and 8.48, around a centre at about 47.12, 8.16 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
