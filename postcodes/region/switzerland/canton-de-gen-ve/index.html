Postal codes of Canton de Genève, Switzerland

60 codes, 1201 to 1298.

The GeoNames data lists 60 postal codes in Canton de Genève, Switzerland, covering 56 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1201 to 1298.

The places with the most codes are Genève (10), Grand-Lancy (1), Onex (1), Petit-Lancy (1), Vernier (1), Cointrin (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Geneva (65). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.14 and 46.35 and longitude 5.97 and 6.26, around a centre at about 46.21, 6.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
