Postal codes of Kanton Schwyz, Switzerland

50 codes, 6402 to 8864.

The GeoNames data lists 50 postal codes in Kanton Schwyz, Switzerland, covering 58 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6402 to 8864.

The places with the most codes are Schwyz (2), Bennau (1), Trachslau (1), Einsiedeln (1), Gross (1), Euthal (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bezirk Schwyz (28), Bezirk March (11), Bezirk Höfe (8), Bezirk Einsiedeln (7). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.94 and 47.21 and longitude 8.41 and 8.98, around a centre at about 47.09, 8.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
