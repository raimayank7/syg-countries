Postal codes of Kanton St. Gallen, Switzerland

196 codes, 7310 to 9658.

The GeoNames data lists 196 postal codes in Kanton St. Gallen, Switzerland, covering 208 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7310 to 9658.

The places with the most codes are St. Gallen (19), Gossau SG (2), Wittenbach (2), Rorschach (2), St. Gallen MS (1), St. Gallen Presse-Serv.Güll (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Wahlkreis St. Gallen (42), Wahlkreis Toggenburg (41), Wahlkreis Sarganserland (34), Wahlkreis Wil (31). Postal codes in Switzerland are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.91 and 47.52 and longitude 8.82 and 9.66, around a centre at about 47.31, 9.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
