Postal codes of Zakarpatska, Ukraine

381 codes, 88000 to 90647.

The GeoNames data lists 381 postal codes in Zakarpatska, Ukraine, covering 602 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 88000 to 90647.

The places with the most codes are Ужгород (16), Мукачеве (6), Новоселиця (Новоселицька сільська рада) (3), Буковець (Буковецька сільська рада) (2), Лази (Лазівська сільська рада) (2), Голубинне (Голубинська сільська рада) (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Mukachivskyi (94), Uzhhorodskyi (65), Khustskyi (62), Tyachivskyi (61). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.18 and 49.01 and longitude 22.21 and 23.43, around a centre at about 48.40, 22.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
