Postal codes of Dnipropetrovska, Ukraine

1,669 codes, 49000 to 53861.

The GeoNames data lists 1,669 postal codes in Dnipropetrovska, Ukraine, covering 1,309 named places and 31 sub-areas. In code order they run from 49000 to 53861.

The places with the most codes are Дніпро (76), Кривий Ріг (63), Кам'янське (26), Нікополь (12), Павлоград (10), Вільне (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Synelnykivskyi (125), Krynychanskyi (111), Solonianskyi (107), Piatykhatskyi (90). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.80 and 50.32 and longitude 28.64 and 39.25, around a centre at about 48.29, 34.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
