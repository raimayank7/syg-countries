Postal codes of Sumska, Ukraine

566 codes, 40000 to 42850.

The GeoNames data lists 566 postal codes in Sumska, Ukraine, covering 1,343 named places and 20 sub-areas. In code order they run from 40000 to 42850.

The places with the most codes are Суми (18), Конотоп (9), Шостка (8), Ромни (8), Степанівка (5), Олександрівка (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Romenskyi (138), Lebedynskyi (136), Bilopilskyi (128), Sumskyi (124). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.55 and 52.33 and longitude 31.93 and 36.52, around a centre at about 51.05, 34.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
