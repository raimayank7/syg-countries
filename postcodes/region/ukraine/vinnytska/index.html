Postal codes of Vinnytska, Ukraine

1,545 codes, 21000 to 24834.

The GeoNames data lists 1,545 postal codes in Vinnytska, Ukraine, covering 1,286 named places and 32 sub-areas. In code order they run from 21000 to 24834.

The places with the most codes are Вінниця (30), Вербівка (6), Павлівка (6), Олександрівка (6), Тарасівка (6), Михайлівка (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Nemyrivskyi (95), Barskyi (95), Khmilnytskyi (80), Koziatynskyi (73). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.86 and 51.67 and longitude 22.36 and 38.45, around a centre at about 49.11, 28.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
