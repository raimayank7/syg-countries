Postal codes of Poltavska, Ukraine

1,833 codes, 36000 to 39896.

The GeoNames data lists 1,833 postal codes in Poltavska, Ukraine, covering 1,549 named places and 27 sub-areas. In code order they run from 36000 to 39896.

The places with the most codes are Полтава (26), Кременчук (17), Новоселівка (9), Андріївка (7), Попівка (7), Павлівка (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Poltavskyi (148), Zinkivskyi (115), Myrhorodskyi (103), Kobeliatskyi (102). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.18 and 51.96 and longitude 28.88 and 37.03, around a centre at about 49.74, 33.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
