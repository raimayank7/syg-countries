Postal codes of Chernivetska, Ukraine

439 codes, 58000 to 60546.

The GeoNames data lists 439 postal codes in Chernivetska, Ukraine, covering 402 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 58000 to 60546.

The places with the most codes are Чернівці (23), Шишківці (3), Вашківці (2), Волока (2), Карапчів (2), Вовчинець (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Putylskyi (51), Kitsmanskyi (46), Novoselytskyi (43), Storozhynetskyi (39). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.27 and 51.66 and longitude 22.74 and 35.22, around a centre at about 48.80, 26.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
