Postal codes of Kirovohradska, Ukraine

944 codes, 25000 to 28646.

The GeoNames data lists 944 postal codes in Kirovohradska, Ukraine, covering 772 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 25000 to 28646.

The places with the most codes are Кропивницький (16), Іванівка (10), Олександрівка (9), Мар'янівка (7), Ганнівка (6), Костянтинівка (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Oleksandriiskyi (84), Novoukrainskyi (75), Kirovohradskyi (74), Bobrynetskyi (73). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.84 and 52.18 and longitude 24.10 and 39.43, around a centre at about 48.69, 33.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
