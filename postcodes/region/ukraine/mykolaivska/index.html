Postal codes of Mykolaivska, Ukraine

959 codes, 54001 to 57555.

The GeoNames data lists 959 postal codes in Mykolaivska, Ukraine, covering 888 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 54001 to 57555.

The places with the most codes are Миколаїв (28), Первомайськ (11), Вознесенськ (9), Дмитрівка (Дмитрівська) (4), Олександрівка (Олександрівська) (4), Іванівка (Іванівська) (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kazankivskyi (71), Pervomaiskyi (62), Domanivskyi (62), Bratskyi (60). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.84 and 50.38 and longitude 28.31 and 36.89, around a centre at about 47.44, 32.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
