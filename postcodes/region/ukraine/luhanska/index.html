Postal codes of Luhanska, Ukraine

551 codes, 91480 to 93891.

The GeoNames data lists 551 postal codes in Luhanska, Ukraine, covering 488 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 91480 to 93891.

The places with the most codes are Лисичанськ (13), Сєвєродонецьк (12), Рубіжне (11), Старобільськ (5), Кремінна (5), Михайлівка (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Svativskyi (62), Starobilskyi (62), Troitskyi (59), Bilokurakynskyi (53). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.37 and 52.01 and longitude 31.08 and 40.13, around a centre at about 49.27, 38.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
