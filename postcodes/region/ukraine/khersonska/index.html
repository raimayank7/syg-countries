Postal codes of Khersonska, Ukraine

722 codes, 73000 to 75841.

The GeoNames data lists 722 postal codes in Khersonska, Ukraine, covering 551 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 73000 to 75841.

The places with the most codes are Херсон (26), Каховка (6), Петрівка (5), Іванівка (5), Миколаївка (5), Федорівка (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Henicheskyi (61), Bilozerskyi (57), Velykooleksandrivskyi (54), Holoprystanskyi (54). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.80 and 49.75 and longitude 30.54 and 37.34, around a centre at about 46.72, 33.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
