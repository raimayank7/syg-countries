Postal codes of Odeska, Ukraine

1,288 codes, 65000 to 68831.

The GeoNames data lists 1,288 postal codes in Odeska, Ukraine, covering 1,127 named places and 29 sub-areas. In code order they run from 65000 to 68831.

The places with the most codes are Одеса (71), Ізмаїл (12), Подільськ (7), Новоселівка (Новоселівська сільська рада) (7), Білгород-Дністровський (7), Чорноморськ (Чорноморська міська рада) (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Rozdilnianskyi (87), Velykomykhailivskyi (83), Odeska (72), Shyriaivskyi (72). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.35 and 49.61 and longitude 28.29 and 37.44, around a centre at about 46.91, 30.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
