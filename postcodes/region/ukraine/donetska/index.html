Postal codes of Donetska, Ukraine

923 codes, 84000 to 87663.

The GeoNames data lists 923 postal codes in Donetska, Ukraine, covering 709 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 84000 to 87663.

The places with the most codes are Маріуполь (46), Краматорськ (17), Слов'янськ (14), Костянтинівка (12), Бахмут (8), Дружківка (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Pokrovskiy (108), Bahmutskiy (103), Volnovaskyi (88), Kostiantynivskyi (71). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.89 and 50.64 and longitude 32.41 and 39.96, around a centre at about 48.19, 37.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
