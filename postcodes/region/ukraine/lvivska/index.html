Postal codes of Lvivska, Ukraine

1,728 codes, 79000 to 82666.

The GeoNames data lists 1,728 postal codes in Lvivska, Ukraine, covering 1,763 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 79000 to 82666.

The places with the most codes are Львів (46), Червоноград (10), Дрогобич (9), Стрий (7), Волиця (5), Руда (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Zhovkivskyi (165), Yavorivskyi (144), Zhydachivskyi (119), Starosambirskyi (115). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.23 and 51.98 and longitude 22.36 and 32.54, around a centre at about 49.77, 24.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
