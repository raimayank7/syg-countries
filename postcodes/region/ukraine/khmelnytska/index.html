Postal codes of Khmelnytska, Ukraine

924 codes, 29000 to 32721.

The GeoNames data lists 924 postal codes in Khmelnytska, Ukraine, covering 1,292 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 29000 to 32721.

The places with the most codes are Хмельницький (24), Кам'янець-Подільський (10), Антонівка (7), Іванівка (6), Іванківці (6), Славута (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Kamianets-Podilskyi (133), Starokostiantynivskyi (100), Krasylivskyi (96), Iziaslavskyi (94). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.68 and 52.31 and longitude 22.85 and 38.11, around a centre at about 49.78, 27.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
