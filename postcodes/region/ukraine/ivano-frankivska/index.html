Postal codes of Ivano-Frankivska, Ukraine

831 codes, 76000 to 78745.

The GeoNames data lists 831 postal codes in Ivano-Frankivska, Ukraine, covering 756 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 76000 to 78745.

The places with the most codes are Івано-Франківськ (15), Калуш (9), Діброва (5), Слобідка (4), Долина (4), Вигода (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Rohatynskyi (89), Kolomyiskyi (85), Halytskyi (69), Kaluskyi (63). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.89 and 51.66 and longitude 22.85 and 29.48, around a centre at about 48.99, 24.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
