Postal codes of Cherkaska, Ukraine

906 codes, 18000 to 20949.

The GeoNames data lists 906 postal codes in Cherkaska, Ukraine, covering 785 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 18000 to 20949.

The places with the most codes are Черкаси (24), Сміла (10), Умань (9), Михайлівка (6), Попівка (4), Тарасівка (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Zolotoniskyi (66), Kanivskyi (62), Umanskyi (62), Korsun-Shevchenkivskyi (54). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.30 and 52.18 and longitude 24.90 and 39.25, around a centre at about 49.43, 31.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
