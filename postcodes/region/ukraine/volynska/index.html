Postal codes of Volynska, Ukraine

1,117 codes, 43005 to 45798.

The GeoNames data lists 1,117 postal codes in Volynska, Ukraine, covering 1,003 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 43005 to 45798.

The places with the most codes are Луцьк (16), Ковель (8), Нововолинськ (8), Діброва (4), Новосілки (4), Мар'янівка (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Kovelskyi (101), Horokhivskyi (94), Lutskyi (86), Volodymyr-Volynskyi (81). Postal codes in Ukraine are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.88 and 51.91 and longitude 23.67 and 38.48, around a centre at about 51.06, 24.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
