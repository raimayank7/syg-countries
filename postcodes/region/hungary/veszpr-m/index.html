Postal codes of Veszprém, Hungary

198 codes, 8100 to 9534.

The GeoNames data lists 198 postal codes in Veszprém, Hungary, covering 224 named places. In code order they run from 8100 to 9534.

The places with the most codes are Ajka (4), Pápa (4), Veszprém (3), Badacsonytomaj (3), Balatonfűzfő (2), Berhida (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.80 and 47.45 and longitude 17.14 and 18.21, around a centre at about 47.12, 17.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
