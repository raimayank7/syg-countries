Postal codes of Nógrád, Hungary

127 codes, 2175 to 3188.

The GeoNames data lists 127 postal codes in Nógrád, Hungary, covering 131 named places. In code order they run from 2175 to 3188.

The places with the most codes are Salgótarján (5), Pásztó (3), Bátonyterenye (3), Kazár (2), Mátranovák (2), Mátraterenye (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.75 and 48.24 and longitude 18.99 and 20.05, around a centre at about 47.99, 19.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
