Postal codes of Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok, Hungary

89 codes, 5000 to 5476.

The GeoNames data lists 89 postal codes in Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok, Hungary, covering 78 named places. In code order they run from 5000 to 5476.

The places with the most codes are Tiszafüred (3), Szolnok (2), Tiszavárkony (2), Jászberény (2), Törökszentmiklós (2), Fegyvernek (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.80 and 47.64 and longitude 19.72 and 20.93, around a centre at about 47.27, 20.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
