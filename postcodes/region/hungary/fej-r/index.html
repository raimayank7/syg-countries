Postal codes of Fejér, Hungary

119 codes, 2060 to 8157.

The GeoNames data lists 119 postal codes in Fejér, Hungary, covering 109 named places. In code order they run from 2060 to 8157.

The places with the most codes are Sárbogárd (4), Gárdony (3), Dunaújváros (2), Baracs (2), Ercsi (2), Székesfehérvár (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.72 and 47.53 and longitude 18.08 and 18.94, around a centre at about 47.15, 18.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
