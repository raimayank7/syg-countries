Postal codes of Vas, Hungary

178 codes, 9500 to 9985.

The GeoNames data lists 178 postal codes in Vas, Hungary, covering 216 named places. In code order they run from 9500 to 9985.

The places with the most codes are Sárvár (3), Szentgotthárd (3), Celldömölk (2), Szombathely (2), Bük (2), Meggyeskovácsi (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.74 and 47.43 and longitude 16.16 and 17.27, around a centre at about 47.15, 16.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
