Postal codes of Baranya, Hungary

177 codes, 7188 to 7985.

The GeoNames data lists 177 postal codes in Baranya, Hungary, covering 298 named places. In code order they run from 7188 to 7985.

The places with the most codes are Pécs (21), Mohács (3), Gödre (2), Szárász (1), Komló (1), Mánfa (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.78 and 46.39 and longitude 17.65 and 18.79, around a centre at about 46.03, 18.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
