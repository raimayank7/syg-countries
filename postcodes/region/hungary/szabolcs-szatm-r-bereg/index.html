Postal codes of Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg, Hungary

234 codes, 4231 to 4977.

The GeoNames data lists 234 postal codes in Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg, Hungary, covering 229 named places. In code order they run from 4231 to 4977.

The places with the most codes are Nyíregyháza (8), Vásárosnamény (3), Tiszaeszlár (2), Tiszalök (2), Gávavencsellő (2), Tiszatelek (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.63 and 48.41 and longitude 21.17 and 22.86, around a centre at about 48.03, 22.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
