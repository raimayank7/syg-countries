Postal codes of Heves, Hungary

126 codes, 3000 to 3399.

The GeoNames data lists 126 postal codes in Heves, Hungary, covering 121 named places. In code order they run from 3000 to 3399.

The places with the most codes are Gyöngyös (4), Lőrinci (3), Mátraszentimre (2), Parád (2), Visonta (2), Hatvan (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.48 and 48.13 and longitude 19.60 and 20.67, around a centre at about 47.82, 20.15 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
