Postal codes of Békés, Hungary

84 codes, 5500 to 5948.

The GeoNames data lists 84 postal codes in Békés, Hungary, covering 75 named places. In code order they run from 5500 to 5948.

The places with the most codes are Orosháza (4), Békéscsaba (3), Gyomaendrőd (2), Medgyesbodzás (2), Medgyesegyháza (2), Gyula (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.28 and 47.20 and longitude 20.48 and 21.65, around a centre at about 46.69, 21.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
