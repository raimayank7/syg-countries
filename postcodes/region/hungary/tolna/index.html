Postal codes of Tolna, Hungary

110 codes, 7020 to 7362.

The GeoNames data lists 110 postal codes in Tolna, Hungary, covering 112 named places. In code order they run from 7020 to 7362.

The places with the most codes are Paks (2), Tolna (2), Bonyhád (2), Dunaföldvár (1), Bölcske (1), Madocsa (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.13 and 46.81 and longitude 18.01 and 18.97, around a centre at about 46.47, 18.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
