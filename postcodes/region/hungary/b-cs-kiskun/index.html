Postal codes of Bács-Kiskun, Hungary

124 codes, 6000 to 6528.

The GeoNames data lists 124 postal codes in Bács-Kiskun, Hungary, covering 119 named places. In code order they run from 6000 to 6528.

The places with the most codes are Kecskemét (3), Tiszakécske (2), Tiszaalpár (2), Harta (2), Baja (2), Szentkirály (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.95 and 47.07 and longitude 18.88 and 20.10, around a centre at about 46.55, 19.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
