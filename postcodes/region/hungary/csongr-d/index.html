Postal codes of Csongrád, Hungary

79 codes, 6600 to 6933.

The GeoNames data lists 79 postal codes in Csongrád, Hungary, covering 60 named places. In code order they run from 6600 to 6933.

The places with the most codes are Szeged (16), Hódmezővásárhely (3), Csongrád (2), Makó (2), Szentes (1), Nagytőke (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.15 and 46.71 and longitude 19.68 and 20.73, around a centre at about 46.36, 20.23 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
