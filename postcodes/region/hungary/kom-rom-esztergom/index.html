Postal codes of Komárom-Esztergom, Hungary

81 codes, 2027 to 2949.

The GeoNames data lists 81 postal codes in Komárom-Esztergom, Hungary, covering 75 named places. In code order they run from 2027 to 2949.

The places with the most codes are Esztergom (3), Komárom (3), Tata (2), Kisbér (2), Tárkány (2), Dömös (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.37 and 47.79 and longitude 17.90 and 18.91, around a centre at about 47.62, 18.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
