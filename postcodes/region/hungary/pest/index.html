Postal codes of Pest, Hungary

196 codes, 2000 to 2769.

The GeoNames data lists 196 postal codes in Pest, Hungary, covering 187 named places. In code order they run from 2000 to 2769.

The places with the most codes are Dabas (3), Tahitótfalu (2), Visegrád (2), Pilisszentkereszt (2), Göd (2), Kerepes (2). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.00 and 48.03 and longitude 18.72 and 20.07, around a centre at about 47.50, 19.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
