Postal codes of Zala, Hungary

160 codes, 8313 to 8999.

The GeoNames data lists 160 postal codes in Zala, Hungary, covering 258 named places. In code order they run from 8313 to 8999.

The places with the most codes are Zalaszentgrót (4), Nagykanizsa (4), Zalakomár (2), Zalaegerszeg (2), Lenti (2), Balatongyörök (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.32 and 47.01 and longitude 16.40 and 17.35, around a centre at about 46.70, 16.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
