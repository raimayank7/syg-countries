Postal codes of Somogy, Hungary

195 codes, 7253 to 8858.

The GeoNames data lists 195 postal codes in Somogy, Hungary, covering 245 named places. In code order they run from 7253 to 8858.

The places with the most codes are Marcali (3), Kaposvár (2), Barcs (2), Balatonboglár (2), Szabadi (1), Csoma (1). Postal codes in Hungary are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.87 and 46.90 and longitude 16.93 and 18.17, around a centre at about 46.45, 17.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
