Postal codes of Jalisco, Mexico

2,025 codes, 44009 to 49994.

The GeoNames data lists 2,025 postal codes in Jalisco, Mexico, covering 4,363 named places and 125 sub-areas. In code order they run from 44009 to 49994.

The places with the most codes are San Antonio (16), Lázaro Cárdenas (14), El Refugio (13), San Francisco (13), San Isidro (13), Emiliano Zapata (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Zapopan (595), Lagos de Moreno (511), Guadalajara (450), Tonalá (343). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 18.98 and 22.74 and longitude -105.63 and -101.57, around a centre at about 20.65, -103.33 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
