Postal codes of México, Mexico

2,382 codes, 50000 to 57950.

The GeoNames data lists 2,382 postal codes in México, Mexico, covering 6,457 named places and 125 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50000 to 57950.

The places with the most codes are San Isidro (29), Emiliano Zapata (26), San Miguel (25), Buenavista (24), San Juan (24), Guadalupe (22). The sub-areas with most codes are Ecatepec de Morelos (543), Toluca (449), Naucalpan de Juárez (353), Tlalnepantla de Baz (258). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 18.38 and 20.27 and longitude -100.59 and -98.65, around a centre at about 19.41, -99.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
