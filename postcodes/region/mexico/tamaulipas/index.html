Postal codes of Tamaulipas, Mexico

996 codes, 87000 to 89970.

The GeoNames data lists 996 postal codes in Tamaulipas, Mexico, covering 2,304 named places and 43 sub-areas. In code order they run from 87000 to 89970.

The places with the most codes are Independencia (14), Emiliano Zapata (12), Jardín (12), Obrera (12), Lázaro Cárdenas (11), Tamaulipas (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Matamoros (465), Victoria (389), Reynosa (365), Nuevo Laredo (239). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.22 and 27.65 and longitude -100.09 and -97.15, around a centre at about 24.51, -98.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
