Postal codes of Tabasco, Mexico

522 codes, 86000 to 86998.

The GeoNames data lists 522 postal codes in Tabasco, Mexico, covering 2,225 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 86000 to 86998.

The places with the most codes are Santa Cruz (10), Benito Juárez (10), San Francisco (9), Lázaro Cárdenas (9), La Ceiba (9), El Sacrificio (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Centro (501), Balancán (483), Huimanguillo (242), Macuspana (222). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.25 and 18.61 and longitude -94.13 and -91.02, around a centre at about 17.94, -92.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Centro (501); Balancán (483); Huimanguillo (242); Macuspana (222); Centla (162); Cárdenas (161); Comalcalco (147); Tenosique (131); Cunduacán (120); Jonuta (83); Tacotalpa (78); Teapa (73).
