Postal codes of Coahuila de Zaragoza, Mexico

1,064 codes, 25000 to 27997.

The GeoNames data lists 1,064 postal codes in Coahuila de Zaragoza, Mexico, covering 3,046 named places and 38 sub-areas. In code order they run from 25000 to 27997.

The places with the most codes are San Isidro (14), Santa María (13), Emiliano Zapata (12), San Antonio (11), Benito Juárez (11), Independencia (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Saltillo (662), Torreón (510), San Pedro (265), Francisco I. Madero (240). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 24.62 and 29.74 and longitude -103.81 and -100.05, around a centre at about 26.54, -101.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
