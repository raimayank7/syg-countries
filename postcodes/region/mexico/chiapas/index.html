Postal codes of Chiapas, Mexico

1,075 codes, 29000 to 30997.

The GeoNames data lists 1,075 postal codes in Chiapas, Mexico, covering 4,613 named places and 118 sub-areas. In code order they run from 29000 to 30997.

The places with the most codes are Guadalupe (47), Emiliano Zapata (38), Santa Cruz (36), San Antonio (35), San Francisco (32), Benito Juárez (32). The sub-areas with most codes are Cintalapa (875), Tapachula (660), Tuxtla Gutiérrez (470), Motozintla (327). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 14.54 and 17.92 and longitude -94.12 and -90.88, around a centre at about 16.25, -92.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
