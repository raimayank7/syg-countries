Postal codes of Zacatecas, Mexico

577 codes, 98000 to 99998.

The GeoNames data lists 577 postal codes in Zacatecas, Mexico, covering 1,445 named places and 58 sub-areas. In code order they run from 98000 to 99998.

The places with the most codes are Magisterial (13), San Francisco (7), Lindavista (7), Emiliano Zapata (7), Las Flores (6), Arboledas (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Guadalupe (266), Zacatecas (197), Fresnillo (129), Jerez (125). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 21.10 and 24.99 and longitude -104.10 and -100.87, around a centre at about 22.78, -102.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
