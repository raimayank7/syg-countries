Postal codes of Tlaxcala, Mexico

396 codes, 90000 to 90990.

The GeoNames data lists 396 postal codes in Tlaxcala, Mexico, covering 1,262 named places and 60 sub-areas. In code order they run from 90000 to 90990.

The places with the most codes are La Cañada (9), Guadalupe (9), Loma Bonita (8), San Isidro (8), San Miguel (7), Los Pinos (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Tlaxco (153), Calpulalpan (103), Tlaxcala (102), Apizaco (91). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 19.11 and 19.71 and longitude -98.62 and -97.67, around a centre at about 19.41, -98.19 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
