Postal codes of Sinaloa, Mexico

1,092 codes, 80000 to 82996.

The GeoNames data lists 1,092 postal codes in Sinaloa, Mexico, covering 2,926 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80000 to 82996.

The places with the most codes are Benito Juárez (11), Las Palmas (9), Ejidal (9), El Aguaje (9), Emiliano Zapata (8), San Antonio (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Culiacán (819), Mazatlán (421), Ahome (416), Badiraguato (328). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.51 and 27.01 and longitude -109.37 and -105.44, around a centre at about 24.94, -107.57 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
