Postal codes of Nuevo León, Mexico

1,107 codes, 64000 to 67996.

The GeoNames data lists 1,107 postal codes in Nuevo León, Mexico, covering 4,315 named places and 51 sub-areas. In code order they run from 64000 to 67996.

The places with the most codes are Los Nogales (12), Las Palmas (11), San Isidro (11), Benito Juárez (10), Lázaro Cárdenas (9), San Pedro (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Monterrey (915), Guadalupe (447), Apodaca (433), San Nicolás de los Garza (403). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 23.25 and 27.70 and longitude -100.89 and -98.58, around a centre at about 25.62, -100.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
