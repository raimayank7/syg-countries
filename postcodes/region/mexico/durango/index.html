Postal codes of Durango, Mexico

1,052 codes, 34000 to 35987.

The GeoNames data lists 1,052 postal codes in Durango, Mexico, covering 4,878 named places and 39 sub-areas. In code order they run from 34000 to 35987.

The places with the most codes are El Ranchito (29), La Joya (25), San Isidro (25), La Esperanza (17), San Antonio (17), El Carrizo (17). The sub-areas with most codes are Mezquital (1221), Durango (773), Tamazula (658), San Dimas (548). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.38 and 26.70 and longitude -107.16 and -102.49, around a centre at about 24.49, -105.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
