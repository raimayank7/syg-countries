Postal codes of Querétaro, Mexico

532 codes, 76000 to 76998.

The GeoNames data lists 532 postal codes in Querétaro, Mexico, covering 2,580 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 76000 to 76998.

The places with the most codes are El Cerrito (10), La Loma (10), San Isidro (9), San José (9), Centro (8), Vista Hermosa (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Querétaro (845), San Juan del Río (326), Cadereyta de Montes (245), Corregidora (238). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 20.03 and 21.61 and longitude -100.59 and -99.05, around a centre at about 20.74, -100.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
