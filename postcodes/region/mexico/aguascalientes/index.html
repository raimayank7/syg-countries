Postal codes of Aguascalientes, Mexico

412 codes, 20000 to 20997.

The GeoNames data lists 412 postal codes in Aguascalientes, Mexico, covering 1,211 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20000 to 20997.

The places with the most codes are Santa Elena (5), El Refugio (4), Santa Fe (3), La Soledad (3), Morelos (3), Los Arcos (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Aguascalientes (571), Jesús María (182), Calvillo (145), Asientos (84). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 21.66 and 22.44 and longitude -102.86 and -101.87, around a centre at about 21.96, -102.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Aguascalientes (571); Jesús María (182); Calvillo (145); Asientos (84); San Francisco de los Romo (84); Rincón de Romos (79); El Llano (60); Pabellón de Arteaga (54); Tepezalá (35); San José de Gracia (29); Cosío (21).
