Postal codes of Baja California Sur, Mexico

341 codes, 23000 to 23997.

The GeoNames data lists 341 postal codes in Baja California Sur, Mexico, covering 838 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 23000 to 23997.

The places with the most codes are Magisterial (6), Buenos Aires (5), Benito Juárez (5), Santa Rosa (5), Las Palmas (4), La Esperanza (4). The sub-areas with most codes are La Paz (380), Los Cabos (253), Loreto (127), Comondú (106). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.88 and 27.84 and longitude -113.98 and -109.43, around a centre at about 24.50, -110.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: La Paz (380); Los Cabos (253); Loreto (127); Comondú (106); Mulegé (102).
