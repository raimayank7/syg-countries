Postal codes of Nayarit, Mexico

451 codes, 63000 to 63996.

The GeoNames data lists 451 postal codes in Nayarit, Mexico, covering 1,579 named places and 20 sub-areas. In code order they run from 63000 to 63996.

The places with the most codes are Emiliano Zapata (11), El Limón (11), Lázaro Cárdenas (9), La Ciénega (9), El Carrizo (8), Juan Escutia (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Del Nayar (504), Tepic (355), Santiago Ixcuintla (137), Compostela (125). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 20.64 and 22.87 and longitude -105.72 and -103.85, around a centre at about 21.74, -104.90 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

20 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Del Nayar (504); Tepic (355); Santiago Ixcuintla (137); Compostela (125); Ruíz (110); Bahía de Banderas (110); Acaponeta (90); Rosamorada (90); San Blas (73); Xalisco (64); Tecuala (55); La Yesca (49).
