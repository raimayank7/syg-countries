Postal codes of Michoacán de Ocampo, Mexico

1,983 codes, 58000 to 61998.

The GeoNames data lists 1,983 postal codes in Michoacán de Ocampo, Mexico, covering 6,936 named places and 113 sub-areas. In code order they run from 58000 to 61998.

The places with the most codes are Lázaro Cárdenas (49), San Miguel (31), Emiliano Zapata (29), El Mirador (28), La Huerta (27), La Loma (26). The sub-areas with most codes are Morelia (936), Hidalgo (405), Uruapan (401), Arteaga (362). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.93 and 20.38 and longitude -103.74 and -100.12, around a centre at about 19.36, -101.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
