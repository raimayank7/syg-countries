Postal codes of Oaxaca, Mexico

1,807 codes, 68000 to 71998.

The GeoNames data lists 1,807 postal codes in Oaxaca, Mexico, covering 4,579 named places and 570 sub-areas. In code order they run from 68000 to 71998.

The places with the most codes are La Soledad (33), Benito Juárez (29), Guadalupe (26), Emiliano Zapata (24), Las Flores (23), San Antonio (23). The sub-areas with most codes are Oaxaca de Juárez (299), San Juan Bautista Tuxtepec (176), Villa Sola de Vega (139), San Pedro Mixtepec -Dto. 22 - (137). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 15.67 and 18.62 and longitude -98.55 and -94.02, around a centre at about 16.92, -96.79 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
