Postal codes of Colima, Mexico

276 codes, 28000 to 28989.

The GeoNames data lists 276 postal codes in Colima, Mexico, covering 737 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 28000 to 28989.

The places with the most codes are Lázaro Cárdenas (6), Emiliano Zapata (5), Bugambilias (4), Cuauhtémoc (4), Francisco Villa (4), San Carlos (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Manzanillo (215), Colima (184), Villa de Álvarez (147), Tecomán (93). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 18.73 and 19.48 and longitude -105.04 and -103.53, around a centre at about 19.17, -103.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

10 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Manzanillo (215); Colima (184); Villa de Álvarez (147); Tecomán (93); Comala (41); Cuauhtémoc (40); Armería (30); Ixtlahuacán (25); Minatitlán (22); Coquimatlán (19).
