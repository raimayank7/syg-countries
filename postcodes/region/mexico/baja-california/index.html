Postal codes of Baja California, Mexico

513 codes, 21000 to 22997.

The GeoNames data lists 513 postal codes in Baja California, Mexico, covering 1,999 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 21000 to 22997.

The places with the most codes are Benito Juárez (9), Reforma (6), Emiliano Zapata (6), Hidalgo (6), Morelos (6), El Porvenir (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Tijuana (881), Mexicali (783), Ensenada (407), Playas de Rosarito (128). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 29.72 and 32.67 and longitude -117.26 and -114.72, around a centre at about 32.24, -116.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
