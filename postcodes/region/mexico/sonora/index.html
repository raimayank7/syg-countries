Postal codes of Sonora, Mexico

991 codes, 83000 to 85994.

The GeoNames data lists 991 postal codes in Sonora, Mexico, covering 7,137 named places and 72 sub-areas. In code order they run from 83000 to 85994.

The places with the most codes are San Francisco (30), El Ranchito (23), San Antonio (23), El Álamo (19), El Porvenir (18), San Juan (18). The sub-areas with most codes are Hermosillo (2360), Cajeme (1856), Navojoa (553), Alamos (548). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 26.32 and 32.47 and longitude -115.01 and -108.51, around a centre at about 28.78, -110.41 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
