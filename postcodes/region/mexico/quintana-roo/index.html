Postal codes of Quintana Roo, Mexico

315 codes, 77000 to 77997.

The GeoNames data lists 315 postal codes in Quintana Roo, Mexico, covering 1,086 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 77000 to 77997.

The places with the most codes are Lázaro Cárdenas (5), San Antonio (5), Miraflores (4), Javier Rojo Gómez (4), La Guadalupana (4), Benito Juárez (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Bacalar (323), Benito Juárez (318), Othón P. Blanco (193), Solidaridad (95). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.90 and 21.52 and longitude -89.12 and -86.73, around a centre at about 19.82, -87.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

10 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Bacalar (323); Benito Juárez (318); Othón P. Blanco (193); Solidaridad (95); Felipe Carrillo Puerto (73); José María Morelos (61); Cozumel (41); Lázaro Cárdenas (37); Isla Mujeres (33); Tulum (19).
