Postal codes of Guerrero, Mexico

1,388 codes, 39000 to 41997.

The GeoNames data lists 1,388 postal codes in Guerrero, Mexico, covering 3,583 named places and 81 sub-areas. In code order they run from 39000 to 41997.

The places with the most codes are Vicente Guerrero (19), Vista Hermosa (18), San José (18), Santa Cruz (17), San Antonio (17), Emiliano Zapata (16). The sub-areas with most codes are Acapulco de Juárez (503), Chilpancingo de los Bravo (332), Coahuayutla de José María Izazaga (289), Olinalá (246). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 16.33 and 18.82 and longitude -102.18 and -98.11, around a centre at about 17.61, -99.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
