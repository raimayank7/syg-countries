Postal codes of Morelos, Mexico

432 codes, 62000 to 62996.

The GeoNames data lists 432 postal codes in Morelos, Mexico, covering 1,405 named places and 33 sub-areas. In code order they run from 62000 to 62996.

The places with the most codes are Emiliano Zapata (21), Benito Juárez (15), Cuauhtémoc (11), Miguel Hidalgo (9), Loma Bonita (9), Vicente Guerrero (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Cuernavaca (309), Jiutepec (158), Cuautla (129), Yautepec (125). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 18.42 and 19.09 and longitude -99.49 and -98.67, around a centre at about 18.81, -99.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

33 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cuernavaca (309); Jiutepec (158); Cuautla (129); Yautepec (125); Emiliano Zapata (79); Temixco (71); Ayala (66); Tlaltizapán de Zapata (62); Axochiapan (59); Puente de Ixtla (54); Jojutla (49); Atlatlahucan (48).
