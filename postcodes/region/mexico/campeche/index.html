Postal codes of Campeche, Mexico

288 codes, 24000 to 24936.

The GeoNames data lists 288 postal codes in Campeche, Mexico, covering 1,091 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 24000 to 24936.

The places with the most codes are Emiliano Zapata (9), San Antonio (8), San José (7), Guadalupe (6), Santa Cruz (6), San Miguel (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Campeche (307), Carmen (279), Candelaria (197), Champotón (130). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.82 and 20.69 and longitude -92.33 and -89.23, around a centre at about 18.95, -90.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Campeche (307); Carmen (279); Candelaria (197); Champotón (130); Escárcega (96); Calakmul (94); Palizada (66); Hopelchén (50); Calkiní (40); Hecelchakán (34); Tenabo (19).
