Postal codes of Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave, Mexico

2,350 codes, 91000 to 96998.

The GeoNames data lists 2,350 postal codes in Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave, Mexico, covering 6,014 named places and 212 sub-areas. In code order they run from 91000 to 96998.

The places with the most codes are Benito Juárez (51), Emiliano Zapata (48), El Mirador (36), Lázaro Cárdenas (35), Vista Hermosa (28), Los Pinos (28). The sub-areas with most codes are Xalapa (380), Santiago Tuxtla (323), San Andrés Tuxtla (260), Las Choapas (257). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 17.16 and 22.42 and longitude -98.61 and -93.65, around a centre at about 19.16, -96.33 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
