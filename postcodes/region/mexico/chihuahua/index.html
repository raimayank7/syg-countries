Postal codes of Chihuahua, Mexico

1,077 codes, 31000 to 33997.

The GeoNames data lists 1,077 postal codes in Chihuahua, Mexico, covering 6,701 named places and 67 sub-areas. In code order they run from 31000 to 33997.

The places with the most codes are San Antonio (31), El Ranchito (29), La Joya (23), San José (22), San Rafael (19), Agua Caliente (18). The sub-areas with most codes are Guachochi (1383), Guadalupe y Calvo (1049), Juárez (1009), Chihuahua (835). Postal codes in Mexico are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 25.61 and 31.77 and longitude -108.75 and -103.81, around a centre at about 28.08, -106.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
