Postal codes of Departamento de Presidente Hayes, Paraguay

75 codes, 150201 to 150905.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in Departamento de Presidente Hayes, Paraguay, covering 327 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 150201 to 150905.

The places with the most codes are 8 de Diciembre (4), Remansito Sub-Urbano (3), Central (2), San Agustín (2), Zona Montelindo (2), Zona Cerrito (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Tte 1ro Manuel Irala Fernandez (139), Villa Hayes (73), Puerto Pinasco (49), Benjamin Aceval (29). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -25.32 and -22.19 and longitude -60.78 and -57.31, around a centre at about -23.61, -58.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
