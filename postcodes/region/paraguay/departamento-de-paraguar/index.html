Postal codes of Departamento de Paraguarí, Paraguay

145 codes, 090101 to 091804.

The GeoNames data lists 145 postal codes in Departamento de Paraguarí, Paraguay, covering 346 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 090101 to 091804.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (7), San Francisco (7), San Blas (6), Sagrado Corazón de Jesús (6), María Auxiliadora (5), San José (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Yvycui (53), Paraguari (38), Acahay (36), Carapegua (34). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.48 and -25.43 and longitude -57.46 and -56.62, around a centre at about -25.87, -57.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
