Postal codes of Departamento del Amambay, Paraguay

77 codes, 130101 to 130504.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Departamento del Amambay, Paraguay, covering 203 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 130101 to 130504.

The places with the most codes are Obrero (6), General Genes (3), San Roque (3), María Victoria (2), Mariscal Estigarribia (2), Bernardino Caballero (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Pedro Juan Caballero (121), Capitan Bado (48), Bella Vista (35), Zanja Pyta (19). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -23.77 and -22.11 and longitude -56.68 and -55.53, around a centre at about -22.82, -55.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
