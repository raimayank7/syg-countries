Postal codes of Departamento del Alto Paraná, Paraguay

297 codes, 100101 to 102204.

The GeoNames data lists 297 postal codes in Departamento del Alto Paraná, Paraguay, covering 551 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 100101 to 102204.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (16), Ciudad Nueva (12), San Antonio (12), Centro (11), San Isidro (10), San Francisco (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Itakyry (90), Ciudad Del Este (87), Minga Guazu (83), Hernandarias (55). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.22 and -24.56 and longitude -55.53 and -54.46, around a centre at about -25.44, -54.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
