Postal codes of Departamento de Caaguazú, Paraguay

255 codes, 050101 to 052204.

The GeoNames data lists 255 postal codes in Departamento de Caaguazú, Paraguay, covering 737 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 050101 to 052204.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (11), Centro (11), San Antonio (10), San Francisco (10), María Auxiliadora (10), San Blas (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Caaguazu (138), Coronel Oviedo (137), Repatriacion (87), Yhu (83). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -25.68 and -24.51 and longitude -56.84 and -54.95, around a centre at about -25.29, -56.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
