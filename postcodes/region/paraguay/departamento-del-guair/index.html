Postal codes of Departamento del Guairá, Paraguay

145 codes, 040101 to 041802.

The GeoNames data lists 145 postal codes in Departamento del Guairá, Paraguay, covering 323 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 040101 to 041802.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (11), Centro (7), San Antonio (7), Santa Rosa (6), San Pedro (6), San Roque (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Paso Yobai (65), Colonia Independencia (55), Villarrica (45), General Eugenio A. Garay (32). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.12 and -25.59 and longitude -56.71 and -55.67, around a centre at about -25.81, -56.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
