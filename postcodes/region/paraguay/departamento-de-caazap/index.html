Postal codes of Departamento de Caazapá, Paraguay

126 codes, 060101 to 061113.

The GeoNames data lists 126 postal codes in Departamento de Caazapá, Paraguay, covering 333 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 060101 to 061113.

The places with the most codes are San Antonio (9), San Roque (6), San Luis (5), San Ramón (5), María Auxiliadora (4), San Miguel (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Abai (80), Tavai (70), San Juan Nepomuceno (64), Caazapa (53). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.70 and -25.63 and longitude -56.72 and -55.38, around a centre at about -26.21, -55.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Abai (80); Tavai (70); San Juan Nepomuceno (64); Caazapa (53); Yuty (37); 3 De Mayo (32); Buena Vista (16); Fulgencio Yegros (16); General Higinio Morinigo (13); Moises Bertoni (11); Maciel (10).
