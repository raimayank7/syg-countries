Postal codes of Departamento de Concepción, Paraguay

94 codes, 010101 to 011206.

The GeoNames data lists 94 postal codes in Departamento de Concepción, Paraguay, covering 320 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 010101 to 011206.

The places with the most codes are San Roque (6), Santa Librada (6), Centro (5), Sagrada Familia (5), San Antonio (5), Fátima (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Concepcion (81), Horqueta (80), Yvy Ya´U (57), Loreto (45). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -23.48 and -22.11 and longitude -57.96 and -56.21, around a centre at about -23.11, -57.09 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Concepcion (81); Horqueta (80); Yvy Ya´U (57); Loreto (45); Belen (37); San Lazaro (23); Azotey (16); Sgto. Jose Felix Lopez (16); San Carlos (8).
