Postal codes of Departamento de Itapúa, Paraguay

284 codes, 070101 to 073006.

The GeoNames data lists 284 postal codes in Departamento de Itapúa, Paraguay, covering 714 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 070101 to 073006.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (17), San Blas (14), San Antonio (12), San Isidro (12), San Juan (10), San Francisco (10). The sub-areas with most codes are San Pedro Del Parana (85), Encarnacion (83), Tomas Romero Pereira (69), Natalio (68). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -27.43 and -26.19 and longitude -56.71 and -54.67, around a centre at about -26.89, -55.65 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
