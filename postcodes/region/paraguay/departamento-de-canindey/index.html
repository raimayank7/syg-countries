Postal codes of Departamento de Canindeyú, Paraguay

121 codes, 140101 to 141409.

The GeoNames data lists 121 postal codes in Departamento de Canindeyú, Paraguay, covering 423 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 140101 to 141409.

The places with the most codes are San Blas (8), San Miguel (7), San José (6), María Auxiliadora (6), Centro (6), San Antonio (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Curuguaty (84), Yasy Kañy (64), Villa Ygatimí (47), Ybyrarobana (43). Postal codes in Paraguay are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -24.77 and -23.70 and longitude -56.15 and -54.30, around a centre at about -24.27, -55.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
