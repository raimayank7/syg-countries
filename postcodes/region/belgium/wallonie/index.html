Postal codes of Wallonie, Belgium

35 codes, 1340 to 7910.

The GeoNames data lists 35 postal codes in Wallonie, Belgium, covering 36 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1340 to 7910.

The places with the most codes are Ottignies-Louvain-La-Neuve (1), Orp-Jauche (1), Hélécine (1), Lasne (1), Rebecq (1), Chastre (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Hainaut (13), Brabant Wallon (7), Liège (6), Namur (6). Postal codes in Belgium are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.54 and 50.76 and longitude 3.00 and 6.13, around a centre at about 50.45, 4.65 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
