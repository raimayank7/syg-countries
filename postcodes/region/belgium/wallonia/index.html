Postal codes of Wallonia, Belgium

606 codes, 1300 to 7973.

The GeoNames data lists 606 postal codes in Wallonia, Belgium, covering 1,527 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1300 to 7973.

The places with the most codes are Celles (3), Saint-Remy (2), Saint-Léger (2), Arbre (2), Liège (2), Ehein (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Province du Hainaut (457), Province de Namur (371), Province de Liège (368), Province du Luxembourg (235). Postal codes in Belgium are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.51 and 50.79 and longitude 2.88 and 6.34, around a centre at about 50.39, 4.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
