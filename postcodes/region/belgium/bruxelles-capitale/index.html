Postal codes of Bruxelles-Capitale, Belgium

22 codes, 1000 to 1210.

The places with the most codes are Bruxelles (1), Laeken (1), Schaerbeek (1), Etterbeek (1), Ixelles (1), Saint-Gilles (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bruxelles-Capitale (22). Postal codes in Belgium are written four digits.
