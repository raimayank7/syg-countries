Postal codes of Flanders, Belgium

519 codes, 1500 to 9992.

The GeoNames data lists 519 postal codes in Flanders, Belgium, covering 1,118 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1500 to 9992.

The places with the most codes are Antwerpen (7), Meldert (3), Deurne (2), Tongerlo (2), Berchem (2), Muizen (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Provincie Oost-Vlaanderen (302), Provincie West-Vlaanderen (247), Provincie Vlaams-Brabant (236), Provincie Limburg (212). Postal codes in Belgium are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.70 and 51.47 and longitude 2.59 and 5.88, around a centre at about 50.97, 4.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
