Postal codes of Łódź Voivodeship, Poland

1,839 codes, 26-300 to 99-440.

The GeoNames data lists 1,839 postal codes in Łódź Voivodeship, Poland, covering 3,947 named places and 24 sub-areas. In code order they run from 26-300 to 99-440.

The places with the most codes are Łódź (1615), Janów (24), Józefów (24), Dąbrowa (16), Stanisławów (16), Adamów (13). The sub-areas with most codes are Lodz (1615), Powiat sieradzki (427), Powiat kutnowski (399), Powiat piotrkowski (375). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.85 and 52.39 and longitude 18.10 and 20.65, around a centre at about 51.67, 19.41 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
