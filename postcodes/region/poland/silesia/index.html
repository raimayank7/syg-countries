Postal codes of Silesia, Poland

1,122 codes, 34-300 to 47-480.

The GeoNames data lists 1,122 postal codes in Silesia, Poland, covering 1,188 named places and 36 sub-areas. In code order they run from 34-300 to 47-480.

The places with the most codes are Katowice (624), Rybnik (26), Bielsko-Biała (25), Gliwice (21), Częstochowa (18), Zabrze (14). The sub-areas with most codes are Katowice (624), Powiat częstochowski (295), Powiat kłobucki (153), Powiat zawierciański (146). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.44 and 51.09 and longitude 18.05 and 19.93, around a centre at about 50.36, 18.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
