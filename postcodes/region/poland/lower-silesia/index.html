Postal codes of Lower Silesia, Poland

1,581 codes, 50-001 to 67-240.

The GeoNames data lists 1,581 postal codes in Lower Silesia, Poland, covering 2,504 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50-001 to 67-240.

The places with the most codes are Wrocław (1235), Wałbrzych (20), Wilczyce (14), Legnica (13), Bielany Wrocławskie (11), Iwiny (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Wrocław (1235), Powiat wrocławski (442), Trzebnica County (233), Kłodzko County (212). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.11 and 51.78 and longitude 14.83 and 17.74, around a centre at about 51.10, 16.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
