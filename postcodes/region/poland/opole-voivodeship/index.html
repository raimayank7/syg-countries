Postal codes of Opole Voivodeship, Poland

396 codes, 45-001 to 49-353.

The GeoNames data lists 396 postal codes in Opole Voivodeship, Poland, covering 1,141 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 45-001 to 49-353.

The places with the most codes are Opole (242), Kędzierzyn-Koźle (10), Brzeg (5), Borek (5), Jakubowice (5), Lipowa (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Opole (242), Powiat nyski (197), Powiat opolski (182), Powiat oleski (148). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.98 and 51.19 and longitude 16.95 and 18.66, around a centre at about 50.65, 17.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
