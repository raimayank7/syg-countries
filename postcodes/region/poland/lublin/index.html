Postal codes of Lublin, Poland

832 codes, 08-500 to 24-350.

The GeoNames data lists 832 postal codes in Lublin, Poland, covering 3,397 named places and 24 sub-areas. In code order they run from 08-500 to 24-350.

The places with the most codes are Lublin (592), Marysin (11), Zalesie (10), Dąbrowa (10), Brzeziny (9), Józefin (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Lublin (592), Chełm County (383), Powiat bialski (363), Powiat lubelski (363). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.27 and 52.27 and longitude 21.66 and 24.11, around a centre at about 51.20, 22.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
