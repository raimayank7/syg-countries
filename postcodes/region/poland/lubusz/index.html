Postal codes of Lubusz, Poland

456 codes, 65-001 to 69-220.

The GeoNames data lists 456 postal codes in Lubusz, Poland, covering 1,300 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 65-001 to 69-220.

The places with the most codes are Zielona Góra (360), Buszów (3), Wysoka (3), Brzeźno (3), Gola (3), Gronów (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Zielona Góra (360), Powiat żarski (169), Powiat krośnieński (153), Powiat strzelecko-drezdenecki (152). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.39 and 53.11 and longitude 14.56 and 16.39, around a centre at about 52.12, 15.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
