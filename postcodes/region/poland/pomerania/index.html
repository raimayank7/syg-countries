Postal codes of Pomerania, Poland

1,472 codes, 76-200 to 89-652.

The GeoNames data lists 1,472 postal codes in Pomerania, Poland, covering 2,762 named places and 20 sub-areas. In code order they run from 76-200 to 89-652.

The places with the most codes are Gdańsk (599), Gdynia (508), Sopot (152), Dąbrówka (10), Ostrowite (8), Dąbrowa (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Gdańsk (599), Gdynia (508), Powiat bytowski (368), Powiat kartuski (356). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.51 and 54.83 and longitude 16.73 and 19.56, around a centre at about 54.22, 18.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
