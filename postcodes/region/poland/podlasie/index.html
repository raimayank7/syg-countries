Postal codes of Podlasie, Poland

745 codes, 15-001 to 19-230.

The GeoNames data lists 745 postal codes in Podlasie, Poland, covering 3,341 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 15-001 to 19-230.

The places with the most codes are Białystok (612), Zalesie (9), Olszanka (8), Ogrodniki (8), Wólka (8), Krasne (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Białystok (612), Powiat białostocki (552), Powiat sokólski (422), Powiat wysokomazowiecki (389). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.29 and 54.40 and longitude 21.62 and 23.93, around a centre at about 53.25, 22.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
