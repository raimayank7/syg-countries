Postal codes of Warmia-Masuria, Poland

779 codes, 10-003 to 82-340.

The GeoNames data lists 779 postal codes in Warmia-Masuria, Poland, covering 3,334 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10-003 to 82-340.

The places with the most codes are Olsztyn (639), Zalesie (10), Elbląg (9), Borki (8), Janowo (8), Dąbrowa (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Olsztyn (639), Powiat olsztyński (432), Powiat ostródzki (330), Powiat kętrzyński (311). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 53.15 and 54.43 and longitude 19.15 and 22.79, around a centre at about 53.86, 20.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
