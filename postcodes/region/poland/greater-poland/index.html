Postal codes of Greater Poland, Poland

1,559 codes, 60-001 to 89-350.

The GeoNames data lists 1,559 postal codes in Greater Poland, Poland, covering 4,638 named places and 35 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60-001 to 89-350.

The places with the most codes are Poznań (1210), Dąbrowa (22), Nowa Wieś (17), Zalesie (13), Józefowo (11), Bielawy (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Poznań (1210), Konin County (640), Powiat poznański (393), Powiat kaliski (381). Postal codes in Poland are written two digits, then a hyphen, then three digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.11 and 53.64 and longitude 15.79 and 19.10, around a centre at about 52.27, 17.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
