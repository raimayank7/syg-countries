Postal codes of San Luis, Argentina

60 codes, 4703 to 6721.

The GeoNames data lists 60 postal codes in San Luis, Argentina, covering 1,250 named places. In code order they run from 4703 to 6721.

The places with the most codes are ALTO (1), LA AURORA (UNION - DPTO. GOBERNADOR V. DUPUY) (1), LA TRANCA (1), DESAGUADERO (1), SANTO DOMINGO (SAN LUIS, DPTO.LA CAPITAL) (1), SANTA TERESA (SAN LUIS, DPTO. LA CAPITAL) (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -35.72 and -28.63 and longitude -67.28 and -59.64, around a centre at about -33.14, -65.90 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
