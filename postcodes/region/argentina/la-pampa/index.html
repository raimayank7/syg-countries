Postal codes of La Pampa, Argentina

62 codes, 3203 to 8336.

The GeoNames data lists 62 postal codes in La Pampa, Argentina, covering 712 named places. In code order they run from 3203 to 8336.

The places with the most codes are LA CHIRLANDIA (1), AGUA DE TORRE (1), REALICO (1), CHANILAO (1), EMBAJADOR MARTINI (1), LA ELINA (EMBAJADOR MARTINI, DPTO. REALICO) (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -39.00 and -31.57 and longitude -68.08 and -58.52, around a centre at about -36.70, -64.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
