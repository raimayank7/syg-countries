Postal codes of Entre Rios, Argentina

110 codes, 1647 to 3826.

The GeoNames data lists 110 postal codes in Entre Rios, Argentina, covering 1,076 named places. In code order they run from 1647 to 3826.

The places with the most codes are ARROYO BRAZO DE LA TINTA (1), ARROYO SANTOS CHICO (1), ARROYO ÑANCAY (1), ARROYO CARBON (1), ARROYO DE LA ROSA (1), ARROYO PERDIDO (MOSQUITO) (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.36 and -27.27 and longitude -61.86 and -55.53, around a centre at about -31.98, -59.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
