Postal codes of Salta, Argentina

56 codes, 3636 to 4651.

The GeoNames data lists 56 postal codes in Salta, Argentina, covering 930 named places. In code order they run from 3636 to 4651.

The places with the most codes are POZO CERCADO (EL CHORRO (F), DPTO. RIVADAVIA (S)) (1), LAS SALADAS (1), EL BRETE (1), CANDELARIA (1), TALA (1), BRETE (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -26.44 and -22.07 and longitude -68.23 and -61.93, around a centre at about -24.63, -64.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
