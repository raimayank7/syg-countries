Postal codes of Neuquen, Argentina

29 codes, 3353 to 8407.

The GeoNames data lists 29 postal codes in Neuquen, Argentina, covering 464 named places. In code order they run from 3353 to 8407.

The places with the most codes are QUEMPU LEUFU (1), BARRIO LIMAY (1), LAS PERLAS (1), BARRIO MIKALCIUS (1), BARRIO SAN LORENZO (1), BARRIO NUESTRO HOGAR (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -41.13 and -27.88 and longitude -71.68 and -55.35, around a centre at about -38.76, -70.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
