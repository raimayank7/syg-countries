Postal codes of Tierra Del Fuego, Argentina

5 codes, 9409 to 9421.

The GeoNames data lists 5 postal codes in Tierra Del Fuego, Argentina, covering 48 named places. In code order they run from 9409 to 9421.

The places with the most codes are ISLAS MALVINAS (1), LAGUNA ESCONDIDA (1), ISLA DE AÑO NUEVO (1), HOSTERIA PETREL (1), BAHIA BUEN SUCESO (1), ALMIRANTE BROWN (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -53.87 and -51.04 and longitude -71.03 and -67.70, around a centre at about -52.18, -69.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
