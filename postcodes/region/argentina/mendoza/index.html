Postal codes of Mendoza, Argentina

78 codes, 5435 to 6279.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in Mendoza, Argentina, covering 566 named places. In code order they run from 5435 to 6279.

The places with the most codes are PUESTO ISLA CHAÑAR (1), PUESTO LOS CHAÑARES (1), PUESTO EL QUEMADO (1), CERRO DE LA GLORIA (1), SECCION SEXTA (1), BARRIO GENERAL SAN MARTIN (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -36.78 and -32.02 and longitude -70.07 and -66.52, around a centre at about -33.66, -68.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
