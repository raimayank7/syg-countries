Postal codes of La Rioja, Argentina

45 codes, 5263 to 5741.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in La Rioja, Argentina, covering 370 named places. In code order they run from 5263 to 5741.

The places with the most codes are ESPERANZA DE LOS CERRILLOS (1), LA LIBERTAD (EL MEDANO, DPTO. CAPITAL) (1), EL ROSARIO (1), EL MEDANO (1), KILOMETRO 619 (1), COMANDANTE LEAL (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -33.15 and -28.32 and longitude -68.56 and -65.60, around a centre at about -30.05, -66.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
