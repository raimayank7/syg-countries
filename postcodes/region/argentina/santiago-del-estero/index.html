Postal codes of Santiago Del Estero, Argentina

109 codes, 2340 to 5714.

The GeoNames data lists 109 postal codes in Santiago Del Estero, Argentina, covering 1,947 named places. In code order they run from 2340 to 5714.

The places with the most codes are NUEVA CERES (1), COLONIA GERALDINA (1), CLAVEL BLANCO (1), COLONIA ALPINA (1), LA GERALDINA (1), SELVA (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -32.24 and -22.73 and longitude -66.41 and -57.13, around a centre at about -28.01, -63.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
