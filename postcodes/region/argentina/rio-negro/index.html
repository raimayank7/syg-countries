Postal codes of Rio Negro, Argentina

47 codes, 8134 to 9138.

The GeoNames data lists 47 postal codes in Rio Negro, Argentina, covering 454 named places. In code order they run from 8134 to 9138.

The places with the most codes are KILOMETRO 823 (1), SAN LEON (1), LA MARIA INES (1), MONTE REDONDO (1), SAN PEDRO (RIO COLORADO, DPTO. PICHI MAHUIDA) (1), CERRO DE LOS VIEJOS (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -43.30 and -37.41 and longitude -71.82 and -62.52, around a centre at about -40.32, -67.76 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
