Postal codes of Formosa, Argentina

24 codes, 3511 to 7401.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Formosa, Argentina, covering 370 named places. In code order they run from 3511 to 7401.

The places with the most codes are KILOMETRO 254 (NAVEGACION RIO BERMEJO) (1), VILLA ESCOLAR (1), KILOMETRO 100 RIO BERMEJO (1), GENERAL LUCIO VICTORIO MANSILLA (1), CABO ADRIANO AYALA (1), KILOMETRO 139 (NAVEGACION RIO BERMEJO) (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -37.07 and -23.03 and longitude -62.33 and -57.58, around a centre at about -25.08, -59.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
