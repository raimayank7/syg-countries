Postal codes of San Juan, Argentina

36 codes, 5400 to 5546.

The GeoNames data lists 36 postal codes in San Juan, Argentina, covering 396 named places. In code order they run from 5400 to 5546.

The places with the most codes are VILLA MARINI (1), PRESBITERO FRANCISCO PEREZ HERNANDEZ (1), BARRIO VILLA PALERMO (1), VILLA CAROLINA (1), VILLA HUASIHUL (1), VILLA DEL CARRIL (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -32.79 and -29.90 and longitude -69.55 and -67.32, around a centre at about -31.21, -68.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
