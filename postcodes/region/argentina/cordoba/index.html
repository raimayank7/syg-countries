Postal codes of Cordoba, Argentina

290 codes, 2189 to 8522.

The GeoNames data lists 290 postal codes in Cordoba, Argentina, covering 2,395 named places. In code order they run from 2189 to 8522.

The places with the most codes are COLONIA LOS VASCOS (1), CRUZ ALTA (1), LOS PORONGOS (1), AROMITO (1), COLONIA MILESSI (1), DOS ROSAS (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -41.23 and -29.02 and longitude -66.15 and -58.35, around a centre at about -31.55, -64.05 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
