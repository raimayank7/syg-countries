Postal codes of Chubut, Argentina

46 codes, 8211 to 9431.

The GeoNames data lists 46 postal codes in Chubut, Argentina, covering 442 named places. In code order they run from 8211 to 9431.

The places with the most codes are CUSHAMEN (1), EL RINCON (1), ÑORQUINCO SUD (1), CERRO RADAL (1), LAS GOLONDRINAS (1), RIO PUELO (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -51.39 and -37.67 and longitude -71.72 and -63.63, around a centre at about -43.89, -68.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
