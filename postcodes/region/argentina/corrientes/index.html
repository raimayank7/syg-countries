Postal codes of Corrientes, Argentina

71 codes, 3185 to 3641.

The GeoNames data lists 71 postal codes in Corrientes, Argentina, covering 1,210 named places. In code order they run from 3185 to 3641.

The places with the most codes are COLONIA BASUALDO (1), RINCON DE TUNAS (1), COLONIA PAIRIRI (1), BASUALDO (1), COSTA ARROYO GARAY (1), PARAJE PORTILLO (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -32.35 and -24.88 and longitude -59.73 and -54.61, around a centre at about -28.70, -58.09 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
