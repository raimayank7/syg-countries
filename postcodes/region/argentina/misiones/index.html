Postal codes of Misiones, Argentina

42 codes, 3300 to 3601.

The GeoNames data lists 42 postal codes in Misiones, Argentina, covering 391 named places. In code order they run from 3300 to 3601.

The places with the most codes are TIRO FEDERAL (1), VILLA JUSTO JOSE DE URQUIZA (1), ARROYO ZAIMAN (1), LA ROTONDA (1), BARRIO ROCAMORA (1), BARTOLOME MITRE (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -28.17 and -25.60 and longitude -58.57 and -53.65, around a centre at about -27.27, -55.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
