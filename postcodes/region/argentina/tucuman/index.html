Postal codes of Tucuman, Argentina

66 codes, 4000 to 5157.

The GeoNames data lists 66 postal codes in Tucuman, Argentina, covering 1,095 named places. In code order they run from 4000 to 5157.

The places with the most codes are RETIRO (2), POCITOS (1), BARRIO FLORESTA (1), MERCADO NORTE (APEADERO FCGB) (1), BARRIO OESTE (1), VILLA NUEVE DE JULIO (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -31.36 and -25.48 and longitude -66.10 and -64.39, around a centre at about -27.03, -65.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
