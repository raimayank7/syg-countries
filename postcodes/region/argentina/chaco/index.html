Postal codes of Chaco, Argentina

43 codes, 3500 to 3734.

The GeoNames data lists 43 postal codes in Chaco, Argentina, covering 448 named places. In code order they run from 3500 to 3734.

The places with the most codes are VILLA ELDORADO (1), TIGRE (1), BARRIO TOBA (1), VILLA BARRIO CENTENARIO (1), BARRIO VILLA CENTENARIO (1), BARRIO VILLA ALVEAR (1). Postal codes in Argentina are written one letter, then four digits, then three letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -27.98 and -24.63 and longitude -63.28 and -58.50, around a centre at about -27.04, -60.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
