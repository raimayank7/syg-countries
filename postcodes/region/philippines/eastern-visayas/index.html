Postal codes of Eastern Visayas, Philippines

143 codes, 6400 to 6822.

The GeoNames data lists 143 postal codes in Eastern Visayas, Philippines, covering 142 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6400 to 6822.

The places with the most codes are San Isidro (2), Borongan (1), Balangkayan (1), Maydolong (1), Llorente (1), Hernani (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Leyte (43), Province of Samar (26), Province of Northern Samar (24), Province of Eastern Samar (23). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.91 and 12.68 and longitude 124.15 and 125.72, around a centre at about 11.44, 124.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
