Postal codes of Central Visayas, Philippines

133 codes, 6000 to 6347.

The GeoNames data lists 133 postal codes in Central Visayas, Philippines, covering 131 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6000 to 6347.

The places with the most codes are Carmen (2), Pilar (2), Tagbilaran City (1), Baclayon (1), Alburquerque (1), Loay (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Cebu (54), Bohol (48), Province of Negros Oriental (25), Province of Siquijor (6). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.06 and 11.30 and longitude 122.64 and 124.58, around a centre at about 9.94, 123.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
