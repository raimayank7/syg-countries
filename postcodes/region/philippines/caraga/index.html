Postal codes of Caraga, Philippines

74 codes, 8300 to 8611.

The GeoNames data lists 74 postal codes in Caraga, Philippines, covering 71 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8300 to 8611.

The places with the most codes are Carmen (2), San Francisco (2), Loreto (2), Butuan City (1), Buenavista (1), Nasipit (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Surigao del Norte (21), Province of Surigao del Sur (20), Province of Agusan del Sur (14), Province of Agusan del Norte (12). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.99 and 10.36 and longitude 125.29 and 126.41, around a centre at about 9.16, 125.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
