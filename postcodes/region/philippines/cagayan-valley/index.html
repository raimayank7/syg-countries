Postal codes of Cagayan Valley, Philippines

93 codes, 3300 to 3905.

The GeoNames data lists 93 postal codes in Cagayan Valley, Philippines, covering 92 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3300 to 3905.

The places with the most codes are Quezon (2), Basco (1), Mahatao (1), Ivana (1), Uyugan (1), Sabtang (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Isabela (37), Province of Cagayan (29), Province of Nueva Vizcaya (15), Province of Batanes (6). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 15.80 and 20.79 and longitude 120.89 and 122.43, around a centre at about 17.41, 121.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
