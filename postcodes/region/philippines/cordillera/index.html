Postal codes of Cordillera, Philippines

79 codes, 2600 to 3814.

The GeoNames data lists 79 postal codes in Cordillera, Philippines, covering 79 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2600 to 3814.

The places with the most codes are Bangued (1), Dolores (1), Lagangilang (1), Tayum (1), Peñarrubia (1), Bucay (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Abra (27), Province of Benguet (16), Province of Ifugao (11), Mountain Province (10). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 16.32 and 18.33 and longitude 120.52 and 121.47, around a centre at about 17.23, 120.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
