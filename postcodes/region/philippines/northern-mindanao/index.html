Postal codes of Northern Mindanao, Philippines

95 codes, 7200 to 9222.

The GeoNames data lists 95 postal codes in Northern Mindanao, Philippines, covering 93 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7200 to 9222.

The places with the most codes are Magsaysay (2), Tagoloan (2), Malaybalay (1), Sumilao (1), Impasugong (1), Manolo Fortich (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Misamis Oriental (26), Province of Bukidnon (24), Province of Lanao del Norte (23), Province of Misamis Occidental (17). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.48 and 9.25 and longitude 123.56 and 125.33, around a centre at about 8.33, 124.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
