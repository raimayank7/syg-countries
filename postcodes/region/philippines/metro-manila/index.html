Postal codes of Metro Manila, Philippines

235 codes, 1000 to 1812.

The GeoNames data lists 235 postal codes in Metro Manila, Philippines, covering 350 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1000 to 1812.

The places with the most codes are San Isidro (3), San Jose (3), Sta. Ana (2), Baesa (2), San Roque (2), Sangandaan (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Eastern Manila District (184), Southern Manila District (108), Northern Manila District (49), Capital District (19). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 14.40 and 14.75 and longitude 120.93 and 121.12, around a centre at about 14.61, 121.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
