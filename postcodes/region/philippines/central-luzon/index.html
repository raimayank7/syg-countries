Postal codes of Central Luzon, Philippines

143 codes, 2000 to 3207.

The GeoNames data lists 143 postal codes in Central Luzon, Philippines, covering 140 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2000 to 3207.

The places with the most codes are San Miguel (2), San Antonio (2), San Luis (2), Balanga (1), Pilar (1), Orion (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Nueva Ecija (34), Province of Pampanga (27), Province of Bulacan (26), Province of Tarlac (19). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 14.43 and 16.40 and longitude 119.90 and 122.21, around a centre at about 15.22, 120.76 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
