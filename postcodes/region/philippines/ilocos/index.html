Postal codes of Ilocos, Philippines

126 codes, 2400 to 2922.

The GeoNames data lists 126 postal codes in Ilocos, Philippines, covering 121 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2400 to 2922.

The places with the most codes are Burgos (4), San Nicolas (2), Santa Maria (2), Laoag City (1), Paoay (1), Currimao (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Pangasinan (48), Province of Ilocos Sur (34), Province of Ilocos Norte (23), Province of La Union (21). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 15.78 and 18.56 and longitude 119.80 and 120.90, around a centre at about 16.85, 120.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
