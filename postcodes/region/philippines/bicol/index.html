Postal codes of Bicol, Philippines

117 codes, 4400 to 5421.

The GeoNames data lists 117 postal codes in Bicol, Philippines, covering 115 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4400 to 5421.

The places with the most codes are San Fernando (2), Bato (2), Legazpi City (1), Daraga (Locsin) (1), Camalig (1), Guinobatan (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Province of Camarines Sur (37), Province of Masbate (22), Province of Albay (18), Province of Sorsogon (16). Postal codes in Philippines are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 11.74 and 14.33 and longitude 122.49 and 124.39, around a centre at about 13.30, 123.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
