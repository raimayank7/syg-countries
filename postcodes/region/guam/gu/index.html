Postal codes of Gu, Guam

21 codes, 96910 to 96932.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Gu, Guam, covering 18 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 96910 to 96932.

The places with the most codes are Hagatna (2), Tamuning (2), Barrigada (2), Dededo (1), Yona (1), Santa Rita (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Guam (21). Postal codes in Guam are written the fixed text "969", then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 13.26 and 13.57 and longitude 144.66 and 144.88, around a centre at about 13.43, 144.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
