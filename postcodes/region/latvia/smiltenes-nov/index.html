Postal codes of Smiltenes nov., Latvia

14 codes, LV-4131 to LV-4729.

The GeoNames data lists 14 postal codes in Smiltenes nov., Latvia, covering 26 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4131 to LV-4729.

The places with the most codes are Apes (1), Gaujiena (1), Trapene (1), Vidaga (1), Vireši (1), Drusti (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Smiltenes pag. (4), Grundzāles pag. (3), Launkalnes pag. (3), Virešu pag. (2). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.23 and 57.63 and longitude 25.61 and 26.89, around a centre at about 57.43, 26.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
