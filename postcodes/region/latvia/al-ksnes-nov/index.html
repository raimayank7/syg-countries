Postal codes of Alūksnes nov., Latvia

16 codes, LV-4301 to LV-4359.

The GeoNames data lists 16 postal codes in Alūksnes nov., Latvia, covering 21 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4301 to LV-4359.

The places with the most codes are Aizupītes (1), Alsviķi (1), Strautiņi (1), Pullans (1), Anna (1), Jaunzemi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Alsviķu pag. (4), Jaunalūksnes pag. (2), Mālupes pag. (2), Ziemera pag. (2). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.28 and 57.59 and longitude 26.68 and 27.47, around a centre at about 57.41, 27.06 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
