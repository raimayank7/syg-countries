Postal codes of Balvu nov., Latvia

24 codes, LV-4501 to LV-4595.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Balvu nov., Latvia, covering 480 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4501 to LV-4595.

The places with the most codes are Sola (3), Robežnieki (3), Plešova (3), Mežarija (2), Aizpurve (2), Līdumnieki (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Šķilbēnu pag. (59), Susāju pag. (42), Kubulu pag. (38), Bērzpils pag. (36). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.59 and 57.41 and longitude 25.74 and 27.85, around a centre at about 57.05, 27.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
