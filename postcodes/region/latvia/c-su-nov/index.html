Postal codes of Cēsu nov., Latvia

26 codes, LV-4101 to LV-4154.

The GeoNames data lists 26 postal codes in Cēsu nov., Latvia, covering 56 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4101 to LV-4154.

The places with the most codes are Cēsis (2), Rāmuļi (1), Krīvi (1), Rīdzene (1), Veismaņi (1), Mežmaļi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Drabešu pag. (7), Raiskuma pag. (6), Vaives pag. (5), Līgatnes pag. (4). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.96 and 57.45 and longitude 24.96 and 26.03, around a centre at about 57.24, 25.33 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

21 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Drabešu pag. (7); Raiskuma pag. (6); Vaives pag. (5); Līgatnes pag. (4); Zaubes pag. (4); Priekuļu pag. (4); Stalbes pag. (3); Straupes pag. (3); Amatas pag. (2); Skujenes pag. (2); Jaunpiebalgas pag. (2); Liepas pag. (2).
