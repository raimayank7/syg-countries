Postal codes of Preiļu nov., Latvia

21 codes, LV-5301 to LV-5687.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Preiļu nov., Latvia, covering 696 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-5301 to LV-5687.

The places with the most codes are Jaudzemi (4), Babri (4), Salenieki (3), Vilcāni (3), Sondori (3), Briškas (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Rušonas pag. (103), Aglonas pag. (85), Riebiņu pag. (61), Preiļu pag. (53). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.01 and 56.54 and longitude 26.27 and 27.37, around a centre at about 56.25, 26.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
