Postal codes of Augšdaugavas nov., Latvia

28 codes, LV-5413 to LV-5481.

The GeoNames data lists 28 postal codes in Augšdaugavas nov., Latvia, covering 535 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-5413 to LV-5481.

The places with the most codes are Baltmuiža (3), Putāni (2), Mazā Baranovska (2), Miglāni (2), Osinovka (2), Zemgale (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Višķu pag. (77), Naujenes pag. (57), Kalupes pag. (50), Vaboles pag. (48). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.71 and 56.17 and longitude 26.08 and 26.97, around a centre at about 55.98, 26.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
