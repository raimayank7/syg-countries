Postal codes of Rēzeknes nov., Latvia

34 codes, LV-4601 to LV-4652.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Rēzeknes nov., Latvia, covering 1,016 named places and 28 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4601 to LV-4652.

The places with the most codes are Tuči (4), Jaunsaimnieki (4), Kaļvi (3), Novaja (3), Kozlova (3), Žogotas (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Dricānu pag. (80), Silmalas pag. (78), Kaunatas pag. (76), Nautrēnu pag. (70). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.18 and 56.77 and longitude 26.79 and 27.67, around a centre at about 56.51, 27.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
