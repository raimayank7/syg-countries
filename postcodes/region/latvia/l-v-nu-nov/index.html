Postal codes of Līvānu nov., Latvia

6 codes, LV-5312 to LV-5334.

The GeoNames data lists 6 postal codes in Līvānu nov., Latvia, covering 187 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-5312 to LV-5334.

The places with the most codes are Jaudzemi (2), Darankas (1), Iztekas (1), Upenieki (1), Kusiņi (1), Jersika (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Rožupes pag. (56), Rudzātu pag. (39), Turku pag. (39), Sutru pag. (36). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.20 and 56.49 and longitude 26.12 and 26.57, around a centre at about 56.37, 26.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Rožupes pag. (56); Rudzātu pag. (39); Turku pag. (39); Sutru pag. (36); Jersikas pag. (18).
