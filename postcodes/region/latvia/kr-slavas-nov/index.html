Postal codes of Krāslavas nov., Latvia

21 codes, LV-5601 to LV-5698.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Krāslavas nov., Latvia, covering 690 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-5601 to LV-5698.

The places with the most codes are Rimšāni (4), Noviki (4), Sloboda (4), Andžāni (4), Kalviši (3), Kazimirova (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kalniešu pag. (65), Andrupenes pag. (57), Kombuļu pag. (55), Skaistas pag. (54). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.79 and 56.24 and longitude 26.94 and 27.96, around a centre at about 56.01, 27.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
