Postal codes of Olaines nov., Latvia

4 codes, LV-2113 to LV-2167.

The GeoNames data lists 4 postal codes in Olaines nov., Latvia, covering 20 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-2113 to LV-2167.

The places with the most codes are Stūnīši (2), Stīpnieki (1), Jāņupe (1), Vaivadi (1), Ielejas (1), Dāvi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Olaines pag. (20). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.74 and 56.86 and longitude 23.83 and 24.14, around a centre at about 56.79, 24.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
