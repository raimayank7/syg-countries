Postal codes of Ogres nov., Latvia

20 codes, LV-5001 to LV-5071.

The GeoNames data lists 20 postal codes in Ogres nov., Latvia, covering 40 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-5001 to LV-5071.

The places with the most codes are Tīnūži (2), Rembate (2), Krape (1), Ķeipene (1), Laubere (1), Madliena (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Tīnūžu pag. (9), Suntažu pag. (5), Tomes pag. (5), Rembates pag. (5). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.63 and 56.91 and longitude 24.57 and 25.48, around a centre at about 56.81, 24.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
