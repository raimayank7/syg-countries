Postal codes of Valmieras nov., Latvia

29 codes, LV-4206 to LV-4731.

The GeoNames data lists 29 postal codes in Valmieras nov., Latvia, covering 47 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-4206 to LV-4731.

The places with the most codes are Ķeiži (2), Jērcēni (1), Plāņi (1), Jaunklidzis (1), Bērzaine (1), Dauguļi (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kauguru pag. (4), Jērcēnu pag. (3), Kocēnu pag. (3), Jeru pag. (3). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.47 and 58.03 and longitude 25.06 and 25.98, around a centre at about 57.70, 25.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
