Postal codes of Talsu nov., Latvia

22 codes, LV-3201 to LV-3298.

The GeoNames data lists 22 postal codes in Talsu nov., Latvia, covering 140 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from LV-3201 to LV-3298.

The places with the most codes are Jaunzemes (2), Zvirgzdi (2), Stūrīši (2), Kaltene (2), Ambrakas (1), Jaunciems (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Dundagas pag. (38), Ģibuļu pag. (15), Lībagu pag. (14), Laidzes pag. (9). Postal codes in Latvia are written the fixed text "LV", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.03 and 57.74 and longitude 22.27 and 23.12, around a centre at about 57.36, 22.58 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Dundagas pag. (38); Ģibuļu pag. (15); Lībagu pag. (14); Laidzes pag. (9); Laucienes pag. (8); Abavas pag. (7); Kolkas pag. (7); Valdgales pag. (6); Ārlavas pag. (5); Vandzenes pag. (5); Virbu pag. (5); Īves pag. (4).
