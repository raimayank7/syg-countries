Postal codes of North Maluku, Indonesia

50 codes, 97711 to 97863.

The GeoNames data lists 50 postal codes in North Maluku, Indonesia, covering 1,089 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 97711 to 97863.

The places with the most codes are Galala (4), Gamsungi (4), Bobo (3), Tuguis (3), Loleo (3), Bajo (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Halmahera Selatan (247), Kabupaten Halmahera Utara (196), Kabupaten Halmahera Barat (169), Kabupaten Kepulauan Sula (136). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.96 and 2.58 and longitude 125.84 and 129.57, around a centre at about 0.65, 127.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Halmahera Selatan (247); Kabupaten Halmahera Utara (196); Kabupaten Halmahera Barat (169); Kabupaten Kepulauan Sula (136); East Halmahera Regency (102); Kabupaten Pulau Morotai (88); Kota Ternate (77); Kota Tidore Kepulauan (72); Kabupaten Halmahera Tengah (61).
