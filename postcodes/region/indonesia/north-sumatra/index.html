Postal codes of North Sumatra, Indonesia

539 codes, 20111 to 22998.

The GeoNames data lists 539 postal codes in North Sumatra, Indonesia, covering 5,234 named places and 32 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20111 to 22998.

The places with the most codes are Pardomuan (14), Simaninggir (9), Suka Maju (9), Sidomulyo (8), Bonan Dolok (6), Huta Raja (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Nias Selatan (458), Kabupaten Deli Serdang (394), Kabupaten Padang Lawas Utara (388), Kabupaten Mandailing Natal (366). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.55 and 4.28 and longitude 97.18 and 100.38, around a centre at about 2.26, 98.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
