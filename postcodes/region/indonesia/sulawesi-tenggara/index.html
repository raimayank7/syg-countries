Postal codes of Sulawesi Tenggara, Indonesia

146 codes, 93111 to 93958.

The GeoNames data lists 146 postal codes in Sulawesi Tenggara, Indonesia, covering 1,987 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 93111 to 93958.

The places with the most codes are Mataiwoi (11), Waworaha (5), Bahari (4), Mekar Jaya (4), Puundoho (4), Silea (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Konawe (372), Kabupaten Konawe Selatan (356), Kabupaten Buton (242), Kabupaten Muna (236). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -6.01 and -2.86 and longitude 120.88 and 124.06, around a centre at about -4.40, 122.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Konawe (372); Kabupaten Konawe Selatan (356); Kabupaten Buton (242); Kabupaten Muna (236); Kabupaten Kolaka (213); Kabupaten Konawe Utara (146); Kabupaten Bombana (139); Kabupaten Kolaka Utara (134); Wakatobi Regency (102); Kabupaten Buton Utara (90); Kota Kendari (64).
