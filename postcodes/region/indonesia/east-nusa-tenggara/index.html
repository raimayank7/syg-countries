Postal codes of East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia

245 codes, 85111 to 87284.

The GeoNames data lists 245 postal codes in East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia, covering 3,104 named places and 20 sub-areas. In code order they run from 85111 to 87284.

The places with the most codes are Merdeka (4), Oinlasi (3), Kampung Baru (2), Pada Eweta (2), Prai Bakul (2), Tema Tana (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Timor Tengah Selatan (278), Kabupaten Ende (271), Kabupaten Flores Timur (249), Kota Kupang (228). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -10.92 and -8.12 and longitude 119.01 and 125.17, around a centre at about -9.13, 122.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
