Postal codes of Provinsi Bali, Indonesia

104 codes, 80111 to 82262.

The GeoNames data lists 104 postal codes in Provinsi Bali, Indonesia, covering 685 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80111 to 82262.

The places with the most codes are Selat (5), Buahan (3), Tista (3), Bengkel (2), Beraban (2), Buruan (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Buleleng (148), Kabupaten Tabanan (131), Kabupaten Karang Asem (78), Kabupaten Bangli (72). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.83 and -8.08 and longitude 114.43 and 115.67, around a centre at about -8.41, 115.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
