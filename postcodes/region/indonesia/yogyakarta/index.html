Postal codes of Yogyakarta, Indonesia

137 codes, 55111 to 55893.

The GeoNames data lists 137 postal codes in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, covering 417 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 55111 to 55893.

The places with the most codes are Triharjo (3), Sidomulyo (3), Sendangsari (3), Sidorejo (3), Bendungan (2), Ngestiharjo (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Gunungkidul (144), Kabupaten Kulon Progo (88), Kabupaten Sleman (86), Kabupaten Bantul (75). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.15 and -7.67 and longitude 110.04 and 110.77, around a centre at about -7.87, 110.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Gunungkidul (144); Kabupaten Kulon Progo (88); Kabupaten Sleman (86); Kabupaten Bantul (75); Kota Yogyakarta (45).
