Postal codes of Jawa Barat, Indonesia

909 codes, 16110 to 46475.

The GeoNames data lists 909 postal codes in Jawa Barat, Indonesia, covering 3,811 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 16110 to 46475.

The places with the most codes are Mekarsari (42), Mekarjaya (38), Sukamulya (35), Sukamaju (32), Neglasari (28), Sindangsari (25). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Bogor (496), Kabupaten Cirebon (446), Kabupaten Garut (442), Kota Bandung (431). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -7.81 and -5.96 and longitude 106.44 and 108.82, around a centre at about -6.86, 107.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
