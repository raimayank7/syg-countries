Postal codes of Banten, Indonesia

246 codes, 15111 to 42455.

The GeoNames data lists 246 postal codes in Banten, Indonesia, covering 1,327 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 15111 to 42455.

The places with the most codes are Mekarsari (13), Sukamanah (7), Banjarsari (6), Sukajaya (5), Sukasari (5), Mekarjaya (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Serang (390), Kabupaten Tangerang (377), Kabupaten Lebak (345), Kabupaten Pandeglang (337). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -6.97 and -5.89 and longitude 105.62 and 106.77, around a centre at about -6.35, 106.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
