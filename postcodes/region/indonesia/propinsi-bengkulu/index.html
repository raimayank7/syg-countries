Postal codes of Propinsi Bengkulu, Indonesia

122 codes, 38113 to 39374.

The GeoNames data lists 122 postal codes in Propinsi Bengkulu, Indonesia, covering 1,289 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 38113 to 39374.

The places with the most codes are Tanjung Agung (9), Pasar Baru (7), Tanjung Aur (6), Pagar Dewa (5), Kota Agung (5), Sukarami (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Bengkulu Utara (220), Kabupaten Seluma (199), Kabupaten Kaur (195), Kabupaten Bengkulu Selatan (156). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -5.35 and -0.77 and longitude 101.58 and 103.53, around a centre at about -3.62, 102.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
