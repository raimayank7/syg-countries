Postal codes of Sumatera Selatan, Indonesia

279 codes, 30111 to 32387.

The GeoNames data lists 279 postal codes in Sumatera Selatan, Indonesia, covering 2,471 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30111 to 32387.

The places with the most codes are Tanjung Baru (14), Sukarami (14), Sukaraja (14), Tanjung Agung (11), Mekar Jaya (11), Tanjung Beringin (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Lahat Regency (376), Kabupaten Banyu Asin (304), Kabupaten Muara Enim (298), Kabupaten Ogan Komering Ulu Timur (296). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.89 and -2.06 and longitude 102.69 and 105.42, around a centre at about -3.76, 104.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
