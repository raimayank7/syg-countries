Postal codes of Provinsi Jawa Tengah, Indonesia

1,037 codes, 50111 to 59583.

The GeoNames data lists 1,037 postal codes in Provinsi Jawa Tengah, Indonesia, covering 5,848 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50111 to 59583.

The places with the most codes are Karanganyar (32), Karangsari (24), Rejosari (24), Sidomulyo (23), Purwosari (20), Sidorejo (20). The sub-areas with most codes are Purworejo (494), Kabupaten Kebumen (460), Kabupaten Semarang (409), Kabupaten Pati (406). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.16 and -5.87 and longitude 108.57 and 111.85, around a centre at about -7.28, 110.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
