Postal codes of Central Kalimantan, Indonesia

145 codes, 73111 to 74874.

The GeoNames data lists 145 postal codes in Central Kalimantan, Indonesia, covering 1,504 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 73111 to 74874.

The places with the most codes are Sumber Makmur (4), Mendawai (3), Sei / Sungai Bakau (3), Batuah (3), Tanjung Harapan (3), Dandang (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Kapuas (230), Kabupaten Kotawaringin Timur (177), Kabupaten Katingan (160), Kabupaten Gunung Mas (127). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -3.20 and 0.20 and longitude 111.07 and 115.66, around a centre at about -1.86, 113.76 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
