Postal codes of Aceh, Indonesia

242 codes, 23111 to 24791.

The GeoNames data lists 242 postal codes in Aceh, Indonesia, covering 5,482 named places and 23 sub-areas. In code order they run from 23111 to 24791.

The places with the most codes are Gampong Baro (14), Blang Dalam (13), Meunasah Blang (10), Keutapang (10), Suka Makmur (9), Meunasah Teungoh (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Aceh Utara (850), Kabupaten Pidie (723), Kabupaten Bireuen (608), Kabupaten Aceh Besar (602). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.25 and 5.90 and longitude 95.05 and 98.21, around a centre at about 4.66, 96.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
