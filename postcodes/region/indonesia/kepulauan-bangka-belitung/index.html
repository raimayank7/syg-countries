Postal codes of Kepulauan Bangka Belitung, Indonesia

76 codes, 33111 to 33791.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Kepulauan Bangka Belitung, Indonesia, covering 364 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 33111 to 33791.

The places with the most codes are Pasir Putih (3), Riding Panjang (2), Petaling (2), Rukam (2), Air Duren (2), Nibung (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Bangka (71), Kabupaten Bangka Barat (64), Kabupaten Bangka Tengah (63), Kabupaten Bangka Selatan (53). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -3.18 and -1.66 and longitude 105.16 and 108.27, around a centre at about -2.33, 106.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Bangka (71); Kabupaten Bangka Barat (64); Kabupaten Bangka Tengah (63); Kabupaten Bangka Selatan (53); Kabupaten Belitung (48); Kota Pangkal Pinang (42); Kabupaten Belitung Timur (39).
