Postal codes of Provinsi Kalimantan Barat, Indonesia

195 codes, 78111 to 79682.

The GeoNames data lists 195 postal codes in Provinsi Kalimantan Barat, Indonesia, covering 1,892 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 78111 to 79682.

The places with the most codes are Suka Maju (6), Beringin (4), Mekar Jaya (4), Tanjung (4), Suka Damai (4), Tengah (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Sintang (287), Kabupaten Kapuas Hulu (282), Kabupaten Ketapang (249), Kabupaten Sambas (183). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -2.35 and 1.43 and longitude 108.92 and 112.98, around a centre at about 0.04, 110.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
