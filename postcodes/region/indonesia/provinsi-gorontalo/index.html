Postal codes of Provinsi Gorontalo, Indonesia

59 codes, 96111 to 96585.

The GeoNames data lists 59 postal codes in Provinsi Gorontalo, Indonesia, covering 650 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 96111 to 96585.

The places with the most codes are Tenilo (4), Padengo (4), Iloheluma (4), Permata (3), Dulohupa (3), Mekar Jaya (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kota Gorontalo (250), Kabupaten Bone Bolango (164), Kabupaten Gorontalo Utara (123), Kabupaten Pohuwato (104). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.32 and 1.00 and longitude 121.35 and 123.46, around a centre at about 0.61, 122.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kota Gorontalo (250); Kabupaten Bone Bolango (164); Kabupaten Gorontalo Utara (123); Kabupaten Pohuwato (104); Kabupaten Boalemo (84).
