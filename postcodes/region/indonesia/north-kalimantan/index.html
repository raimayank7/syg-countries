Postal codes of North Kalimantan, Indonesia

32 codes, 77111 to 77573.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in North Kalimantan, Indonesia, covering 471 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 77111 to 77573.

The places with the most codes are Long Nawang (2), Labuk (2), Tanjung Harapan (2), Karang Anyar (2), Long Kebinu (1), Long Simau (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Nunukan (240), Kabupaten Malinau (109), Kabupaten Tana Tidung (25), Kabupaten Tarakan (20). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.02 and 4.32 and longitude 115.26 and 117.77, around a centre at about 3.53, 116.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

4 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Nunukan (240); Kabupaten Malinau (109); Kabupaten Tana Tidung (25); Kabupaten Tarakan (20).
