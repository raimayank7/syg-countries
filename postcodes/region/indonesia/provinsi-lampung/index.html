Postal codes of Provinsi Lampung, Indonesia

239 codes, 34111 to 35686.

The GeoNames data lists 239 postal codes in Provinsi Lampung, Indonesia, covering 2,070 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 34111 to 35686.

The places with the most codes are Sidodadi (13), Sumber Agung (11), Sukarame (9), Sumber Rejo (9), Sukaraja (9), Sidomulyo (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Lampung Tengah (304), Kabupaten Tanggamus (300), Kabupaten Lampung Timur (264), Kabupaten Lampung Selatan (259). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -5.88 and -4.02 and longitude 103.69 and 105.82, around a centre at about -5.08, 105.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
