Postal codes of Jawa Timur, Indonesia

1,086 codes, 60111 to 69493.

The GeoNames data lists 1,086 postal codes in Jawa Timur, Indonesia, covering 6,029 named places and 31 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60111 to 69493.

The places with the most codes are Sukorejo (38), Sidomulyo (33), Sidorejo (29), Wonorejo (28), Karangrejo (22), Sumberejo (22). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Lamongan (474), Kota Malang (447), Kabupaten Bojonegoro (428), Kabupaten Pasuruan (399). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -8.59 and -5.73 and longitude 110.91 and 115.90, around a centre at about -7.59, 112.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
