Postal codes of Sulawesi Utara, Indonesia

197 codes, 95111 to 95999.

The GeoNames data lists 197 postal codes in Sulawesi Utara, Indonesia, covering 1,705 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 95111 to 95999.

The places with the most codes are Paslaten (5), Talikuran (5), Sendangan (5), Sawangan (4), Kolongan (4), Mala (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Minahasa (269), Kabupaten Kepulauan Sangihe (167), Kabupaten Minahasa Selatan (163), Kabupaten Kepulauan Talaud (153). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.31 and 5.55 and longitude 123.13 and 127.13, around a centre at about 1.66, 124.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kabupaten Minahasa (269); Kabupaten Kepulauan Sangihe (167); Kabupaten Minahasa Selatan (163); Kabupaten Kepulauan Talaud (153); Kabupaten Minahasa Tenggara (144); Kabupaten Minahasa Utara (129); Kabupaten Bolaang Mongondow Utara (107); Kota Manado (87); Kabupaten Bolaang Mongondow Timur (81); Kota Bitung (69); Kabupaten Bolaang Mongondow Selatan (59); Kota Tomohon (44).
