Postal codes of Provinsi Sulawesi Selatan, Indonesia

361 codes, 90111 to 92985.

The GeoNames data lists 361 postal codes in Provinsi Sulawesi Selatan, Indonesia, covering 2,610 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 90111 to 92985.

The places with the most codes are Benteng (12), Tanete (11), Polewali (10), Mario (8), Bontomanai (7), Samaenre (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kabupaten Bone (370), Kabupaten Luwu (227), Kabupaten Wajo (176), Kabupaten Luwu Utara (173). Postal codes in Indonesia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -7.42 and -2.10 and longitude 119.26 and 121.75, around a centre at about -4.21, 119.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
