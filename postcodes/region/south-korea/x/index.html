Postal codes of 세종특별자치시, South Korea

142 codes, 30000 to 30154.

The GeoNames data lists 142 postal codes in 세종특별자치시, South Korea, covering 11 named places. In code order they run from 30000 to 30154.

The places with the most codes are 세종특별자치시 (42), 조치원읍 (27), 연기면 (16), 금남면 (14), 연서면 (11), 연동면 (10). Postal codes in South Korea are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.42 and 36.72 and longitude 127.15 and 127.39, around a centre at about 36.57, 127.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
