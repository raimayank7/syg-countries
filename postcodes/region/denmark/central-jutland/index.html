Postal codes of Central Jutland, Denmark

144 codes, 6880 to 9632.

The GeoNames data lists 144 postal codes in Central Jutland, Denmark, covering 144 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6880 to 9632.

The places with the most codes are Horsens (1), Hovedgård (1), Brædstrup (1), Gedved (1), Østbirk (1), Endelave (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Aarhus Kommune (22), Hedensted Kommune (12), Herning Kommune (10), Randers Kommune (10). Postal codes in Denmark are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.71 and 56.82 and longitude 8.14 and 11.54, around a centre at about 56.22, 9.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
