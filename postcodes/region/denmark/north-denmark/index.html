Postal codes of North Denmark, Denmark

75 codes, 7700 to 9990.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in North Denmark, Denmark, covering 75 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7700 to 9990.

The places with the most codes are Nykøbing M (1), Erslev (1), Karby (1), Redsted M (1), Vils (1), Øster Assels (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Ålborg Kommune (18), Thisted Kommune (8), Frederikshavn Kommune (8), Hjørring Kommune (7). Postal codes in Denmark are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 56.65 and 57.72 and longitude 8.28 and 11.01, around a centre at about 57.06, 9.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
