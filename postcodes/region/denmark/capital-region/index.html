Postal codes of Capital Region, Denmark

577 codes, 1050 to 4050.

The GeoNames data lists 577 postal codes in Capital Region, Denmark, covering 93 named places and 29 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1050 to 4050.

The places with the most codes are København K (232), København V (150), Frederiksberg C (105), København Ø (1), Nordhavn (1), København N (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Copenhagen (391), Frederiksberg Kommune (106), Bornholm Kommune (9), Helsingør Kommune (8). Postal codes in Denmark are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 55.04 and 56.12 and longitude 11.88 and 15.13, around a centre at about 55.69, 12.58 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
