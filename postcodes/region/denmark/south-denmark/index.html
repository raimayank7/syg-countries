Postal codes of South Denmark, Denmark

164 codes, 5000 to 7323.

The GeoNames data lists 164 postal codes in South Denmark, Denmark, covering 164 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 5000 to 7323.

The places with the most codes are Harndrup (1), Brenderup Fyn (1), Asperup (1), Middelfart (1), Nørre Aaby (1), Gelsted (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Varde Kommune (14), Faaborg-Midtfyn Kommune (12), Odense Kommune (11), Kolding Kommune (11). Postal codes in Denmark are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.75 and 55.86 and longitude 8.13 and 10.88, around a centre at about 55.35, 9.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
