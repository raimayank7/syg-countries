Postal codes of Zealand, Denmark

129 codes, 2670 to 4990.

The GeoNames data lists 129 postal codes in Zealand, Denmark, covering 129 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2670 to 4990.

The places with the most codes are Greve (1), Karlslunde (1), Tune (1), Borup (1), Køge (1), Lille Skensved (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Lolland Kommune (17), Guldborgsund Kommune (14), Holbæk Kommune (12), Vordingborg Kommune (11). Postal codes in Denmark are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.60 and 55.97 and longitude 11.07 and 12.47, around a centre at about 55.31, 11.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
