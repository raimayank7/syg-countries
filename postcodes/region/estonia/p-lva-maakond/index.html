Postal codes of Põlva maakond, Estonia

204 codes, 62502 to 64618.

The GeoNames data lists 204 postal codes in Põlva maakond, Estonia, covering 189 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 62502 to 64618.

The places with the most codes are Põlva (8), Räpina (4), Põlgaste (2), Karilatsi (2), Prangli (2), Taevaskoja (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Põlva vald (83), Räpina vald (70), Kanepi vald (52). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.91 and 58.39 and longitude 26.59 and 27.54, around a centre at about 58.07, 27.09 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
