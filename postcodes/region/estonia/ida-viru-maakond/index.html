Postal codes of Ida-Viru maakond, Estonia

242 codes, 29021 to 43331.

The GeoNames data lists 242 postal codes in Ida-Viru maakond, Estonia, covering 191 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 29021 to 43331.

The places with the most codes are Järve (15), Jõhvi (14), Ahtme (12), Narva-Jõesuu (5), Kiviõli (4), Sompa (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Alutaguse vald (74), Lüganuse vald (59), Kohtla-Järve linn (31), Toila vald (30). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.99 and 59.46 and longitude 26.77 and 28.13, around a centre at about 59.30, 27.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
