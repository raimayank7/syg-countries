Postal codes of Võru maakond, Estonia

660 codes, 64001 to 66712.

The GeoNames data lists 660 postal codes in Võru maakond, Estonia, covering 638 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 64001 to 66712.

The places with the most codes are Antsla (4), Savioja (2), Holdi (2), Jaanimäe (2), Saagri (2), Tsolli (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Rõuge vald (275), Võru vald (187), Setomaa vald (157), Antsla vald (42). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.55 and 58.09 and longitude 26.43 and 27.80, around a centre at about 57.76, 27.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
