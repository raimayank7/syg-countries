Postal codes of Järva maakond, Estonia

223 codes, 72001 to 79221.

The GeoNames data lists 223 postal codes in Järva maakond, Estonia, covering 200 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 72001 to 79221.

The places with the most codes are Paide (13), Türi (8), Ülejõe (3), Puiatu (2), Päinurme (2), Kahala (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Järva vald (110), Türi vald (67), Paide linn (54). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.64 and 59.23 and longitude 25.11 and 26.14, around a centre at about 58.92, 25.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
