Postal codes of Lääne maakond, Estonia

188 codes, 76218 to 91314.

The GeoNames data lists 188 postal codes in Lääne maakond, Estonia, covering 165 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 76218 to 91314.

The places with the most codes are Haapsalu (12), Saunja (4), Uuemõisa (2), Paralepa (2), Liivaküla (2), Vilkla (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Lääne-Nigula vald (105), Haapsalu linn (74), Vormsi vald (14). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.73 and 59.24 and longitude 23.14 and 24.14, around a centre at about 58.92, 23.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
