Postal codes of Viljandi maakond, Estonia

293 codes, 69001 to 71513.

The GeoNames data lists 293 postal codes in Viljandi maakond, Estonia, covering 266 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 69001 to 71513.

The places with the most codes are Abja-Paluoja (5), Võhma (5), Karksi-Nuia (4), Mõisaküla (3), Suure-Jaani (3), Mäeküla (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Viljandi vald (139), Põhja-Sakala vald (84), Mulgi vald (75). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.00 and 58.67 and longitude 25.03 and 26.09, around a centre at about 58.34, 25.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
