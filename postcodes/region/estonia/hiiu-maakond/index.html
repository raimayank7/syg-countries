Postal codes of Hiiu maakond, Estonia

194 codes, 92001 to 92424.

The GeoNames data lists 194 postal codes in Hiiu maakond, Estonia, covering 185 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 92001 to 92424.

The places with the most codes are Kärdla (5), Selja (3), Ligema (2), Lelu (2), Hiiessaare (2), Tareste (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Hiiumaa vald (195). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.69 and 59.07 and longitude 22.07 and 23.07, around a centre at about 58.88, 22.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
