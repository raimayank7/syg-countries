Postal codes of Jõgeva maakond, Estonia

226 codes, 42001 to 49604.

The GeoNames data lists 226 postal codes in Jõgeva maakond, Estonia, covering 216 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 42001 to 49604.

The places with the most codes are Jõgeva (7), Põltsamaa (4), Mustvee (2), Kaave (1), Alavere (1), Ellakvere (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Jõgeva vald (101), Põltsamaa vald (65), Mustvee vald (60). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.55 and 59.05 and longitude 25.81 and 27.08, around a centre at about 58.76, 26.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
