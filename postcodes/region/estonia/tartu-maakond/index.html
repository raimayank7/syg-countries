Postal codes of Tartu maakond, Estonia

469 codes, 49103 to 68709.

The GeoNames data lists 469 postal codes in Tartu maakond, Estonia, covering 397 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 49103 to 68709.

The places with the most codes are Tartu (58), Elva (8), Kõduküla (2), Kureküla (2), Saare (2), Aakaru (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Elva vald (92), Peipsiääre vald (91), Tartu vald (78), Tartu linn (70). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.07 and 58.75 and longitude 26.09 and 27.53, around a centre at about 58.39, 26.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
