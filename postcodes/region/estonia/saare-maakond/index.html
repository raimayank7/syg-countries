Postal codes of Saare maakond, Estonia

508 codes, 93001 to 94766.

The GeoNames data lists 508 postal codes in Saare maakond, Estonia, covering 479 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 93001 to 94766.

The places with the most codes are Kuressaare (13), Laheküla (3), Liiva (2), Linnuse (2), Nurme (2), Vanamõisa (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Saaremaa vald (456), Muhu vald (52), Ruhnu vald (1). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.80 and 58.67 and longitude 21.85 and 23.43, around a centre at about 58.40, 22.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
