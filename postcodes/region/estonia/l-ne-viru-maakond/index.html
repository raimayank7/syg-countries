Postal codes of Lääne-Viru maakond, Estonia

428 codes, 43303 to 73615.

The GeoNames data lists 428 postal codes in Lääne-Viru maakond, Estonia, covering 401 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 43303 to 73615.

The places with the most codes are Kunda (6), Tapa (4), Tamsalu (4), Tokolopi (3), Sootaguse (3), Palmse (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Vinni vald (78), Haljala vald (75), Väike-Maarja vald (70), Tapa vald (66). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.90 and 59.62 and longitude 25.65 and 26.91, around a centre at about 59.30, 26.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
