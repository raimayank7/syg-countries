Postal codes of Pärnu maakond, Estonia

458 codes, 80010 to 90305.

The GeoNames data lists 458 postal codes in Pärnu maakond, Estonia, covering 403 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80010 to 90305.

The places with the most codes are Pärnu (40), Kilingi-Nõmme (5), Lihula (4), Sindi (3), Metsaküla (2), Selja (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Lääneranna vald (157), Pärnu linn (93), Põhja-Pärnumaa vald (89), Tori vald (47). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.88 and 58.75 and longitude 23.52 and 25.27, around a centre at about 58.47, 24.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
