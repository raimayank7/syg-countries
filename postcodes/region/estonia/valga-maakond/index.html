Postal codes of Valga maakond, Estonia

163 codes, 67003 to 68716.

The GeoNames data lists 163 postal codes in Valga maakond, Estonia, covering 146 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 67003 to 68716.

The places with the most codes are Otepää (8), Valga (8), Tõrva (5), Kibena (2), Kolli (2), Nõuni (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Otepää vald (64), Valga vald (60), Tõrva vald (44). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 57.59 and 58.16 and longitude 25.65 and 26.57, around a centre at about 57.94, 26.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
