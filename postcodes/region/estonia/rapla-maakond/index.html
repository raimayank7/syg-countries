Postal codes of Rapla maakond, Estonia

327 codes, 78001 to 79852.

The GeoNames data lists 327 postal codes in Rapla maakond, Estonia, covering 266 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 78001 to 79852.

The places with the most codes are Aespa (26), Kohila (7), Vilivere (5), Rapla (5), Märjamaa (4), Pirgu (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Märjamaa vald (119), Rapla vald (102), Kohila vald (61), Kehtna vald (51). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.69 and 59.22 and longitude 24.10 and 25.26, around a centre at about 58.97, 24.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
