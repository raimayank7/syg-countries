Postal codes of Harju maakond, Estonia

710 codes, 10001 to 76925.

The GeoNames data lists 710 postal codes in Harju maakond, Estonia, covering 429 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 10001 to 76925.

The places with the most codes are Kesklinna (85), Lasnamäe (45), Nõmme (40), Mustamäe (33), Põhja-Tallinna (28), Kristiine (25). The sub-areas with most codes are Tallinn (291), Kuusalu vald (70), Kose vald (65), Lääne-Harju vald (61). Postal codes in Estonia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.01 and 59.65 and longitude 23.76 and 25.87, around a centre at about 59.37, 24.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
