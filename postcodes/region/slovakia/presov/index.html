Postal codes of Presov, Slovakia

354 codes, 053 02 to 094 35.

The GeoNames data lists 354 postal codes in Presov, Slovakia, covering 685 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 053 02 to 094 35.

The places with the most codes are Prešov 1 (25), Vranov nad Topľou 1 (25), Bardejov 1 (19), Humenné 1 (16), Levoča (12), Snina 1 (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Prešov (94), Okres Bardejov (88), Okres Vranov nad Topľou (69), Okres Svidník (68). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.78 and 49.44 and longitude 20.03 and 22.51, around a centre at about 49.11, 21.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
