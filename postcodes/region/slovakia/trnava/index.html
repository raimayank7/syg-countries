Postal codes of Trnava, Slovakia

301 codes, 905 01 to 932 15.

The GeoNames data lists 301 postal codes in Trnava, Slovakia, covering 274 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 905 01 to 932 15.

The places with the most codes are Trnava 1 (50), Piešťany 1 (22), Senica 1 (16), Galanta 1 (13), Dunajská Streda 1 (12), Hlohovec 1 (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Dunajská Streda (73), Okres Trnava (49), Okres Galanta (37), Okres Senica (31). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.80 and 48.84 and longitude 17.00 and 17.94, around a centre at about 48.37, 17.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
