Postal codes of Kosice, Slovakia

509 codes, 040 01 to 980 46.

The GeoNames data lists 509 postal codes in Kosice, Slovakia, covering 515 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 040 01 to 980 46.

The places with the most codes are Košice 1 (204), Trebišov 1 (60), Kráľovský Chlmec 1 (22), Spišská Nová Ves 1 (11), Michalovce 1 (9), Rožňava 1 (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Kosice-okolie (139), Okres Trebišov (86), Okres Michalovce (83), Okres Rožňava (62). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.35 and 48.99 and longitude 20.27 and 22.36, around a centre at about 48.68, 21.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
