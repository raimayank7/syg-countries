Postal codes of Zilina, Slovakia

371 codes, 010 01 to 039 46.

The GeoNames data lists 371 postal codes in Zilina, Slovakia, covering 352 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 010 01 to 039 46.

The places with the most codes are Žilina 1 (118), Martin 1 (15), Liptovský Mikuláš 1 (13), Dolný Kubín 1 (10), Žilina (7), Ružomberok 1 (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Žilina (62), Okres Liptovský Mikuláš (59), Okres Martin (44), Okres Ružomberok (31). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.76 and 49.53 and longitude 18.47 and 19.98, around a centre at about 49.16, 19.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
