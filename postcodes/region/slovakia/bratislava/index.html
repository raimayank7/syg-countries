Postal codes of Bratislava, Slovakia

977 codes, 810 00 to 925 26.

The GeoNames data lists 977 postal codes in Bratislava, Slovakia, covering 138 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 810 00 to 925 26.

The places with the most codes are Bratislava 1 (431), Bratislava 3 (56), Bratislava 25 (37), Bratislava 37 (37), Bratislava 32 (35), Bratislava 45 (33). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Senec (29), Okres Malacky (26), Okres Pezinok (18), Okres Bratislava III (12). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.03 and 48.54 and longitude 16.85 and 17.48, around a centre at about 48.17, 17.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
