Postal codes of Nitra, Slovakia

381 codes, 925 71 to 966 54.

The GeoNames data lists 381 postal codes in Nitra, Slovakia, covering 391 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 925 71 to 966 54.

The places with the most codes are Nitra 1 (41), Topoľčany 1 (20), Levice 1 (18), Komárno 1 (13), Zlaté Moravce 1 (13), Komárno 5 (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Levice (97), Okres Nitra (72), Okres Nové Zámky (65), Okres Topoľčany (56). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.74 and 48.67 and longitude 17.77 and 19.05, around a centre at about 48.20, 18.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
