Postal codes of Trencin, Slovakia

242 codes, 017 01 to 972 71.

The GeoNames data lists 242 postal codes in Trencin, Slovakia, covering 320 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 017 01 to 972 71.

The places with the most codes are Prievidza 1 (21), Trenčín 1 (16), Považská Bystrica 1 (11), Nové Mesto nad Váhom 1 (9), Partizánske 1 (6), Považská Bystrica (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Okres Nové Mesto nad Váhom (55), Okres Prievidza (53), Okres Bánovce nad Bebravou (44), Okres Trenčín (40). Postal codes in Slovakia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.55 and 49.25 and longitude 17.37 and 18.76, around a centre at about 48.84, 18.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
