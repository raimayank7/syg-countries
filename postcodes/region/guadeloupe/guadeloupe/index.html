Postal codes of Guadeloupe, Guadeloupe

105 codes, 97001 CEDEX to 97199 CEDEX.

The GeoNames data lists 105 postal codes in Guadeloupe, Guadeloupe, covering 33 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 97001 CEDEX to 97199 CEDEX.
