Postal codes of Mersch, Luxembourg

248 codes, L-6155 to L-9184.

The GeoNames data lists 248 postal codes in Mersch, Luxembourg, covering 51 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-6155 to L-9184.

The places with the most codes are Mersch (52), Bissen (35), Colmar-Berg (24), Lintgen (20), Larochette (17), Lorentzweiler (16). The sub-areas with most codes are Mersch (83), Lorentzweiler (41), Bissen (36), Berg (26). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.69 and 49.82 and longitude 6.01 and 6.27, around a centre at about 49.75, 6.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
