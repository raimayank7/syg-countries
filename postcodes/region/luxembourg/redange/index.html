Postal codes of Redange, Luxembourg

87 codes, L-7470 to L-9190.

The GeoNames data lists 87 postal codes in Redange, Luxembourg, covering 61 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-7470 to L-9190.

The places with the most codes are Redange/Attert (7), Useldange (4), Vichten (4), Beckerich (3), Hostert (3), Rambrouch (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Rambrouch (26), Redange-sur-Attert (13), Beckerich (12), Wahl (8). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.72 and 49.87 and longitude 5.74 and 6.02, around a centre at about 49.79, 5.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
