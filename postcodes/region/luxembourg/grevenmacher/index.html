Postal codes of Grevenmacher, Luxembourg

256 codes, L-5401 to L-7639.

The GeoNames data lists 256 postal codes in Grevenmacher, Luxembourg, covering 46 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-5401 to L-7639.

The places with the most codes are Grevenmacher (73), Junglinster (37), Wasserbillig (33), Mertert (22), Gonderange (11), Roodt-sur-Syre (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Grevenmacher (73), Junglinster (64), Mertert (55), Betzdorf (21). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.60 and 49.74 and longitude 6.18 and 6.51, around a centre at about 49.69, 6.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
