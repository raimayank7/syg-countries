Postal codes of Luxembourg, Luxembourg

1,318 codes, L-1111 to L-8268.

The GeoNames data lists 1,318 postal codes in Luxembourg, Luxembourg, covering 36 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-1111 to L-8268.

The places with the most codes are Luxembourg (788), Strassen (68), Bertrange (52), Howald (42), Sandweiler (34), Hesperange (32). The sub-areas with most codes are Ville de Luxembourg (788), Hesperange (154), Strassen (68), Niederanven (67). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.53 and 49.68 and longitude 6.04 and 6.28, around a centre at about 49.61, 6.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
