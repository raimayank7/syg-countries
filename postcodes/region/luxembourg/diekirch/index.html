Postal codes of Diekirch, Luxembourg

250 codes, L-6311 to L-9456.

The GeoNames data lists 250 postal codes in Diekirch, Luxembourg, covering 42 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-6311 to L-9456.

The places with the most codes are Diekirch (85), Ettelbruck (64), Schieren (24), Warken (9), Medernach (9), Gilsdorf (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Diekirch (85), Ettelbruck (74), Schieren (26), Bourscheid (18). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.79 and 49.95 and longitude 6.00 and 6.29, around a centre at about 49.86, 6.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
