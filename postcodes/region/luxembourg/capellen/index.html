Postal codes of Capellen, Luxembourg

375 codes, L-4795 to L-8480.

The GeoNames data lists 375 postal codes in Capellen, Luxembourg, covering 42 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-4795 to L-8480.

The places with the most codes are Mamer (64), Bridel (42), Bascharage (39), Steinfort (31), Olm (24), Capellen (24). The sub-areas with most codes are Mamer (92), Käerjeng (76), Kopstal (55), Kehlen (49). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.56 and 49.71 and longitude 5.87 and 6.09, around a centre at about 49.63, 5.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
