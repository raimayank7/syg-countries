Postal codes of Clervaux, Luxembourg

113 codes, L-9183 to L-9999.

The GeoNames data lists 113 postal codes in Clervaux, Luxembourg, covering 83 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-9183 to L-9999.

The places with the most codes are Clervaux (12), Troisvierges (11), Hosingen (8), Weiswampach (5), Eselborn (3), Hoscheid (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Clervaux (40), Wincrange (28), Parc Hosingen (22), Troisvierges (18). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.93 and 50.16 and longitude 5.84 and 6.14, around a centre at about 50.07, 6.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

5 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Clervaux (40); Wincrange (28); Parc Hosingen (22); Troisvierges (18); Weiswampach (15).
