Postal codes of Vianden, Luxembourg

46 codes, L-9350 to L-9466.

The GeoNames data lists 46 postal codes in Vianden, Luxembourg, covering 19 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from L-9350 to L-9466.

The places with the most codes are Vianden (23), Bastendorf (3), Brandenbourg (3), Bettel (2), Fouhren (2), Landscheid (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Vianden (23), Tandel (18), Putscheid (8). Postal codes in Luxembourg are written the fixed text "L", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.88 and 49.97 and longitude 6.10 and 6.23, around a centre at about 49.93, 6.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

3 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Vianden (23); Tandel (18); Putscheid (8).
