Postal codes of Ararat, Armenia

82 codes, 0601 to 0823.

The GeoNames data lists 82 postal codes in Ararat, Armenia, covering 77 named places. In code order they run from 0601 to 0823.

The places with the most codes are Ararat (3), Artashat (3), Masis (2), Vedi (1), Aygavan (1), Avshar (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.74 and 40.16 and longitude 44.34 and 45.04, around a centre at about 39.98, 44.57 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
