Postal codes of Tavush, Armenia

53 codes, 3901 to 4216.

The GeoNames data lists 53 postal codes in Tavush, Armenia, covering 48 named places. In code order they run from 3901 to 4216.

The places with the most codes are Dilijan (5), Ijevan (2), Teghut (1), Gosh (1), Haghartsin (1), Azatamut (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.72 and 41.28 and longitude 44.81 and 45.58, around a centre at about 40.97, 45.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
