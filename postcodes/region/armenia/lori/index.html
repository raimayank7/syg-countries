Postal codes of Lori, Armenia

101 codes, 1701 to 2117.

The GeoNames data lists 101 postal codes in Lori, Armenia, covering 75 named places. In code order they run from 1701 to 2117.

The places with the most codes are Vanadzor (17), Alaverdi (4), Spitak (4), Stepanavan (3), Akhtala (2), Vanzadzor (2). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.72 and 41.20 and longitude 44.04 and 44.84, around a centre at about 40.94, 44.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
