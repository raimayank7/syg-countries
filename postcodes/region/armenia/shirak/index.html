Postal codes of Shirak, Armenia

83 codes, 2601 to 3126.

The GeoNames data lists 83 postal codes in Shirak, Armenia, covering 65 named places. In code order they run from 2601 to 3126.

The places with the most codes are Gyumri (13), Artik (5), Akhuryan (2), Musaelyan (2), Azatan (1), Akhurik (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.46 and 41.12 and longitude 43.64 and 44.07, around a centre at about 40.75, 43.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
