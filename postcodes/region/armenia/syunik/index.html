Postal codes of Syunik, Armenia

61 codes, 3201 to 3519.

The GeoNames data lists 61 postal codes in Syunik, Armenia, covering 49 named places. In code order they run from 3201 to 3519.

The places with the most codes are Kapan (7), Goris (4), Sisian (3), Meghri (2), Khnatsakh (1), Khndzoresk (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.86 and 39.68 and longitude 45.76 and 46.56, around a centre at about 39.37, 46.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
