Postal codes of Gegharkunik, Armenia

69 codes, 1201 to 1624.

The GeoNames data lists 69 postal codes in Gegharkunik, Armenia, covering 61 named places. In code order they run from 1201 to 1624.

The places with the most codes are Gavar (4), Martuni (3), Sevan (3), Vardenis (2), Gandzak (1), Gegharkunik (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.08 and 40.70 and longitude 44.79 and 45.82, around a centre at about 40.36, 45.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
