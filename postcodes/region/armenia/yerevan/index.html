Postal codes of Yerevan, Armenia

94 codes, 0001 to 0109.

The GeoNames data lists 94 postal codes in Yerevan, Armenia, covering 1 named places. In code order they run from 0001 to 0109.

The places with the most codes are Yerevan (94). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.09 and 40.22 and longitude 44.43 and 44.59, around a centre at about 40.18, 44.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
