Postal codes of Aragatsotn, Armenia

47 codes, 0201 to 0514.

The GeoNames data lists 47 postal codes in Aragatsotn, Armenia, covering 45 named places. In code order they run from 0201 to 0514.

The places with the most codes are Ashtarak (3), Agarak (1), Aghdzk (Dzorap) (1), Avan (1), Aragatsotn (1), Aruch (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.25 and 40.72 and longitude 43.66 and 44.39, around a centre at about 40.43, 44.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
