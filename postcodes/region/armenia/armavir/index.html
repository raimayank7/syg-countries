Postal codes of Armavir, Armenia

93 codes, 0901 to 1149.

The GeoNames data lists 93 postal codes in Armavir, Armenia, covering 81 named places. In code order they run from 0901 to 1149.

The places with the most codes are Armavir (5), Etchmiadzin (5), Metsamor (2), Aygeshat (2), Araks (2), Baghramyan (2). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.03 and 40.25 and longitude 43.66 and 44.42, around a centre at about 40.14, 44.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
