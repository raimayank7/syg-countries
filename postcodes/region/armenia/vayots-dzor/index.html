Postal codes of Vayots Dzor, Armenia

29 codes, 3601 to 3818.

The GeoNames data lists 29 postal codes in Vayots Dzor, Armenia, covering 27 named places. In code order they run from 3601 to 3818.

The places with the most codes are Jermuk (2), Vayk (2), Yeghegnadzor (1), Agarakadzor (1), Aghavnadzor (1), Areni (1). Postal codes in Armenia are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.52 and 39.89 and longitude 45.11 and 45.67, around a centre at about 39.74, 45.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
