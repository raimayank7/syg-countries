Postal codes of Moravskoslezský kraj, Czechia

217 codes, 700 30 to 795 01.

The GeoNames data lists 217 postal codes in Moravskoslezský kraj, Czechia, covering 668 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 700 30 to 795 01.

The places with the most codes are Staré Město (4), Janovice (4), Lhotka (4), Podlesí (3), Nová Ves (3), Pocheň (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Bruntál (170), Opava (151), Nový Jičín (120), Frýdek-Místek (110). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.44 and 50.31 and longitude 17.16 and 18.83, around a centre at about 49.84, 18.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
