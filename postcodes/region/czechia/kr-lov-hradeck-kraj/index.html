Postal codes of Královéhradecký kraj, Czechia

235 codes, 500 02 to 552 25.

The GeoNames data lists 235 postal codes in Královéhradecký kraj, Czechia, covering 1,039 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 500 02 to 552 25.

The places with the most codes are Petrovice (4), Pražské Předměstí (3), Moravské Předměstí (3), Komárov (3), Olešnice (3), Nové Město (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Jičín (306), Náchod (222), Hradec Králové (210), Rychnov nad Kněžnou (185). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.05 and 50.74 and longitude 15.13 and 16.47, around a centre at about 50.37, 15.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
