Postal codes of Zlínský kraj, Czechia

134 codes, 686 01 to 769 01.

The GeoNames data lists 134 postal codes in Zlínský kraj, Czechia, covering 429 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 686 01 to 769 01.

The places with the most codes are Lhota (4), Lhotka (3), Příluky (2), Karlovice (2), Veselá (2), Březová (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kroměříž (133), Zlín (130), Uherské Hradiště (100), Vsetín (87). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.88 and 49.53 and longitude 17.13 and 18.30, around a centre at about 49.22, 17.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
