Postal codes of Plzeňský kraj, Czechia

137 codes, 301 00 to 385 01.

The GeoNames data lists 137 postal codes in Plzeňský kraj, Czechia, covering 1,413 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 301 00 to 385 01.

The places with the most codes are Nová Ves (7), Nový Dvůr (6), Hradiště (6), Bezděkov (6), Březí (5), Lhota (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Klatovy (486), Domažlice (249), Plzeň-sever (244), Tachov (224). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.02 and 50.09 and longitude 12.47 and 13.81, around a centre at about 49.58, 13.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
