Postal codes of Jihočeský kraj, Czechia

243 codes, 262 42 to 399 01.

The GeoNames data lists 243 postal codes in Jihočeský kraj, Czechia, covering 1,752 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 262 42 to 399 01.

The places with the most codes are Nová Ves (11), Březí (7), Záluží (6), Chlum (5), Záhoří (5), Zahrádka (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Tábor (349), České Budějovice (323), Jindřichův Hradec (287), Písek (268). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.59 and 49.60 and longitude 13.58 and 15.59, around a centre at about 49.16, 14.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
