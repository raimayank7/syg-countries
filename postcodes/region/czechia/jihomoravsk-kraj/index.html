Postal codes of Jihomoravský kraj, Czechia

289 codes, 602 00 to 698 01.

The GeoNames data lists 289 postal codes in Jihomoravský kraj, Czechia, covering 879 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 602 00 to 698 01.

The places with the most codes are Březina (3), Uhřice (3), Němčičky (3), Lažánky (2), Petrovice (2), Vysočany (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Blansko (194), Znojmo (176), Brno-venkov (158), Vyškov (120). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.72 and 49.62 and longitude 15.60 and 17.63, around a centre at about 49.15, 16.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
