Postal codes of Liberecký kraj, Czechia

134 codes, 294 13 to 514 01.

The GeoNames data lists 134 postal codes in Liberecký kraj, Czechia, covering 749 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 294 13 to 514 01.

The places with the most codes are Žďár (4), Borek (3), Víska (3), Nová Ves (3), Vesec (3), Česká Lípa (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Liberec (263), Semily (219), Česká Lípa (200), Jablonec nad Nisou (113). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 50.48 and 51.02 and longitude 14.36 and 15.58, around a centre at about 50.68, 15.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
