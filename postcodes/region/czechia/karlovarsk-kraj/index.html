Postal codes of Karlovarský kraj, Czechia

52 codes, 330 41 to 441 01.

The GeoNames data lists 52 postal codes in Karlovarský kraj, Czechia, covering 486 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 330 41 to 441 01.

The places with the most codes are Tisová (4), Podlesí (2), Háje (2), Milhostov (2), Podhoří (2), Hradiště (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Karlovy Vary (256), Cheb (161), Sokolov (101). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.92 and 50.44 and longitude 12.15 and 13.29, around a centre at about 50.17, 12.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
