Postal codes of Olomoucký kraj, Czechia

175 codes, 750 02 to 798 62.

The GeoNames data lists 175 postal codes in Olomoucký kraj, Czechia, covering 763 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 750 02 to 798 62.

The places with the most codes are Prostějov (3), Podolí (3), Bukovice (3), Čechovice (2), Dětkovice (2), Alojzov (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Olomouc (220), Přerov (181), Šumperk (168), Prostějov (162). Postal codes in Czechia are written three digits, then a space, then two digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.28 and 50.44 and longitude 16.73 and 17.90, around a centre at about 49.69, 17.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
