Postal codes of Murcia, Spain

213 codes, 03657 to 30893.

The GeoNames data lists 213 postal codes in Murcia, Spain, covering 624 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 03657 to 30893.

The places with the most codes are Murcia (21), Cartagena (10), Molina De Segura (5), Raspay (1), Espinardo (1), Puntal, El (Espinardo) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Murcia (657). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.40 and 38.61 and longitude -2.28 and -0.69, around a centre at about 37.86, -1.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
