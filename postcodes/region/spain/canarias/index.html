Postal codes of Canarias, Spain

368 codes, 35001 to 38917.

The GeoNames data lists 368 postal codes in Canarias, Spain, covering 1,698 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 35001 to 38917.

The places with the most codes are Las Palmas De Gran Canaria (24), Santa Cruz De Tenerife (19), San Cristobal De La Laguna (15), Telde (10), Lomo Del Moral (2), Aldea Blanca (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Las Palmas (1058), Santa Cruz de Tenerife (723). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 27.64 and 29.22 and longitude -18.10 and -13.45, around a centre at about 28.23, -15.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
