Postal codes of Navarra, Spain

266 codes, 31001 to 50686.

The GeoNames data lists 266 postal codes in Navarra, Spain, covering 909 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 31001 to 50686.

The places with the most codes are Pamplona/Iruña (19), Mendillorri (1), Gares (1), Puente La Reina/Gares (1), Señorio De Sarria (1), Artazu (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Navarra (927). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.92 and 43.29 and longitude -2.48 and -0.92, around a centre at about 42.82, -1.68 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
