Postal codes of La Rioja, Spain

132 codes, 26001 to 26589.

The GeoNames data lists 132 postal codes in La Rioja, Spain, covering 262 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 26001 to 26589.

The places with the most codes are Logroño (12), Torrecilla En Cameros (1), Ribabellosa (1), Nestares (1), Pinillos (1), Almarza De Cameros (1). The sub-areas with most codes are La Rioja (273). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.96 and 42.63 and longitude -3.10 and -1.75, around a centre at about 42.33, -2.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
