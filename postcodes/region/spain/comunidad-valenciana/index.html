Postal codes of Comunidad Valenciana, Spain

657 codes, 03001 to 46989.

The GeoNames data lists 657 postal codes in Comunidad Valenciana, Spain, covering 1,646 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 03001 to 46989.

The places with the most codes are Valencia (30), Alicante/Alacant (24), Elx/Elche (17), Castellon De La Plana/Castello De La Pla (10), Torrevieja (8), Gandia (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Alicante (1054), Valencia (475), Castellón (221). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.87 and 40.73 and longitude -1.43 and 0.48, around a centre at about 38.92, -0.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
