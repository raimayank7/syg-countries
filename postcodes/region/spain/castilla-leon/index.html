Postal codes of Castilla - Leon, Spain

2,020 codes, 01118 to 49882.

The GeoNames data lists 2,020 postal codes in Castilla - Leon, Spain, covering 5,853 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01118 to 49882.

The places with the most codes are Zamora (37), Valladolid (24), Leon (15), Salamanca (11), Burgos (10), Avila (9). The sub-areas with most codes are León (1420), Burgos (1270), Salamanca (728), Zamora (549). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.14 and 43.22 and longitude -7.04 and -1.81, around a centre at about 41.94, -4.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
