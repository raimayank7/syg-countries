Postal codes of Galicia, Spain

1,540 codes, 15001 to 36995.

The GeoNames data lists 1,540 postal codes in Galicia, Spain, covering 7,277 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 15001 to 36995.

The places with the most codes are Vigo (33), Ferrol (16), A Coruña (15), Santiago De Compostela (15), Pontevedra (15), Lugo (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Ourense (2674), A Coruña (2190), Lugo (1360), Pontevedra (1211). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.85 and 43.77 and longitude -9.26 and -6.80, around a centre at about 42.67, -8.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
