Postal codes of Cataluna, Spain

1,163 codes, 08001 to 43897.

The GeoNames data lists 1,163 postal codes in Cataluna, Spain, covering 2,343 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 08001 to 43897.

The places with the most codes are Barcelona (46), Lleida (21), Tarragona (12), Sabadell (10), Girona (10), Sant Cugat Del Valles (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Lleida (939), Girona (576), Barcelona (575), Tarragona (420). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.54 and 42.84 and longitude 0.25 and 3.29, around a centre at about 41.84, 1.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
