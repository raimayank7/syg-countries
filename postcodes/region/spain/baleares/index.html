Postal codes of Baleares, Spain

164 codes, 07001 to 07872.

The GeoNames data lists 164 postal codes in Baleares, Spain, covering 369 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 07001 to 07872.

The places with the most codes are Palma De Mallorca (27), Mao (5), Son Roca-Son Ximelis (Barri) (1), S'Horta (Soller) (1), Soller (1), Biniaraix (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Baleares (399). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.66 and 40.06 and longitude 1.24 and 4.30, around a centre at about 39.55, 2.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
