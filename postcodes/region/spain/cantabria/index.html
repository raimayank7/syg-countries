Postal codes of Cantabria, Spain

215 codes, 33554 to 39880.

The GeoNames data lists 215 postal codes in Cantabria, Spain, covering 1,089 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 33554 to 39880.

The places with the most codes are Santander (15), Tresviso (1), Santa Cruz De Bezana (1), Mompia (1), Azoños (1), Maoño (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantabria (1103). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.77 and 43.50 and longitude -4.81 and -3.17, around a centre at about 43.24, -3.97 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
