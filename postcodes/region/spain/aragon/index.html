Postal codes of Aragon, Spain

767 codes, 22001 to 50840.

The GeoNames data lists 767 postal codes in Aragon, Spain, covering 1,454 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 22001 to 50840.

The places with the most codes are Zaragoza (27), Huesca (9), Teruel (7), Figueruelas (2), Santa Engracia (2), Sangarren (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Huesca (777), Zaragoza (407), Teruel (312). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.91 and 42.79 and longitude -2.15 and 0.75, around a centre at about 41.77, -0.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
