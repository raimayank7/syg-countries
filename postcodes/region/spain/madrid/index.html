Postal codes of Madrid, Spain

323 codes, 28001 to 28991.

The GeoNames data lists 323 postal codes in Madrid, Spain, covering 382 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 28001 to 28991.

The places with the most codes are Madrid (63), Leganes (10), Alcala De Henares (9), Getafe (9), Mostoles (9), Fuenlabrada (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Madrid (514). Postal codes in Spain are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 39.91 and 41.13 and longitude -4.48 and -3.10, around a centre at about 40.48, -3.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
