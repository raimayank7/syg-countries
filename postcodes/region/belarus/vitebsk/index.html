Postal codes of Vitebsk, Belarus

575 codes, 210000 to 211999.

The GeoNames data lists 575 postal codes in Vitebsk, Belarus, covering 550 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 210000 to 211999.

The places with the most codes are Слобода (5), Заболотье (3), Замосточье (2), Старое Село (2), Бабиничи (2), Заполье (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Vitebsk (51), Polotsk (36), Orcha (35), Gloubokoe (34). Postal codes in Belarus are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 54.05 and 56.08 and longitude 26.31 and 31.17, around a centre at about 55.15, 28.97 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

22 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Vitebsk (51); Polotsk (36); Orcha (35); Gloubokoe (34); Braslav (31); Tolotchin (29); Postavy (29); Miory (29); Senno (28); Gorodok (28); Lepel (26); Dokchitsy (26).

Minsk; Gomel; Brest; Moghilev; Grodno.
