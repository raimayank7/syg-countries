Postal codes of Grodno, Belarus

376 codes, 211227 to 231988.

The GeoNames data lists 376 postal codes in Grodno, Belarus, covering 364 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 211227 to 231988.

The places with the most codes are Поперечье (3), Каменка (3), Шиловичи (2), Конюхи (2), Свислочь (2), Новый Двор (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Grodno (35), Chtchoutchin (30), Lida (27), Volkovyssk (25). Postal codes in Belarus are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.80 and 54.95 and longitude 23.61 and 30.39, around a centre at about 53.67, 25.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
