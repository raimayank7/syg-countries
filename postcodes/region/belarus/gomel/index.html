Postal codes of Gomel, Belarus

538 codes, 211657 to 247995.

The GeoNames data lists 538 postal codes in Gomel, Belarus, covering 501 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 211657 to 247995.

The places with the most codes are Дуброва (4), Новоселки (3), Александровка (3), Борщевка (3), Моисеевка (3), Прудок (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Kalinkovitchi (45), Gomel (42), Jlobin (40), Retchitsa (38). Postal codes in Belarus are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.33 and 55.30 and longitude 27.36 and 31.68, around a centre at about 52.40, 29.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
