Postal codes of Brest, Belarus

525 codes, 224000 to 225927.

The GeoNames data lists 525 postal codes in Brest, Belarus, covering 504 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 224000 to 225927.

The places with the most codes are Новоселки (3), Старое Село (2), Соколово (2), Подлесье (2), Речица (2), Осовцы (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Brest (55), Pinsk (50), Stolin (48), Ivatsevitchi (44). Postal codes in Belarus are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.55 and 53.36 and longitude 23.22 and 27.86, around a centre at about 52.41, 25.40 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Brest (55); Pinsk (50); Stolin (48); Ivatsevitchi (44); Baranovitchi (43); Proujany (39); Kobrin (38); Louninets (36); Droghitchin (33); Kamenets (31); Ivanovo (27); Liakhovitchi (26).

Other regions of Belarus

Minsk; Vitebsk; Gomel; Moghilev; Grodno.
