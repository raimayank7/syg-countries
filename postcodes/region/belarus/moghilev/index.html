Postal codes of Moghilev, Belarus

406 codes, 212000 to 247711.

The GeoNames data lists 406 postal codes in Moghilev, Belarus, covering 385 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 212000 to 247711.

The places with the most codes are Городец (3), Староселье (3), Барсуки (3), Селец (3), Павловичи (3), Березовка (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Moghilev (36), Bykhov (33), Bobrouisk (29), Chklov (26). Postal codes in Belarus are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.25 and 54.75 and longitude 28.23 and 32.70, around a centre at about 53.63, 30.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
