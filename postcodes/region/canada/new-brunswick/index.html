Postal codes of New Brunswick, Canada

111 codes, E1A to E9H.

The GeoNames data lists 111 postal codes in New Brunswick, Canada, covering 110 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from E1A to E9H.

The places with the most codes are Bathurst (2), Dieppe Moncton East (1), Riverview (1), Moncton Central (1), Moncton West (1), Moncton Northwest (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Saint John (9), Moncton (3), Fredericton (3), Miramichi (2). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.70 and 48.05 and longitude -68.75 and -64.02, around a centre at about 46.33, -66.09 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
