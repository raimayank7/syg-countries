Postal codes of British Columbia, Canada

193 codes, V0A to V9Z.

The GeoNames data lists 193 postal codes in British Columbia, Canada, covering 192 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from V0A to V9Z.

The places with the most codes are Pitt Meadows (2), Vancouver (North Hastings-Sunrise) (1), Vancouver (North Grandview-Woodlands) (1), Vancouver (South Hastings-Sunrise / North Renfrew-Collingwood) (1), Vancouver (South Grandview-Woodlands / NE Kensington) (1), Vancouver (SE Kensington / Victoria-Fraserview) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Vancouver (44), Surrey (9), Richmond (8), Burnaby (7). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.37 and 59.64 and longitude -133.54 and -115.56, around a centre at about 49.80, -122.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
