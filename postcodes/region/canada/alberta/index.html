Postal codes of Alberta, Canada

155 codes, T0A to T9X.

The GeoNames data lists 155 postal codes in Alberta, Canada, covering 154 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from T0A to T9X.

The places with the most codes are St. Albert (2), Eastern Alberta (St. Paul) (1), Wainwright Region (Tofield) (1), Central Alberta (Stettler) (1), Western Alberta (Jasper) (1), North Central Alberta (Slave Lake) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Edmonton (38), Calgary (35), Sherwood Park (6), Red Deer (4). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.47 and 59.90 and longitude -118.91 and -110.09, around a centre at about 52.64, -113.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
