Postal codes of Ontario, Canada

527 codes, K0A to P9N.

The GeoNames data lists 527 postal codes in Ontario, Canada, covering 528 named places and 67 sub-areas. In code order they run from K0A to P9N.

The places with the most codes are Milton (2), Barrie (2), Algoma Southwest (Blind River) (1), Brantford Northeast (1), Brantford Central (1), Brantford Southeast (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Toronto (41), Mississauga (23), Hamilton (17), Ottawa (17). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.05 and 52.96 and longitude -95.10 and -74.62, around a centre at about 44.19, -79.85 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
