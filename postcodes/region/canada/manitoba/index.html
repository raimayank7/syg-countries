Postal codes of Manitoba, Canada

67 codes, R0A to R9A.

The GeoNames data lists 67 postal codes in Manitoba, Canada, covering 67 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from R0A to R9A.

The places with the most codes are Lorette (1), Southeastern Manitoba (Lorette) (1), Northern Manitoba (Norway House) (1), North Interlake (Stonewall) (1), Eastern Manitoba (Beausejour) (1), South Central Manitoba (Altona) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Winnipeg (33), Brandon (3), South (2), Headingley (2). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 49.18 and 56.27 and longitude -101.86 and -95.96, around a centre at about 50.25, -97.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
