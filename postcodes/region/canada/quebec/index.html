Postal codes of Quebec, Canada

422 codes, G0A to J9Z.

The GeoNames data lists 422 postal codes in Quebec, Canada, covering 422 named places and 60 sub-areas. In code order they run from G0A to J9Z.

The places with the most codes are Matane (1), Mont-Joli (1), Amqui (1), Rimouski Central (1), Rimouski Northeast (1), Rimouski Southwest (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Montérégie (41), Capitale-Nationale (24), Lanaudière (13), Laurentides (13). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.10 and 90.00 and longitude -79.43 and 0.00, around a centre at about 46.39, -72.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
