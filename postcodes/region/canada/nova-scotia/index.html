Postal codes of Nova Scotia, Canada

77 codes, B0C to B9A.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Nova Scotia, Canada, covering 76 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from B0C to B9A.

The places with the most codes are Truro (2), North Victoria County (Dingwall) (1), West Cape Breton Island (Baddeck) (1), Canso region (Havre Boucher) (1), Mainland east shore (Lunenburg) (1), Southern Northumberland Strait (Pictou) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Halifax (9), Dartmouth (7), Sydney (6), Lower Sackville (3). Postal codes in Canada are written one letter, then one digit, then one letter, then a space, then one digit, then one letter, then one digit.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.83 and 46.65 and longitude -66.12 and -59.91, around a centre at about 45.26, -62.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
