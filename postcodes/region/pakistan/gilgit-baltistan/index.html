Postal codes of Gilgit Baltistan, Pakistan

51 codes, 14100 to 16920.

The GeoNames data lists 51 postal codes in Gilgit Baltistan, Pakistan, covering 51 named places. In code order they run from 14100 to 16920.

The places with the most codes are Chilas (Lsg) (1), Jaglot Tangir (1), Bunji (1), Astore (1), Ghorikot (1), Chilam (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.49 and 36.61 and longitude 72.76 and 76.80, around a centre at about 35.55, 74.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
