Postal codes of Sindh, Pakistan

353 codes, 65010 to 79220.

The GeoNames data lists 353 postal codes in Sindh, Pakistan, covering 353 named places. In code order they run from 65010 to 79220.

The places with the most codes are Dharki (1), Fertilizer Factory Dharki (1), Ubauro (1), Khambara (1), Mirpur Mathelo (1), Pak Saudi Fertilizar Mirpur Mathelo (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 24.32 and 34.20 and longitude 66.96 and 72.09, around a centre at about 26.26, 68.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
