Postal codes of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan

495 codes, 17000 to 29540.

The GeoNames data lists 495 postal codes in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan, covering 493 named places. In code order they run from 17000 to 29540.

The places with the most codes are Pipal Bazar (2), Musa Zai (2), Mera Gram (1), Brep (1), Mastaj (1), Harichin (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 28.43 and 36.24 and longitude 67.92 and 75.52, around a centre at about 33.92, 71.93 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
