Postal codes of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Pakistan

138 codes, 10010 to 13250.

The GeoNames data lists 138 postal codes in Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Pakistan, covering 138 named places. In code order they run from 10010 to 13250.

The places with the most codes are Watala (1), Moil (1), Barnala (1), Bharing (1), Kadhala (1), Pitho Rani (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 30.24 and 34.83 and longitude 72.19 and 74.49, around a centre at about 33.56, 73.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
