Postal codes of Federal Capital, Pakistan

46 codes, 44000 to 45900.

The GeoNames data lists 46 postal codes in Federal Capital, Pakistan, covering 46 named places. In code order they run from 44000 to 45900.

The places with the most codes are Islamabad Gpo (1), Islamabad Primr Minister Sectt. (1), Islamabad B-Block Pak Sectt. (1), Islamabad National Assembly (1), Islamabad Aiwan-E-Saddar (1), Islamabad G-5 Foreign Office (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.55 and 33.75 and longitude 72.98 and 73.26, around a centre at about 33.68, 73.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
