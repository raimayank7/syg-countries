Postal codes of Balochistan, Pakistan

175 codes, 80000 to 96100.

The GeoNames data lists 175 postal codes in Balochistan, Pakistan, covering 172 named places. In code order they run from 80000 to 96100.

The places with the most codes are Khattan (2), Nasirabad (2), Basima (2), Sui Gas Field Sibi (1), Dera Bugti (1), Manjhi Pur (1). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 25.05 and 34.89 and longitude 61.75 and 73.93, around a centre at about 28.78, 66.72 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
