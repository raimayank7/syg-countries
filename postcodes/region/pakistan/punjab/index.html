Postal codes of Punjab, Pakistan

1,302 codes, 22420 to 65280.

The GeoNames data lists 1,302 postal codes in Punjab, Pakistan, covering 1,292 named places. In code order they run from 22420 to 65280.

The places with the most codes are Tatral (3), Fateh Pur (2), Kala (2), Gojra (2), Nara (2), Dhurnal (2). Postal codes in Pakistan are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 24.87 and 34.46 and longitude 66.30 and 75.31, around a centre at about 31.89, 72.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
