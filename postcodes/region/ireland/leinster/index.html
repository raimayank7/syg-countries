Postal codes of Leinster, Ireland

68 codes, A41 to Y35.

The GeoNames data lists 68 postal codes in Leinster, Ireland, covering 68 named places. In code order they run from A41 to Y35.

The places with the most codes are Ballyboughal (1), Garristown (1), Oldtown (1), Greystones (1), Wicklow (1), Kells (1). Postal codes in Ireland are written three letters, then a space, then four letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 52.33 and 54.00 and longitude -7.94 and -6.05, around a centre at about 53.28, -6.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
