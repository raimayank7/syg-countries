Postal codes of Munster, Ireland

43 codes, E21 to X91.

The GeoNames data lists 43 postal codes in Munster, Ireland, covering 43 named places. In code order they run from E21 to X91.

The places with the most codes are Cahir (1), Cashel (1), Carrick on Suir (1), Tipperary (1), Thurles (1), Nenagh (1). Postal codes in Ireland are written three letters, then a space, then four letters.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 51.55 and 52.95 and longitude -10.22 and -7.11, around a centre at about 52.17, -8.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
