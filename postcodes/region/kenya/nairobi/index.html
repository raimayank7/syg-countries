Postal codes of Nairobi, Kenya

77 codes, 00100 to 90406.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Nairobi, Kenya, covering 78 named places. In code order they run from 00100 to 90406.

The places with the most codes are Nairobi Gpo (1), Jamia (1), State Law Office-Sheria HSE (1), City Square (1), Kenyatta Hospital (1), Mbari Ya Njiku (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.34 and 0.94 and longitude 34.15 and 39.43, around a centre at about -1.11, 36.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
