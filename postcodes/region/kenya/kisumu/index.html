Postal codes of Kisumu, Kenya

33 codes, 10311 to 40325.

The GeoNames data lists 33 postal codes in Kisumu, Kenya, covering 33 named places. In code order they run from 10311 to 40325.

The places with the most codes are Kibigoti (1), Kisumu (1), Ahero (1), Kombewa (1), Kondele (1), Koru (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.53 and -0.01 and longitude 34.45 and 37.34, around a centre at about -0.16, 34.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
