Postal codes of Mombasa, Kenya

15 codes, 80100 to 80123.

The GeoNames data lists 15 postal codes in Mombasa, Kenya, covering 15 named places. In code order they run from 80100 to 80123.

The places with the most codes are Mombasa (1), Bamburi (1), Changamwe (1), Coast Gen. Hospital (1), Docks (1), Mkomani (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.08 and -3.92 and longitude 39.47 and 39.72, around a centre at about -3.97, 39.65 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
