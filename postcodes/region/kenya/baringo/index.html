Postal codes of Baringo, Kenya

17 codes, 20103 to 30413.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Baringo, Kenya, covering 18 named places. In code order they run from 20103 to 30413.

The places with the most codes are Eldama Ravine (1), Mogotio (1), Kisanana (1), Maji Mazuri (1), Torongo (1), Kapkenda (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.02 and 0.95 and longitude 35.44 and 36.10, around a centre at about 0.47, 35.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
