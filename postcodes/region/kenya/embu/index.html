Postal codes of Embu, Kenya

23 codes, 01031 to 90209.

The GeoNames data lists 23 postal codes in Embu, Kenya, covering 23 named places. In code order they run from 01031 to 90209.

The places with the most codes are Kindaruma (1), Embu (1), Manyatta (1), Ishiara (1), Runyenjes (1), Siakago (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.82 and -0.38 and longitude 36.94 and 37.79, around a centre at about -0.59, 37.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
