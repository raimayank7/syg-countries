Postal codes of Nakuru, Kenya

47 codes, 00242 to 50238.

The GeoNames data lists 47 postal codes in Nakuru, Kenya, covering 48 named places. In code order they run from 00242 to 50238.

The places with the most codes are Ewaso Kedong (1), Nakuru (1), Bondeni (1), Elburgon (1), Menengai (1), Molo (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.48 and 0.72 and longitude 34.53 and 36.96, around a centre at about -0.32, 35.90 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
