Postal codes of Kericho, Kenya

26 codes, 20200 to 20403.

The GeoNames data lists 26 postal codes in Kericho, Kenya, covering 26 named places. In code order they run from 20200 to 20403.

The places with the most codes are Kericho (1), Kabianga (1), Kipkelion (1), Londiani (1), Roret (1), Sosiot (1). Postal codes in Kenya are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.82 and -0.04 and longitude 35.06 and 35.59, around a centre at about -0.39, 35.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
