Postal codes of Ehime Ken, Japan

2,036 codes, 790-0001 to 799-3793.

The GeoNames data lists 2,036 postal codes in Ehime Ken, Japan, covering 1,784 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 790-0001 to 799-3793.

The places with the most codes are Minatomachi (10), Chifunemachi (9), Miyatamachi (8), Sambammachi (8), Asahimachi (7), Ichibammachi (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Matsuyama Shi (489), Imabari Shi (265), Uwajima Shi (185), Saijo Shi (150). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.91 and 34.27 and longitude 132.04 and 133.65, around a centre at about 33.71, 132.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

18 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Matsuyama Shi (489); Imabari Shi (265); Uwajima Shi (185); Saijo Shi (150); Niihama Shi (128); Ozu Shi (112); Yawatahama Shi (109); Seiyo Shi (106); Minamiuwa Gun (74); Iyo Gun (66); Shikokuchuo Shi (66); Kitauwa Gun (60).
