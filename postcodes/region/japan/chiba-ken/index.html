Postal codes of Chiba Ken, Japan

4,219 codes, 100-8677 to 299-5506.

The GeoNames data lists 4,219 postal codes in Chiba Ken, Japan, covering 3,186 named places and 43 sub-areas. In code order they run from 100-8677 to 299-5506.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (23), Nakase (18), Maihama (15), Fujimi (15), Kashiwanoha (12), Shimmachi (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Chiba Shi (451), Ichihara Shi (250), Kashiwa Shi (191), Narita Shi (181). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.92 and 36.09 and longitude 139.76 and 140.86, around a centre at about 35.59, 140.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
