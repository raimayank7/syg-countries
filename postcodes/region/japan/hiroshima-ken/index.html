Postal codes of Hiroshima Ken, Japan

2,619 codes, 720-0001 to 739-8616.

The GeoNames data lists 2,619 postal codes in Hiroshima Ken, Japan, covering 2,232 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 720-0001 to 739-8616.

The places with the most codes are Motomachi (20), Shokosenta (17), Kagamiyama (14), Kamihatchobori (13), Chuo (11), Hommachi (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Hiroshima Shi (682), Fukuyama Shi (301), Kure Shi (285), Hatsukaichi Shi (173). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.12 and 34.95 and longitude 132.09 and 133.44, around a centre at about 34.45, 132.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
