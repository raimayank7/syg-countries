Postal codes of Kagawa Ken, Japan

964 codes, 760-0000 to 769-2992.

The GeoNames data lists 964 postal codes in Kagawa Ken, Japan, covering 740 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 760-0000 to 769-2992.

The places with the most codes are Bancho (15), Kotobukicho (10), Ikeda (8), Marunochi (7), Nishikimachi (6), Marugamemachi (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Takamatsu Shi (341), Marugame Shi (113), Nakatado Gun (83), Shozu Gun (78). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.08 and 34.56 and longitude 133.54 and 134.43, around a centre at about 34.28, 133.96 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Takamatsu Shi (341); Marugame Shi (113); Nakatado Gun (83); Shozu Gun (78); Sakaide Shi (65); Kanonji Shi (56); Mitoyo Shi (55); Higashikagawa Shi (48); Ayauta Gun (47); Sanuki Shi (34); Zentsuji Shi (29); Kita Gun (17).
