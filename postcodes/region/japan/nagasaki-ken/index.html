Postal codes of Nagasaki Ken, Japan

2,108 codes, 811-5100 to 859-6492.

The GeoNames data lists 2,108 postal codes in Nagasaki Ken, Japan, covering 1,886 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 811-5100 to 859-6492.

The places with the most codes are Sakaemachi (10), Manzaimachi (9), Shiroyamacho (6), Hamacho (6), Morimachi (6), Nunomakimachi (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Nagasaki Shi (455), Sasebo Shi (331), Isahaya Shi (184), Shimabara Shi (134). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.56 and 34.41 and longitude 128.77 and 130.38, around a centre at about 33.08, 129.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
