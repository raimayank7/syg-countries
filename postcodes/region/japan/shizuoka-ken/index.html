Postal codes of Shizuoka Ken, Japan

3,636 codes, 410-0000 to 439-8651.

The GeoNames data lists 3,636 postal codes in Shizuoka Ken, Japan, covering 2,646 named places and 28 sub-areas. In code order they run from 410-0000 to 439-8651.

The places with the most codes are Otemachi (24), Naka (13), Chuocho (11), Minamimachi (10), Chuo (10), Hinodecho (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Shizuoka Shi (799), Hamamatsu Shi (606), Iwata Shi (223), Fuji Shi (208). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.60 and 35.45 and longitude 137.49 and 139.17, around a centre at about 34.93, 138.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
