Postal codes of Ishikawa Ken, Japan

2,912 codes, 920-0000 to 939-0171.

The GeoNames data lists 2,912 postal codes in Ishikawa Ken, Japan, covering 2,490 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 920-0000 to 939-0171.

The places with the most codes are Hiroka (11), Toiyacho (10), Sainen (9), Asahimachi (8), Kamitsutsumicho (7), Hommachi (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kanazawa Shi (766), Hakusan Shi (296), Komatsu Shi (292), Kaga Shi (277). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.13 and 37.85 and longitude 136.25 and 137.32, around a centre at about 36.72, 136.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
