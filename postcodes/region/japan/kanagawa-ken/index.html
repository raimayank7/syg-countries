Postal codes of Kanagawa Ken, Japan

3,106 codes, 210-0000 to 259-1392.

The GeoNames data lists 3,106 postal codes in Kanagawa Ken, Japan, covering 2,206 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 210-0000 to 259-1392.

The places with the most codes are Shinyokohama (24), Yamashitacho (21), Chuo (13), Horikawacho (13), Sakuragicho (12), Enokicho (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Yokohama Shi (1282), Kawasaki Shi (427), Sagamihara Shi (180), Yokosuka Shi (148). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.14 and 35.65 and longitude 139.00 and 139.78, around a centre at about 35.44, 139.50 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
