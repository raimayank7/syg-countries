Postal codes of Tochigi Ken, Japan

2,058 codes, 311-4411 to 349-1221.

The GeoNames data lists 2,058 postal codes in Tochigi Ken, Japan, covering 1,739 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 311-4411 to 349-1221.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (9), Honcho (7), Hommachi (7), Midori (6), Kiyoharakogyodanchi (6), Chuocho (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Utsunomiya Shi (347), Nasushiobara Shi (158), Tochigi Shi (157), Nikko Shi (155). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.19 and 37.10 and longitude 139.38 and 140.37, around a centre at about 36.58, 139.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
