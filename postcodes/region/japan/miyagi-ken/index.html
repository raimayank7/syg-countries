Postal codes of Miyagi Ken, Japan

3,266 codes, 980-0000 to 989-6941.

The GeoNames data lists 3,266 postal codes in Miyagi Ken, Japan, covering 3,317 named places and 24 sub-areas. In code order they run from 980-0000 to 989-6941.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (34), Chuo Ss30-Sumitomoseimeisendaichuob (31), Ichibammachi (24), Hommachi (23), Oroshimachi (20), Oroshimachihigashi (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Sendai Shi (742), Kurihara Shi (430), Kesennuma Shi (372), Tome Shi (316). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 37.83 and 38.99 and longitude 140.53 and 141.63, around a centre at about 38.44, 141.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

24 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Sendai Shi (742); Kurihara Shi (430); Kesennuma Shi (372); Tome Shi (316); Osaki Shi (271); Igu Gun (221); Ishinomaki Shi (208); Toda Gun (147); Kami Gun (140); Katta Gun (125); Shibata Gun (123); Motoyoshi Gun (109).
