Postal codes of Hyogo Ken, Japan

5,619 codes, 539-8794 to 679-5654.

The GeoNames data lists 5,619 postal codes in Hyogo Ken, Japan, covering 5,027 named places and 37 sub-areas. In code order they run from 539-8794 to 679-5654.

The places with the most codes are Minatojimanakamachi (11), Sakaemachi (9), Hommachi (9), Higashikawasakicho (8), Midorigaoka (6), Yamada (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kobe Shi (927), Himeji Shi (589), Nishinomiya Shi (348), Toyoka Shi (329). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.17 and 35.66 and longitude 134.27 and 135.46, around a centre at about 34.89, 134.95 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
