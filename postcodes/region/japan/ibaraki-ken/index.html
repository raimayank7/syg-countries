Postal codes of Ibaraki Ken, Japan

3,154 codes, 300-0000 to 319-3705.

The GeoNames data lists 3,154 postal codes in Ibaraki Ken, Japan, covering 2,583 named places and 39 sub-areas. In code order they run from 300-0000 to 319-3705.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (14), Kannondai (10), Koya (8), Kasaharacho (8), Higashi (7), Tennodai (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Tsukuba Shi (306), Mito Shi (190), Chikusei Shi (186), Tsuchiura Shi (127). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.75 and 36.85 and longitude 139.69 and 140.84, around a centre at about 36.21, 140.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
