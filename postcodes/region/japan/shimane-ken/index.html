Postal codes of Shimane Ken, Japan

1,326 codes, 684-0100 to 699-5695.

The GeoNames data lists 1,326 postal codes in Shimane Ken, Japan, covering 1,203 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 684-0100 to 699-5695.

The places with the most codes are Tonomachi (12), Imaichicho (8), Higashiasahimachi (7), Horomachi (6), Sakaemachi (4), Yakami (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Matsue Shi (269), Izumo Shi (202), Hamada Shi (127), Masuda Shi (107). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.33 and 36.33 and longitude 131.69 and 133.37, around a centre at about 35.20, 132.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
