Postal codes of Nara Ken, Japan

2,074 codes, 630-0000 to 648-0392.

The GeoNames data lists 2,074 postal codes in Nara Ken, Japan, covering 1,879 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 630-0000 to 648-0392.

The places with the most codes are Hommachi (9), Shijocho (7), Noboriojicho (6), Omiyacho (5), Omoricho (5), Shimmachi (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Nara Shi (450), Yoshino Gun (269), Uda Shi (141), Gojo Shi (129). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.73 and 34.75 and longitude 135.61 and 136.20, around a centre at about 34.54, 135.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
