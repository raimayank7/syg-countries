Postal codes of Akita Ken, Japan

2,298 codes, 010-0000 to 019-2742.

The GeoNames data lists 2,298 postal codes in Akita Ken, Japan, covering 2,149 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 010-0000 to 019-2742.

The places with the most codes are Nakadori (11), Omachi (7), Sanno (7), Asahikawa (5), Oroshimachi (5), Kawaguchi (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Akita Shi (374), Yokote Shi (334), Yurihonjo Shi (262), Daisen Shi (239). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.95 and 40.46 and longitude 139.91 and 140.82, around a centre at about 39.66, 140.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
