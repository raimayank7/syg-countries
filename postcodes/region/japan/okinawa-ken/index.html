Postal codes of Okinawa Ken, Japan

1,047 codes, 900-0000 to 907-8503.

The GeoNames data lists 1,047 postal codes in Okinawa Ken, Japan, covering 824 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 900-0000 to 907-8503.

The places with the most codes are Kumoji (15), Makiminato (12), Asahimachi (9), Izumizaki (8), Nishi (7), Omoromachi (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Naha Shi (165), Kunigami Gun (141), Shimajiri Gun (133), Nakagami Gun (130). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 24.06 and 26.86 and longitude 122.99 and 131.31, around a centre at about 26.16, 127.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Naha Shi (165); Kunigami Gun (141); Shimajiri Gun (133); Nakagami Gun (130); Uruma Shi (69); Nago Shi (62); Urasoe Shi (57); Nanjo Shi (53); Okinawa Shi (50); Miyakojima Shi (47); Itoman Shi (39); Tomigusuku Shi (28).
