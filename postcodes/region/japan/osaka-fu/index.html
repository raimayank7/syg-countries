Postal codes of Osaka Fu, Japan

4,693 codes, 530-0000 to 630-0272.

The GeoNames data lists 4,693 postal codes in Osaka Fu, Japan, covering 3,653 named places and 38 sub-areas. In code order they run from 530-0000 to 630-0272.

The places with the most codes are Oyodonaka Umedasukaibirutawauesuto( (40), Umeda (31), Nakanoshima (21), Otemae (21), Hommachi (18), Shimmachi (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Osaka Shi (1451), Sakai Shi (394), Higashiosaka Shi (232), Takatsuki Shi (230). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.29 and 35.04 and longitude 135.11 and 135.73, around a centre at about 34.67, 135.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
