Postal codes of Iwate Ken, Japan

2,107 codes, 020-0000 to 029-5703.

The GeoNames data lists 2,107 postal codes in Iwate Ken, Japan, covering 2,021 named places and 24 sub-areas. In code order they run from 020-0000 to 029-5703.

The places with the most codes are Uchimaru (11), Chuodori (9), Ryutsusentaminami (6), Ofunatocho (6), Odori (6), Nakanohashidori (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Oshu Shi (418), Morioka Shi (236), Hanamaki Shi (170), Miyako Shi (160). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 38.89 and 40.35 and longitude 140.79 and 142.01, around a centre at about 39.56, 141.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

24 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Oshu Shi (418); Morioka Shi (236); Hanamaki Shi (170); Miyako Shi (160); Ninohe Shi (159); Ichinoseki Shi (152); Takizawa Shi (146); Hachimantai Shi (132); Shimohei Gun (113); Iwate Gun (86); Kitakami Shi (83); Shiwa Gun (78).
