Postal codes of Tokushima Ken, Japan

1,140 codes, 770-0000 to 779-5453.

The GeoNames data lists 1,140 postal codes in Tokushima Ken, Japan, covering 1,492 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 770-0000 to 779-5453.

The places with the most codes are Kawachimachi (14), Kuramotocho (6), Minamijosanjimacho (5), Sumiyoshi (5), Takabo (5), Terashimahonchonishi (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Tokushima Shi (328), Yoshinogawa Shi (240), Miyoshi Shi (195), Awa Shi (156). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.56 and 34.24 and longitude 133.85 and 134.81, around a centre at about 34.03, 134.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Tokushima Shi (328); Yoshinogawa Shi (240); Miyoshi Shi (195); Awa Shi (156); Itano Gun (131); Anan Shi (90); Naka Gun (85); Naruto Shi (77); Myozai Gun (71); Mima Shi (71); Kaifu Gun (70); Komatsushima Shi (31).
