Postal codes of Niigata Ken, Japan

5,954 codes, 389-2261 to 959-4636.

The GeoNames data lists 5,954 postal codes in Niigata Ken, Japan, covering 4,821 named places and 29 sub-areas. In code order they run from 389-2261 to 959-4636.

The places with the most codes are Higashiodori (23), Bandai (21), Shinkocho (21), Yamada (12), Ryutsusenta (12), Honcho (11). The sub-areas with most codes are Niigata Shi (1221), Joetsu Shi (816), Nagaoka Shi (664), Tokamachi Shi (302). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.85 and 38.55 and longitude 137.84 and 139.72, around a centre at about 37.58, 138.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
