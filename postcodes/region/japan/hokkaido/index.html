Postal codes of Hokkaido, Japan

9,205 codes, 001-0000 to 099-6592.

The GeoNames data lists 9,205 postal codes in Hokkaido, Japan, covering 6,598 named places and 96 sub-areas. In code order they run from 001-0000 to 099-6592.

The places with the most codes are Honcho (41), Sakaemachi (41), Higashimachi (35), Asahimachi (33), Motomachi (32), Midorimachi (30). The sub-areas with most codes are Sapporo Shi (1220), Kamikawa Gun (463), Asahikawa Shi (423), Nakagawa Gun (258). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.41 and 45.52 and longitude 139.90 and 145.79, around a centre at about 43.22, 142.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
