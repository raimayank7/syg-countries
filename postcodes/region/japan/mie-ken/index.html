Postal codes of Mie Ken, Japan

2,763 codes, 498-0000 to 647-1325.

The GeoNames data lists 2,763 postal codes in Mie Ken, Japan, covering 2,428 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 498-0000 to 647-1325.

The places with the most codes are Sakurabashi (15), Sakaemachi (12), Takegahanacho (12), Chuocho (11), Hommachi (7), Marunochi (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Tsu Shi (377), Yokkaichi Shi (326), Matsusaka Shi (278), Kuwana Shi (244). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.73 and 35.12 and longitude 135.91 and 136.98, around a centre at about 34.71, 136.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
