Postal codes of Fukuoka Ken, Japan

4,137 codes, 800-0000 to 871-8585.

The GeoNames data lists 4,137 postal codes in Fukuoka Ken, Japan, covering 3,148 named places and 40 sub-areas. In code order they run from 800-0000 to 871-8585.

The places with the most codes are Tenjin (52), Hommachi (15), Momochihama (14), Chuo (12), Watanabedori (10), Daimyo (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Kitakyushu Shi (878), Fukuoka Shi (779), Kurume Shi (237), Omuta Shi (178). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.00 and 33.96 and longitude 130.16 and 131.20, around a centre at about 33.59, 130.62 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
