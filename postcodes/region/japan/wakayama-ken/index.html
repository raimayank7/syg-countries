Postal codes of Wakayama Ken, Japan

1,733 codes, 640-0000 to 649-7216.

The GeoNames data lists 1,733 postal codes in Wakayama Ken, Japan, covering 1,531 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 640-0000 to 649-7216.

The places with the most codes are Hommachi (9), Komatsubaradori (6), Minato (5), Naka (5), Yoshihara (4), Nibancho (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Wakayama Shi (499), Tanabe Shi (156), Hidaka Gun (142), Higashimuro Gun (129). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.45 and 34.37 and longitude 135.08 and 136.01, around a centre at about 34.05, 135.39 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Wakayama Shi (499); Tanabe Shi (156); Hidaka Gun (142); Higashimuro Gun (129); Kinokawa Shi (128); Arida Gun (121); Ito Gun (106); Hashimoto Shi (81); Kainan Shi (76); Nishimuro Gun (74); Shingu Shi (70); Kaiso Gun (58).
