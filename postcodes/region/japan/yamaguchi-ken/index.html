Postal codes of Yamaguchi Ken, Japan

1,893 codes, 740-0000 to 759-6615.

The GeoNames data lists 1,893 postal codes in Yamaguchi Ken, Japan, covering 1,836 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 740-0000 to 759-6615.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (13), Kotobukicho (6), Chuocho (6), Higashiyamatomachi (6), Minamimachi (5), Nakagawaracho (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Shimonoseki Shi (414), Shunan Shi (246), Yamaguchi Shi (232), Iwakuni Shi (188). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.79 and 34.78 and longitude 130.89 and 132.43, around a centre at about 34.06, 131.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
