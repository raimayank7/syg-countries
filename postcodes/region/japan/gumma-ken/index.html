Postal codes of Gumma Ken, Japan

1,779 codes, 370-0000 to 389-0121.

The GeoNames data lists 1,779 postal codes in Gumma Ken, Japan, covering 1,532 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 370-0000 to 389-0121.

The places with the most codes are Hommachi (13), Odecho (12), Shimmachi (7), Showamachi (6), Shibukawa (6), Fujioka (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Takasaki Shi (265), Maebashi Shi (254), Ota Shi (184), Isesaki Shi (137). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.13 and 36.91 and longitude 138.44 and 139.60, around a centre at about 36.38, 139.11 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
