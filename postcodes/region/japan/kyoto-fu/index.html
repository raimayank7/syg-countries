Postal codes of Kyoto Fu, Japan

6,680 codes, 520-0461 to 629-3579.

The GeoNames data lists 6,680 postal codes in Kyoto Fu, Japan, covering 6,302 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 520-0461 to 629-3579.

The places with the most codes are Nakanocho (11), Daikokucho (9), Masuyacho (7), Bishamoncho (7), Nishimachi (7), Shimizucho (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kyoto Shi (4833), Fukuchiyama Shi (300), Yawata Shi (290), Maizuru Shi (200). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.72 and 35.73 and longitude 134.89 and 136.02, around a centre at about 35.06, 135.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
