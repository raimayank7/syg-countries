Postal codes of Toyama Ken, Japan

3,326 codes, 930-0001 to 939-8650.

The GeoNames data lists 3,326 postal codes in Toyama Ken, Japan, covering 3,036 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 930-0001 to 939-8650.

The places with the most codes are Hongo (11), Hommachi (11), Sakurabashidori (8), Nagonoe (8), Aramachi (7), Sogawa (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Toyama Shi (1301), Takaoka Shi (477), Nanto Shi (349), Nakaniikawa Gun (279). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.32 and 36.97 and longitude 136.79 and 137.69, around a centre at about 36.69, 137.16 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

12 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Toyama Shi (1301); Takaoka Shi (477); Nanto Shi (349); Nakaniikawa Gun (279); Imizu Shi (265); Oyabe Shi (159); Namerikawa Shi (149); Tonami Shi (138); Himi Shi (130); Kurobe Shi (121); Uozu Shi (120); Shimoniikawa Gun (119).
