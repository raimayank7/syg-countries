Postal codes of Oita Ken, Japan

1,996 codes, 839-1421 to 879-7885.

The GeoNames data lists 1,996 postal codes in Oita Ken, Japan, covering 1,896 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 839-1421 to 879-7885.

The places with the most codes are Niagemachi (9), Funaicho (5), Chuomachi (4), Motomachi (4), Ota (4), Higashihama (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Oita Shi (490), Usa Shi (235), Nakatsu Shi (194), Saiki Shi (170). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.89 and 33.66 and longitude 130.75 and 131.92, around a centre at about 33.27, 131.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
