Postal codes of Kumamoto Ken, Japan

2,139 codes, 860-0001 to 869-6405.

The GeoNames data lists 2,139 postal codes in Kumamoto Ken, Japan, covering 1,873 named places and 23 sub-areas. In code order they run from 860-0001 to 869-6405.

The places with the most codes are Higashimachi (9), Hanabatamachi (9), Suizenji (9), Kasuga (8), Oe (8), Tetorihoncho (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Kumamoto Shi (559), Yatsushiro Shi (211), Kamimashiki Gun (191), Amakusa Shi (109). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.11 and 33.10 and longitude 130.01 and 131.21, around a centre at about 32.69, 130.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

23 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kumamoto Shi (559); Yatsushiro Shi (211); Kamimashiki Gun (191); Amakusa Shi (109); Yamaga Shi (95); Tamana Shi (93); Tamana Gun (93); Hitoyoshi Shi (85); Kikuchi Shi (78); Kuma Gun (78); Uki Shi (76); Minamata Shi (65).
