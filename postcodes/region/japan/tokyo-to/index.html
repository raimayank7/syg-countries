Postal codes of Tokyo To, Japan

8,747 codes, 100-0000 to 370-8686.

The GeoNames data lists 8,747 postal codes in Tokyo To, Japan, covering 3,504 named places and 55 sub-areas. In code order they run from 100-0000 to 370-8686.

The places with the most codes are Nishishinshuku (94), Nishishinjuku Sumitomofudosan Shinj (79), Odecho (70), Ginza (66), Marunochi (60), Akasaka (54). The sub-areas with most codes are Minato Ku (1538), Chiyoda Ku (1262), Shinjuku Ku (1027), Chuo Ku (662). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 26.66 and 35.86 and longitude 139.00 and 142.21, around a centre at about 35.68, 139.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
