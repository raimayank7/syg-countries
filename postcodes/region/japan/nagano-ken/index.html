Postal codes of Nagano Ken, Japan

2,254 codes, 380-0801 to 949-8321.

The GeoNames data lists 2,254 postal codes in Nagano Ken, Japan, covering 1,873 named places and 33 sub-areas. In code order they run from 380-0801 to 949-8321.

The places with the most codes are Chuo (15), Ote (10), Toyoshina (9), Fukushima (8), Yoshida (7), Tokida (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Nagano Shi (451), Matsumoto Shi (215), Iida Shi (156), Ueda Shi (136). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.25 and 37.09 and longitude 137.54 and 138.74, around a centre at about 36.26, 138.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
