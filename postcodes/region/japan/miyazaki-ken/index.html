Postal codes of Miyazaki Ken, Japan

1,042 codes, 880-0000 to 889-4602.

The GeoNames data lists 1,042 postal codes in Miyazaki Ken, Japan, covering 942 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 880-0000 to 889-4602.

The places with the most codes are Takachihodori (9), Hommachi (8), Hiroshima (6), Minamimachi (5), Asahi (4), Nakamachi (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Miyazaki Shi (294), Nobeoka Shi (189), Miyakonojo Shi (113), Nichinan Shi (72). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 31.46 and 32.75 and longitude 130.80 and 131.82, around a centre at about 32.09, 131.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Miyazaki Shi (294); Nobeoka Shi (189); Miyakonojo Shi (113); Nichinan Shi (72); Hyuga Shi (66); Higashiusuki Gun (50); Saito Shi (48); Koyu Gun (46); Kobayashi Shi (34); Ebino Shi (33); Higashimorokata Gun (28); Nishiusuki Gun (27).
