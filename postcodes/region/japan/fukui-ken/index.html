Postal codes of Fukui Ken, Japan

2,413 codes, 910-0001 to 922-0679.

The GeoNames data lists 2,413 postal codes in Fukui Ken, Japan, covering 2,179 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 910-0001 to 922-0679.

The places with the most codes are Ote (17), Chuo (9), Haruyama (9), Keya (8), Kaihotsu (8), Hommachi (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Fukui Shi (662), Sakai Shi (289), Echizen Shi (230), Obama Shi (174). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.40 and 36.28 and longitude 135.45 and 136.81, around a centre at about 35.92, 136.17 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
