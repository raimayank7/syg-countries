Postal codes of Kochi Ken, Japan

1,837 codes, 780-0000 to 789-1992.

The GeoNames data lists 1,837 postal codes in Kochi Ken, Japan, covering 1,624 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 780-0000 to 789-1992.

The places with the most codes are Hommachi (24), Marunochi (8), Minamikubo (8), Ekimaecho (7), Saiwaicho (6), Otsu (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Kochi Shi (399), Takaoka Gun (304), Agawa Gun (151), Kami Shi (133). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.73 and 33.84 and longitude 132.66 and 134.20, around a centre at about 33.43, 133.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

17 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kochi Shi (399); Takaoka Gun (304); Agawa Gun (151); Kami Shi (133); Shimanto Shi (123); Nagaoka Gun (88); Nankoku Shi (87); Hata Gun (84); Susaki Shi (74); Sukumo Shi (70); Aki Gun (67); Tosashimizu Shi (58).
