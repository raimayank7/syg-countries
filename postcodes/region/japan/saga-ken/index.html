Postal codes of Saga Ken, Japan

988 codes, 840-0001 to 849-8588.

The GeoNames data lists 988 postal codes in Saga Ken, Japan, covering 929 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 840-0001 to 849-8588.

The places with the most codes are Matsubara (5), Jonai (4), Kobira (4), Yayoigaoka (3), Hommachi (3), Fujinokimachi (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Karatsu Shi (247), Saga Shi (217), Imari Shi (93), Tosu Shi (78). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.06 and 33.54 and longitude 129.82 and 130.54, around a centre at about 33.31, 130.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
