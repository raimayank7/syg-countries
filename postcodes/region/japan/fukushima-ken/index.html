Postal codes of Fukushima Ken, Japan

3,948 codes, 960-0000 to 979-3204.

The GeoNames data lists 3,948 postal codes in Fukushima Ken, Japan, covering 3,736 named places and 26 sub-areas. In code order they run from 960-0000 to 979-3204.

The places with the most codes are Nakamachi (18), Sakaemachi (14), Motomachi (12), Minamimachi (11), Shimmachi (9), Taira (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Koriyama Shi (314), Onuma Gun (313), Iwaki Shi (289), Kitakata Shi (288). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.86 and 37.89 and longitude 139.28 and 141.02, around a centre at about 37.44, 140.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
