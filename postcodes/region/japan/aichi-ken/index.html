Postal codes of Aichi Ken, Japan

8,755 codes, 431-4121 to 498-8505.

The GeoNames data lists 8,755 postal codes in Aichi Ken, Japan, covering 7,090 named places and 45 sub-areas. In code order they run from 431-4121 to 498-8505.

The places with the most codes are Sakae (53), Nishiki (45), Meieki (28), Higashisakura (26), Marunochi (24), Sannomaru (23). The sub-areas with most codes are Nagoya Shi (2589), Toyota Shi (541), Toyokawa Shi (476), Inazawa Shi (427). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.58 and 35.39 and longitude 136.67 and 137.78, around a centre at about 35.09, 137.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
