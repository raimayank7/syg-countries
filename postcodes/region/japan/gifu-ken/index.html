Postal codes of Gifu Ken, Japan

3,764 codes, 500-0000 to 509-9298.

The GeoNames data lists 3,764 postal codes in Gifu Ken, Japan, covering 3,302 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 500-0000 to 509-9298.

The places with the most codes are Kandacho (12), Hommachi (8), Shimmachi (8), Kanemachi (8), Hashimotomachi (7), Hinodemachi (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Gifu Shi (977), Takayama Shi (283), Ogaki Shi (276), Seki Shi (242). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.24 and 36.33 and longitude 136.35 and 137.60, around a centre at about 35.53, 136.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

30 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Gifu Shi (977); Takayama Shi (283); Ogaki Shi (276); Seki Shi (242); Kakamigahara Shi (199); Hida Shi (145); Ibi Gun (143); Tajimi Shi (128); Gujo Shi (123); Ena Shi (94); Hashima Shi (91); Gero Shi (89).
