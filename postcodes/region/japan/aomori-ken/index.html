Postal codes of Aomori Ken, Japan

2,237 codes, 018-5501 to 039-5346.

The GeoNames data lists 2,237 postal codes in Aomori Ken, Japan, covering 2,468 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 018-5501 to 039-5346.

The places with the most codes are Shimmachi (15), Nagashima (12), Chuo (7), Midoricho (5), Matsubara (5), Aoba (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Kamikita Gun (550), Hirakawa Shi (360), Hirosaki Shi (330), Sannohe Gun (241). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.28 and 41.37 and longitude 139.87 and 141.57, around a centre at about 40.72, 140.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
