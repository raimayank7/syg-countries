Postal codes of Yamanashi Ken, Japan

1,092 codes, 400-0000 to 409-3898.

The GeoNames data lists 1,092 postal codes in Yamanashi Ken, Japan, covering 953 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 400-0000 to 409-3898.

The places with the most codes are Marunochi (15), Kitaguchi (7), Ono (6), Fujimi (5), Uenohara (4), Chuo (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Kofu Shi (175), Minamikoma Gun (159), Fuefuki Shi (108), Hokuto Shi (82). Postal codes in Japan are written three digits, then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.20 and 35.90 and longitude 138.28 and 139.12, around a centre at about 35.63, 138.61 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

18 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Kofu Shi (175); Minamikoma Gun (159); Fuefuki Shi (108); Hokuto Shi (82); Minamiarupusu Shi (76); Minamitsuru Gun (61); Yamanashi Shi (56); Nirasaki Shi (53); Koshu Shi (53); Kai Shi (45); Otsuki Shi (41); Chuo Shi (38).
