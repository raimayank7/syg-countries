Postal codes of Trøndelag, Norway

517 codes, 6657 to 7995.

The GeoNames data lists 517 postal codes in Trøndelag, Norway, covering 191 named places and 38 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6657 to 7995.

The places with the most codes are Trondheim (148), Steinkjer (26), Stjørdal (13), Levanger (11), Verdal (9), Namsos (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Trondheim (177), Steinkjer (34), Orkland (23), Stjørdal (21). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 62.57 and 65.08 and longitude 8.23 and 13.64, around a centre at about 63.63, 10.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
