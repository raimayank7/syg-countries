Postal codes of Buskerud, Norway

192 codes, 3001 to 3648.

The GeoNames data lists 192 postal codes in Buskerud, Norway, covering 63 named places and 18 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3001 to 3648.

The places with the most codes are Drammen (51), Kongsberg (16), Hønefoss (15), Lier (6), Tranby (4), Hokksund (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Drammen (62), Kongsberg (23), Ringerike (23), Lier (20). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.50 and 60.57 and longitude 8.01 and 10.39, around a centre at about 59.99, 9.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
