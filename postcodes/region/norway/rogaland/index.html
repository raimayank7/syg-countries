Postal codes of Rogaland, Norway

312 codes, 4001 to 5595.

The GeoNames data lists 312 postal codes in Rogaland, Norway, covering 113 named places and 23 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4001 to 5595.

The places with the most codes are Stavanger (48), Sandnes (35), Haugesund (31), Hafrsfjord (12), Egersund (7), Bryne (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Stavanger (83), Sandnes (44), Haugesund (32), Karmøy (25). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.34 and 59.75 and longitude 4.89 and 6.80, around a centre at about 59.04, 5.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
