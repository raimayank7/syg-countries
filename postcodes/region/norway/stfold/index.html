Postal codes of Østfold, Norway

236 codes, 1501 to 1894.

The GeoNames data lists 236 postal codes in Østfold, Norway, covering 54 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1501 to 1894.

The places with the most codes are Moss (40), Halden (30), Sarpsborg (18), Fredrikstad (17), Askim (15), Gamle Fredrikstad (9). The sub-areas with most codes are Fredrikstad (57), Moss (46), Halden (37), Sarpsborg (37). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.96 and 59.72 and longitude 10.61 and 11.72, around a centre at about 59.32, 11.05 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
