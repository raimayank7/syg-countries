Postal codes of Telemark, Norway

189 codes, 3650 to 3999.

The GeoNames data lists 189 postal codes in Telemark, Norway, covering 52 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3650 to 3999.

The places with the most codes are Skien (50), Porsgrunn (40), Notodden (13), Bø I Telemark (7), Stathelle (6), Kragerø (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Skien (50), Porsgrunn (43), Notodden (14), Kragerø (12). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.82 and 60.06 and longitude 7.45 and 9.82, around a centre at about 59.27, 9.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
