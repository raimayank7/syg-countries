Postal codes of Oslo County, Norway

631 codes, 0001 to 1295.

The GeoNames data lists 631 postal codes in Oslo County, Norway, covering 1 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 0001 to 1295.

The places with the most codes are Oslo (631). The sub-areas with most codes are Oslo (631). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.91 and 59.91 and longitude 10.75 and 10.75, around a centre at about 59.91, 10.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
