Postal codes of Agder, Norway

289 codes, 4400 to 4994.

The GeoNames data lists 289 postal codes in Agder, Norway, covering 100 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4400 to 4994.

The places with the most codes are Kristiansand S (57), Arendal (22), Grimstad (14), Mandal (12), Søgne (8), Flekkefjord (8). The sub-areas with most codes are Kristiansand (79), Arendal (39), Lindesnes (22), Grimstad (17). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.02 and 59.56 and longitude 6.56 and 9.23, around a centre at about 58.36, 8.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
