Postal codes of Akershus, Norway

322 codes, 1300 to 3521.

The GeoNames data lists 322 postal codes in Akershus, Norway, covering 142 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1300 to 3521.

The places with the most codes are Jessheim (11), Drøbak (10), Sandvika (9), Asker (9), Ski (7), Lillestrøm (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Bærum (63), Asker (47), Lillestrøm (32), Nordre Follo (25). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.49 and 60.56 and longitude 10.09 and 11.80, around a centre at about 59.93, 10.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
