Postal codes of Vestfold, Norway

195 codes, 3070 to 3297.

The GeoNames data lists 195 postal codes in Vestfold, Norway, covering 42 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3070 to 3297.

The places with the most codes are Sandefjord (47), Tønsberg (23), Larvik (22), Horten (12), Holmestrand (10), Sande I Vestfold (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Sandefjord (56), Tønsberg (42), Larvik (36), Holmestrand (21). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 58.97 and 59.64 and longitude 9.86 and 10.49, around a centre at about 59.24, 10.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
