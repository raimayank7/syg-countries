Postal codes of Innlandet, Norway

390 codes, 2100 to 3528.

The GeoNames data lists 390 postal codes in Innlandet, Norway, covering 197 named places and 46 sub-areas. In code order they run from 2100 to 3528.

The places with the most codes are Lillehammer (20), Hamar (18), Gjøvik (17), Kongsvinger (15), Elverum (15), Brumunddal (10). The sub-areas with most codes are Ringsaker (29), Gjøvik (27), Lillehammer (24), Kongsvinger (23). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.93 and 62.58 and longitude 7.63 and 12.68, around a centre at about 61.04, 10.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
