Postal codes of Troms, Norway

247 codes, 8409 to 9498.

The GeoNames data lists 247 postal codes in Troms, Norway, covering 108 named places and 21 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8409 to 9498.

The places with the most codes are Tromsø (62), Harstad (26), Kvaløya (6), Finnsnes (6), Storslett (5), Tromsdalen (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Tromsø (91), Harstad (34), Senja (27), Tjeldsund (11). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 68.43 and 70.21 and longitude 15.40 and 22.04, around a centre at about 69.39, 18.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
