Postal codes of Nordland, Norway

403 codes, 7979 to 8986.

The GeoNames data lists 403 postal codes in Nordland, Norway, covering 190 named places and 41 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7979 to 8986.

The places with the most codes are Bodø (50), Narvik (17), Mo I Rana (16), Mosjøen (14), Fauske (14), Sortland (12). The sub-areas with most codes are Bodø (63), Narvik (30), Rana (21), Fauske (18). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 65.05 and 69.31 and longitude 12.12 and 17.60, around a centre at about 67.17, 14.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
