Postal codes of Møre og Romsdal, Norway

332 codes, 6001 to 6763.

The GeoNames data lists 332 postal codes in Møre og Romsdal, Norway, covering 157 named places and 27 sub-areas. In code order they run from 6001 to 6763.

The places with the most codes are Ålesund (32), Molde (24), Kristiansund N (20), Frei (9), Ørsta (7), Volda (7). The sub-areas with most codes are Molde (42), Ålesund (42), Kristiansund (29), Ørsta (17). Postal codes in Norway are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.97 and 63.42 and longitude 5.48 and 9.11, around a centre at about 62.61, 6.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
