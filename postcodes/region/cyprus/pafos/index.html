Postal codes of Pafos, Cyprus

172 codes, 8010 to 8884.

The GeoNames data lists 172 postal codes in Pafos, Cyprus, covering 151 named places. In code order they run from 8010 to 8884.

The places with the most codes are Pafos (20), Geroskipou (2), Chlorakas (2), Agia Marinouda (1), Empa (1), Lempa (1). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.71 and 35.16 and longitude 32.36 and 32.75, around a centre at about 34.88, 32.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
