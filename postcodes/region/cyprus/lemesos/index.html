Postal codes of Lemesos, Cyprus

260 codes, 3010 to 4879.

The GeoNames data lists 260 postal codes in Lemesos, Cyprus, covering 145 named places. In code order they run from 3010 to 4879.

The places with the most codes are Lemesos (67), Ypsonas (15), Kato Polemidia (11), Germasogeia (10), Mesa Geitonia (9), Agios Athanasios (9). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.60 and 34.95 and longitude 32.68 and 33.26, around a centre at about 34.75, 32.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
