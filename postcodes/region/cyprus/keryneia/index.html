Postal codes of Keryneia, Cyprus

57 codes, 9000 to 9639.

The GeoNames data lists 57 postal codes in Keryneia, Cyprus, covering 57 named places. In code order they run from 9000 to 9639.

The places with the most codes are Keryneia (1), Karavas (1), Lapithos (1), Agios Georgios Keryneias (1), Agios Epiktitos (1), Thermeia (1). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.26 and 35.38 and longitude 32.95 and 33.63, around a centre at about 35.31, 33.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
