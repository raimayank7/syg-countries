Postal codes of Lefkosia, Cyprus

388 codes, 1010 to 2965.

The GeoNames data lists 388 postal codes in Lefkosia, Cyprus, covering 208 named places. In code order they run from 1010 to 2965.

The places with the most codes are Strovolos (54), Lefkosia (30), Lakatameia (26), Aglantzia (16), Egkomi (16), Latsia (14). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.67 and 35.26 and longitude 32.58 and 33.56, around a centre at about 35.12, 33.22 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
