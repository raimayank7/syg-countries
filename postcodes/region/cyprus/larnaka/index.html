Postal codes of Larnaka, Cyprus

121 codes, 6010 to 7743.

The GeoNames data lists 121 postal codes in Larnaka, Cyprus, covering 78 named places. In code order they run from 6010 to 7743.

The places with the most codes are Larnaka (39), Aradippou (6), Anglikos Stratos Dekeleias (1), Meneou (1), Dromolaxia (1), Voroklini (1). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.73 and 35.09 and longitude 33.15 and 33.85, around a centre at about 34.91, 33.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
