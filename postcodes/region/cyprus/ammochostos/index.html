Postal codes of Ammochostos, Cyprus

127 codes, 5280 to 5878.

The GeoNames data lists 127 postal codes in Ammochostos, Cyprus, covering 113 named places. In code order they run from 5280 to 5878.

The places with the most codes are Paralimni (15), Liopetri (1), Agia Napa (1), Frenaros (1), Kato Deryneia (1), Deryneia (1). Postal codes in Cyprus are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.98 and 35.60 and longitude 33.57 and 34.38, around a centre at about 35.26, 33.91 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
