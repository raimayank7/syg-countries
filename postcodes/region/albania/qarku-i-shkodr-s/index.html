Postal codes of Qarku i Shkodrës, Albania

45 codes, 4001 to 4409.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Qarku i Shkodrës, Albania, covering 45 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 4001 to 4409.

The places with the most codes are Koplik (1), Gruemire (1), Grizhe (1), Tamare (1), Dedaj (1), Bajze (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bashkia Shkodër (30), Bashkia Pukë (9), Bashkia Malësi e Madhe (6). Postal codes in Albania are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.88 and 42.46 and longitude 19.41 and 20.19, around a centre at about 42.08, 19.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
