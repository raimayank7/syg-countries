Postal codes of Qarku i Korçës, Albania

45 codes, 7001 to 7406.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Qarku i Korçës, Albania, covering 45 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7001 to 7406.

The places with the most codes are Qender Ersek (1), Qender Leskovik (1), Mollas (1), Barmash (1), Novosele (1), Clirim (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bashkia Korçë (29), Bashkia Pogradec (10), Bashkia Kolonjë (6). Postal codes in Albania are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 40.28 and 40.99 and longitude 20.39 and 21.00, around a centre at about 40.66, 20.74 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
