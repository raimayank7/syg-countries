Postal codes of Qarku i Kukësit, Albania

32 codes, 8501 to 8707.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in Qarku i Kukësit, Albania, covering 33 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8501 to 8707.

The places with the most codes are Qender (3), Krume 1 (1), Golaj (1), Fajza (1), Gjinaj (1), Kukes 2 (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bashkia Kukës (20), Bashkia Tropojë (10), Bashkia Has (5). Postal codes in Albania are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.84 and 42.36 and longitude 19.93 and 20.61, around a centre at about 42.11, 20.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
