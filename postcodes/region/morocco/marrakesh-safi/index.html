Postal codes of Marrakesh-Safi, Morocco

183 codes, 40023 to 46322.

The GeoNames data lists 183 postal codes in Marrakesh-Safi, Morocco, covering 183 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 40023 to 46322.

The places with the most codes are Oukaimeden (1), Ait Ourir (1), Had Abadallah Rhiate (1), Abadou (1), Ait Aadel (1), Arbaa Tirhedouine (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kelaa-Des-Sraghna (51), Essaouira (36), Al-Haouz (30), Safi (30). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 30.98 and 32.71 and longitude -9.80 and -7.06, around a centre at about 31.73, -8.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
