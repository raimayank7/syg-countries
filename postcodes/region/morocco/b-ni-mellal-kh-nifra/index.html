Postal codes of Béni Mellal-Khénifra, Morocco

151 codes, 22000 to 54524.

The GeoNames data lists 151 postal codes in Béni Mellal-Khénifra, Morocco, covering 151 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 22000 to 54524.

The places with the most codes are Azilal (1), Zaouia Ahensal (1), Skatt (1), Afourer (1), Ait Ouarda (1), Ait Attab (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Beni-Mellal (44), Azilal Province (40), Khenifra (37), Khouribga Province (30). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 31.49 and 33.17 and longitude -7.28 and -4.75, around a centre at about 32.51, -6.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
