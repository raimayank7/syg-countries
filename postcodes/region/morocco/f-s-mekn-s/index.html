Postal codes of Fès-Meknès, Morocco

222 codes, 30023 to 53124.

The GeoNames data lists 222 postal codes in Fès-Meknès, Morocco, covering 222 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30023 to 53124.

The places with the most codes are Ain El Orma (1), Ain Kerma (1), Ait Aissa Addi (1), Oued Rommane (1), Ait Rahhou Mjatt (1), Zoualet (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Taza (62), Taounate (54), Boulemane (28), Meknes (22). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.83 and 34.76 and longitude -5.79 and -3.35, around a centre at about 34.05, -4.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
