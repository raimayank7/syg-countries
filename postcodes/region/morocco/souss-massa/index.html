Postal codes of Souss-Massa, Morocco

195 codes, 80022 to 87702.

The GeoNames data lists 195 postal codes in Souss-Massa, Morocco, covering 195 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80022 to 87702.

The places with the most codes are Taghazoute (1), Tamraght (1), Amskroud (1), Imouzzer Ida Ou Tanane (1), Aksri (1), Idmine (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Taroudannt (71), Tiznit (60), Chtouka-Ait-Baha (27), Tata (20). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 29.39 and 30.87 and longitude -9.92 and -6.74, around a centre at about 30.09, -8.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
