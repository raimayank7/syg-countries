Postal codes of Oriental, Morocco

89 codes, 60005 to 65823.

The GeoNames data lists 89 postal codes in Oriental, Morocco, covering 89 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60005 to 65823.

The places with the most codes are Ahfir (1), Arhbal (1), Ain Erreggada (1), Aklim (1), Madagh (1), Zayest (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Nador (37), Berkane (12), Figuig (12), Jerada (12). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 31.83 and 35.29 and longitude -3.58 and -1.73, around a centre at about 34.45, -2.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
