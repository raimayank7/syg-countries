Postal codes of Drâa-Tafilalet, Morocco

125 codes, 45000 to 52623.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Drâa-Tafilalet, Morocco, covering 125 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 45000 to 52623.

The places with the most codes are Errachidia (1), Asrir Oued Ferkla (1), Ait Othman (1), Chorfa Mdarhra (1), Kheng (1), Meski (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Errachidia (54), Ouarzazate (45), Zagora (26). Postal codes in Morocco are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 29.82 and 32.32 and longitude -7.60 and -3.44, around a centre at about 31.20, -5.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
