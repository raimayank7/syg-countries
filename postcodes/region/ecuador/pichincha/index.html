Postal codes of Pichincha, Ecuador

147 codes, 170101 to 171204.

The GeoNames data lists 147 postal codes in Pichincha, Ecuador, covering 80 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 170101 to 171204.

The places with the most codes are Calderon (Carapungo) (8), Cayambe (6), Sangolqui (6), Conocoto (5), Puengasi (4), Guamani (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Quito (116), Cantón Cayambe (9), Cantón Mejía (9), Cantón Rumiñahui (7). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.58 and 0.15 and longitude -79.21 and -77.98, around a centre at about -0.19, -78.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

8 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Quito (116); Cantón Cayambe (9); Cantón Mejía (9); Cantón Rumiñahui (7); Cantón Pedro Moncayo (2); Puerto Quito (2); San Miguel De Los Bancos (1); Canton Pedro Vicente Maldonado (1).
