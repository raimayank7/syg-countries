Postal codes of Morona Santiago, Ecuador

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -3.43 and -1.62 and longitude -78.80 and -77.26, around a centre at about -2.62, -78.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
