Postal codes of Cañar, Ecuador

24 codes, 030101 to 030304.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Cañar, Ecuador, covering 19 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 030101 to 030304.

The places with the most codes are Azogues (3), La Troncal (3), Gualleturo-San Antonio-Chorocopte (2), Luis Cordero (1), San Miguel (1), Cojitambo-Javier Loyola (Chuquipata) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Azogues (8), Cantón Cañar (6), La Troncal (4), Cantón Biblián (3). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -2.80 and -2.38 and longitude -79.42 and -78.69, around a centre at about -2.60, -78.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Azogues (8); Cantón Cañar (6); La Troncal (4); Cantón Biblián (3); Canton El Tambo (1); Déleg (1); Suscal (1).
