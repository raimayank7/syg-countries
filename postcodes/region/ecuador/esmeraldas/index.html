Postal codes of Esmeraldas, Ecuador

52 codes, 080101 to 080602.

The GeoNames data lists 52 postal codes in Esmeraldas, Ecuador, covering 27 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 080101 to 080602.

The places with the most codes are Esmeraldas (17), Rosa Zarate (Quininde) (7), Vuelta Larga (3), La Union (2), Tachina-Camarones (Cab. En San Vicente) (1), Chinca-Majua-San Mateo (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Esmeraldas (23), Cantón Quinindé (11), Cantón Eloy Alfaro (4), Cantón Muisne (4). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.04 and 1.27 and longitude -80.02 and -78.65, around a centre at about 0.76, -79.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Esmeraldas (23); Cantón Quinindé (11); Cantón Eloy Alfaro (4); Cantón Muisne (4); Cantón San Lorenzo (4); Atacames (4); Rio Verde (2).
