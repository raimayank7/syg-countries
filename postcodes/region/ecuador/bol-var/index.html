Postal codes of Bolívar, Ecuador

23 codes, 020101 to 020403.

The GeoNames data lists 23 postal codes in Bolívar, Ecuador, covering 18 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 020101 to 020403.

The places with the most codes are Guaranda (6), Santafe (Santa Fe)-Julio E. Moreno (Catanahuan Grande)-San Simon (Yacoto)-Sanlorenzo (1), Salinas (1), Simiatug (1), San Luis De Pambil (1), Facundo Vela (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Guaranda (11), Cantón San Miguel (4), Cantón Chimbo (3), Cantón Chillanes (2). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.96 and -1.20 and longitude -79.31 and -78.94, around a centre at about -1.58, -79.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

7 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Guaranda (11); Cantón San Miguel (4); Cantón Chimbo (3); Cantón Chillanes (2); Canton Echeandia (1); Canton Caluma (1); Canton Las Naves (1).
