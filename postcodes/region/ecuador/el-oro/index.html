Postal codes of El Oro, Ecuador

73 codes, 070101 to 070607.

The GeoNames data lists 73 postal codes in El Oro, Ecuador, covering 38 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 070101 to 070607.

The places with the most codes are Machala (23), Pasaje (6), Huaquillas (4), Santa Rosa (3), Arenillas (2), El Guabo (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Machala (24), Cantón Pasaje (11), Cantón Santa Rosa (7), Cantón Guabo (5). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -3.81 and -3.08 and longitude -80.23 and -79.47, around a centre at about -3.42, -79.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

14 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Machala (24); Cantón Pasaje (11); Cantón Santa Rosa (7); Cantón Guabo (5); Cantón Arenillas (4); Cantón Huaquillas (4); Cantón Piñas (4); Cantón Zaruma (4); Cantón Portovelo (3); Atahualpa (2); Las Lajas (2); Balsas (1).
