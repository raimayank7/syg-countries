Postal codes of Guayas, Ecuador

166 codes, 090101 to 092410.

The GeoNames data lists 166 postal codes in Guayas, Ecuador, covering 103 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 090101 to 092410.

The places with the most codes are Milagro (13), Eloy Alfaro (Duran) (10), Guayaquil-Monte Sinai (5), Balzar (5), Daule (4), Guayaquil-Alborada (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Guayaquil (70), Cantón Milagro (16), Durán (10), Cantón Daule (7). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -2.90 and -0.94 and longitude -80.48 and -79.27, around a centre at about -2.07, -79.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

25 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Guayaquil (70); Cantón Milagro (16); Durán (10); Cantón Daule (7); Cantón El Empalme (6); Cantón Naranjal (6); Cantón Balzar (5); Cantón Salitre (5); Cantón Yaguachi (5); Pedro Carbo (4); Cantón Samborondón (4); Canton Colimes (3).
