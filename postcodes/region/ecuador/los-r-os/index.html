Postal codes of Los Ríos, Ecuador

68 codes, 120101 to 120610.

The GeoNames data lists 68 postal codes in Los Ríos, Ecuador, covering 27 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 120101 to 120610.

The places with the most codes are Quevedo (9), Babahoyo (8), Vinces (7), Valencia (5), San Jacinto De Buena Fe (4), Pimocha (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Babahoyo (14), Cantón Quevedo (11), Cantón Vinces (9), Cantón Ventanas (6). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.83 and -0.57 and longitude -79.78 and -79.29, around a centre at about -1.40, -79.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

13 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Babahoyo (14); Cantón Quevedo (11); Cantón Vinces (9); Cantón Ventanas (6); Cantón Buena Fe (5); Cantón Valencia (5); Cantón Baba (4); Cantón Puebloviejo (3); Cantón Urdaneta (3); Mocache (3); Montalvo (2); Palenque (2).
