Postal codes of Sucumbíos, Ecuador

21 codes, 210101 to 210405.

The GeoNames data lists 21 postal codes in Sucumbíos, Ecuador, covering 17 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 210101 to 210405.

The places with the most codes are Nueva Loja (4), Shushufindi (2), Santa Cecilia-Jambeli (1), General Farfan (1), Dureno-Pacayacu (1), El Eno (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Lago Agrio (8), Shushufindi (5), Cantón Putumayo (3), Cuyabeno (2). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -0.42 and 0.40 and longitude -77.62 and -75.41, around a centre at about -0.02, -76.65 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
