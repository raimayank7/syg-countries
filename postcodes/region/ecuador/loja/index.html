Postal codes of Loja, Ecuador

72 codes, 110101 to 110906.

The GeoNames data lists 72 postal codes in Loja, Ecuador, covering 56 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 110101 to 110906.

The places with the most codes are Loja (15), Cariamanga (2), Catamayo (La Toma) (2), Malacatos (Valladolid) (1), Chantaco-Taquil (Miguel Riofrio)-Chiquiribamaba (1), San Lucas-Santiago (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Loja (22), Cantón Saraguro (6), Zapotillo (6), Cantón Calvas (5). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.60 and -3.41 and longitude -80.40 and -79.17, around a centre at about -4.07, -79.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Loja (22); Cantón Saraguro (6); Zapotillo (6); Cantón Calvas (5); Cantón Catamayo (5); Cantón Paltas (5); Cantón Espindola (3); Cantón Gonzanamá (3); Cantón Macará (3); Cantón Puyango (3); Cantón Celica (2); Chaguarpamba (2).
