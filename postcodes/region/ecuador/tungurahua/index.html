Postal codes of Tungurahua, Ecuador

41 codes, 180101 to 180605.

The GeoNames data lists 41 postal codes in Tungurahua, Ecuador, covering 26 named places and 9 sub-areas. In code order they run from 180101 to 180605.

The places with the most codes are Ambato (14), Pelileo (3), Ambatillo-San Bartolome De Pinllog (1), Constatino Fernandez (Cab En Cullit)-Augusto N. Martinez (Mundugleo)-Atahualpa (Chisalata) (1), Izamba (1), Pasa-San Fernando (Pasa San Fernando) (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Ambato (24), Cantón Pelileo (6), Cantón Píllaro (3), Cantón Baños (2). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.42 and -1.08 and longitude -78.75 and -78.25, around a centre at about -1.27, -78.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

9 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Ambato (24); Cantón Pelileo (6); Cantón Píllaro (3); Cantón Baños (2); Cantón Quero (2); Cevallos (1); Mocha (1); Cantón Patate (1); Tisaleo (1).
