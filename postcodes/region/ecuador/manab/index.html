Postal codes of Manabí, Ecuador

142 codes, 130101 to 131205.

The GeoNames data lists 142 postal codes in Manabí, Ecuador, covering 67 named places and 22 sub-areas. In code order they run from 130101 to 131205.

The places with the most codes are Manta (18), Portoviejo (16), Jipijapa (7), Chone (6), El Carmen (6), Montecristi (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Portoviejo (21), Cantón Manta (19), Cantón Chone (14), Cantón Jipijapa (12). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -1.69 and 0.37 and longitude -80.81 and -79.46, around a centre at about -0.90, -80.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

The ones with the most codes: Cantón Portoviejo (21); Cantón Manta (19); Cantón Chone (14); Cantón Jipijapa (12); Cantón El Carmen (8); Cantón Santa Ana (8); Cantón Montecristi (7); Canton Pedernales (7); Cantón Paján (6); Pichincha (6); Cantón Bolívar (5); Flavio Alfaro (5).
