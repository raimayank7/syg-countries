Postal codes of Carchi, Ecuador

24 codes, 040101 to 040304.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Carchi, Ecuador, covering 17 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 040101 to 040304.

The places with the most codes are Tulcan (7), San Gabriel (2), Urbina (Taya) (1), Julio Andrade (Orejuela)-El Carmelo (El Pun) (1), Pioter-Santa Martha De Cuba (1), Tufiño (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Cantón Tulcán (12), Cantón Montúfar (4), Canton Bolivar (3), Cantón Espejo (2). Postal codes in Ecuador are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.42 and 0.98 and longitude -78.31 and -77.72, around a centre at about 0.70, -77.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

6 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cantón Tulcán (12); Cantón Montúfar (4); Canton Bolivar (3); Cantón Espejo (2); Cantón Mira (2); San Pedro De Huaca (1).
