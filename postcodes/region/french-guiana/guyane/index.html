Postal codes of Guyane, French Guiana

77 codes, 97300 to 97399 CEDEX.

The GeoNames data lists 77 postal codes in Guyane, French Guiana, covering 22 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 97300 to 97399 CEDEX.

The places with the most codes are Cayenne (34), Kourou (17), Roura (2), Mana (2), Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni (2), Matoury (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Guyane (77). Postal codes in French Guiana are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 3.17 and 5.74 and longitude -54.38 and -51.67, around a centre at about 4.92, -52.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
