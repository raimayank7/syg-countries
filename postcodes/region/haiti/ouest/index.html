Postal codes of Ouest, Haiti

52 codes, HT3408 to HT6520.

The GeoNames data lists 52 postal codes in Ouest, Haiti, covering 58 named places. In code order they run from HT3408 to HT6520.

The places with the most codes are Puits Blain 38 (1), Port-au-Prince (1), Bel-Air et Bourdon (1), Turgeau (1), Bois Verna (1), Lalue (1). Postal codes in Haiti are written the fixed text "HT", then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 18.37 and 19.88 and longitude -72.93 and -71.86, around a centre at about 18.57, -72.41 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
