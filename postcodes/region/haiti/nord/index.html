Postal codes of Nord, Haiti

32 codes, HT1110 to HT1723.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in Nord, Haiti, covering 32 named places. In code order they run from HT1110 to HT1723.

The places with the most codes are Cap-Haïtien (la ville) (1), Bande-du-Nord (quartier du Cap) (1), Labadie (quartier du Cap) (1), Haut-du-Cap (quartier du Cap) (1), Petite-Anse (1), Quartier-Morin (1). Postal codes in Haiti are written the fixed text "HT", then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 19.34 and 19.85 and longitude -72.55 and -72.02, around a centre at about 19.66, -72.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
