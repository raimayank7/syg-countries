Postal codes of Comrat, Moldova

34 codes, MD-3801 to MD-6118.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Comrat, Moldova, covering 40 named places. In code order they run from MD-3801 to MD-6118.

The places with the most codes are Comrat 1 (1), Comrat 2 (1), Comrat 5 (1), Besalma (1), Bugeac (1), Chirsova (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.52 and 46.42 and longitude 28.38 and 28.94, around a centre at about 46.10, 28.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
