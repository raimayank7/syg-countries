Postal codes of Cantemir, Moldova

33 codes, MD-7301 to MD-7344.

The GeoNames data lists 33 postal codes in Cantemir, Moldova, covering 49 named places. In code order they run from MD-7301 to MD-7344.

The places with the most codes are Taraclia (2), Cantemir 1 (1), Acui (1), Leca (1), Antonesti (1), Baimaclia (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.11 and 46.38 and longitude 28.16 and 28.51, around a centre at about 46.24, 28.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
