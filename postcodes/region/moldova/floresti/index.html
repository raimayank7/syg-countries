Postal codes of Floresti, Moldova

45 codes, MD-5001 to MD-6654.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Floresti, Moldova, covering 74 named places. In code order they run from MD-5001 to MD-6654.

The places with the most codes are Nicolaevca (2), Floresti 1 (1), Floresti 2 (1), Lunga (1), Marculesti(Oras) (1), Alexeevca (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.71 and 48.16 and longitude 27.88 and 28.74, around a centre at about 47.89, 28.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
