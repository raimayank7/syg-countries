Postal codes of Nisporeni, Moldova

28 codes, MD-6401 to MD-6442.

The GeoNames data lists 28 postal codes in Nisporeni, Moldova, covering 40 named places. In code order they run from MD-6401 to MD-6442.

The places with the most codes are Nisporeni (1), Bacseni (1), Balanesti (1), Balauresti (1), Barboieni (1), Bolduresti (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.94 and 47.22 and longitude 27.98 and 28.33, around a centre at about 47.08, 28.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
