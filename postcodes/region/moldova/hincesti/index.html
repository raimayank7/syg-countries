Postal codes of Hincesti, Moldova

45 codes, MD-3401 to MD-6426.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Hincesti, Moldova, covering 65 named places. In code order they run from MD-3401 to MD-6426.

The places with the most codes are Hincesti (1), Anini (1), Rusca (1), Balceana (1), Bobeica (1), Dahnovici (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.65 and 47.06 and longitude 28.12 and 28.69, around a centre at about 46.85, 28.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
