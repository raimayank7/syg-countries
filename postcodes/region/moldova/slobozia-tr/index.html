Postal codes of Slobozia Tr., Moldova

20 codes, MD-5701 to MD-5728.

The GeoNames data lists 20 postal codes in Slobozia Tr., Moldova, covering 28 named places. In code order they run from MD-5701 to MD-5728.

The places with the most codes are Slobozia 1 (1), Slobozia 2 (1), Crasnoe (1), Andriasevca Veche (1), Andriasevca Noua (1), Blijnii Hutor (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.61 and 46.88 and longitude 29.51 and 29.97, around a centre at about 46.77, 29.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
