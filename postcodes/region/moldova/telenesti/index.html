Postal codes of Telenesti, Moldova

34 codes, MD-5801 to MD-5841.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Telenesti, Moldova, covering 57 named places. In code order they run from MD-5801 to MD-5841.

The places with the most codes are Telenesti (1), Izvoras (1), Mihalasa (1), Mihalasa Noua (1), Budai (1), Banesti (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.42 and 47.71 and longitude 28.17 and 28.66, around a centre at about 47.55, 28.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
