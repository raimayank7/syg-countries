Postal codes of Calarasi, Moldova

36 codes, MD-3631 to MD-4442.

The GeoNames data lists 36 postal codes in Calarasi, Moldova, covering 49 named places. In code order they run from MD-3631 to MD-4442.

The places with the most codes are Veverita (1), Tuzara (1), Selistea Noua (1), Calarasi 1 (1), Calarasi 2 (1), Oricova (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.98 and 47.42 and longitude 28.07 and 28.82, around a centre at about 47.29, 28.28 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
