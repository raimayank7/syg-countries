Postal codes of Criuleni, Moldova

29 codes, MD-4801 to MD-4841.

The GeoNames data lists 29 postal codes in Criuleni, Moldova, covering 43 named places. In code order they run from MD-4801 to MD-4841.

The places with the most codes are Criuleni (1), Zolonceni (1), Malaiesti (1), Balabanesti (1), Malaiestii Noi (1), Baltata De Sus (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.01 and 47.29 and longitude 28.78 and 29.20, around a centre at about 47.15, 28.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
