Postal codes of Cahul, Moldova

43 codes, MD-3901 to MD-7415.

The GeoNames data lists 43 postal codes in Cahul, Moldova, covering 59 named places. In code order they run from MD-3901 to MD-7415.

The places with the most codes are Cahul 1 (1), Cahul 5 (1), Cahul 7 (1), Cahul 9 (1), Andrusul De Jos (1), Andrusul De Sus (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.48 and 46.14 and longitude 28.16 and 28.52, around a centre at about 45.92, 28.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
