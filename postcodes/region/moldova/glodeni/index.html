Postal codes of Glodeni, Moldova

23 codes, MD-4901 to MD-4934.

The GeoNames data lists 23 postal codes in Glodeni, Moldova, covering 34 named places. In code order they run from MD-4901 to MD-4934.

The places with the most codes are Soroca (1), Stircea (1), Glodeni (1), Lipovat (1), Balatina (1), Clococenii Vechi (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.62 and 47.84 and longitude 27.32 and 27.72, around a centre at about 47.73, 27.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
