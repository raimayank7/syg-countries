Postal codes of Falesti, Moldova

45 codes, MD-5901 to MD-5951.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Falesti, Moldova, covering 78 named places. In code order they run from MD-5901 to MD-5951.

The places with the most codes are Falesti 1 (1), Falesti 2 (1), Falesti 3 (1), Falesti St (1), Rediul De Jos (1), Rediul De Sus (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.39 and 47.74 and longitude 27.45 and 27.98, around a centre at about 47.56, 27.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
