Postal codes of Anenii Noi, Moldova

31 codes, MD-6501 to MD-7724.

The GeoNames data lists 31 postal codes in Anenii Noi, Moldova, covering 46 named places. In code order they run from MD-6501 to MD-7724.

The places with the most codes are Anenii Noi (1), Beriozchi (1), Hirbovatul Nou (1), Botnaresti (1), Salcia (1), Botnarestii Noi (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.
