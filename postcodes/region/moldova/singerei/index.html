Postal codes of Singerei, Moldova

37 codes, MD-5020 to MD-6243.

The GeoNames data lists 37 postal codes in Singerei, Moldova, covering 69 named places. In code order they run from MD-5020 to MD-6243.

The places with the most codes are Marasesti (1), Singerei (1), Biruinta (1), Vranesti (1), Alexandreni (1), Heciul Vechi (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.47 and 47.87 and longitude 27.91 and 28.38, around a centre at about 47.67, 28.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
