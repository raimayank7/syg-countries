Postal codes of Edinet, Moldova

37 codes, MD-4601 to MD-7115.

The GeoNames data lists 37 postal codes in Edinet, Moldova, covering 50 named places. In code order they run from MD-4601 to MD-7115.

The places with the most codes are Edinet (1), Alexeevca (1), Alexandreni (1), Badragii Vechi (1), Badragii Noi (1), Volodeni (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.97 and 48.31 and longitude 27.04 and 27.50, around a centre at about 48.14, 27.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
