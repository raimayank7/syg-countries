Postal codes of Ungheni, Moldova

48 codes, MD-3601 to MD-3652.

The GeoNames data lists 48 postal codes in Ungheni, Moldova, covering 76 named places. In code order they run from MD-3601 to MD-3652.

The places with the most codes are Ungheni-Gara (1), Ungheni 2 (1), Ungheni 3 (1), Ungheni 4 (1), Novaia Nicolaevca (1), Ungheni 6 (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.03 and 47.46 and longitude 27.60 and 28.17, around a centre at about 47.30, 27.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
