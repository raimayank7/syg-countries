Postal codes of Soroca, Moldova

53 codes, MD-3004 to MD-6647.

The GeoNames data lists 53 postal codes in Soroca, Moldova, covering 70 named places. In code order they run from MD-3004 to MD-6647.

The places with the most codes are Soroca 4 (1), Soroca 5 (1), Soroca 6 (1), Badiceni (1), Baxani (1), Balintii Noi (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.00 and 48.33 and longitude 27.90 and 28.61, around a centre at about 48.16, 28.19 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
