Postal codes of Dubasari Tr., Moldova

15 codes, MD-4501 to MD-4524.

The GeoNames data lists 15 postal codes in Dubasari Tr., Moldova, covering 25 named places. In code order they run from MD-4501 to MD-4524.

The places with the most codes are Dubasari 1 (1), Mahala (1), Dubasari 2 (1), Coicova (1), Comisarovca Noua (1), Cosnita Noua (1). Postal codes in Moldova are written the fixed text "MD", then a hyphen, then four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.22 and 47.76 and longitude 27.93 and 29.38, around a centre at about 47.35, 29.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
