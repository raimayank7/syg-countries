Postal codes of Centre-Val de Loire, France

847 codes, 18000 to 45980 CEDEX 9.

The GeoNames data lists 847 postal codes in Centre-Val de Loire, France, covering 1,832 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 18000 to 45980 CEDEX 9.

The places with the most codes are Orléans (126), Tours (80), Blois (75), Bourges (45), Chartres (39), Châteauroux (34). The sub-areas with most codes are Loiret (540), Eure-et-Loir (497), Indre-et-Loire (397), Loir-et-Cher (392). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.37 and 48.92 and longitude 0.07 and 3.07, around a centre at about 47.62, 1.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
