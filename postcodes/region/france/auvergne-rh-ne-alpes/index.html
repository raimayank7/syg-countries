Postal codes of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France

2,397 codes, 01000 to 74999 CEDEX 9.

The GeoNames data lists 2,397 postal codes in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France, covering 4,110 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 01000 to 74999 CEDEX 9.

The places with the most codes are Lyon (258), Grenoble (68), Clermont-Ferrand (66), Saint-Étienne (61), Annecy (45), Valence (44). The sub-areas with most codes are Isère (788), Rhône (769), Puy-de-Dôme (578), Ain (539). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.14 and 46.77 and longitude 2.13 and 7.05, around a centre at about 45.56, 4.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
