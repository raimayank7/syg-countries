Postal codes of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France

1,825 codes, 16000 to 87950 CEDEX 9.

The GeoNames data lists 1,825 postal codes in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France, covering 4,366 named places and 12 sub-areas. In code order they run from 16000 to 87950 CEDEX 9.

The places with the most codes are Bordeaux (127), Niort (83), Limoges (81), Poitiers (73), Pau (66), La Rochelle (64). The sub-areas with most codes are Gironde (807), Pyrénées-Atlantiques (684), Dordogne (629), Charente-Maritime (614). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.87 and 47.14 and longitude -1.77 and 2.56, around a centre at about 45.15, 0.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
