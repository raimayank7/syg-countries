Postal codes of Normandie, France

990 codes, 14000 to 76980.

The GeoNames data lists 990 postal codes in Normandie, France, covering 3,241 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 14000 to 76980.

The places with the most codes are Caen (104), Rouen (75), Évreux (51), Le Havre (42), Alençon (26), Saint-Lô (21). The sub-areas with most codes are Seine-Maritime (962), Calvados (891), Eure (816), Manche (714). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.23 and 50.06 and longitude -1.93 and 1.78, around a centre at about 49.18, 0.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
