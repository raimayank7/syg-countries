Postal codes of Grand Est, France

1,846 codes, 02160 to 88809 CEDEX.

The GeoNames data lists 1,846 postal codes in Grand Est, France, covering 5,149 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 02160 to 88809 CEDEX.

The places with the most codes are Strasbourg (131), Reims (73), Metz (71), Nancy (61), Mulhouse (48), Troyes (38). The sub-areas with most codes are Moselle (946), Bas-Rhin (807), Marne (755), Meurthe-et-Moselle (754). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.42 and 50.14 and longitude 3.41 and 8.18, around a centre at about 48.70, 5.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
