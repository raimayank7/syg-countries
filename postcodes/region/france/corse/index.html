Postal codes of Corse, France

185 codes, 20000 to 20900 CEDEX 9.

The GeoNames data lists 185 postal codes in Corse, France, covering 362 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20000 to 20900 CEDEX 9.

The places with the most codes are Ajaccio (44), Bastia (33), Porto-Vecchio (3), Coggia Maio (2), Vico (2), Cognocoli-Monticchi (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Haute-Corse (274), Corse-du-Sud (177). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 41.39 and 42.98 and longitude 8.60 and 9.52, around a centre at about 42.25, 9.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

2 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Haute-Corse (274); Corse-du-Sud (177).
