Postal codes of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France

1,576 codes, 04000 to 84977 CEDEX.

The GeoNames data lists 1,576 postal codes in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France, covering 980 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 04000 to 84977 CEDEX.

The places with the most codes are Marseille (244), Nice (105), Avignon (77), Aix-en-Provence (66), Toulon (57), Vitrolles (26). The sub-areas with most codes are Bouches-du-Rhône (665), Alpes-Maritimes (420), Vaucluse (331), Var (327). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.08 and 45.05 and longitude 4.43 and 7.62, around a centre at about 43.74, 5.91 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
