Postal codes of Pays de la Loire, France

1,055 codes, 44000 to 85934 CEDEX 9.

The GeoNames data lists 1,055 postal codes in Pays de la Loire, France, covering 1,531 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 44000 to 85934 CEDEX 9.

The places with the most codes are Nantes (176), Le Mans (73), Angers (64), Laval (49), La Roche-sur-Yon (40), Saint-Herblain (24). The sub-areas with most codes are Maine-et-Loire (561), Loire-Atlantique (550), Sarthe (483), Vendée (410). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.32 and 48.54 and longitude -2.55 and 0.83, around a centre at about 47.47, -0.80 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
