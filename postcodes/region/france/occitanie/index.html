Postal codes of Occitanie, France

1,759 codes, 09000 to 82800.

The GeoNames data lists 1,759 postal codes in Occitanie, France, covering 4,379 named places and 13 sub-areas. In code order they run from 09000 to 82800.

The places with the most codes are Toulouse (148), Montpellier (142), Nîmes (76), Perpignan (68), Carcassonne (44), Tarbes (40). The sub-areas with most codes are Haute-Garonne (838), Hérault (590), Hautes-Pyrénées (553), Aude (517). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 42.36 and 45.02 and longitude -0.29 and 4.79, around a centre at about 43.60, 2.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
