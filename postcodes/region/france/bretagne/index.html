Postal codes of Bretagne, France

934 codes, 22000 to 56998 CEDEX.

The GeoNames data lists 934 postal codes in Bretagne, France, covering 1,270 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 22000 to 56998 CEDEX.

The places with the most codes are Rennes (128), Quimper (45), Saint-Brieuc (41), Brest (36), Vannes (36), Lorient (24). The sub-areas with most codes are Ille-et-Vilaine (592), Côtes-d'Armor (465), Finistère (427), Morbihan (407). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.29 and 48.85 and longitude -5.09 and -1.04, around a centre at about 48.19, -2.76 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
