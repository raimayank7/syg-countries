Postal codes of Île-de-France, France

4,361 codes, 75000 to 95978 CEDEX 2.

The GeoNames data lists 4,361 postal codes in Île-de-France, France, covering 1,307 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 75000 to 95978 CEDEX 2.

The places with the most codes are Cergy-Pontoise (135), Paris La Défense (119), Rungis (97), Créteil (86), Marne-la-Vallée (82), Roissy Charles-de-Gaulle (79). The sub-areas with most codes are Paris (866), Seine-et-Marne (865), Hauts-de-Seine (687), Val-de-Marne (603). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.14 and 49.21 and longitude 1.48 and 3.48, around a centre at about 48.81, 2.37 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
