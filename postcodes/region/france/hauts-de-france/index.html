Postal codes of Hauts-de-France, France

1,649 codes, 02000 to 80980.

The GeoNames data lists 1,649 postal codes in Hauts-de-France, France, covering 3,779 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 02000 to 80980.

The places with the most codes are Lille (111), Amiens (81), Arras (56), Roubaix (39), Creil (35), Beauvais (29). The sub-areas with most codes are Pas-de-Calais (1100), Nord (1089), Somme (899), Aisne (889). Postal codes in France are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 48.86 and 51.08 and longitude 1.39 and 4.23, around a centre at about 50.01, 2.77 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
