Postal codes of Caldas, Colombia

87 codes, 170001 to 178057.

The GeoNames data lists 87 postal codes in Caldas, Colombia, covering 27 named places and 27 sub-areas. In code order they run from 170001 to 178057.

The places with the most codes are Manizales (9), Riosucio (5), Aguadas (4), Anserma (4), La Dorada (4), Pensilvania (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Manizales (9), Riosucio (5), Aguadas (4), Anserma (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.98 and 5.61 and longitude -75.87 and -74.66, around a centre at about 5.28, -75.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

27 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Manizales (9); Riosucio (5); Aguadas (4); Anserma (4); La Dorada (4); Pensilvania (4); Salamina (4); Samaná (4); Chinchiná (3); Filadelfia (3); Manzanares (3); Marquetalia (3).
