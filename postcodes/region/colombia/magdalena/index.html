Postal codes of Magdalena, Colombia

96 codes, 470001 to 478067.

The GeoNames data lists 96 postal codes in Magdalena, Colombia, covering 30 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 470001 to 478067.

The places with the most codes are Santa Marta (10), Zona Bananera (5), Ariguani (4), Cienaga (4), El Banco (4), Guamal (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Santa Marta (10), Zona Bananera (5), Ariguaní (4), Ciénaga (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 9.00 and 11.24 and longitude -74.92 and -73.98, around a centre at about 10.22, -74.41 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

30 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Santa Marta (10); Zona Bananera (5); Ariguaní (4); Ciénaga (4); El Banco (4); Guamal (4); Sitionuevo (4); Aracataca (3); Chibolo (3); El Piñon (3); Fundación (3); Nueva Granada (3).
