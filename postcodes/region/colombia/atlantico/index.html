Postal codes of Atlantico, Colombia

76 codes, 080001 to 085068.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Atlantico, Colombia, covering 23 named places and 23 sub-areas. In code order they run from 080001 to 085068.

The places with the most codes are Barranquilla (15), Soledad (8), Malambo (4), Sabanalarga (4), Baranoa (3), Juan De Acosta (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Barranquilla (15), Soledad (8), Malambo (4), Sabanalarga (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 10.32 and 10.99 and longitude -75.15 and -74.75, around a centre at about 10.77, -74.88 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

23 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Barranquilla (15); Soledad (8); Malambo (4); Sabanalarga (4); Baranoa (3); Juan de Acosta (3); Luruaco (3); Ponedera (3); Puerto Colombia (3); Repelón (3); Tubará (3); Campo de La Cruz (2).
