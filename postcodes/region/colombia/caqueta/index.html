Postal codes of Caqueta, Colombia

61 codes, 180001 to 186078.

The GeoNames data lists 61 postal codes in Caqueta, Colombia, covering 16 named places and 16 sub-areas. In code order they run from 180001 to 186078.

The places with the most codes are Florencia (6), Cartagena Del Chaira (5), La Montañita (5), Curillo (4), El Doncello (4), El Paujil (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Florencia (6), Cartagena del Chairá (5), La Montañita (5), Curillo (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.70 and 2.12 and longitude -75.97 and -74.77, around a centre at about 1.42, -75.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

16 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Florencia (6); Cartagena del Chairá (5); La Montañita (5); Curillo (4); El Doncello (4); El Paujil (4); Puerto Rico (4); San Vicente del Caguán (4); Solano (4); Albania (3); Belén de Los Andaquies (3); Milán (3).
