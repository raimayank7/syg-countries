Postal codes of Antioquia, Colombia

384 codes, 050001 to 057878.

The GeoNames data lists 384 postal codes in Antioquia, Colombia, covering 125 named places and 125 sub-areas. In code order they run from 050001 to 057878.

The places with the most codes are Medellin (41), Bello (8), Envigado (5), Itagüi (5), Arboletes (4), Ituango (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Medellín (41), Bello (8), Envigado (5), Itagui (5). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.55 and 8.85 and longitude -76.89 and -74.40, around a centre at about 6.53, -75.58 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

125 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Medellín (41); Bello (8); Envigado (5); Itagui (5); Arboletes (4); Ituango (4); San Pedro de Uraba (4); Sonson (4); Turbo (4); Abejorral (3); Amagá (3); Amalfi (3).

Other regions of Colombia

Boyaca; Cundinamarca; Santander; Nariño; Cauca; Valle Del Cauca; Tolima; Bolivar.
