Postal codes of Cundinamarca, Colombia

295 codes, 250001 to 254057.

The GeoNames data lists 295 postal codes in Cundinamarca, Colombia, covering 116 named places and 116 sub-areas. In code order they run from 250001 to 254057.

The places with the most codes are Soacha (7), Fusagasuga (5), Chia (4), Facatativa (4), Guaduas (4), Zipaquira (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Soacha (7), Fusagasugá (5), Chía (4), Facatativá (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 3.99 and 5.50 and longitude -74.83 and -73.22, around a centre at about 4.82, -74.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

116 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Soacha (7); Fusagasugá (5); Chía (4); Facatativá (4); Guaduas (4); Zipaquirá (4); Anapoima (3); Anolaima (3); Arbeláez (3); Caparrapí (3); Carmen de Carupa (3); Chipaque (3).
