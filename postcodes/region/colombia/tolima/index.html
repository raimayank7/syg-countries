Postal codes of Tolima, Colombia

150 codes, 730001 to 735589.

The GeoNames data lists 150 postal codes in Tolima, Colombia, covering 47 named places and 47 sub-areas. In code order they run from 730001 to 735589.

The places with the most codes are Ibague (13), Guamo (6), Coyaima (5), Ortega (5), Chaparral (4), Espinal (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Ibagué (13), Guamo (6), Coyaima (5), Ortega (5). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 3.20 and 5.21 and longitude -75.68 and -74.53, around a centre at about 4.30, -75.07 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

47 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Ibagué (13); Guamo (6); Coyaima (5); Ortega (5); Chaparral (4); Espinal (4); Purificación (4); Rioblanco (4); Alvarado (3); Anzoátegui (3); Armero (3); Ataco (3).
