Postal codes of Casanare, Colombia

67 codes, 850001 to 856058.

The GeoNames data lists 67 postal codes in Casanare, Colombia, covering 19 named places and 19 sub-areas. In code order they run from 850001 to 856058.

The places with the most codes are Yopal (5), Aguazul (4), Hato Corozal (4), Mani (4), Orocue (4), Paz De Ariporo (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Yopal (5), Aguazul (4), Hato Corozal (4), Maní (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.56 and 6.13 and longitude -73.04 and -71.14, around a centre at about 5.34, -72.24 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

19 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Yopal (5); Aguazul (4); Hato Corozal (4); Maní (4); Orocué (4); Paz de Ariporo (4); San Luis de Palenque (4); Tauramena (4); Trinidad (4); Villanueva (4); Chameza (3); Monterrey (3).
