Postal codes of Amazonas, Colombia

32 codes, 910001 to 917018.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in Amazonas, Colombia, covering 11 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 910001 to 917018.

The places with the most codes are El Encanto (4), Puerto Arica (4), Puerto Santander (4), Tarapaca (4), Leticia (3), La Pedrera (3). The sub-areas with most codes are El Encanto (4), Puerto Arica (4), Puerto Santander (4), Tarapacá (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -4.22 and 0.06 and longitude -74.03 and -69.57, around a centre at about -1.95, -71.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

11 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: El Encanto (4); Puerto Arica (4); Puerto Santander (4); Tarapacá (4); Leticia (3); La Pedrera (3); Puerto Nariño (3); La Chorrera (2); Miriti - Paraná (2); Puerto Alegría (2); La Victoria (1).
