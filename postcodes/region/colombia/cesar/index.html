Postal codes of Cesar, Colombia

75 codes, 200001 to 205078.

The GeoNames data lists 75 postal codes in Cesar, Colombia, covering 25 named places and 25 sub-areas. In code order they run from 200001 to 205078.

The places with the most codes are Valledupar (10), San Martin (4), Aguachica (3), Agustin Codazzi (3), Astrea (3), Chimichagua (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Valledupar (10), San Martín (4), Aguachica (3), Agustín Codazzi (3). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.76 and 10.46 and longitude -73.98 and -72.99, around a centre at about 9.39, -73.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

25 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Valledupar (10); San Martín (4); Aguachica (3); Agustín Codazzi (3); Astrea (3); Chimichagua (3); Chiriguaná (3); El Copey (3); El Paso (3); Gamarra (3); La Gloria (3); Pueblo Bello (3).
