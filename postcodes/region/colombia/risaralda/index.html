Postal codes of Risaralda, Colombia

47 codes, 660001 to 664048.

The GeoNames data lists 47 postal codes in Risaralda, Colombia, covering 14 named places and 14 sub-areas. In code order they run from 660001 to 664048.

The places with the most codes are Pereira (10), Dosquebradas (4), Apia (3), Belen De Umbria (3), Guatica (3), Marsella (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Pereira (10), Dosquebradas (4), Apía (3), Belén de Umbría (3). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.81 and 5.34 and longitude -76.04 and -75.62, around a centre at about 5.03, -75.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

14 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Pereira (10); Dosquebradas (4); Apía (3); Belén de Umbría (3); Guática (3); Marsella (3); Mistrató (3); Pueblo Rico (3); Quinchía (3); Santa Rosa de Cabal (3); Santuario (3); Balboa (2).
