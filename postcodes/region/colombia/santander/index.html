Postal codes of Santander, Colombia

284 codes, 680001 to 687587.

The GeoNames data lists 284 postal codes in Santander, Colombia, covering 87 named places and 87 sub-areas. In code order they run from 680001 to 687587.

The places with the most codes are Bucaramanga (9), Barrancabermeja (7), Giron (7), Cimitarra (6), Floridablanca (6), Piedecuesta (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Bucaramanga (9), Barrancabermeja (7), Girón (7), Cimitarra (6). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.76 and 7.47 and longitude -74.06 and -72.59, around a centre at about 6.61, -73.26 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

87 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Bucaramanga (9); Barrancabermeja (7); Girón (7); Cimitarra (6); Floridablanca (6); Piedecuesta (6); El Carmen de Chucurí (5); Lebríja (5); Oiba (5); Onzaga (5); Rionegro (5); San Vicente de Chucurí (5).
