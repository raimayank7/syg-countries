Postal codes of Meta, Colombia

98 codes, 500001 to 507058.

The GeoNames data lists 98 postal codes in Meta, Colombia, covering 29 named places and 29 sub-areas. In code order they run from 500001 to 507058.

The places with the most codes are Villavicencio (9), Puerto Gaitan (7), Acacias (5), Mapiripan (5), San Martin (5), Puerto Lopez (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Villavicencio (9), Puerto Gaitán (7), Acacías (5), Mapiripán (5). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 2.18 and 4.57 and longitude -74.44 and -72.08, around a centre at about 3.67, -73.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

29 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Villavicencio (9); Puerto Gaitán (7); Acacías (5); Mapiripán (5); San Martín (5); Puerto López (4); Cabuyaro (3); Castilla la Nueva (3); Cubarral (3); Cumaral (3); El Castillo (3); Fuente de Oro (3).

Other regions of Colombia

Antioquia; Boyaca; Cundinamarca; Santander; Nariño; Cauca; Valle Del Cauca; Tolima.
