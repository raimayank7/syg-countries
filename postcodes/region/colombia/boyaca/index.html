Postal codes of Boyaca, Colombia

304 codes, 150001 to 155219.

The GeoNames data lists 304 postal codes in Boyaca, Colombia, covering 123 named places and 123 sub-areas. In code order they run from 150001 to 155219.

The places with the most codes are Puerto Boyaca (7), Tunja (6), Duitama (6), Saboya (5), Sogamoso (5), Aquitania (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Puerto Boyacá (7), Tunja (6), Duitama (6), Saboyá (5). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 4.82 and 7.00 and longitude -74.59 and -72.11, around a centre at about 5.67, -73.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

123 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Puerto Boyacá (7); Tunja (6); Duitama (6); Saboyá (5); Sogamoso (5); Aquitania (4); Chita (4); Moniquirá (4); Paipa (4); Pesca (4); Ráquira (4); Ventaquemada (4).
