Postal codes of Nariño, Colombia

241 codes, 520001 to 528568.

The GeoNames data lists 241 postal codes in Nariño, Colombia, covering 64 named places and 64 sub-areas. In code order they run from 520001 to 528568.

The places with the most codes are Pasto (18), San Andres De Tumaco (15), Ipiales (7), Barbacoas (6), Buesaco (6), Samaniego (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Pasto (18), San Andres de Tumaco (15), Ipiales (7), Barbacoas (6). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 0.81 and 2.51 and longitude -78.82 and -76.92, around a centre at about 1.43, -77.64 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

64 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Pasto (18); San Andres de Tumaco (15); Ipiales (7); Barbacoas (6); Buesaco (6); Samaniego (6); El Charco (5); Magüi (5); Ricaurte (5); Roberto Payán (5); Túquerres (5); Córdoba (4).
