Postal codes of Cauca, Colombia

180 codes, 190001 to 196068.

The GeoNames data lists 180 postal codes in Cauca, Colombia, covering 42 named places and 42 sub-areas. In code order they run from 190001 to 196068.

The places with the most codes are Popayan (9), El Tambo (8), Bolivar (7), Santander De Quilichao (7), Cajibio (6), Inza (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Popayán (9), El Tambo (8), Bolívar (7), Santander de Quilichao (7). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 1.12 and 3.25 and longitude -77.89 and -75.99, around a centre at about 2.49, -76.66 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

42 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Popayán (9); El Tambo (8); Bolívar (7); Santander de Quilichao (7); Cajibío (6); Inzá (6); La Vega (6); Paez (6); Piendamó (6); Balboa (5); Buenos Aires (5); Caldono (5).
