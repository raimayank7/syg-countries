Postal codes of La Guajira, Colombia

64 codes, 440001 to 446017.

The GeoNames data lists 64 postal codes in La Guajira, Colombia, covering 15 named places and 15 sub-areas. In code order they run from 440001 to 446017.

The places with the most codes are Uribia (13), Riohacha (7), Manaure (6), Dibulla (5), Albania (4), Barrancas (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Uribia (13), Riohacha (7), Manaure (6), Dibulla (5). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 10.52 and 11.78 and longitude -73.31 and -72.24, around a centre at about 11.25, -72.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

15 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Uribia (13); Riohacha (7); Manaure (6); Dibulla (5); Albania (4); Barrancas (4); Maicao (4); Distracción (3); Fonseca (3); Hatonuevo (3); San Juan del Cesar (3); Villanueva (3).
