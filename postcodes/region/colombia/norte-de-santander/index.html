Postal codes of Norte De Santander, Colombia

125 codes, 540001 to 548058.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Norte De Santander, Colombia, covering 40 named places and 40 sub-areas. In code order they run from 540001 to 548058.

The places with the most codes are Cucuta (14), Ocaña (5), Abrego (4), Convencion (4), Cucutilla (4), El Carmen (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Cúcuta (14), Ocaña (5), Abrego (4), Convención (4). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.14 and 8.76 and longitude -73.45 and -72.41, around a centre at about 7.89, -72.83 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

40 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Cúcuta (14); Ocaña (5); Abrego (4); Convención (4); Cucutilla (4); El Carmen (4); Sardinata (4); Teorama (4); Arboledas (3); Bochalema (3); Bucarasica (3); Cachirá (3).
