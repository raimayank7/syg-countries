Postal codes of Cordoba, Colombia

125 codes, 230001 to 235048.

The GeoNames data lists 125 postal codes in Cordoba, Colombia, covering 30 named places and 30 sub-areas. In code order they run from 230001 to 235048.

The places with the most codes are Monteria (13), Lorica (9), Sahagun (7), Cienaga De Oro (6), San Pelayo (6), Tierralta (6). The sub-areas with most codes are Montería (13), Lorica (9), Sahagún (7), Ciénaga de Oro (6). Postal codes in Colombia are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.71 and 9.37 and longitude -76.35 and -75.14, around a centre at about 8.76, -75.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Sub-areas

30 sub-areas are named in the data. The ones with the most codes: Montería (13); Lorica (9); Sahagún (7); Ciénaga de Oro (6); San Pelayo (6); Tierralta (6); Planeta Rica (5); San Carlos (5); Ayapel (4); Cereté (4); Chinú (4); Pueblo Nuevo (4).

Other regions of Colombia

Antioquia; Boyaca; Cundinamarca; Santander; Nariño; Cauca; Valle Del Cauca; Tolima.
