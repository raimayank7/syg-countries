Postal codes of Hovd, Mongolia

18 codes, 84010 to 84211.

The GeoNames data lists 18 postal codes in Hovd, Mongolia, covering 17 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 84010 to 84211.

The places with the most codes are Bürenhayrhan (2), Dörgön (1), Erdenebüren (1), Buyant (1), Bayanhoshuu (1), Jargalant (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Bulgan District (2), Dörgön (1), Erdenebüren (1), Buyant (1). Postal codes in Mongolia are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.80 and 48.48 and longitude 91.43 and 93.62, around a centre at about 47.16, 92.21 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
