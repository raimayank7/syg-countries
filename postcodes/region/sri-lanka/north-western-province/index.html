Postal codes of North Western Province, Sri Lanka

282 codes, 60000 to 61534.

The GeoNames data lists 282 postal codes in North Western Province, Sri Lanka, covering 282 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60000 to 61534.

The places with the most codes are Kurunegala (1), Kudagalgamuwa (1), Minhettiya (1), Doratiyawa (1), Indulgodakanda (1), Demataluwa (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kurunegala (201), Puttalam (81). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.18 and 8.24 and longitude 79.74 and 80.60, around a centre at about 7.65, 80.13 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
