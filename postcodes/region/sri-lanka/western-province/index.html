Postal codes of Western Province, Sri Lanka

263 codes, 00100 to 12580.

The GeoNames data lists 263 postal codes in Western Province, Sri Lanka, covering 266 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00100 to 12580.

The places with the most codes are Fort (1), Slave lsland (1), Slave Island (1), Colpetty (1), Bambalapitiya (1), Havelock Town (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Gampaha (126), Kalutara (81), Colombo (59). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 6.36 and 7.50 and longitude 79.82 and 80.53, around a centre at about 6.90, 80.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
