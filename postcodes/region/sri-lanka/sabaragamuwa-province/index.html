Postal codes of Sabaragamuwa Province, Sri Lanka

219 codes, 70000 to 71724.

The GeoNames data lists 219 postal codes in Sabaragamuwa Province, Sri Lanka, covering 219 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 70000 to 71724.

The places with the most codes are Kegalle (1), Deldeniya (1), Hiriwadunna (1), Molagoda (1), Deewala Pallegama (1), Yattogoda (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Ratnapura (118), Kegalle (101). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 6.27 and 7.49 and longitude 79.91 and 80.90, around a centre at about 6.87, 80.43 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
