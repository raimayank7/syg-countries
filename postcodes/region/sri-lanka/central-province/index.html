Postal codes of Central Province, Sri Lanka

305 codes, 20000 to 22287.

The GeoNames data lists 305 postal codes in Central Province, Sri Lanka, covering 305 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20000 to 22287.

The places with the most codes are Kandy (1), Halloluwa (1), Yatihalagala (1), lhala Kobbekaduwa (1), Sangarajapura (1), Kulugammana (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Kandy (164), Matale (71), Nuwara Eliya (70). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 6.80 and 7.95 and longitude 80.08 and 81.00, around a centre at about 7.28, 80.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
