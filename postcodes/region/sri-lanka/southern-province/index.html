Postal codes of Southern Province, Sri Lanka

235 codes, 80000 to 82638.

The GeoNames data lists 235 postal codes in Southern Province, Sri Lanka, covering 235 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 80000 to 82638.

The places with the most codes are Galle (1), Wakwella (1), Anangoda (1), Walahanduwa (1), Ankokkawala (1), Gonamulla Junction (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Galle (93), Matara (78), Hambantota (64). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 5.93 and 6.46 and longitude 79.99 and 81.33, around a centre at about 6.18, 80.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
