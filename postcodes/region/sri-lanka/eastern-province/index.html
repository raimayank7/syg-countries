Postal codes of Eastern Province, Sri Lanka

146 codes, 30000 to 32514.

The GeoNames data lists 146 postal codes in Eastern Province, Sri Lanka, covering 146 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 30000 to 32514.

The places with the most codes are Ampara (1), Deegawapiya (1), Digamadulla Weeragoda (1), Hingurana (1), Moragahapallama (1), Damana (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Ampara (61), Batticaloa (46), Trincomalee (39). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 6.76 and 8.81 and longitude 80.95 and 81.86, around a centre at about 7.73, 81.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
