Postal codes of North Central Province, Sri Lanka

189 codes, 50000 to 51518.

The GeoNames data lists 189 postal codes in North Central Province, Sri Lanka, covering 190 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 50000 to 51518.

The places with the most codes are Anuradhapura (1), Perimiyankulama (1), Galkadawala (1), Saliyapura (1), Viharapalugama (1), Elayapattuwa (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Anuradhapura (128), Polonnaruwa (62). Postal codes in Sri Lanka are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 7.47 and 8.72 and longitude 79.87 and 81.25, around a centre at about 8.14, 80.59 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
