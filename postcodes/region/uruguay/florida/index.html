Postal codes of Florida, Uruguay

17 codes, 34200 to 98000.

The GeoNames data lists 17 postal codes in Florida, Uruguay, covering 111 named places. In code order they run from 34200 to 98000.

The places with the most codes are Puntas De Illescas (1), Nico Perez (1), Molles Del Pescado (1), Arrayan (1), Pueblo De Los Morochos (1), Costa De La Cruz (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.91 and -32.37 and longitude -57.72 and -54.97, around a centre at about -33.86, -56.08 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
