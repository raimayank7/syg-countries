Postal codes of Rio Negro, Uruguay

9 codes, 45100 to 97000.

The GeoNames data lists 9 postal codes in Rio Negro, Uruguay, covering 63 named places. In code order they run from 45100 to 97000.

The places with the most codes are Paso De Soca (1), Rolon (1), El Progreso (1), Estacion Francia (1), La Coronilla (1), La Palma (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.50 and -32.35 and longitude -58.36 and -56.02, around a centre at about -32.84, -57.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
