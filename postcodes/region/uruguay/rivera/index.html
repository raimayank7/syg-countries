Postal codes of Rivera, Uruguay

6 codes, 40000 to 45000.

The GeoNames data lists 6 postal codes in Rivera, Uruguay, covering 111 named places. In code order they run from 40000 to 45000.

The places with the most codes are Curticeiras (1), La Caillava (1), Arroyo Sauzal (1), Cerrillada (1), Lagos Del Norte (1), Platon (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.83 and -30.91 and longitude -57.72 and -53.78, around a centre at about -31.88, -55.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
