Postal codes of Maldonado, Uruguay

7 codes, 20000 to 30100.

The GeoNames data lists 7 postal codes in Maldonado, Uruguay, covering 103 named places. In code order they run from 20000 to 30100.

The places with the most codes are Buenos Aires (1), Maldonado (1), El Tesoro (1), El Chorro (1), Barrio Hipodromo (1), Arenas De Jose Ignacio (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.95 and -32.93 and longitude -57.62 and -53.86, around a centre at about -34.68, -55.01 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
