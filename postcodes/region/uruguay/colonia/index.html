Postal codes of Colonia, Uruguay

10 codes, 70000 to 75200.

The GeoNames data lists 10 postal codes in Colonia, Uruguay, covering 146 named places. In code order they run from 70000 to 75200.

The places with the most codes are El Semillero (1), Reducto (1), Laguna De Los Patos (1), San Pedro (1), Santa Rosa (1), Martin Chico De Conchillas (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.88 and -33.73 and longitude -58.41 and -55.40, around a centre at about -34.22, -57.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
