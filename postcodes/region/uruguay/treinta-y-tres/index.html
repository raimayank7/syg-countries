Postal codes of Treinta y Tres, Uruguay

7 codes, 30300 to 37100.

The GeoNames data lists 7 postal codes in Treinta y Tres, Uruguay, covering 88 named places. In code order they run from 30300 to 37100.

The places with the most codes are Panteon (1), Sauce De Corrales (1), Paso Del Sauce (1), El Bellaco (1), Maria Albina (1), Corrales De Cebollati (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.88 and -31.45 and longitude -55.42 and -53.58, around a centre at about -33.13, -54.33 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
