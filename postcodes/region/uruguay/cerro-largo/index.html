Postal codes of Cerro Largo, Uruguay

7 codes, 35300 to 97000.

The GeoNames data lists 7 postal codes in Cerro Largo, Uruguay, covering 125 named places. In code order they run from 35300 to 97000.

The places with the most codes are Esperanza (1), Arevalo (1), Cuchilla Del Carmen (1), Tupambae (1), Laguna Del Junco (1), Paso De Los Carros (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.86 and -31.78 and longitude -57.72 and -53.30, around a centre at about -32.76, -54.46 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
