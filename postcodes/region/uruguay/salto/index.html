Postal codes of Salto, Uruguay

3 codes, 50000 to 50200.

The GeoNames data lists 3 postal codes in Salto, Uruguay, covering 80 named places. In code order they run from 50000 to 50200.

The places with the most codes are Campo De Todos (1), Pueblo Fernandez (1), Corral De Piedra (1), La Caballada (1), Las Flores (1), La Viticola (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -33.10 and -30.74 and longitude -58.00 and -55.13, around a centre at about -31.30, -57.15 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
