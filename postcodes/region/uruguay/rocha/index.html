Postal codes of Rocha, Uruguay

5 codes, 27000 to 27400.

The GeoNames data lists 5 postal codes in Rocha, Uruguay, covering 92 named places. In code order they run from 27000 to 27400.

The places with the most codes are Arbolito (1), Paralle (1), Garzon Al Medio (1), Sarandi De La India Muerta (1), Garzon Arriba (1), Sauce De Rocha (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.78 and -33.27 and longitude -56.44 and -53.38, around a centre at about -34.22, -54.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
