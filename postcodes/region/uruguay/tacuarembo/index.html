Postal codes of Tacuarembo, Uruguay

3 codes, 45000 to 45200.

The GeoNames data lists 3 postal codes in Tacuarembo, Uruguay, covering 126 named places. In code order they run from 45000 to 45200.

The places with the most codes are Montevideo Chico (1), Las Chircas (1), Paso Bonilla (1), Pueblo Del Barro (1), Cerro De Pereira (1), Los Feos (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -32.84 and -30.85 and longitude -56.52 and -54.87, around a centre at about -32.02, -55.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
