Postal codes of Soriano, Uruguay

8 codes, 70100 to 75500.

The GeoNames data lists 8 postal codes in Soriano, Uruguay, covering 82 named places. In code order they run from 70100 to 75500.

The places with the most codes are Castillos (1), Lares (1), Perseverano (1), Agraciada (1), Puntas De Arenales (1), Villa Alejandrina (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.82 and -32.93 and longitude -58.43 and -53.72, around a centre at about -33.59, -57.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
