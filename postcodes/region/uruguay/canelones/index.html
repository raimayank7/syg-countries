Postal codes of Canelones, Uruguay

24 codes, 15000 to 96200.

The GeoNames data lists 24 postal codes in Canelones, Uruguay, covering 291 named places. In code order they run from 15000 to 96200.

The places with the most codes are Barrio Obrero (1), El Bosque (1), San Jose De Carrasco (1), Parque Carrasco (1), Colonia Nicolich (1), Parque Miramar (1). Postal codes in Uruguay are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude -34.88 and -31.39 and longitude -57.90 and -54.23, around a centre at about -34.49, -56.07 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
