Postal codes of Chittagong, Bangladesh

336 codes, 3400 to 4762.

The GeoNames data lists 336 postal codes in Chittagong, Bangladesh, covering 335 named places and 11 sub-areas. In code order they run from 3400 to 4762.

The places with the most codes are Azampur (2), Bandarban Sadar (1), Roanchhari (1), Ruma (1), Thanchi (1), Lama (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Chittagong (99), Noakhali (58), Comilla (38), Brahmanbaria (28). Postal codes in Bangladesh are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 20.86 and 24.23 and longitude 90.60 and 92.41, around a centre at about 22.86, 91.42 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
