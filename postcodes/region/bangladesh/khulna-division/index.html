Postal codes of Khulna Division, Bangladesh

167 codes, 7000 to 9461.

The GeoNames data lists 167 postal codes in Khulna Division, Bangladesh, covering 166 named places and 10 sub-areas. In code order they run from 7000 to 9461.

The places with the most codes are Amjhupi (2), Bagerhat Sadar (1), P.C College (1), Rangdia (1), Kachua (1), Sonarkola (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Khulna (39), Bagherhat (26), Satkhira (25), Jessore (21). Postal codes in Bangladesh are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.28 and 24.02 and longitude 88.69 and 89.89, around a centre at about 23.06, 89.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
