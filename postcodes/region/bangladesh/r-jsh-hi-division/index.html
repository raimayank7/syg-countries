Postal codes of Rājshāhi Division, Bangladesh

134 codes, 5800 to 6780.

The GeoNames data lists 134 postal codes in Rājshāhi Division, Bangladesh, covering 133 named places and 8 sub-areas. In code order they run from 5800 to 6780.

The places with the most codes are Rajapur (2), Bogra Sadar (1), Bogra Canttonment (1), Shibganj (1), Gabtoli (1), Sukhanpukur (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Rajshahi (23), Sirajganj (23), Bogra (20), Naogaon (18). Postal codes in Bangladesh are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 23.85 and 25.19 and longitude 88.20 and 89.73, around a centre at about 24.56, 89.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
