Postal codes of Dhaka Division, Bangladesh

375 codes, 1000 to 8142.

The GeoNames data lists 375 postal codes in Dhaka Division, Bangladesh, covering 373 named places and 17 sub-areas. In code order they run from 1000 to 8142.

The places with the most codes are Gouragonj (2), Jamalpur (2), Dhaka GPO (1), Dhaka Sadar HO (1), Wari TSO (1), Gendaria TSO (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Dhaka (51), Tangail (43), Munshiganj (38), Mymensingh (31). Postal codes in Bangladesh are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.89 and 25.19 and longitude 89.35 and 91.17, around a centre at about 24.00, 90.30 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
