Postal codes of Barisal Division, Bangladesh

115 codes, 8200 to 8741.

The GeoNames data lists 115 postal codes in Barisal Division, Bangladesh, covering 115 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 8200 to 8741.

The places with the most codes are Barguna Sadar (1), Nali Bandar (1), Amtali (1), Patharghata (1), Kakchira (1), Bamna (1). The sub-areas with most codes are Barishal (41), Pirojpur (25), Patuakhali (15), Bhola (14). Postal codes in Bangladesh are written four digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 21.86 and 23.06 and longitude 89.94 and 90.80, around a centre at about 22.55, 90.29 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
