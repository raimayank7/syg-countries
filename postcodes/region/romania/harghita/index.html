Postal codes of Harghita, Romania

338 codes, 530001 to 537366.

The GeoNames data lists 338 postal codes in Harghita, Romania, covering 265 named places. In code order they run from 530001 to 537366.

The places with the most codes are Miercurea-Ciuc (74), Ciba (1), Harghita-Băi (1), Jigodin-Băi (1), Băile Tuşnad (1), Carpitus (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.12 and 47.07 and longitude 24.87 and 26.25, around a centre at about 46.44, 25.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
