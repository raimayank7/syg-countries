Postal codes of Tulcea, Romania

329 codes, 820001 to 827237.

The GeoNames data lists 329 postal codes in Tulcea, Romania, covering 141 named places. In code order they run from 820001 to 827237.

The places with the most codes are Tulcea (189), Tudor Vladimirescu (1), Babadag (1), Isaccea (1), Revărsarea (1), Tichileşti (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.70 and 45.42 and longitude 28.12 and 29.65, around a centre at about 45.11, 28.75 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Bucureşti; Cluj; Vâlcea; Alba; Iaşi; Dolj; Argeş; Mureş.
