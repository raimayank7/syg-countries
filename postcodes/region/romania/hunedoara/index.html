Postal codes of Hunedoara, Romania

811 codes, 330001 to 337564.

The GeoNames data lists 811 postal codes in Hunedoara, Romania, covering 487 named places. In code order they run from 330001 to 337564.

The places with the most codes are Deva (127), Hunedoara (125), Petroşani (75), Archia (1), Bârcea Mică (1), Cristur (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.30 and 46.32 and longitude 22.39 and 23.47, around a centre at about 45.79, 22.93 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
