Postal codes of Iaşi, Romania

1,001 codes, 700001 to 707604.

The GeoNames data lists 1,001 postal codes in Iaşi, Romania, covering 430 named places. In code order they run from 700001 to 707604.

The places with the most codes are Iaşi (572), Hârlău (1), Pârcovaci (1), Paşcani (1), Blăgeşti (1), Boşteni (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.85 and 47.57 and longitude 26.52 and 28.10, around a centre at about 47.17, 27.47 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Other regions of Romania

Bucureşti; Cluj; Vâlcea; Alba; Dolj; Argeş; Mureş; Bacău.
