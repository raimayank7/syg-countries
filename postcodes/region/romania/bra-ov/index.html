Postal codes of Braşov, Romania

394 codes, 500001 to 507272.

The GeoNames data lists 394 postal codes in Braşov, Romania, covering 172 named places. In code order they run from 500001 to 507272.

The places with the most codes are Braşov (223), Poiana Braşov (1), Codlea (1), Făgăraş (1), Predeal (1), Pârâul Rece (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.42 and 46.16 and longitude 24.67 and 26.03, around a centre at about 45.71, 25.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
