Postal codes of Maramureş, Romania

474 codes, 430001 to 437390.

The GeoNames data lists 474 postal codes in Maramureş, Romania, covering 247 named places. In code order they run from 430001 to 437390.

The places with the most codes are Baia Mare (228), Blidari (1), Firiza (1), Valea Neagră (1), Baia Sprie (1), Chiuzbaia (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 47.34 and 48.00 and longitude 22.99 and 24.71, around a centre at about 47.63, 23.63 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
