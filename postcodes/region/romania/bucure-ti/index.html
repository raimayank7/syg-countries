Postal codes of Bucureşti, Romania

12,399 codes, 010011 to 062401.

The GeoNames data lists 12,399 postal codes in Bucureşti, Romania, covering 46 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 010011 to 062401.

The places with the most codes are Bucureşti 11 (506), Bucureşti 86 (486), Bucureşti 39 (479), Bucureşti 75 (451), Bucureşti 7 (448), Bucureşti 68 (419). The sub-areas with most codes are Bucureşti (12399). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.38 and 44.49 and longitude 26.02 and 26.17, around a centre at about 44.43, 26.10 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
