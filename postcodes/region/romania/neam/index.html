Postal codes of Neamţ, Romania

668 codes, 610001 to 617516.

The GeoNames data lists 668 postal codes in Neamţ, Romania, covering 363 named places. In code order they run from 610001 to 617516.

The places with the most codes are Piatra-Neamţ (199), Roman (108), Ciritei (1), Doamna (1), Văleni (Piatra-Neamţ) (1), Bicaz (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.70 and 47.32 and longitude 25.74 and 27.20, around a centre at about 46.95, 26.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
