Postal codes of Mehedinţi, Romania

549 codes, 220001 to 227513.

The GeoNames data lists 549 postal codes in Mehedinţi, Romania, covering 361 named places. In code order they run from 220001 to 227513.

The places with the most codes are Drobeta-Turnu Severin (189), Dudaşu Schelei (1), Gura Văii (1), Schela Cladovei (1), Baia de Aramă (1), Bratilovu (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.14 and 45.02 and longitude 22.11 and 23.44, around a centre at about 44.64, 22.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
