Postal codes of Timiş, Romania

861 codes, 300001 to 307471.

The GeoNames data lists 861 postal codes in Timiş, Romania, covering 325 named places. In code order they run from 300001 to 307471.

The places with the most codes are Timişoara (537), Buziaş (1), Bacova (1), Silagiu (1), Deta (1), Opatiţa (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.21 and 46.15 and longitude 20.32 and 22.45, around a centre at about 45.75, 21.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Other regions of Romania

Bucureşti; Cluj; Vâlcea; Alba; Iaşi; Dolj; Argeş; Mureş.
