Postal codes of Călăraşi, Romania

279 codes, 910001 to 917296.

The GeoNames data lists 279 postal codes in Călăraşi, Romania, covering 166 named places. In code order they run from 910001 to 917296.

The places with the most codes are Călăraşi (114), Budeşti (1), Aprozi (1), Buciumeni (1), Crivăţ (1), Gruiu (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.08 and 44.59 and longitude 26.28 and 27.67, around a centre at about 44.28, 27.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
