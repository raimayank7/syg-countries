Postal codes of Constanţa, Romania

616 codes, 900001 to 907305.

The GeoNames data lists 616 postal codes in Constanţa, Romania, covering 215 named places. In code order they run from 900001 to 907305.

The places with the most codes are Constanţa (402), Mamaia (1), Palazu Mare (1), Murfatlar (1), Siminoc (1), Cernavodă (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.75 and 44.77 and longitude 27.34 and 28.74, around a centre at about 44.18, 28.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Bucureşti; Cluj; Vâlcea; Alba; Iaşi; Dolj; Argeş; Mureş.
