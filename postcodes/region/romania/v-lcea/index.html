Postal codes of Vâlcea, Romania

1,093 codes, 240001 to 247777.

The GeoNames data lists 1,093 postal codes in Vâlcea, Romania, covering 614 named places. In code order they run from 240001 to 247777.

The places with the most codes are Râmnicu Vâlcea (480), Aranghel (1), Căzăneşti (Râmnicu Vâlcea) (1), Copăcelu (1), Dealu Malului (1), Poenari (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.50 and 45.52 and longitude 23.78 and 24.50, around a centre at about 45.03, 24.25 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
