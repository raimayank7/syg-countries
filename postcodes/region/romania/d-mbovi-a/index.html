Postal codes of Dâmboviţa, Romania

491 codes, 130001 to 137543.

The GeoNames data lists 491 postal codes in Dâmboviţa, Romania, covering 382 named places. In code order they run from 130001 to 137543.

The places with the most codes are Târgovişte (110), Priseaca (1), Fieni (1), Berevoeşti (1), Costeşti (1), Găeşti (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.48 and 45.29 and longitude 25.16 and 25.98, around a centre at about 44.87, 25.48 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
