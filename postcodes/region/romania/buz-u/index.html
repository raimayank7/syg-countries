Postal codes of Buzău, Romania

678 codes, 120001 to 127726.

The GeoNames data lists 678 postal codes in Buzău, Romania, covering 490 named places. In code order they run from 120001 to 127726.

The places with the most codes are Buzău (189), Nehoiu (1), Bâsca Rozilei (1), Chirleşti (1), Curmătura (1), Lunca Priporului (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.82 and 45.61 and longitude 26.18 and 27.36, around a centre at about 45.24, 26.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
