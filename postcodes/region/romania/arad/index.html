Postal codes of Arad, Romania

690 codes, 310001 to 317427.

The GeoNames data lists 690 postal codes in Arad, Romania, covering 283 named places. In code order they run from 310001 to 317427.

The places with the most codes are Arad (408), Chişineu-Criş (1), Nădab (1), Curtici (1), Dorobanţi (1), Ineu (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 45.95 and 46.64 and longitude 20.75 and 22.65, around a centre at about 46.22, 21.56 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
