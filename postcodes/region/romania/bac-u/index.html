Postal codes of Bacău, Romania

917 codes, 600001 to 607691.

The GeoNames data lists 917 postal codes in Bacău, Romania, covering 511 named places. In code order they run from 600001 to 607691.

The places with the most codes are Bacău (295), Oneşti (113), Borzeşti (1), Slobozia (Oneşti) (1), Buhuşi (1), Marginea (Buhuşi) (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.10 and 46.82 and longitude 26.01 and 27.48, around a centre at about 46.46, 26.90 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.

Bucureşti; Cluj; Vâlcea; Alba; Iaşi; Dolj; Argeş; Mureş.
