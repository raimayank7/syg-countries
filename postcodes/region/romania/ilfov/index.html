Postal codes of Ilfov, Romania

105 codes, 070000 to 077191.

The GeoNames data lists 105 postal codes in Ilfov, Romania, covering 105 named places. In code order they run from 070000 to 077191.

The places with the most codes are Buftea (1), Buciumeni (1), Otopeni (1), Odăile (1), 1 Decembrie (1), Copăceni (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.26 and 44.74 and longitude 25.87 and 26.39, around a centre at about 44.52, 26.14 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
