Postal codes of Giurgiu, Romania

680 codes, 080011 to 087261.

The GeoNames data lists 680 postal codes in Giurgiu, Romania, covering 171 named places. In code order they run from 080011 to 087261.

The places with the most codes are Giurgiu (510), Bolintin-Vale (1), Crivina (1), Malu Spart (1), Suseni (1), Mihăileşti (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 43.73 and 44.53 and longitude 25.49 and 26.37, around a centre at about 43.97, 25.94 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
