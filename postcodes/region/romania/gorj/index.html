Postal codes of Gorj, Romania

570 codes, 210001 to 217553.

The GeoNames data lists 570 postal codes in Gorj, Romania, covering 436 named places. In code order they run from 210001 to 217553.

The places with the most codes are Târgu Jiu (135), Bârseşti (1), Drăgoeni (1), Iezureni (1), Polata (1), Preajba Mare (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 44.59 and 45.19 and longitude 22.68 and 23.84, around a centre at about 44.95, 23.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
