Postal codes of Bistriţa-Năsăud, Romania

429 codes, 420001 to 427389.

The GeoNames data lists 429 postal codes in Bistriţa-Năsăud, Romania, covering 253 named places. In code order they run from 420001 to 427389.

The places with the most codes are Bistriţa (177), Ghinda (1), Sărata (1), Sigmir (1), Slătiniţa (1), Unirea (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.76 and 47.58 and longitude 23.94 and 25.01, around a centre at about 47.14, 24.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
