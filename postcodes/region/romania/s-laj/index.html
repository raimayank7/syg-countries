Postal codes of Sălaj, Romania

400 codes, 450001 to 457374.

The GeoNames data lists 400 postal codes in Sălaj, Romania, covering 290 named places. In code order they run from 450001 to 457374.

The places with the most codes are Zalău (111), Ortelec (1), Stâna (1), Cehu Silvaniei (1), Horoatu Cehului (1), Motiş (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.88 and 47.43 and longitude 22.50 and 23.80, around a centre at about 47.20, 23.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
