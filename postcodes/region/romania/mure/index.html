Postal codes of Mureş, Romania

938 codes, 540001 to 547669.

The GeoNames data lists 938 postal codes in Mureş, Romania, covering 518 named places. In code order they run from 540001 to 547669.

The places with the most codes are Târgu Mureş (421), Mureşeni (1), Remetea (1), Iernut (1), Cipău (1), Deag (1). Postal codes in Romania are written six digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 46.12 and 46.98 and longitude 23.97 and 25.27, around a centre at about 46.56, 24.55 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
