Postal codes of North Karelia, Finland

181 codes, 58350 to 83985.

The GeoNames data lists 181 postal codes in North Karelia, Finland, covering 147 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 58350 to 83985.

The places with the most codes are Joensuu (14), Nurmes (4), Lieksa (3), Ylämylly (2), Onttola (2), Kontioranta (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Joensuu (119), Pielisen Karjala (33), Keski-Karjala (29). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.81 and 63.83 and longitude 28.58 and 31.28, around a centre at about 62.76, 29.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
