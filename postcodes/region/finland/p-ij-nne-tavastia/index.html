Postal codes of Päijänne Tavastia, Finland

131 codes, 15100 to 19850.

The GeoNames data lists 131 postal codes in Päijänne Tavastia, Finland, covering 75 named places and 1 sub-areas. In code order they run from 15100 to 19850.

The places with the most codes are Lahti (37), Heinola (6), Nastola (4), Hollola (4), Artjärvi (2), Orimattila (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Lahti (131). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.72 and 61.72 and longitude 24.92 and 26.27, around a centre at about 61.10, 25.69 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
