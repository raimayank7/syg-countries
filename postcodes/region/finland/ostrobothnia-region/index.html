Postal codes of Ostrobothnia Region, Finland

144 codes, 61500 to 68970.

The GeoNames data lists 144 postal codes in Ostrobothnia Region, Finland, covering 112 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 61500 to 68970.

The places with the most codes are Vaasa (16), Pietarsaari (5), Isokyrö (2), Laihia (2), Tervajoki (2), Mustasaari (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Vaasa (70), Jakobstadsregionen (36), Sydösterbotten (24), Kyrönmaa (14). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 62.02 and 63.84 and longitude 21.13 and 23.62, around a centre at about 63.09, 22.00 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
