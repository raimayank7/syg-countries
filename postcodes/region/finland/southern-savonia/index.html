Postal codes of Southern Savonia, Finland

198 codes, 00101 to 79940.

The GeoNames data lists 198 postal codes in Southern Savonia, Finland, covering 150 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00101 to 79940.

The places with the most codes are Mikkeli (17), Savonlinna (15), Pieksämäki (5), Punkaharju (3), Kangasniemi (2), Haukivuori (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Mikkeli (84), Savonlinna (68), Pieksämäki (46). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.21 and 62.58 and longitude 26.35 and 29.58, around a centre at about 61.89, 27.78 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
