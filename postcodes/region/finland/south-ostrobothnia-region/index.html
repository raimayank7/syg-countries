Postal codes of South Ostrobothnia Region, Finland

210 codes, 60060 to 66370.

The GeoNames data lists 210 postal codes in South Ostrobothnia Region, Finland, covering 181 named places and 4 sub-areas. In code order they run from 60060 to 66370.

The places with the most codes are Seinäjoki (10), Kauhajoki (2), Teuva (2), Isojoki (2), Hyllykallio (2), Ilmajoki (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Seinäjoki (102), Järviseutu (41), Kuusiokunnat (37), Suupohja (30). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 62.06 and 63.43 and longitude 21.58 and 24.48, around a centre at about 62.77, 23.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
