Postal codes of Central Finland Region, Finland

216 codes, 17780 to 44970.

The GeoNames data lists 216 postal codes in Central Finland Region, Finland, covering 158 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 17780 to 44970.

The places with the most codes are Jyväskylä (25), Keuruu (4), Äänekoski (4), Suolahti (3), Palokka (2), Vaajakoski (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Jyväskylä (87), Saarijärvi-Viitasaari (58), Jämsä (23), Äänekoski (23). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.48 and 63.54 and longitude 24.17 and 26.60, around a centre at about 62.45, 25.53 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
