Postal codes of Pirkanmaa, Finland

276 codes, 14960 to 42870.

The GeoNames data lists 276 postal codes in Pirkanmaa, Finland, covering 148 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 14960 to 42870.

The places with the most codes are Tampere (59), Sastamala (21), Lempäälä (9), Akaa (7), Nokia (7), Pirkkala (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Tampere (167), Ylä-Pirkanmaa (35), Lounais-Pirkanmaa (29), Etelä-Pirkanmaa (26). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 61.01 and 62.41 and longitude 22.58 and 24.85, around a centre at about 61.56, 23.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
