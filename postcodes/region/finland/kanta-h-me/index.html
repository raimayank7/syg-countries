Postal codes of Kanta-Häme, Finland

111 codes, 00002 to 32140.

The GeoNames data lists 111 postal codes in Kanta-Häme, Finland, covering 70 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 00002 to 32140.

The places with the most codes are Hämeenlinna (18), Riihimäki (9), Forssa (5), Tuulos (4), Tervakoski (2), Parolannummi (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Hämeenlinna (57), Riihimäki (28), Forssa (26). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.62 and 61.27 and longitude 23.21 and 25.13, around a centre at about 60.91, 24.35 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
