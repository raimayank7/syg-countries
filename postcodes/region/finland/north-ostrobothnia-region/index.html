Postal codes of North Ostrobothnia Region, Finland

242 codes, 68100 to 95160.

The GeoNames data lists 242 postal codes in North Ostrobothnia Region, Finland, covering 156 named places and 7 sub-areas. In code order they run from 68100 to 95160.

The places with the most codes are Oulu (36), Nivala (9), Ylivieska (7), Sievi (6), Raahe (5), Kuusamo (5). The sub-areas with most codes are Oulu (77), Ylivieska (36), Oulunkaari (34), Raahe (33). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 63.51 and 66.29 and longitude 23.68 and 29.37, around a centre at about 64.70, 25.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
