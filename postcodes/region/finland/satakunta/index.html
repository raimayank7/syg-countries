Postal codes of Satakunta, Finland

161 codes, 26100 to 39990.

The GeoNames data lists 161 postal codes in Satakunta, Finland, covering 93 named places and 3 sub-areas. In code order they run from 26100 to 39990.

The places with the most codes are Pori (28), Rauma (7), Eurajoki (7), Lappi (5), Säkylä (5), Köyliö (4). The sub-areas with most codes are Pori (77), Rauma (55), Pohjois-Satakunta (29). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.96 and 62.25 and longitude 21.44 and 22.89, around a centre at about 61.45, 22.02 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
