Postal codes of Southwest Finland, Finland

240 codes, 20100 to 32560.

The GeoNames data lists 240 postal codes in Southwest Finland, Finland, covering 155 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 20100 to 32560.

The places with the most codes are Turku (41), Raisio (7), Salo (6), Pertteli (4), Naantali (3), Loimaa (3). The sub-areas with most codes are Turku (95), Salo (52), Loimaa (37), Åboland-Turunmaa (34). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 59.79 and 61.04 and longitude 21.28 and 23.78, around a centre at about 60.49, 22.49 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
