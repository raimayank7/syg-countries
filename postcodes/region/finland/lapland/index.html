Postal codes of Lapland, Finland

306 codes, 94100 to 99999.

The GeoNames data lists 306 postal codes in Lapland, Finland, covering 239 named places and 6 sub-areas. In code order they run from 94100 to 99999.

The places with the most codes are Tornio (19), Rovaniemi (18), Kemi (10), Kemijärvi (5), Keminmaa (3), Ranua (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Itä-Lappi (66), Rovaniemi (62), Tunturi-Lappi (54), Kemi-Tornio (53). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 65.63 and 70.04 and longitude 20.95 and 29.33, around a centre at about 66.88, 25.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
