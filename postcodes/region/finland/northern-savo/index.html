Postal codes of Northern Savo, Finland

238 codes, 70006 to 79520.

The GeoNames data lists 238 postal codes in Northern Savo, Finland, covering 160 named places and 5 sub-areas. In code order they run from 70006 to 79520.

The places with the most codes are Kuopio (36), Varkaus (13), Iisalmi (11), Pielavesi (3), Nilsiä (3), Keitele (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kuopio (96), Ylä-Savo (70), Varkaus (32), Koillis-Savo (20). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 62.23 and 63.96 and longitude 26.14 and 28.88, around a centre at about 63.05, 27.52 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
