Postal codes of Kymenlaakso, Finland

135 codes, 19160 to 49980.

The GeoNames data lists 135 postal codes in Kymenlaakso, Finland, covering 84 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 19160 to 49980.

The places with the most codes are Kotka (26), Kouvola (8), Hamina (5), Valkeala (4), Kuusankoski (4), Utti (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Kouvola (69), Kotka-Hamina (66). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.42 and 61.20 and longitude 26.15 and 27.89, around a centre at about 60.72, 26.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
