Postal codes of South Karelia, Finland

107 codes, 46530 to 59730.

The GeoNames data lists 107 postal codes in South Karelia, Finland, covering 65 named places and 2 sub-areas. In code order they run from 46530 to 59730.

The places with the most codes are Lappeenranta (22), Imatra (12), Uukuniemi (3), Joutseno (2), Taavetti (2), Savitaipale (2). The sub-areas with most codes are Lappeenranta (63), Imatra (44). Postal codes in Finland are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 60.71 and 61.79 and longitude 27.23 and 30.00, around a centre at about 61.19, 28.51 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
