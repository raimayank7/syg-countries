Postal codes of Bejaia, Algeria

111 codes, 06000 to 06110.

The GeoNames data lists 111 postal codes in Bejaia, Algeria, covering 748 named places. In code order they run from 06000 to 06110.

The places with the most codes are Taourirt (9), Tazrout (5), Takhlicht (4), Tigrine (4), Takorabt (4), Tighilt (4). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.03 and 36.86 and longitude 2.98 and 5.36, around a centre at about 36.58, 4.82 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
