Postal codes of Biskra, Algeria

88 codes, 07000 to 07800.

The GeoNames data lists 88 postal codes in Biskra, Algeria, covering 182 named places. In code order they run from 07000 to 07800.

The places with the most codes are Biskra Korra (2), Biskra Rp (1), M'Cid (1), Biskra Air (1), Biskra Star Melouk (1), Faidh Hellal (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.14 and 35.09 and longitude 4.99 and 6.69, around a centre at about 34.72, 5.73 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
