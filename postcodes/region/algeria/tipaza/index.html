Postal codes of Tipaza, Algeria

65 codes, 42000 to 42064.

The GeoNames data lists 65 postal codes in Tipaza, Algeria, covering 330 named places. In code order they run from 42000 to 42064.

The places with the most codes are Quartier Benazouz (2), Douar Zaouia (2), Ghardous (2), Douar Hamiti (1), Tipasa Rp (1), Douar Tikarouchine (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.41 and 36.67 and longitude 1.73 and 3.00, around a centre at about 36.55, 2.34 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
