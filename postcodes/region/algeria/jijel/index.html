Postal codes of Jijel, Algeria

50 codes, 18000 to 18049.

The GeoNames data lists 50 postal codes in Jijel, Algeria, covering 126 named places. In code order they run from 18000 to 18049.

The places with the most codes are El Haddada (1), Rebta (1), Jijel Kalaa (1), Grand Phare (1), Jijel Rp (1), El Araich (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.59 and 36.85 and longitude 5.48 and 6.36, around a centre at about 36.72, 5.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
