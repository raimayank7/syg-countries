Postal codes of Tebessa, Algeria

58 codes, 12000 to 12057.

The GeoNames data lists 58 postal codes in Tebessa, Algeria, covering 99 named places. In code order they run from 12000 to 12057.

The places with the most codes are Tebessa Rp (1), Rafana (1), Ain Zarroug (1), El Hamaima (1), Doukkane (Apm) (1), Ain Sari (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.49 and 35.95 and longitude 7.40 and 8.32, around a centre at about 35.25, 7.95 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
