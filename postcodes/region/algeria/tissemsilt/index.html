Postal codes of Tissemsilt, Algeria

32 codes, 38000 to 38031.

The GeoNames data lists 32 postal codes in Tissemsilt, Algeria, covering 174 named places. In code order they run from 38000 to 38031.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Hadj (2), Ouled Kouider (2), Ouled Benchohra (2), Oum Lallou (1), Tissemsilt Rp (1), Beni Maida (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.62 and 35.87 and longitude 1.45 and 2.27, around a centre at about 35.69, 1.99 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
