Postal codes of Ouargla, Algeria

62 codes, 30000 to 30803.

The GeoNames data lists 62 postal codes in Ouargla, Algeria, covering 155 named places. In code order they run from 30000 to 30803.

The places with the most codes are Ouargla Ctr (2), Nezla (2), Sokra (2), Berkaoui (1), Chorfa (1), El Gara Sud (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 31.68 and 33.50 and longitude 5.31 and 6.41, around a centre at about 32.60, 5.87 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
