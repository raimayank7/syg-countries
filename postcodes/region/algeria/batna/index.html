Postal codes of Batna, Algeria

132 codes, 05000 to 05131.

The GeoNames data lists 132 postal codes in Batna, Algeria, covering 385 named places. In code order they run from 05000 to 05131.

The places with the most codes are Kenda (2), El Hadjadj (2), Nirdi (2), Taghit (2), Azzab (1), Kechida (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.86 and 35.83 and longitude 5.18 and 6.65, around a centre at about 35.45, 6.11 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
