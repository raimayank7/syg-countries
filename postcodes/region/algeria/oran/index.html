Postal codes of Oran, Algeria

94 codes, 31000 to 31806.

The GeoNames data lists 94 postal codes in Oran, Algeria, covering 171 named places. In code order they run from 31000 to 31806.

The places with the most codes are Oran Tafna (1), Oran Usto (1), Oran Mouloud Feraoun (1), Oran Rp (1), Oran Ben Abderrezak (1), Oran Bougueri (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.54 and 35.88 and longitude -0.89 and -0.16, around a centre at about 35.70, -0.54 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
