Postal codes of El-Oued, Algeria

78 codes, 39000 to 39077.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in El-Oued, Algeria, covering 192 named places. In code order they run from 39000 to 39077.

The places with the most codes are Chouaiha (2), Ouaziten (2), El Oued Sahan (1), El Oued Rp (1), El Oued Nezla (1), El Oued El Qods (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.25 and 34.25 and longitude 5.75 and 7.01, around a centre at about 33.51, 6.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
