Postal codes of Khenchela, Algeria

45 codes, 40000 to 40044.

The GeoNames data lists 45 postal codes in Khenchela, Algeria, covering 184 named places. In code order they run from 40000 to 40044.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Si Moussa (2), Khenchela Rp (1), Ain El Kerma (1), Marir Lahcene (1), Boudjelbana (1), Belle Vue (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.84 and 35.52 and longitude 6.68 and 7.38, around a centre at about 35.20, 7.03 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
