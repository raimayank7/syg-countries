Postal codes of Skikda, Algeria

76 codes, 21000 to 21075.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Skikda, Algeria, covering 217 named places. In code order they run from 21000 to 21075.

The places with the most codes are Zitouna (2), Skikda Rp (1), Arb Skikda (1), Menzel El Moudjahid (1), Ferfour (1), Mechta Heloufa (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.66 and 37.05 and longitude 6.34 and 7.31, around a centre at about 36.83, 6.81 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
