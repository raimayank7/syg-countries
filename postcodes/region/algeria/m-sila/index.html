Postal codes of M'Sila, Algeria

69 codes, 28000 to 28068.

The GeoNames data lists 69 postal codes in M'Sila, Algeria, covering 223 named places. In code order they run from 28000 to 28068.

The places with the most codes are Bouira (2), Ouled Attia (2), Dialem (2), Bechilga (1), Ghezal (1), M'Sila Cnel Amirouche (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.82 and 35.93 and longitude 3.68 and 5.09, around a centre at about 35.43, 4.38 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
