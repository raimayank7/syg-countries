Postal codes of Constantine, Algeria

61 codes, 25000 to 25806.

The GeoNames data lists 61 postal codes in Constantine, Algeria, covering 300 named places. In code order they run from 25000 to 25806.

The places with the most codes are Constantine Aouati Mostefa (1), Constantine Ain El Bey (1), Constantine Gare (1), Constantine Saidani (1), Arcades Romaines (1), Constantine Loucif (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.18 and 36.53 and longitude 6.47 and 7.02, around a centre at about 36.34, 6.70 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
