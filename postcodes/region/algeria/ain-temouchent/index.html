Postal codes of Ain-Temouchent, Algeria

55 codes, 46000 to 46054.

The GeoNames data lists 55 postal codes in Ain-Temouchent, Algeria, covering 132 named places. In code order they run from 46000 to 46054.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Boudjemaa (2), Ain Temouchent Sidi Said (1), Keroulis (1), Hai Moulay Mustapha (1), Domaine Maata Mohamed (1), Akid Othmane (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.14 and 35.57 and longitude -1.55 and -0.66, around a centre at about 35.34, -1.12 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
