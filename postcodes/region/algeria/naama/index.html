Postal codes of Naama, Algeria

33 codes, 45000 to 45032.

The GeoNames data lists 33 postal codes in Naama, Algeria, covering 67 named places. In code order they run from 45000 to 45032.

The places with the most codes are Naama Rp (1), Msif (1), Mogta Deli (1), Ain Sefra Saddok Ali (1), Ain Sefra Terre (1), Ain Sefra (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.73 and 33.76 and longitude -0.87 and -0.08, around a centre at about 33.09, -0.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
