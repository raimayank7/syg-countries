Postal codes of Setif, Algeria

129 codes, 19000 to 19801.

The GeoNames data lists 129 postal codes in Setif, Algeria, covering 755 named places. In code order they run from 19000 to 19801.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Athmane (3), Cheurfa (3), Boudjenada (2), Kherba (2), Oued Safsaf (2), Ouled Ayad (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.75 and 36.50 and longitude 4.62 and 6.02, around a centre at about 36.11, 5.45 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
