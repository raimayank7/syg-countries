Postal codes of Mascara, Algeria

76 codes, 29000 to 29076.

The GeoNames data lists 76 postal codes in Mascara, Algeria, covering 937 named places. In code order they run from 29000 to 29076.

The places with the most codes are El Amairia (3), Ouled Sidi Safi (2), Ouled Ben Aouali (2), Domaine Sidi Benyekhlef (1), Sidi Daho (1), Ouled Bouhzem (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.08 and 35.68 and longitude -0.35 and 0.79, around a centre at about 35.40, 0.27 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
