Postal codes of El-Bayadh, Algeria

35 codes, 32000 to 32034.

The GeoNames data lists 35 postal codes in El-Bayadh, Algeria, covering 134 named places. In code order they run from 32000 to 32034.

The places with the most codes are Bougtob Hai Nasr (2), El Bayadh Laamari Med (1), Ouled Omrane (1), Thenia (1), Laguermi (1), El Bayadh Rp (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.86 and 34.04 and longitude 0.02 and 1.73, around a centre at about 33.35, 0.67 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
