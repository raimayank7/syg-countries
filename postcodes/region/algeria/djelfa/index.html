Postal codes of Djelfa, Algeria

66 codes, 17000 to 17065.

The GeoNames data lists 66 postal codes in Djelfa, Algeria, covering 144 named places. In code order they run from 17000 to 17065.

The places with the most codes are Djelfa Achour Ziane (1), Djelfa Terre (1), Zina (1), Djelfa El Wiam (1), Djelfa Rp (1), Boussedraya (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.15 and 35.71 and longitude 2.53 and 3.60, around a centre at about 34.79, 3.20 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
