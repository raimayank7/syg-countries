Postal codes of Tamanrasset, Algeria

25 codes, 11000 to 11024.

The GeoNames data lists 25 postal codes in Tamanrasset, Algeria, covering 85 named places. In code order they run from 11000 to 11024.

The places with the most codes are Amouidir (2), Adriane Ouled Bahmane (1), Adriane Ouled Messaoud (1), Adriane Tabrakat (1), Ihelfene (1), Imechouene (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 22.66 and 27.34 and longitude 2.48 and 6.26, around a centre at about 23.66, 5.04 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
