Postal codes of El-Taref, Algeria

50 codes, 36000 to 36049.

The GeoNames data lists 50 postal codes in El-Taref, Algeria, covering 194 named places. In code order they run from 36000 to 36049.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Anane (2), El Tarf Rp (1), Bordj Forestier (1), El Tarf Cite Guergour (1), Mechta Laca (1), Forestier Borj (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.58 and 36.90 and longitude 7.85 and 8.60, around a centre at about 36.78, 8.23 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
