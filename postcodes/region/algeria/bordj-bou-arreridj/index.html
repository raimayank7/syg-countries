Postal codes of Bordj-Bou-Arreridj, Algeria

64 codes, 34000 to 34063.

The GeoNames data lists 64 postal codes in Bordj-Bou-Arreridj, Algeria, covering 476 named places. In code order they run from 34000 to 34063.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Abdelouahed (2), Zbir (2), Ouled Sellini (2), Merabtine (2), Boutouil (2), Rabta (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.79 and 36.32 and longitude 4.40 and 5.13, around a centre at about 36.08, 4.89 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
