Postal codes of Chlef, Algeria

90 codes, 02000 to 02804.

The GeoNames data lists 90 postal codes in Chlef, Algeria, covering 394 named places. In code order they run from 02000 to 02804.

The places with the most codes are Cite Caper (3), Ouled Ahmed (3), Ouled Ali (3), Zaouia (2), Ouled Larbi (2), Chekallil (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.01 and 36.54 and longitude 0.92 and 1.62, around a centre at about 36.24, 1.31 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
