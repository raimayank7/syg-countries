Postal codes of Alger, Algeria

138 codes, 16000 to 16809.

The GeoNames data lists 138 postal codes in Alger, Algeria, covering 723 named places. In code order they run from 16000 to 16809.

The places with the most codes are Ravin De La Femme Sauvage (3), Domaine Emir Abdelkader (2), Cite Des Annassers (2), Cite Des Ptt (2), Fontaine Fraiche (2), Village Negre (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.65 and 36.81 and longitude 3.03 and 3.29, around a centre at about 36.75, 3.18 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
