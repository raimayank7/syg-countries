Postal codes of Bouira, Algeria

98 codes, 10000 to 10097.

The GeoNames data lists 98 postal codes in Bouira, Algeria, covering 568 named places. In code order they run from 10000 to 10097.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Aissa (5), Ighil Ouzarem (2), Ouled Brahim (2), Belbara (2), Ouled Gacem (2), Ouled Zidane (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.02 and 36.56 and longitude 3.43 and 4.33, around a centre at about 36.34, 3.86 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
