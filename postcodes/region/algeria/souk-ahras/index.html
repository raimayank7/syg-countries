Postal codes of Souk-Ahras, Algeria

40 codes, 41000 to 41039.

The GeoNames data lists 40 postal codes in Souk-Ahras, Algeria, covering 112 named places. In code order they run from 41000 to 41039.

The places with the most codes are Souk Ahras Rp (1), Hammam Tessa (1), Ain Hadjar (1), M'Daourouch (1), Maida (1), Hamimine (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.08 and 36.35 and longitude 7.53 and 8.32, around a centre at about 36.20, 7.98 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
