Postal codes of Relizane, Algeria

74 codes, 48000 to 48073.

The GeoNames data lists 74 postal codes in Relizane, Algeria, covering 327 named places. In code order they run from 48000 to 48073.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Abed (3), Cheraitia (2), Ouled Ali (2), Nouaiel (2), Ouled Rafaa (2), Ouled Yahia (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.49 and 36.14 and longitude 0.33 and 1.33, around a centre at about 35.78, 0.71 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
