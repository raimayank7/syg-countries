Postal codes of Ghardaia, Algeria

34 codes, 47000 to 47801.

The GeoNames data lists 34 postal codes in Ghardaia, Algeria, covering 115 named places. In code order they run from 47000 to 47801.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Zid (2), Sid El Hadj Messaoud (1), Ourighnou (1), Ghardaia Rp (1), Ksar Belkacem (1), El Meniaa (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 32.27 and 32.83 and longitude 3.63 and 4.50, around a centre at about 32.48, 3.84 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
