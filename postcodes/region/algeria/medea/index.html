Postal codes of Medea, Algeria

78 codes, 26000 to 26800.

The GeoNames data lists 78 postal codes in Medea, Algeria, covering 700 named places. In code order they run from 26000 to 26800.

The places with the most codes are Zaouia (5), Ouled Brahim (5), Ouled Aissa (4), Ouled Salem (4), Merabtine (3), Ouled Abid (3). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 35.41 and 36.41 and longitude 0.04 and 3.58, around a centre at about 36.08, 2.92 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
