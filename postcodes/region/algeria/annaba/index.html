Postal codes of Annaba, Algeria

56 codes, 23000 to 23807.

The GeoNames data lists 56 postal codes in Annaba, Algeria, covering 102 named places. In code order they run from 23000 to 23807.

The places with the most codes are Annaba Rp (1), Annaba Gare Routiere (1), Annaba Cite Universitaire (1), Annaba Cite Seybouse (1), Annaba Cite Didouche Mourad (1), Annaba Siderurgie (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 36.65 and 36.99 and longitude 7.45 and 7.77, around a centre at about 36.85, 7.60 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
