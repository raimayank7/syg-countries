Postal codes of L.Aghouat, Algeria

47 codes, 03000 to 03046.

The GeoNames data lists 47 postal codes in L.Aghouat, Algeria, covering 123 named places. In code order they run from 03000 to 03046.

The places with the most codes are Hassi R'Mel (2), El Fetha (1), Bouchakeur (1), Laghouat Rp (1), Seridja (1), Laghouat Dca (1). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 33.50 and 34.30 and longitude 1.74 and 3.18, around a centre at about 33.96, 2.32 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
