Postal codes of Tlemcen, Algeria

83 codes, 13000 to 13082.

The GeoNames data lists 83 postal codes in Tlemcen, Algeria, covering 791 named places. In code order they run from 13000 to 13082.

The places with the most codes are Ouled Youcef (3), Sidi Lahcene (2), Sidi Aissa (2), Ouled Kaddour (2), Ouled Moussa (2), Tafna (2). Postal codes in Algeria are written five digits.

Taken together, the map positions of these codes lie between latitude 34.22 and 35.48 and longitude -2.17 and 2.23, around a centre at about 34.90, -1.44 (see it on OpenStreetMap). The positions are estimates, so use them to get your bearings, not for navigation.
