Iraq

Iraq is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Republic of Iraq. In the languages of the place it is called العراق, ܩܘܼܛܢܵܐ, کۆماری. The capital is Baghdad.

Land and position

Iraq covers about 437,072 square kilometres. By area it ranks 60th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Morocco and a little larger than Paraguay. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Iran, Jordan, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Türkiye. Its centre lies at about 33° north, 44° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 47 million people live in Iraq (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 34th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.57 percent of the world's people. On average there are 108 people to each square kilometre. About 70 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 72 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, Kurdish, Armenian. A person from Iraq is called Iraqi.

Money and economy

The currency is the Iraqi dinar (code IQD, symbol ع.د). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 254 billion (2025), which is about US$ 5,410 for each person (2025).

Time

Iraq uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Asia/Baghdad). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
