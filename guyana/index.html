Guyana

Guyana is a country in South America. Its official name is Co-operative Republic of Guyana. The capital is Georgetown.

Land and position

Guyana covers about 214,970 square kilometres. By area it ranks 86th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Uganda and a little larger than Belarus. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Brazil, Suriname, Venezuela. Its centre lies at about 5° north, 59° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 835,986 people live in Guyana (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 165th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 3.9 people to each square kilometre. About 26 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 70 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English. A person from Guyana is called Guyanese.

Money and economy

The currency is the Guyanese dollar (code GYD, symbol $). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 27.1 billion (2025), which is about US$ 32,414 for each person (2025).

Time

Guyana uses one time zone, UTC-4:00 (America/Guyana). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
