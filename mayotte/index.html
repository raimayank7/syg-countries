Mayotte

Mayotte is in Eastern Africa. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. Its official name is Department of Mayotte. The capital is Mamoudzou.

Land and position

Mayotte covers about 374 square kilometres. By area it ranks 211th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and a little larger than United States Virgin Islands. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 13° south, 45° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 279,471 people live in Mayotte (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 185th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 747 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: French. A person from Mayotte is called Mahoran.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more.

Time

Mayotte uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Indian/Mayotte). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
