What the Common Core of Data is

The Common Core of Data (CCD) is the National Center for Education Statistics' yearly record of every public school and school district in the United States and its territories. This site uses the school directory and the student membership files for the 2023-24 school year.

The NCES school ID

Each school has a 12-digit NCES ID: the first seven digits identify the district (two for the state, five for the district) and the last five the school within it. For example the first school of the file, in Alabama, is 010000500870 in district 0100005. (The structure of the ID is general knowledge about the CCD and can be seen in the sample row.)

Statuses

The file marks each school as open, new, reopened, closed, inactive or future and so on. This site shows open, new, reopened, added and boundary-changed schools (100,458) and leaves out the closed, inactive and future ones.

What it cannot tell you

  • Anything about quality. There are no ratings, test scores or rankings in this site.
  • Private schools, which are not in the CCD.
  • Changes after the 2023-24 school year.
