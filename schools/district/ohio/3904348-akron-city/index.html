Akron City: public schools

48 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in the Akron City district in Ohio: 27 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 1 other. The file reports 19,930 students in 48 of them; the median school has 350 students and the largest has 1,085. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 1.34% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Akron.
