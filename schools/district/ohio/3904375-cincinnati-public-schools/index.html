Cincinnati Public Schools: public schools

66 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 66 public schools in the Cincinnati Public Schools district in Ohio: 41 elementary, 13 high, 6 other, 5 prekindergarten, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 34,858 students in 66 of them; the median school has 449 students and the largest has 2,500. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 1.85% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Cincinnati.
