Columbus City Schools District: public schools

116 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 116 public schools in the Columbus City Schools District district in Ohio: 73 elementary, 21 high, 18 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 45,181 students in 116 of them; the median school has 339 students and the largest has 984. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 3.24% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Columbus.
