Cleveland Municipal: public schools

93 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 93 public schools in the Cleveland Municipal district in Ohio: 54 elementary, 27 high, 12 other. The file reports 33,744 students in 93 of them; the median school has 360 students and the largest has 801. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 2.60% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Cleveland.
