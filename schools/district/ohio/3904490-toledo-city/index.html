Toledo City: public schools

57 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in the Toledo City district in Ohio: 40 elementary, 11 high, 3 prekindergarten, 2 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,983 students in 57 of them; the median school has 328 students and the largest has 1,111. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 1.59% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Toledo.
