South-Western City: public schools

34 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the South-Western City district in Ohio: 16 elementary, 10 middle, 5 high, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,813 students in 34 of them; the median school has 552 students and the largest has 1,864. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 0.95% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Grove City (18), Columbus (11), Galloway (4), Grove (1).
