Dublin City: public schools

24 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Dublin City district in Ohio: 15 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 16,736 students in 24 of them; the median school has 568 students and the largest has 2,009. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 0.67% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Dublin (20), Plain City (2), Powell (1), Columbus (1).
