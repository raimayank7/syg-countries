Hilliard City: public schools

24 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Hilliard City district in Ohio: 14 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 16,164 students in 24 of them; the median school has 513 students and the largest has 1,944. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 0.67% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Hilliard (22), Columbus (1), Dublin (1).
