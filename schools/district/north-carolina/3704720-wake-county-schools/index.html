Wake County Schools: public schools

197 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 197 public schools in the Wake County Schools district in North Carolina: 121 elementary, 40 middle, 36 high. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 161,481 students in 197 of them; the median school has 645 students and the largest has 2,761. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 1st largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 7.25% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 14 towns, most in Raleigh (88), Cary (31), Apex (19), Garner (11).
