Union County Public Schools: public schools

52 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Union County Public Schools district in North Carolina: 30 elementary, 13 high, 9 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 41,687 students in 52 of them; the median school has 709 students and the largest has 2,050. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 8th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.91% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Monroe (22), Waxhaw (11), Indian Trail (7), Marshville (4).
