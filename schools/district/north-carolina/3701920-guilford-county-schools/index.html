Guilford County Schools: public schools

124 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 124 public schools in the Guilford County Schools district in North Carolina: 69 elementary, 30 high, 21 middle, 4 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 67,832 students in 124 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 2,042. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 3rd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 4.57% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Greensboro (74), High Point (26), Jamestown (7), McLeansville (4).
