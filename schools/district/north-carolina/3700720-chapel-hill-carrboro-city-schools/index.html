Chapel Hill-Carrboro City Schools: public schools

21 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Chapel Hill-Carrboro City Schools district in North Carolina: 11 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,386 students in 21 of them; the median school has 442 students and the largest has 1,638. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 37th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.77% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Chapel Hill (19), Carrboro (2).
