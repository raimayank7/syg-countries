Wilson County Schools: public schools

25 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Wilson County Schools district in North Carolina: 13 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,260 students in 25 of them; the median school has 373 students and the largest has 1,115. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 28th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.92% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Wilson (18), Elm City (2), Lucama (2), Stantonsburg (2).
