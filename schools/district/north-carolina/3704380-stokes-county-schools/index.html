Stokes County Schools: public schools

19 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Stokes County Schools district in North Carolina: 11 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,585 students in 19 of them; the median school has 199 students and the largest has 776. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 40th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.70% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in King (6), Walnut Cove (5), Lawsonville (2), Germanton (1).
