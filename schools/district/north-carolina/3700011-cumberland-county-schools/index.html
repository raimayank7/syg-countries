Cumberland County Schools: public schools

86 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 86 public schools in the Cumberland County Schools district in North Carolina: 51 elementary, 18 high, 17 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 49,314 students in 86 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 1,916. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 4th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 3.17% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Fayetteville (66), Hope Mills (10), Spring Lake (3), Eastover (2).
