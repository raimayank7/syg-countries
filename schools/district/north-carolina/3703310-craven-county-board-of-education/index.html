Craven County Board of Education: public schools

26 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Craven County Board of Education district in North Carolina: 15 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high, 1 other. The file reports 12,584 students in 26 of them; the median school has 401 students and the largest has 1,632. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 27th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.96% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in New Bern (14), Havelock (9), Vanceboro (2), Cove City (1).
