Currituck County Schools: public schools

10 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in the Currituck County Schools district in North Carolina: 6 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle. The file reports 4,554 students in 10 of them; the median school has 327 students and the largest has 1,041. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 72nd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Barco (3), Moyock (2), Poplar Branch (1), Knotts Island (1).
