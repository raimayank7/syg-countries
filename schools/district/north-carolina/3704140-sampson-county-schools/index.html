Sampson County Schools: public schools

18 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Sampson County Schools district in North Carolina: 9 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle. The file reports 8,086 students in 18 of them; the median school has 438 students and the largest has 758. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 42nd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.66% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Clinton (4), Newton Grove (4), Dunn (3), Roseboro (2).
