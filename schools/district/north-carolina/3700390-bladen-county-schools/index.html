Bladen County Schools: public schools

12 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Bladen County Schools district in North Carolina: 6 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high. The file reports 3,843 students in 12 of them; the median school has 265 students and the largest has 700. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 65th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.44% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Elizabethtown (4), Bladenboro (3), Dublin (2), Clarkton (1).
