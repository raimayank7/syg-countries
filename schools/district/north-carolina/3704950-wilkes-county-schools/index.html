Wilkes County Schools: public schools

22 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Wilkes County Schools district in North Carolina: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle. The file reports 8,625 students in 22 of them; the median school has 375 students and the largest has 737. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 35th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.81% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in North Wilkesboro (4), Wilkesboro (4), Ronda (3), Moravian Falls (2).
