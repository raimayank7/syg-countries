Johnston County Public Schools: public schools

48 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in the Johnston County Public Schools district in North Carolina: 24 elementary, 12 middle, 10 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 37,289 students in 48 of them; the median school has 679 students and the largest has 2,270. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 9th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.77% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Clayton (12), Smithfield (9), Benson (5), Selma (3).
