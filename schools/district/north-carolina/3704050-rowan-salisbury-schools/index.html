Rowan-Salisbury Schools: public schools

32 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Rowan-Salisbury Schools district in North Carolina: 18 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 18,110 students in 32 of them; the median school has 548 students and the largest has 1,135. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Salisbury (15), China Grove (6), Spencer (3), Landis (2).
