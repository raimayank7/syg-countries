Cabarrus County Schools: public schools

44 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Cabarrus County Schools district in North Carolina: 21 elementary, 12 high, 9 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 35,021 students in 44 of them; the median school has 817 students and the largest has 1,863. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 11th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.62% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Concord (34), Harrisburg (4), Mount Pleasant (3), Kannapolis (2).
