New Hanover County Schools: public schools

42 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the New Hanover County Schools district in North Carolina: 26 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 25,028 students in 42 of them; the median school has 455 students and the largest has 2,248. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 12th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.55% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Wilmington (36), Castle Hayne (4), Carolina Beach (1), Wrightsville Beach (1).
