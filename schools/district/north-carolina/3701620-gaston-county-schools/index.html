Gaston County Schools: public schools

56 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Gaston County Schools district in North Carolina: 30 elementary, 13 high, 11 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 30,253 students in 56 of them; the median school has 503 students and the largest has 1,341. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 6th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 2.06% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Gastonia (23), Belmont (7), Dallas (6), Bessemer City (5).
