Harnett County Schools: public schools

29 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Harnett County Schools district in North Carolina: 16 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 20,033 students in 29 of them; the median school has 609 students and the largest has 2,024. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 22nd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.07% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Lillington (5), Angier (4), Dunn (4), Sanford (3).
