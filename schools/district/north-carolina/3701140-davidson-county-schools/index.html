Davidson County Schools: public schools

35 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Davidson County Schools district in North Carolina: 18 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 17,868 students in 35 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 986. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 18th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.29% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Lexington (19), Thomasville (9), Winston-Salem (5), Denton (2).
