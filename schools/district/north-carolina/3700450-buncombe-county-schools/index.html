Buncombe County Schools: public schools

45 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Buncombe County Schools district in North Carolina: 23 elementary, 11 middle, 10 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,172 students in 45 of them; the median school has 470 students and the largest has 1,495. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 10th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.66% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Asheville (20), Candler (6), Weaverville (6), Black Mountain (3).
