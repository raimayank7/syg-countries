Wayne County Public Schools: public schools

33 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Wayne County Public Schools district in North Carolina: 13 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,368 students in 33 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 1,125. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 19th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.22% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Goldsboro (21), Dudley (3), Pikeville (3), Mount Olive (2).
