Randolph County School System: public schools

32 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Randolph County School System district in North Carolina: 18 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle. The file reports 15,261 students in 32 of them; the median school has 442 students and the largest has 872. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 21st largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Asheboro (7), Trinity (6), Archdale (4), Ramseur (4).
