Onslow County Schools: public schools

39 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Onslow County Schools district in North Carolina: 22 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 1 other. The file reports 27,592 students in 39 of them; the median school has 632 students and the largest has 1,449. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.44% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Jacksonville (25), Richlands (4), Swansboro (4), Holly Ridge (3).
