Rutherford County Schools: public schools

18 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Rutherford County Schools district in North Carolina: 10 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,409 students in 18 of them; the median school has 423 students and the largest has 750. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 43rd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.66% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Forest City (7), Rutherfordton (4), Bostic (3), Spindale (2).
