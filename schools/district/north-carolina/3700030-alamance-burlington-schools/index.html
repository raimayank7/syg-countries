Alamance-Burlington Schools: public schools

38 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in the Alamance-Burlington Schools district in North Carolina: 20 elementary, 10 high, 7 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,477 students in 38 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 1,215. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 16th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.40% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Burlington (15), Graham (10), Mebane (6), Elon (4).
