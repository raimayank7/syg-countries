Hoke County Schools: public schools

14 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Hoke County Schools district in North Carolina: 8 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,769 students in 14 of them; the median school has 592 students and the largest has 2,115. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 55th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.52% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Raeford (11), Lumber Bridge (2), Red Springs (1).
