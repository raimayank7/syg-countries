Public Schools of Robeson County: public schools

37 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Public Schools of Robeson County district in North Carolina: 22 elementary, 8 middle, 6 high, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 20,983 students in 37 of them; the median school has 473 students and the largest has 1,950. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 17th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.36% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Lumberton (14), Fairmont (4), Pembroke (4), Maxton (3).
