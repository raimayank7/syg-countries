Winston Salem / Forsyth County Schools: public schools

81 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in the Winston Salem / Forsyth County Schools district in North Carolina: 44 elementary, 18 high, 16 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 52,157 students in 81 of them; the median school has 586 students and the largest has 2,269. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 2.98% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Winston Salem (35), Winston-Salem (22), Kernersville (11), Clemmons (4).
