Duplin County Schools: public schools

13 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Duplin County Schools district in North Carolina: 8 elementary, 5 high. The file reports 9,835 students in 13 of them; the median school has 775 students and the largest has 1,114. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 58th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Beulaville (2), Warsaw (2), Kenansville (2), Mt. Olive (2).
