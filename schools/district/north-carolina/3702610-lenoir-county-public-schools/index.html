Lenoir County Public Schools: public schools

17 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Lenoir County Public Schools district in North Carolina: 9 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,372 students in 17 of them; the median school has 445 students and the largest has 989. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 44th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.63% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Kinston (12), La Grange (3), Pink Hill (1), Deep Run (1).
