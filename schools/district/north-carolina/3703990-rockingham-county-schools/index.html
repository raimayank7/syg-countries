Rockingham County Schools: public schools

23 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Rockingham County Schools district in North Carolina: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,379 students in 23 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 899. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 31st largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.85% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Reidsville (11), Eden (5), Madison (3), Stoneville (1).
