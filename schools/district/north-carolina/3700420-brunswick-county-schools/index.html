Brunswick County Schools: public schools

20 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Brunswick County Schools district in North Carolina: 10 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,262 students in 20 of them; the median school has 580 students and the largest has 1,560. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 39th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.74% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Bolivia (4), Leland (4), Shallotte (3), Southport (3).
