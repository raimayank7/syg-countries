Elizabeth City-Pasquotank Public Schools: public schools

13 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Elizabeth City-Pasquotank Public Schools district in North Carolina: 7 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,719 students in 13 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 666. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 56th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Elizabeth City.
