Catawba County Schools: public schools

28 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Catawba County Schools district in North Carolina: 16 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,880 students in 28 of them; the median school has 517 students and the largest has 1,298. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.03% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Hickory (7), Newton (5), Claremont (5), Maiden (4).
