Stanly County Schools: public schools

23 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Stanly County Schools district in North Carolina: 11 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,739 students in 23 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 785. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 30th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.85% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Albemarle (10), Norwood (4), Locust (2), New London (2).
