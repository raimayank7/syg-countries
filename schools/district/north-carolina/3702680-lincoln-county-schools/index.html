Lincoln County Schools: public schools

23 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Lincoln County Schools district in North Carolina: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,642 students in 23 of them; the median school has 419 students and the largest has 1,139. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 33rd largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.85% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Lincolnton (14), Denver (5), Iron Station (2), Vale (2).
