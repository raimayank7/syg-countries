Vance County Schools: public schools

16 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Vance County Schools district in North Carolina: 10 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,368 students in 16 of them; the median school has 264 students and the largest has 1,046. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

This is the 46th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 0.59% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Henderson (15), Kittrell (1).
