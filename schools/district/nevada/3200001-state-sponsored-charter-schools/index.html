State Sponsored Charter Schools: public schools

81 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in the State Sponsored Charter Schools district in Nevada: 45 elementary, 18 high, 12 other, 4 middle, 2 secondary. 81 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 61,883 students in 81 of them; the median school has 674 students and the largest has 2,393. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 10.63% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Las Vegas (48), Henderson (15), North Las Vegas (8), Reno (5).
