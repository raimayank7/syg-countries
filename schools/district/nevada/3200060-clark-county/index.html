Clark County: public schools

383 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 383 public schools in the Clark County district in Nevada: 242 elementary, 73 high, 60 middle, 4 other, 4 ungraded. 11 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 21 alternative schools, 9 special education schools. The file reports 304,540 students in 383 of them; the median school has 606 students and the largest has 3,270. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 1st largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 50.26% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 18 towns, most in Las Vegas (267), N Las Vegas (44), Henderson (41), Indian Springs (6).
