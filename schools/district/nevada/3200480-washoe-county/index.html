Washoe County: public schools

115 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 115 public schools in the Washoe County district in Nevada: 73 elementary, 18 middle, 18 high, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten. 11 of them (9.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 63,765 students in 115 of them; the median school has 446 students and the largest has 2,302. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 15.09% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Reno (76), Sparks (32), Incline Village (3), Gerlach (1).
