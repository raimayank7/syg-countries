Elko: public schools

36 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in the Elko district in Nevada: 17 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 9,888 students in 36 of them; the median school has 126 students and the largest has 1,433. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 4th largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 4.72% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Elko (12), Spring Creek (6), Carlin (4), Wells (4).
