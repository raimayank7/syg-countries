Nye: public schools

25 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Nye district in Nevada: 10 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 5,657 students in 25 of them; the median school has 81 students and the largest has 1,349. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 5th largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 3.28% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Pahrump (11), Beatty (3), Gabbs (3), Round Mountain (3).
