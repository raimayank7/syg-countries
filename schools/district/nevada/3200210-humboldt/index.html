Humboldt: public schools

15 schools

Nevada, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Humboldt district in Nevada: 8 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 3,349 students in 15 of them; the median school has 37 students and the largest has 989. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 8th largest of the 20 districts in Nevada by number of public schools, and holds 1.97% of the state's 762 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Winnemucca (8), McDermitt (3), Denio (1), Kings River (1).
