Francis Howell R-Iii: public schools

23 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Francis Howell R-Iii district in Missouri: 10 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 3 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,241 students in 23 of them; the median school has 758 students and the largest has 1,844. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 0.93% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in St Charles (18), St Peters (2), Wentzville (1), O'Fallon (1).
