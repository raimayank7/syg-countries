Kansas City 33: public schools

33 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Kansas City 33 district in Missouri: 21 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,694 students in 33 of them; the median school has 410 students and the largest has 1,066. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.33% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Kansas City.
