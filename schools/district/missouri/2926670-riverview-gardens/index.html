Riverview Gardens: public schools

13 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Riverview Gardens district in Missouri: 9 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 5,100 students in 13 of them; the median school has 294 students and the largest has 1,262. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 27th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 0.53% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in St Louis (12), Saint Louis (1).
