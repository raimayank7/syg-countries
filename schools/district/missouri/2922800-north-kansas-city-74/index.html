North Kansas City 74: public schools

34 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the North Kansas City 74 district in Missouri: 22 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 21,015 students in 34 of them; the median school has 501 students and the largest has 2,006. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.37% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Kansas City (28), Gladstone (6).
