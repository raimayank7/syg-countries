Fort Zumwalt School District: public schools

27 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Fort Zumwalt School District district in Missouri: 16 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 16,829 students in 27 of them; the median school has 445 students and the largest has 1,701. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.09% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in O'Fallon (16), St Peters (9), Wentzville (1), St. Peters (1).
