Wentzville R-Iv: public schools

22 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Wentzville R-Iv district in Missouri: 13 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 17,944 students in 22 of them; the median school has 662 students and the largest has 1,669. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 0.89% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Wentzville (14), O'Fallon (4), Lake St Louis (2), Dardenne Prairie (1).
