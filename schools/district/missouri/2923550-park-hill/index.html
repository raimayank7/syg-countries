Park Hill: public schools

20 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Park Hill district in Missouri: 11 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,915 students in 20 of them; the median school has 456 students and the largest has 1,936. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 0.81% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Kansas City (18), Parkville (1), Riverside (1).
