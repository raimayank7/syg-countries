Independence 30: public schools

31 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Independence 30 district in Missouri: 20 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,168 students in 31 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 1,700. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.25% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Independence (28), Sugar Creek (2), Independnce (1).
