Division Of Youth Service: public schools

41 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Division Of Youth Service district in Missouri: 41 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 570 students in 41 of them; the median school has 12 students and the largest has 61. Grades offered run from grade 5 to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.66% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 26 towns, most in St Louis (8), Springfield (4), Poplar Bluff (2), Cape Girardeau (2).
