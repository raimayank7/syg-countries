Lee'S Summit R-Vii: public schools

29 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Lee'S Summit R-Vii district in Missouri: 18 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,797 students in 29 of them; the median school has 432 students and the largest has 1,998. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 1.17% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Lee'S Summit (24), Greenwood (1), Kansas City (1), Lees Summit (1).
