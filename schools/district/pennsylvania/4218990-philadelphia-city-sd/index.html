Philadelphia City SD: public schools

220 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 220 public schools in the Philadelphia City SD district in Pennsylvania: 149 elementary, 55 high, 14 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 6 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 114,090 students in 220 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 3,264. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 7.52% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Philadelphia (219), Philadephia (1).
