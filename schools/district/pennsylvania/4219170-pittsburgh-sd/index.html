Pittsburgh SD: public schools

56 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Pittsburgh SD district in Pennsylvania: 34 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools. The file reports 19,367 students in 56 of them; the median school has 282 students and the largest has 1,294. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 1.91% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Pittsburgh.
