Central Dauphin SD: public schools

19 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Central Dauphin SD district in Pennsylvania: 13 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high. The file reports 12,446 students in 19 of them; the median school has 579 students and the largest has 1,998. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 0.65% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Harrisburg (16), Steelton (2), Dauphin (1).
