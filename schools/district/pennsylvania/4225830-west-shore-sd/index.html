West Shore SD: public schools

13 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the West Shore SD district in Pennsylvania: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 7,448 students in 13 of them; the median school has 504 students and the largest has 1,359. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 0.44% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Lewisberry (3), New Cumberland (3), Camp Hill (3), Etters (2).
