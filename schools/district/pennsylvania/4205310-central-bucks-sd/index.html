Central Bucks SD: public schools

23 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Central Bucks SD district in Pennsylvania: 15 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. The file reports 17,189 students in 23 of them; the median school has 685 students and the largest has 1,759. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 0.79% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Doylestown (11), Warrington (5), Jamison (2), Chalfont (2).
