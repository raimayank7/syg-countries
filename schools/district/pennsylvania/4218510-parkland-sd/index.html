Parkland SD: public schools

12 schools

Pennsylvania, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Parkland SD district in Pennsylvania: 9 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high. The file reports 9,957 students in 12 of them; the median school has 493 students and the largest has 3,242. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 752 districts in Pennsylvania by number of public schools, and holds 0.41% of the state's 2,926 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Allentown (5), Breinigsville (3), Orefield (2), Coplay (1).
