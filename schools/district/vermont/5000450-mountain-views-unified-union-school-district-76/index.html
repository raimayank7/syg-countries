Mountain Views Unified Union School District #76: public schools

6 schools

Vermont, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 6 public schools in the Mountain Views Unified Union School District #76 district in Vermont: 4 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 1,018 students in 6 of them; the median school has 92 students and the largest has 456. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 110 districts in Vermont by number of public schools, and holds 1.96% of the state's 306 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Woodstock (2), Barnard (1), Reading (1), Killington (1).
