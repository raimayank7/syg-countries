Windham Southeast Unified Union School District #96: public schools

9 schools

Vermont, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in the Windham Southeast Unified Union School District #96 district in Vermont: 6 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 2,286 students in 9 of them; the median school has 192 students and the largest has 729. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 110 districts in Vermont by number of public schools, and holds 2.94% of the state's 306 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Brattleboro (6), Guilford (1), Putney (1), East Dummerston (1).
