Kingdom East Unified Union School District #64: public schools

7 schools

Vermont, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 7 public schools in the Kingdom East Unified Union School District #64 district in Vermont: 7 elementary. The file reports 1,220 students in 7 of them; the median school has 134 students and the largest has 451. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 6th largest of the 110 districts in Vermont by number of public schools, and holds 2.29% of the state's 306 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Newark (1), Sutton (1), Sheffield (1), West Burke (1).
