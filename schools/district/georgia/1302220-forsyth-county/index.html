Forsyth County: public schools

42 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Forsyth County district in Georgia: 23 elementary, 11 middle, 8 high. The file reports 54,984 students in 42 of them; the median school has 1,132 students and the largest has 3,201. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.80% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Cumming (30), Suwanee (5), Alpharetta (4), Gainesville (2).
