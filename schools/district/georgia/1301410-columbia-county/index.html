Columbia County: public schools

30 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in the Columbia County district in Georgia: 17 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high. The file reports 28,854 students in 30 of them; the median school has 829 students and the largest has 2,280. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.29% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Evans (14), Grovetown (8), Martinez (4), Harlem (3).
