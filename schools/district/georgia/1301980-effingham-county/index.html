Effingham County: public schools

13 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Effingham County district in Georgia: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 14,394 students in 13 of them; the median school has 976 students and the largest has 2,164. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 40th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 0.56% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Guyton (7), Rincon (4), Springfield (2).
