Douglas County: public schools

35 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Douglas County district in Georgia: 21 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high, 1 other. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 25,770 students in 35 of them; the median school has 554 students and the largest has 1,921. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.50% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Douglasville (25), Lithia Springs (6), Winston (3), Villa Rica (1).
