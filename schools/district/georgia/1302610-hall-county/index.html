Hall County: public schools

37 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Hall County district in Georgia: 20 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle. The file reports 27,245 students in 37 of them; the median school has 694 students and the largest has 1,350. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.59% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Gainesville (23), Flowery Branch (9), Oakwood (3), Lula (1).
