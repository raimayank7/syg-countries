Newton County: public schools

22 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Newton County district in Georgia: 14 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. The file reports 18,886 students in 22 of them; the median school has 646 students and the largest has 2,563. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 24th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 0.95% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Covington (20), Mansfield (1), Oxford (1).
