Floyd County: public schools

15 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Floyd County district in Georgia: 8 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. The file reports 8,847 students in 15 of them; the median school has 513 students and the largest has 919. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 36th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 0.64% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Rome (10), Lindale (4), Armuchee (1).
