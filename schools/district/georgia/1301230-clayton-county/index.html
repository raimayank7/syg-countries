Clayton County: public schools

67 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 67 public schools in the Clayton County district in Georgia: 39 elementary, 15 middle, 13 high. 3 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 50,832 students in 67 of them; the median school has 649 students and the largest has 2,030. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 2.88% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Jonesboro (22), Riverdale (10), Forest Park (7), Morrow (6).
