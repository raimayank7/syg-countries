Department of Juvenile Justice: public schools

28 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Department of Juvenile Justice district in Georgia: 26 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 19 alternative schools, 9 special education schools. The file reports 422 students in 28 of them; the median school has 14 students and the largest has 43. Grades offered run from grade 3 to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.20% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 23 towns, most in Macon (3), Augusta (2), Savannah (2), Midland (2).
