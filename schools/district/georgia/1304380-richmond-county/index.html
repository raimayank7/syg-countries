Richmond County: public schools

50 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the Richmond County district in Georgia: 30 elementary, 11 high, 8 middle, 1 other. The file reports 29,384 students in 50 of them; the median school has 537 students and the largest has 1,175. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 2.15% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Augusta (39), Hephzibah (9), Blythe (1), Fort Gordon (1).
