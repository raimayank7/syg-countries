Gwinnett County: public schools

140 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 140 public schools in the Gwinnett County district in Georgia: 82 elementary, 29 middle, 27 high, 2 other. 1 of them (0.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 182,214 students in 140 of them; the median school has 1,065 students and the largest has 3,880. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 6.02% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Lawrenceville (37), Snellville (14), Lilburn (13), Duluth (13).
