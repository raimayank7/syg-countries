Atlanta Public Schools: public schools

87 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 87 public schools in the Atlanta Public Schools district in Georgia: 55 elementary, 15 middle, 15 high, 2 other. 19 of them (21.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 49,660 students in 87 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 2,332. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 3.74% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Atlanta.
