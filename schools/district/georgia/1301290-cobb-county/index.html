Cobb County: public schools

110 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 110 public schools in the Cobb County district in Georgia: 66 elementary, 26 middle, 17 high, 1 other. 1 of them (0.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 106,358 students in 110 of them; the median school has 827 students and the largest has 3,030. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 4.73% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Marietta (46), Kennesaw (15), Smyrna (12), Powder Springs (11).
