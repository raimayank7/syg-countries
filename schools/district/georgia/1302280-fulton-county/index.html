Fulton County: public schools

106 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 106 public schools in the Fulton County district in Georgia: 63 elementary, 21 high, 20 middle, 2 other. 7 of them (6.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 88,043 students in 106 of them; the median school has 690 students and the largest has 2,156. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 4.56% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Alpharetta (25), Atlanta (19), Roswell (16), College Park (13).
