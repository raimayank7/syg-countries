DeKalb County: public schools

131 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 131 public schools in the DeKalb County district in Georgia: 82 elementary, 23 high, 22 middle, 4 other. 8 of them (6.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 91,398 students in 131 of them; the median school has 587 students and the largest has 2,186. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 5.63% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Decatur (30), Stone Mountain (24), Lithonia (20), Atlanta (19).
