Carroll County: public schools

24 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Carroll County district in Georgia: 12 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 1 other. The file reports 16,299 students in 24 of them; the median school has 641 students and the largest has 1,795. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 1.03% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Carrollton (8), Temple (5), Villa Rica (5), Bowdon (4).
