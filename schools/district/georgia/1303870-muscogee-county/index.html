Muscogee County: public schools

53 schools

Georgia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in the Muscogee County district in Georgia: 32 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high. The file reports 29,698 students in 53 of them; the median school has 461 students and the largest has 1,417. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 236 districts in Georgia by number of public schools, and holds 2.28% of the state's 2,327 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Columbus (49), Midland (4).
