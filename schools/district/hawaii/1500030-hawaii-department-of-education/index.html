Hawaii, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 295 public schools in the Hawaii Department of Education district in Hawaii: 184 elementary, 44 high, 42 middle, 24 other, 1 secondary. 37 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 169,308 students in 295 of them; the median school has 483 students and the largest has 2,875. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 1 districts in Hawaii by number of public schools, and holds 100.00% of the state's 295 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 61 towns, most in Honolulu (74), Hilo (16), Kailua (13), Kaneohe (12).
