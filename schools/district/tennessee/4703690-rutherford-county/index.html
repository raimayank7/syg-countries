Rutherford County: public schools

51 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 51 public schools in the Rutherford County district in Tennessee: 25 elementary, 13 high, 11 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 51,595 students in 51 of them; the median school has 902 students and the largest has 2,446. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 2.68% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Murfreesboro (20), Smyrna (17), Lavergne (3), Christiana (3).
