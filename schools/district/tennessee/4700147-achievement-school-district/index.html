Achievement School District: public schools

13 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Achievement School District district in Tennessee: 6 middle, 4 elementary, 3 high. 13 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 4,501 students in 13 of them; the median school has 366 students and the largest has 630. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 36th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 0.68% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Memphis (12), Nashville (1).
