Hawkins County: public schools

18 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Hawkins County district in Tennessee: 9 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,223 students in 18 of them; the median school has 324 students and the largest has 1,007. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 0.94% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Rogersville (6), Church Hill (5), Bulls Gap (2), Surgoinsville (2).
