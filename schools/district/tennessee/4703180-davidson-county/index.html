Davidson County: public schools

162 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 162 public schools in the Davidson County district in Tennessee: 82 elementary, 37 middle, 33 high, 7 prekindergarten, 3 other. 27 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 80,468 students in 162 of them; the median school has 441 students and the largest has 2,131. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 8.50% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Nashville (121), Antioch (19), Madison (7), Hermitage (5).
