Memphis-Shelby County Schools: public schools

222 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 222 public schools in the Memphis-Shelby County Schools district in Tennessee: 121 elementary, 50 high, 33 middle, 13 prekindergarten, 5 other. 53 of them (23.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 110,057 students in 222 of them; the median school has 434 students and the largest has 2,194. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 11.65% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Memphis (201), Cordova (10), Germantown (4), Millington (3).
