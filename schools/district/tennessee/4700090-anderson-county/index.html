Anderson County: public schools

19 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Anderson County district in Tennessee: 9 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,157 students in 19 of them; the median school has 268 students and the largest has 1,073. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 1.00% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Clinton (9), Lake City (2), Norris (2), Oliver Springs (2).
