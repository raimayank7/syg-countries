Hamilton County: public schools

81 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in the Hamilton County district in Tennessee: 42 elementary, 22 high, 15 middle, 2 other. 8 of them (9.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 45,790 students in 81 of them; the median school has 518 students and the largest has 1,501. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 4.25% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Chattanooga (48), Ooltewah (8), Hixson (7), Soddy Daisy (7).
