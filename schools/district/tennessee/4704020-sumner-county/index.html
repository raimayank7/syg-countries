Sumner County: public schools

52 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Sumner County district in Tennessee: 27 elementary, 12 middle, 11 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 30,661 students in 52 of them; the median school has 563 students and the largest has 1,677. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 2.73% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Gallatin (17), Hendersonville (16), Portland (8), Westmoreland (3).
