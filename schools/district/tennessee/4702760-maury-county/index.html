Maury County: public schools

23 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Maury County district in Tennessee: 10 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high, 3 other. The file reports 13,084 students in 23 of them; the median school has 538 students and the largest has 1,409. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 1.21% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Columbia (12), Spring Hill (5), Mt Pleasant (2), Culleoka (1).
