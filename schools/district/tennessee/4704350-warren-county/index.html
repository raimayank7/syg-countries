Warren County: public schools

12 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Warren County district in Tennessee: 8 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,447 students in 12 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 1,845. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 37th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 0.63% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in McMinnville (10), Rock Island (1), Morrison (1).
