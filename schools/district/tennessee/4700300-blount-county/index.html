Blount County: public schools

22 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Blount County district in Tennessee: 14 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. The file reports 10,191 students in 22 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 1,513. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 1.15% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Maryville (14), Friendsville (3), Rockford (1), Townsend (1).
