Montgomery County: public schools

44 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Montgomery County district in Tennessee: 25 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle, 1 other. The file reports 39,345 students in 44 of them; the median school has 930 students and the largest has 1,654. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 2.31% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Clarksville (40), Cunningham (3), Woodlawn (1).
