Hamblen County: public schools

18 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Hamblen County district in Tennessee: 11 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,287 students in 18 of them; the median school has 561 students and the largest has 1,520. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 21st largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 0.94% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Morristown (15), Whitesburg (2), Russellville (1).
