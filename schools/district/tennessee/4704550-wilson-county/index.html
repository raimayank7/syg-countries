Wilson County: public schools

25 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Wilson County district in Tennessee: 13 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 20,548 students in 25 of them; the median school has 747 students and the largest has 1,808. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Mt Juliet (8), Lebanon (8), Mt. Juliet (5), Watertown (3).
