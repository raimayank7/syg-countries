Chandler Unified District #80 (4242): public schools

44 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Chandler Unified District #80 (4242) district in Arizona: 31 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 1 other. The file reports 42,832 students in 44 of them; the median school has 699 students and the largest has 3,675. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.81% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Chandler (34), Gilbert (6), Queen Creek (3), Sun Lakes (1).
