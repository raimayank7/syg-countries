Avondale Elementary District (4272): public schools

9 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in the Avondale Elementary District (4272) district in Arizona: 8 elementary, 1 middle. The file reports 5,960 students in 9 of them; the median school has 646 students and the largest has 1,016. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 62nd largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Goodyear (6), Avondale (3).
