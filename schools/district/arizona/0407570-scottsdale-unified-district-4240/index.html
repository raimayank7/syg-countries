Scottsdale Unified District (4240): public schools

30 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in the Scottsdale Unified District (4240) district in Arizona: 19 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high, 1 other. The file reports 20,940 students in 30 of them; the median school has 563 students and the largest has 1,940. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.24% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Scottsdale (24), Phoenix (4), Paradise Valley (2).
