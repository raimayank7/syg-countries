Gilbert Unified District (4239): public schools

38 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in the Gilbert Unified District (4239) district in Arizona: 26 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. The file reports 32,402 students in 38 of them; the median school has 653 students and the largest has 3,027. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.57% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Gilbert (29), Mesa (9).
