19 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Northern Arizona Vocational Institute of Technolog (78786) district in Arizona: 19 high. Besides regular schools there are 19 career and technical schools. The file reports 34 students in 19 of them; the median school has 1 students and the largest has 10. Grades offered run from grade 6 to grade 12.

This is the 26th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.78% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Show Low (3), Snowflake (2), Holbrook (2), Winslow (2).
