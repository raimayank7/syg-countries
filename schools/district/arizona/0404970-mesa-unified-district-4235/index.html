Mesa Unified District (4235): public schools

78 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in the Mesa Unified District (4235) district in Arizona: 56 elementary, 10 middle, 7 high, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 57,204 students in 78 of them; the median school has 540 students and the largest has 3,469. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 3.21% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Mesa (74), Chandler (4).
