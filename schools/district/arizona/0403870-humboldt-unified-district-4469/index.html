Humboldt Unified District (4469): public schools

11 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the Humboldt Unified District (4469) district in Arizona: 6 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 5,432 students in 11 of them; the median school has 365 students and the largest has 1,666. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 47th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.45% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Prescott Valley (9), Dewey-Humboldt (2).
