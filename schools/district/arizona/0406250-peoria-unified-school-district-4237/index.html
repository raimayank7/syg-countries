Peoria Unified School District (4237): public schools

44 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Peoria Unified School District (4237) district in Arizona: 33 elementary, 8 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 35,466 students in 44 of them; the median school has 696 students and the largest has 2,562. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.81% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Peoria (27), Glendale (15), Sun City (2).
