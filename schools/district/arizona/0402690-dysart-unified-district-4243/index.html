Dysart Unified District (4243): public schools

25 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Dysart Unified District (4243) district in Arizona: 20 elementary, 5 high. The file reports 23,118 students in 25 of them; the median school has 798 students and the largest has 2,443. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.03% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Surprise (17), El Mirage (6), Glendale (1), Waddell (1).
