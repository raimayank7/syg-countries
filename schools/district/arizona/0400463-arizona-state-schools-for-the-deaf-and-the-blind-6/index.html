Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Arizona State Schools for the Deaf and the Blind (6393) district in Arizona: 12 prekindergarten, 7 other. Besides regular schools there are 19 special education schools. The file reports 425 students in 19 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 215. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.78% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Phoenix (10), Tucson (5), Holbrook (1), Yuma (1).
