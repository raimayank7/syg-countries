Deer Valley Unified District (4246): public schools

41 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Deer Valley Unified District (4246) district in Arizona: 31 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 32,837 students in 41 of them; the median school has 597 students and the largest has 2,787. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.69% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Glendale (17), Phoenix (16), Anthem (5), Peoria (2).
