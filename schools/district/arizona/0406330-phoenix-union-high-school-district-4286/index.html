Phoenix Union High School District (4286): public schools

20 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Phoenix Union High School District (4286) district in Arizona: 20 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 26,753 students in 20 of them; the median school has 1,787 students and the largest has 2,803. Grades offered run from grade 9 to grade 12.

This is the 21st largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.82% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Phoenix (18), Laveen (2).
