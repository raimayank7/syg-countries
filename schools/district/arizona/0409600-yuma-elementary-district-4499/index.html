Yuma Elementary District (4499): public schools

19 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Yuma Elementary District (4499) district in Arizona: 14 elementary, 5 middle. The file reports 8,980 students in 19 of them; the median school has 509 students and the largest has 917. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 23rd largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 0.78% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Yuma.
