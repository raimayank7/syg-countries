West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923): public schools

59 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 59 public schools in the West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) district in Arizona: 59 high. Besides regular schools there are 59 career and technical schools. The file reports 423 students in 59 of them; the median school has 6 students and the largest has 27. Grades offered run from grade 7 to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 2.43% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Glendale (14), Phoenix (14), Surprise (5), Avondale (5).
