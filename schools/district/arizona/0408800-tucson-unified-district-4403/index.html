Tucson Unified District (4403): public schools

90 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 90 public schools in the Tucson Unified District (4403) district in Arizona: 62 elementary, 13 high, 12 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 40,929 students in 90 of them; the median school has 330 students and the largest has 3,039. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 3.71% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Tucson.
