Washington Elementary School District (4260): public schools

33 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Washington Elementary School District (4260) district in Arizona: 28 elementary, 5 middle. The file reports 18,766 students in 33 of them; the median school has 549 students and the largest has 982. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 11th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.36% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Phoenix (29), Glendale (4).
