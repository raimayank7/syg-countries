Pima County JTED (89380): public schools

55 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the Pima County JTED (89380) district in Arizona: 55 high. Besides regular schools there are 55 career and technical schools. The file reports 160 students in 55 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 48. Grades offered run from grade 9 to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 2.27% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Tucson (43), Sahuarita (3), Nogales (2), Topawa (1).
