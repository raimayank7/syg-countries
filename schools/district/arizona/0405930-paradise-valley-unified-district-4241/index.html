Paradise Valley Unified District (4241): public schools

45 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Paradise Valley Unified District (4241) district in Arizona: 27 elementary, 9 middle, 5 high, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 27,034 students in 45 of them; the median school has 479 students and the largest has 2,501. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.85% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Phoenix (33), Scottsdale (12).
