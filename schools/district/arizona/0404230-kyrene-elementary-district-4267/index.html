Kyrene Elementary District (4267): public schools

26 schools

Arizona, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Kyrene Elementary District (4267) district in Arizona: 20 elementary, 6 middle. The file reports 13,800 students in 26 of them; the median school has 488 students and the largest has 914. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 13th largest of the 658 districts in Arizona by number of public schools, and holds 1.07% of the state's 2,427 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Phoenix (12), Chandler (7), Tempe (7).
