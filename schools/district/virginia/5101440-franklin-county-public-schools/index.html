Franklin County Public Schools: public schools

14 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Franklin County Public Schools district in Virginia: 12 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 6,160 students in 14 of them; the median school has 234 students and the largest has 1,899. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 39th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.66% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Rocky Mount (5), Wirtz (2), Boones Mill (1), Callaway (1).
