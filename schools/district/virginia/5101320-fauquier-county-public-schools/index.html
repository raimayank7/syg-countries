Fauquier County Public Schools: public schools

19 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Fauquier County Public Schools district in Virginia: 11 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,844 students in 18 of them; the median school has 516 students and the largest has 1,360. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 29th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.89% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Warrenton (6), Bealeton (4), Marshall (2), Nokesville (2).
