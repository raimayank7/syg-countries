Richmond City Public Schools: public schools

54 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Richmond City Public Schools district in Virginia: 26 elementary, 10 high, 7 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 2 other. 2 of them (3.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 20,819 students in 48 of them; the median school has 376 students and the largest has 1,507. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 2.54% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Richmond.
