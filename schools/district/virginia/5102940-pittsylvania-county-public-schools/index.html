Pittsylvania County Public Schools: public schools

21 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Pittsylvania County Public Schools district in Virginia: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,860 students in 18 of them; the median school has 435 students and the largest has 850. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 24th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.99% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Chatham (7), Gretna (4), Ringgold (3), Danville (3).
