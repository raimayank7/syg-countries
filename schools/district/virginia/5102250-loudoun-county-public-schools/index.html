Loudoun County Public Schools: public schools

100 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 100 public schools in the Loudoun County Public Schools district in Virginia: 61 elementary, 17 middle, 13 high, 5 other. 2 of them (2.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 81,636 students in 96 of them; the median school has 695 students and the largest has 2,073. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 4.70% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Ashburn (25), Leesburg (23), Sterling (19), Aldie (10).
