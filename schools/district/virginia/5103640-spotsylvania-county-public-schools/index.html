Spotsylvania County Public Schools: public schools

34 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Spotsylvania County Public Schools district in Virginia: 17 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,012 students in 29 of them; the median school has 713 students and the largest has 1,950. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 1.60% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Spotsylvania (20), Fredericksburg (14).
