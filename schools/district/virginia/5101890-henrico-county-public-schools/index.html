Henrico County Public Schools: public schools

81 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in the Henrico County Public Schools district in Virginia: 46 elementary, 12 middle, 10 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 50,463 students in 69 of them; the median school has 565 students and the largest has 2,039. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.80% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Richmond (46), Glen Allen (20), Highland Springs (8), Sandston (4).
