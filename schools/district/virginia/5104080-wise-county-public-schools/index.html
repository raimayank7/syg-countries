Wise County Public Schools: public schools

13 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Wise County Public Schools district in Virginia: 5 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,883 students in 11 of them; the median school has 542 students and the largest has 830. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 41st largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.61% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Wise (4), Coeburn (3), Big Stone Gap (3), Norton (1).
