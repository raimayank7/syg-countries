Roanoke County Public Schools: public schools

29 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Roanoke County Public Schools district in Virginia: 16 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,799 students in 26 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 1,127. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 1.36% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Roanoke (18), Salem (7), Vinton (4).
