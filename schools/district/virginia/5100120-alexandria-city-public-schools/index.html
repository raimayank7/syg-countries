Alexandria City Public Schools: public schools

19 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Alexandria City Public Schools district in Virginia: 14 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 16,406 students in 18 of them; the median school has 647 students and the largest has 4,551. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 30th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.89% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Alexandria.
