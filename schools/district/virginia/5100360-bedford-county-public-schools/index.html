Bedford County Public Schools: public schools

22 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Bedford County Public Schools district in Virginia: 13 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,059 students in 19 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 1,311. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 23rd largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 1.03% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Bedford (7), Forest (5), Moneta (3), Goodview (2).
