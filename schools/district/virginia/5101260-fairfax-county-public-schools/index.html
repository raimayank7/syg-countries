Fairfax County Public Schools: public schools

223 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 223 public schools in the Fairfax County Public Schools district in Virginia: 141 elementary, 28 high, 24 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 11 career and technical schools, 7 special education schools. The file reports 180,714 students in 199 of them; the median school has 699 students and the largest has 4,397. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 10.47% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 21 towns, most in Alexandria (44), Falls Church (27), Springfield (25), Fairfax (25).
