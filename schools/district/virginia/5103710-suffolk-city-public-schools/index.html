Suffolk City Public Schools: public schools

22 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Suffolk City Public Schools district in Virginia: 11 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,520 students in 19 of them; the median school has 704 students and the largest has 1,681. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 1.03% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Suffolk.
