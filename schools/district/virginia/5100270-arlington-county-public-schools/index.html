Arlington County Public Schools: public schools

42 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Arlington County Public Schools district in Virginia: 25 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,508 students in 36 of them; the median school has 563 students and the largest has 2,900. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 1.97% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Arlington.
