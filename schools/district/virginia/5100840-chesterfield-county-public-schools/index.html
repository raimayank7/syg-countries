Chesterfield County Public Schools: public schools

73 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 73 public schools in the Chesterfield County Public Schools district in Virginia: 41 elementary, 12 middle, 11 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 4 career and technical schools. The file reports 64,132 students in 65 of them; the median school has 798 students and the largest has 2,570. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.43% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Midlothian (26), North Chesterfield (16), Chester (11), Chesterfield (10).
