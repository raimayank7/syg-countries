Norfolk City Public Schools: public schools

49 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Norfolk City Public Schools district in Virginia: 30 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 26,882 students in 44 of them; the median school has 503 students and the largest has 1,850. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 2.30% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Norfolk.
