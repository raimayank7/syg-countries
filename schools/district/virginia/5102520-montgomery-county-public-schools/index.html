Montgomery County Public Schools: public schools

21 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Montgomery County Public Schools district in Virginia: 11 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,534 students in 19 of them; the median school has 387 students and the largest has 1,281. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.99% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Christiansburg (7), Blacksburg (7), Riner (3), Elliston (2).
