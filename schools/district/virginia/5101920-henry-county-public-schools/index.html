Henry County Public Schools: public schools

13 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Henry County Public Schools district in Virginia: 9 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle. The file reports 6,938 students in 13 of them; the median school has 386 students and the largest has 1,140. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 42nd largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.61% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Martinsville (5), Bassett (3), Ridgeway (2), Collinsville (1).
