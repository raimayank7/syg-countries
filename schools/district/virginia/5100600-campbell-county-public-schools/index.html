Campbell County Public Schools: public schools

15 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Campbell County Public Schools district in Virginia: 7 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,896 students in 13 of them; the median school has 631 students and the largest has 1,015. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 36th largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 0.70% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Rustburg (6), Lynchburg (4), Altavista (2), Concord (1).
