Prince William County Public Schools: public schools

99 schools

Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 99 public schools in the Prince William County Public Schools district in Virginia: 65 elementary, 17 middle, 13 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 90,550 students in 96 of them; the median school has 743 students and the largest has 2,945. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 208 districts in Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 4.65% of the state's 2,129 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Woodbridge (40), Manassas (24), Dumfries (10), Gainesville (7).
