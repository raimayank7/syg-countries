Guam Department of Education: public schools

41 schools

Guam, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Guam Department of Education district in Guam: 26 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 5 districts in Guam by number of public schools, and holds 91.11% of the state's 45 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 18 towns, most in Dededo (8), Yigo (5), Barrigada (4), Tamuning (4).
