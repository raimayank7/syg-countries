Blue Valley (district 2012000): public schools

37 schools

Kansas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Blue Valley (district 2012000) district in Kansas: 22 elementary, 9 middle, 6 high. The file reports 22,365 students in 37 of them; the median school has 520 students and the largest has 1,558. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 290 districts in Kansas by number of public schools, and holds 2.74% of the state's 1,348 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Overland Park (29), Leawood (5), Stilwell (2), Olathe (1).
