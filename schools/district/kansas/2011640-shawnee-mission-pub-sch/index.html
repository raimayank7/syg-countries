Shawnee Mission Pub Sch: public schools

45 schools

Kansas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Shawnee Mission Pub Sch district in Kansas: 34 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle. The file reports 26,269 students in 45 of them; the median school has 458 students and the largest has 1,671. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 290 districts in Kansas by number of public schools, and holds 3.34% of the state's 1,348 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Overland Park (16), Shawnee (10), Prairie Village (6), Lenexa (5).
