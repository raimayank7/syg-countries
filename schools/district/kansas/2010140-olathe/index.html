Olathe: public schools

52 schools

Kansas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Olathe district in Kansas: 36 elementary, 10 middle, 6 high. The file reports 28,590 students in 52 of them; the median school has 404 students and the largest has 2,036. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 290 districts in Kansas by number of public schools, and holds 3.86% of the state's 1,348 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Olathe (48), Overland Park (2), Lenexa (2).
