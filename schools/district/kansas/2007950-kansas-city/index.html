Kansas City: public schools

43 schools

Kansas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Kansas City district in Kansas: 28 elementary, 7 middle, 7 high, 1 other. The file reports 21,132 students in 43 of them; the median school has 400 students and the largest has 1,841. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 290 districts in Kansas by number of public schools, and holds 3.19% of the state's 1,348 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Kansas City.
