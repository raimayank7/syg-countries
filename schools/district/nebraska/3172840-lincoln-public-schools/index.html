Lincoln Public Schools: public schools

78 schools

Nebraska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in the Lincoln Public Schools district in Nebraska: 41 elementary, 16 high, 14 middle, 7 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 41,654 students in 78 of them; the median school has 427 students and the largest has 2,118. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 272 districts in Nebraska by number of public schools, and holds 7.10% of the state's 1,098 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Lincoln.
