Millard Public Schools: public schools

36 schools

Nebraska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in the Millard Public Schools district in Nebraska: 25 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 23,300 students in 36 of them; the median school has 463 students and the largest has 2,579. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 272 districts in Nebraska by number of public schools, and holds 3.28% of the state's 1,098 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Omaha (35), Gretna (1).
