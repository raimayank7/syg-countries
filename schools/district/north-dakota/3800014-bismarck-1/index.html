Bismarck 1: public schools

29 schools

North Dakota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Bismarck 1 district in North Dakota: 19 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 14,106 students in 29 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,411. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 171 districts in North Dakota by number of public schools, and holds 5.72% of the state's 507 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Bismarck (28), Lincoln (1).
