Wisconsin Department of Corrections: public schools

33 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Wisconsin Department of Corrections district in Wisconsin: 33 high. Besides regular schools there are 33 alternative schools. The file reports 167 students in 33 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 47. Grades offered run from grade 6 to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 1.48% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 27 towns, most in Black River Falls (2), Irma (2), Waupun (2), Milwaukee (2).
