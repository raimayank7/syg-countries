Madison Metropolitan School District: public schools

54 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Madison Metropolitan School District district in Wisconsin: 32 elementary, 13 middle, 7 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (3.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 25,247 students in 54 of them; the median school has 385 students and the largest has 2,137. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 2.43% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Madison (52), Verona (1), Monona (1).
