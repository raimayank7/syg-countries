Kenosha School District: public schools

41 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Kenosha School District district in Wisconsin: 25 elementary, 8 high, 5 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. 4 of them (9.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 18,719 students in 41 of them; the median school has 352 students and the largest has 2,001. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 1.84% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Kenosha (37), Pleasant Prairie (4).
