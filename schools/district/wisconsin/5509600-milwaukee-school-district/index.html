Milwaukee School District: public schools

156 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 156 public schools in the Milwaukee School District district in Wisconsin: 107 elementary, 34 high, 8 middle, 5 other, 2 prekindergarten. 19 of them (12.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 13 alternative schools. The file reports 66,864 students in 156 of them; the median school has 349 students and the largest has 1,894. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 7.01% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Milwaukee.
