Racine Unified School District: public schools

27 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Racine Unified School District district in Wisconsin: 16 elementary, 6 high, 2 prekindergarten, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 15,963 students in 27 of them; the median school has 495 students and the largest has 1,845. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 1.21% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Racine (25), Sturtevant (2).
