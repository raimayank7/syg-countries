Waukesha School District: public schools

27 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Waukesha School District district in Wisconsin: 14 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (18.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,318 students in 27 of them; the median school has 345 students and the largest has 1,192. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 1.21% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Waukesha.
