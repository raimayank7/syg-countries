Green Bay Area Public School District: public schools

44 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Green Bay Area Public School District district in Wisconsin: 28 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 3 other, 3 prekindergarten. 2 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 18,579 students in 44 of them; the median school has 307 students and the largest has 2,147. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 1.98% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Green Bay.
