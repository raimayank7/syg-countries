Beloit School District: public schools

12 schools

Wisconsin, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Beloit School District district in Wisconsin: 6 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,098 students in 12 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 1,378. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 28th largest of the 454 districts in Wisconsin by number of public schools, and holds 0.54% of the state's 2,224 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Beloit.
