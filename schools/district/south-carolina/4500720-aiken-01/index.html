Aiken 01: public schools

41 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Aiken 01 district in South Carolina: 21 elementary, 11 middle, 9 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 23,103 students in 41 of them; the median school has 501 students and the largest has 1,697. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 3.23% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Aiken (15), North Augusta (7), Graniteville (4), Warrenville (4).
