Richland 01: public schools

48 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in the Richland 01 district in South Carolina: 29 elementary, 9 middle, 9 high, 1 other. 2 of them (4.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,123 students in 48 of them; the median school has 376 students and the largest has 1,334. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 3.78% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Columbia (41), Hopkins (5), Gadsden (1), Eastover (1).
