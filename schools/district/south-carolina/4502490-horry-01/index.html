Horry 01: public schools

57 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in the Horry 01 district in South Carolina: 30 elementary, 14 high, 13 middle. 4 of them (7.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 48,205 students in 57 of them; the median school has 791 students and the largest has 3,181. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 4.49% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Myrtle Beach (25), Conway (15), Loris (4), Little River (4).
