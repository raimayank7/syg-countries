Pickens 01: public schools

24 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Pickens 01 district in South Carolina: 14 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 16,223 students in 24 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 1,885. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.89% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Easley (9), Pickens (5), Liberty (5), Central (3).
