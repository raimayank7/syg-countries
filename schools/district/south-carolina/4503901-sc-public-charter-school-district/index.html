SC Public Charter School District: public schools

39 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the SC Public Charter School District district in South Carolina: 17 elementary, 11 high, 8 other, 3 middle. 39 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 18,404 students in 39 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 1,681. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 3.07% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 25 towns, most in Greenville (5), Spartanburg (3), Columbia (3), N Charleston (3).
