Orangeburg: public schools

27 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Orangeburg district in South Carolina: 15 elementary, 8 high, 4 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,620 students in 27 of them; the median school has 367 students and the largest has 1,050. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 2.13% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Orangeburg (9), Neeses (2), Rowesville (2), Holly Hill (2).
