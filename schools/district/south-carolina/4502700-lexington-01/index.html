Lexington 01: public schools

31 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Lexington 01 district in South Carolina: 17 elementary, 8 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 27,105 students in 31 of them; the median school has 790 students and the largest has 2,475. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 2.44% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Lexington (21), Gilbert (4), Pelion (4), West Columbia (2).
