Charleston 01: public schools

82 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in the Charleston 01 district in South Carolina: 51 elementary, 18 high, 12 middle, 1 other. 9 of them (11.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 50,400 students in 82 of them; the median school has 507 students and the largest has 2,593. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 6.46% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Charleston (29), North Charleston (26), Mt. Pleasant (10), Mount Pleasant (5).
