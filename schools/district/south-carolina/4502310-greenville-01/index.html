Greenville 01: public schools

93 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 93 public schools in the Greenville 01 district in South Carolina: 51 elementary, 20 middle, 19 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 1 of them (1.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 78,371 students in 93 of them; the median school has 796 students and the largest has 2,274. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 7.32% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Greenville (45), Simpsonville (14), Greer (12), Taylors (8).
