Berkeley 01: public schools

47 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in the Berkeley 01 district in South Carolina: 27 elementary, 11 middle, 9 high. The file reports 39,265 students in 47 of them; the median school has 724 students and the largest has 2,710. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 3.70% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Goose Creek (13), Moncks Corner (9), Summerville (7), Hanahan (4).
