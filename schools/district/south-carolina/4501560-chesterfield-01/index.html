Chesterfield 01: public schools

16 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Chesterfield 01 district in South Carolina: 9 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. The file reports 6,975 students in 16 of them; the median school has 420 students and the largest has 653. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 26th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.26% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Cheraw (4), Chesterfield (3), Pageland (3), Jefferson (2).
