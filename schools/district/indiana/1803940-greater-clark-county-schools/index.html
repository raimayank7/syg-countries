Greater Clark County Schools: public schools

18 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Greater Clark County Schools district in Indiana: 10 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,497 students in 18 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 2,141. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 0.94% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Jeffersonville (11), Charlestown (4), New Washington (2), Clarksville (1).
