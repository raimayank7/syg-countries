Fort Wayne Community Schools: public schools

50 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the Fort Wayne Community Schools district in Indiana: 33 elementary, 10 middle, 5 high, 2 other. The file reports 28,500 students in 50 of them; the median school has 497 students and the largest has 2,158. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.61% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Fort Wayne.
