Indianapolis Public Schools: public schools

48 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in the Indianapolis Public Schools district in Indiana: 35 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle, 3 other. The file reports 21,863 students in 48 of them; the median school has 402 students and the largest has 2,524. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.50% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Indianapolis.
