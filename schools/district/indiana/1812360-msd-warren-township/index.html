MSD Warren Township: public schools

16 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the MSD Warren Township district in Indiana: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,440 students in 16 of them; the median school has 502 students and the largest has 3,398. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 0.83% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Indianapolis.
