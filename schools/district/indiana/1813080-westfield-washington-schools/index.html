Westfield-Washington Schools: public schools

10 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in the Westfield-Washington Schools district in Indiana: 6 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 9,701 students in 10 of them; the median school has 673 students and the largest has 2,798. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 38th largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 0.52% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Westfield.
