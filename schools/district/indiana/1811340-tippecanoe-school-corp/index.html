Tippecanoe School Corp: public schools

20 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Tippecanoe School Corp district in Indiana: 11 elementary, 6 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,526 students in 20 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 2,169. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 1.04% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Lafayette (13), West Lafayette (4), Battle Ground (1), West Lafeyette (1).
