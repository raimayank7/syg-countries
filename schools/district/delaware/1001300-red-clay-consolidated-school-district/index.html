Red Clay Consolidated School District: public schools

28 schools

Delaware, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Red Clay Consolidated School District district in Delaware: 15 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 14,546 students in 27 of them; the median school has 493 students and the largest has 1,163. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 42 districts in Delaware by number of public schools, and holds 11.97% of the state's 234 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Wilmington (22), Hockessin (3), Newark (2), Greenville (1).
