Brandywine School District: public schools

17 schools

Delaware, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Brandywine School District district in Delaware: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 10,350 students in 16 of them; the median school has 612 students and the largest has 1,074. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 42 districts in Delaware by number of public schools, and holds 7.26% of the state's 234 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Wilmington (14), Claymont (3).
