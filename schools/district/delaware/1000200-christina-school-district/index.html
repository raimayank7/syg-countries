Christina School District: public schools

29 schools

Delaware, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Christina School District district in Delaware: 17 elementary, 4 other, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,076 students in 28 of them; the median school has 399 students and the largest has 1,340. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 42 districts in Delaware by number of public schools, and holds 12.39% of the state's 234 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Newark (23), Wilmington (5), Bear (1).
