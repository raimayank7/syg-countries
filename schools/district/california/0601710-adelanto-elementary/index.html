Adelanto Elementary: public schools

15 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Adelanto Elementary district in California: 12 elementary, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,635 students in 15 of them; the median school has 519 students and the largest has 798. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 163rd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.15% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Adelanto (9), Victorville (6).
