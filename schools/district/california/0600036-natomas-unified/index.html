Natomas Unified: public schools

20 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Natomas Unified district in California: 12 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 5 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,928 students in 20 of them; the median school has 636 students and the largest has 2,242. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 108th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.19% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Sacramento.
