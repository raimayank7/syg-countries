Paramount Unified: public schools

20 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Paramount Unified district in California: 11 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 12,015 students in 20 of them; the median school has 467 students and the largest has 3,547. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 105th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.19% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Paramount (16), Lakewood (2), Long Beach (1), South Gate (1).
