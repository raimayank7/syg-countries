Corona-Norco Unified: public schools

53 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in the Corona-Norco Unified district in California: 33 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 50,157 students in 53 of them; the median school has 741 students and the largest has 4,464. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.51% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Corona (34), Norco (10), Eastvale (8), Jurupa Valley (1).
