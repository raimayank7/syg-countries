Napa Valley Unified: public schools

26 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Napa Valley Unified district in California: 16 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 16,096 students in 26 of them; the median school has 480 students and the largest has 1,757. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 66th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.25% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Napa (21), American Canyon (5).
