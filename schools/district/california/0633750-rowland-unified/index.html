Rowland Unified: public schools

23 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Rowland Unified district in California: 14 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,607 students in 22 of them; the median school has 425 students and the largest has 1,954. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 83rd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.22% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Rowland Heights (9), La Puente (9), West Covina (3), Walnut (2).
