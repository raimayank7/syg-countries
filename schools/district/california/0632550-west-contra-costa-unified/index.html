West Contra Costa Unified: public schools

54 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the West Contra Costa Unified district in California: 38 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 25,459 students in 53 of them; the median school has 405 students and the largest has 1,476. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.52% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Richmond (22), San Pablo (9), Pinole (7), El Cerrito (6).
