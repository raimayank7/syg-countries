Alum Rock Union Elementary: public schools

22 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Alum Rock Union Elementary district in California: 17 elementary, 5 middle. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 7,362 students in 22 of them; the median school has 337 students and the largest has 498. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 94th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.21% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Jose.
