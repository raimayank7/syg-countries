San Jose Unified: public schools

41 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the San Jose Unified district in California: 27 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 24,932 students in 41 of them; the median school has 481 students and the largest has 1,596. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 28th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.40% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Jose.
