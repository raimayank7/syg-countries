Lodi Unified: public schools

49 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Lodi Unified district in California: 32 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (2.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,108 students in 49 of them; the median school has 443 students and the largest has 2,088. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.47% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Lodi (23), Stockton (22), Acampo (2), Lockeford (1).
