Riverside Unified: public schools

49 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Riverside Unified district in California: 29 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 38,830 students in 48 of them; the median school has 675 students and the largest has 2,813. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.47% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Riverside.
