Vista Unified: public schools

28 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Vista Unified district in California: 16 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 18,445 students in 28 of them; the median school has 553 students and the largest has 1,962. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 56th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.27% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Vista (20), Oceanside (8).
