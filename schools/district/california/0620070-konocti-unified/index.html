Konocti Unified: public schools

11 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the Konocti Unified district in California: 4 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 3,873 students in 10 of them; the median school has 355 students and the largest has 931. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 222nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.11% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Lower Lake (6), Clearlake (4), Clearlake Oaks (1).
