San Bernardino City Unified: public schools

76 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 76 public schools in the San Bernardino City Unified district in California: 52 elementary, 11 middle, 10 high, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 44,606 students in 76 of them; the median school has 489 students and the largest has 2,730. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.74% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in San Bernardino (67), Highland (9).
