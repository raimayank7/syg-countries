Westminster: public schools

17 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Westminster district in California: 13 elementary, 3 middle. The file reports 7,943 students in 16 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 864. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 126th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.16% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Westminster (10), Huntington Beach (3), Midway City (2), Garden Grove (2).
