Torrance Unified: public schools

32 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Torrance Unified district in California: 17 elementary, 8 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 21,972 students in 31 of them; the median school has 608 students and the largest has 1,980. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 44th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Torrance.
