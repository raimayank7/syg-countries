Mt. Diablo Unified: public schools

52 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Mt. Diablo Unified district in California: 31 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (1.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 29,070 students in 51 of them; the median school has 502 students and the largest has 1,997. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Concord (25), Pleasant Hill (13), Walnut Creek (6), Bay Point (4).
