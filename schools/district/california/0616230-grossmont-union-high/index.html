Grossmont Union High: public schools

15 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Grossmont Union High district in California: 15 high. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 16,499 students in 15 of them; the median school has 1,515 students and the largest has 2,414. Grades offered run from grade 9 to grade 12.

This is the 157th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.15% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in El Cajon (9), Spring Valley (2), Santee (2), Lakeside (1).
