Antelope Valley Union High: public schools

13 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Antelope Valley Union High district in California: 13 high. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 21,716 students in 13 of them; the median school has 1,526 students and the largest has 3,170. Grades offered run from grade 6 to grade 12.

This is the 189th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.13% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Lancaster (6), Palmdale (4), Quartz Hill (2), Littlerock (1).
