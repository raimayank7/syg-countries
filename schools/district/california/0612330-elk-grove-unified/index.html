Elk Grove Unified: public schools

67 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 67 public schools in the Elk Grove Unified district in California: 43 elementary, 13 high, 9 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (1.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 62,434 students in 67 of them; the median school has 837 students and the largest has 2,659. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.65% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Elk Grove (37), Sacramento (26), Rancho Cordova (2), Sloughhouse (1).
