South Bay Union: public schools

13 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the South Bay Union district in California: 12 elementary. 2 of them (15.4%) are charter schools. The file reports 5,591 students in 12 of them; the median school has 389 students and the largest has 1,070. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 179th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.13% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Imperial Beach (6), San Diego (6), San Ysidro (1).
