Hayward Unified: public schools

29 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Hayward Unified district in California: 19 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,522 students in 28 of them; the median school has 508 students and the largest has 1,892. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 55th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.28% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Hayward.
