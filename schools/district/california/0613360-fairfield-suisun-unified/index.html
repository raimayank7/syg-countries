Fairfield-Suisun Unified: public schools

31 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Fairfield-Suisun Unified district in California: 20 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,365 students in 30 of them; the median school has 572 students and the largest has 2,053. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 46th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.30% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Fairfield (27), Suisun City (3), Suisun (1).
