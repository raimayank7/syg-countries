Stockton Unified: public schools

54 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Stockton Unified district in California: 41 elementary, 11 high, 2 other. 5 of them (9.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 34,492 students in 54 of them; the median school has 530 students and the largest has 2,414. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.52% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Stockton.
