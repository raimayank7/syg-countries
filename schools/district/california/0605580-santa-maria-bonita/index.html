Santa Maria-Bonita: public schools

21 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Santa Maria-Bonita district in California: 17 elementary, 4 middle. The file reports 17,165 students in 21 of them; the median school has 873 students and the largest has 1,043. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 99th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.20% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Santa Maria.
