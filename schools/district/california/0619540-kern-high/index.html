Kern High: public schools

25 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Kern High district in California: 25 high. 1 of them (4.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 42,729 students in 25 of them; the median school has 2,148 students and the largest has 3,024. Grades offered run from grade 9 to grade 12.

This is the 71st largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Bakersfield (20), Shafter (2), Arvin (1), Lake Isabella (1).
