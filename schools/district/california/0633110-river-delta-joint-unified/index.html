River Delta Joint Unified: public schools

11 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the River Delta Joint Unified district in California: 4 high, 4 elementary, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 1,747 students in 11 of them; the median school has 163 students and the largest has 427. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 214th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.11% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Rio Vista (4), Courtland (3), Clarksburg (2), Isleton (1).
