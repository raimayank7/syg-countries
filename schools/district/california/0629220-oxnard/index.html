Oxnard: public schools

21 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Oxnard district in California: 17 elementary, 3 middle. The file reports 13,399 students in 20 of them; the median school has 639 students and the largest has 1,038. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 97th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.20% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Oxnard.
