Marysville Joint Unified: public schools

23 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Marysville Joint Unified district in California: 14 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 10,329 students in 23 of them; the median school has 482 students and the largest has 1,214. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 86th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.22% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Marysville (12), Olivehurst (8), Browns Valley (1), Dobbins (1).
