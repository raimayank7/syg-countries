Tustin Unified: public schools

28 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Tustin Unified district in California: 18 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 21,474 students in 28 of them; the median school has 528 students and the largest has 2,713. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 57th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.27% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Tustin (17), Santa Ana (7), Irvine (4).
