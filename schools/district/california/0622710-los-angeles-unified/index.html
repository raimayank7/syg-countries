Los Angeles Unified: public schools

785 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 785 public schools in the Los Angeles Unified district in California: 506 elementary, 175 high, 83 middle, 19 other, 1 secondary. 51 of them (6.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 103 alternative schools, 12 special education schools. The file reports 418,503 students in 784 of them; the median school has 434 students and the largest has 2,784. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 7.60% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 58 towns, most in Los Angeles (400), North Hollywood (25), South Gate (22), Carson (21).
