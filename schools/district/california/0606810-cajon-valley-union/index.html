Cajon Valley Union: public schools

26 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Cajon Valley Union district in California: 20 elementary, 5 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,585 students in 25 of them; the median school has 647 students and the largest has 1,483. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 68th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.25% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in El Cajon (25), La Mesa (1).
