Jurupa Unified: public schools

25 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Jurupa Unified district in California: 17 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,946 students in 24 of them; the median school has 620 students and the largest has 2,482. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 72nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Jurupa Valley.
