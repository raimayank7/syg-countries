Conejo Valley Unified: public schools

27 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Conejo Valley Unified district in California: 17 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 15,977 students in 26 of them; the median school has 411 students and the largest has 2,080. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 64th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.26% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Thousand Oaks (16), Newbury Park (8), Westlake Village (3).
