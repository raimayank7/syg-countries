Chula Vista Elementary: public schools

43 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Chula Vista Elementary district in California: 42 elementary. The file reports 22,278 students in 42 of them; the median school has 469 students and the largest has 1,104. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 6.

This is the 25th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.42% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Chula Vista (36), San Diego (4), Bonita (3).
