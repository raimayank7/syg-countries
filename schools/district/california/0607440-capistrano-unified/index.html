Capistrano Unified: public schools

59 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 59 public schools in the Capistrano Unified district in California: 36 elementary, 13 middle, 9 high. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 40,733 students in 58 of them; the median school has 523 students and the largest has 2,768. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.57% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in San Clemente (10), San Juan Capistrano (10), Mission Viejo (8), Laguna Niguel (7).
