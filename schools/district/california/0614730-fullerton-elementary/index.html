Fullerton Elementary: public schools

20 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Fullerton Elementary district in California: 17 elementary, 3 middle. The file reports 11,433 students in 20 of them; the median school has 599 students and the largest has 921. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 107th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.19% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Fullerton.
