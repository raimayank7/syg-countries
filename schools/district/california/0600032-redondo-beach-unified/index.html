Redondo Beach Unified: public schools

14 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Redondo Beach Unified district in California: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 9,464 students in 14 of them; the median school has 508 students and the largest has 2,971. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 175th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.14% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Redondo Beach.
