Beaumont Unified: public schools

15 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Beaumont Unified district in California: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 12,104 students in 14 of them; the median school has 751 students and the largest has 3,336. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 162nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.15% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Beaumont (13), Calimesa (1), Banning (1).
