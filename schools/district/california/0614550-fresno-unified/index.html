Fresno Unified: public schools

101 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 101 public schools in the Fresno Unified district in California: 70 elementary, 15 high, 14 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (1.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 68,345 students in 100 of them; the median school has 586 students and the largest has 2,749. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.98% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Fresno.
