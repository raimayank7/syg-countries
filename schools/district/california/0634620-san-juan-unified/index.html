San Juan Unified: public schools

68 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 68 public schools in the San Juan Unified district in California: 42 elementary, 15 high, 9 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (1.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 38,232 students in 67 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 1,926. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.66% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Sacramento (22), Carmichael (14), Citrus Heights (12), Fair Oaks (10).
