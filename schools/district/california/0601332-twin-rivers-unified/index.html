Twin Rivers Unified: public schools

43 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Twin Rivers Unified district in California: 28 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (7.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 24,318 students in 43 of them; the median school has 469 students and the largest has 2,016. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 26th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.42% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Sacramento (25), North Highlands (12), Rio Linda (6).
