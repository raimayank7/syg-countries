Porterville Unified: public schools

22 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Porterville Unified district in California: 10 elementary, 8 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (13.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 13,963 students in 22 of them; the median school has 497 students and the largest has 2,136. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 95th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.21% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Porterville (20), Strathmore (2).
