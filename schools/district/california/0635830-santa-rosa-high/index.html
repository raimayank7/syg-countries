Santa Rosa High: public schools

12 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Santa Rosa High district in California: 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,791 students in 12 of them; the median school has 735 students and the largest has 1,636. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 195th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.12% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Santa Rosa.
