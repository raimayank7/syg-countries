San Mateo-Foster City: public schools

22 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the San Mateo-Foster City district in California: 18 elementary, 3 middle. The file reports 9,919 students in 21 of them; the median school has 407 students and the largest has 934. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 89th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.21% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in San Mateo (16), Foster City (6).
