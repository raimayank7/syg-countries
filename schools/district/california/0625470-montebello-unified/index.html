Montebello Unified: public schools

29 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Montebello Unified district in California: 17 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 19,920 students in 29 of them; the median school has 581 students and the largest has 2,139. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 54th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.28% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Montebello (13), Bell Gardens (7), Los Angeles (3), City Of Commerce (2).
