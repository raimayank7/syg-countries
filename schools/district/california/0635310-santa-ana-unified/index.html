Santa Ana Unified: public schools

52 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Santa Ana Unified district in California: 34 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (1.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 37,998 students in 51 of them; the median school has 500 students and the largest has 2,853. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Santa Ana.
