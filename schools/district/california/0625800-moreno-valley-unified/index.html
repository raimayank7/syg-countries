Moreno Valley Unified: public schools

39 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Moreno Valley Unified district in California: 23 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 31,319 students in 38 of them; the median school has 670 students and the largest has 2,784. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 31st largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.38% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Moreno Valley.
