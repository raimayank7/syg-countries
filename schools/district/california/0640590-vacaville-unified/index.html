Vacaville Unified: public schools

18 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Vacaville Unified district in California: 10 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle. 3 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,675 students in 17 of them; the median school has 671 students and the largest has 2,027. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 115th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.17% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Vacaville.
