Newport-Mesa Unified: public schools

32 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Newport-Mesa Unified district in California: 22 elementary, 7 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 17,684 students in 31 of them; the median school has 380 students and the largest has 2,217. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 45th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Costa Mesa (21), Newport Beach (9), Corona Del Mar (2).
