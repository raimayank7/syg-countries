Hesperia Unified: public schools

25 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Hesperia Unified district in California: 15 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 23,361 students in 25 of them; the median school has 809 students and the largest has 2,513. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 77th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Hesperia (23), Victorville (1), Oak Hills (1).
