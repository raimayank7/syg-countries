Sweetwater Union High: public schools

29 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Sweetwater Union High district in California: 17 high, 12 middle. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 34,445 students in 29 of them; the median school has 927 students and the largest has 2,710. Grades offered run from grade 7 to grade 12.

This is the 52nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.28% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Chula Vista (18), San Diego (7), National City (3), Imperial Beach (1).
