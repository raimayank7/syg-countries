Bakersfield City: public schools

45 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Bakersfield City district in California: 34 elementary, 10 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 28,761 students in 44 of them; the median school has 666 students and the largest has 1,043. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 22nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.44% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bakersfield.
