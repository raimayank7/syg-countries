Santa Rosa Elementary: public schools

13 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Santa Rosa Elementary district in California: 12 elementary. 3 of them (23.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 4,722 students in 12 of them; the median school has 358 students and the largest has 811. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 180th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.13% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Santa Rosa.
