San Francisco Unified: public schools

113 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 113 public schools in the San Francisco Unified district in California: 76 elementary, 17 high, 13 middle. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 48,670 students in 106 of them; the median school has 369 students and the largest has 2,540. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 1.09% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Francisco.
