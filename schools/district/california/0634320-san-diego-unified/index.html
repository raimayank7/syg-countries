San Diego Unified: public schools

175 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 175 public schools in the San Diego Unified district in California: 119 elementary, 25 high, 24 middle, 7 other. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 95,300 students in 175 of them; the median school has 433 students and the largest has 2,512. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 1.69% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in San Diego (170), La Jolla (5).
