San Ramon Valley Unified: public schools

37 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the San Ramon Valley Unified district in California: 22 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 29,183 students in 36 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 3,118. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 32nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.36% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in San Ramon (20), Danville (14), Alamo (3).
