Desert Sands Unified: public schools

35 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Desert Sands Unified district in California: 20 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (5.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 25,897 students in 35 of them; the median school has 598 students and the largest has 2,488. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 37th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.34% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Indio (19), La Quinta (8), Palm Desert (6), Bermuda Dunes (1).
