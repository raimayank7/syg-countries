Palo Alto Unified: public schools

19 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Palo Alto Unified district in California: 13 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other. The file reports 10,254 students in 19 of them; the median school has 366 students and the largest has 1,932. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 111th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.18% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Palo Alto (17), Stanford (2).
