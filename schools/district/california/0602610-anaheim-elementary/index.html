Anaheim Elementary: public schools

24 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Anaheim Elementary district in California: 24 elementary. The file reports 14,247 students in 24 of them; the median school has 611 students and the largest has 842. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 6.

This is the 81st largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.23% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Anaheim.
