Sacramento City Unified: public schools

73 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 73 public schools in the Sacramento City Unified district in California: 51 elementary, 13 high, 8 middle, 1 other. 5 of them (6.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 38,022 students in 73 of them; the median school has 417 students and the largest has 2,481. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.71% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Sacramento.
