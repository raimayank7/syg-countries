Pomona Unified: public schools

37 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Pomona Unified district in California: 24 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 2 of them (5.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,913 students in 37 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 1,604. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 33rd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.36% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Pomona (33), Diamond Bar (4).
