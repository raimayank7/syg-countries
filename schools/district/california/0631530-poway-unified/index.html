Poway Unified: public schools

39 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Poway Unified district in California: 26 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 34,755 students in 39 of them; the median school has 659 students and the largest has 2,564. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 30th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.38% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in San Diego (28), Poway (11).
