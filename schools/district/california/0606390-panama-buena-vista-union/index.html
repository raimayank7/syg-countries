Panama-Buena Vista Union: public schools

25 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Panama-Buena Vista Union district in California: 20 elementary, 5 middle. The file reports 19,369 students in 25 of them; the median school has 762 students and the largest has 1,092. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 74th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bakersfield.
