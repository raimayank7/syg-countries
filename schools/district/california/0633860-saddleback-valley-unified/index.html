Saddleback Valley Unified: public schools

34 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Saddleback Valley Unified district in California: 23 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 23,165 students in 34 of them; the median school has 546 students and the largest has 2,507. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 38th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.33% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Mission Viejo (14), Lake Forest (8), Rancho Santa Margarita (4), Laguna Hills (4).
