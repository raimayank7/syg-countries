Tulare City: public schools

18 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Tulare City district in California: 12 elementary, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 9,252 students in 18 of them; the median school has 591 students and the largest has 725. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 116th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.17% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Tulare.
