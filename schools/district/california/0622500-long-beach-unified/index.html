Long Beach Unified: public schools

82 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in the Long Beach Unified district in California: 53 elementary, 15 middle, 13 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 63,557 students in 82 of them; the median school has 573 students and the largest has 3,954. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.79% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Long Beach (69), Lakewood (8), Signal Hill (3), Santa Catalina (1).
