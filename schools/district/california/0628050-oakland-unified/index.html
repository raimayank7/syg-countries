Oakland Unified: public schools

82 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in the Oakland Unified district in California: 51 elementary, 17 high, 11 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 33,835 students in 81 of them; the median school has 371 students and the largest has 1,813. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.79% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Oakland.
