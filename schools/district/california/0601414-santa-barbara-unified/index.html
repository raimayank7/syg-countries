Santa Barbara Unified: public schools

21 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Santa Barbara Unified district in California: 10 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 12,484 students in 20 of them; the median school has 443 students and the largest has 2,075. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 101st largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.20% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Santa Barbara (18), Goleta (3).
