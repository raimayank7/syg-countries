Chino Valley Unified: public schools

34 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Chino Valley Unified district in California: 22 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 25,480 students in 34 of them; the median school has 561 students and the largest has 2,767. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 42nd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.33% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Chino Hills (16), Chino (15), Ontario (3).
