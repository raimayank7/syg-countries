Boston: public schools

109 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 109 public schools in the Boston district in Massachusetts: 75 elementary, 28 high, 3 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 45,742 students in 109 of them; the median school has 343 students and the largest has 2,401. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 5.95% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Dorchester (25), Roxbury (13), Boston (11), East Boston (11).
