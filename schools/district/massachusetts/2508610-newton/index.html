Newton: public schools

22 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Newton district in Massachusetts: 15 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 11,752 students in 22 of them; the median school has 360 students and the largest has 2,118. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 1.20% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Newton Centre (6), Newton (5), Newtonville (4), Waban (2).
