Chicopee: public schools

15 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Chicopee district in Massachusetts: 9 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,807 students in 15 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 1,212. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 0.82% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Chicopee.
