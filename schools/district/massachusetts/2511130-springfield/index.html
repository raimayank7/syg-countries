Springfield: public schools

66 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 66 public schools in the Springfield district in Massachusetts: 33 elementary, 15 middle, 13 high, 3 prekindergarten, 1 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 23,693 students in 66 of them; the median school has 287 students and the largest has 2,042. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 2nd largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 3.60% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Springfield (64), Indian Orchard (1), Holyoke (1).
