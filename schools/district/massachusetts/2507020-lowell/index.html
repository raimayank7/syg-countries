Lowell: public schools

27 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Lowell district in Massachusetts: 15 elementary, 7 middle, 3 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 14,273 students in 27 of them; the median school has 464 students and the largest has 3,402. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 1.47% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Lowell (26), Chelmsford (1).
