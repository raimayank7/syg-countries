Brockton: public schools

24 schools

Massachusetts, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Brockton district in Massachusetts: 11 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,954 students in 24 of them; the median school has 497 students and the largest has 3,586. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 400 districts in Massachusetts by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,832 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Brockton.
