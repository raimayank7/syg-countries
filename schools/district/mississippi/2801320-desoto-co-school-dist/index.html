Desoto Co School Dist: public schools

42 schools

Mississippi, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Desoto Co School Dist district in Mississippi: 23 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 34,819 students in 42 of them; the median school has 758 students and the largest has 2,011. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 152 districts in Mississippi by number of public schools, and holds 4.07% of the state's 1,032 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Olive Branch (15), Southaven (11), Hernando (7), Horn Lake (5).
