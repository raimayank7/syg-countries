Jackson Public School District: public schools

52 schools

Mississippi, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Jackson Public School District district in Mississippi: 30 elementary, 9 middle, 9 high, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,747 students in 52 of them; the median school has 291 students and the largest has 1,279. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 152 districts in Mississippi by number of public schools, and holds 5.04% of the state's 1,032 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Jackson.
