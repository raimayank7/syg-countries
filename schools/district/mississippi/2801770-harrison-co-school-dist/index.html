Harrison Co School Dist: public schools

24 schools

Mississippi, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Harrison Co School Dist district in Mississippi: 15 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,299 students in 24 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 1,508. Grades offered run from kindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 4th largest of the 152 districts in Mississippi by number of public schools, and holds 2.33% of the state's 1,032 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Gulfport (15), Biloxi (4), Diberville (2), Saucier (2).
