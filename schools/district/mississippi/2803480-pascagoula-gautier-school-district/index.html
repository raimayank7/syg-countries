Pascagoula-Gautier School District: public schools

19 schools

Mississippi, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Pascagoula-Gautier School District district in Mississippi: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,501 students in 19 of them; the median school has 331 students and the largest has 1,117. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 152 districts in Mississippi by number of public schools, and holds 1.84% of the state's 1,032 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Pascagoula (13), Gautier (6).
