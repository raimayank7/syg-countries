Jackson Co School Dist: public schools

14 schools

Mississippi, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Jackson Co School Dist district in Mississippi: 7 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,936 students in 14 of them; the median school has 600 students and the largest has 1,320. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 152 districts in Mississippi by number of public schools, and holds 1.36% of the state's 1,032 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Vancleave (5), Moss Point (4), Ocean Springs (4), Biloxi (1).
