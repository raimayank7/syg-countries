Harrison County Schools: public schools

25 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Harrison County Schools district in West Virginia: 11 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,687 students in 24 of them; the median school has 404 students and the largest has 814. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.67% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Clarksburg (10), Bridgeport (4), Lost Creek (3), Shinnston (3).
