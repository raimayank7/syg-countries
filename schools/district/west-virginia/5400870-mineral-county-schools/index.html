Mineral County Schools: public schools

14 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Mineral County Schools district in West Virginia: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 3,964 students in 13 of them; the median school has 226 students and the largest has 689. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 2.05% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Keyser (6), Ridgeley (2), Fort Ashby (2), Burlington (1).
