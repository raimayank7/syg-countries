Marion County Schools: public schools

21 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Marion County Schools district in West Virginia: 11 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,280 students in 19 of them; the median school has 327 students and the largest has 779. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.08% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Fairmont (9), Mannington (2), Fairview (2), Monongah (2).
