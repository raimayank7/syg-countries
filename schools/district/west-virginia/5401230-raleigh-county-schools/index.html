Raleigh County Schools: public schools

27 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Raleigh County Schools district in West Virginia: 17 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,584 students in 26 of them; the median school has 366 students and the largest has 1,265. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.96% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Beckley (11), Shady Spring (3), Coal City (2), Sophia (2).
