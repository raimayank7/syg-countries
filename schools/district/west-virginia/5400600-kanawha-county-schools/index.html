Kanawha County Schools: public schools

66 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 66 public schools in the Kanawha County Schools district in West Virginia: 42 elementary, 12 middle, 10 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 23,346 students in 64 of them; the median school has 289 students and the largest has 1,186. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 9.68% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 18 towns, most in Charleston (28), Saint Albans (8), South Charleston (5), Dunbar (5).
