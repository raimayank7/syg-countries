Cabell County Schools: public schools

26 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Cabell County Schools district in West Virginia: 18 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,426 students in 25 of them; the median school has 316 students and the largest has 1,721. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.81% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Huntington (14), Barboursville (5), Milton (2), Ona (2).
