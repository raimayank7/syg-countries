Wood County Schools: public schools

27 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Wood County Schools district in West Virginia: 17 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,291 students in 24 of them; the median school has 394 students and the largest has 1,552. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.96% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Parkersburg (19), Vienna (4), Williamstown (2), Davisville (1).
