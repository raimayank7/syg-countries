Berkeley County Schools: public schools

32 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Berkeley County Schools district in West Virginia: 21 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,906 students in 31 of them; the median school has 612 students and the largest has 1,780. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 4.69% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Martinsburg (18), Hedgesville (5), Bunker Hill (3), Gerrardstown (3).
