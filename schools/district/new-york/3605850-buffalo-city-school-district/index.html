Buffalo City School District: public schools

61 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 61 public schools in the Buffalo City School District district in New York: 38 elementary, 21 high, 1 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools, 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 29,200 students in 61 of them; the median school has 449 students and the largest has 1,162. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 7th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.27% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Buffalo.
