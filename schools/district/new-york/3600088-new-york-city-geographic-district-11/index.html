New York City Geographic District #11: public schools

62 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 62 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #11 district in New York: 31 elementary, 17 high, 14 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 30,149 students in 62 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,700. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.29% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bronx.
