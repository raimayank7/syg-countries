New York City Geographic District #24: public schools

57 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #24 district in New York: 32 elementary, 16 high, 8 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 48,755 students in 57 of them; the median school has 776 students and the largest has 2,170. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Corona (12), Elmhurst (11), Long Island City (10), Ridgewood (8).
