New York City Geographic District #12: public schools

47 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #12 district in New York: 21 elementary, 15 high, 10 middle, 1 secondary. The file reports 15,882 students in 47 of them; the median school has 295 students and the largest has 838. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.98% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bronx.
