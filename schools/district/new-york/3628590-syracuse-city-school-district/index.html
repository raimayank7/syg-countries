Syracuse City School District: public schools

31 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Syracuse City School District district in New York: 19 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 18,029 students in 31 of them; the median school has 482 students and the largest has 1,443. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 31st largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.64% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Syracuse.
