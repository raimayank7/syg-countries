New York City Geographic District #28: public schools

50 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #28 district in New York: 27 elementary, 15 high, 8 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 36,179 students in 50 of them; the median school has 586 students and the largest has 3,425. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.04% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Jamaica (32), Forest Hills (9), Rego Park (5), Richmond Hill (2).
