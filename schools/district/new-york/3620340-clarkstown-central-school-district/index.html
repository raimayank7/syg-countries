Clarkstown Central School District: public schools

16 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Clarkstown Central School District district in New York: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,808 students in 16 of them; the median school has 385 students and the largest has 1,386. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 40th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.33% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in New City (7), West Nyack (7), Bardonia (1), Congers (1).
