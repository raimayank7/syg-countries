Sachem Central School District: public schools

15 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Sachem Central School District district in New York: 10 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 11,593 students in 15 of them; the median school has 524 students and the largest has 2,055. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 45th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Holbrook (5), Holtsville (3), Lake Ronkonkoma (3), Lake Grove (2).
