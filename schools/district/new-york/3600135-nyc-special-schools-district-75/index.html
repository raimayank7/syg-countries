Nyc Special Schools - District 75: public schools

59 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 59 public schools in the Nyc Special Schools - District 75 district in New York: 28 other, 16 elementary, 12 high, 2 ungraded, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 59 special education schools. The file reports 27,255 students in 59 of them; the median school has 493 students and the largest has 706. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 8th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.23% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Brooklyn (18), Bronx (14), New York (9), Staten Island (4).
