New York City Geographic District #20: public schools

45 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #20 district in New York: 32 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high. The file reports 44,274 students in 45 of them; the median school has 843 students and the largest has 3,984. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.93% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Brooklyn.
