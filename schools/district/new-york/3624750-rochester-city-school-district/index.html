Rochester City School District: public schools

46 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Rochester City School District district in New York: 30 elementary, 10 high, 5 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 21,750 students in 46 of them; the median school has 367 students and the largest has 1,161. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.96% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Rochester.
