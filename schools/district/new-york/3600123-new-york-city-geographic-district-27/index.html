New York City Geographic District #27: public schools

63 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 63 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #27 district in New York: 39 elementary, 15 high, 9 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 38,859 students in 63 of them; the median school has 510 students and the largest has 2,431. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Far Rockaway (13), South Ozone Park (10), Ozone Park (10), Richmond Hill (8).
