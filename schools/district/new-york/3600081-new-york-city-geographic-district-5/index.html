New York City Geographic District # 5: public schools

28 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the New York City Geographic District # 5 district in New York: 16 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,856 students in 28 of them; the median school has 218 students and the largest has 718. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 34th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.58% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in New York.
