New York City Geographic District #16: public schools

23 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the New York City Geographic District #16 district in New York: 13 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle. The file reports 5,320 students in 23 of them; the median school has 209 students and the largest has 485. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 37th largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Brooklyn.
