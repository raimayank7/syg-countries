New York City Geographic District # 2: public schools

119 schools

New York, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 119 public schools in the New York City Geographic District # 2 district in New York: 69 high, 36 elementary, 12 middle, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 13 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 54,681 students in 119 of them; the median school has 401 students and the largest has 3,257. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 1,103 districts in New York by number of public schools, and holds 2.47% of the state's 4,814 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in New York (118), Roosevelt Island (1).
