Meriden School District: public schools

13 schools

Connecticut, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Meriden School District district in Connecticut: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 8,129 students in 13 of them; the median school has 504 students and the largest has 1,301. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 199 districts in Connecticut by number of public schools, and holds 1.29% of the state's 1,010 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Meriden.
