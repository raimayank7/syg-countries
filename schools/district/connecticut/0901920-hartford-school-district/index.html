Hartford School District: public schools

41 schools

Connecticut, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in the Hartford School District district in Connecticut: 22 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. The file reports 16,094 students in 41 of them; the median school has 370 students and the largest has 934. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 199 districts in Connecticut by number of public schools, and holds 4.06% of the state's 1,010 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Hartford (39), Manchester (1), East Hartford (1).
