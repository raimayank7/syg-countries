Harford County Public Schools: public schools

55 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the Harford County Public Schools district in Maryland: 33 elementary, 11 high, 9 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 38,105 students in 55 of them; the median school has 592 students and the largest has 1,465. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 3.91% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 16 towns, most in Bel Air (16), Aberdeen (7), Joppa (5), Edgewood (4).
