Queen Anne's County Public Schools: public schools

14 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Queen Anne's County Public Schools district in Maryland: 8 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high. The file reports 7,502 students in 14 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 1,223. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 0.99% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Stevensville (6), Centreville (4), Sudlersville (2), Church Hill (1).
