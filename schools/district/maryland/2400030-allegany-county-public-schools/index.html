Allegany County Public Schools: public schools

22 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Allegany County Public Schools district in Maryland: 14 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,205 students in 22 of them; the median school has 273 students and the largest has 734. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 1.56% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Cumberland (9), Frostburg (3), Lonaconing (2), Cresaptown (2).
