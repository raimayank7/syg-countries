Washington County Public Schools: public schools

43 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Washington County Public Schools district in Maryland: 25 elementary, 10 high, 7 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,549 students in 43 of them; the median school has 461 students and the largest has 1,645. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 3.05% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Hagerstown (23), Boonsboro (4), Williamsport (4), Clear Spring (3).
