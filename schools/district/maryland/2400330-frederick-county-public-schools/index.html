Frederick County Public Schools: public schools

69 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 69 public schools in the Frederick County Public Schools district in Maryland: 41 elementary, 13 middle, 13 high, 2 other. 4 of them (5.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 47,681 students in 69 of them; the median school has 625 students and the largest has 2,031. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 4.90% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Frederick (32), Ijamsville (6), Walkersville (5), Thurmont (5).
