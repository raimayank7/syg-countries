Montgomery County Public Schools: public schools

211 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 211 public schools in the Montgomery County Public Schools district in Maryland: 138 elementary, 40 middle, 30 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 160,223 students in 211 of them; the median school has 594 students and the largest has 3,261. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 14.99% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 20 towns, most in Silver Spring (55), Rockville (34), Gaithersburg (29), Germantown (20).
