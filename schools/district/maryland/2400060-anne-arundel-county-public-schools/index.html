Anne Arundel County Public Schools: public schools

126 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 126 public schools in the Anne Arundel County Public Schools district in Maryland: 81 elementary, 20 middle, 17 high, 6 other, 2 prekindergarten. 2 of them (1.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 4 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 84,346 students in 126 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 2,507. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 8.95% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 27 towns, most in Glen Burnie (21), Annapolis (19), Pasadena (12), Severna Park (8).
