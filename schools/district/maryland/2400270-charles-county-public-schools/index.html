Charles County Public Schools: public schools

40 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in the Charles County Public Schools district in Maryland: 22 elementary, 9 middle, 8 high, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (2.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 27,765 students in 40 of them; the median school has 622 students and the largest has 1,914. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 2.84% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Waldorf (20), La Plata (6), Indian Head (5), Pomfret (3).
