Baltimore County Public Schools: public schools

179 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 179 public schools in the Baltimore County Public Schools district in Maryland: 112 elementary, 33 high, 29 middle, 4 other, 1 secondary. 1 of them (0.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 13 alternative schools, 4 career and technical schools, 4 special education schools. The file reports 110,275 students in 179 of them; the median school has 513 students and the largest has 2,159. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 12.71% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 25 towns, most in Baltimore (115), Towson (13), Reisterstown (7), Owings Mills (7).
