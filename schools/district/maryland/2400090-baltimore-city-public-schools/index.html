Baltimore City Public Schools: public schools

151 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 151 public schools in the Baltimore City Public Schools district in Maryland: 108 elementary, 33 high, 9 middle, 1 other. 31 of them (20.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 career and technical schools, 5 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 75,811 students in 151 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 1,757. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 10.72% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Baltimore.
