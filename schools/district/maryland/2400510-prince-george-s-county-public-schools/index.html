Prince George's County Public Schools: public schools

199 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 199 public schools in the Prince George's County Public Schools district in Maryland: 138 elementary, 25 high, 24 middle, 6 prekindergarten, 6 other. 9 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 131,310 students in 199 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 2,960. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 14.13% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 37 towns, most in Upper Marlboro (16), Bowie (14), Hyattsville (13), Landover (12).
