St. Mary's County Public Schools: public schools

27 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the St. Mary's County Public Schools district in Maryland: 19 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle. 1 of them (3.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,223 students in 27 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 2,072. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 1.92% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Lexington Park (6), Leonardtown (5), Mechanicsville (5), Great Mills (3).
