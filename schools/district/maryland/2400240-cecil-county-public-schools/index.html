Cecil County Public Schools: public schools

29 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Cecil County Public Schools district in Maryland: 17 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,997 students in 29 of them; the median school has 446 students and the largest has 1,105. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 2.06% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Elkton (11), North East (5), Chesapeake City (3), Rising Sun (3).
