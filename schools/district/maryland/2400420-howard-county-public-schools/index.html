Howard County Public Schools: public schools

78 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in the Howard County Public Schools district in Maryland: 42 elementary, 20 middle, 14 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 57,633 students in 78 of them; the median school has 676 students and the largest has 1,729. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 5.54% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 14 towns, most in Columbia (23), Ellicott City (22), Laurel (6), Elkridge (6).
