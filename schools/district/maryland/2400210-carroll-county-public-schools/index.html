Carroll County Public Schools: public schools

44 schools

Maryland, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Carroll County Public Schools district in Maryland: 23 elementary, 11 high, 9 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 26,123 students in 44 of them; the median school has 600 students and the largest has 1,480. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 25 districts in Maryland by number of public schools, and holds 3.12% of the state's 1,408 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Westminster (18), Sykesville (11), Hampstead (4), Manchester (3).
