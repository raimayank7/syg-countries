Santa Fe: public schools

30 schools

New Mexico, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in the Santa Fe district in New Mexico: 19 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,389 students in 29 of them; the median school has 304 students and the largest has 1,599. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 153 districts in New Mexico by number of public schools, and holds 3.38% of the state's 888 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Santa Fe (29), Tesuque (1).
