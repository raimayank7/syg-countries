Gadsden: public schools

28 schools

New Mexico, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Gadsden district in New Mexico: 16 elementary, 5 high, 4 prekindergarten, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,130 students in 26 of them; the median school has 422 students and the largest has 1,288. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 153 districts in New Mexico by number of public schools, and holds 3.15% of the state's 888 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Anthony (11), Sunland Park (5), Chaparral (3), Santa Teresa (3).
