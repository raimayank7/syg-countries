Roswell: public schools

22 schools

New Mexico, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Roswell district in New Mexico: 13 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,381 students in 22 of them; the median school has 364 students and the largest has 1,460. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 153 districts in New Mexico by number of public schools, and holds 2.48% of the state's 888 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Roswell.
