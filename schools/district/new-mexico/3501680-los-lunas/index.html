Los Lunas: public schools

16 schools

New Mexico, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Los Lunas district in New Mexico: 10 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,237 students in 16 of them; the median school has 415 students and the largest has 1,450. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 153 districts in New Mexico by number of public schools, and holds 1.80% of the state's 888 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Los Lunas.
