Albuquerque: public schools

174 schools

New Mexico, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 174 public schools in the Albuquerque district in New Mexico: 100 elementary, 36 high, 29 middle, 9 other. 30 of them (17.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 76,152 students in 174 of them; the median school has 335 students and the largest has 2,177. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 153 districts in New Mexico by number of public schools, and holds 19.59% of the state's 888 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Albuquerque (168), Tijeras (2), Sandia Park (2), Corrales (1).
