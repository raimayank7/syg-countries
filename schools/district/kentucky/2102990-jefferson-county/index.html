Jefferson County: public schools

171 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 171 public schools in the Jefferson County district in Kentucky: 93 elementary, 34 high, 24 middle, 11 other, 6 prekindergarten, 2 ungraded, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 22 alternative schools, 7 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 94,749 students in 171 of them; the median school has 494 students and the largest has 2,033. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 1st largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 11.08% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Louisville (161), Fairdale (4), Middletown (3), Jeffersontown (2).
