Kentucky Tech System: public schools

55 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the Kentucky Tech System district in Kentucky: 55 high. Besides regular schools there are 55 career and technical schools. The file reports 2 students in 55 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 1. Grades offered run from grade 8 to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 3.56% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 52 towns, most in Carrollton (2), Corbin (2), Beattyville (2), Paducah (1).
