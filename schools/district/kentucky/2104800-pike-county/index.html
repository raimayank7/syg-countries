Pike County: public schools

20 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Pike County district in Kentucky: 11 elementary, 7 high, 1 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 7,719 students in 20 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 880. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 1.30% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Pikeville (9), Belfry (3), Sidney (1), Dorton (1).
