Bullitt County: public schools

25 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Bullitt County district in Kentucky: 13 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 12,954 students in 25 of them; the median school has 457 students and the largest has 1,620. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 1.62% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Shepherdsville (12), Mount Washington (7), Louisville (2), Brooks (1).
