Owensboro Independent: public schools

13 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Owensboro Independent district in Kentucky: 5 elementary, 3 high, 2 ungraded, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,961 students in 13 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 1,329. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 24th largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 0.84% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Owensboro.
