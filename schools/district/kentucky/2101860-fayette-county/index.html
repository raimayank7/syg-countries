Fayette County: public schools

79 schools

Kentucky, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 79 public schools in the Fayette County district in Kentucky: 41 elementary, 20 high, 12 middle, 3 ungraded, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 6 career and technical schools. The file reports 41,697 students in 79 of them; the median school has 458 students and the largest has 2,373. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 2nd largest of the 175 districts in Kentucky by number of public schools, and holds 5.12% of the state's 1,544 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Lexington.
