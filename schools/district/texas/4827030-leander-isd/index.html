Leander Isd: public schools

52 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Leander Isd district in Texas: 29 elementary, 11 high, 10 middle, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 42,593 students in 52 of them; the median school has 683 students and the largest has 2,617. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 28th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.56% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Leander (23), Cedar Park (16), Austin (9), Georgetown (4).
