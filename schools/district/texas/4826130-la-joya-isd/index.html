La Joya Isd: public schools

37 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the La Joya Isd district in Texas: 23 elementary, 8 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 23,998 students in 37 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 2,624. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 56th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.40% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Mission (23), La Joya (7), Penitas (4), Sullivan City (2).
