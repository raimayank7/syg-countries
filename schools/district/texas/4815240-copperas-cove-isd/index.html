Copperas Cove Isd: public schools

11 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the Copperas Cove Isd district in Texas: 6 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,959 students in 11 of them; the median school has 579 students and the largest has 2,273. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 163rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.12% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Copperas Cove.
