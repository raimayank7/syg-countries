Abilene Isd: public schools

28 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Abilene Isd district in Texas: 13 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 adult education. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 14,652 students in 28 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 1,903. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

This is the 70th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.30% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Abilene.
