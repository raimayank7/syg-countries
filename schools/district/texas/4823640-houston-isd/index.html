Houston Isd: public schools

274 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 274 public schools in the Houston Isd district in Texas: 178 elementary, 46 high, 43 middle, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 7 of them (2.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 184,109 students in 274 of them; the median school has 530 students and the largest has 8,332. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 2.97% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Houston (269), Bellaire (5).
