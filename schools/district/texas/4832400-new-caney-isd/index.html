New Caney Isd: public schools

21 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the New Caney Isd district in Texas: 11 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 18,987 students in 21 of them; the median school has 813 students and the largest has 2,363. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 91st largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.23% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Porter (11), New Caney (8), Kingwood (2).
