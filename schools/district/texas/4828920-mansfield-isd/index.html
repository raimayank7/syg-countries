Mansfield Isd: public schools

48 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in the Mansfield Isd district in Texas: 24 elementary, 14 middle, 9 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 35,851 students in 48 of them; the median school has 616 students and the largest has 2,772. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 37th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.52% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Mansfield (27), Arlington (17), Grand Prairie (3), Burleson (1).
