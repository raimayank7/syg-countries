Harlandale Isd: public schools

22 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Harlandale Isd district in Texas: 10 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 11,814 students in 22 of them; the median school has 545 students and the largest has 1,611. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 89th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Antonio.
