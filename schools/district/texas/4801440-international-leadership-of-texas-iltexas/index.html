International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas): public schools

44 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) district in Texas: 18 elementary, 18 middle, 7 high, 1 secondary. 44 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 22,780 students in 44 of them; the median school has 457 students and the largest has 949. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 47th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 14 towns, most in Houston (7), Fort Worth (5), Grand Prairie (5), Cleveland (5).
