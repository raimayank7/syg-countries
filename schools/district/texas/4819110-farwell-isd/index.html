Farwell Isd: public schools

3 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 3 public schools in the Farwell Isd district in Texas: 1 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 605 students in 3 of them; the median school has 172 students and the largest has 287. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 693rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.03% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Farwell.
