Mission Cisd: public schools

24 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Mission Cisd district in Texas: 14 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,068 students in 24 of them; the median school has 452 students and the largest has 2,209. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 83rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.26% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Mission (16), Alton (6), Palmhurst (2).
