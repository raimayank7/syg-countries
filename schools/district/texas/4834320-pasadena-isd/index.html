Pasadena Isd: public schools

67 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 67 public schools in the Pasadena Isd district in Texas: 36 elementary, 22 middle, 8 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 47,486 students in 67 of them; the median school has 549 students and the largest has 3,739. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.73% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Pasadena (38), Houston (23), So Houston (5), South Houston (1).
