Ector County Isd: public schools

46 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Ector County Isd district in Texas: 28 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 4 prekindergarten, 1 secondary, 1 other. 3 of them (6.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 33,426 students in 46 of them; the median school has 537 students and the largest has 4,126. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 43rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Odessa.
