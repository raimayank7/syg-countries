Comal Isd: public schools

38 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in the Comal Isd district in Texas: 20 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 29,263 students in 38 of them; the median school has 666 students and the largest has 2,400. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 55th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.41% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in New Braunfels (14), San Antonio (8), Spring Branch (6), Bulverde (4).
