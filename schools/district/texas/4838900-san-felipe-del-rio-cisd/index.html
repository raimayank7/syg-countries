San Felipe-Del Rio Cisd: public schools

14 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the San Felipe-Del Rio Cisd district in Texas: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,907 students in 14 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 2,491. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 127th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.15% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Del Rio (13), Laughlin Afb (1).
