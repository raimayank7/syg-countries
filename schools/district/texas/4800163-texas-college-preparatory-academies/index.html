Texas College Preparatory Academies: public schools

44 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Texas College Preparatory Academies district in Texas: 22 elementary, 16 other, 6 high. 44 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 16,918 students in 44 of them; the median school has 294 students and the largest has 1,038. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 48th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 33 towns, most in Lewisville (5), Houston (5), Corinth (2), Conroe (2).
