Canyon Isd: public schools

21 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Canyon Isd district in Texas: 10 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 11,378 students in 21 of them; the median school has 556 students and the largest has 1,162. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 96th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.23% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Amarillo (14), Canyon (7).
