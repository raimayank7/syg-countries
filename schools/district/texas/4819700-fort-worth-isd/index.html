Fort Worth Isd: public schools

138 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 138 public schools in the Fort Worth Isd district in Texas: 83 elementary, 28 high, 21 middle, 5 other, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (3.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 16 alternative schools. The file reports 71,060 students in 138 of them; the median school has 435 students and the largest has 2,147. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.50% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Fort Worth (133), Ft Worth (5).
