Waco Isd: public schools

24 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Waco Isd district in Texas: 15 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 13,517 students in 24 of them; the median school has 471 students and the largest has 1,778. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 80th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.26% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Waco.
