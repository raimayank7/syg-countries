Spring Branch Isd: public schools

47 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in the Spring Branch Isd district in Texas: 26 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high, 5 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 33,407 students in 47 of them; the median school has 583 students and the largest has 2,640. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 38th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.51% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Houston.
