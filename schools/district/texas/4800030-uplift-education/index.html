Uplift Education: public schools

45 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Uplift Education district in Texas: 19 elementary, 13 middle, 11 high, 2 secondary. 45 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 22,815 students in 45 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 943. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 45th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.49% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Dallas (21), Fort Worth (11), Irving (6), Arlington (3).
