Judson Isd: public schools

33 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Judson Isd district in Texas: 20 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 23,848 students in 33 of them; the median school has 656 students and the largest has 2,527. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 62nd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.36% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in San Antonio (19), Converse (9), Universal City (4), Live Oak (1).
