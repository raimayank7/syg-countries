Kipp Texas Public Schools: public schools

56 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Kipp Texas Public Schools district in Texas: 23 elementary, 21 middle, 11 high, 1 secondary. 56 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 34,111 students in 56 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 1,092. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 23rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.61% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Houston (29), Austin (10), San Antonio (7), Dallas (6).
