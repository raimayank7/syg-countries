Lamar Cisd: public schools

47 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in the Lamar Cisd district in Texas: 31 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 44,512 students in 47 of them; the median school has 720 students and the largest has 2,897. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 39th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.51% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Richmond (27), Rosenberg (11), Fulshear (4), Sugar Land (2).
