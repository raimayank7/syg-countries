Clear Creek Isd: public schools

47 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in the Clear Creek Isd district in Texas: 27 elementary, 10 middle, 9 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 40,132 students in 47 of them; the median school has 713 students and the largest has 2,666. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 40th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.51% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in League City (19), Houston (13), Friendswood (5), Webster (4).
