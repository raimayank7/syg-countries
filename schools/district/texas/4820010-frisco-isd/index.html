Frisco Isd: public schools

77 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 77 public schools in the Frisco Isd district in Texas: 43 elementary, 20 middle, 13 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 66,698 students in 77 of them; the median school has 693 students and the largest has 2,191. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.84% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Frisco (61), Mckinney (9), Plano (5), Little Elm (2).
