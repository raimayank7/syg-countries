Manor Isd: public schools

16 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Manor Isd district in Texas: 9 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,658 students in 16 of them; the median school has 549 students and the largest has 2,411. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 114th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.17% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Manor (11), Austin (5).
