Grand Prairie Isd: public schools

39 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Grand Prairie Isd district in Texas: 23 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 26,638 students in 39 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 3,364. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 53rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.42% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Grand Prairie (38), Dallas (1).
