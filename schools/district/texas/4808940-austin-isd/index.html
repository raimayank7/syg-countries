Austin Isd: public schools

123 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 123 public schools in the Austin Isd district in Texas: 79 elementary, 20 high, 19 middle, 3 secondary, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 72,830 students in 123 of them; the median school has 456 students and the largest has 2,899. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.33% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Austin (122), Manchaca (1).
