Pflugerville Isd: public schools

35 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Pflugerville Isd district in Texas: 22 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 25,297 students in 35 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 2,106. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 57th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.38% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Pflugerville (24), Austin (9), Round Rock (2).
