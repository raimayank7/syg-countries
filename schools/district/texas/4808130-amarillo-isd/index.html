Amarillo Isd: public schools

55 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the Amarillo Isd district in Texas: 37 elementary, 12 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 29,729 students in 55 of them; the median school has 406 students and the largest has 2,208. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.60% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Amarillo.
