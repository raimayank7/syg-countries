Klein Isd: public schools

52 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Klein Isd district in Texas: 32 elementary, 10 middle, 8 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 53,093 students in 52 of them; the median school has 773 students and the largest has 3,987. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 29th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.56% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Klein.
