Dallas Isd: public schools

240 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 240 public schools in the Dallas Isd district in Texas: 152 elementary, 42 middle, 40 high, 4 prekindergarten, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 139,246 students in 240 of them; the median school has 481 students and the largest has 3,780. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 2.60% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Dallas (232), Seagoville (2), Mesqute (1), Carrollton (1).
