Mesquite Isd: public schools

51 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 51 public schools in the Mesquite Isd district in Texas: 34 elementary, 10 middle, 7 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 38,265 students in 51 of them; the median school has 596 students and the largest has 2,874. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 31st largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.55% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Mesquite (44), Balch Springs (4), Dallas (2), Garland (1).
