Weslaco Isd: public schools

20 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Weslaco Isd district in Texas: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 3 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 16,430 students in 20 of them; the median school has 775 students and the largest has 2,707. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 100th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.22% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Weslaco (18), Mercedes (1), Mcallen (1).
