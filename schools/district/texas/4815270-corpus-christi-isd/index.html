Corpus Christi Isd: public schools

54 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Corpus Christi Isd district in Texas: 32 elementary, 10 middle, 10 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 33,053 students in 54 of them; the median school has 544 students and the largest has 2,008. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 27th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.59% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Corpus Christi.
