Socorro Isd: public schools

51 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 51 public schools in the Socorro Isd district in Texas: 31 elementary, 11 middle, 9 high. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 47,304 students in 51 of them; the median school has 743 students and the largest has 2,947. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 30th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.55% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in El Paso.
