Denton Isd: public schools

46 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Denton Isd district in Texas: 26 elementary, 9 middle, 8 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 32,866 students in 46 of them; the median school has 646 students and the largest has 2,758. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 44th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Denton (27), Aubrey (8), Lantana (4), Corinth (2).
