Northwest Isd: public schools

34 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Northwest Isd district in Texas: 22 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 30,736 students in 34 of them; the median school has 715 students and the largest has 3,262. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 59th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Haslet (9), Roanoke (6), Fort Worth (6), Justin (4).
