Garland Isd: public schools

78 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in the Garland Isd district in Texas: 51 elementary, 14 middle, 11 high, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 51,659 students in 78 of them; the median school has 499 students and the largest has 2,908. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.85% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Garland (60), Rowlett (13), Sachse (4), Dallas (1).
