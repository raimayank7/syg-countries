Keller Isd: public schools

42 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Keller Isd district in Texas: 23 elementary, 10 middle, 5 high, 2 secondary, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 33,250 students in 42 of them; the median school has 570 students and the largest has 3,026. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 50th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.46% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Keller (25), Fort Worth (14), Southlake (1), Watauga (1).
