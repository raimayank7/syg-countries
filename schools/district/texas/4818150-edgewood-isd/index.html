Edgewood Isd (district 4818150): public schools

26 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Edgewood Isd (district 4818150) district in Texas: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 10 of them (38.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 7,930 students in 26 of them; the median school has 251 students and the largest has 990. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 72nd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.28% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Antonio.
