Killeen Isd: public schools

54 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Killeen Isd district in Texas: 32 elementary, 12 middle, 8 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 43,864 students in 54 of them; the median school has 718 students and the largest has 2,268. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 26th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.59% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Killeen (37), Fort Cavazos (8), Harker Heights (7), Nolanville (2).
