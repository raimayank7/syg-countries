Burleson Isd: public schools

19 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Burleson Isd district in Texas: 10 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 12,718 students in 19 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 2,099. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 104th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.21% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Burleson (17), Fort Worth (2).
