Plano Isd: public schools

77 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 77 public schools in the Plano Isd district in Texas: 45 elementary, 13 middle, 6 high, 6 secondary, 4 prekindergarten, 2 other, 1 adult education. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 47,899 students in 77 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 3,050. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

This is the 13th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.84% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Plano (65), Richardson (4), Murphy (4), Dallas (3).
