Allen Isd: public schools

24 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Allen Isd district in Texas: 16 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 secondary, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 21,370 students in 24 of them; the median school has 690 students and the largest has 5,273. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 86th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.26% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Allen (22), Parker (1), Mckinney (1).
