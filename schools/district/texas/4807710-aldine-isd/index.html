Aldine Isd: public schools

80 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 80 public schools in the Aldine Isd district in Texas: 50 elementary, 15 middle, 11 high, 2 other, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 57,844 students in 80 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 3,573. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.87% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Houston (72), Humble (8).
