Sherman Isd: public schools

16 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Sherman Isd district in Texas: 7 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 7,843 students in 16 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 2,160. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 111th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.17% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Sherman (11), Denison (5).
