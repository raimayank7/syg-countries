Lewisville Isd: public schools

62 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 62 public schools in the Lewisville Isd district in Texas: 39 elementary, 15 middle, 6 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 48,440 students in 62 of them; the median school has 591 students and the largest has 4,162. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.67% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Lewisville (20), Flower Mound (19), Carrollton (9), The Colony (8).
