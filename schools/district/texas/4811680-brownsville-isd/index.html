Brownsville Isd: public schools

56 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Brownsville Isd district in Texas: 34 elementary, 10 middle, 10 high, 1 other, 1 adult education. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 37,065 students in 56 of them; the median school has 535 students and the largest has 2,294. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

This is the 22nd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.61% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Brownsville (55), San Benito (1).
