Conroe Isd: public schools

66 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 66 public schools in the Conroe Isd district in Texas: 37 elementary, 18 middle, 8 high, 2 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 72,352 students in 66 of them; the median school has 864 students and the largest has 5,178. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.72% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Conroe (34), The Woodlands (18), Spring (12), Montgomery (2).
