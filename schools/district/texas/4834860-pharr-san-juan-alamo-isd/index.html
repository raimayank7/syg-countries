Pharr-San Juan-Alamo Isd: public schools

44 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Pharr-San Juan-Alamo Isd district in Texas: 26 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 30,008 students in 44 of them; the median school has 593 students and the largest has 2,360. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 46th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Pharr (24), San Juan (12), Alamo (8).
