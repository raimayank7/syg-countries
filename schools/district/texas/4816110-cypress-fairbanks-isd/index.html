Cypress-Fairbanks Isd: public schools

93 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 93 public schools in the Cypress-Fairbanks Isd district in Texas: 58 elementary, 20 middle, 14 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 118,470 students in 93 of them; the median school has 1,006 students and the largest has 3,647. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.01% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Houston (47), Cypress (32), Katy (13), Cyress (1).
