Fort Bend Isd: public schools

83 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 83 public schools in the Fort Bend Isd district in Texas: 53 elementary, 16 middle, 13 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 80,206 students in 83 of them; the median school has 752 students and the largest has 3,142. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.90% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Sugar Land (28), Missouri City (25), Richmond (12), Houston (11).
