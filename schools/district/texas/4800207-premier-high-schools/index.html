Premier High Schools: public schools

49 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Premier High Schools district in Texas: 47 high, 2 elementary. 49 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 47 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 7,851 students in 49 of them; the median school has 112 students and the largest has 2,677. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 36th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.53% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 38 towns, most in Houston (5), San Antonio (3), Lubbock (2), Austin (2).
