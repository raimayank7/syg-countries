University Of Texas University Charter School: public schools

18 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the University Of Texas University Charter School district in Texas: 10 high, 3 other, 3 secondary, 1 middle, 1 elementary. 18 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 alternative schools. The file reports 509 students in 18 of them; the median school has 20 students and the largest has 90. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 106th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.20% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 14 towns, most in Austin (4), San Antonio (2), Driftwood (1), Mountain Home (1).
