El Paso Isd: public schools

75 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 75 public schools in the El Paso Isd district in Texas: 49 elementary, 16 high, 9 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 49,139 students in 75 of them; the median school has 499 students and the largest has 3,048. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.81% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in El Paso.
