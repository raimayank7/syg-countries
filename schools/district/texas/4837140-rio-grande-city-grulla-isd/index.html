Rio Grande City Grulla Isd: public schools

15 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Rio Grande City Grulla Isd district in Texas: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high. The file reports 9,482 students in 15 of them; the median school has 584 students and the largest has 1,674. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 120th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.16% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Rio Grande City (12), Grulla (2), Garciasville (1).
