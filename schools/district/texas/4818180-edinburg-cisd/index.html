Edinburg Cisd: public schools

46 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Edinburg Cisd district in Texas: 31 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 33,810 students in 46 of them; the median school has 572 students and the largest has 2,707. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 42nd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Edinburg (44), Hargill (1), Mcallen (1).
