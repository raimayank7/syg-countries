Idea Public Schools: public schools

123 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 123 public schools in the Idea Public Schools district in Texas: 61 elementary, 32 high, 23 middle, 7 secondary. 123 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 76,819 students in 123 of them; the median school has 678 students and the largest has 878. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.33% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 27 towns, most in San Antonio (28), Brownsville (10), Austin (10), Mission (6).
