Humble Isd: public schools

50 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the Humble Isd district in Texas: 30 elementary, 10 middle, 9 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 48,552 students in 50 of them; the median school has 720 students and the largest has 3,790. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 33rd largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.54% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Humble (30), Kingwood (14), Houston (6).
