Harmony Public Schools - North Texas: public schools

16 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the Harmony Public Schools - North Texas district in Texas: 8 elementary, 7 high, 1 other. 16 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 10,272 students in 16 of them; the median school has 606 students and the largest has 1,068. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 117th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.17% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Dallas (3), Fort Worth (2), Grand Prairie (2), Euless (2).
