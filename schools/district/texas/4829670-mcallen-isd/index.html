Mcallen Isd: public schools

31 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Mcallen Isd district in Texas: 18 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,095 students in 31 of them; the median school has 486 students and the largest has 2,152. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 64th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.34% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Mcallen (30), Mc Allen (1).
