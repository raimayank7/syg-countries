Lubbock Isd: public schools

49 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Lubbock Isd district in Texas: 30 elementary, 9 middle, 8 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. 4 of them (8.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 24,329 students in 49 of them; the median school has 417 students and the largest has 2,081. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 35th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.53% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Lubbock.
