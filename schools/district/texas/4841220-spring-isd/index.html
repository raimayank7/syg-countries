Spring Isd: public schools

43 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Spring Isd district in Texas: 26 elementary, 9 middle, 7 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 33,935 students in 43 of them; the median school has 691 students and the largest has 2,760. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 49th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.47% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Houston (29), Spring (14).
