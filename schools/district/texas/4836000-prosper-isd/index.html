Prosper Isd: public schools

27 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Prosper Isd district in Texas: 17 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 secondary, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 28,467 students in 27 of them; the median school has 836 students and the largest has 3,449. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 71st largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.29% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Prosper (15), Mckinney (6), Frisco (3), Celina (2).
