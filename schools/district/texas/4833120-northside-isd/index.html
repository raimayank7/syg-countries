Northside Isd: public schools

126 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 126 public schools in the Northside Isd district in Texas: 83 elementary, 23 middle, 16 high, 2 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools. The file reports 101,095 students in 126 of them; the median school has 579 students and the largest has 3,052. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.37% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in San Antonio (121), Helotes (5).
