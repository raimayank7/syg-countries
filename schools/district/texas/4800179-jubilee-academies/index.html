Jubilee Academies: public schools

13 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Jubilee Academies district in Texas: 7 other, 4 elementary, 2 middle. 13 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 6,017 students in 13 of them; the median school has 421 students and the largest has 1,054. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 141st largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.14% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in San Antonio (7), Brownsville (3), Harlingen (1), Kingsville (1).
