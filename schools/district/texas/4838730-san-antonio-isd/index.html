San Antonio Isd: public schools

98 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 98 public schools in the San Antonio Isd district in Texas: 60 elementary, 19 high, 12 middle, 6 prekindergarten, 1 other. 31 of them (31.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 44,670 students in 98 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 1,656. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 1.06% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in San Antonio.
