Waxahachie Isd: public schools

17 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Waxahachie Isd district in Texas: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 11,052 students in 17 of them; the median school has 497 students and the largest has 3,110. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 107th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.18% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Waxahachie.
