Manchester School District: public schools

21 schools

New Hampshire, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Manchester School District district in New Hampshire: 13 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle. The file reports 11,936 students in 21 of them; the median school has 457 students and the largest has 1,355. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 197 districts in New Hampshire by number of public schools, and holds 4.14% of the state's 507 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Manchester.
