South Washington County Schools: public schools

29 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the South Washington County Schools district in Minnesota: 17 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 19,360 students in 29 of them; the median school has 533 students and the largest has 2,042. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.08% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Cottage Grove (14), Woodbury (12), Saint Paul Park (2), Newport (1).
