Rochester Public School District: public schools

49 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Rochester Public School District district in Minnesota: 20 elementary, 14 high, 6 middle, 5 prekindergarten, 4 other. Besides regular schools there are 10 special education schools, 9 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,342 students in 49 of them; the median school has 201 students and the largest has 1,820. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.82% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Rochester.
