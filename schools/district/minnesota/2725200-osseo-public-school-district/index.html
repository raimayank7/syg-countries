Osseo Public School District: public schools

34 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Osseo Public School District district in Minnesota: 18 elementary, 10 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 20,776 students in 34 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 2,408. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.26% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Brooklyn Park (18), Maple Grove (11), Osseo (3), Brooklyn Center (2).
