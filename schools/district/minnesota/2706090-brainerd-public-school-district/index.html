Brainerd Public School District: public schools

13 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Brainerd Public School District district in Minnesota: 6 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 6,134 students in 13 of them; the median school has 255 students and the largest has 1,833. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 44th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Brainerd (9), Baxter (3), Nisswa (1).
