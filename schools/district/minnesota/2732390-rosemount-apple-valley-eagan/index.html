Rosemount-Apple Valley-Eagan: public schools

40 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in the Rosemount-Apple Valley-Eagan district in Minnesota: 20 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 29,115 students in 40 of them; the median school has 677 students and the largest has 2,380. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.48% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Apple Valley (17), Eagan (12), Rosemount (9), Burnsville (1).
