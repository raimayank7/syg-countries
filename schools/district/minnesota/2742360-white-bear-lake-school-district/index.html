White Bear Lake School District: public schools

25 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the White Bear Lake School District district in Minnesota: 11 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 8,549 students in 25 of them; the median school has 246 students and the largest has 1,322. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.93% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in White Bear Lake (21), Hugo (4).
