Minneapolis Public School District: public schools

98 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 98 public schools in the Minneapolis Public School District district in Minnesota: 43 elementary, 38 high, 8 middle, 6 other, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 29 alternative schools, 6 special education schools. The file reports 29,063 students in 98 of them; the median school has 260 students and the largest has 1,555. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 3.64% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Minneapolis.
