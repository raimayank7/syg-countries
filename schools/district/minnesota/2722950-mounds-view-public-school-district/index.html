Mounds View Public School District: public schools

27 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Mounds View Public School District district in Minnesota: 10 high, 9 elementary, 3 prekindergarten, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 7 alternative schools. The file reports 11,632 students in 27 of them; the median school has 120 students and the largest has 1,901. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.00% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in New Brighton (8), Shoreview (7), Mounds View (6), Arden Hills (2).
