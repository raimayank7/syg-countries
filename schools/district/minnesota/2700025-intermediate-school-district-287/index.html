Intermediate School District 287: public schools

50 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the Intermediate School District 287 district in Minnesota: 41 high, 8 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 45 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 858 students in 50 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 258. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.85% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Minnetonka (10), Plymouth (9), Eden Prairie (7), Richfield (6).
