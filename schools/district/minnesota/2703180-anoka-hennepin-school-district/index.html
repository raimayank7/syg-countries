Anoka-Hennepin School District: public schools

51 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 51 public schools in the Anoka-Hennepin School District district in Minnesota: 26 elementary, 16 high, 6 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 38,631 students in 51 of them; the median school has 542 students and the largest has 3,103. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 1.89% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Coon Rapids (16), Anoka (11), Blaine (8), Andover (6).
