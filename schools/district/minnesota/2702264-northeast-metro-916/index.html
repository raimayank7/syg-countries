Northeast Metro 916: public schools

15 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Northeast Metro 916 district in Minnesota: 10 high, 2 elementary, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 6 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 830 students in 15 of them; the median school has 33 students and the largest has 190. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 32nd largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.56% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in White Bear Lake (5), Little Canada (2), Blaine (2), Fridley (2).
