Saint Paul Public Schools: public schools

102 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 102 public schools in the Saint Paul Public Schools district in Minnesota: 41 elementary, 24 high, 17 prekindergarten, 11 middle, 9 other. Besides regular schools there are 21 special education schools, 19 alternative schools. The file reports 32,145 students in 102 of them; the median school has 265 students and the largest has 1,597. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 3.78% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Saint Paul (101), Woodbury (1).
