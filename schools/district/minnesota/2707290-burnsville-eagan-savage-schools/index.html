Burnsville-Eagan-Savage Schools: public schools

20 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Burnsville-Eagan-Savage Schools district in Minnesota: 9 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 7,594 students in 20 of them; the median school has 341 students and the largest has 2,100. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.74% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Burnsville (14), Eagan (3), Savage (3).
