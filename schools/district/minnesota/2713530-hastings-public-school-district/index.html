Hastings Public School District: public schools

12 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Hastings Public School District district in Minnesota: 5 high, 4 elementary, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 4,095 students in 12 of them; the median school has 69 students and the largest has 1,390. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 49th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.45% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Hastings.
