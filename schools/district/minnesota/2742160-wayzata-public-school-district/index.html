Wayzata Public School District: public schools

21 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Wayzata Public School District district in Minnesota: 9 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 12,625 students in 21 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 3,548. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.78% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Plymouth (20), Wayzata (1).
