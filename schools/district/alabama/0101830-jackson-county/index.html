Jackson County: public schools

18 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Jackson County district in Alabama: 8 elementary, 6 other, 2 middle, 2 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,181 students in 16 of them; the median school has 234 students and the largest has 703. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Hollywood (3), Stevenson (3), Bridgeport (2), Pisgah (2).
