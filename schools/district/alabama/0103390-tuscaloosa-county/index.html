Tuscaloosa County: public schools

36 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in the Tuscaloosa County district in Alabama: 19 elementary, 9 middle, 7 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 19,228 students in 35 of them; the median school has 491 students and the largest has 1,562. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 2.36% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Northport (14), Tuscaloosa (7), Buhl (3), Brookwood (2).
