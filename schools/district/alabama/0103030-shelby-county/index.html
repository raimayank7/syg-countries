Shelby County: public schools

33 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Shelby County district in Alabama: 16 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,937 students in 30 of them; the median school has 666 students and the largest has 1,652. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 2.17% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Birmingham (6), Columbiana (5), Chelsea (4), Helena (4).
