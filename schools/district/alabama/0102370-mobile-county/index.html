Mobile County: public schools

92 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 92 public schools in the Mobile County district in Alabama: 54 elementary, 17 middle, 17 high, 2 prekindergarten, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 50,929 students in 89 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 2,421. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 6.04% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Mobile (56), Irvington (6), Prichard (5), Semmes (4).
