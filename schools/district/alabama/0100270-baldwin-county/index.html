Baldwin County: public schools

44 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Baldwin County district in Alabama: 25 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 31,818 students in 43 of them; the median school has 761 students and the largest has 1,799. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 2.89% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Daphne (7), Robertsdale (7), Bay Minette (6), Fairhope (5).
