Etowah County: public schools

24 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Etowah County district in Alabama: 11 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,714 students in 21 of them; the median school has 373 students and the largest has 818. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.57% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Attalla (4), Gadsden (4), Glencoe (3), Hokes Bluff (3).
