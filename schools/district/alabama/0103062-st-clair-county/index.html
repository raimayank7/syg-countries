St Clair County: public schools

20 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the St Clair County district in Alabama: 7 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,592 students in 18 of them; the median school has 510 students and the largest has 803. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Ashville (6), Odenville (4), Moody (4), Springville (3).
