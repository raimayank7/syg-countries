Bessemer City: public schools

9 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in the Bessemer City district in Alabama: 5 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 3,121 students in 7 of them; the median school has 350 students and the largest has 805. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 55th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 0.59% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bessemer.
