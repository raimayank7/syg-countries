Talladega County: public schools

18 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Talladega County district in Alabama: 7 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,157 students in 17 of them; the median school has 348 students and the largest has 899. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Childersburg (4), Sylacauga (3), Alpine (3), Lincoln (3).
