Jefferson County: public schools

57 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in the Jefferson County district in Alabama: 30 elementary, 13 high, 11 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 35,671 students in 56 of them; the median school has 627 students and the largest has 1,388. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 3.74% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 22 towns, most in Birmingham (8), Pinson (5), Adamsville (4), Gardendale (4).
