DeKalb County: public schools

15 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the DeKalb County district in Alabama: 8 other, 3 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,704 students in 12 of them; the median school has 709 students and the largest has 1,218. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 28th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 0.98% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Crossville (3), Rainsville (3), Collinsville (1), Fyffe (1).
