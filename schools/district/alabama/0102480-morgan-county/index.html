Morgan County: public schools

18 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Morgan County district in Alabama: 8 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,664 students in 17 of them; the median school has 485 students and the largest has 755. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Somerville (4), Danville (3), Trinity (3), Falkville (2).
