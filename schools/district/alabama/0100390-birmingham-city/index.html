Birmingham City: public schools

49 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Birmingham City district in Alabama: 29 elementary, 9 middle, 9 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 20,884 students in 43 of them; the median school has 440 students and the largest has 1,123. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 3.22% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Birmingham.
