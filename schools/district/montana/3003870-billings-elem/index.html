Billings Elem: public schools

28 schools

Montana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Billings Elem district in Montana: 22 elementary, 6 middle. The file reports 11,089 students in 28 of them; the median school has 317 students and the largest has 742. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 1st largest of the 397 districts in Montana by number of public schools, and holds 3.41% of the state's 821 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Billings.
