Nebo District: public schools

49 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Nebo District district in Utah: 30 elementary, 10 middle, 5 high, 4 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 44,446 students in 49 of them; the median school has 622 students and the largest has 8,702. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 4.47% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Spanish Fork (14), Springville (11), Payson (8), Salem (7).
