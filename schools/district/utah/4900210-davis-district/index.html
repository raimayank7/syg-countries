Davis District: public schools

96 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 96 public schools in the Davis District district in Utah: 63 elementary, 17 middle, 14 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 72,499 students in 96 of them; the median school has 639 students and the largest has 2,566. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 8.76% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Layton (19), Kaysville (17), Bountiful (15), Farmington (9).
