Cache District: public schools

26 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Cache District district in Utah: 17 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,265 students in 26 of them; the median school has 723 students and the largest has 1,861. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 2.37% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Hyrum (4), North Logan (4), Smithfield (4), Logan (2).
