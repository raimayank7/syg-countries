Canyons District: public schools

50 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in the Canyons District district in Utah: 30 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 33,196 students in 50 of them; the median school has 543 students and the largest has 2,451. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 4.56% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Sandy (32), Midvale (6), Salt Lake City (5), Draper (5).
