Weber District: public schools

52 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Weber District district in Utah: 31 elementary, 10 middle, 8 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 32,443 students in 52 of them; the median school has 564 students and the largest has 2,252. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 4.74% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Ogden (27), Roy (9), Pleasant View (3), West Haven City (2).
