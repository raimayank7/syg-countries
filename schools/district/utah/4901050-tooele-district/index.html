Tooele District: public schools

27 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Tooele District district in Utah: 16 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 16,087 students in 27 of them; the median school has 593 students and the largest has 2,296. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 2.46% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Tooele (14), Grantsville (5), Stansbury Park (3), Wendover (2).
