Duchesne District: public schools

15 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Duchesne District district in Utah: 8 elementary, 4 high, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 5,328 students in 15 of them; the median school has 357 students and the largest has 1,155. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 1.37% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Roosevelt (6), Altamont (2), Myton (2), Duchesne (2).
