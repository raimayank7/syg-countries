Iron District: public schools

18 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Iron District district in Utah: 9 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,790 students in 18 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 4,769. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 1.64% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Cedar City (14), Parowan (2), Beryl (1), Enoch (1).
