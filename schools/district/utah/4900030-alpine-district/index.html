Alpine District: public schools

91 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 91 public schools in the Alpine District district in Utah: 62 elementary, 13 high, 12 middle, 2 other, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 87,051 students in 91 of them; the median school has 819 students and the largest has 3,190. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 8.30% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Orem (19), Lehi (17), American Fork (12), Saratoga Springs (11).
