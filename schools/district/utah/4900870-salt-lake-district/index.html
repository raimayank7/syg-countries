Salt Lake District: public schools

40 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in the Salt Lake District district in Utah: 27 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 19,477 students in 40 of them; the median school has 371 students and the largest has 2,505. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 3.65% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Salt Lake City.
