Jordan District: public schools

69 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 69 public schools in the Jordan District district in Utah: 42 elementary, 13 middle, 12 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 59,145 students in 69 of them; the median school has 730 students and the largest has 2,959. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 6.30% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in West Jordan (28), South Jordan (15), Riverton (12), Herriman (11).
