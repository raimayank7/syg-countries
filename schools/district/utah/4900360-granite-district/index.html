Granite District: public schools

86 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 86 public schools in the Granite District district in Utah: 58 elementary, 15 middle, 9 high, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 60,270 students in 86 of them; the median school has 521 students and the largest has 3,434. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 7.85% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Salt Lake City (35), West Valley City (24), Kearns (10), Taylorsville (8).
