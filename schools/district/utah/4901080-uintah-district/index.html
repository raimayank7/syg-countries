Uintah District: public schools

13 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Uintah District district in Utah: 8 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,942 students in 13 of them; the median school has 498 students and the largest has 1,856. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 1.19% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Vernal (11), La Point (1), Roosevelt (1).
