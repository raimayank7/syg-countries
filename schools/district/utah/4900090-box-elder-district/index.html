Box Elder District: public schools

23 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in the Box Elder District district in Utah: 13 elementary, 4 secondary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,578 students in 23 of them; the median school has 586 students and the largest has 1,603. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 2.10% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Brigham City (7), Garland (3), Tremonton (3), Grouse Creek (2).
