Washington District: public schools

56 schools

Utah, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Washington District district in Utah: 30 elementary, 10 high, 6 other, 5 middle, 4 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 37,575 students in 56 of them; the median school has 584 students and the largest has 3,324. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 155 districts in Utah by number of public schools, and holds 5.11% of the state's 1,096 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in St George (22), Saint George (10), Washington (8), Hurricane (5).
