Edmond: public schools

28 schools

Oklahoma, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Edmond district in Oklahoma: 19 elementary, 6 middle, 3 high. The file reports 25,878 students in 28 of them; the median school has 794 students and the largest has 2,780. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 542 districts in Oklahoma by number of public schools, and holds 1.57% of the state's 1,778 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Edmond (26), Arcadia (1), Okc (1).
