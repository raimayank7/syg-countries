Oklahoma City: public schools

58 schools

Oklahoma, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in the Oklahoma City district in Oklahoma: 32 elementary, 16 middle, 10 high. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 32,899 students in 58 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 1,902. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 542 districts in Oklahoma by number of public schools, and holds 3.26% of the state's 1,778 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Oklahoma City (55), Spencer (2), Nichols Hills (1).
