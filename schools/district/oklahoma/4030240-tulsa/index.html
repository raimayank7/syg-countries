Tulsa: public schools

69 schools

Oklahoma, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 69 public schools in the Tulsa district in Oklahoma: 46 elementary, 12 middle, 11 high. The file reports 33,562 students in 69 of them; the median school has 385 students and the largest has 1,285. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 542 districts in Oklahoma by number of public schools, and holds 3.88% of the state's 1,778 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Tulsa (68), TUlsa (1).
