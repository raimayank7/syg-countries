Putnam City: public schools

26 schools

Oklahoma, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Putnam City district in Oklahoma: 18 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. The file reports 18,733 students in 26 of them; the median school has 568 students and the largest has 2,002. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 542 districts in Oklahoma by number of public schools, and holds 1.46% of the state's 1,778 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Oklahoma City (19), Bethany (5), Warr Acres (2).
