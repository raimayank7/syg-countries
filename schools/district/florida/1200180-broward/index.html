Broward: public schools

325 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 325 public schools in the Broward district in Florida: 194 elementary, 63 high, 48 middle, 14 other, 6 prekindergarten. 87 of them (26.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 20 alternative schools, 6 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 251,408 students in 325 of them; the median school has 604 students and the largest has 4,632. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 7.69% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 28 towns, most in Hollywood (36), Fort Lauderdale (30), Coral Springs (30), Pembroke Pines (28).
