Baker: public schools

10 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in the Baker district in Florida: 7 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. The file reports 4,929 students in 10 of them; the median school has 551 students and the largest has 1,436. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 55th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.24% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Macclenny (8), Glen St Mary (2).
