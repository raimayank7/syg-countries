Monroe: public schools

25 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Monroe district in Florida: 13 elementary, 11 high, 1 other. 7 of them (28.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 8,908 students in 25 of them; the median school has 180 students and the largest has 1,302. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 33rd largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.59% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Key West (15), Tavernier (3), Marathon (2), Key Largo (2).
