Indian River: public schools

28 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Indian River district in Florida: 17 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 2 other. 5 of them (17.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 16,950 students in 28 of them; the median school has 533 students and the largest has 2,783. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 31st largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.66% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Vero Beach (20), Sebastian (7), Fellsmere (1).
