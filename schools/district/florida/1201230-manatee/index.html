Manatee: public schools

82 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in the Manatee district in Florida: 44 elementary, 13 high, 12 middle, 11 other, 2 prekindergarten. 15 of them (18.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 52,895 students in 82 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 2,368. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.94% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Bradenton (53), Palmetto (15), Sarasota (4), Parrish (4).
