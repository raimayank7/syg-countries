St. Lucie: public schools

53 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in the St. Lucie district in Florida: 29 elementary, 14 high, 6 other, 4 middle. 7 of them (13.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 46,987 students in 53 of them; the median school has 702 students and the largest has 3,187. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.25% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Port St Lucie (28), Fort Pierce (21), Ft. Lauderdale (2), Port Saint Lucie (1).
