Martin: public schools

35 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Martin district in Florida: 13 elementary, 9 high, 5 middle, 5 other, 3 prekindergarten. 4 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 18,264 students in 35 of them; the median school has 505 students and the largest has 2,122. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 29th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.83% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Stuart (24), Palm City (4), Hobe Sound (2), Indiantown (2).
