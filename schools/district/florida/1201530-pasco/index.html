Pasco: public schools

106 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 106 public schools in the Pasco district in Florida: 59 elementary, 25 high, 16 middle, 5 other, 1 prekindergarten. 14 of them (13.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 4 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 85,855 students in 106 of them; the median school has 688 students and the largest has 2,278. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 2.51% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in New Port Richey (29), Wesley Chapel (19), Land O Lakes (17), Dade City (10).
