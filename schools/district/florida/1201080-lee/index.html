Lee: public schools

120 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 120 public schools in the Lee district in Florida: 61 elementary, 34 high, 18 middle, 6 other, 1 prekindergarten. 21 of them (17.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 100,064 students in 120 of them; the median school has 808 students and the largest has 2,977. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 2.84% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 13 towns, most in Fort Myers (50), Cape Coral (27), Lehigh Acres (16), North Fort Myers (7).
