Seminole: public schools

76 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 76 public schools in the Seminole district in Florida: 43 elementary, 12 middle, 12 high, 7 other, 2 prekindergarten. 6 of them (7.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 65,443 students in 76 of them; the median school has 740 students and the largest has 3,919. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.80% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Sanford (24), Lake Mary (11), Oviedo (10), Winter Springs (8).
