Collier: public schools

74 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 74 public schools in the Collier district in Florida: 35 elementary, 18 high, 13 middle, 8 other. 9 of them (12.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 48,282 students in 74 of them; the median school has 583 students and the largest has 2,055. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.75% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Naples (57), Immokalee (13), Marco Island (3), Everglades City (1).
