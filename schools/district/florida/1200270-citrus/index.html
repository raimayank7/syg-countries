Citrus: public schools

27 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Citrus district in Florida: 11 elementary, 8 high, 4 middle, 4 other. 1 of them (3.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,889 students in 27 of them; the median school has 667 students and the largest has 1,763. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 32nd largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.64% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Inverness (7), Lecanto (7), Crystal River (4), Citrus Springs (3).
