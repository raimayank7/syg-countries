Marion: public schools

64 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 64 public schools in the Marion district in Florida: 35 elementary, 16 high, 10 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (7.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 45,547 students in 64 of them; the median school has 687 students and the largest has 2,997. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.51% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Ocala (45), Dunnellon (4), Belleview (4), Anthony (2).
