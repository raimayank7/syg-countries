Lake: public schools

61 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 61 public schools in the Lake district in Florida: 31 elementary, 13 high, 9 middle, 7 other, 1 prekindergarten. 12 of them (19.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 47,634 students in 61 of them; the median school has 735 students and the largest has 2,757. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 21st largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.44% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 17 towns, most in Eustis (11), Clermont (11), Leesburg (7), Tavares (7).
