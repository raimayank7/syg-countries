Walton: public schools

24 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Walton district in Florida: 10 high, 6 elementary, 4 other, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,779 students in 24 of them; the median school has 532 students and the largest has 1,161. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 35th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.57% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Defuniak Springs (13), Santa Rosa Beach (5), Freeport (3), Paxton (1).
