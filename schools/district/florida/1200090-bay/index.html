Bay: public schools

49 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Bay district in Florida: 24 elementary, 11 high, 9 other, 5 middle. 12 of them (24.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 27,672 students in 49 of them; the median school has 525 students and the largest has 1,838. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 27th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.16% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Panama City (36), Panama City Beach (7), Lynn Haven (3), Southport (1).
