Alachua: public schools

68 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 68 public schools in the Alachua district in Florida: 33 elementary, 20 high, 10 middle, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten. 16 of them (23.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 28,749 students in 68 of them; the median school has 321 students and the largest has 2,444. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.61% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Gainesville (50), Alachua (6), Newberry (4), Hawthorne (2).
