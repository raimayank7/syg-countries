Miami-Dade: public schools

536 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 536 public schools in the Miami-Dade district in Florida: 293 elementary, 135 high, 77 middle, 26 other, 4 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 158 of them (29.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 41 alternative schools, 8 special education schools, 5 career and technical schools. The file reports 337,719 students in 536 of them; the median school has 505 students and the largest has 4,389. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 12.67% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 30 towns, most in Miami (299), Hialeah (45), Homestead (44), Doral (23).
