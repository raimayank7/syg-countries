St. Johns: public schools

55 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the St. Johns district in Florida: 27 elementary, 16 high, 7 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (5.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 51,432 students in 55 of them; the median school has 805 students and the largest has 2,554. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 23rd largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.30% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in St Augustine (32), Saint Johns (11), Ponte Vedra (4), Ponte Vedra Beach (3).
