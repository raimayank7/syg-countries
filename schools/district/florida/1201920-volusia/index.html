Volusia: public schools

89 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 89 public schools in the Volusia district in Florida: 48 elementary, 16 high, 12 middle, 11 other, 2 prekindergarten. 6 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 62,741 students in 89 of them; the median school has 640 students and the largest has 2,869. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 2.10% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 17 towns, most in Daytona Beach (19), Deltona (16), Deland (11), Port Orange (11).
