Escambia: public schools

70 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 70 public schools in the Escambia district in Florida: 35 elementary, 16 high, 10 middle, 7 other, 2 prekindergarten. 7 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 37,203 students in 70 of them; the median school has 488 students and the largest has 2,156. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.66% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Pensacola (60), Cantonment (4), Century (3), Walnut Hill (1).
