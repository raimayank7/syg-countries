Clay: public schools

54 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Clay district in Florida: 29 elementary, 11 high, 8 other, 6 middle. 4 of them (7.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 39,362 students in 54 of them; the median school has 686 students and the largest has 2,364. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 24th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.28% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Orange Park (25), Green Cove Springs (11), Middleburg (10), Keystone Heights (3).
