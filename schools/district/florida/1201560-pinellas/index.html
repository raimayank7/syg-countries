Pinellas: public schools

153 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 153 public schools in the Pinellas district in Florida: 90 elementary, 31 high, 20 middle, 12 other. 17 of them (11.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 16 alternative schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 91,021 students in 153 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 2,502. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 3.62% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in St Petersburg (41), Clearwater (30), Largo (19), Pinellas Park (12).
