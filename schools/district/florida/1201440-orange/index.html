Orange: public schools

275 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 275 public schools in the Orange district in Florida: 164 elementary, 53 high, 43 middle, 13 other, 2 prekindergarten. 42 of them (15.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 29 alternative schools, 8 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 207,695 students in 275 of them; the median school has 591 students and the largest has 4,546. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 6.50% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Orlando (200), Winter Garden (23), Apopka (17), Ocoee (11).
