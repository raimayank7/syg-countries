Sarasota: public schools

63 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 63 public schools in the Sarasota district in Florida: 31 elementary, 11 middle, 11 high, 6 other, 4 prekindergarten. 14 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 45,314 students in 63 of them; the median school has 636 students and the largest has 2,681. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 20th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.49% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Sarasota (39), Venice (10), North Port (9), Nokomis (1).
