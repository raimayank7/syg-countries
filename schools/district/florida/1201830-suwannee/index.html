Suwannee: public schools

13 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Suwannee district in Florida: 5 other, 4 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,968 students in 13 of them; the median school has 619 students and the largest has 1,202. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 45th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Live Oak (11), Branford (2).
