Santa Rosa: public schools

42 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Santa Rosa district in Florida: 19 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 6 other, 2 prekindergarten. 2 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 29,823 students in 42 of them; the median school has 702 students and the largest has 2,430. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 28th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.99% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Milton (20), Gulf Breeze (6), Pace (6), Navarre (6).
