Madison: public schools

13 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Madison district in Florida: 5 elementary, 4 other, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (23.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 2,300 students in 13 of them; the median school has 96 students and the largest has 802. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 48th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Madison (8), Greenville (3), Lee (1), Pinetta (1).
