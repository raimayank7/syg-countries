Polk: public schools

167 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 167 public schools in the Polk district in Florida: 87 elementary, 38 high, 27 middle, 10 other, 4 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 35 of them (21.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 alternative schools, 8 special education schools. The file reports 116,031 students in 167 of them; the median school has 614 students and the largest has 2,914. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 3.95% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 20 towns, most in Lakeland (62), Winter Haven (22), Bartow (17), Davenport (11).
