Okaloosa: public schools

52 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Okaloosa district in Florida: 21 elementary, 14 high, 9 other, 8 middle. 4 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 32,426 students in 52 of them; the median school has 566 students and the largest has 2,397. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 26th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 1.23% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Crestview (17), Fort Walton Beach (15), Niceville (6), Shalimar (3).
