Palm Beach: public schools

235 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 235 public schools in the Palm Beach district in Florida: 133 elementary, 52 high, 37 middle, 13 other. 47 of them (20.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 alternative schools, 13 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 191,398 students in 235 of them; the median school has 731 students and the largest has 3,278. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 5.56% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 24 towns, most in West Palm Beach (54), Boca Raton (25), Boynton Beach (24), Lake Worth (17).
