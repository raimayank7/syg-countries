Brevard: public schools

109 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 109 public schools in the Brevard district in Florida: 67 elementary, 22 high, 11 middle, 8 other, 1 prekindergarten. 14 of them (12.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 73,810 students in 109 of them; the median school has 581 students and the largest has 2,314. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 2.58% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 16 towns, most in Palm Bay (20), Melbourne (18), Cocoa (13), Titusville (10).
