Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands Public School: public schools

35 schools

Northern Marianas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands Public School district in Northern Marianas: 14 prekindergarten, 9 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 ungraded. The file reports 9,217 students in 35 of them; the median school has 183 students and the largest has 1,626. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 1st largest of the 1 districts in Northern Marianas by number of public schools, and holds 100.00% of the state's 35 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Saipan (29), Rota (3), Tinian (3).
