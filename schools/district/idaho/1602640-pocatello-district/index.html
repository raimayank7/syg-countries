Pocatello District: public schools

24 schools

Idaho, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Pocatello District district in Idaho: 14 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,908 students in 24 of them; the median school has 427 students and the largest has 1,539. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 189 districts in Idaho by number of public schools, and holds 3.00% of the state's 801 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Pocatello (22), Chubbuck (2).
