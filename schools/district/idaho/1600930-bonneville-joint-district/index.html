Bonneville Joint District: public schools

26 schools

Idaho, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Bonneville Joint District district in Idaho: 15 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,646 students in 26 of them; the median school has 468 students and the largest has 1,731. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 189 districts in Idaho by number of public schools, and holds 3.25% of the state's 801 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Idaho Falls (19), Ammon (6), Iona (1).
