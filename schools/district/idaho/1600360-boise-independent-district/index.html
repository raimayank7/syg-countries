Boise Independent District: public schools

52 schools

Idaho, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Boise Independent District district in Idaho: 34 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,425 students in 52 of them; the median school has 336 students and the largest has 1,517. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 189 districts in Idaho by number of public schools, and holds 6.49% of the state's 801 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Boise (51), Hidden Springs (1).
