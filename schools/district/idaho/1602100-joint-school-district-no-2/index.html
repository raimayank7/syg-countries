Joint School District No. 2: public schools

61 schools

Idaho, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 61 public schools in the Joint School District No. 2 district in Idaho: 34 elementary, 15 high, 11 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 38,991 students in 61 of them; the median school has 485 students and the largest has 2,521. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 189 districts in Idaho by number of public schools, and holds 7.62% of the state's 801 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Meridian (36), Boise (16), Eagle (7), Star (2).
