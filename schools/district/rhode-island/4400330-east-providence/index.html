East Providence: public schools

12 schools

Rhode Island, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the East Providence district in Rhode Island: 7 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,192 students in 11 of them; the median school has 255 students and the largest has 1,697. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 64 districts in Rhode Island by number of public schools, and holds 3.85% of the state's 312 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in East Providence (8), Riverside (3), Rumford (1).
