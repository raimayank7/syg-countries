Providence: public schools

39 schools

Rhode Island, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Providence district in Rhode Island: 20 elementary, 12 high, 7 middle. 2 of them (5.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,853 students in 39 of them; the median school has 459 students and the largest has 1,119. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 64 districts in Rhode Island by number of public schools, and holds 12.50% of the state's 312 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Providence.
