Sioux Falls School District 49-5: public schools

44 schools

South Dakota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Sioux Falls School District 49-5 district in South Dakota: 25 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 25,227 students in 44 of them; the median school has 488 students and the largest has 1,934. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 151 districts in South Dakota by number of public schools, and holds 6.18% of the state's 712 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Sioux Falls (43), Renner (1).
