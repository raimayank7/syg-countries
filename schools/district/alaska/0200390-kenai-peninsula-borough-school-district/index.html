Kenai Peninsula Borough School District: public schools

42 schools

Alaska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in the Kenai Peninsula Borough School District district in Alaska: 17 elementary, 12 other, 9 high, 4 middle. 4 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 8,499 students in 42 of them; the median school has 153 students and the largest has 1,118. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 54 districts in Alaska by number of public schools, and holds 8.50% of the state's 494 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 19 towns, most in Kenai (8), Homer (8), Soldotna (8), Seward (3).
