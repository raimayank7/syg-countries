Matanuska-Susitna Borough School District: public schools

49 schools

Alaska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in the Matanuska-Susitna Borough School District district in Alaska: 27 elementary, 10 high, 9 other, 3 middle. 8 of them (16.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,421 students in 49 of them; the median school has 347 students and the largest has 2,558. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 54 districts in Alaska by number of public schools, and holds 9.92% of the state's 494 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Wasilla (26), Palmer (14), Sutton (2), Talkeetna (2).
