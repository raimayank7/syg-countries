Fairbanks North Star Borough School District: public schools

34 schools

Alaska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Fairbanks North Star Borough School District district in Alaska: 20 elementary, 8 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 5 of them (14.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,541 students in 34 of them; the median school has 373 students and the largest has 940. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 54 districts in Alaska by number of public schools, and holds 6.88% of the state's 494 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Fairbanks (26), North Pole (5), Eielson AFB (2), Fort Wainwright (1).
