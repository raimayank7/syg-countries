Lower Kuskokwim School District: public schools

29 schools

Alaska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Lower Kuskokwim School District district in Alaska: 23 other, 3 elementary, 3 high. 1 of them (3.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 3,917 students in 29 of them; the median school has 120 students and the largest has 434. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 54 districts in Alaska by number of public schools, and holds 5.87% of the state's 494 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 23 towns, most in Bethel (6), Kasigluk (2), Atmautluak (1), Chefornak (1).
