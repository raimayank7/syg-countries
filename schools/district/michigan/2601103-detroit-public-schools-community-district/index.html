Detroit Public Schools Community District: public schools

106 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 106 public schools in the Detroit Public Schools Community District district in Michigan: 69 elementary, 25 high, 5 middle, 5 other, 2 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 47,915 students in 106 of them; the median school has 391 students and the largest has 2,485. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 1st largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 3.04% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Detroit.
