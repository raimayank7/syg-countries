Mount Clemens Community School District: public schools

3 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 3 public schools in the Mount Clemens Community School District district in Michigan: 1 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 754 students in 3 of them; the median school has 221 students and the largest has 391. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 354th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.09% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Mount Clemens.
