Chippewa Valley Schools: public schools

20 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Chippewa Valley Schools district in Michigan: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,721 students in 20 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 2,625. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.57% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Clinton Twp (10), Macomb (9), Mount Clemens (1).
