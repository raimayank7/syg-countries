West Ottawa Public School District: public schools

13 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the West Ottawa Public School District district in Michigan: 8 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,671 students in 13 of them; the median school has 435 students and the largest has 2,185. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 30th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Holland (12), West Olive (1).
