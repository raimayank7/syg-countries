Ann Arbor Public Schools: public schools

32 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Ann Arbor Public Schools district in Michigan: 21 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 16,670 students in 32 of them; the median school has 412 students and the largest has 1,683. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.92% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Ann Arbor.
