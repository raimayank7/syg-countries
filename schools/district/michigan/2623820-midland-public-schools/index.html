Midland Public Schools: public schools

13 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Midland Public Schools district in Michigan: 7 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high, 1 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 7,359 students in 13 of them; the median school has 601 students and the largest has 1,265. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 34th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Midland.
