Livonia Public Schools School District: public schools

26 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Livonia Public Schools School District district in Michigan: 14 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,974 students in 26 of them; the median school has 478 students and the largest has 1,590. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 7th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.75% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Livonia (23), Westland (3).
