Michigan Department of Corrections: public schools

25 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Michigan Department of Corrections district in Michigan: 25 high. Besides regular schools there are 25 alternative schools. The file reports 49 students in 25 of them; the median school has 0 students and the largest has 9. Grades offered run from grade 9 to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.72% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 17 towns, most in Jackson (5), Ionia (3), Saint Louis (2), Kincheloe (2).
