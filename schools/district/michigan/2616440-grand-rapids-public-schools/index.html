Grand Rapids Public Schools: public schools

51 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 51 public schools in the Grand Rapids Public Schools district in Michigan: 25 elementary, 14 high, 8 middle, 4 other. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 13,662 students in 51 of them; the median school has 239 students and the largest has 939. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 1.46% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Grand Rapids.
