Utica Community Schools: public schools

38 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in the Utica Community Schools district in Michigan: 25 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 25,356 students in 38 of them; the median school has 548 students and the largest has 1,951. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 1.09% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Sterling Heights (21), Shelby Township (12), Utica (3), Macomb (2).
