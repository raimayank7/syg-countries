Allegan Public Schools: public schools

8 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 8 public schools in the Allegan Public Schools district in Michigan: 5 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 2,119 students in 8 of them; the median school has 265 students and the largest has 614. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 102nd largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.23% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Allegan.
