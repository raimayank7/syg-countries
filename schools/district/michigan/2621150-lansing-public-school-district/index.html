Lansing Public School District: public schools

26 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in the Lansing Public School District district in Michigan: 15 elementary, 5 high, 3 other, 2 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,050 students in 26 of them; the median school has 310 students and the largest has 1,090. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.75% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Lansing.
