American Samoa Department of Education: public schools

29 schools

American Samoa, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the American Samoa Department of Education district in American Samoa: 23 elementary, 6 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 1 districts in American Samoa by number of public schools, and holds 100.00% of the state's 29 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 25 towns, most in Leone (2), Nuuuli (2), Tafuna (2), Pago Pago (2).
