Kent School District: public schools

46 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Kent School District district in Washington: 29 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 25,700 students in 46 of them; the median school has 420 students and the largest has 2,124. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.80% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Kent (32), Covington (7), Renton (5), Black Diamond (1).
