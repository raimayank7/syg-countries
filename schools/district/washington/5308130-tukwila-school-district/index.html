Tukwila School District: public schools

8 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 8 public schools in the Tukwila School District district in Washington: 4 high, 3 elementary, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 2,828 students in 8 of them; the median school has 410 students and the largest has 893. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 85th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Tukwila (4), Seattle (2), Des Moines (1), Renton (1).
