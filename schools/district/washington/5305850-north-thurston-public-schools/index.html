North Thurston Public Schools: public schools

24 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the North Thurston Public Schools district in Washington: 14 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 15,197 students in 24 of them; the median school has 534 students and the largest has 1,524. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 25th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.94% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Lacey.
