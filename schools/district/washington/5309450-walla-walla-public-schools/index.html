Walla Walla Public Schools: public schools

17 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Walla Walla Public Schools district in Washington: 6 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,534 students in 17 of them; the median school has 188 students and the largest has 1,586. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 36th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.67% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Walla Walla.
