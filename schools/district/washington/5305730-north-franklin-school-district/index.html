North Franklin School District: public schools

9 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in the North Franklin School District district in Washington: 3 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 2,074 students in 9 of them; the median school has 222 students and the largest has 609. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 77th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.35% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Connell (7), Mesa (2).
