Lake Washington School District: public schools

57 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in the Lake Washington School District district in Washington: 32 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high, 2 prekindergarten, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 13 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 30,882 students in 57 of them; the median school has 481 students and the largest has 2,367. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 2.23% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Kirkland (25), Redmond (23), Sammamish (8), Woodinville (1).
