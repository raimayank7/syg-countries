Kennewick School District: public schools

33 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Kennewick School District district in Washington: 17 elementary, 9 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,805 students in 33 of them; the median school has 449 students and the largest has 1,950. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.29% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Kennewick (32), Richland (1).
