Tacoma School District: public schools

69 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 69 public schools in the Tacoma School District district in Washington: 36 elementary, 16 high, 13 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 28,272 students in 69 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 1,609. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 2.70% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Tacoma.
