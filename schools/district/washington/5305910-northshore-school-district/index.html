Northshore School District: public schools

40 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in the Northshore School District district in Washington: 23 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 23,186 students in 40 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 1,917. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.57% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Bothell (21), Woodinville (13), Kenmore (5), Redmond (1).
