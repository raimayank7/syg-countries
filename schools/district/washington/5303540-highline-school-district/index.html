Highline School District: public schools

43 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Highline School District district in Washington: 18 elementary, 15 high, 5 middle, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 17,823 students in 43 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 1,889. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.68% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Burien (14), Seattle (13), SeaTac (7), Des Moines (7).
