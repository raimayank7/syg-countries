Evergreen School District (Clark): public schools

39 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Evergreen School District (Clark) district in Washington: 22 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,053 students in 39 of them; the median school has 444 students and the largest has 1,987. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.53% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Vancouver (37), Camas (2).
