Pasco School District: public schools

29 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Pasco School District district in Washington: 19 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 18,840 students in 29 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 3,163. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.14% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Pasco.
