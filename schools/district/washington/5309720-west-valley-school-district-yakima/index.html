West Valley School District (Yakima): public schools

16 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in the West Valley School District (Yakima) district in Washington: 7 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 5,520 students in 16 of them; the median school has 215 students and the largest has 1,574. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 41st largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.63% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Yakima.
