Seattle School District No. 1: public schools

109 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 109 public schools in the Seattle School District No. 1 district in Washington: 73 elementary, 20 high, 12 middle, 4 other. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 50,770 students in 109 of them; the median school has 361 students and the largest has 1,697. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 4.27% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Seattle.
