Wenatchee School District: public schools

19 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Wenatchee School District district in Washington: 8 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,089 students in 19 of them; the median school has 339 students and the largest has 2,027. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 32nd largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 0.74% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Wenatchee.
