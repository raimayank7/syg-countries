Vancouver School District: public schools

45 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in the Vancouver School District district in Washington: 23 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 5 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 21,949 students in 45 of them; the median school has 437 students and the largest has 1,780. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.76% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Vancouver.
