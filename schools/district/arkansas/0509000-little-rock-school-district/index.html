Little Rock School District: public schools

39 schools

Arkansas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Little Rock School District district in Arkansas: 24 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,105 students in 39 of them; the median school has 387 students and the largest has 2,260. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 285 districts in Arkansas by number of public schools, and holds 3.54% of the state's 1,101 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Little Rock (35), Mabelvale (4).
