District Of Columbia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 117 public schools in the District of Columbia Public Schools district in District Of Columbia: 79 elementary, 20 high, 14 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 50,839 students in 117 of them; the median school has 384 students and the largest has 1,994. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 64 districts in District Of Columbia by number of public schools, and holds 47.76% of the state's 245 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Washington.
