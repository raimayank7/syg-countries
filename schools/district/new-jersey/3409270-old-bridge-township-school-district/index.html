Old Bridge Township School District: public schools

14 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Old Bridge Township School District district in New Jersey: 11 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high. The file reports 7,830 students in 14 of them; the median school has 309 students and the largest has 2,637. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.55% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Old Bridge (10), Matawan (1), Cliffwood Beach (1), Parlin (1).
