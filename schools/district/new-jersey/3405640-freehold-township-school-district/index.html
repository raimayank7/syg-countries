Freehold Township School District: public schools

8 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 8 public schools in the Freehold Township School District district in New Jersey: 5 elementary, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 3,453 students in 8 of them; the median school has 453 students and the largest has 605. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 81st largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Freehold.
