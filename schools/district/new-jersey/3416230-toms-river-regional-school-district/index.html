Toms River Regional School District: public schools

18 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Toms River Regional School District district in New Jersey: 12 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high. The file reports 14,488 students in 18 of them; the median school has 650 students and the largest has 1,962. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.70% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Toms River (14), Beachwood (2), Pine Beach (1), South Toms River (1).
