Township of Union School District: public schools

10 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in the Township of Union School District district in New Jersey: 6 elementary, 3 middle, 1 high. The file reports 7,796 students in 10 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 2,363. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 45th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.39% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Union.
