Elizabeth Public Schools: public schools

37 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Elizabeth Public Schools district in New Jersey: 24 elementary, 8 high, 3 prekindergarten, 2 middle. The file reports 27,563 students in 37 of them; the median school has 723 students and the largest has 1,404. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 1.45% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Elizabeth.
