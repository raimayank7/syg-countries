Woodbridge Township School District: public schools

24 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in the Woodbridge Township School District district in New Jersey: 16 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. The file reports 13,881 students in 24 of them; the median school has 445 students and the largest has 1,654. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.94% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Iselin (6), Colonia (6), Woodbridge (4), Avenel (3).
