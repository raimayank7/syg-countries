Newark Public School District: public schools

64 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 64 public schools in the Newark Public School District district in New Jersey: 44 elementary, 15 high, 2 other, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools. The file reports 39,939 students in 64 of them; the median school has 584 students and the largest has 2,110. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 2.50% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Newark.
