Paterson Public School District: public schools

43 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Paterson Public School District district in New Jersey: 30 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 21,730 students in 43 of them; the median school has 482 students and the largest has 2,087. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 1.68% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Paterson.
