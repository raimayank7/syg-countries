Trenton Public School District: public schools

25 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Trenton Public School District district in New Jersey: 13 elementary, 9 middle, 2 high, 1 secondary. The file reports 13,108 students in 25 of them; the median school has 456 students and the largest has 2,138. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.98% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Trenton.
