East Brunswick Township School District: public schools

11 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the East Brunswick Township School District district in New Jersey: 8 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high. The file reports 8,125 students in 11 of them; the median school has 361 students and the largest has 2,104. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 39th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.43% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in East Brunswick.
