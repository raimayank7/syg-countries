Brick Township Public School District: public schools

12 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Brick Township Public School District district in New Jersey: 6 elementary, 2 prekindergarten, 2 high, 2 middle. The file reports 8,214 students in 12 of them; the median school has 576 students and the largest has 1,297. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 32nd largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.47% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Brick (8), Brick Town (4).
