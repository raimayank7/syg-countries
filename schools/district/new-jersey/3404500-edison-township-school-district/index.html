Edison Township School District: public schools

19 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in the Edison Township School District district in New Jersey: 13 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high. The file reports 16,812 students in 19 of them; the median school has 766 students and the largest has 2,675. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 0.74% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Edison.
