Jersey City Public Schools: public schools

39 schools

New Jersey, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Jersey City Public Schools district in New Jersey: 26 elementary, 8 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 24,426 students in 39 of them; the median school has 626 students and the largest has 1,856. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 679 districts in New Jersey by number of public schools, and holds 1.52% of the state's 2,558 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Jersey City.
