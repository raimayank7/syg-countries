Three Rivers/Josephine County SD: public schools

17 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Three Rivers/Josephine County SD district in Oregon: 9 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. 3 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. The file reports 4,462 students in 17 of them; the median school has 279 students and the largest has 523. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 17th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Grants Pass (9), Cave Junction (4), Applegate (1), Williams (1).
