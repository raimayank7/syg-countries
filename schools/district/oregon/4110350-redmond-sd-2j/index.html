Redmond SD 2J: public schools

13 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Redmond SD 2J district in Oregon: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 6,850 students in 13 of them; the median school has 469 students and the largest has 939. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.00% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Redmond (11), Terrebonne (1), Bend (1).
