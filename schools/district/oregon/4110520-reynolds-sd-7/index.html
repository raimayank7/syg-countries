Reynolds SD 7: public schools

20 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Reynolds SD 7 district in Oregon: 15 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. 4 of them (20.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,552 students in 20 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 2,381. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.54% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Portland (9), Fairview (6), Troutdale (5).
