Lincoln County SD: public schools

18 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in the Lincoln County SD district in Oregon: 6 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 3 other. 3 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 4,971 students in 18 of them; the median school has 269 students and the largest has 684. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.39% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Lincoln City (5), Toledo (4), Newport (4), Waldport (3).
