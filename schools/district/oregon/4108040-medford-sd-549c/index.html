Medford SD 549C: public schools

25 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in the Medford SD 549C district in Oregon: 17 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 4 of them (16.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,712 students in 25 of them; the median school has 414 students and the largest has 1,945. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.93% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Medford (22), Jacksonville (2), Central Point (1).
