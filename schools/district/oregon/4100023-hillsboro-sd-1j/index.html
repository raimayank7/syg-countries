Hillsboro SD 1J: public schools

37 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in the Hillsboro SD 1J district in Oregon: 28 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (2.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 18,432 students in 37 of them; the median school has 382 students and the largest has 1,565. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 2.85% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Hillsboro (28), Aloha (4), North Plains (2), Portland (1).
