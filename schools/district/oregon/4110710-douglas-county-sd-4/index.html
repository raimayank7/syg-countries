Douglas County SD 4: public schools

13 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Douglas County SD 4 district in Oregon: 9 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 5,651 students in 13 of them; the median school has 335 students and the largest has 1,665. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 21st largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.00% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Roseburg (12), Winchester (1).
