Gresham-Barlow SD 10J: public schools

21 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Gresham-Barlow SD 10J district in Oregon: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 1 other. 4 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 11,236 students in 21 of them; the median school has 447 students and the largest has 1,698. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 1.62% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Gresham (20), Damascus (1).
