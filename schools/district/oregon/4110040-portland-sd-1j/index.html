Portland SD 1J: public schools

86 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 86 public schools in the Portland SD 1J district in Oregon: 60 elementary, 15 middle, 10 high, 1 other. 5 of them (5.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 42,518 students in 86 of them; the median school has 373 students and the largest has 2,179. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 6.64% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Portland.
