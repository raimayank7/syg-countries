North Clackamas SD 12: public schools

31 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the North Clackamas SD 12 district in Oregon: 19 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (12.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 16,771 students in 31 of them; the median school has 418 students and the largest has 1,430. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 2.39% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Milwaukie (16), Happy Valley (8), Clackamas (6), Damascus (1).
