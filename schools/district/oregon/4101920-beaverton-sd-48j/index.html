Beaverton SD 48J: public schools

56 schools

Oregon, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Beaverton SD 48J district in Oregon: 36 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (3.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 37,651 students in 56 of them; the median school has 532 students and the largest has 2,413. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 206 districts in Oregon by number of public schools, and holds 4.32% of the state's 1,296 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Beaverton (34), Portland (19), Hillsboro (2), Aloha (1).
