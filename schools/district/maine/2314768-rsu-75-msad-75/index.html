Rsu 75/Msad 75: public schools

7 schools

Maine, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 7 public schools in the Rsu 75/Msad 75 district in Maine: 5 elementary, 1 middle, 1 high. The file reports 2,342 students in 7 of them; the median school has 232 students and the largest has 748. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 213 districts in Maine by number of public schools, and holds 1.18% of the state's 593 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Topsham (4), Bowdoin (1), Bowdoinham (1), Harpswell (1).
