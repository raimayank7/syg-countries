Ames Comm School District: public schools

8 schools

Iowa, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 8 public schools in the Ames Comm School District district in Iowa: 6 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 4,701 students in 8 of them; the median school has 407 students and the largest has 1,427. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 24th largest of the 327 districts in Iowa by number of public schools, and holds 0.61% of the state's 1,318 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Ames.
