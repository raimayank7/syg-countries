Cedar Rapids Comm School District: public schools

34 schools

Iowa, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Cedar Rapids Comm School District district in Iowa: 21 elementary, 7 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 15,357 students in 34 of them; the median school has 343 students and the largest has 1,719. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 327 districts in Iowa by number of public schools, and holds 2.58% of the state's 1,318 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Cedar Rapids (32), Hiawatha (2).
