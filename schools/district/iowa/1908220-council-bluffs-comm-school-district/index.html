Council Bluffs Comm School District: public schools

15 schools

Iowa, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Council Bluffs Comm School District district in Iowa: 10 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 8,499 students in 15 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 1,358. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 327 districts in Iowa by number of public schools, and holds 1.14% of the state's 1,318 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Council Bluffs (14), Carter Lake (1).
