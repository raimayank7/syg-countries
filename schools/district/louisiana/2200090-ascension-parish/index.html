Ascension Parish: public schools

31 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Ascension Parish district in Louisiana: 19 elementary, 8 middle, 4 high. The file reports 24,683 students in 31 of them; the median school has 636 students and the largest has 2,731. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.36% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Prairieville (10), Gonzales (8), Donaldsonville (4), Geismar (4).
