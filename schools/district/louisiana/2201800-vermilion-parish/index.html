Vermilion Parish: public schools

20 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Vermilion Parish district in Louisiana: 11 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle. The file reports 9,587 students in 20 of them; the median school has 505 students and the largest has 849. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 1.52% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Abbeville (8), Erath (3), Kaplan (3), Maurice (3).
