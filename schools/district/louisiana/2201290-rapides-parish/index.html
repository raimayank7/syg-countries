Rapides Parish: public schools

43 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in the Rapides Parish district in Louisiana: 25 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 5 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 22,546 students in 43 of them; the median school has 436 students and the largest has 1,558. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 3.27% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Alexandria (20), Pineville (8), Ball (4), Deville (3).
