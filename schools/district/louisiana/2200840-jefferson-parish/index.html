Jefferson Parish: public schools

75 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 75 public schools in the Jefferson Parish district in Louisiana: 50 elementary, 16 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 7 of them (9.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 50,467 students in 75 of them; the median school has 604 students and the largest has 2,570. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 5.70% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 14 towns, most in Metairie (18), Marrero (11), Gretna (11), Kenner (10).
