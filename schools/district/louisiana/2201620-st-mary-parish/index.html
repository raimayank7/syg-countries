St. Mary Parish: public schools

21 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the St. Mary Parish district in Louisiana: 10 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle, 1 other. The file reports 7,794 students in 21 of them; the median school has 353 students and the largest has 711. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 18th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 1.60% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Morgan City (6), Franklin (4), Berwick (3), Baldwin (3).
