Tangipahoa Parish: public schools

33 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Tangipahoa Parish district in Louisiana: 17 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 20,513 students in 33 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 2,254. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.51% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Ponchatoula (7), Kentwood (6), Hammond (6), Amite (3).
