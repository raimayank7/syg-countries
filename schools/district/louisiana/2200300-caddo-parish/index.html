Caddo Parish: public schools

58 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in the Caddo Parish district in Louisiana: 35 elementary, 13 high, 8 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (5.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 35,044 students in 58 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 1,845. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 4.41% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Shreveport (53), Vivian (2), Belcher (1), Mooringsport (1).
