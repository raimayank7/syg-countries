East Baton Rouge Parish: public schools

83 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 83 public schools in the East Baton Rouge Parish district in Louisiana: 50 elementary, 14 high, 13 middle, 6 other. 14 of them (16.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 42,590 students in 83 of them; the median school has 483 students and the largest has 1,602. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 6.31% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Baton Rouge (79), Baker (2), Pride (2).
