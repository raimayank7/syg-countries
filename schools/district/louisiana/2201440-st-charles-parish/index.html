St. Charles Parish: public schools

15 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the St. Charles Parish district in Louisiana: 9 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high. The file reports 9,293 students in 15 of them; the median school has 529 students and the largest has 1,511. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 23rd largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 1.14% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Destrehan (4), Luling (4), Paradis (2), St. Rose (2).
