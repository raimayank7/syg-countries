Bossier Parish: public schools

34 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the Bossier Parish district in Louisiana: 20 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 23,587 students in 34 of them; the median school has 598 students and the largest has 1,951. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.58% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Bossier City (21), Benton (5), Haughton (5), Plain Dealing (1).
