Beauregard Parish: public schools

12 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the Beauregard Parish district in Louisiana: 5 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 2 other. The file reports 5,959 students in 12 of them; the median school has 447 students and the largest has 796. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 30th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 0.91% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in DeRidder (7), Longville (3), Merryville (1), Singer (1).
