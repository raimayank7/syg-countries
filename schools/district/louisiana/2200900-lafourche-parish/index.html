Lafourche Parish: public schools

30 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in the Lafourche Parish district in Louisiana: 19 elementary, 7 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 2 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 14,005 students in 30 of them; the median school has 391 students and the largest has 1,427. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.28% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Thibodaux (11), Raceland (4), Houma (3), Golden Meadow (3).
