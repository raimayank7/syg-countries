St. Landry Parish: public schools

34 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the St. Landry Parish district in Louisiana: 16 elementary, 9 middle, 9 high. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,980 students in 34 of them; the median school has 292 students and the largest has 863. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 2.58% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Opelousas (10), Eunice (7), Washington (4), Port Barre (3).
