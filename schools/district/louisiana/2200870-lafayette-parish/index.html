Lafayette Parish: public schools

44 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the Lafayette Parish district in Louisiana: 24 elementary, 10 high, 10 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 30,991 students in 44 of them; the median school has 635 students and the largest has 1,891. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 3.34% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Lafayette (27), Duson (4), Scott (4), Youngsville (4).
