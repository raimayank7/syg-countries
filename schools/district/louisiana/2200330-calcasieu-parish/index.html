Calcasieu Parish: public schools

56 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Calcasieu Parish district in Louisiana: 33 elementary, 11 middle, 9 high, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 29,282 students in 56 of them; the median school has 401 students and the largest has 2,012. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 4.26% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Lake Charles (29), Sulphur (12), Westlake (4), DeQuincy (4).
