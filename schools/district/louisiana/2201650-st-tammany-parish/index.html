St. Tammany Parish: public schools

55 schools

Louisiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in the St. Tammany Parish district in Louisiana: 28 elementary, 19 middle, 8 high. The file reports 38,794 students in 55 of them; the median school has 619 students and the largest has 2,188. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 190 districts in Louisiana by number of public schools, and holds 4.18% of the state's 1,316 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Slidell (20), Mandeville (14), Covington (6), Pearl River (5).
