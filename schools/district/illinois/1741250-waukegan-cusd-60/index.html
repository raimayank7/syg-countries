Waukegan CUSD 60: public schools

22 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Waukegan CUSD 60 district in Illinois: 15 elementary, 5 middle, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 13,666 students in 22 of them; the median school has 462 students and the largest has 4,325. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Waukegan.
