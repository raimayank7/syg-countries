Plainfield SD 202: public schools

31 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in the Plainfield SD 202 district in Illinois: 18 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 24,683 students in 31 of them; the median school has 585 students and the largest has 2,379. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.70% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Plainfield (26), Joliet (4), Bolingbrook (1).
