Cusd 308: public schools

22 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in the Cusd 308 district in Illinois: 13 elementary, 5 middle, 2 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 16,851 students in 22 of them; the median school has 594 students and the largest has 2,866. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.50% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Oswego (13), Aurora (4), Montgomery (3), Plainfield (2).
