Addison SD 4: public schools

9 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in the Addison SD 4 district in Illinois: 7 elementary, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 3,442 students in 9 of them; the median school has 265 students and the largest has 1,150. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 82nd largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.20% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Addison.
