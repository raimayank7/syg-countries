Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 3 public schools in the Williamsfield CUSD 210 district in Illinois: 1 middle, 1 high, 1 elementary. The file reports 291 students in 3 of them; the median school has 100 students and the largest has 121. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.
