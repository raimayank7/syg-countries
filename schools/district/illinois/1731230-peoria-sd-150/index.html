Peoria SD 150: public schools

32 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in the Peoria SD 150 district in Illinois: 13 elementary, 9 high, 6 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (3.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 12,713 students in 32 of them; the median school has 350 students and the largest has 1,576. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.73% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Peoria (31), West Peoria (1).
