New Lenox SD 122: public schools

12 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in the New Lenox SD 122 district in Illinois: 6 middle, 6 elementary. The file reports 4,982 students in 12 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 587. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 38th largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.27% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in New Lenox.
