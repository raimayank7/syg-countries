Evanston CCSD 65: public schools

17 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the Evanston CCSD 65 district in Illinois: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,273 students in 17 of them; the median school has 367 students and the largest has 686. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 23rd largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.39% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Evanston (16), Skokie (1).
