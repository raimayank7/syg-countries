Wheeling CCSD 21: public schools

13 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Wheeling CCSD 21 district in Illinois: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 6,205 students in 13 of them; the median school has 565 students and the largest has 694. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 32nd largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 0.30% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Wheeling (7), Buffalo Grove (3), Arlington Heights (2), Mount Prospect (1).
