Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 643 public schools in the Chicago Public Schools Dist 299 district in Illinois: 458 elementary, 167 high, 11 middle, 4 other, 3 prekindergarten. 111 of them (17.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 21 alternative schools. The file reports 322,809 students in 643 of them; the median school has 406 students and the largest has 4,525. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 14.61% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Chicago.
