Sd U-46: public schools

56 schools

Illinois, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Sd U-46 district in Illinois: 38 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 34,553 students in 56 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 2,770. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 960 districts in Illinois by number of public schools, and holds 1.27% of the state's 4,400 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 8 towns, most in Elgin (25), Bartlett (9), Streamwood (9), South Elgin (5).
