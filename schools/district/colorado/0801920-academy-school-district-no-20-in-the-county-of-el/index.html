Academy School District No. 20 in the county of El Paso an: public schools

39 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Academy School District No. 20 in the county of El Paso an district in Colorado: 22 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle. 5 of them (12.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 25,674 students in 39 of them; the median school has 546 students and the largest has 1,913. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 13th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.04% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Colorado Springs (37), Usaf Academy (2).
