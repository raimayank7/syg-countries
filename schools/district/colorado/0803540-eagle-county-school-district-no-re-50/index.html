Eagle County School District No. Re 50: public schools

21 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in the Eagle County School District No. Re 50 district in Colorado: 10 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 6,492 students in 21 of them; the median school has 291 students and the largest has 1,003. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 21st largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.10% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Edwards (7), Gypsum (6), Eagle (4), Avon (2).
