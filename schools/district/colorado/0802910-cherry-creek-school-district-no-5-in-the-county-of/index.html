Cherry Creek School District No. 5 in the county of Arapah: public schools

70 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 70 public schools in the Cherry Creek School District No. 5 in the county of Arapah district in Colorado: 46 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high, 3 prekindergarten. 3 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 51,808 students in 70 of them; the median school has 518 students and the largest has 3,829. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 3.67% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Aurora (40), Centennial (16), Greenwood Village (7), Englewood (3).
