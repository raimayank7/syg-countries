Colorado Springs School District No. 11 in the county of E: public schools

58 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in the Colorado Springs School District No. 11 in the county of E district in Colorado: 37 elementary, 11 high, 9 middle, 1 other. 7 of them (12.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 22,740 students in 58 of them; the median school has 317 students and the largest has 1,861. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 3.04% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Colorado Springs.
