Adams 12 Five Star Schools: public schools

54 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in the Adams 12 Five Star Schools district in Colorado: 34 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (7.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 34,998 students in 54 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 2,158. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 8th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.83% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Thornton (25), Northglenn (13), Broomfield (8), Westminster (4).
