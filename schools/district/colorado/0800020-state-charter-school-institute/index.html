State Charter School Institute: public schools

44 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in the State Charter School Institute district in Colorado: 21 elementary, 11 high, 8 other, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 44 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 19,593 students in 44 of them; the median school has 297 students and the largest has 1,909. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.31% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 24 towns, most in Colorado Springs (7), Aurora (6), Commerce City (3), Fort Collins (3).
