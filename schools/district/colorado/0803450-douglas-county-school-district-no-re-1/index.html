Douglas County School District No. Re 1: public schools

89 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 89 public schools in the Douglas County School District No. Re 1 district in Colorado: 61 elementary, 15 high, 10 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 18 of them (20.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 61,409 students in 89 of them; the median school has 498 students and the largest has 2,579. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 4.66% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 10 towns, most in Highlands Ranch (26), Parker (24), Castle Rock (22), Littleton (5).
