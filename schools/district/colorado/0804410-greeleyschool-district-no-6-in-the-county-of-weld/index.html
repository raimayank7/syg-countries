GreeleySchool District No. 6 in the county of Weld and Sta: public schools

34 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the GreeleySchool District No. 6 in the county of Weld and Sta district in Colorado: 20 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 3 other. 6 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 22,505 students in 34 of them; the median school has 546 students and the largest has 1,987. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.78% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Greeley (28), Evans (6).
