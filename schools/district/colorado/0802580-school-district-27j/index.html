School District 27J: public schools

33 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the School District 27J district in Colorado: 18 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 6 of them (18.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 23,139 students in 33 of them; the median school has 592 students and the largest has 1,805. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 16th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.73% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Brighton (15), Commerce City (7), Henderson (5), Thornton (5).
