Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Harrison School District No. 2 in the county of El Paso an district in Colorado: 16 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 9 of them (32.1%) are charter schools. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.47% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Colorado Springs.
