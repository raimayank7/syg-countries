Boulder Valley School District No. Re2: public schools

56 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in the Boulder Valley School District No. Re2 district in Colorado: 34 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (8.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 28,357 students in 56 of them; the median school has 393 students and the largest has 2,022. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 7th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.94% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 9 towns, most in Boulder (29), Lafayette (8), Broomfield (6), Louisville (6).
