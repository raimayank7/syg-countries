Jefferson County School District No. R-1: public schools

148 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 148 public schools in the Jefferson County School District No. R-1 district in Colorado: 87 elementary, 29 high, 20 middle, 9 other, 3 prekindergarten. 18 of them (12.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 74,251 students in 148 of them; the median school has 402 students and the largest has 1,878. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 2nd largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 7.76% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 15 towns, most in Lakewood (40), Arvada (30), Littleton (25), Golden (15).
