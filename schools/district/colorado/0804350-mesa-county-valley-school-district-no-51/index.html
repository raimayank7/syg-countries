Mesa County Valley School District No. 51: public schools

46 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Mesa County Valley School District No. 51 district in Colorado: 28 elementary, 7 middle, 7 high, 2 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (8.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,078 students in 46 of them; the median school has 375 students and the largest has 1,590. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.41% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Grand Junction (33), Fruita (6), Clifton (3), Palisade (2).
