Aurora Joint District No. 28 of the counties of Adams and A: public schools

58 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in the Aurora Joint District No. 28 of the counties of Adams and A district in Colorado: 38 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 4 prekindergarten, 3 other, 1 secondary. 11 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 38,165 students in 58 of them; the median school has 516 students and the largest has 2,256. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 3.04% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Aurora (57), Denver (1).
