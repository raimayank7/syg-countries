Poudre School District R-1: public schools

52 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in the Poudre School District R-1 district in Colorado: 31 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high, 4 other, 2 secondary. 5 of them (9.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 29,754 students in 52 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 2,056. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 10th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 2.73% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Fort Collins (42), Wellington (3), Timnath (3), Laporte (2).
