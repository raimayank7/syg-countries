Littleton School District No. 6 in the county of Arapahoe: public schools

20 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Littleton School District No. 6 in the county of Arapahoe district in Colorado: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 13,251 students in 20 of them; the median school has 519 students and the largest has 1,781. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 23rd largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.05% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Littleton (12), Centennial (8).
