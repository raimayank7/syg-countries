Pueblo School District No. 60 in the county of Pueblo and: public schools

35 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in the Pueblo School District No. 60 in the county of Pueblo and district in Colorado: 21 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (8.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,567 students in 35 of them; the median school has 321 students and the largest has 1,053. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.83% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Pueblo.
