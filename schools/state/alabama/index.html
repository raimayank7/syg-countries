Public schools in Alabama

1,524 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,524 public schools in Alabama: 703 elementary, 412 high, 268 middle, 110 other, 10 prekindergarten, 9 secondary. 18 of them (1.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 73 career and technical schools, 57 alternative schools, 34 special education schools. The file reports 748,650 students in 1,383 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 7,245. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 152 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 378 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Mobile County (92), Jefferson County (57), Montgomery County (52), Birmingham City (49), Huntsville City (45), Baldwin County (44).
