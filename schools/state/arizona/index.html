Public schools in Arizona

2,427 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,427 public schools in Arizona: 1,218 elementary, 775 high, 222 middle, 158 other, 48 prekindergarten, 4 secondary. 571 of them (23.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 289 career and technical schools, 156 alternative schools, 37 special education schools. The file reports 1,117,053 students in 2,425 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 6,820. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 658 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 201 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Tucson Unified District (4403) (90), Mesa Unified District (4235) (78), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (59), Pima County JTED (89380) (55), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (50), Paradise Valley Unified District (4241) (45).
