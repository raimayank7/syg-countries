Public schools in Washington

2,553 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,553 public schools in Washington: 1,237 elementary, 656 high, 384 middle, 178 other, 92 prekindergarten, 6 secondary. 18 of them (0.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 342 alternative schools, 86 special education schools, 20 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,093,718 students in 2,551 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 3,163. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 331 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 350 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Seattle School District No. 1 (109), Tacoma School District (69), Spokane School District (69), Lake Washington School District (57), Kent School District (46), Federal Way School District (46).
