Public schools in Iowa

1,318 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,318 public schools in Iowa: 674 elementary, 344 high, 249 middle, 34 prekindergarten, 10 other, 7 secondary. 5 of them (0.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 13 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 501,801 students in 1,318 of them; the median school has 314 students and the largest has 2,441. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 327 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 437 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Des Moines Independent Comm School District (59), Cedar Rapids Comm School District (34), Iowa City Comm School District (29), Davenport Comm School District (27), Sioux City Comm School District (21), Waukee Comm School District (18).
