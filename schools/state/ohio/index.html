Public schools in Ohio

3,577 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 3,577 public schools in Ohio: 1,757 elementary, 940 high, 659 middle, 110 prekindergarten, 100 other, 11 secondary. 335 of them (9.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 75 career and technical schools, 14 special education schools. The file reports 1,674,543 students in 3,577 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 13,997. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 1,003 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 664 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Columbus City Schools District (116), Cleveland Municipal (93), Cincinnati Public Schools (66), Toledo City (57), Akron City (48), South-Western City (34).
