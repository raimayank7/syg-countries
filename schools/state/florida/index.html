Public schools in Florida

4,229 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 4,229 public schools in Florida: 2,244 elementary, 940 high, 582 middle, 388 other, 72 prekindergarten, 3 secondary. 733 of them (17.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 372 alternative schools, 153 special education schools, 41 career and technical schools. The file reports 2,871,192 students in 4,229 of them; the median school has 580 students and the largest has 5,880. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 76 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 439 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Miami-Dade (536), Broward (325), Hillsborough (305), Orange (275), Palm Beach (235), Duval (208).
