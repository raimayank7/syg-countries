Public schools in Illinois

4,400 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 4,400 public schools in Illinois: 2,337 elementary, 977 high, 707 middle, 119 prekindergarten, 48 other, 6 secondary. 133 of them (3.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 273 alternative schools, 230 special education schools, 38 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,846,264 students in 4,194 of them; the median school has 347 students and the largest has 4,614. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 960 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 861 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Chicago Public Schools Dist 299 (643), Sd U-46 (56), Rockford SD 205 (45), Springfield SD 186 (35), Indian Prairie CUSD 204 (34), Peoria SD 150 (32).
