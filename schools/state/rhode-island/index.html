Public schools in Rhode Island

312 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 312 public schools in Rhode Island: 172 elementary, 68 high, 59 middle, 9 prekindergarten, 3 other, 1 secondary. 41 of them (13.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 career and technical schools, 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 134,471 students in 305 of them; the median school has 361 students and the largest has 1,698. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 64 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 52 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Providence (39), Cranston (23), Warwick (19), Pawtucket (16), East Providence (12), Woonsocket (10).
