Public schools in Pennsylvania

2,926 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,926 public schools in Pennsylvania: 1,631 elementary, 715 high, 515 middle, 57 other, 5 secondary, 3 prekindergarten. 175 of them (6.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 85 career and technical schools, 7 alternative schools, 4 special education schools. The file reports 1,669,364 students in 2,926 of them; the median school has 461 students and the largest has 23,589. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 752 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 789 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Philadelphia City SD (220), Pittsburgh SD (56), Central Bucks SD (23), Bethlehem Area SD (22), Allentown City SD (21), Reading SD (19).
