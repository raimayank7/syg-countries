Public schools in Michigan

3,485 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 3,485 public schools in Michigan: 1,641 elementary, 986 high, 500 middle, 315 other, 26 ungraded, 11 prekindergarten, 6 secondary. 365 of them (10.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 338 alternative schools, 184 special education schools, 46 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,377,459 students in 3,485 of them; the median school has 348 students and the largest has 3,517. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 881 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 597 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Detroit Public Schools Community District (106), Grand Rapids Public Schools (51), Utica Community Schools (38), Dearborn City School District (37), Ann Arbor Public Schools (32), Kalamazoo Public Schools (31).
