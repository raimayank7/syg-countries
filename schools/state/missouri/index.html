Public schools in Missouri

2,475 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,475 public schools in Missouri: 1,218 elementary, 667 high, 397 middle, 103 prekindergarten, 85 other, 5 secondary. 82 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 62 career and technical schools, 61 alternative schools, 48 special education schools. The file reports 891,248 students in 2,475 of them; the median school has 299 students and the largest has 2,652. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 565 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 569 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: St. Louis City (68), Springfield R-Xii (57), Division Of Youth Service (41), Columbia 93 (36), Mo Schls For The Sev Disabled (35), North Kansas City 74 (34).
