Public schools in South Carolina

1,270 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,270 public schools in South Carolina: 689 elementary, 296 high, 250 middle, 25 other, 7 prekindergarten, 3 secondary. 95 of them (7.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 48 career and technical schools, 15 alternative schools, 8 special education schools. The file reports 793,860 students in 1,270 of them; the median school has 532 students and the largest has 5,955. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 94 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 243 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Greenville 01 (93), Charleston 01 (82), Horry 01 (57), Richland 01 (48), Berkeley 01 (47), Aiken 01 (41).
