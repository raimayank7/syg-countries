Public schools in Colorado

1,908 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,908 public schools in Colorado: 1,028 elementary, 439 high, 283 middle, 93 other, 57 prekindergarten, 8 secondary. 261 of them (13.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 113 alternative schools, 6 career and technical schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 865,600 students in 1,902 of them; the median school has 368 students and the largest has 6,142. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 186 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 270 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: School District No. 1 in the county of Denver and State of C (197), Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (148), Douglas County School District No. Re 1 (89), Cherry Creek School District No. 5 in the county of Arapah (70), Colorado Springs School District No. 11 in the county of E (58), Aurora Joint District No. 28 of the counties of Adams and A (58).
