Public schools in Maine

593 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 593 public schools in Maine: 353 elementary, 114 high, 83 middle, 9 other, 4 prekindergarten. 11 of them (1.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 27 career and technical schools. The file reports 167,702 students in 566 of them; the median school has 245 students and the largest has 1,645. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 213 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 310 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Portland Public Schools (17), Rsu 17/Msad 17 (10), Bangor Public Schools (10), Rsu 09 (8), Rsu 18 (8), Rsu 54/Msad 54 (8).
