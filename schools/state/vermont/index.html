Public schools in Vermont

306 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 306 public schools in Vermont: 206 elementary, 64 high, 28 middle, 8 other. Besides regular schools there are 16 career and technical schools. The file reports 78,828 students in 306 of them; the median school has 203 students and the largest has 1,291. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 110 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 192 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Burlington School District (10), Essex Westford Educational Community Unified Union SD #51 (10), Windham Southeast Unified Union School District #96 (9), Addison Central Unified Union School District #55 (9), Mount Mansfield Unified Union School District #401 (8), Kingdom East Unified Union School District #64 (7).
