Public schools in Minnesota

2,696 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,696 public schools in Minnesota: 1,042 elementary, 1,002 high, 352 middle, 151 other, 141 prekindergarten, 8 secondary. 308 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 474 alternative schools, 318 special education schools, 10 career and technical schools. The file reports 869,967 students in 2,696 of them; the median school has 204 students and the largest has 3,548. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 550 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 467 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Saint Paul Public Schools (102), Minneapolis Public School District (98), Anoka-Hennepin School District (51), Intermediate School District 287 (50), Rochester Public School District (49), Rosemount-Apple Valley-Eagan (40).
