Public schools in South Dakota

712 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 712 public schools in South Dakota: 327 elementary, 194 high, 171 middle, 11 prekindergarten, 9 other. Besides regular schools there are 28 alternative schools, 17 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 141,022 students in 712 of them; the median school has 97 students and the largest has 2,020. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 151 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 206 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Sioux Falls School District 49-5 (44), Rapid City Area School District 51-4 (26), Todd County School District 66-1 (13), Meade School District 46-1 (11), Harrisburg School District 41-2 (11), Huron School District 02-2 (10).
