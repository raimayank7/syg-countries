Public schools in New Mexico

888 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 888 public schools in New Mexico: 455 elementary, 217 high, 162 middle, 32 other, 17 prekindergarten, 5 secondary. 101 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 45 alternative schools, 9 special education schools. The file reports 309,955 students in 884 of them; the median school has 281 students and the largest has 2,625. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 153 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 158 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Albuquerque (174), Las Cruces (39), Gallup (33), Santa Fe (30), Gadsden (28), Roswell (22).
