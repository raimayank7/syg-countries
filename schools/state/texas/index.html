Public schools in Texas

9,217 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9,217 public schools in Texas: 4,767 elementary, 1,943 high, 1,760 middle, 389 other, 199 secondary, 155 prekindergarten, 4 adult education. 1,045 of them (11.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 851 alternative schools, 17 special education schools. The file reports 5,532,518 students in 9,217 of them; the median school has 501 students and the largest has 16,358. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They are run by 1,210 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 1,088 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Houston Isd (274), Dallas Isd (240), Fort Worth Isd (138), Northside Isd (126), Austin Isd (123), Idea Public Schools (123).
