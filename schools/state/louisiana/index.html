Public schools in Louisiana

1,316 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,316 public schools in Louisiana: 711 elementary, 275 high, 221 middle, 106 other, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 147 of them (11.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 31 alternative schools, 13 special education schools, 10 career and technical schools. The file reports 708,190 students in 1,316 of them; the median school has 464 students and the largest has 4,079. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They are run by 190 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 322 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: East Baton Rouge Parish (83), Jefferson Parish (75), Caddo Parish (58), Calcasieu Parish (56), St. Tammany Parish (55), Livingston Parish (44).
