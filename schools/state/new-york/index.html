Public schools in New York

4,814 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 4,814 public schools in New York: 2,567 elementary, 1,186 high, 792 middle, 232 other, 23 secondary, 8 prekindergarten, 6 ungraded. 343 of them (7.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 131 special education schools, 50 career and technical schools, 27 alternative schools. The file reports 2,510,607 students in 4,814 of them; the median school has 426 students and the largest has 5,808. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 1,103 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 885 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: New York City Geographic District # 2 (119), New York City Geographic District #10 (83), New York City Geographic District #31 (74), New York City Geographic District # 9 (69), New York City Geographic District #27 (63), New York City Geographic District #11 (62).
