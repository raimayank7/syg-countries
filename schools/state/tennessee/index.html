Public schools in Tennessee

1,905 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,905 public schools in Tennessee: 1,019 elementary, 418 high, 345 middle, 76 other, 42 prekindergarten, 5 secondary. 115 of them (6.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 23 alternative schools, 15 special education schools, 13 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,003,495 students in 1,905 of them; the median school has 461 students and the largest has 2,998. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 148 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 381 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Memphis-Shelby County Schools (222), Davidson County (162), Knox County (94), Hamilton County (81), Williamson County (52), Sumner County (52).
