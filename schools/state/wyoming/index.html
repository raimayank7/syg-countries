Public schools in Wyoming

358 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 358 public schools in Wyoming: 187 elementary, 88 high, 60 middle, 22 other, 1 prekindergarten. 7 of them (2.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 31 alternative schools, 12 special education schools. The file reports 91,036 students in 357 of them; the median school has 212 students and the largest has 2,073. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 61 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 93 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Laramie County School District #1 (38), Natrona County School District #1 (27), Campbell County School District #1 (23), Albany County School District #1 (17), Sweetwater County School District #1 (15), Goshen County School District #1 (12).
