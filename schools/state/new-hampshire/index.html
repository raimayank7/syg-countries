Public schools in New Hampshire

507 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 507 public schools in New Hampshire: 288 elementary, 102 high, 99 middle, 18 prekindergarten. 43 of them (8.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 165,404 students in 507 of them; the median school has 281 students and the largest has 3,057. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They are run by 197 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 208 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Nashua School District (21), Manchester School District (21), Fall Mountain Regional School District (12), Contoocook Valley School District (11), Rochester School District (10), Salem School District (8).
