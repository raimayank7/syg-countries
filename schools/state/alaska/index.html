Public schools in Alaska

494 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 494 public schools in Alaska: 215 other, 169 elementary, 74 high, 36 middle. 31 of them (6.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 18 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 131,243 students in 494 of them; the median school has 179 students and the largest has 7,486. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 54 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 221 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Anchorage School District (94), Matanuska-Susitna Borough School District (49), Kenai Peninsula Borough School District (42), Fairbanks North Star Borough School District (34), Lower Kuskokwim School District (29), Juneau Borough School District (15).
