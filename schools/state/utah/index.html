Public schools in Utah

1,096 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,096 public schools in Utah: 624 elementary, 198 high, 144 middle, 84 other, 35 prekindergarten, 11 secondary. 140 of them (12.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 59 special education schools, 29 alternative schools, 7 career and technical schools. The file reports 689,883 students in 1,096 of them; the median school has 526 students and the largest has 8,702. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 155 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 190 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Davis District (96), Alpine District (91), Granite District (86), Jordan District (69), Washington District (56), Weber District (52).
