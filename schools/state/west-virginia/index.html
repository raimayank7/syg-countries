Public schools in West Virginia

682 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 682 public schools in West Virginia: 408 elementary, 149 high, 110 middle, 12 other, 2 prekindergarten, 1 adult education. 5 of them (0.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 30 career and technical schools, 14 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 245,837 students in 648 of them; the median school has 305 students and the largest has 1,824. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They are run by 61 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 293 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Kanawha County Schools (66), Berkeley County Schools (32), Wood County Schools (27), Raleigh County Schools (27), Cabell County Schools (26), Mercer County Schools (25).
