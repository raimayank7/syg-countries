Public schools in Indiana

1,917 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,917 public schools in Indiana: 1,060 elementary, 427 high, 333 middle, 62 other, 33 prekindergarten, 2 secondary. 126 of them (6.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 27 career and technical schools, 19 special education schools, 8 alternative schools. The file reports 1,032,536 students in 1,917 of them; the median school has 448 students and the largest has 5,475. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 426 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 407 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Fort Wayne Community Schools (50), Indianapolis Public Schools (48), Evansville Vanderburgh School Corp (39), South Bend Community School Corp (31), Vigo County School Corp (26), Monroe County Community Sch Corp (24).
