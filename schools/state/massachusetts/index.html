Public schools in Massachusetts

1,832 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,832 public schools in Massachusetts: 1,029 elementary, 378 high, 310 middle, 71 prekindergarten, 31 other, 5 ungraded, 3 secondary. 76 of them (4.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 36 career and technical schools, 29 alternative schools, 12 special education schools. The file reports 914,958 students in 1,827 of them; the median school has 416 students and the largest has 3,586. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 400 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 385 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Boston (109), Springfield (66), Worcester (45), Lynn (27), Lowell (27), Lawrence (26).
