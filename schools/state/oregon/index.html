Public schools in Oregon

1,296 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,296 public schools in Oregon: 719 elementary, 274 high, 208 middle, 94 other, 1 prekindergarten. 132 of them (10.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 38 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 541,269 students in 1,296 of them; the median school has 344 students and the largest has 2,810. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 206 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 259 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Portland SD 1J (86), Salem-Keizer SD 24J (65), Beaverton SD 48J (56), Hillsboro SD 1J (37), Eugene SD 4J (36), Bend-LaPine Administrative SD 1 (35).
