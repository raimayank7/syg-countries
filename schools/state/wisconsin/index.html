Public schools in Wisconsin

2,224 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,224 public schools in Wisconsin: 1,106 elementary, 566 high, 393 middle, 79 other, 77 prekindergarten, 3 secondary. 236 of them (10.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 100 alternative schools, 12 special education schools, 8 career and technical schools. The file reports 814,202 students in 2,224 of them; the median school has 299 students and the largest has 2,237. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 454 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 484 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Milwaukee School District (156), Madison Metropolitan School District (54), Green Bay Area Public School District (44), Kenosha School District (41), Appleton Area School District (38), Wisconsin Department of Corrections (33).
