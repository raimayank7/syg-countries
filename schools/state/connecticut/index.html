Public schools in Connecticut

1,010 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,010 public schools in Connecticut: 597 elementary, 210 high, 180 middle, 13 prekindergarten, 10 other. 21 of them (2.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 career and technical schools, 12 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 495,490 students in 1,010 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 3,603. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 199 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 205 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Hartford School District (41), New Haven School District (37), Bridgeport School District (36), Waterbury School District (29), Norwalk School District (22), Stamford School District (21).
