Public schools in Arkansas

1,101 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,101 public schools in Arkansas: 519 elementary, 319 high, 211 middle, 19 secondary, 18 other, 13 prekindergarten, 1 ungraded, 1 adult education. 105 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 30 career and technical schools, 3 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 484,978 students in 1,101 of them; the median school has 385 students and the largest has 3,804. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They are run by 285 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 270 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Little Rock School District (39), Springdale School District (29), Fort Smith School District (27), Pulaski County Special School District (26), Bentonville School District (24), Rogers School District (23).
