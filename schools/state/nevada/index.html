Public schools in Nevada

762 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 762 public schools in Nevada: 441 elementary, 152 high, 118 middle, 24 other, 18 ungraded, 7 prekindergarten, 2 secondary. 104 of them (13.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 53 alternative schools, 14 special education schools. The file reports 479,516 students in 762 of them; the median school has 503 students and the largest has 3,270. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 20 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 75 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Clark County (383), Washoe County (115), State Sponsored Charter Schools (81), Elko (36), Nye (25), Lyon (21).
