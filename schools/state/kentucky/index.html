Public schools in Kentucky

1,544 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,544 public schools in Kentucky: 721 elementary, 446 high, 220 middle, 69 other, 45 ungraded, 41 prekindergarten, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 186 alternative schools, 134 career and technical schools, 9 special education schools. The file reports 656,407 students in 1,544 of them; the median school has 410 students and the largest has 2,373. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 175 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 340 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Jefferson County (171), Fayette County (79), Kentucky Tech System (55), Warren County (34), Boone County (28), Hardin County (27).
