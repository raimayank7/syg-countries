Public schools in Virginia

2,129 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,129 public schools in Virginia: 1,150 elementary, 341 middle, 328 high, 35 prekindergarten, 28 other, 1 secondary. 7 of them (0.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 127 alternative schools, 85 career and technical schools, 41 special education schools. The file reports 1,258,852 students in 1,868 of them; the median school has 555 students and the largest has 4,551. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 208 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 383 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Fairfax County Public Schools (223), Loudoun County Public Schools (100), Prince William County Public Schools (99), Virginia Beach City Public Schools (88), Henrico County Public Schools (81), Chesterfield County Public Schools (73).
