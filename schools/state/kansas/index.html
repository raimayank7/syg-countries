Public schools in Kansas

1,348 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,348 public schools in Kansas: 716 elementary, 370 high, 226 middle, 34 other, 2 ungraded. 8 of them (0.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 479,468 students in 1,348 of them; the median school has 289 students and the largest has 3,618. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 290 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 370 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Wichita (87), Olathe (52), Shawnee Mission Pub Sch (45), Kansas City (43), Blue Valley (37), Topeka Public Schools (27).
