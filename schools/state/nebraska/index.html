Public schools in Nebraska

1,098 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,098 public schools in Nebraska: 537 elementary, 319 high, 145 middle, 76 prekindergarten, 17 other, 4 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 53 alternative schools, 23 special education schools. The file reports 329,162 students in 1,098 of them; the median school has 213 students and the largest has 2,674. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 272 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 283 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Omaha Public Schools (112), Lincoln Public Schools (78), Millard Public Schools (36), Grand Island Public Schools (23), Papillion La Vista Community Schools (22), Bellevue Public Schools (21).
