Public schools in Mississippi

1,032 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,032 public schools in Mississippi: 437 elementary, 317 high, 185 middle, 79 other, 7 prekindergarten, 4 secondary, 3 ungraded. 10 of them (1.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 96 career and technical schools, 67 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 436,523 students in 1,032 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 2,101. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 152 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 239 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Jackson Public School District (52), Desoto Co School Dist (42), Rankin Co School Dist (28), Harrison Co School Dist (24), Madison Co School Dist (23), Pascagoula-Gautier School District (19).
