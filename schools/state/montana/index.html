Public schools in Montana

821 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 821 public schools in Montana: 423 elementary, 225 middle, 173 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 148,897 students in 821 of them; the median school has 87 students and the largest has 2,210. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 397 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 241 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Billings Elem (28), Great Falls Elem (17), Helena Elem (13), Missoula Elem (12), Bozeman Elem (11), Kalispell Elem (7).
