Public schools in Maryland

1,408 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,408 public schools in Maryland: 886 elementary, 250 high, 230 middle, 28 other, 11 prekindergarten, 3 secondary. 49 of them (3.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 39 alternative schools, 35 special education schools, 24 career and technical schools. The file reports 890,122 students in 1,408 of them; the median school has 523 students and the largest has 3,261. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 25 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 258 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Montgomery County Public Schools (211), Prince George's County Public Schools (199), Baltimore County Public Schools (179), Baltimore City Public Schools (151), Anne Arundel County Public Schools (126), Howard County Public Schools (78).
