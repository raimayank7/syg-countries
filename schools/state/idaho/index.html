Public schools in Idaho

801 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 801 public schools in Idaho: 393 elementary, 216 high, 115 middle, 65 other, 9 prekindergarten, 3 secondary. 82 of them (10.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 83 alternative schools, 14 special education schools, 12 career and technical schools. The file reports 316,265 students in 801 of them; the median school has 316 students and the largest has 7,751. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 189 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 179 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Joint School District No. 2 (61), Boise Independent District (52), Nampa School District (26), Bonneville Joint District (26), Pocatello District (24), Idaho Falls District (22).
