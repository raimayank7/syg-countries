Public schools in California

10,326 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10,326 public schools in California: 5,978 elementary, 2,220 high, 1,389 middle, 566 other, 8 secondary, 2 adult education. 1,280 of them (12.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1,091 alternative schools, 153 special education schools, 62 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,823,628 students in 10,164 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 9,069. Grades offered run from kindergarten to adult education.

They are run by 2,048 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 1,010 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Los Angeles Unified (785), San Diego Unified (175), San Francisco Unified (113), Fresno Unified (101), Oakland Unified (82), Long Beach Unified (82).
