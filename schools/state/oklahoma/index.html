Public schools in Oklahoma

1,778 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 1,778 public schools in Oklahoma: 929 elementary, 466 high, 361 middle, 14 prekindergarten, 8 secondary. 67 of them (3.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 698,761 students in 1,778 of them; the median school has 300 students and the largest has 13,702. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 542 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 417 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Tulsa (69), Oklahoma City (58), Moore (34), Edmond (28), Broken Arrow (27), Putnam City (26).
