Public schools in New Jersey

2,558 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,558 public schools in New Jersey: 1,503 elementary, 473 high, 443 middle, 64 prekindergarten, 59 other, 10 secondary. 85 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 66 career and technical schools, 59 alternative schools, 58 special education schools. The file reports 1,357,255 students in 2,545 of them; the median school has 428 students and the largest has 6,476. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They are run by 679 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 572 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Newark Public School District (64), Paterson Public School District (43), Jersey City Public Schools (39), Elizabeth Public Schools (37), Trenton Public School District (25), Woodbridge Township School District (24).
