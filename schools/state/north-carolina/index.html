Public schools in North Carolina

2,716 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 2,716 public schools in North Carolina: 1,484 elementary, 605 high, 479 middle, 139 other, 7 secondary, 2 ungraded. 210 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 75 alternative schools, 25 special education schools, 10 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,544,289 students in 2,715 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 3,605. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They are run by 336 school districts (including charter and state agencies) in 498 towns. The largest districts by number of schools: Wake County Schools (197), Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (184), Guilford County Schools (124), Cumberland County Schools (86), Winston Salem / Forsyth County Schools (81), Gaston County Schools (56).
