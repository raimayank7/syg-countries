Public schools in Paducah, Kentucky

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Paducah, Kentucky: 9 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 1 ungraded, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,995 students in 19 of them; the median school has 426 students and the largest has 1,958. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 3 school districts: McCracken County (11), Paducah Independent (7), Kentucky Tech System (1).
