Public schools in Ashland, Kentucky

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Ashland, Kentucky: 8 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 2 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,253 students in 21 of them; the median school has 247 students and the largest has 928. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 4 school districts: Ashland Independent (9), Boyd County (8), Fairview Independent (2), Raceland-Worthington Independent (2).
