Public schools in Bowling Green, Kentucky

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Bowling Green, Kentucky: 18 elementary, 13 high, 5 middle, 4 ungraded, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools, 5 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,042 students in 42 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 1,523. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 3 school districts: Warren County (31), Bowling Green Independent (10), Kentucky Tech System (1).
