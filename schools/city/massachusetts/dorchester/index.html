Public schools in Dorchester, Massachusetts

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Dorchester, Massachusetts: 18 elementary, 7 high, 3 other, 1 middle. 4 of them (13.8%) are charter schools. The file reports 13,266 students in 29 of them; the median school has 359 students and the largest has 1,693. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Boston (25), Neighborhood House Charter (District) (1), Boston Collegiate Charter (District) (1), Conservatory Lab Charter (District) (1), Codman Academy Charter Public (District) (1).
