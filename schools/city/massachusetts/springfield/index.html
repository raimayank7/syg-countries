Public schools in Springfield, Massachusetts

71 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 71 public schools in Springfield, Massachusetts: 34 elementary, 16 middle, 14 high, 3 prekindergarten, 2 other, 1 secondary, 1 ungraded. 7 of them (9.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,128 students in 71 of them; the median school has 290 students and the largest has 2,042. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 8 school districts: Springfield (64), Springfield International Charter (District) (1), Martin Luther King Jr. Charter School of Excellence (Distric (1), Veritas Preparatory Charter School (District) (1), Baystate Academy Charter Public School (District) (1), Phoenix Academy Public Charter High School Springfield (Dist (1), Springfield Preparatory Charter School (District) (1), Libertas Academy Charter School (District) (1).
