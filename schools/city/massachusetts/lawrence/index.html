Public schools in Lawrence, Massachusetts

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Lawrence, Massachusetts: 16 elementary, 8 middle, 4 high, 1 other. 3 of them (10.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 15,201 students in 29 of them; the median school has 462 students and the largest has 3,246. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Lawrence (26), Community Day Charter Public School (District) (1), Lawrence Family Development Charter (District) (1), Phoenix Academy Public Charter High School Lawrence (Distric (1).
