Public schools in Cambridge, Massachusetts

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Cambridge, Massachusetts: 13 elementary, 4 middle, 2 high, 1 other. 3 of them (15.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,439 students in 20 of them; the median school has 312 students and the largest has 1,979. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Cambridge (17), Benjamin Banneker Charter Public (District) (1), Prospect Hill Academy Charter (District) (1), Community Charter School of Cambridge (District) (1).
