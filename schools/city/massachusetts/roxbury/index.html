Public schools in Roxbury, Massachusetts

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Roxbury, Massachusetts: 9 elementary, 7 high, 1 middle. 4 of them (23.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,272 students in 17 of them; the median school has 340 students and the largest has 1,538. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Boston (13), City on a Hill Charter Public School (District) (1), Boston Day and Evening Academy Charter (District) (1), Roxbury Preparatory Charter (District) (1), Bridge Boston Charter School (District) (1).
