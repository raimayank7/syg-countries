Public schools in Haverhill, Massachusetts

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Haverhill, Massachusetts: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,966 students in 16 of them; the median school has 461 students and the largest has 1,991. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Haverhill (14), Hill View Montessori Charter Public (District) (1), Whittier Regional Vocational Technical (1).
