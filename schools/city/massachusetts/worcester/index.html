Public schools in Worcester, Massachusetts

49 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in Worcester, Massachusetts: 35 elementary, 8 high, 4 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (6.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 26,671 students in 49 of them; the median school has 411 students and the largest has 1,791. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Worcester (45), Ma Academy for Math and Science (1), Learning First Charter Public School (District) (1), Abby Kelley Foster Charter Public (District) (1), Worcester Cultural Academy Charter Public School (1).
