Public schools in New Bedford, Massachusetts

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in New Bedford, Massachusetts: 19 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. 2 of them (7.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 16,178 students in 27 of them; the median school has 301 students and the largest has 2,893. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: New Bedford (24), Global Learning Charter Public (District) (1), Alma del Mar Charter School (District) (1), Greater New Bedford Regional Vocational Technical (1).
