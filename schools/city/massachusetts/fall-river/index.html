Public schools in Fall River, Massachusetts

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Fall River, Massachusetts: 10 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. 2 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,930 students in 21 of them; the median school has 618 students and the largest has 2,435. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Fall River (18), Atlantis Charter (District) (1), Argosy Collegiate Charter School (District) (1), Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical (1).
