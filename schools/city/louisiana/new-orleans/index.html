Public schools in New Orleans, Louisiana

75 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 75 public schools in New Orleans, Louisiana: 42 elementary, 26 high, 5 other, 1 middle, 1 secondary. 73 of them (97.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 49,395 students in 75 of them; the median school has 649 students and the largest has 2,120. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 71 school districts: Orleans Parish (5), International School of Louisiana (1), New Orleans Center for Creative Arts (1), Voices for International Business & Education (1), Lycee Francais de la Nouvelle-Orleans (1), New Orleans Military & Maritime Academy (1), Sophie B Wright Institute of Academic Excellence (1), Success Preparatory Academy (1).
