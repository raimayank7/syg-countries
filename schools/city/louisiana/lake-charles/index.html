Public schools in Lake Charles, Louisiana

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Lake Charles, Louisiana: 23 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle. 3 of them (8.8%) are charter schools. The file reports 19,436 students in 34 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 2,012. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Calcasieu Parish (29), Cameron Parish (2), Lake Charles Charter Academy Foundation Inc. (1), Southwest Louisiana Charter Academy (1), Lake Charles College Prep (1).
