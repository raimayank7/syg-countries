Public schools in Monroe, Louisiana

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Monroe, Louisiana: 20 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 15,226 students in 34 of them; the median school has 398 students and the largest has 1,190. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: City of Monroe School District (19), Ouachita Parish (13), New Vision Learning Academy (1), Office of Juvenile Justice (1).
