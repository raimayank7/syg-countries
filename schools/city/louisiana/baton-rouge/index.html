Public schools in Baton Rouge, Louisiana

101 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 101 public schools in Baton Rouge, Louisiana: 56 elementary, 18 high, 14 middle, 13 other. 28 of them (27.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 57,950 students in 101 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 4,079. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 20 school districts: East Baton Rouge Parish (79), Southern University Lab School (2), Central Community School District (2), Special School District (2), LSU Laboratory School (1), Community School for Apprenticeship Learning Inc. (1), University View Academy Inc. (FRM LA Connections) (1), Dalton Charter School (1).
