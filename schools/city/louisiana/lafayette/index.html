Public schools in Lafayette, Louisiana

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Lafayette, Louisiana: 14 elementary, 10 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,892 students in 30 of them; the median school has 637 students and the largest has 1,862. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Lafayette Parish (27), Lafayette Charter Foundation (1), Willow Charter Academy (1), JCFA Lafayette (1).
