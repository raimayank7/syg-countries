Public schools in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

143 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 143 public schools in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma: 85 elementary, 33 middle, 24 high, 1 secondary. 25 of them (17.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 106,934 students in 143 of them; the median school has 500 students and the largest has 13,702. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 22 school districts: Oklahoma City (55), Putnam City (19), Moore (16), Santa Fe South (Charter) (10), Midwest City-Del City (8), Western Heights (8), Astec Charters (3), Crooked Oak (3).
