Public schools in Bartlesville, Oklahoma

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Bartlesville, Oklahoma: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. 3 of them (23.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 7,557 students in 13 of them; the median school has 507 students and the largest has 1,708. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Bartlesville (9), Oklahoma Connections Academy (Charter) (3), Osage Hills (1).
