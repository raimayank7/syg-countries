Public schools in Norman, Oklahoma

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Norman, Oklahoma: 21 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high. 1 of them (3.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 17,553 students in 30 of them; the median school has 483 students and the largest has 2,369. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Norman (25), Little Axe (3), Le Monde International (Charter) (1), Robin Hill (1).
