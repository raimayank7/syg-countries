Public schools in Yukon, Oklahoma

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Yukon, Oklahoma: 11 elementary, 6 middle, 1 high. The file reports 13,358 students in 18 of them; the median school has 620 students and the largest has 2,914. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Yukon (11), Mustang (5), Piedmont (2).
