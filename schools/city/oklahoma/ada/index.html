Public schools in Ada, Oklahoma

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Ada, Oklahoma: 7 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high. The file reports 5,865 students in 16 of them; the median school has 369 students and the largest has 581. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Ada (6), Byng (5), Latta (3), Vanoss (2).
