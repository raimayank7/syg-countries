Public schools in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma: 22 elementary, 7 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 2 secondary, 1 high. The file reports 25,284 students in 35 of them; the median school has 583 students and the largest has 4,672. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Broken Arrow (27), Union (6), Bixby (2).
