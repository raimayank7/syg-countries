Public schools in Tulsa, Oklahoma

102 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 102 public schools in Tulsa, Oklahoma: 66 elementary, 19 middle, 17 high. 16 of them (15.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 53,985 students in 102 of them; the median school has 411 students and the largest has 3,516. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Tulsa (68), Union (11), Dove Schools Of Tulsa (Charter) (3), Honor Academy(Charter) (3), Berryhill (3), Jenks (3), Schl Arts/Sci (Charter) (2), Kipp (Charter) (2).
