Public schools in Billings, Montana

43 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in Billings, Montana: 30 elementary, 10 middle, 3 high. The file reports 18,538 students in 43 of them; the median school has 299 students and the largest has 2,210. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Billings Elem (28), Billings H S (3), Canyon Creek Elem (2), Elder Grove Elem (2), Elysian Elem (2), Yellowstone Academy Elem (2), Blue Creek Elem (1), Independent Elem (1).
