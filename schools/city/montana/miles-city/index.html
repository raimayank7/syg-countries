Public schools in Miles City, Montana

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Miles City, Montana: 7 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 1,553 students in 11 of them; the median school has 99 students and the largest has 492. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Miles City Elem (5), Dept of Corrections-Youth (2), Custer County H S (1), S Y Elem (1), Kircher Elem (1), Trail Creek Elem (1).
