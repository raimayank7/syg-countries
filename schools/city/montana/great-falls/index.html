Public schools in Great Falls, Montana

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Great Falls, Montana: 17 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 10,018 students in 22 of them; the median school has 391 students and the largest has 1,612. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Great Falls Elem (17), Mont Sch for Deaf Blind (2), Great Falls H S (2), Centerville Elem (1).
