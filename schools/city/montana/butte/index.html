Public schools in Butte, Montana

8 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 8 public schools in Butte, Montana: 6 elementary, 1 middle, 1 high. The file reports 4,221 students in 8 of them; the median school has 411 students and the largest has 1,272. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Butte Elem (7), Butte H S (1).
