Public schools in Kalispell, Montana

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Kalispell, Montana: 13 elementary, 7 middle, 2 high. The file reports 8,608 students in 22 of them; the median school has 275 students and the largest has 1,628. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Kalispell Elem (7), Smith Valley Elem (2), Cayuse Prairie Elem (2), Fair-Mont-Egan Elem (2), Evergreen Elem (2), Helena Flats Elem (2), Flathead H S (2), West Valley Elem (2).
