Public schools in York, Pennsylvania

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in York, Pennsylvania: 26 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,926 students in 39 of them; the median school has 558 students and the largest has 1,800. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: York City SD (9), Central York SD (7), York Suburban SD (6), Dallastown Area SD (5), West York Area SD (5), Crispus Attucks CS (1), Lincoln CS (1), York Academy Regional CS (1).
