Public schools in Lancaster, Pennsylvania

37 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in Lancaster, Pennsylvania: 23 elementary, 9 middle, 5 high. 1 of them (2.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 21,479 students in 37 of them; the median school has 448 students and the largest has 2,642. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Lancaster SD (19), Manheim Township SD (8), Conestoga Valley SD (4), Hempfield SD (3), Penn Manor SD (2), La Academia Partnership CS (1).
