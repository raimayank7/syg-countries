Public schools in Reading, Pennsylvania

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Reading, Pennsylvania: 18 elementary, 9 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 25,584 students in 32 of them; the median school has 589 students and the largest has 4,779. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Reading SD (19), Exeter Township SD (6), Muhlenberg SD (4), Antietam SD (2), Reading Muhlenberg Career and Technology Center (1).
