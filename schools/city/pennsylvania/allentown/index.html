Public schools in Allentown, Pennsylvania

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Allentown, Pennsylvania: 19 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high, 3 other. 5 of them (14.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 27,814 students in 34 of them; the median school has 606 students and the largest has 3,242. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Allentown City SD (21), Parkland SD (5), Salisbury Township SD (3), Roberto Clemente CS (1), Lincoln Leadership Academy CS (1), Arts Academy CS (1), Executive Education Academy CS (1), Arts Academy Elementary CS (1).
