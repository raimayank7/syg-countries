Public schools in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

303 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 303 public schools in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania: 194 elementary, 69 high, 22 other, 18 middle. 84 of them (27.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 178,924 students in 303 of them; the median school has 499 students and the largest has 3,264. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 85 school districts: Philadelphia City SD (219), Community Academy of Philadelphia CS (1), Harambee Institute of Science and Technology CS (1), Youth Build Phila CS (1), Preparatory CS of Mathematics Science Tech and Careers (1), Imhotep Institute CHS (1), Alliance for Progress CS (1), Multicultural Academy CS (1).
