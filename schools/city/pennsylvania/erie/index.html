Public schools in Erie, Pennsylvania

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Erie, Pennsylvania: 22 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle. 3 of them (8.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,307 students in 35 of them; the median school has 536 students and the largest has 2,417. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Erie City SD (15), Millcreek Township SD (7), Harbor Creek SD (3), Wattsburg Area SD (3), Iroquois SD (2), Robert Benjamin Wiley Community CS (1), Perseus House CS of Excellence (1), Montessori Regional CS (1).
