Public schools in Doylestown, Pennsylvania

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Doylestown, Pennsylvania: 6 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 8,247 students in 11 of them; the median school has 781 students and the largest has 1,465. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Central Bucks SD (11).
