Public schools in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

145 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 145 public schools in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania: 87 elementary, 25 high, 24 middle, 7 other, 2 prekindergarten. 19 of them (13.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools. The file reports 68,671 students in 145 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 2,019. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 38 school districts: Pittsburgh SD (56), Mt Lebanon SD (10), Fox Chapel Area SD (6), North Hills SD (6), Upper St. Clair SD (6), Keystone Oaks SD (5), North Allegheny SD (5), Avonworth SD (4).
