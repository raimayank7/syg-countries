Public schools in West Chester, Pennsylvania

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in West Chester, Pennsylvania: 11 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (10.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 16,352 students in 19 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 3,173. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: West Chester Area SD (16), 21st Century Cyber CS (1), Pennsylvania Leadership CS (1), Unionville-Chadds Ford SD (1).
