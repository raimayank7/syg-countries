Public schools in Pacoima, California

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Pacoima, California: 7 elementary, 4 middle, 1 high. 6 of them (50.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 6,174 students in 12 of them; the median school has 481 students and the largest has 890. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (6), Pacoima Charter Elementary District (1), PUC Inspire Charter Academy District (1), Bert Corona Charter District (1), Bert Corona Charter High District (1), Fenton Primary Center District (1), Montague Charter Academy District (1).
