Public schools in Redding, California

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Redding, California: 21 elementary, 10 high, 6 other, 4 middle. 11 of them (26.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,110 students in 41 of them; the median school has 287 students and the largest has 1,284. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 18 school districts: Enterprise Elementary (9), Redding Elementary (9), Shasta Union High (4), Shasta County Office of Education (4), Columbia Elementary (3), Shasta-Trinity ROP (1), California Heritage Youthbuild Academy II District (1), Redding STEM Academy District (1).
