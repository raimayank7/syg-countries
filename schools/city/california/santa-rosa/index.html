Public schools in Santa Rosa, California

65 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 65 public schools in Santa Rosa, California: 43 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle, 7 other. 22 of them (33.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 28,886 students in 64 of them; the median school has 394 students and the largest has 1,636. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 21 school districts: Santa Rosa Elementary (13), Santa Rosa High (12), Rincon Valley Union Elementary (9), Bellevue Union (4), Mark West Union Elementary (4), Roseland (3), Wright Elementary (3), Sonoma County Office of Education (3).
