Public schools in Covina, California

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Covina, California: 8 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 11,326 students in 17 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 1,420. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Covina-Valley Unified (12), Charter Oak Unified (5), Azusa Unified (1).
