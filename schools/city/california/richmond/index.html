Public schools in Richmond, California

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Richmond, California: 21 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle. 11 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 15,843 students in 33 of them; the median school has 407 students and the largest has 1,341. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: West Contra Costa Unified (22), Making Waves Academy District (1), Aspire Richmond Technology Academy District (1), Richmond Charter Elementary-Benito Juarez District (1), Richmond College Preparatory District (1), John Henry High District (1), Leadership Public Schools: Richmond District (1), Voices College-Bound Lang Acad @ W. Contra Costa Co District (1).
