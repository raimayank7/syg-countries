Public schools in Santa Maria, California

37 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in Santa Maria, California: 24 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 2 other, 1 adult education. 2 of them (5.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 30,895 students in 37 of them; the median school has 739 students and the largest has 3,112. Grades offered run from kindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 7 school districts: Santa Maria-Bonita (21), Orcutt Union Elementary (8), Santa Maria Joint Union High (4), Santa Barbara County ROP (1), Family Partnership Charter District (1), Trivium Charter District (1), Blochman Union Elementary (1).
