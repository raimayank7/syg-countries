Public schools in Bakersfield, California

149 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 149 public schools in Bakersfield, California: 94 elementary, 29 middle, 21 high, 4 other. 2 of them (1.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 123,020 students in 148 of them; the median school has 715 students and the largest has 3,024. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 18 school districts: Bakersfield City (45), Panama-Buena Vista Union (25), Kern High (20), Greenfield Union (13), Rosedale Union Elementary (9), Fruitvale Elementary (5), Norris Elementary (5), Beardsley Elementary (4).
