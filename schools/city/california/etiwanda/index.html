Public schools in Etiwanda, California

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Etiwanda, California: 5 elementary, 4 middle, 1 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,384 students in 10 of them; the median school has 796 students and the largest has 3,540. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Etiwanda Elementary (9), Chaffey Joint Union High (1).
