Public schools in La Puente, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in La Puente, California: 19 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 10,957 students in 28 of them; the median school has 336 students and the largest has 1,608. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Hacienda la Puente Unified (13), Rowland Unified (9), Bassett Unified (6).
