Public schools in San Francisco, California

132 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 132 public schools in San Francisco, California: 81 elementary, 27 high, 16 middle, 1 other. 14 of them (10.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 56,362 students in 125 of them; the median school has 352 students and the largest has 3,172. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 17 school districts: San Francisco Unified (113), San Francisco County Office of Education (4), San Francisco County ROP (1), KIPP Bayview Elementary District (1), KIPP San Francisco Bay Academy District (1), Gateway Middle District (1), KIPP San Francisco College Preparatory District (1), Five Keys Charter (SF Sheriff's) District (1).
