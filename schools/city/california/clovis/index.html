Public schools in Clovis, California

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Clovis, California: 20 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 3 other. 3 of them (9.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 26,999 students in 32 of them; the median school has 666 students and the largest has 2,923. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Clovis Unified (30), Clovis Global Academy District (1), Sanger Unified (1).
