Public schools in Concord, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Concord, California: 16 elementary, 8 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (7.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 15,090 students in 28 of them; the median school has 431 students and the largest has 2,390. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Mt. Diablo Unified (25), Rocketship Futuro Academy District (1), Clayton Valley Charter High District (1), Contra Costa County Office of Education (1).
