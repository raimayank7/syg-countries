Public schools in Oceanside, California

38 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in Oceanside, California: 22 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 4 other. 5 of them (13.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,241 students in 38 of them; the median school has 546 students and the largest has 2,526. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Oceanside Unified (23), Vista Unified (8), Bonsall Unified (1), Pacific View Charter District (1), Scholarship Prep - Oceanside District (1), SIATech District (1), Audeo Charter II District (1), Coastal Academy Charter District (1).
