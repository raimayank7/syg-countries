Public schools in Hayward, California

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in Hayward, California: 24 elementary, 11 high, 5 middle, 2 other. 6 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 22,099 students in 42 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 1,892. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Hayward Unified (29), San Lorenzo Unified (4), New Haven Unified (3), Alameda County Office of Education (3), Eden Area ROP (1), Impact Academy of Arts & Technology District (1), Leadership Public Schools - Hayward District (1), Knowledge Enlightens You (KEY) Academy District (1).
