Public schools in Chula Vista, California

70 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 70 public schools in Chula Vista, California: 42 elementary, 14 high, 8 middle, 5 other. 15 of them (21.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 51,882 students in 69 of them; the median school has 553 students and the largest has 2,710. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 18 school districts: Chula Vista Elementary (36), Sweetwater Union High (18), Altus Schools South Bay District (1), Howard Gardner Community Charter District (1), Mueller Charter (Robert L.) District (1), Arroyo Vista Charter District (1), High Tech Middle Chula Vista District (1), MAAC Community Charter District (1).
