Public schools in Westminster, California

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Westminster, California: 13 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 11,860 students in 18 of them; the median school has 447 students and the largest has 2,543. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Westminster (10), Garden Grove Unified (6), Ocean View (2), Huntington Beach Union High (1).
