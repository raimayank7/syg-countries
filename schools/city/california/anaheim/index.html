Public schools in Anaheim, California

71 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 71 public schools in Anaheim, California: 49 elementary, 13 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 5 of them (7.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 51,293 students in 71 of them; the median school has 592 students and the largest has 2,734. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Anaheim Elementary (24), Anaheim Union High (15), Orange Unified (10), Magnolia Elementary (8), Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified (4), Savanna Elementary (3), Centralia Elementary (2), North Orange County ROP-Adult (1).
