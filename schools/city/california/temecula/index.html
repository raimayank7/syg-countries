Public schools in Temecula, California

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Temecula, California: 15 elementary, 6 other, 5 middle, 5 high. 7 of them (22.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 34,241 students in 31 of them; the median school has 736 students and the largest has 7,548. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Temecula Valley Unified (24), Harbor Springs Charter District (1), JCS - Pine Hills District (1), Empire Springs Charter District (1), River Springs Charter District (1), Elite Academic Academy - Lucerne District (1), Temecula International Academy District (1), Elite Academic Academy - Mountain Empire District (1).
