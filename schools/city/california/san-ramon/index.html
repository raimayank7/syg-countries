Public schools in San Ramon, California

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in San Ramon, California: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 17,076 students in 20 of them; the median school has 622 students and the largest has 3,118. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: San Ramon Valley Unified (20).
