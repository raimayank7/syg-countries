Public schools in Fairfield, California

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Fairfield, California: 18 elementary, 10 high, 4 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 21,339 students in 35 of them; the median school has 564 students and the largest has 2,053. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Fairfield-Suisun Unified (27), Travis Unified (6), Solano County Office of Education (3).
