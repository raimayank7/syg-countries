Public schools in Rocklin, California

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Rocklin, California: 15 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (18.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,267 students in 22 of them; the median school has 499 students and the largest has 2,025. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Rocklin Unified (18), Maria Montessori Charter Academy District (1), Rocklin Academy Gateway District (1), Rocklin Academy District (1), Western Sierra Collegiate Academy District (1).
