Public schools in Highland, California

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Highland, California: 10 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. 2 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,877 students in 15 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 1,108. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: San Bernardino City Unified (9), Redlands Unified (4), New Vision Middle District (1), Entrepreneur High District (1).
