Public schools in Chatsworth, California

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Chatsworth, California: 5 elementary, 4 high, 1 middle. 3 of them (30.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 4,901 students in 10 of them; the median school has 435 students and the largest has 1,724. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (9), Our Community Charter District (1).
