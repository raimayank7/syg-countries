Public schools in La Mesa, California

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in La Mesa, California: 10 elementary, 2 other, 2 high, 2 middle. 4 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 12,068 students in 16 of them; the median school has 525 students and the largest has 2,544. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: La Mesa-Spring Valley (9), JCS - Manzanita District (1), Helix High District (1), Sparrow Academy District (1), The Learning Choice Academy - East County District (1), Cajon Valley Union (1), Grossmont Union High (1), Lemon Grove (1).
