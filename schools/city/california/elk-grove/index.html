Public schools in Elk Grove, California

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Elk Grove, California: 24 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 39,276 students in 39 of them; the median school has 844 students and the largest has 2,659. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Elk Grove Unified (37), California Montessori Project - Elk Grove Campus District (1), SAVA - Sacramento ACAD and Vocational Acad - EGUSD DIST (1).
