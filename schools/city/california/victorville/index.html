Public schools in Victorville, California

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Victorville, California: 24 elementary, 10 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 6 of them (14.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 30,473 students in 40 of them; the median school has 726 students and the largest has 2,171. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Victor Elementary (19), Victor Valley Union High (10), Adelanto Elementary (6), Hesperia Unified (1), Mojave River Academy - Route 66 District (1), Mojave River Academy - Oro Grande District (1), Excelsior Charter District (1), Options for Youth-Victor Valley Charter District (1).
