Public schools in Escondido, California

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Escondido, California: 22 elementary, 9 high, 6 middle, 2 other. 8 of them (20.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 28,017 students in 39 of them; the median school has 593 students and the largest has 1,871. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Escondido Union (24), Escondido Union High (5), Community Montessori District (1), Classical Academy District (1), Motivated Youth Academy District (1), Heritage K-8 Charter District (1), Classical Academy High District (1), Dimensions Collaborative District (1).
