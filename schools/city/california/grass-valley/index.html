Public schools in Grass Valley, California

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Grass Valley, California: 11 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle. 7 of them (29.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 7,526 students in 21 of them; the median school has 322 students and the largest has 1,457. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Grass Valley Elementary (5), Nevada Joint Union High (5), Pleasant Ridge Union Elementary (5), Chicago Park Elementary (2), Union Hill Elementary (2), Nevada County Office of Education (2), John Muir Charter District (1), Yuba River Charter District (1).
