Public schools in Saratoga, California

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Saratoga, California: 6 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 5,485 students in 9 of them; the median school has 399 students and the largest has 1,492. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Saratoga Union Elementary (5), Cupertino Union (2), Campbell Union (1), Campbell Union High (1), Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High (1).
