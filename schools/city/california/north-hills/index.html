Public schools in North Hills, California

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in North Hills, California: 11 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. 3 of them (18.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 9,572 students in 16 of them; the median school has 501 students and the largest has 1,944. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (13), Valley Charter Elementary District (1), Valor Academy Middle District (1), Valor Academy High District (1).
