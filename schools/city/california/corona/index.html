Public schools in Corona, California

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Corona, California: 24 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 3 other. 3 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 32,441 students in 39 of them; the median school has 671 students and the largest has 3,517. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Corona-Norco Unified (34), Lake Elsinore Unified (1), Scale Leadership Academy - East District (1), Excelsior Charter School Corona-Norco District (1), Pivot Charter School Riverside District (1), Alvord Unified (1).
