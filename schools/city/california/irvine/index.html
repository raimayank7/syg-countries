Public schools in Irvine, California

53 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in Irvine, California: 35 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 3 other. 4 of them (7.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 45,271 students in 52 of them; the median school has 720 students and the largest has 2,713. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Irvine Unified (45), Tustin Unified (4), Excel Academy Charter District (1), Excel Academy Charter District (1), Irvine International Academy District (1), Irvine Chinese Immersion Academy District (1).
