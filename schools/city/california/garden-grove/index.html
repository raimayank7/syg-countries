Public schools in Garden Grove, California

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Garden Grove, California: 35 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 29,355 students in 51 of them; the median school has 470 students and the largest has 2,198. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Garden Grove Unified (49), Westminster (2), Orange Unified (1).
