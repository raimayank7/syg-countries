Public schools in Petaluma, California

38 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 38 public schools in Petaluma, California: 26 elementary, 6 high, 3 other, 3 middle. 13 of them (34.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,142 students in 38 of them; the median school has 207 students and the largest has 1,631. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 13 school districts: Petaluma Joint Union High (10), Petaluma City Elementary (7), Old Adobe Union (5), Liberty Elementary (3), Cinnabar Elementary (2), Dunham Elementary (2), Laguna Joint Elementary (2), Waugh Elementary (2).
