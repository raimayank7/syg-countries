Public schools in El Cajon, California

44 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in El Cajon, California: 24 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 3 other. 7 of them (15.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 4 special education schools. The file reports 27,842 students in 43 of them; the median school has 572 students and the largest has 2,414. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Cajon Valley Union (25), Grossmont Union High (9), EJE Elementary Academy Charter District (1), EJE Middle Academy District (1), Altus Schools East County District (1), Literacy First Charter District (1), Diego Valley East Public Charter District (1), Kidinnu Academy District (1).
