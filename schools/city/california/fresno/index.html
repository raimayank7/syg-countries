Public schools in Fresno, California

185 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 185 public schools in Fresno, California: 115 elementary, 35 high, 20 middle, 13 other. 20 of them (10.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 16 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 119,604 students in 183 of them; the median school has 580 students and the largest has 2,749. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 31 school districts: Fresno Unified (101), Central Unified (25), Clovis Unified (18), Washington Unified (7), Sanger Unified (4), Fresno County Office of Education (4), West Park Elementary (2), Fresno ROP (1).
