Public schools in Gardena, California

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Gardena, California: 10 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle. 4 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,081 students in 16 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 1,407. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (12), Environmental Charter Middle - Gardena District (1), New Millennium Secondary District (1), Environmental Charter High - Gardena District (1), KIPP Generations Academy District (1).
