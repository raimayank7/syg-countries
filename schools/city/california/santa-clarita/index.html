Public schools in Santa Clarita, California

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Santa Clarita, California: 6 high, 3 elementary, 2 middle. 1 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 6,842 students in 11 of them; the median school has 390 students and the largest has 1,993. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: William S. Hart Union High (6), Sulphur Springs Union (3), Hart ROP (1), Mission View Public District (1).
