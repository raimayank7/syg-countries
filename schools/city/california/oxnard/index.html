Public schools in Oxnard, California

53 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in Oxnard, California: 32 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (1.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 40,510 students in 51 of them; the median school has 583 students and the largest has 2,928. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Oxnard (21), Oxnard Union High (9), Rio Elementary (9), Hueneme Elementary (7), Ocean View (5), Vista Real Charter High District (1), Ventura County Office of Education (1).
