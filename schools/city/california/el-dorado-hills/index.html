Public schools in El Dorado Hills, California

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in El Dorado Hills, California: 8 elementary, 4 other, 2 middle, 1 high. 6 of them (40.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 14,411 students in 15 of them; the median school has 539 students and the largest has 3,099. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Buckeye Union Elementary (6), Rescue Union Elementary (4), Rising Sun Montessori District (1), John Adams Academy - El Dorado Hills District (1), Clarksville Charter District (1), Cottonwood District (1), El Dorado Union High (1).
