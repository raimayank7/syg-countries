Public schools in Huntington Park, California

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Huntington Park, California: 13 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle. 9 of them (36.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,767 students in 25 of them; the median school has 557 students and the largest has 1,431. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.
