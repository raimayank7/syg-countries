Public schools in Vista, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Vista, California: 15 elementary, 6 high, 4 other, 3 middle. 7 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 15,811 students in 28 of them; the median school has 460 students and the largest has 1,962. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Vista Unified (20), Vista Springs Charter District (1), Classical Academy Vista District (1), Guajome Park Academy Charter District (1), Bella Mente Montessori Academy District (1), Dual Language Immersion North County District (1), North County Trade Tech High District (1), Guajome Learning Centers District (1).
