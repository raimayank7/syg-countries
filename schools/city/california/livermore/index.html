Public schools in Livermore, California

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Livermore, California: 11 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,026 students in 19 of them; the median school has 582 students and the largest has 2,234. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Livermore Valley Joint Unified (18), Tri-Valley ROP (1).
