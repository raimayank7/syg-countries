Public schools in Pasadena, California

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Pasadena, California: 12 elementary, 7 high, 1 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,882 students in 21 of them; the median school has 433 students and the largest has 3,385. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Pasadena Unified (18), Learning Works District (1), Alma Fuerte Public District (1), Options For Youth - Duarte Inc District (1).
