Public schools in Orange, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Orange, California: 17 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 3 other. 4 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 13,971 students in 28 of them; the median school has 393 students and the largest has 1,914. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Orange Unified (24), Santiago Middle District (1), Unity Middle College High District (1), Tomorrow's Leadership Collaborative (TLC) Charter District (1), Orange County Classical Academy District (1).
