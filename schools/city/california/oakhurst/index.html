Public schools in Oakhurst, California

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Oakhurst, California: 5 high, 2 elementary, 1 other, 1 middle. 2 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 1,490 students in 9 of them; the median school has 82 students and the largest has 557. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Yosemite Unified (5), Bass Lake Joint Union Elementary (2), Glacier High School Charter District (1), Mountain Home Charter (Alternative) District (1).
