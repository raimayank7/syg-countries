Public schools in Los Angeles, California

571 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 571 public schools in Los Angeles, California: 331 elementary, 146 high, 79 middle, 12 other, 1 adult education, 1 secondary. 172 of them (30.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 57 alternative schools, 6 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 259,254 students in 570 of them; the median school has 400 students and the largest has 2,223. Grades offered run from kindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 169 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (400), Montebello Unified (3), Los Angeles County Office of Education (2), Los Angeles Unified ROCP (1), Crete Academy District (1), Accelerated Charter Elementary District (1), New Heights Charter District (1), Los Angeles Leadership Academy District (1).
