Public schools in Modesto, California

77 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 77 public schools in Modesto, California: 53 elementary, 11 high, 9 middle, 4 other. 10 of them (13.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 51,087 students in 77 of them; the median school has 555 students and the largest has 2,414. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 19 school districts: Modesto City Elementary (26), Sylvan Union Elementary (12), Modesto City High (8), Stanislaus Union Elementary (6), Empire Union Elementary (5), Ceres Unified (3), Hart-Ransom Union Elementary (2), Paradise Elementary (2).
