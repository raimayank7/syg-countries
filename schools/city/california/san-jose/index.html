Public schools in San Jose, California

234 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 234 public schools in San Jose, California: 144 elementary, 47 high, 41 middle, 2 other. 50 of them (21.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 18 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 128,790 students in 234 of them; the median school has 455 students and the largest has 2,749. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 60 school districts: San Jose Unified (41), Alum Rock Union Elementary (22), Oak Grove Elementary (18), East Side Union High (16), Evergreen Elementary (16), Franklin-McKinley Elementary (16), Berryessa Union Elementary (14), Moreland (7).
