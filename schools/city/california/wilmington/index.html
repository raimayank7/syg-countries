Public schools in Wilmington, California

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Wilmington, California: 8 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,866 students in 12 of them; the median school has 491 students and the largest has 2,437. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (11), Scholarship Prep - South Bay District (1).
