Public schools in Paso Robles, California

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Paso Robles, California: 9 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. 1 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 6,936 students in 14 of them; the median school has 398 students and the largest has 2,037. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Paso Robles Joint Unified (13), Almond Acres Charter Academy District (1), San Miguel Joint Union (1).
