Public schools in Madera, California

48 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in Madera, California: 24 elementary, 12 high, 7 other, 5 middle. 6 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 24,878 students in 48 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 2,057. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Madera Unified (28), Golden Valley Unified (10), Madera County Superintendent of Schools (5), Chawanakee Unified (1), Sherman Thomas Charter High District (1), Liberty Charter District (1), Sherman Thomas STEM Academy District (1), Sherman Thomas Charter District (1).
