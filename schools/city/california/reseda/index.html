Public schools in Reseda, California

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Reseda, California: 9 elementary, 8 high, 3 other, 1 middle. 5 of them (23.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 11,767 students in 21 of them; the median school has 360 students and the largest has 2,784. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (18), Magnolia Science Academy District (1), Magnolia Science Academy 5 District (1), James Jordan Middle District (1).
