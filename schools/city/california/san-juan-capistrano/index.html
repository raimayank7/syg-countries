Public schools in San Juan Capistrano, California

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in San Juan Capistrano, California: 7 high, 6 elementary, 1 other, 1 middle. 4 of them (26.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,510 students in 15 of them; the median school has 225 students and the largest has 5,189. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Capistrano Unified (10), College and Career Advantage (1), California Connections Academy Southern California District (1), Opportunities for Learning - Capistrano District (1), OCASA College Prep District (1), California Republic Leadership Academy Capistrano District (1).
