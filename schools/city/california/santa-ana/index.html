Public schools in Santa Ana, California

81 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in Santa Ana, California: 51 elementary, 16 high, 9 middle, 3 other, 1 secondary. 15 of them (18.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 55,360 students in 80 of them; the median school has 467 students and the largest has 2,853. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 18 school districts: Santa Ana Unified (52), Tustin Unified (7), Garden Grove Unified (6), Orange Unified (2), Magnolia Science Academy Santa Ana District (1), Nova Academy Early College High District (1), Scholarship Prep - Orange County District (1), El Sol Santa Ana Science and Arts Academy District (1).
