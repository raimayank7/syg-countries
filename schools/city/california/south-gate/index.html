Public schools in South Gate, California

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in South Gate, California: 17 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle. 4 of them (14.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 16,957 students in 27 of them; the median school has 443 students and the largest has 2,092. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (22), KIPP Corazon Academy District (1), Valiente College Preparatory Charter District (1), Aspire Firestone Academy Charter District (1), Aspire Gateway Academy Charter District (1), Paramount Unified (1).
