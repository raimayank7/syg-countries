Public schools in Roseville, California

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Roseville, California: 23 elementary, 8 middle, 6 high, 2 other. 2 of them (5.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 26,778 students in 39 of them; the median school has 552 students and the largest has 1,902. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Roseville City Elementary (21), Roseville Joint Union High (6), Dry Creek Joint Elementary (5), Eureka Union (3), Center Joint Unified (2), John Adams Academy - Roseville District (1), New Pacific School - Roseville District (1).
