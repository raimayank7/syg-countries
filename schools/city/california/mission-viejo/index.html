Public schools in Mission Viejo, California

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Mission Viejo, California: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 14,461 students in 23 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 2,507. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Saddleback Valley Unified (14), Capistrano Unified (8), Oxford Preparatory Academy - South Orange County District (1).
