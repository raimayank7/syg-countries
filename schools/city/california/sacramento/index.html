Public schools in Sacramento, California

202 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 202 public schools in Sacramento, California: 123 elementary, 46 high, 20 middle, 11 other. 36 of them (17.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 16 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 130,569 students in 200 of them; the median school has 490 students and the largest has 9,069. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 32 school districts: Sacramento City Unified (73), Elk Grove Unified (26), Twin Rivers Unified (25), San Juan Unified (22), Natomas Unified (20), Robla Elementary (6), Sacramento County Office of Education (5), Sacramento County ROP (1).
