Public schools in Lincoln, California

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Lincoln, California: 7 elementary, 3 other, 3 high, 2 middle. 2 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 10,810 students in 15 of them; the median school has 547 students and the largest has 1,572. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Western Placer Unified (13), Horizon Charter District (1), John Adams Academy - Lincoln District (1).
