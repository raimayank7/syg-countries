Public schools in Huntington Beach, California

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Huntington Beach, California: 20 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high. 2 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,194 students in 32 of them; the median school has 509 students and the largest has 2,734. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Ocean View (11), Huntington Beach City Elementary (8), Huntington Beach Union High (5), Fountain Valley Elementary (3), Westminster (3), Kinetic Academy District (1), Sycamore Creek Community Charter District (1).
