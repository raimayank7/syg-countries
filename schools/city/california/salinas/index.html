Public schools in Salinas, California

65 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 65 public schools in Salinas, California: 39 elementary, 12 high, 8 middle, 4 other. 3 of them (4.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 38,207 students in 63 of them; the median school has 536 students and the largest has 2,791. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Salinas City Elementary (15), Alisal Union (13), Salinas Union High (12), Santa Rita Union Elementary (7), Monterey County Office of Education (5), North Monterey County Unified (4), Washington Union Elementary (4), Mission Trails ROP (1).
