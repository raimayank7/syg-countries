Public schools in Daly City, California

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Daly City, California: 14 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle. 1 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,127 students in 20 of them; the median school has 321 students and the largest has 1,256. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Jefferson Elementary (12), Jefferson Union High (3), South San Francisco Unified (2), Summit Public School: Shasta District (1), Bayshore Elementary (1), Brisbane Elementary (1).
