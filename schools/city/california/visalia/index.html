Public schools in Visalia, California

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Visalia, California: 31 elementary, 12 high, 5 middle, 4 other. 11 of them (21.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 32,812 students in 52 of them; the median school has 567 students and the largest has 2,468. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Visalia Unified (40), Tulare County Office of Education (5), Sycamore Valley Academy District (1), Crescent Valley Public Charter II District (1), Valley Life Charter District (1), Eleanor Roosevelt Community Learning Center District (1), Blue Oak Academy District (1), Outside Creek Elementary (1).
