Public schools in Corning, California

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Corning, California: 7 elementary, 3 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 3,377 students in 11 of them; the median school has 263 students and the largest has 956. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Corning Union Elementary (6), Corning Union High (3), Kirkwood Elementary (1), Richfield Elementary (1).
