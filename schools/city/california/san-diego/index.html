Public schools in San Diego, California

285 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 285 public schools in San Diego, California: 177 elementary, 51 high, 40 middle, 16 other. 49 of them (17.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 164,818 students in 284 of them; the median school has 438 students and the largest has 2,644. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 59 school districts: San Diego Unified (170), Poway Unified (28), Del Mar Union Elementary (7), Sweetwater Union High (7), South Bay Union (6), Chula Vista Elementary (4), San Dieguito Union High (4), Solana Beach Elementary (4).
