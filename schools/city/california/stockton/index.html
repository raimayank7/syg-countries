Public schools in Stockton, California

126 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 126 public schools in Stockton, California: 83 elementary, 24 high, 9 other, 8 middle. 28 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 74,458 students in 124 of them; the median school has 504 students and the largest has 2,891. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 30 school districts: Stockton Unified (54), Lodi Unified (22), Lincoln Unified (14), Manteca Unified (6), Linden Unified (3), San Joaquin County Office of Education (3), San Joaquin County ROP (1), ABLE Charter District (1).
