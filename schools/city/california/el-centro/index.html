Public schools in El Centro, California

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in El Centro, California: 13 elementary, 6 high, 3 other, 2 middle, 1 secondary. 3 of them (11.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,948 students in 25 of them; the median school has 398 students and the largest has 1,955. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: El Centro Elementary (13), Central Union High (5), Imperial County Office of Education (4), Imperial Valley ROP (1), Ballington Academy for the Arts and Sciences District (1), McCabe Union Elementary (1), Meadows Union (1).
