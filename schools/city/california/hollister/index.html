Public schools in Hollister, California

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Hollister, California: 11 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 1 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 10,902 students in 19 of them; the median school has 534 students and the largest has 3,448. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Hollister (10), San Benito County Office of Education (4), San Benito High (2), Hollister Prep District (1), Cienega Union Elementary (1), North County Joint Union Elementary (1), Southside Elementary (1).
