Public schools in Camarillo, California

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Camarillo, California: 10 elementary, 6 high, 5 other, 2 middle. 4 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,428 students in 23 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 1,932. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Pleasant Valley (12), Ventura County Office of Education (4), Oxnard Union High (3), Ventura County ROP (1), UNIV Preparation Charter SCH @ CSU Channel Islands District (1), Camarillo Academy of Progressive Education District (1), Architecture Construction & Engr Charter High (ACE) DIST (1), Peak Prep Pleasant Valley District (1).
