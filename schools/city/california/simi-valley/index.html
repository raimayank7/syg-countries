Public schools in Simi Valley, California

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Simi Valley, California: 19 elementary, 13 other, 5 high, 3 middle. 13 of them (32.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 36,831 students in 40 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 5,700. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Simi Valley Unified (27), Insight @ San Diego District (1), California Virtual Academy at Fresno District (1), California Virtual Academy @ Los Angeles District (1), Insight School of California District (1), California Virtual Academy San Mateo District (1), California Virtual Academy @ San Diego District (1), California Virtual Academy at Sutter District (1).
