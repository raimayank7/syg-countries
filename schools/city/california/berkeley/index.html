Public schools in Berkeley, California

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Berkeley, California: 12 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 9,040 students in 17 of them; the median school has 386 students and the largest has 3,220. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Berkeley Unified (18).
