Public schools in San Pedro, California

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in San Pedro, California: 11 elementary, 5 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (5.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 9,436 students in 17 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 2,481. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (16), Port of Los Angeles High District (1).
