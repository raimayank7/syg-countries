Public schools in Oroville, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Oroville, California: 13 elementary, 5 other, 5 high, 4 middle. 4 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 7,635 students in 27 of them; the median school has 258 students and the largest has 1,299. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Oroville City Elementary (7), Thermalito Union Elementary (6), Butte County Office of Education (5), Oroville Union High (4), Palermo Union Elementary (3), Ipakanni Early College Charter District (1), STREAM Charter District (1), Golden Feather Union Elementary (1).
