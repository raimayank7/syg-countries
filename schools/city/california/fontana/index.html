Public schools in Fontana, California

58 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in Fontana, California: 40 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (6.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 42,757 students in 58 of them; the median school has 573 students and the largest has 2,626. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Fontana Unified (44), Etiwanda Elementary (6), Colton Joint Unified (3), Options for Youth-Acton District (1), Mojave River Academy - Gold Canyon District (1), Entrepreneur High Fontana District (1), Rialto Unified (1), Allegiance STEAM Academy - Thrive Fontana District (1).
