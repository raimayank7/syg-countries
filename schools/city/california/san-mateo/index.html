Public schools in San Mateo, California

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in San Mateo, California: 14 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 12,066 students in 22 of them; the median school has 383 students and the largest has 1,686. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: San Mateo-Foster City (16), San Mateo Union High (3), San Mateo County Office of Education (3).
