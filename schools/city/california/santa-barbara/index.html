Public schools in Santa Barbara, California

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Santa Barbara, California: 20 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 3 other. 4 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 13,751 students in 34 of them; the median school has 313 students and the largest has 2,046. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Santa Barbara Unified (18), Goleta Union Elementary (4), Hope Elementary (3), Santa Barbara County Office of Education (3), Santa Barbara County ROP (1), Peabody Charter District (1), Trivium Charter School Voyage District (1), Adelante Charter District (1).
