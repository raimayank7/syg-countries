Public schools in Porterville, California

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Porterville, California: 19 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 3 other. 4 of them (11.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 21,046 students in 34 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 2,426. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Porterville Unified (20), Burton Elementary (7), Pleasant View Elementary (2), Alta Vista Elementary (1), Monarch River Academy District (1), Hope Elementary (1), Rockford Elementary (1), Woodville Union Elementary (1).
