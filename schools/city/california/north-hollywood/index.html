Public schools in North Hollywood, California

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in North Hollywood, California: 18 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 2 of them (7.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 15,191 students in 27 of them; the median school has 430 students and the largest has 2,482. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Los Angeles Unified (25), ISANA Palmati Academy District (1), New Horizons Charter Academy District (1).
