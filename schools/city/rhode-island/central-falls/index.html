Public schools in Central Falls, Rhode Island

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Central Falls, Rhode Island: 5 elementary, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 high. 3 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 3,686 students in 9 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 805. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Central Falls (6), Learning Community (1), Segue Institute for Learning (1), Blackstone Valley Prep A RI Mayoral Academy (1).
