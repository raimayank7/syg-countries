Public schools in Buffalo, New York

88 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 88 public schools in Buffalo, New York: 55 elementary, 24 high, 5 other, 3 middle, 1 ungraded. 21 of them (23.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools, 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 44,928 students in 88 of them; the median school has 454 students and the largest has 2,312. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 23 school districts: Buffalo City School District (61), Kenmore-Tonawanda Union Free School District (6), Charter School For Applied Technologies (1), King Center Charter School (1), South Buffalo Charter School (1), Tapestry Charter School (1), Buffalo United Charter School (1), Enterprise Charter School (1).
