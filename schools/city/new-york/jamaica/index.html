Public schools in Jamaica, New York

49 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in Jamaica, New York: 25 elementary, 18 high, 6 middle. 5 of them (10.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 29,536 students in 49 of them; the median school has 521 students and the largest has 2,333. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: New York City Geographic District #28 (32), New York City Geographic District #29 (7), New York City Geographic District #27 (3), New York City Geographic District #26 (1), Nyc Special Schools - District 75 (1), Rochdale Early Advantage Charter School (1), New Visions Chtr Hs-Adv Ma/Sci Iv (1), Success Academy Charter School-South Jamaica (1).
