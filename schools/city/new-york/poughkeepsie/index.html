Public schools in Poughkeepsie, New York

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Poughkeepsie, New York: 16 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high, 1 other. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,879 students in 21 of them; the median school has 382 students and the largest has 1,161. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Poughkeepsie City School District (7), Arlington Central School District (4), Spackenkill Union Free School District (4), Wappingers Central School District (3), Hyde Park Central School District (1), Dutchess Boces (1), Little Water Preparatory Charter School (1).
