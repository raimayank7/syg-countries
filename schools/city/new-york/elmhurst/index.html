Public schools in Elmhurst, New York

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Elmhurst, New York: 7 high, 6 elementary, 2 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 12,915 students in 16 of them; the median school has 695 students and the largest has 1,892. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: New York City Geographic District #24 (11), Nyc Special Schools - District 75 (1), Central Queens Academy Charter School (1), Forte Preparatory Academy Charter School (1), Elm Community Charter School (1), Renaissance Charter School 2 (The) (1).
