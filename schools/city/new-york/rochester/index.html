Public schools in Rochester, New York

111 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 111 public schools in Rochester, New York: 71 elementary, 21 high, 14 middle, 5 other. 17 of them (15.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 55,808 students in 111 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 1,313. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 27 school districts: Rochester City School District (46), Greece Central School District (17), West Irondequoit Central School District (10), East Irondequoit Central School District (6), Gates Chili Central School District (6), Brighton Central School District (4), Rush-Henrietta Central School District (2), Eugenio Maria De Hostos Charter School (1).
