Public schools in Schenectady, New York

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Schenectady, New York: 21 elementary, 7 middle, 4 high. 1 of them (3.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 18,587 students in 32 of them; the median school has 448 students and the largest has 2,860. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Schenectady City School District (15), Niskayuna Central School District (7), Rotterdam-Mohonasen Central School District (4), Schalmont Central School District (3), Guilderland Central School District (2), Destine Preparatory Charter School (1).
