Public schools in Huntington, New York

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Huntington, New York: 6 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high. The file reports 4,750 students in 9 of them; the median school has 418 students and the largest has 1,422. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Huntington Union Free School District (6), Cold Spring Harbor Central School District (1), Elwood Union Free School District (1), South Huntington Union Free School District (1).
