Public schools in Long Island City, New York

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Long Island City, New York: 18 high, 15 elementary, 6 middle, 1 secondary. 2 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools. The file reports 29,478 students in 40 of them; the median school has 581 students and the largest has 1,959. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: New York City Geographic District #30 (28), New York City Geographic District #24 (10), Voice Charter School Of New York (1), Growing Up Green Charter School (1).
