Public schools in Staten Island, New York

87 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 87 public schools in Staten Island, New York: 55 elementary, 13 high, 13 middle, 4 other, 2 secondary. 8 of them (9.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 61,997 students in 87 of them; the median school has 537 students and the largest has 3,797. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: New York City Geographic District #31 (74), Nyc Special Schools - District 75 (4), New York State Office Mental Health (Omh) (1), John W Lavelle Preparatory Charter School (1), New World Preparatory Charter School (1), New Ventures Charter School (1), Early College Charter School (1), Bridge Preparatory Charter School (1).
