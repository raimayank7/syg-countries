Public schools in Albany, New York

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Albany, New York: 22 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle, 1 ungraded, 1 other. 7 of them (20.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 17,524 students in 34 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 2,768. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 12 school districts: Albany City School District (16), South Colonie Central School District (8), Brighter Choice Charter School For Boys (1), Brighter Choice Charter School For Girls (1), New York State Department Of Corrections (1), Kipp Tech Valley Charter School (1), Kipp Albany Community Charter School (1), Green Tech High Charter School (1).
