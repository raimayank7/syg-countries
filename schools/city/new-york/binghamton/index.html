Public schools in Binghamton, New York

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Binghamton, New York: 11 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 secondary, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 8,949 students in 20 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,290. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Binghamton City School District (10), Chenango Valley Central School District (4), Chenango Forks Central School District (3), New York State Office Mental Health (Omh) (1), Susquehanna Valley Central School District (1), Broome-Delaware-Tioga Boces (1).
