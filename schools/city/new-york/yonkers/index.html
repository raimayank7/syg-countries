Public schools in Yonkers, New York

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Yonkers, New York: 29 elementary, 8 high, 2 other, 2 middle. 1 of them (2.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 24,537 students in 41 of them; the median school has 546 students and the largest has 1,240. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Yonkers City School District (39), Charter School Of Educational Excellence (1), Greenburgh-North Castle Union Free School District (1).
