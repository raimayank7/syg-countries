Public schools in Flushing, New York

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in Flushing, New York: 26 elementary, 12 high, 7 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools. The file reports 33,609 students in 45 of them; the median school has 611 students and the largest has 2,741. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: New York City Geographic District #25 (35), New York City Geographic District #26 (5), Nyc Special Schools - District 75 (3), New York City Geographic District #24 (1), New York City Geographic District #30 (1).
