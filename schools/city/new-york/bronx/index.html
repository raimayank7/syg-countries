Public schools in Bronx, New York

462 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 462 public schools in Bronx, New York: 222 elementary, 142 high, 76 middle, 16 other, 5 secondary, 1 ungraded. 94 of them (20.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 special education schools, 7 career and technical schools. The file reports 201,266 students in 462 of them; the median school has 389 students and the largest has 2,979. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 101 school districts: New York City Geographic District #10 (83), New York City Geographic District # 9 (69), New York City Geographic District #11 (62), New York City Geographic District # 8 (52), New York City Geographic District #12 (47), New York City Geographic District # 7 (41), Nyc Special Schools - District 75 (14), Bronx Preparatory Charter School (1).
