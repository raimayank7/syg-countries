Public schools in Davenport, Iowa

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Davenport, Iowa: 12 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,166 students in 23 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 1,442. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Davenport Comm School District (23).
