Public schools in Fort Dodge, Iowa

7 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 7 public schools in Fort Dodge, Iowa: 5 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. The file reports 3,470 students in 7 of them; the median school has 382 students and the largest has 1,057. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Fort Dodge Comm School District (7).
