Public schools in Des Moines, Iowa

65 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 65 public schools in Des Moines, Iowa: 42 elementary, 11 middle, 7 high, 4 prekindergarten, 1 other. 1 of them (1.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 32,261 students in 65 of them; the median school has 403 students and the largest has 2,441. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Des Moines Independent Comm School District (59), Saydel Comm School District (3), Southeast Polk Comm School District (1), West Des Moines Comm School District (1), Horizon Science Academy (1).
