Public schools in Waterloo, Iowa

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Waterloo, Iowa: 10 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,659 students in 16 of them; the median school has 473 students and the largest has 1,713. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Waterloo Comm School District (16).
