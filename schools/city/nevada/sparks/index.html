Public schools in Sparks, Nevada

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Sparks, Nevada: 25 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle. 2 of them (5.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 22,717 students in 35 of them; the median school has 460 students and the largest has 2,119. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Washoe County (32), State Sponsored Charter Schools (2), Storey (1).
