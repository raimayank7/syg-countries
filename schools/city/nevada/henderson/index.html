Public schools in Henderson, Nevada

56 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in Henderson, Nevada: 34 elementary, 9 high, 8 middle, 4 other, 1 secondary. 15 of them (26.8%) are charter schools. The file reports 53,475 students in 56 of them; the median school has 646 students and the largest has 3,270. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Clark County (41), State Sponsored Charter Schools (15).
