Public schools in Reno, Nevada

82 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in Reno, Nevada: 50 elementary, 16 high, 11 middle, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten. 16 of them (19.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 43,249 students in 82 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 2,302. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Washoe County (76), State Sponsored Charter Schools (5), Davidson Academy (1).
