Public schools in Oshkosh, Wisconsin

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Oshkosh, Wisconsin: 13 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,114 students in 22 of them; the median school has 317 students and the largest has 1,669. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Oshkosh Area School District (21), Wisconsin Department of Corrections (1).
