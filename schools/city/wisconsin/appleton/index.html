Public schools in Appleton, Wisconsin

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Appleton, Wisconsin: 21 elementary, 9 high, 6 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 12 of them (30.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 16,272 students in 40 of them; the median school has 345 students and the largest has 1,612. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Appleton Area School District (37), Kimberly Area School District (3).
