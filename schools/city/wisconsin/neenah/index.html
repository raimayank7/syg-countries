Public schools in Neenah, Wisconsin

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Neenah, Wisconsin: 9 elementary, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 middle. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 6,497 students in 12 of them; the median school has 294 students and the largest has 1,944. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Neenah Joint School District (12).
