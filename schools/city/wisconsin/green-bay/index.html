Public schools in Green Bay, Wisconsin

60 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 60 public schools in Green Bay, Wisconsin: 37 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 3 other. 3 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 27,595 students in 60 of them; the median school has 325 students and the largest has 2,147. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Green Bay Area Public School District (44), Howard-Suamico School District (8), Ashwaubenon School District (5), Wisconsin Department of Corrections (1), New Leaf Prep Academy Inc (1), Pulaski Community School District (1).
