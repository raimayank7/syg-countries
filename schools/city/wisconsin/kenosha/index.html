Public schools in Kenosha, Wisconsin

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Kenosha, Wisconsin: 23 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other, 1 secondary. 5 of them (12.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,356 students in 39 of them; the median school has 309 students and the largest has 2,001. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Kenosha School District (37), Paris J1 School District (1), Kenosha Schools of Technology Enhanced Curriculum Inc (1).
