Public schools in De Pere, Wisconsin

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in De Pere, Wisconsin: 5 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,177 students in 13 of them; the median school has 639 students and the largest has 1,473. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: De Pere School District (6), West De Pere School District (6), Brown County CDEB (1).
