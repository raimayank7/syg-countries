Public schools in Caguas, Puerto Rico

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Caguas, Puerto Rico: 20 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,685 students in 33 of them; the median school has 191 students and the largest has 740. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (33).
