Public schools in Ponce, Puerto Rico

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Ponce, Puerto Rico: 21 elementary, 8 middle, 8 high, 2 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,355 students in 40 of them; the median school has 207 students and the largest has 1,245. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (40).
