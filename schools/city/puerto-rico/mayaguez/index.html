Public schools in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico: 9 elementary, 3 other, 3 high, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,394 students in 16 of them; the median school has 231 students and the largest has 941. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (16).
