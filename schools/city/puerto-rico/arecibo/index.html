Public schools in Arecibo, Puerto Rico

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Arecibo, Puerto Rico: 9 elementary, 6 other, 5 high, 2 ungraded, 1 middle. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 6,971 students in 23 of them; the median school has 257 students and the largest has 1,156. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (23).
