Public schools in San Juan, Puerto Rico

87 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 87 public schools in San Juan, Puerto Rico: 43 elementary, 24 high, 14 other, 6 middle. 6 of them (6.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 21,243 students in 87 of them; the median school has 208 students and the largest has 845. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (87).
