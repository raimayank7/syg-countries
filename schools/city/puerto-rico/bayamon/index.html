Public schools in Bayamon, Puerto Rico

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Bayamon, Puerto Rico: 23 elementary, 11 high, 4 other, 4 middle. 1 of them (2.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,529 students in 42 of them; the median school has 262 students and the largest has 779. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Puerto Rico Department Of Education (42).
