Public schools in Charleston, West Virginia

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Charleston, West Virginia: 18 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 2 other. 1 of them (3.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,301 students in 28 of them; the median school has 320 students and the largest has 1,186. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Kanawha County Schools (28), West Virginia Virtual Academy (1).
