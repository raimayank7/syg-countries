Public schools in Milford, Connecticut

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Milford, Connecticut: 8 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,983 students in 14 of them; the median school has 363 students and the largest has 829. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Milford School District (13), Connecticut Technical Education and Career System (1).
