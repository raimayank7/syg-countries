Public schools in West Hartford, Connecticut

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in West Hartford, Connecticut: 12 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high. The file reports 9,485 students in 17 of them; the median school has 375 students and the largest has 1,402. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: West Hartford School District (16), Capitol Region Education Council (1).
