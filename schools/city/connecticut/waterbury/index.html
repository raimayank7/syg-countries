Public schools in Waterbury, Connecticut

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Waterbury, Connecticut: 22 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle. 1 of them (3.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 18,974 students in 33 of them; the median school has 469 students and the largest has 1,452. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Waterbury School District (29), Connecticut Technical Education and Career System (1), Area Cooperative Educational Services (1), Brass City Charter School District (1), Judicial Centers (1).
