Public schools in Hartford, Connecticut

47 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in Hartford, Connecticut: 23 elementary, 13 high, 6 middle, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten. 2 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,440 students in 47 of them; the median school has 370 students and the largest has 961. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Hartford School District (39), Judicial Centers (3), Capitol Region Education Council (2), Connecticut Technical Education and Career System (1), Jumoke Academy District (1), Achievement First Hartford Academy District (1).
