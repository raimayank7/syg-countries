Public schools in Norwalk, Connecticut

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Norwalk, Connecticut: 14 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 11,640 students in 23 of them; the median school has 402 students and the largest has 1,766. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Norwalk School District (22), Side By Side Charter School District (1).
