Public schools in New Haven, Connecticut

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in New Haven, Connecticut: 29 elementary, 9 high, 3 other, 1 middle. 6 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 20,767 students in 42 of them; the median school has 440 students and the largest has 1,721. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: New Haven School District (36), Common Ground High School District (1), Amistad Academy District (1), Highville Charter School District (1), Elm City College Preparatory School District (1), Elm City Montessori School District (1), Booker T. Washington Academy District (1).
