Public schools in Bridgeport, Connecticut

46 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in Bridgeport, Connecticut: 30 elementary, 12 high, 3 other, 1 middle. 6 of them (13.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,230 students in 46 of them; the median school has 440 students and the largest has 1,704. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Bridgeport School District (36), Judicial Centers (2), Connecticut Technical Education and Career System (1), The Bridge Academy District (1), New Beginnings Inc Family Academy District (1), Park City Prep Charter School District (1), Achievement First Bridgeport Academy District (1), Great Oaks Charter School District (1).
