Public schools in Atlanta, Georgia

141 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 141 public schools in Atlanta, Georgia: 80 elementary, 26 high, 26 middle, 9 other. 35 of them (24.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 94,465 students in 141 of them; the median school has 484 students and the largest has 9,762. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 19 school districts: Atlanta Public Schools (87), DeKalb County (19), Fulton County (19), Department of Juvenile Justice (1), Department of Human Resources (1), State Specialty Schools II- Atlanta Heights Charter School (1), State Specialty Schools I- Ivy Preparatory Academy- Inc (1), State Specialty Schools I- Georgia Cyber Academy (1).
