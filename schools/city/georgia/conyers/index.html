Public schools in Conyers, Georgia

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Conyers, Georgia: 10 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 13,894 students in 17 of them; the median school has 644 students and the largest has 2,391. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Rockdale County (16), Department of Juvenile Justice (1).
