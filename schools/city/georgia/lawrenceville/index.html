Public schools in Lawrenceville, Georgia

37 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in Lawrenceville, Georgia: 20 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 44,587 students in 37 of them; the median school has 1,041 students and the largest has 3,073. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Gwinnett County (37).
