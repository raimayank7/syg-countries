Public schools in Stone Mountain, Georgia

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Stone Mountain, Georgia: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (4.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,345 students in 24 of them; the median school has 560 students and the largest has 1,312. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: DeKalb County (24).
