Public schools in Thomasville, Georgia

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Thomasville, Georgia: 6 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,460 students in 13 of them; the median school has 731 students and the largest has 1,664. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Thomas County (7), Thomasville City (5), Department of Juvenile Justice (1).
