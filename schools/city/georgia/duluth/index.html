Public schools in Duluth, Georgia

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Duluth, Georgia: 11 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 3 of them (15.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 26,417 students in 20 of them; the median school has 996 students and the largest has 6,939. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Gwinnett County (13), Fulton County (5), State Specialty Schools I- Georgia Connections Academy (1), State Specialty Schools II- Yi Hwang Academy of Language Ex (1).
