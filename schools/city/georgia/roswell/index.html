Public schools in Roswell, Georgia

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Roswell, Georgia: 12 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. 2 of them (11.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,207 students in 17 of them; the median school has 589 students and the largest has 2,036. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Fulton County (16), State Specialty Schools I- International Charter Sch of Atl (1).
