Public schools in Alpharetta, Georgia

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Alpharetta, Georgia: 16 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high. 1 of them (3.4%) are charter schools. The file reports 29,799 students in 29 of them; the median school has 734 students and the largest has 2,490. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Fulton County (25), Forsyth County (4).
