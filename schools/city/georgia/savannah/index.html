Public schools in Savannah, Georgia

50 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in Savannah, Georgia: 28 elementary, 12 high, 7 middle, 3 other. 5 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,721 students in 50 of them; the median school has 520 students and the largest has 1,179. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Savannah-Chatham County (48), Department of Juvenile Justice (2).
