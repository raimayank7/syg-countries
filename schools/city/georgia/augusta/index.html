Public schools in Augusta, Georgia

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Augusta, Georgia: 23 elementary, 12 high, 5 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 23,343 students in 41 of them; the median school has 467 students and the largest has 1,175. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Richmond County (39), Department of Juvenile Justice (2).
