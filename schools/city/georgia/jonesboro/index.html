Public schools in Jonesboro, Georgia

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Jonesboro, Georgia: 11 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle. The file reports 16,564 students in 22 of them; the median school has 656 students and the largest has 1,618. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Clayton County (22).
