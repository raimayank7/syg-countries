Public schools in Dalton, Georgia

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Dalton, Georgia: 16 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 17,144 students in 28 of them; the median school has 497 students and the largest has 1,512. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Whitfield County (17), Dalton Public Schools (10), Department of Juvenile Justice (1).
