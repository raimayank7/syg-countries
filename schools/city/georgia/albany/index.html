Public schools in Albany, Georgia

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Albany, Georgia: 14 elementary, 4 high, 4 middle. The file reports 13,059 students in 22 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 1,323. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Dougherty County (22).
