Public schools in Decatur, Georgia

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Decatur, Georgia: 27 elementary, 9 middle, 6 high. 4 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,170 students in 42 of them; the median school has 471 students and the largest has 1,834. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: DeKalb County (30), City Schools of Decatur (9), Department of Juvenile Justice (1), State Specialty Schools II- DeKalb Brilliance Academy (1), State Specialty Schools II- The Anchor School (1).
