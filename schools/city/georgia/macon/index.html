Public schools in Macon, Georgia

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Macon, Georgia: 22 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 4 other. 2 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 24,424 students in 42 of them; the median school has 535 students and the largest has 1,808. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Bibb County (34), Department of Juvenile Justice (3), Jones County (2), State Specialty Schools II- Cirrus Charter Academy (1), State Specialty Schools II- Academy For Classical Education (1), State Schools (1).
