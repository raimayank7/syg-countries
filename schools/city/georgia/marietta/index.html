Public schools in Marietta, Georgia

60 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 60 public schools in Marietta, Georgia: 37 elementary, 12 middle, 10 high, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (1.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 53,072 students in 60 of them; the median school has 706 students and the largest has 2,773. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Cobb County (46), Marietta City (13), Department of Juvenile Justice (1).
