Public schools in Washington, District Of Columbia

244 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 244 public schools in Washington, District Of Columbia: 146 elementary, 43 middle, 42 high, 9 prekindergarten, 2 other, 2 secondary. 125 of them (51.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 92,621 students in 244 of them; the median school has 338 students and the largest has 1,994. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 64 school districts: District of Columbia Public Schools (117), Kipp Dc Pcs (20), Friendship PCS (15), DC Prep PCS (6), AppleTree Early Learning PCS (6), Center City PCS (6), Washington Latin PCS (3), Capital City PCS (3).
