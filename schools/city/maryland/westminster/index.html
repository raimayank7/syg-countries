Public schools in Westminster, Maryland

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Westminster, Maryland: 8 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,542 students in 18 of them; the median school has 527 students and the largest has 1,480. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Carroll County Public Schools (18).
