Public schools in Silver Spring, Maryland

55 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in Silver Spring, Maryland: 37 elementary, 10 middle, 8 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 42,899 students in 55 of them; the median school has 619 students and the largest has 3,261. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Montgomery County Public Schools (55).
