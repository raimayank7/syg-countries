Public schools in Laurel, Maryland

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Laurel, Maryland: 14 elementary, 3 middle, 1 high, 1 other. 1 of them (5.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 13,997 students in 19 of them; the median school has 582 students and the largest has 2,179. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Prince George's County Public Schools (10), Howard County Public Schools (6), Anne Arundel County Public Schools (3).
