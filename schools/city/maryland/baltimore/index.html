Public schools in Baltimore, Maryland

271 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 271 public schools in Baltimore, Maryland: 182 elementary, 52 high, 33 middle, 4 other. 31 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 career and technical schools, 8 special education schools, 7 alternative schools. The file reports 151,639 students in 271 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 2,159. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Baltimore City Public Schools (151), Baltimore County Public Schools (115), Anne Arundel County Public Schools (4), SEED School of Maryland (1).
