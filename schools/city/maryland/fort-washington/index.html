Public schools in Fort Washington, Maryland

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Fort Washington, Maryland: 10 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 5,615 students in 12 of them; the median school has 355 students and the largest has 943. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Prince George's County Public Schools (12).
