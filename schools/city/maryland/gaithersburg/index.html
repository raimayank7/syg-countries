Public schools in Gaithersburg, Maryland

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Gaithersburg, Maryland: 21 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. The file reports 21,213 students in 29 of them; the median school has 594 students and the largest has 2,454. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Montgomery County Public Schools (29).
