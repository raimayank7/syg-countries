Public schools in Ellicott City, Maryland

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Ellicott City, Maryland: 11 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 16,389 students in 22 of them; the median school has 715 students and the largest has 1,529. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Howard County Public Schools (22).
