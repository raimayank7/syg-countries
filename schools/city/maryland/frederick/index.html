Public schools in Frederick, Maryland

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Frederick, Maryland: 19 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (9.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,604 students in 32 of them; the median school has 622 students and the largest has 2,031. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Frederick County Public Schools (32).
