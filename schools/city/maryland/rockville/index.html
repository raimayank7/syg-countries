Public schools in Rockville, Maryland

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Rockville, Maryland: 20 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 23,689 students in 34 of them; the median school has 535 students and the largest has 2,386. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Montgomery County Public Schools (34).
