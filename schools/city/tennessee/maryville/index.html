Public schools in Maryville, Tennessee

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Maryville, Tennessee: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 other, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 13,293 students in 22 of them; the median school has 556 students and the largest has 1,513. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Blount County (14), Maryville (8).
