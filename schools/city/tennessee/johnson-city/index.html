Public schools in Johnson City, Tennessee

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Johnson City, Tennessee: 11 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 10,210 students in 18 of them; the median school has 512 students and the largest has 2,423. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Johnson City (12), Washington County (4), Carter County (2).
