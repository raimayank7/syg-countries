Public schools in Knoxville, Tennessee

87 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 87 public schools in Knoxville, Tennessee: 50 elementary, 17 high, 14 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 2 other. 1 of them (1.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 55,454 students in 87 of them; the median school has 507 students and the largest has 2,091. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Knox County (83), Tennessee Schools for the Deaf (2), Union County (2).
