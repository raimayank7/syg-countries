Public schools in Chattanooga, Tennessee

48 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in Chattanooga, Tennessee: 25 elementary, 13 high, 8 middle, 2 other. 6 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 25,044 students in 48 of them; the median school has 467 students and the largest has 1,501. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Hamilton County (48).
