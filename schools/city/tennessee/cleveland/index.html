Public schools in Cleveland, Tennessee

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Cleveland, Tennessee: 17 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,839 students in 26 of them; the median school has 450 students and the largest has 1,799. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Bradley County (17), Cleveland (9).
