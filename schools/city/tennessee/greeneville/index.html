Public schools in Greeneville, Tennessee

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Greeneville, Tennessee: 8 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 2 other. The file reports 5,976 students in 18 of them; the median school has 325 students and the largest has 938. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Greene County (11), Greeneville (7).
