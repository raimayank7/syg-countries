Public schools in Laredo, Texas

83 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 83 public schools in Laredo, Texas: 50 elementary, 16 high, 16 middle, 1 secondary. 6 of them (7.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools. The file reports 63,107 students in 83 of them; the median school has 638 students and the largest has 3,567. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: United Isd (47), Laredo Isd (30), Harmony Public Schools - South Texas (3), Triumph Public High Schools-Laredo (2), Premier High Schools (1).
