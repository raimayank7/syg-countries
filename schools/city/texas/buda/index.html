Public schools in Buda, Texas

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Buda, Texas: 7 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 2 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 12,491 students in 15 of them; the median school has 703 students and the largest has 2,802. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Hays Cisd (13), University Of Texas University Charter School (1), Doral Academy Of Texas (1).
