Public schools in Temple, Texas

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Temple, Texas: 13 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 1 of them (4.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 15,777 students in 25 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 2,342. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Temple Isd (16), Belton Isd (8), Priority Charter Schools (1).
