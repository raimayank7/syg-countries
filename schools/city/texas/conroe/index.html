Public schools in Conroe, Texas

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Conroe, Texas: 18 elementary, 9 middle, 7 high, 3 other, 3 secondary. 2 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 34,853 students in 40 of them; the median school has 704 students and the largest has 5,178. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Conroe Isd (34), Texas College Preparatory Academies (2), Magnolia Isd (1), Montgomery Isd (1), Splendora Isd (1), Willis Isd (1).
