Public schools in Garland, Texas

70 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 70 public schools in Garland, Texas: 46 elementary, 11 high, 11 middle, 2 prekindergarten. 7 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 42,494 students in 70 of them; the median school has 471 students and the largest has 2,785. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Garland Isd (60), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (3), Harmony Public Schools - North Texas (2), Richardson Isd (2), Texans Can Academies (1), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1), Mesquite Isd (1).
