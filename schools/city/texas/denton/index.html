Public schools in Denton, Texas

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Denton, Texas: 16 elementary, 8 high, 3 secondary, 3 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. 3 of them (8.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools. The file reports 18,627 students in 34 of them; the median school has 525 students and the largest has 2,570. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Denton Isd (27), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1), North Texas Collegiate Academy (1), Ki Charter (1), Carrollton-Farmers Branch Isd (1), Lewisville Isd (1), Ponder Isd (1), Sanger Isd (1).
