Public schools in Arlington, Texas

103 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 103 public schools in Arlington, Texas: 63 elementary, 17 high, 17 middle, 3 other, 3 prekindergarten. 17 of them (16.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 71,024 students in 103 of them; the median school has 538 students and the largest has 3,670. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 13 school districts: Arlington Isd (67), Mansfield Isd (17), Uplift Education (3), Arlington Classics Academy (3), Newman International Academy Of Arlington (3), Premier High Schools (2), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (2), Jean Massieu Academy (1).
