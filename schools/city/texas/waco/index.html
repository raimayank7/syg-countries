Public schools in Waco, Texas

56 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 56 public schools in Waco, Texas: 28 elementary, 13 high, 8 middle, 5 secondary, 2 other. 10 of them (17.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools. The file reports 26,539 students in 56 of them; the median school has 416 students and the largest has 2,730. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 15 school districts: Waco Isd (24), La Vega Isd (5), Midway Isd (5), Connally Isd (4), Rapoport Academy Public School (3), Bosqueville Isd (3), China Spring Isd (3), Gholson Isd (2).
