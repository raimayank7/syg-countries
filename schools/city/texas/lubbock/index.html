Public schools in Lubbock, Texas

82 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 82 public schools in Lubbock, Texas: 46 elementary, 16 high, 15 middle, 3 secondary, 2 other. 11 of them (13.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools. The file reports 42,420 students in 82 of them; the median school has 453 students and the largest has 2,081. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Lubbock Isd (49), Frenship Isd (12), Lubbock-Cooper Isd (10), Roosevelt Isd (3), Premier High Schools (2), Richard Milburn Alter High School (Killeen) (1), Rise Academy (1), Triumph Public High Schools-Lubbock (1).
