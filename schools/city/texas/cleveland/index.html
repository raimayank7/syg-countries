Public schools in Cleveland, Texas

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Cleveland, Texas: 10 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 1 secondary, 1 other. 5 of them (25.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 16,412 students in 20 of them; the median school has 762 students and the largest has 3,592. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Cleveland Isd (11), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (5), Tarkington Isd (4).
