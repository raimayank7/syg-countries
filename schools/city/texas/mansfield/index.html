Public schools in Mansfield, Texas

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Mansfield, Texas: 14 elementary, 10 middle, 6 high. 3 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 23,271 students in 30 of them; the median school has 644 students and the largest has 2,772. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Mansfield Isd (27), Newman International Academy Of Arlington (2), Ume Preparatory Academy (1).
