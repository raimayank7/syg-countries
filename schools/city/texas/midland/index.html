Public schools in Midland, Texas

49 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in Midland, Texas: 29 elementary, 9 high, 5 middle, 3 secondary, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 14 of them (28.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 33,788 students in 49 of them; the median school has 607 students and the largest has 2,656. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Midland Isd (40), Greenwood Isd (4), Richard Milburn Alter High School (Killeen) (1), Midland Academy Charter School (1), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1), Premier High Schools (1), Texas Leadership Public Schools (1).
