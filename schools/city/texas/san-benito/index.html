Public schools in San Benito, Texas

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in San Benito, Texas: 15 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 3 secondary, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 3 of them (8.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools. The file reports 13,634 students in 35 of them; the median school has 359 students and the largest has 1,944. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: San Benito Cisd (24), Los Fresnos Cisd (5), Idea Public Schools (2), Triumph Public High Schools-Rio Grande Valley (1), Brownsville Isd (1), Driscoll Isd (1), South Texas Isd (1).
