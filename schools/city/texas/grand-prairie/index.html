Public schools in Grand Prairie, Texas

62 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 62 public schools in Grand Prairie, Texas: 40 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 17 of them (27.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 38,325 students in 62 of them; the median school has 518 students and the largest has 3,364. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Grand Prairie Isd (38), Arlington Isd (7), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (5), Uplift Education (3), Mansfield Isd (3), Harmony Public Schools - North Texas (2), Advantage Academy (1), Trinity Basin Preparatory (1).
