Public schools in San Antonio, Texas

553 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 553 public schools in San Antonio, Texas: 309 elementary, 110 high, 90 middle, 18 prekindergarten, 17 other, 9 secondary. 153 of them (27.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 60 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 332,746 students in 553 of them; the median school has 514 students and the largest has 3,409. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 48 school districts: Northside Isd (121), San Antonio Isd (98), North East Isd (75), Idea Public Schools (28), Edgewood Isd (26), Harlandale Isd (22), Judson Isd (19), South San Antonio Isd (15).
