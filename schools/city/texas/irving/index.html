Public schools in Irving, Texas

58 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in Irving, Texas: 30 elementary, 12 high, 11 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 2 other. 12 of them (20.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 42,818 students in 58 of them; the median school has 701 students and the largest has 2,596. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Irving Isd (37), Uplift Education (6), Carrollton-Farmers Branch Isd (6), Manara Academy (2), Great Hearts Texas (2), Coppell Isd (2), Universal Academy (1), Winfree Academy Charter Schools (1).
