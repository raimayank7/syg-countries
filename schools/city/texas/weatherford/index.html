Public schools in Weatherford, Texas

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Weatherford, Texas: 8 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 1 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,409 students in 16 of them; the median school has 529 students and the largest has 2,487. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Weatherford Isd (10), Peaster Isd (4), Crosstimbers Academy (1), Garner Isd (1).
