Public schools in Richardson, Texas

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Richardson, Texas: 20 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 secondary, 1 other. 2 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 18,739 students in 30 of them; the median school has 452 students and the largest has 2,754. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Richardson Isd (23), Plano Isd (4), Winfree Academy Charter Schools (1), Evolution Academy Charter School (1), Cedar Hill Isd (1).
