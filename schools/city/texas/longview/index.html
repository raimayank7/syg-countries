Public schools in Longview, Texas

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Longview, Texas: 13 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 16 of them (55.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 15,083 students in 29 of them; the median school has 465 students and the largest has 2,185. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Longview Isd (15), Pine Tree Isd (7), Spring Hill Isd (4), East Texas Charter Schools (1), Premier High Schools (1), Ut Tyler University Academy (1).
