Public schools in Stephenville, Texas

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Stephenville, Texas: 5 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,385 students in 11 of them; the median school has 442 students and the largest has 1,115. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Stephenville Isd (6), Three Way Isd (2), Erath Excels Academy Inc (1), Huckabay Isd (1), Morgan Mill Isd (1).
