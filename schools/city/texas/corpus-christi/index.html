Public schools in Corpus Christi, Texas

91 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 91 public schools in Corpus Christi, Texas: 48 elementary, 20 high, 17 middle, 3 other, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 9 of them (9.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools. The file reports 53,825 students in 91 of them; the median school has 511 students and the largest has 2,455. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Corpus Christi Isd (54), Calallen Isd (7), Flour Bluff Isd (7), Tuloso-Midway Isd (5), West Oso Isd (5), London Isd (4), Seashore Charter Schools (2), Por Vida Academy (1).
