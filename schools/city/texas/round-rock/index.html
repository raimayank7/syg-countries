Public schools in Round Rock, Texas

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Round Rock, Texas: 26 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high, 1 other. 3 of them (7.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 33,045 students in 42 of them; the median school has 617 students and the largest has 3,740. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Round Rock Isd (36), Idea Public Schools (2), Pflugerville Isd (2), Meridian World School Llc (1), Hutto Isd (1).
