Public schools in Wichita Falls, Texas

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Wichita Falls, Texas: 17 elementary, 8 high, 5 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 secondary, 1 other. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 14,592 students in 34 of them; the median school has 437 students and the largest has 1,569. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Wichita Falls Isd (25), City View Isd (3), Burkburnett Isd (2), Premier High Schools (1), Iowa Park Cisd (1), Paint Rock Isd (1), Vernon Isd (1).
