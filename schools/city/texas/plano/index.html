Public schools in Plano, Texas

74 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 74 public schools in Plano, Texas: 43 elementary, 12 middle, 6 high, 5 secondary, 4 prekindergarten, 3 other, 1 adult education. 4 of them (5.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 45,584 students in 74 of them; the median school has 493 students and the largest has 3,050. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 6 school districts: Plano Isd (65), Frisco Isd (5), Premier High Schools (1), Harmony Public Schools - North Texas (1), Legacy Preparatory (1), Lone Star Language Academy (1).
