Public schools in Sugar Land, Texas

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Sugar Land, Texas: 21 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle. 3 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 32,708 students in 33 of them; the median school has 753 students and the largest has 2,616. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Fort Bend Isd (28), Harmony Public Schools - Houston West (3), Lamar Cisd (2).
