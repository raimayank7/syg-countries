Public schools in Orange, Texas

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Orange, Texas: 6 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 6,463 students in 13 of them; the median school has 477 students and the largest has 1,103. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Little Cypress-Mauriceville Cisd (6), West Orange-Cove Cisd (4), Deweyville Isd (2), Tekoa Academy Of Accelerated Studies Stem School (1).
