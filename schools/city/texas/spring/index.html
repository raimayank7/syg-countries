Public schools in Spring, Texas

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Spring, Texas: 18 elementary, 7 middle, 5 high. 4 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 30,077 students in 30 of them; the median school has 871 students and the largest has 3,739. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Spring Isd (14), Conroe Isd (12), Evolution Academy Charter School (1), School Of Science And Technology (1), Sam Houston State University Charter School (1), Legacy School Of Sport Sciences (1).
