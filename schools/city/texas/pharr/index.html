Public schools in Pharr, Texas

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Pharr, Texas: 21 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 7 of them (19.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 23,443 students in 36 of them; the median school has 578 students and the largest has 2,229. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Pharr-San Juan-Alamo Isd (24), Idea Public Schools (4), Vanguard Academy (3), Valley View Isd (3), Hidalgo Isd (2).
