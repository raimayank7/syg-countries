Public schools in Killeen, Texas

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Killeen, Texas: 22 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 2 secondary, 1 other. 3 of them (7.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools. The file reports 29,721 students in 41 of them; the median school has 690 students and the largest has 2,258. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Killeen Isd (37), Inspire Academies (1), Richard Milburn Alter High School (Killeen) (1), Priority Charter Schools (1), Belton Isd (1).
