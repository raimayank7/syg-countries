Public schools in Mission, Texas

57 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in Mission, Texas: 35 elementary, 10 high, 9 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 10 of them (17.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 36,852 students in 57 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 2,209. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: La Joya Isd (23), Mission Cisd (16), Sharyland Isd (8), Idea Public Schools (6), Vanguard Academy (1), Premier High Schools (1), Excellence In Leadership Academy (1), Brillante Academy (1).
