Public schools in Brownsville, Texas

78 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in Brownsville, Texas: 47 elementary, 16 high, 12 middle, 2 other, 1 adult education. 19 of them (24.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 50,376 students in 78 of them; the median school has 562 students and the largest has 2,294. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 9 school districts: Brownsville Isd (55), Idea Public Schools (10), Los Fresnos Cisd (4), Jubilee Academies (3), Harmony Public Schools - South Texas (2), Raul Yzaguirre Schools For Success (1), Triumph Public High Schools-Rio Grande Valley (1), Premier High Schools (1).
