Public schools in Magnolia, Texas

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Magnolia, Texas: 9 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 secondary, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 15,188 students in 18 of them; the median school has 757 students and the largest has 2,325. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Magnolia Isd (16), Brookesmith Isd (1), Tomball Isd (1).
