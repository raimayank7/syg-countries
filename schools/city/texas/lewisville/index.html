Public schools in Lewisville, Texas

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Lewisville, Texas: 15 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 8 of them (28.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,874 students in 28 of them; the median school has 608 students and the largest has 4,162. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Lewisville Isd (20), Texas College Preparatory Academies (5), North Texas Collegiate Academy (1), Winfree Academy Charter Schools (1), Premier High Schools (1).
