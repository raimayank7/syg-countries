Public schools in San Angelo, Texas

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in San Angelo, Texas: 19 elementary, 12 high, 9 secondary, 4 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (4.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 18 alternative schools. The file reports 16,031 students in 45 of them; the median school has 267 students and the largest has 3,029. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: San Angelo Isd (23), Grape Creek Isd (6), Ballinger Isd (2), Robert Lee Isd (2), Sterling City Isd (2), Wall Isd (2), Premier High Schools (1), Texas Leadership Public Schools (1).
