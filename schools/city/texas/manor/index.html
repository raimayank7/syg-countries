Public schools in Manor, Texas

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Manor, Texas: 6 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,753 students in 12 of them; the median school has 588 students and the largest has 2,411. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Manor Isd (11), Compass Rose Public Schools (1).
