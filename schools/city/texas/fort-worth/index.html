Public schools in Fort Worth, Texas

272 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 272 public schools in Fort Worth, Texas: 158 elementary, 50 high, 47 middle, 8 other, 7 secondary, 2 prekindergarten. 53 of them (19.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 33 alternative schools. The file reports 154,037 students in 272 of them; the median school has 477 students and the largest has 3,262. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 34 school districts: Fort Worth Isd (133), Eagle Mt-Saginaw Isd (27), Crowley Isd (17), Keller Isd (14), Uplift Education (11), Castleberry Isd (8), Idea Public Schools (6), Northwest Isd (6).
