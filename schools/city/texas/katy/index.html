Public schools in Katy, Texas

94 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 94 public schools in Katy, Texas: 56 elementary, 19 middle, 16 high, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 10 of them (10.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 105,245 students in 94 of them; the median school has 963 students and the largest has 3,754. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Katy Isd (69), Cypress-Fairbanks Isd (13), Aristoi Classical Academy (2), Calvin Nelms Charter Schools (2), Harmony Public Schools - Houston West (2), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (2), Lamar Cisd (2), Excel Academy (1).
