Public schools in El Paso, Texas

217 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 217 public schools in El Paso, Texas: 126 elementary, 48 high, 34 middle, 3 other, 3 secondary, 2 prekindergarten, 1 adult education. 28 of them (12.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 alternative schools. The file reports 154,068 students in 217 of them; the median school has 585 students and the largest has 3,048. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 16 school districts: El Paso Isd (75), Socorro Isd (51), Ysleta Isd (49), Clint Isd (10), Idea Public Schools (6), Harmony Public Schools - West Texas (4), Canutillo Isd (4), Burnham Wood Charter School District (3).
