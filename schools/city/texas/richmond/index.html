Public schools in Richmond, Texas

44 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in Richmond, Texas: 29 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 41,874 students in 44 of them; the median school has 795 students and the largest has 3,142. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Lamar Cisd (27), Fort Bend Isd (12), International Leadership Of Texas (Iltexas) (3), School Of Science And Technology Discovery (2).
