Public schools in Carrollton, Texas

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Carrollton, Texas: 21 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 3 other. 5 of them (13.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 25,060 students in 36 of them; the median school has 507 students and the largest has 3,673. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Carrollton-Farmers Branch Isd (21), Lewisville Isd (9), Harmony Public Schools - North Texas (2), Life School (1), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1), Trivium Academy (1), Dallas Isd (1).
