Public schools in Pasadena, Texas

48 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in Pasadena, Texas: 25 elementary, 16 middle, 7 high. 4 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 30,588 students in 48 of them; the median school has 559 students and the largest has 3,120. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Pasadena Isd (38), Deer Park Isd (6), Raul Yzaguirre Schools For Success (1), University Of Texas University Charter School (1), Richard Milburn Alter High School (Killeen) (1), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1).
