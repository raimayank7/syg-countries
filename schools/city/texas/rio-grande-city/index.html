Public schools in Rio Grande City, Texas

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Rio Grande City, Texas: 9 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. 2 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 10,510 students in 16 of them; the median school has 605 students and the largest has 1,674. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Rio Grande City Grulla Isd (12), Idea Public Schools (2), Roma Isd (2).
