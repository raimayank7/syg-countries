Public schools in Pflugerville, Texas

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Pflugerville, Texas: 17 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 2 secondary. 7 of them (22.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 21,557 students in 31 of them; the median school has 632 students and the largest has 2,106. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Pflugerville Isd (24), Idea Public Schools (2), Basis Texas (2), Premier High Schools (1), Harmony Public Schools - Central Texas (1), Austin Achieve Public Schools (1).
