Public schools in Cypress, Texas

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Cypress, Texas: 18 elementary, 10 middle, 6 high. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 51,470 students in 34 of them; the median school has 1,180 students and the largest has 3,647. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Cypress-Fairbanks Isd (32), Harmony Public Schools - Houston North (1), Tomball Isd (1).
