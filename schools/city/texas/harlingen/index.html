Public schools in Harlingen, Texas

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Harlingen, Texas: 19 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 2 secondary, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (11.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 18,208 students in 35 of them; the median school has 416 students and the largest has 1,931. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Harlingen Cisd (30), Idea Public Schools (2), Horizon Montessori Public Schools (1), Jubilee Academies (1), Driscoll Isd (1).
