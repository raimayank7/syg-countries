Public schools in Flower Mound, Texas

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Flower Mound, Texas: 13 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 1 other. 1 of them (4.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 20,375 students in 22 of them; the median school has 646 students and the largest has 3,442. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Lewisville Isd (19), Argyle Isd (2), Texas College Preparatory Academies (1).
