Public schools in Victoria, Texas

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Victoria, Texas: 16 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 13,164 students in 26 of them; the median school has 450 students and the largest has 1,847. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Victoria Isd (25), Nursery Isd (1).
