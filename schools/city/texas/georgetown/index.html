Public schools in Georgetown, Texas

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Georgetown, Texas: 14 high, 13 elementary, 9 secondary, 4 middle, 2 other. 4 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 22 alternative schools. The file reports 17,132 students in 42 of them; the median school has 61 students and the largest has 2,152. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Georgetown Isd (21), Leander Isd (4), Liberty Hill Isd (3), Round Rock Isd (3), Orenda Charter School (2), Hutto Isd (2), Jarrell Isd (2), Taylor Isd (2).
