Public schools in Austin, Texas

262 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 262 public schools in Austin, Texas: 156 elementary, 47 high, 43 middle, 10 other, 4 secondary, 2 prekindergarten. 66 of them (25.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 24 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 158,434 students in 262 of them; the median school has 511 students and the largest has 3,677. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 34 school districts: Austin Isd (122), Round Rock Isd (21), Idea Public Schools (10), Kipp Texas Public Schools (10), Eanes Isd (10), Del Valle Isd (9), Leander Isd (9), Pflugerville Isd (9).
