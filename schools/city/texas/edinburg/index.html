Public schools in Edinburg, Texas

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Edinburg, Texas: 31 elementary, 12 high, 7 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 7 of them (13.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 39,208 students in 54 of them; the median school has 580 students and the largest has 2,707. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Edinburg Cisd (44), Idea Public Schools (5), South Texas Isd (2), Vanguard Academy (1), Premier High Schools (1), Lone Star School District (1).
