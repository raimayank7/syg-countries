Public schools in Texarkana, Texas

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Texarkana, Texas: 12 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 11,099 students in 23 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 1,762. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Texarkana Isd (11), Liberty-Eylau Isd (4), Pleasant Grove Isd (4), Red Lick Isd (2), Premier High Schools (1), Leary Isd (1).
