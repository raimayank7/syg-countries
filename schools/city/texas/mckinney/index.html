Public schools in Mckinney, Texas

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Mckinney, Texas: 33 elementary, 7 middle, 7 high, 4 secondary, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. 1 of them (1.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 36,782 students in 54 of them; the median school has 550 students and the largest has 2,804. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Mckinney Isd (33), Frisco Isd (9), Prosper Isd (6), Melissa Isd (2), Imagine International Academy Of North Texas (1), Allen Isd (1), Anna Isd (1), Celina Isd (1).
