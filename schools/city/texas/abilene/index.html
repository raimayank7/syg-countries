Public schools in Abilene, Texas

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Abilene, Texas: 17 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 3 prekindergarten, 2 other, 1 adult education, 1 secondary. 2 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools. The file reports 21,221 students in 40 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 1,903. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to adult education.

They belong to 6 school districts: Abilene Isd (28), Wylie Isd (8), Premier High Schools (1), Texas Leadership Public Schools (1), Merkel Isd (1), Mullin Isd (1).
