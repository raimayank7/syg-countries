Public schools in Laurel, Mississippi

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Laurel, Mississippi: 7 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,251 students in 15 of them; the median school has 427 students and the largest has 1,574. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Laurel School District (8), Jones Co School Dist (7).
