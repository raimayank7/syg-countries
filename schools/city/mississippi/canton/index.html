Public schools in Canton, Mississippi

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Canton, Mississippi: 7 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. 1 of them (7.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,272 students in 14 of them; the median school has 381 students and the largest has 749. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Canton Public School Dist (10), Madison Co School Dist (3), Sr1 College Prep & Stem Academy (1).
