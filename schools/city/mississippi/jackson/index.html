Public schools in Jackson, Mississippi

60 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 60 public schools in Jackson, Mississippi: 33 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high, 5 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 20,640 students in 60 of them; the median school has 296 students and the largest has 1,279. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Jackson Public School District (52), Ms Schls For The Deaf And The Blind (2), Reimagine Prep (1), Midtown Public Charter School (1), Joel E. Smillow Prep (1), Joel E Smilow Collegiate (1), Ambition Preparatory Charter School (1), Revive (1).
