Public schools in Columbus, Mississippi

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Columbus, Mississippi: 7 elementary, 6 high, 2 other, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 6,404 students in 17 of them; the median school has 268 students and the largest has 1,199. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Columbus Municipal School Dist (9), Lowndes Co School Dist (7), Ms School For Math And Science (1).
