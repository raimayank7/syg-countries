Public schools in Holly Springs, Mississippi

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Holly Springs, Mississippi: 4 high, 3 elementary, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 1,737 students in 10 of them; the median school has 183 students and the largest has 362. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Holly Springs School Dist (5), Marshall Co School Dist (5).
