Public schools in Moss Point, Mississippi

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Moss Point, Mississippi: 4 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,120 students in 10 of them; the median school has 450 students and the largest has 793. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Moss Point Separate School Dist (6), Jackson Co School Dist (4).
