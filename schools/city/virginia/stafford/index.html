Public schools in Stafford, Virginia

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Stafford, Virginia: 11 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,177 students in 21 of them; the median school has 841 students and the largest has 2,234. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Stafford County Public Schools (22), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1).
