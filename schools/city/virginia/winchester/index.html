Public schools in Winchester, Virginia

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Winchester, Virginia: 12 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,770 students in 20 of them; the median school has 592 students and the largest has 1,573. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Frederick County Public Schools (13), Winchester City Public Schools (7), Northwestern Regional Education Program (1), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1).
