Public schools in Richmond, Virginia

111 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 111 public schools in Richmond, Virginia: 59 elementary, 18 high, 15 middle, 4 other, 3 prekindergarten. 2 of them (1.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 6 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 56,231 students in 96 of them; the median school has 486 students and the largest has 2,032. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Richmond City Public Schools (54), Henrico County Public Schools (46), Chesterfield County Public Schools (3), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (2), Medical College of Virginia (1), MCV-Virginia Treatment Center For Children (1), Maggie L. Walker Governor's School (1), Department of Juvenile Justice (1).
