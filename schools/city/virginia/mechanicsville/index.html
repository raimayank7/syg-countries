Public schools in Mechanicsville, Virginia

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Mechanicsville, Virginia: 10 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,788 students in 16 of them; the median school has 664 students and the largest has 1,509. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Hanover County Public Schools (18).
