Public schools in Lynchburg, Virginia

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Lynchburg, Virginia: 14 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,062 students in 21 of them; the median school has 454 students and the largest has 1,257. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Lynchburg City Public Schools (20), Campbell County Public Schools (4), Laurel Regional Special Education Center (1), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Fort Hill Community School (1), Bedford County Public Schools (1), Central Virginia Governor's School (1).
