Public schools in Roanoke, Virginia

47 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in Roanoke, Virginia: 28 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 22,634 students in 41 of them; the median school has 483 students and the largest has 2,016. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Roanoke City Public Schools (26), Roanoke County Public Schools (18), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Reg Alternative Plus Self Project/Roanoke City (1), Roanoke Valley Governor's School (1).
