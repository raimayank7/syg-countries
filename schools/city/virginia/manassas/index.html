Public schools in Manassas, Virginia

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Manassas, Virginia: 19 elementary, 7 middle, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 28,851 students in 31 of them; the median school has 662 students and the largest has 2,945. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Prince William County Public Schools (24), Manassas City Public Schools (11), Northern Virginia Regional Special Education Pgm (1), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Independence Nontraditional School - Middle (1), The Governor's School @ Innovation Park (1).
