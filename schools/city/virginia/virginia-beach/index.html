Public schools in Virginia Beach, Virginia

89 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 89 public schools in Virginia Beach, Virginia: 55 elementary, 15 middle, 12 high. 1 of them (1.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 64,986 students in 82 of them; the median school has 575 students and the largest has 2,212. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Virginia Beach City Public Schools (88), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1).
