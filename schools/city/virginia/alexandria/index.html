Public schools in Alexandria, Virginia

65 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 65 public schools in Alexandria, Virginia: 39 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 50,002 students in 55 of them; the median school has 671 students and the largest has 4,551. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Fairfax County Public Schools (44), Alexandria City Public Schools (19), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Transition Support Resource Ctr/Fairfax (1).
