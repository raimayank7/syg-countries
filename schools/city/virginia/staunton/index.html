Public schools in Staunton, Virginia

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Staunton, Virginia: 5 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,532 students in 12 of them; the median school has 403 students and the largest has 789. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Staunton City Public Schools (6), Virginia School for the Deaf and Blind-Staunton (3), Augusta County Public Schools (3), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Western State Hospital (1), Commonwealth Center for Children & Adolescents (1).
