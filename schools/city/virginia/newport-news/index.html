Public schools in Newport News, Virginia

47 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in Newport News, Virginia: 24 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools, 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 25,715 students in 39 of them; the median school has 510 students and the largest has 1,810. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Newport News City Public Schools (42), New Horizons Regional Education Center - Special Education (2), New Horizons Career and Technical Center (1), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Enterprise Academy/Newport News City (1).
