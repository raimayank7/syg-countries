Public schools in Chesapeake, Virginia

50 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in Chesapeake, Virginia: 28 elementary, 10 middle, 7 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 40,576 students in 45 of them; the median school has 765 students and the largest has 2,313. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Chesapeake City Public Schools (48), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1), Southeastern Virginia Training Center (1).
