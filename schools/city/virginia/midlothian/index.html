Public schools in Midlothian, Virginia

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Midlothian, Virginia: 11 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 25,628 students in 21 of them; the median school has 972 students and the largest has 2,536. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Chesterfield County Public Schools (26).
