Public schools in Los Lunas, New Mexico

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Los Lunas, New Mexico: 10 elementary, 4 high, 2 other, 2 middle. 1 of them (5.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 8,792 students in 18 of them; the median school has 415 students and the largest has 1,450. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Los Lunas (16), Nm Corrections (1), School Of Dreams Academy (1).
