Public schools in Hobbs, New Mexico

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Hobbs, New Mexico: 13 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,153 students in 19 of them; the median school has 409 students and the largest has 2,291. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Hobbs (18), Nm Corrections (1).
