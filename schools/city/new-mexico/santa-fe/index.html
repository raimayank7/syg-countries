Public schools in Santa Fe, New Mexico

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Santa Fe, New Mexico: 22 elementary, 12 high, 4 other, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 8 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 16,456 students in 41 of them; the median school has 304 students and the largest has 1,599. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Santa Fe (29), Pojoaque (4), Nm Corrections (1), Nm Sch For The Deaf (1), Nm School For The Arts (1), The Masters Program (1), New Mexico Connections Academy (1), Monte Del Sol Charter (1).
