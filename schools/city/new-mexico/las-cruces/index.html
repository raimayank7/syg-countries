Public schools in Las Cruces, New Mexico

46 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in Las Cruces, New Mexico: 27 elementary, 10 high, 8 middle, 1 other. 7 of them (15.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 24,285 students in 46 of them; the median school has 409 students and the largest has 2,063. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Las Cruces (37), Juvenile Justice (1), Nm Corrections (1), Alma D'Arte Charter (1), J Paul Taylor Academy (1), New America School - Las Cruces (1), La Academia Dolores Huerta (1), Las Montanas Charter (1).
