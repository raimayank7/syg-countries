Public schools in Tolleson, Arizona

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Tolleson, Arizona: 9 elementary, 6 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,133 students in 16 of them; the median school has 657 students and the largest has 2,467. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Tolleson Union High School District (4288) (4), Littleton Elementary District (4278) (3), Tolleson Elementary District (4264) (3), Union Elementary District (4277) (3), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (2), P.L.C. Charter Schools (6235) (1).
