Public schools in Queen Creek, Arizona

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Queen Creek, Arizona: 16 elementary, 12 high, 5 other, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 10 of them (25.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 28,003 students in 39 of them; the median school has 611 students and the largest has 2,971. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Queen Creek Unified District (4245) (12), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (6), Chandler Unified District #80 (4242) (3), Higley Unified School District (4248) (3), Benjamin Franklin Charter School - Queen Creek (4355) (2), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (2), Central Arizona Valley Institute of Technology (79385) (2), Eduprize Schools LLC (89412) (2).
