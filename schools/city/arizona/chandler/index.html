Public schools in Chandler, Arizona

69 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 69 public schools in Chandler, Arizona: 43 elementary, 14 high, 7 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten. 19 of them (27.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 4 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 53,703 students in 69 of them; the median school has 588 students and the largest has 6,820. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 24 school districts: Chandler Unified District #80 (4242) (34), Kyrene Elementary District (4267) (7), Mesa Unified District (4235) (4), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (4), Bright Beginnings School Inc. (4362) (1), Kaizen Education Foundation dba El Dorado High Sch (4342) (1), Ball Charter Schools (Dobson) (79204) (1), Fit Kids Inc. dba Champion Schools (78783) (1).
