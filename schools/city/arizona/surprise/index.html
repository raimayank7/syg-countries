Public schools in Surprise, Arizona

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Surprise, Arizona: 19 elementary, 13 high, 2 other, 1 middle. 12 of them (34.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 28,231 students in 35 of them; the median school has 582 students and the largest has 2,443. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Dysart Unified District (4243) (17), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (5), Paragon Management Inc. (5180) (2), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (1), Calibre Academy (79055) (1), ThrivePoint High School Inc. (79059) (1), Success School (79453) (1), Rosefield Charter Elementary School Inc. (87399) (1).
