Public schools in Eloy, Arizona

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Eloy, Arizona: 4 high, 2 elementary, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,644 students in 9 of them; the median school has 233 students and the largest has 426. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Eloy Elementary District (4448) (3), Santa Cruz Valley Union High School District (4454) (2), Toltec School District (4450) (2), Central Arizona Valley Institute of Technology (79385) (1), Mary C O'Brien Accommodation District (4435) (1).
