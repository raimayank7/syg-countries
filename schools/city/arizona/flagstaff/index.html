Public schools in Flagstaff, Arizona

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Flagstaff, Arizona: 16 elementary, 13 high, 2 other, 2 middle. 9 of them (27.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,556 students in 33 of them; the median school has 224 students and the largest has 1,625. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 13 school districts: Flagstaff Unified District (4192) (16), Coconino Association for Vocation Industry and Tec (79381) (5), Coconino County Accommodation School District (10386) (2), Pine Forest Education Association Inc. (4201) (1), Flagstaff Arts And Leadership Academy (4202) (1), Flagstaff Montessori (4205) (1), Mountain School Inc. (4203) (1), Northland Preparatory Academy (4204) (1).
