Public schools in Goodyear, Arizona

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Goodyear, Arizona: 16 elementary, 9 high, 1 other, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 9 of them (32.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,315 students in 28 of them; the median school has 644 students and the largest has 2,253. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 15 school districts: Avondale Elementary District (4272) (6), Litchfield Elementary District (4281) (4), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (3), Liberty Elementary District (4266) (3), Agua Fria Union High School District (4289) (2), The Odyssey Preparatory Academy Inc. (90287) (1), Archway Classical Academy Trivium West (90915) (1), Trivium Preparatory Academy (90859) (1).
