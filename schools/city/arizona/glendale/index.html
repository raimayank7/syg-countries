Public schools in Glendale, Arizona

97 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 97 public schools in Glendale, Arizona: 53 elementary, 38 high, 3 other, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 21 of them (21.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 14 career and technical schools, 9 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 58,853 students in 97 of them; the median school has 537 students and the largest has 2,787. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 28 school districts: Deer Valley Unified District (4246) (17), Peoria Unified School District (4237) (15), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (14), Glendale Elementary District (4271) (14), Glendale Union High School District (4285) (5), Washington Elementary School District (4260) (4), ThrivePoint High School Inc. (79059) (3), Pendergast Elementary District (4283) (3).
