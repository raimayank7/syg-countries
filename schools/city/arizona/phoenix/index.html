Public schools in Phoenix, Arizona

455 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 455 public schools in Phoenix, Arizona: 254 elementary, 113 high, 45 middle, 28 other, 15 prekindergarten. 159 of them (34.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 41 alternative schools, 17 special education schools, 17 career and technical schools. The file reports 220,928 students in 455 of them; the median school has 428 students and the largest has 4,499. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 168 school districts: Paradise Valley Unified District (4241) (33), Washington Elementary School District (4260) (29), Cartwright Elementary District (4282) (23), Roosevelt Elementary District (4279) (20), Phoenix Union High School District (4286) (18), Deer Valley Unified District (4246) (16), Phoenix Elementary District (4256) (15), Alhambra Elementary District (4280) (14).
