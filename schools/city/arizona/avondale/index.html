Public schools in Avondale, Arizona

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Avondale, Arizona: 17 elementary, 13 high, 2 middle, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 8 of them (23.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 20,140 students in 34 of them; the median school has 417 students and the largest has 2,877. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 15 school districts: Littleton Elementary District (4278) (7), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (5), Avondale Elementary District (4272) (3), Litchfield Elementary District (4281) (3), Pendergast Elementary District (4283) (3), Tolleson Union High School District (4288) (3), Agua Fria Union High School District (4289) (2), ThrivePoint High School Inc. (79059) (1).
