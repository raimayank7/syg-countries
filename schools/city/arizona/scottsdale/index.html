Public schools in Scottsdale, Arizona

57 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in Scottsdale, Arizona: 33 elementary, 14 high, 6 middle, 4 other. 12 of them (21.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 30,560 students in 57 of them; the median school has 441 students and the largest has 1,940. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 17 school districts: Scottsdale Unified District (4240) (24), Paradise Valley Unified District (4241) (12), Cave Creek Unified District (4244) (4), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (4), Salt River Pima-Maricopa Community Schools (4320) (1), Little Lamb Community School (79050) (1), BASIS Charter Schools Inc. (81078) (1), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (1).
