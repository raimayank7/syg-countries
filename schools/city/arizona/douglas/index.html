Public schools in Douglas, Arizona

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Douglas, Arizona: 6 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (40.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,823 students in 15 of them; the median school has 252 students and the largest has 1,338. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Douglas Unified District (4174) (8), Center for Academic Success Inc. (4191) (3), Portable Practical Educational Preparation Inc. ( (4431) (1), Liberty Traditional Charter School (10968) (1), Omega Alpha Academy (79503) (1), Cochise Technology District (79403) (1).
