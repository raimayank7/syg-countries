Public schools in Tucson, Arizona

308 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 308 public schools in Tucson, Arizona: 140 elementary, 101 high, 35 middle, 24 other, 7 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 79 of them (25.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 43 career and technical schools, 30 alternative schools, 9 special education schools. The file reports 116,072 students in 308 of them; the median school has 295 students and the largest has 3,039. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 70 school districts: Tucson Unified District (4403) (90), Pima County JTED (89380) (43), Sunnyside Unified District (4407) (22), Amphitheater Unified District (4406) (17), Vail Unified District (4413) (14), Marana Unified District (4404) (12), Flowing Wells Unified District (4405) (11), Catalina Foothills Unified District (4410) (8).
