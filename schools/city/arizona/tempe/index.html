Public schools in Tempe, Arizona

55 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in Tempe, Arizona: 25 elementary, 23 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 16 of them (29.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 career and technical schools. The file reports 27,068 students in 55 of them; the median school has 371 students and the largest has 3,563. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 17 school districts: Tempe School District (4258) (21), Kyrene Elementary District (4267) (7), Tempe Union High School District (4287) (6), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (5), Humanities and Sciences Academy of the United Stat (4337) (2), Pinnacle Education-WMCB Inc. (79439) (2), Pinnacle Education-Tempe Inc. (81001) (2), New School For The Arts (4316) (1).
