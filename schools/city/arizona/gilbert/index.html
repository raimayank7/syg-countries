Public schools in Gilbert, Arizona

78 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in Gilbert, Arizona: 44 elementary, 21 high, 8 other, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 22 of them (28.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 55,312 students in 78 of them; the median school has 576 students and the largest has 3,027. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 20 school districts: Gilbert Unified District (4239) (29), Higley Unified School District (4248) (13), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (8), Chandler Unified District #80 (4242) (6), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (3), LEAD Charter Schools (79967) (3), Benjamin Franklin Charter School - Queen Creek (4355) (2), CAFA Inc. dba Learning Foundation and Performing (90327) (2).
