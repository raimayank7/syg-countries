Public schools in Buckeye, Arizona

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Buckeye, Arizona: 20 elementary, 12 high, 2 other, 1 middle. 8 of them (22.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 23,030 students in 35 of them; the median school has 587 students and the largest has 2,100. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Buckeye Elementary District (4269) (8), West-MEC - Western Maricopa Education Center (80923) (4), Buckeye Union High School District (4284) (4), Liberty Elementary District (4266) (4), The Odyssey Preparatory Academy Inc. (90287) (3), Litchfield Elementary District (4281) (3), Saddle Mountain Unified School District (4254) (2), Edkey Inc. - Sequoia Choice Schools (4329) (1).
