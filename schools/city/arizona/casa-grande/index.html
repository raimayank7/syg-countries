Public schools in Casa Grande, Arizona

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Casa Grande, Arizona: 13 elementary, 10 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 8 of them (28.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,216 students in 28 of them; the median school has 459 students and the largest has 1,970. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Casa Grande Elementary District (4446) (14), Casa Grande Union High School District (4453) (3), Central Arizona Valley Institute of Technology (79385) (2), Kaizen Education Foundation dba Mission Heights Pr (90548) (2), The Grande Innovation Academy (92978) (2), Portable Practical Educational Preparation Inc. ( (4431) (1), Pinnacle Education-Casa Grande Inc. (81011) (1), Legacy Traditional School - Casa Grande (92199) (1).
