Public schools in Nogales, Arizona

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Nogales, Arizona: 9 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,455 students in 18 of them; the median school has 266 students and the largest has 1,795. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Nogales Unified District (4457) (11), Pima County JTED (89380) (2), Mexicayotl Academy Inc. (4463) (1), Pinnacle Education-Kino Inc. (81009) (1), Educational Options Foundation (90201) (1), Kaizen Education Foundation dba Colegio Petite Pho (92989) (1), Santa Cruz Elementary District (4459) (1).
