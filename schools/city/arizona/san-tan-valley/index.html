Public schools in San Tan Valley, Arizona

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in San Tan Valley, Arizona: 13 elementary, 4 high, 1 other. 6 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,383 students in 18 of them; the median school has 591 students and the largest has 2,168. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Florence Unified School District (4437) (6), J O Combs Unified School District (4445) (5), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (2), Fit Kids Inc. dba Champion Schools (78783) (1), Happy Valley East (90906) (1), American Charter Schools Foundation dba Ridgeview (1001346) (1), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (1), Legacy Traditional School-San Tan (1001927) (1).
