Public schools in Yuma, Arizona

55 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in Yuma, Arizona: 26 elementary, 19 high, 7 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 8 of them (14.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 25,959 students in 55 of them; the median school has 367 students and the largest has 2,411. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Yuma Elementary District (4499) (19), Crane Elementary District (4501) (12), Southwest Technical Education District of Yuma (ST (92705) (9), Yuma Union High School District (4507) (6), The Charter Foundation Inc. (6355) (2), Carpe Diem Collegiate High School (80001) (2), Yuma Private Industry Council Inc. (4509) (1), Juniper Tree Academy (79064) (1).
