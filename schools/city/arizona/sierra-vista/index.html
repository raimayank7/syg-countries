Public schools in Sierra Vista, Arizona

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Sierra Vista, Arizona: 9 elementary, 6 high, 2 other, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (26.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,359 students in 19 of them; the median school has 343 students and the largest has 1,750. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Sierra Vista Unified District (4175) (11), Center for Academic Success Inc. (4191) (2), Cochise Technology District (79403) (2), Portable Practical Educational Preparation Inc. ( (4431) (1), Cochise Community Development Corporation (81027) (1), Leman Academy of Excellence Inc. (92730) (1), Palominas Elementary School District 49 (4180) (1).
