Public schools in Mesa, Arizona

147 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 147 public schools in Mesa, Arizona: 86 elementary, 29 high, 15 middle, 14 other, 2 prekindergarten. 49 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 career and technical schools, 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 85,055 students in 146 of them; the median school has 482 students and the largest has 3,469. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 44 school districts: Mesa Unified District (4235) (74), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (12), Gilbert Unified District (4239) (9), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (3), Edkey Inc. - Pathfinder Academy (81045) (3), Queen Creek Unified District (4245) (3), Heritage Academy Inc. (4336) (2), Montessori Education Centre Charter School (4363) (2).
