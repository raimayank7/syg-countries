Public schools in Tooele, Utah

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Tooele, Utah: 9 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,234 students in 15 of them; the median school has 638 students and the largest has 1,878. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Tooele District (14), Scholar Academy (1).
