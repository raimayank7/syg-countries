Public schools in West Jordan, Utah

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in West Jordan, Utah: 18 elementary, 8 high, 5 middle, 3 other. 5 of them (14.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,988 students in 34 of them; the median school has 611 students and the largest has 2,959. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Jordan District (28), Navigator Pointe Academy (1), Itineris Early College High (1), Hawthorn Academy (1), Mountain Heights Academy (1), Ascent Academies of Utah (1), Granite District (1).
