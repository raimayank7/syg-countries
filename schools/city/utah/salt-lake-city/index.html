Public schools in Salt Lake City, Utah

95 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 95 public schools in Salt Lake City, Utah: 59 elementary, 16 high, 13 middle, 5 other, 2 prekindergarten. 12 of them (12.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 50,503 students in 95 of them; the median school has 429 students and the largest has 2,505. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 16 school districts: Salt Lake District (40), Granite District (35), Canyons District (5), Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind (3), City Academy (1), Academy for Math Engineering & Science (1), Salt Lake Arts Academy (1), Salt Lake School for the Performing Arts (1).
