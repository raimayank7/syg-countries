Public schools in Sandy, Utah

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Sandy, Utah: 19 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (5.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 18,447 students in 34 of them; the median school has 428 students and the largest has 2,350. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Canyons District (32), Beehive Science & Technology Academy (2).
