Public schools in Kaysville, Utah

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Kaysville, Utah: 10 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (10.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 11,823 students in 19 of them; the median school has 571 students and the largest has 2,225. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Davis District (17), Jefferson Academy (1), Utah Career Path High School (1).
