Public schools in Orem, Utah

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Orem, Utah: 14 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 16,636 students in 24 of them; the median school has 616 students and the largest has 1,541. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Alpine District (19), Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind (2), Utah County Academy of Science (1), Noah Webster Academy (1), Lumen Scholar Institute (1).
