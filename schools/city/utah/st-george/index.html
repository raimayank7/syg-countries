Public schools in St George, Utah

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in St George, Utah: 13 elementary, 5 high, 3 other, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 16,690 students in 24 of them; the median school has 541 students and the largest has 3,324. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Washington District (22), Success Academy (1), George Washington Academy (1).
