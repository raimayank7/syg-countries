Public schools in West Valley City, Utah

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in West Valley City, Utah: 24 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 11 of them (31.4%) are charter schools. The file reports 23,828 students in 35 of them; the median school has 505 students and the largest has 3,434. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Granite District (24), American Preparatory Academy (2), East Hollywood High (1), Monticello Academy (1), Endeavor Hall (1), Esperanza School (1), Mana Academy Charter School (1), Ascent Academies of Utah (1).
