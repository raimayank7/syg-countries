Public schools in Ogden, Utah

57 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in Ogden, Utah: 30 elementary, 11 high, 8 other, 6 middle, 2 prekindergarten. 6 of them (10.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 4 alternative schools. The file reports 28,743 students in 57 of them; the median school has 463 students and the largest has 2,074. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Weber District (27), Ogden City District (22), Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind (2), Ogden Preparatory Academy (1), No. UT. Acad. for Math Engineering & Science (1), Davinci Academy (1), Maria Montessori Academy (1), Leadership Learning Academy (1).
