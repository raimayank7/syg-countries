Public schools in Lehi, Utah

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Lehi, Utah: 13 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 4 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 21,490 students in 21 of them; the median school has 833 students and the largest has 2,469. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Alpine District (17), Renaissance Academy (1), Ascent Academies of Utah (1), Utah Military Academy (1), Ignite Entrepreneurship Academy (1).
