Public schools in American Fork, Utah

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in American Fork, Utah: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 other, 1 middle. 2 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 9,270 students in 14 of them; the median school has 544 students and the largest has 2,335. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Alpine District (12), Odyssey Charter School (1), Athenian eAcademy (1).
