Public schools in Spanish Fork, Utah

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Spanish Fork, Utah: 9 elementary, 2 other, 2 high, 2 middle. 1 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,233 students in 15 of them; the median school has 593 students and the largest has 1,837. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Nebo District (14), American Leadership Academy (1).
