Public schools in Springville, Utah

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Springville, Utah: 7 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 2 other. 2 of them (15.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,385 students in 13 of them; the median school has 511 students and the largest has 1,675. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Nebo District (11), Reagan Academy (1), Merit College Preparatory Academy (1).
