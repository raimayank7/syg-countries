Public schools in Winston-Salem, North Carolina

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Winston-Salem, North Carolina: 16 elementary, 5 other, 5 middle, 5 high. 4 of them (12.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 16,693 students in 31 of them; the median school has 522 students and the largest has 1,190. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Winston Salem / Forsyth County Schools (22), Davidson County Schools (5), Quality Education Academy (1), Carter G Woodson School (1), Forsyth Academy (1), Arts Based School (1).
