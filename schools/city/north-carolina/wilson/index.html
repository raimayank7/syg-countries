Public schools in Wilson, North Carolina

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Wilson, North Carolina: 9 elementary, 5 other, 5 high, 2 middle. 2 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,171 students in 21 of them; the median school has 443 students and the largest has 1,251. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 4 school districts: Wilson County Schools (18), Sallie B Howard School (1), Deaf and Blind Schools (1), Wilson Preparatory Academy (1).
