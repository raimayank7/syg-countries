Public schools in Rockingham, North Carolina

7 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 7 public schools in Rockingham, North Carolina: 4 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 3,709 students in 7 of them; the median school has 462 students and the largest has 1,358. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Richmond County Schools (7).
