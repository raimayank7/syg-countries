Public schools in Kannapolis, North Carolina

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Kannapolis, North Carolina: 9 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 7,578 students in 11 of them; the median school has 494 students and the largest has 1,776. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Kannapolis City Schools (8), Cabarrus County Schools (2), Concord Lake STEAM Academy (1).
