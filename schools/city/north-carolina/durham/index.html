Public schools in Durham, North Carolina

70 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 70 public schools in Durham, North Carolina: 39 elementary, 15 high, 9 middle, 7 other. 15 of them (21.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 44,212 students in 70 of them; the median school has 500 students and the largest has 2,959. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 16 school districts: Durham Public Schools (55), Maureen Joy Charter (1), Durham Charter School (1), Community School of Digital and Visual A (1), Kestrel Heights School (1), Research Triangle Charter (1), Central Park School For Children (1), Voyager Academy (1).
