Public schools in Cary, North Carolina

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Cary, North Carolina: 18 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (6.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 29,541 students in 33 of them; the median school has 694 students and the largest has 2,561. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 3 school districts: Wake County Schools (31), Triangle Math and Science Academy (1), Cardinal Charter (1).
