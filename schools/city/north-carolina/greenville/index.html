Public schools in Greenville, North Carolina

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Greenville, North Carolina: 14 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 13,396 students in 23 of them; the median school has 470 students and the largest has 1,707. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 2 school districts: Pitt County Schools (22), East Carolina Community School (1).
