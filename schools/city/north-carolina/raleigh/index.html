Public schools in Raleigh, North Carolina

100 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 100 public schools in Raleigh, North Carolina: 63 elementary, 18 middle, 18 high, 1 other. 10 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 65,553 students in 99 of them; the median school has 537 students and the largest has 2,538. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 13 school districts: Wake County Schools (88), The Exploris School (1), Magellan Charter (1), Raleigh Charter High School (1), Quest Academy (1), PreEminent Charter School (1), Deaf and Blind Schools (1), Longleaf School of the Arts (1).
