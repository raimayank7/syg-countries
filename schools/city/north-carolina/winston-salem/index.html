Public schools in Winston Salem, North Carolina

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Winston Salem, North Carolina: 19 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 20,659 students in 35 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 1,747. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Winston Salem / Forsyth County Schools (35).
