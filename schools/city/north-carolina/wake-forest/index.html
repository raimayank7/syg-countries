Public schools in Wake Forest, North Carolina

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Wake Forest, North Carolina: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 other, 2 middle. 6 of them (40.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 15,047 students in 15 of them; the median school has 761 students and the largest has 2,203. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 7 school districts: Wake County Schools (9), Franklin Academy (1), Casa Esperanza Montessori (1), Endeavor Charter (1), Envision Science Academy (1), Wake Forest Charter Academy (1), Wake Preparatory Academy (1).
