Public schools in Murphy, North Carolina

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Murphy, North Carolina: 5 elementary, 4 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 2,348 students in 10 of them; the median school has 179 students and the largest has 516. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 1 school district: Cherokee County Schools (10).
