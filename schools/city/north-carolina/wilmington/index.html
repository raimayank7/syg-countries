Public schools in Wilmington, North Carolina

44 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in Wilmington, North Carolina: 29 elementary, 8 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 7 of them (15.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 24,961 students in 44 of them; the median school has 448 students and the largest has 2,248. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: New Hanover County Schools (36), Cape Fear Center for Inquiry (1), Wilmington Preparatory Academy (1), Island Montessori Charter (1), Classical Charter Schools of Wilmington (1), Girls Leadership Academy of Wilmington (1), American Leadership Academy-Coastal (1), D.C. Virgo Preparatory Academy (1).
