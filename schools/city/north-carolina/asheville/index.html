Public schools in Asheville, North Carolina

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Asheville, North Carolina: 18 elementary, 9 high, 6 middle, 2 other. 6 of them (17.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 17,251 students in 35 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 1,495. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 8 school districts: Buncombe County Schools (20), Asheville City Schools (9), Francine Delany New School (1), Evergreen Community Charter (1), The Franklin School of Innovation (1), Invest Collegiate - Imagine (1), Asheville PEAK Academy (1), Mountain City Public Montessori (1).
