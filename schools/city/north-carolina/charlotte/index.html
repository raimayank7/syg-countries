Public schools in Charlotte, North Carolina

180 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 180 public schools in Charlotte, North Carolina: 116 elementary, 30 high, 24 middle, 9 other, 1 secondary. 25 of them (13.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 131,466 students in 180 of them; the median school has 594 students and the largest has 3,605. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 13.

They belong to 27 school districts: Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (154), Sugar Creek Charter (1), Metrolina Regional Scholars Academy (1), KIPP: Charlotte (1), Charlotte Secondary School (1), Corvian Community School (1), ALA Charlotte (1), Bradford Preparatory School (1).
