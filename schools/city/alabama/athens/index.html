Public schools in Athens, Alabama

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Athens, Alabama: 8 elementary, 4 high, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,193 students in 14 of them; the median school has 529 students and the largest has 7,245. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Athens City (8), Limestone County (7).
