Public schools in Tuscaloosa, Alabama

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Tuscaloosa, Alabama: 15 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,361 students in 23 of them; the median school has 539 students and the largest has 1,422. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Tuscaloosa City (19), Tuscaloosa County (7).
