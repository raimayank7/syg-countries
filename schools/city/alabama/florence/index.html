Public schools in Florence, Alabama

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Florence, Alabama: 7 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,753 students in 14 of them; the median school has 566 students and the largest has 1,074. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Florence City (9), Lauderdale County (6).
