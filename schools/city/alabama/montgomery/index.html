Public schools in Montgomery, Alabama

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Montgomery, Alabama: 31 elementary, 10 middle, 10 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (11.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 27,549 students in 53 of them; the median school has 485 students and the largest has 1,592. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Montgomery County (50), LEAD Academy (2), JF Ingram State Technical College (1), LIFE Academy (1).
