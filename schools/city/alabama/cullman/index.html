Public schools in Cullman, Alabama

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Cullman, Alabama: 8 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 7,693 students in 16 of them; the median school has 500 students and the largest has 986. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Cullman County (13), Cullman City (7).
