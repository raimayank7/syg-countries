Public schools in Bend, Oregon

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Bend, Oregon: 19 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high. 2 of them (6.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 15,508 students in 31 of them; the median school has 438 students and the largest has 1,326. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Bend-LaPine Administrative SD 1 (30), Redmond SD 2J (1).
