Public schools in Beaverton, Oregon

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Beaverton, Oregon: 22 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle. 2 of them (5.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 22,821 students in 35 of them; the median school has 498 students and the largest has 1,722. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Beaverton SD 48J (34), Hillsboro SD 1J (1).
