Public schools in Hillsboro, Oregon

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Hillsboro, Oregon: 19 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 3 other. 1 of them (3.2%) are charter schools. The file reports 16,970 students in 31 of them; the median school has 397 students and the largest has 1,565. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Hillsboro SD 1J (28), Beaverton SD 48J (2), Northwest Regional ESD (1).
