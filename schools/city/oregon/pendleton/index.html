Public schools in Pendleton, Oregon

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Pendleton, Oregon: 4 elementary, 3 high, 1 other, 1 middle. 1 of them (11.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 3,097 students in 9 of them; the median school has 253 students and the largest has 773. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Pendleton SD 16 (8), InterMountain ESD (1).
