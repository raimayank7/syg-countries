Public schools in Grants Pass, Oregon

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Grants Pass, Oregon: 11 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,483 students in 21 of them; the median school has 336 students and the largest has 1,657. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Grants Pass SD 7 (11), Three Rivers/Josephine County SD (9), ODE YCEP District (1).
