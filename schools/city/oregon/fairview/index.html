Public schools in Fairview, Oregon

6 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 6 public schools in Fairview, Oregon: 4 elementary, 1 middle, 1 high. 1 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 2,529 students in 6 of them; the median school has 364 students and the largest has 755. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Reynolds SD 7 (6).
