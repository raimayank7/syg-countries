Public schools in Eugene, Oregon

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Eugene, Oregon: 30 elementary, 11 high, 10 middle, 3 other. 6 of them (11.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 21,998 students in 54 of them; the median school has 351 students and the largest has 1,521. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Eugene SD 4J (36), Bethel SD 52 (11), Lane ESD (4), Crow-Applegate-Lorane SD 66 (2), Marcola SD 79J (1).
