Public schools in Portland, Oregon

153 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 153 public schools in Portland, Oregon: 105 elementary, 25 middle, 20 high, 3 other. 11 of them (7.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 75,315 students in 153 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 2,810. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Portland SD 1J (86), Beaverton SD 48J (19), David Douglas SD 40 (14), Reynolds SD 7 (9), Multnomah ESD (7), Centennial SD 28J (6), Parkrose SD 3 (6), Oregon Department of Education (2).
