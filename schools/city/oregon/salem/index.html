Public schools in Salem, Oregon

58 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 58 public schools in Salem, Oregon: 38 elementary, 11 middle, 8 high, 1 other. 4 of them (6.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 32,601 students in 58 of them; the median school has 440 students and the largest has 2,310. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Salem-Keizer SD 24J (54), Oregon Department of Education (2), Willamette ESD (1), Silver Falls SD 4J (1).
