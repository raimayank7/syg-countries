Public schools in Springfield, Oregon

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Springfield, Oregon: 12 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. The file reports 9,142 students in 21 of them; the median school has 363 students and the largest has 1,300. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Springfield SD 19 (21).
