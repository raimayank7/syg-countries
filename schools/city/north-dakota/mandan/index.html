14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Mandan, North Dakota: 9 elementary, 4 high, 1 middle. The file reports 4,466 students in 14 of them; the median school has 233 students and the largest has 1,152. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.
