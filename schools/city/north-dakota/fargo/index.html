Public schools in Fargo, North Dakota

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Fargo, North Dakota: 20 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 13,597 students in 29 of them; the median school has 437 students and the largest has 1,339. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Fargo 1 (25), West Fargo 6 (4).
