Public schools in Cheyenne, Wyoming

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Cheyenne, Wyoming: 26 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (5.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 13,566 students in 38 of them; the median school has 308 students and the largest has 1,506. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Laramie County School District #1 (36), Wyoming Department of Health (1), Northeast Wyoming BOCES (1), Rite of Passage (1).
