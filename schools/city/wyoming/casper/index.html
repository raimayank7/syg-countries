Public schools in Casper, Wyoming

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Casper, Wyoming: 18 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 12,334 students in 26 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 2,073. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Natrona County School District #1 (25), Wyoming Behavioral Institute - Administration Office (1).
