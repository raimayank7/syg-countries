Public schools in Omaha, Nebraska

187 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 187 public schools in Omaha, Nebraska: 112 elementary, 34 high, 27 middle, 9 prekindergarten, 3 other, 2 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 21 alternative schools, 6 special education schools. The file reports 90,133 students in 187 of them; the median school has 376 students and the largest has 2,674. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Omaha Public Schools (110), Millard Public Schools (35), Westside Community Schools (14), Elkhorn Public Schools (9), Gretna Public Schools (6), Ralston Public Schools (4), Radius Omaha (2), Nova Alternative School (1).
