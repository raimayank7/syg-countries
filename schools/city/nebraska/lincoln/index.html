Public schools in Lincoln, Nebraska

83 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 83 public schools in Lincoln, Nebraska: 41 elementary, 21 high, 14 middle, 7 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 41,858 students in 83 of them; the median school has 396 students and the largest has 2,118. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Lincoln Public Schools (78), University Of Nebraska High School (1), Morton School (1), Pathfinder Ed Prg-Lancaster Co (1), Child Guidance School (1), Nebraska Youth Academy (1).
