Public schools in Springfield, Illinois

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Springfield, Illinois: 21 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (2.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 12,920 students in 39 of them; the median school has 274 students and the largest has 1,446. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Springfield SD 186 (35), Menard/Sangamon ROE (5), Sangamon Area Spec Ed Dist (1).
