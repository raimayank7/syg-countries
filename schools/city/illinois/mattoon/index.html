Public schools in Mattoon, Illinois

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Mattoon, Illinois: 3 high, 2 elementary, 1 other, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 3,075 students in 8 of them; the median school has 393 students and the largest has 952. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Mattoon CUSD 2 (6), Eastern IL Area of Spec Educ (2), Clk/Cls/Cmbn/Dglas/Edgr/Mltr/Shlb (1).
