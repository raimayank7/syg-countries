Public schools in Orland Park, Illinois

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Orland Park, Illinois: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 1 high. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 8,977 students in 13 of them; the median school has 541 students and the largest has 2,906. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Orland SD 135 (10), Southwest Cook Coop Spec Ed (2), Cons HSD 230 (1), Kirby SD 140 (1), Ccsd 146 (1).
