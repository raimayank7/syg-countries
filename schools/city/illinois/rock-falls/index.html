Public schools in Rock Falls, Illinois

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Rock Falls, Illinois: 4 elementary, 3 high, 1 other, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 1,855 students in 10 of them; the median school has 213 students and the largest has 547. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Rock Falls ESD 13 (4), Lee/Ogle/Whiteside ROE (2), Bi-County Special Educ Coop (2), East Coloma - Nelson CESD 20 (1), Montmorency CCSD 145 (1), Rock Falls Twp HSD 301 (1).
