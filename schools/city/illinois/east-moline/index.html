Public schools in East Moline, Illinois

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in East Moline, Illinois: 3 middle, 3 elementary, 2 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,366 students in 9 of them; the median school has 310 students and the largest has 1,859. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: East Moline SD 37 (6), Black Hawk Area Sp Ed District (3), Rock Island ROE (1), Silvis SD 34 (1), United Twp HSD 30 (1).
