Public schools in Lincoln, Illinois

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Lincoln, Illinois: 6 elementary, 2 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 2,354 students in 9 of them; the median school has 229 students and the largest has 779. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Lincoln ESD 27 (5), DeWitt/Livingstn/Logan/McLean ROE (1), Chester-East Lincoln CCSD 61 (1), Lincoln CHSD 404 (1), West Lincoln-Broadwell ESD 92 (1).
