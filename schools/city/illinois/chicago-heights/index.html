Public schools in Chicago Heights, Illinois

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Chicago Heights, Illinois: 10 elementary, 4 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 5,368 students in 15 of them; the median school has 199 students and the largest has 1,832. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Chicago Heights SD 170 (9), Speed SEJA #802 (5), Bloom Twp HSD 206 (3), Region 07 South Cook ISC 4 (1), Flossmoor SD 161 (1), Park Forest SD 163 (1).
