Public schools in Rockford, Illinois

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in Rockford, Illinois: 27 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high, 4 prekindergarten, 1 other. 3 of them (6.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 27,661 students in 45 of them; the median school has 475 students and the largest has 2,170. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Rockford SD 205 (45).
