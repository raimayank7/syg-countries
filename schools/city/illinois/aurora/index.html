Public schools in Aurora, Illinois

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Aurora, Illinois: 28 elementary, 11 middle, 8 high, 4 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 36,131 students in 52 of them; the median school has 536 students and the largest has 4,019. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Aurora East USD 131 (20), Aurora West USD 129 (12), Indian Prairie CUSD 204 (12), Cusd 308 (4), Kane ROE (3), Il Mathematics & Science Academy (1).
