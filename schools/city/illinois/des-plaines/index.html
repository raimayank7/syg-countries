Public schools in Des Plaines, Illinois

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Des Plaines, Illinois: 12 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,491 students in 20 of them; the median school has 313 students and the largest has 1,886. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Ccsd 62 (11), Region 05 North Cook ISC 1 (3), Comm Cons SD 59 (3), East Maine SD 63 (2), NW Suburban Spec Educ Org (1), Maine Township HSD 207 (1).
