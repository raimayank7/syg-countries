Public schools in Joliet, Illinois

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Joliet, Illinois: 24 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,758 students in 38 of them; the median school has 415 students and the largest has 3,436. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Joliet PSD 86 (22), Joliet Twp HSD 204 (4), Plainfield SD 202 (4), Will ROE (3), Troy CCSD 30C (2), S Will Co Coop for Spec Ed (1), Laraway CCSD 70C (1), Minooka CCSD 201 (1).
