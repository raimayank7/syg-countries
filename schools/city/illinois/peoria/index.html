Public schools in Peoria, Illinois

47 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in Peoria, Illinois: 20 elementary, 13 high, 8 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (2.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,403 students in 45 of them; the median school has 308 students and the largest has 1,576. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Peoria SD 150 (31), Norwood ESD 63 (3), Peoria ROE (2), Spec Educ Assoc of Peoria County (2), Dunlap CUSD 323 (2), Limestone CHSD 310 (2), Pleasant Valley SD 62 (2), Hollis Cons SD 328 (1).
