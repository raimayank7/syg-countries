Public schools in Saint Charles, Illinois

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Saint Charles, Illinois: 8 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 10,240 students in 15 of them; the median school has 485 students and the largest has 1,984. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: St Charles CUSD 303 (14), IDJJ Sch Dist 428 (1).
