Public schools in Mount Vernon, Illinois

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Mount Vernon, Illinois: 6 elementary, 4 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 3,316 students in 13 of them; the median school has 145 students and the largest has 1,240. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Clintn/Jeffrsn/Marin/Washngtn ROE (4), Mount Vernon SD 80 (4), Franklin-Jefferson Co Sp Ed Dist (2), Spring Garden CCSD 178 (1), Bethel SD 82 (1), McClellan CCSD 12 (1), Mt Vernon Twp HSD 201 (1), Summersville SD 79 (1).
