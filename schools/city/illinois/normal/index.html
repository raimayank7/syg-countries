Public schools in Normal, Illinois

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Normal, Illinois: 9 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle. 1 of them (5.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 9,869 students in 19 of them; the median school has 441 students and the largest has 2,111. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: McLean County USD 5 (16), ISU Laboratory Schools (2), DeWitt/Livingstn/Logan/McLean ROE (1).
