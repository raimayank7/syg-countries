Public schools in Miami, Florida

316 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 316 public schools in Miami, Florida: 171 elementary, 82 high, 40 middle, 18 other, 4 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 93 of them (29.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 31 alternative schools, 5 special education schools, 4 career and technical schools. The file reports 178,122 students in 316 of them; the median school has 446 students and the largest has 4,389. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Miami-Dade (299), Broward (7), Osceola (4), Orange (2), Palm Beach (2), Lake (1), Polk (1).
