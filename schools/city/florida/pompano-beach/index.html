Public schools in Pompano Beach, Florida

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Pompano Beach, Florida: 14 elementary, 4 other, 3 high, 2 middle. 4 of them (17.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 13,143 students in 23 of them; the median school has 487 students and the largest has 1,930. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Broward (23).
