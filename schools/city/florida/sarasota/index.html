Public schools in Sarasota, Florida

43 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in Sarasota, Florida: 21 elementary, 8 high, 5 other, 5 middle, 4 prekindergarten. 7 of them (16.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 25,481 students in 43 of them; the median school has 562 students and the largest has 2,681. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Sarasota (39), Manatee (4).
