Public schools in Homestead, Florida

44 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 44 public schools in Homestead, Florida: 24 elementary, 11 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 21 of them (47.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 33,230 students in 44 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 3,254. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Miami-Dade (44).
