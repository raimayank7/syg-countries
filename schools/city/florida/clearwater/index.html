Public schools in Clearwater, Florida

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Clearwater, Florida: 14 elementary, 7 other, 6 high, 3 middle. 3 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 13,711 students in 30 of them; the median school has 429 students and the largest has 1,637. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Pinellas (30).
