Public schools in Tallahassee, Florida

61 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 61 public schools in Tallahassee, Florida: 29 elementary, 12 high, 11 other, 8 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 7 of them (11.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 34,277 students in 61 of them; the median school has 477 students and the largest has 1,928. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Leon (56), Fsu Lab Sch (2), Famu Lab Sch (2), Tcc (1).
