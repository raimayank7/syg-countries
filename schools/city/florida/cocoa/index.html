Public schools in Cocoa, Florida

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Cocoa, Florida: 8 elementary, 4 high, 1 other. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 7,638 students in 13 of them; the median school has 517 students and the largest has 1,488. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Brevard (13).
