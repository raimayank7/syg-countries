Public schools in New Port Richey, Florida

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in New Port Richey, Florida: 16 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (17.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 17,152 students in 29 of them; the median school has 565 students and the largest has 1,814. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Pasco (29).
