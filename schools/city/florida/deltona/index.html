Public schools in Deltona, Florida

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Deltona, Florida: 8 elementary, 3 middle, 3 other, 2 high. The file reports 11,937 students in 16 of them; the median school has 679 students and the largest has 1,787. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.
