Public schools in Doral, Florida

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Doral, Florida: 11 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle. 15 of them (65.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 18,004 students in 23 of them; the median school has 684 students and the largest has 2,067. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Miami-Dade (23).
