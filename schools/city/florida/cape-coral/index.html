Public schools in Cape Coral, Florida

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Cape Coral, Florida: 13 elementary, 7 middle, 7 high. 7 of them (25.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 26,174 students in 27 of them; the median school has 919 students and the largest has 2,116. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Lee (27).
