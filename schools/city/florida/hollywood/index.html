Public schools in Hollywood, Florida

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Hollywood, Florida: 22 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 1 other. 13 of them (36.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 23,548 students in 36 of them; the median school has 491 students and the largest has 2,307. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Broward (36).
