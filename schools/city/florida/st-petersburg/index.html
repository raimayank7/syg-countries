Public schools in St Petersburg, Florida

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in St Petersburg, Florida: 28 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle. 2 of them (4.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 23,888 students in 41 of them; the median school has 462 students and the largest has 1,762. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Pinellas (41).
