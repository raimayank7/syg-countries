Public schools in Fort Lauderdale, Florida

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Fort Lauderdale, Florida: 19 elementary, 5 high, 4 other, 3 middle. 3 of them (9.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 20,600 students in 31 of them; the median school has 512 students and the largest has 2,199. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Broward (30), Manatee (1).
