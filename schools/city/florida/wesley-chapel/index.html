Public schools in Wesley Chapel, Florida

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Wesley Chapel, Florida: 11 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. 4 of them (21.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 20,177 students in 19 of them; the median school has 939 students and the largest has 2,183. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Pasco (19).
