Public schools in Lake Worth, Florida

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Lake Worth, Florida: 12 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. 2 of them (11.8%) are charter schools. The file reports 19,956 students in 17 of them; the median school has 986 students and the largest has 2,915. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Palm Beach (17).
