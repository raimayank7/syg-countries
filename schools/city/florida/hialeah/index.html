Public schools in Hialeah, Florida

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in Hialeah, Florida: 27 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 14 of them (31.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 35,107 students in 45 of them; the median school has 783 students and the largest has 1,721. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Miami-Dade (45).
