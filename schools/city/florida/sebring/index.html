Public schools in Sebring, Florida

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Sebring, Florida: 5 high, 4 elementary, 3 other, 2 prekindergarten, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 6,442 students in 15 of them; the median school has 206 students and the largest has 1,901. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Highlands (15).
