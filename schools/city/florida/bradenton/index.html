Public schools in Bradenton, Florida

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Bradenton, Florida: 27 elementary, 9 high, 9 middle, 7 other, 2 prekindergarten. 9 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 32,250 students in 54 of them; the median school has 570 students and the largest has 2,368. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Manatee (53), Sarasota (1).
