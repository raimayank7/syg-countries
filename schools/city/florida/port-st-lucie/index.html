Public schools in Port St Lucie, Florida

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Port St Lucie, Florida: 19 elementary, 5 high, 3 other, 2 middle. 4 of them (13.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 29,735 students in 29 of them; the median school has 866 students and the largest has 3,114. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: St. Lucie (28), Fau Lab Sch (1).
