Public schools in Fort Myers, Florida

50 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 50 public schools in Fort Myers, Florida: 22 elementary, 15 high, 6 middle, 6 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (12.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 36,222 students in 50 of them; the median school has 629 students and the largest has 2,977. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Lee (50).
