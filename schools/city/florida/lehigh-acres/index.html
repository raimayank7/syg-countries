Public schools in Lehigh Acres, Florida

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Lehigh Acres, Florida: 8 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high. 2 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 19,017 students in 16 of them; the median school has 1,222 students and the largest has 2,509. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Lee (16).
