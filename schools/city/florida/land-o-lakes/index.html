Public schools in Land O Lakes, Florida

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Land O Lakes, Florida: 9 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 2 of them (11.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,032 students in 17 of them; the median school has 861 students and the largest has 2,278. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Pasco (17).
