Public schools in Kissimmee, Florida

55 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 55 public schools in Kissimmee, Florida: 29 elementary, 16 high, 7 middle, 3 other. 15 of them (27.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 45,691 students in 55 of them; the median school has 718 students and the largest has 2,890. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Osceola (55).
