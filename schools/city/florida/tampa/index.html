Public schools in Tampa, Florida

175 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 175 public schools in Tampa, Florida: 98 elementary, 32 high, 26 middle, 15 other, 4 prekindergarten. 29 of them (16.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 alternative schools, 10 special education schools. The file reports 104,826 students in 175 of them; the median school has 526 students and the largest has 2,930. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Hillsborough (173), Idea Public Schools (2).
