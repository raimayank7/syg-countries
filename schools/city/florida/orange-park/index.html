Public schools in Orange Park, Florida

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Orange Park, Florida: 13 elementary, 5 high, 4 other, 3 middle. 1 of them (4.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 19,994 students in 25 of them; the median school has 693 students and the largest has 2,364. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Clay (25).
