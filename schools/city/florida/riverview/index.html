Public schools in Riverview, Florida

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Riverview, Florida: 18 elementary, 7 high, 2 middle, 2 other. 14 of them (48.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 28,888 students in 29 of them; the median school has 928 students and the largest has 3,722. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Hillsborough (28), Osceola (1).
