Public schools in Jacksonville, Florida

199 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 199 public schools in Jacksonville, Florida: 117 elementary, 39 high, 25 middle, 16 other, 2 prekindergarten. 45 of them (22.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 17 alternative schools, 6 special education schools. The file reports 121,896 students in 199 of them; the median school has 494 students and the largest has 2,768. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Duval (194), Idea Public Schools (2), Clay (2), Osceola (1).
