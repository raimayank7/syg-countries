Public schools in Orlando, Florida

205 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 205 public schools in Orlando, Florida: 122 elementary, 39 high, 32 middle, 10 other, 2 prekindergarten. 33 of them (16.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 26 alternative schools, 6 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 154,455 students in 205 of them; the median school has 574 students and the largest has 4,546. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Orange (200), Fl Virtual (3), Brevard (1), Osceola (1).
