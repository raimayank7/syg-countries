Public schools in St Augustine, Florida

37 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 37 public schools in St Augustine, Florida: 17 elementary, 11 high, 5 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (8.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 special education schools, 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,947 students in 37 of them; the median school has 532 students and the largest has 2,375. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: St. Johns (32), Deaf/Blind (5).
