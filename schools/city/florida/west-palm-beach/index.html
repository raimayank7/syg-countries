Public schools in West Palm Beach, Florida

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in West Palm Beach, Florida: 29 elementary, 14 high, 6 middle, 5 other. 11 of them (20.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 34,474 students in 54 of them; the median school has 553 students and the largest has 2,783. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Palm Beach (54).
