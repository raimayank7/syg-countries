Public schools in Gainesville, Florida

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Gainesville, Florida: 23 elementary, 16 high, 7 middle, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten. 9 of them (17.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,603 students in 52 of them; the median school has 298 students and the largest has 2,444. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Alachua (50), Uf Lab Sch (2).
