Public schools in Pembroke Pines, Florida

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Pembroke Pines, Florida: 18 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle. 15 of them (51.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 25,926 students in 29 of them; the median school has 649 students and the largest has 2,531. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Broward (28), Fsu Lab Sch (1).
