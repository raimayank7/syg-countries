Public schools in Fort Pierce, Florida

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Fort Pierce, Florida: 10 elementary, 6 high, 3 other, 2 middle. 1 of them (4.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 15,254 students in 21 of them; the median school has 640 students and the largest has 3,187. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: St. Lucie (21).
