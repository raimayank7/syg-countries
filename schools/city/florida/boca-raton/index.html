Public schools in Boca Raton, Florida

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Boca Raton, Florida: 15 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 1 other. 3 of them (11.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 29,869 students in 26 of them; the median school has 1,021 students and the largest has 2,876. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Palm Beach (25), Fau Lab Sch (1).
