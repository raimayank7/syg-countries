Public schools in Coral Springs, Florida

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Coral Springs, Florida: 17 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle. 11 of them (35.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 27,719 students in 31 of them; the median school has 729 students and the largest has 2,744. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Broward (30), Hillsborough (1).
