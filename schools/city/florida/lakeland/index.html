Public schools in Lakeland, Florida

62 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 62 public schools in Lakeland, Florida: 33 elementary, 15 high, 8 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 11 of them (17.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 38,917 students in 62 of them; the median school has 525 students and the largest has 2,517. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Polk (62).
