Public schools in Palatka, Florida

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Palatka, Florida: 6 elementary, 2 other, 1 high, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (18.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 4,268 students in 11 of them; the median school has 260 students and the largest has 1,606. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Putnam (11).
