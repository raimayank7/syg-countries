Public schools in Poplar Bluff, Missouri

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Poplar Bluff, Missouri: 5 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,195 students in 13 of them; the median school has 318 students and the largest has 1,459. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Poplar Bluff R-I (10), Division Of Youth Service (2), Mo Schls For The Sev Disabled (1).
