Public schools in St Joseph, Missouri

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in St Joseph, Missouri: 12 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,294 students in 26 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 1,720. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: St. Joseph (21), Department Of Corrections (2), Division Of Youth Service (1), Mo Schls For The Sev Disabled (1), Savannah R-Iii (1).
