Public schools in St Louis, Missouri

161 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 161 public schools in St Louis, Missouri: 94 elementary, 37 high, 22 middle, 5 prekindergarten, 3 other. 7 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 63,790 students in 161 of them; the median school has 350 students and the largest has 2,033. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 24 school districts: St. Louis City (60), Mehlville R-Ix (17), Riverview Gardens (12), Webster Groves (9), Division Of Youth Service (8), Hazelwood (8), Ritenour (8), Ladue (6).
