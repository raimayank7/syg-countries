Public schools in Florissant, Missouri

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Florissant, Missouri: 18 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high, 2 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,833 students in 30 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,782. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Hazelwood (17), Ferguson-Florissant R-Ii (10), Specl. Sch. Dst. St. Louis Co. (3).
