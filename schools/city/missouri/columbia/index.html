Public schools in Columbia, Missouri

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Columbia, Missouri: 22 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,789 students in 39 of them; the median school has 464 students and the largest has 2,056. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Columbia 93 (36), Division Of Youth Service (1), Mo Schls For The Sev Disabled (1), University Of Mo - Columbia (1).
