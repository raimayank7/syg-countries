Public schools in Springfield, Missouri

67 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 67 public schools in Springfield, Missouri: 37 elementary, 12 high, 10 middle, 4 other, 4 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,191 students in 67 of them; the median school has 338 students and the largest has 1,918. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Springfield R-Xii (56), Division Of Youth Service (4), Willard R-Ii (4), Ozarks Technical Comm College (1), Mo Schls For The Sev Disabled (1), Sturgeon R-V (1).
