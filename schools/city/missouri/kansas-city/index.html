Public schools in Kansas City, Missouri

166 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 166 public schools in Kansas City, Missouri: 92 elementary, 29 high, 28 middle, 10 other, 5 prekindergarten, 2 secondary. 45 of them (27.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 70,051 students in 166 of them; the median school has 370 students and the largest has 2,006. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 32 school districts: Kansas City 33 (33), North Kansas City 74 (28), Park Hill (18), Hickman Mills C-1 (13), Center 58 (8), Raytown C-2 (7), Allen Village (5), Frontier Schools (5).
