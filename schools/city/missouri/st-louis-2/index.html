Public schools in St. Louis, Missouri

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in St. Louis, Missouri: 26 elementary, 7 middle, 6 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 25 of them (59.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,844 students in 42 of them; the median school has 319 students and the largest has 2,266. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 15 school districts: St. Louis City (7), Confluence Academies (5), Kipp St Louis Public Schools (5), Lindbergh Schools (5), Gateway Science Acad/St Louis (4), Momentum Academy (4), Kairos Academies (2), Ladue (2).
