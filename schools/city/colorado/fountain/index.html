Public schools in Fountain, Colorado

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Fountain, Colorado: 5 elementary, 2 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,028 students in 10 of them; the median school has 449 students and the largest has 1,968. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Fountain School District No. 8 in the county of El Paso an (8), Hanover School District No. 28 in the county of El Paso an (1), School District No. 3 in the county of El Paso and State of (1).
