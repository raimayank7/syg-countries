Public schools in Loveland, Colorado

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Loveland, Colorado: 16 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (8.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,806 students in 25 of them; the median school has 301 students and the largest has 1,479. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Thompson School District R-2J (24), Poudre School District R-1 (1).
