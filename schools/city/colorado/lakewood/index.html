Public schools in Lakewood, Colorado

48 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in Lakewood, Colorado: 22 elementary, 15 high, 7 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,520 students in 48 of them; the median school has 380 students and the largest has 2,578. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (40), Byers School District No. 32J (8).
