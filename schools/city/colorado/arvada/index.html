Public schools in Arvada, Colorado

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Arvada, Colorado: 20 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (18.2%) are charter schools. The file reports 17,792 students in 33 of them; the median school has 434 students and the largest has 1,878. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (30), State Charter School Institute (2), Westminster Public Schools (1).
