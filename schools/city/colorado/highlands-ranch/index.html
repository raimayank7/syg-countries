Public schools in Highlands Ranch, Colorado

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Highlands Ranch, Colorado: 17 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 4 of them (15.4%) are charter schools. The file reports 20,106 students in 26 of them; the median school has 483 students and the largest has 2,328. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Douglas County School District No. Re 1 (26).
