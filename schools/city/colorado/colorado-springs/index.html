Public schools in Colorado Springs, Colorado

185 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 185 public schools in Colorado Springs, Colorado: 107 elementary, 39 high, 28 middle, 9 other, 2 prekindergarten. 36 of them (19.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 12 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 90,418 students in 184 of them; the median school has 387 students and the largest has 1,913. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 13 school districts: Colorado Springs School District No. 11 in the county of E (58), Academy School District No. 20 in the county of El Paso an (37), Harrison School District No. 2 in the county of El Paso an (28), El Paso County Colorado School District 49 (20), School District No. 3 in the county of El Paso and State of (17), Cheyenne Mountain School District No. 12 in the county of E (9), State Charter School Institute (7), Education Reenvisioned Boces (2).
