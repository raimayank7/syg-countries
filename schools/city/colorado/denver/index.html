Public schools in Denver, Colorado

228 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 228 public schools in Denver, Colorado: 128 elementary, 59 high, 32 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 3 other, 2 secondary. 56 of them (24.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 25 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 98,362 students in 226 of them; the median school has 378 students and the largest has 2,359. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: School District No. 1 in the county of Denver and State of C (197), Mapleton School District No. 1 in the county of Adams & St (10), Westminster Public Schools (7), Cherry Creek School District No. 5 in the county of Arapah (3), State Charter School Institute (2), Division of Youth Services (2), Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (2), Adams 12 Five Star Schools (2).
