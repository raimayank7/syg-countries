Public schools in Littleton, Colorado

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Littleton, Colorado: 27 elementary, 9 middle, 5 high, 1 other. 4 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 24,759 students in 42 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 1,812. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (25), Littleton School District No. 6 in the county of Arapahoe (12), Douglas County School District No. Re 1 (5).
