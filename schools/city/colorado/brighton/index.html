Public schools in Brighton, Colorado

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Brighton, Colorado: 9 elementary, 5 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 11,282 students in 18 of them; the median school has 535 students and the largest has 1,981. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: School District 27J (15), Adams 12 Five Star Schools (2), State Charter School Institute (1).
