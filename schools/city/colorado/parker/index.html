Public schools in Parker, Colorado

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Parker, Colorado: 18 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. The file reports 19,686 students in 27 of them; the median school has 606 students and the largest has 2,206. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Douglas County School District No. Re 1 (24), Elizabeth School District (2), State Charter School Institute (1).
