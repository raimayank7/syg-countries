Public schools in Longmont, Colorado

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Longmont, Colorado: 17 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 15,391 students in 30 of them; the median school has 375 students and the largest has 1,254. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: St. Vrain Valley School District No. Re1J (30).
