Public schools in Grand Junction, Colorado

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Grand Junction, Colorado: 22 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (16.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 14,912 students in 35 of them; the median school has 372 students and the largest has 1,590. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Mesa County Valley School District No. 51 (33), State Charter School Institute (2), Division of Youth Services (1).
