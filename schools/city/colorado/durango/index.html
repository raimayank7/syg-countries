Public schools in Durango, Colorado

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Durango, Colorado: 7 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (20.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 5,620 students in 15 of them; the median school has 301 students and the largest has 1,343. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Durango School District No. 9-R (12), State Charter School Institute (2), San Juan BOCES (1).
