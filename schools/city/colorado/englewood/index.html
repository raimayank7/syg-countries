Public schools in Englewood, Colorado

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Englewood, Colorado: 8 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. 5 of them (21.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 8,924 students in 23 of them; the median school has 275 students and the largest has 1,652. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Englewood School District No. 1 in the county of Arapahoe (9), Douglas County School District No. Re 1 (4), Sheridan School District No. 2 (4), Cherry Creek School District No. 5 in the county of Arapah (3), State Charter School Institute (1), Education Reenvisioned Boces (1), School District 27J (1).
