Public schools in Greeley, Colorado

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Greeley, Colorado: 15 elementary, 10 high, 3 middle, 3 other. 5 of them (16.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,574 students in 30 of them; the median school has 540 students and the largest has 1,987. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: GreeleySchool District No. 6 in the county of Weld and Sta (28), Centennial BOCES (2), Division of Youth Services (1).
