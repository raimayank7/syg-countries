Public schools in Broomfield, Colorado

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Broomfield, Colorado: 10 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 2 other. 4 of them (23.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 12,513 students in 17 of them; the median school has 505 students and the largest has 2,158. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Adams 12 Five Star Schools (8), Boulder Valley School District No. Re2 (6), Jefferson County School District No. R-1 (3).
