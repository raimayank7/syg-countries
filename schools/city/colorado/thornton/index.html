Public schools in Thornton, Colorado

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Thornton, Colorado: 21 elementary, 8 high, 5 middle, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten. 3 of them (7.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 22,406 students in 40 of them; the median school has 453 students and the largest has 1,636. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Adams 12 Five Star Schools (25), Mapleton School District No. 1 in the county of Adams & St (10), School District 27J (5).
