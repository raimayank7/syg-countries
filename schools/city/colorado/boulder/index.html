Public schools in Boulder, Colorado

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Boulder, Colorado: 16 elementary, 6 high, 6 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (10.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 12,944 students in 30 of them; the median school has 328 students and the largest has 2,022. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Boulder Valley School District No. Re2 (29), Education Reenvisioned Boces (1).
