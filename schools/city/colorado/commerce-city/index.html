Public schools in Commerce City, Colorado

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Commerce City, Colorado: 12 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 5 of them (22.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 10,714 students in 22 of them; the median school has 442 students and the largest has 1,586. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: School District N. 14 in the county of Adams & State of Colo (12), School District 27J (7), State Charter School Institute (3).
