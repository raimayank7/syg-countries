Public schools in Saint Cloud, Minnesota

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Saint Cloud, Minnesota: 9 elementary, 5 high, 4 other, 3 middle. 5 of them (23.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 8,699 students in 21 of them; the median school has 256 students and the largest has 1,538. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: St. Cloud Public School District (15), Saint Cloud Math and Science Academ (2), STRIDE Academy (1), Minnesota Department Of Corrections (1), Success Academy (1), Athlos Academy of Saint Cloud (1).
