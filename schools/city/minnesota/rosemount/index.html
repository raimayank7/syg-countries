Public schools in Rosemount, Minnesota

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Rosemount, Minnesota: 5 high, 5 other, 4 elementary, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 6 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 6,044 students in 17 of them; the median school has 85 students and the largest has 2,380. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Rosemount-Apple Valley-Eagan (9), Intermediate School District 917 (8).
