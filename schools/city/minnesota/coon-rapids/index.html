Public schools in Coon Rapids, Minnesota

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Coon Rapids, Minnesota: 8 high, 8 elementary, 3 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (20.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 8,592 students in 20 of them; the median school has 305 students and the largest has 2,147. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Anoka-Hennepin School District (16), Innovation Sci & Tech Academy (2), Northwest Passage High School (1), Paladin Career and Tech High School (1).
