Public schools in New Hope, Minnesota

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in New Hope, Minnesota: 6 high, 5 elementary, 1 other. 1 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 3,536 students in 12 of them; the median school has 129 students and the largest has 1,474. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Robbinsdale Public School District (8), Intermediate School District 287 (3), Escuela Exitos Charter School (1).
