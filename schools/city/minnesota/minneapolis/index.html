Public schools in Minneapolis, Minnesota

158 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 158 public schools in Minneapolis, Minnesota: 73 elementary, 54 high, 20 middle, 6 other, 3 prekindergarten, 2 secondary. 60 of them (38.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 29 alternative schools, 6 special education schools. The file reports 40,182 students in 158 of them; the median school has 187 students and the largest has 1,555. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 31 school districts: Minneapolis Public School District (98), Minnesota Transitions Charter Sch (6), Cyber Village Academy (5), Metro Schools Charter (5), Hiawatha Academies (4), Kipp Minnesota Charter School (4), Best Academy (3), Minnesota Internship Center (3).
