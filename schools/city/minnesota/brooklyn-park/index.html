Public schools in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota: 17 elementary, 9 high, 5 middle, 2 other. 11 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools. The file reports 11,009 students in 33 of them; the median school has 241 students and the largest has 1,991. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Osseo Public School District (18), Intermediate School District 287 (3), Prairie Seeds Academy (3), Noble Academy (2), Athlos Leadership Academy (1), Excell Academy Charter (1), Sage Academy Charter School (1), Spero Academy (1).
