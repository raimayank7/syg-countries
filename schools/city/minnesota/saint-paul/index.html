Public schools in Saint Paul, Minnesota

175 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 175 public schools in Saint Paul, Minnesota: 70 elementary, 52 high, 24 middle, 17 prekindergarten, 10 other, 2 secondary. 73 of them (41.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 22 special education schools, 20 alternative schools. The file reports 52,898 students in 175 of them; the median school has 207 students and the largest has 3,021. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 41 school districts: Saint Paul Public Schools (101), Community School Of Excellence (6), Community Of Peace Academy (3), Higher Ground Academy (3), St. Paul City School (3), Avalon School (3), Hope Community Academy (3), Hmong College Prep Academy (3).
