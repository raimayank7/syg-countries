Public schools in Monticello, Minnesota

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Monticello, Minnesota: 9 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 1 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,461 students in 20 of them; the median school has 25 students and the largest has 1,330. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Sherburne and Northern Wright Speci (10), Monticello Public School District (9), Swan River Montessori Charter Sch (1).
