Public schools in Duluth, Minnesota

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Duluth, Minnesota: 15 elementary, 8 high, 7 other, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (12.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 10,918 students in 33 of them; the median school has 271 students and the largest has 1,486. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Duluth Public School District (27), Duluth Public Schools Academy (2), Proctor Public School District (2), North Shore Community School (1), Harbor City International Charter (1).
