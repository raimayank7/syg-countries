Public schools in Bloomington, Minnesota

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Bloomington, Minnesota: 14 elementary, 8 high, 5 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (17.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 11,793 students in 28 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 1,685. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Bloomington Public School District (23), Success Academy (2), Bluesky Charter School (1), Seven Hills Preparatory Academy (1), New Century School (1).
