Public schools in Honolulu, Hawaii

74 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 74 public schools in Honolulu, Hawaii: 49 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high, 4 other. 6 of them (8.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 39,175 students in 74 of them; the median school has 446 students and the largest has 2,072. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Hawaii Department of Education (74).
