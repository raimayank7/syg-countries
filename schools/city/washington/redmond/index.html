Public schools in Redmond, Washington

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Redmond, Washington: 13 elementary, 5 middle, 3 high, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,390 students in 24 of them; the median school has 468 students and the largest has 2,275. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Lake Washington School District (23), Northshore School District (1).
