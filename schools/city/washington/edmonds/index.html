Public schools in Edmonds, Washington

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in Edmonds, Washington: 10 elementary, 2 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 6,793 students in 13 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 1,578. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Edmonds School District (11), Mukilteo School District (2).
