Public schools in Bremerton, Washington

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Bremerton, Washington: 13 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 9,196 students in 23 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 1,279. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Bremerton School District (13), Central Kitsap School District (8), Washington Military Department (1), Catalyst Public Schools (1).
