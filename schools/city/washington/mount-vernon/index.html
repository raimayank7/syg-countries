Public schools in Mount Vernon, Washington

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Mount Vernon, Washington: 8 elementary, 4 high, 3 other, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,156 students in 17 of them; the median school has 362 students and the largest has 1,958. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Mount Vernon School District (14), Northwest Educational Service District 189 (1), Conway School District (1), Sedro-Woolley School District (1).
