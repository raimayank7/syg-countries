Public schools in Moses Lake, Washington

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Moses Lake, Washington: 11 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,740 students in 20 of them; the median school has 333 students and the largest has 1,979. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Moses Lake School District (20).
