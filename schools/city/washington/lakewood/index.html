Public schools in Lakewood, Washington

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Lakewood, Washington: 11 elementary, 9 high, 4 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,651 students in 27 of them; the median school has 306 students and the largest has 1,195. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Clover Park School District (26), Clover Park Technical College (1).
