Public schools in Yakima, Washington

53 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in Yakima, Washington: 23 elementary, 20 high, 7 middle, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,077 students in 53 of them; the median school has 412 students and the largest has 2,315. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Yakima School District (29), West Valley School District (Yakima) (16), East Valley School District (Yakima) (4), Educational Service District 105 (1), Naches Valley School District (1), Selah School District (1), Wapato School District (1).
