Public schools in Spokane Valley, Washington

35 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 35 public schools in Spokane Valley, Washington: 18 elementary, 7 high, 6 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,439 students in 35 of them; the median school has 373 students and the largest has 1,395. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Central Valley School District (27), East Valley School District (Spokane) (7), West Valley School District (Spokane) (1).
