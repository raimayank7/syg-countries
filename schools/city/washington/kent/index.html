Public schools in Kent, Washington

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Kent, Washington: 22 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 18,403 students in 36 of them; the median school has 415 students and the largest has 2,124. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Kent School District (32), Federal Way School District (4).
