Public schools in Renton, Washington

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Renton, Washington: 19 elementary, 10 high, 5 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (2.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 18,936 students in 39 of them; the median school has 418 students and the largest has 1,828. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Renton School District (24), Issaquah School District (5), Kent School District (5), Renton Technical College (2), Puget Sound Educational Service District 121 (1), Tukwila School District (1), Impact | Black River Elementary (1).
