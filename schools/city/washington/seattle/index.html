Public schools in Seattle, Washington

133 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 133 public schools in Seattle, Washington: 82 elementary, 29 high, 16 middle, 5 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (3.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 58,751 students in 133 of them; the median school has 361 students and the largest has 1,697. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Seattle School District No. 1 (109), Highline School District (13), Renton School District (5), Tukwila School District (2), Summit Public School: Sierra (1), Rainier Prep Charter School District (1), Rainier Valley Leadership Academy (1), Summit Public School: Atlas (1).
