Public schools in Spokane, Washington

101 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 101 public schools in Spokane, Washington: 52 elementary, 26 high, 14 middle, 6 other, 3 prekindergarten. 3 of them (3.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 41,229 students in 101 of them; the median school has 374 students and the largest has 1,805. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Spokane School District (69), West Valley School District (Spokane) (12), Mead School District (10), Educational Service District 101 (3), Cheney School District (2), Spokane International Academy (1), PRIDE Prep Charter School District (1), Lumen Public School (1).
