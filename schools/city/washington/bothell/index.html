Public schools in Bothell, Washington

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Bothell, Washington: 14 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 16,464 students in 25 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 1,917. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Northshore School District (21), Everett School District (3), Edmonds School District (1).
