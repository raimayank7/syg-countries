Public schools in Burien, Washington

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Burien, Washington: 6 high, 5 elementary, 2 other, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools. The file reports 4,833 students in 14 of them; the median school has 317 students and the largest has 1,408. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Highline School District (14).
