Public schools in Tacoma, Washington

101 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 101 public schools in Tacoma, Washington: 51 elementary, 25 high, 16 middle, 7 prekindergarten, 1 secondary, 1 other. 2 of them (2.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 42,592 students in 101 of them; the median school has 377 students and the largest has 1,609. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Tacoma School District (69), Franklin Pierce School District (15), Bethel School District (7), Fife School District (4), Bates Technical College (2), Summit Public School: Olympus (1), Impact | Commencement Bay Elementary (1), Puyallup School District (1).
