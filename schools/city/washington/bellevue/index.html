Public schools in Bellevue, Washington

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Bellevue, Washington: 17 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,299 students in 31 of them; the median school has 555 students and the largest has 1,844. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Bellevue School District (29), Issaquah School District (2).
