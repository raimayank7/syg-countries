Public schools in Auburn, Washington

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Auburn, Washington: 20 elementary, 6 middle, 5 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 22,636 students in 33 of them; the median school has 536 students and the largest has 1,935. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Auburn School District (25), Federal Way School District (8).
