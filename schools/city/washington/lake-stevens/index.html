Public schools in Lake Stevens, Washington

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Lake Stevens, Washington: 7 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 9,788 students in 16 of them; the median school has 597 students and the largest has 2,187. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Lake Stevens School District (16).
