Public schools in Everett, Washington

43 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in Everett, Washington: 22 elementary, 12 high, 6 middle, 3 other. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 23,067 students in 43 of them; the median school has 515 students and the largest has 2,100. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Everett School District (26), Mukilteo School District (14), Northwest Educational Service District 189 (2), Snohomish School District (1).
