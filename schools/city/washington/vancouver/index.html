Public schools in Vancouver, Washington

93 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 93 public schools in Vancouver, Washington: 46 elementary, 20 high, 14 middle, 11 other, 2 prekindergarten. 1 of them (1.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 8 alternative schools, 6 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 44,208 students in 93 of them; the median school has 436 students and the largest has 1,780. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Vancouver School District (45), Evergreen School District (Clark) (37), Battle Ground School District (4), Educational Service District 112 (2), Washington Center for Deaf and Hard of Hearing Youth (1), Office of the Governor (Sch for Blind) (1), Educational Service Agency 112 (1), Toppenish School District (1).
