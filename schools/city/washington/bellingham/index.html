Public schools in Bellingham, Washington

34 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in Bellingham, Washington: 17 elementary, 11 high, 4 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (2.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 12,663 students in 34 of them; the median school has 335 students and the largest has 1,241. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Bellingham School District (25), Meridian School District (4), Northwest Educational Service District 189 (1), Intergenerational High School (1), Blaine School District (1), Ferndale School District (1), Mount Baker School District (1).
