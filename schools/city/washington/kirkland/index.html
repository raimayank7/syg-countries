Public schools in Kirkland, Washington

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Kirkland, Washington: 14 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools. The file reports 12,794 students in 27 of them; the median school has 409 students and the largest has 2,037. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Lake Washington School District (25), Lake Washington Institute of Technology (2).
