Public schools in Charleston, South Carolina

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Charleston, South Carolina: 21 elementary, 6 high, 4 middle, 1 other. 7 of them (21.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 19,067 students in 32 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 2,043. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Charleston 01 (29), Berkeley 01 (2), SC Public Charter School District (1).
