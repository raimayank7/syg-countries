Public schools in Greenville, South Carolina

57 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 57 public schools in Greenville, South Carolina: 28 elementary, 14 high, 11 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 9 of them (15.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 42,664 students in 57 of them; the median school has 670 students and the largest has 3,493. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Greenville 01 (45), SC Public Charter School District (5), Anderson 01 (3), Charter Institute at Erskine (2), Governor's School for the Arts and Humanities (1), Limestone Charters Association (1).
