Public schools in Simpsonville, South Carolina

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Simpsonville, South Carolina: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high. 3 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. The file reports 15,918 students in 17 of them; the median school has 909 students and the largest has 2,085. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Greenville 01 (14), Charter Institute at Erskine (2), SC Public Charter School District (1).
