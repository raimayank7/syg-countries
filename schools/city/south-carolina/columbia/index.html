Public schools in Columbia, South Carolina

78 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in Columbia, South Carolina: 43 elementary, 19 high, 12 middle, 4 other. 7 of them (9.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 47,914 students in 78 of them; the median school has 478 students and the largest has 5,955. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Richland 01 (41), Richland 02 (18), Lexington 05 (7), Dept Of Correction N04 (4), Dept of Juvenile Justice (3), SC Public Charter School District (3), Charter Institute at Erskine (2).
