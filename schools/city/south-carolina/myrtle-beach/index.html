Public schools in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina: 14 elementary, 7 middle, 7 high. 5 of them (17.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 24,580 students in 28 of them; the median school has 846 students and the largest has 3,181. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Horry 01 (25), Limestone Charters Association (2), SC Public Charter School District (1).
