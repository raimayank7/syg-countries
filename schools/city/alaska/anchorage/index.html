Public schools in Anchorage, Alaska

78 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 78 public schools in Anchorage, Alaska: 50 elementary, 11 high, 9 middle, 8 other. 7 of them (9.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 36,772 students in 78 of them; the median school has 383 students and the largest has 1,768. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Anchorage School District (77), Chugach School District (1).
