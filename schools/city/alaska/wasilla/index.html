Public schools in Wasilla, Alaska

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Wasilla, Alaska: 15 elementary, 6 other, 4 high, 1 middle. 6 of them (23.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,226 students in 26 of them; the median school has 380 students and the largest has 2,558. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Matanuska-Susitna Borough School District (26).
