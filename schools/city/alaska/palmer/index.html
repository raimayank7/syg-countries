Public schools in Palmer, Alaska

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Palmer, Alaska: 7 elementary, 4 high, 2 other, 1 middle. 2 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 5,596 students in 14 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 1,092. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Matanuska-Susitna Borough School District (14).
