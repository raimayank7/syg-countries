Public schools in Rogers, Arkansas

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Rogers, Arkansas: 17 elementary, 5 high, 5 middle. 7 of them (25.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 17,424 students in 27 of them; the median school has 477 students and the largest has 2,379. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Rogers School District (20), Arkansas Arts Academy (2), Lisa Academy (2), Haas Hall Academy (1), Founders Classical Academies Of Arkansas (1), Bentonville School District (1).
