Public schools in Little Rock, Arkansas

75 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 75 public schools in Little Rock, Arkansas: 40 elementary, 15 high, 12 middle, 3 other, 3 prekindergarten, 1 ungraded, 1 secondary. 23 of them (30.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 32,591 students in 75 of them; the median school has 382 students and the largest has 2,260. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 18 school districts: Little Rock School District (35), Pulaski County Special School District (11), Estem Public Charter School (5), Lisa Academy (4), Sheridan School District (2), Ark. School For The Blind (2), Arkansas Virtual Academy (2), Premier High Schools Of Arkansas (2).
