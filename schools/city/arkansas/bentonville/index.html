Public schools in Bentonville, Arkansas

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Bentonville, Arkansas: 13 elementary, 8 middle, 5 high, 1 other. 7 of them (25.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,905 students in 27 of them; the median school has 531 students and the largest has 3,804. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Bentonville School District (19), Founders Classical Academies Of Arkansas (4), Haas Hall Academy (1), Arkansas Connections Academy (1), Hope Academy Of Northwest Arkansas (1), Northwest Ark Community College Technical Center (1).
