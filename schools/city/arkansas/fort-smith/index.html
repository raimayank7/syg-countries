Public schools in Fort Smith, Arkansas

30 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 30 public schools in Fort Smith, Arkansas: 18 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 other. 4 of them (13.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 14,097 students in 30 of them; the median school has 325 students and the largest has 2,253. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Fort Smith School District (26), Westark Technical Center (1), Haas Hall Academy (1), Premier High Schools Of Arkansas (1), Future School Of Fort Smith (1).
