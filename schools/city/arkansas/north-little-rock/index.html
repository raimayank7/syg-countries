Public schools in North Little Rock, Arkansas

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in North Little Rock, Arkansas: 14 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 5 of them (21.7%) are charter schools. The file reports 11,252 students in 23 of them; the median school has 414 students and the largest has 1,872. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: North Little Rock School District (14), Pulaski County Special School District (4), Division Of Youth Services School System (1), Arkansas Virtual Academy (1), Friendship Aspire Academies Arkansas (1), Responsive Ed Solutions Premier High School N Little Rock (1), Westwind School For Performing Arts (1).
