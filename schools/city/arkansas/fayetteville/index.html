Public schools in Fayetteville, Arkansas

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Fayetteville, Arkansas: 10 elementary, 5 middle, 4 high, 1 other. 3 of them (15.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,899 students in 20 of them; the median school has 482 students and the largest has 2,599. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Fayetteville School District (17), Lisa Academy (1), Haas Hall Academy (1), Northwest Arkansas Community College Regional Center (1).
