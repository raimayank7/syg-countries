Public schools in Dearborn Heights, Michigan

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Dearborn Heights, Michigan: 10 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 4 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 9,777 students in 21 of them; the median school has 365 students and the largest has 1,643. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Crestwood School District (6), Dearborn Heights School District #7 (6), Dearborn City School District (3), Westwood Community School District (2), Star International Academy (1), Clara B. Ford Academy (SDA) (1), Vista Meadows Academy (1), Global Heights Academy (1).
