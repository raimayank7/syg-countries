Public schools in Lake Orion, Michigan

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Lake Orion, Michigan: 6 elementary, 2 other, 2 middle, 2 high. They belong to 1 school district: Lake Orion Community Schools (12).
