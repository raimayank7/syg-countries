Public schools in Saint Clair Shores, Michigan

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Saint Clair Shores, Michigan: 9 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 8,806 students in 16 of them; the median school has 469 students and the largest has 1,441. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Lakeview Public Schools (Macomb) (6), Lake Shore Public Schools (Macomb) (6), South Lake Schools (4).
