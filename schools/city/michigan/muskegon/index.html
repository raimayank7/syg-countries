Public schools in Muskegon, Michigan

48 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 48 public schools in Muskegon, Michigan: 22 elementary, 15 high, 6 middle, 5 other. 9 of them (18.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 7 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,939 students in 48 of them; the median school has 370 students and the largest has 1,232. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 16 school districts: Muskegon Public Schools of the City of (7), Mona Shores Public School District (6), Reeths-Puffer Schools (6), Oakridge Public Schools (5), Orchard View Schools (5), Muskegon Heights Public School Academy System (3), North Muskegon Public Schools (3), Muskegon Area ISD (3).
