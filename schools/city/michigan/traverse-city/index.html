Public schools in Traverse City, Michigan

26 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 26 public schools in Traverse City, Michigan: 13 elementary, 6 high, 4 other, 2 middle, 1 ungraded. 5 of them (19.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,275 students in 26 of them; the median school has 284 students and the largest has 1,471. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 6 school districts: Traverse City Area Public Schools (16), Northwest Education Services (5), The Greenspire School (2), Woodland School (1), Grand Traverse Academy (1), Old Mission Peninsula School (1).
