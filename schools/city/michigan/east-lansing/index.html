Public schools in East Lansing, Michigan

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in East Lansing, Michigan: 8 elementary, 4 high, 1 middle. 3 of them (23.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 4,760 students in 13 of them; the median school has 274 students and the largest has 1,240. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: East Lansing School District (8), Great Lakes Learning Academy (2), Ingham ISD (2), Cole Academy (1).
