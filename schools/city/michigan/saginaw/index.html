Public schools in Saginaw, Michigan

46 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in Saginaw, Michigan: 24 elementary, 12 high, 5 other, 5 middle. 5 of them (10.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,898 students in 46 of them; the median school has 331 students and the largest has 1,324. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 11 school districts: Saginaw School District of the City of (16), Saginaw Township Community Schools (8), Carrollton Public Schools (5), Swan Valley School District (5), Saginaw ISD (4), Bridgeport-Spaulding Community School District (3), Saginaw Preparatory Academy (1), Francis Reh PSA (1).
