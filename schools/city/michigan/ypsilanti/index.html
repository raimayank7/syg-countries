Public schools in Ypsilanti, Michigan

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Ypsilanti, Michigan: 17 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 8 of them (25.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 11,158 students in 31 of them; the median school has 324 students and the largest has 888. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 13 school districts: Ypsilanti Community Schools (12), Lincoln Consolidated School District (6), Washtenaw ISD (3), Michigan Department of Corrections (1), South Arbor Charter Academy (1), Fortis Academy (1), Arbor Preparatory High School (1), East Arbor Charter Academy (1).
