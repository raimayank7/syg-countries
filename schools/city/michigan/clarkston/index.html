Public schools in Clarkston, Michigan

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Clarkston, Michigan: 6 elementary, 4 high, 2 other, 1 middle, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,367 students in 14 of them; the median school has 421 students and the largest has 1,560. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 2 school districts: Clarkston Community School District (13), Oakland Schools (1).
