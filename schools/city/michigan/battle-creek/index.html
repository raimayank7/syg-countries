Public schools in Battle Creek, Michigan

36 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 36 public schools in Battle Creek, Michigan: 18 elementary, 10 high, 6 middle, 2 other. 2 of them (5.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 13,320 students in 36 of them; the median school has 287 students and the largest has 1,239. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Battle Creek Public Schools (11), Lakeview Sch. District (Calhoun) (7), Harper Creek Community Schools (5), Pennfield Schools (5), Berrien Springs Public Schools (2), Calhoun Intermediate School District (2), Arbor Academy (1), Endeavor Charter Academy (1).
