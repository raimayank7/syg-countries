Public schools in Ann Arbor, Michigan

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Ann Arbor, Michigan: 22 elementary, 9 high, 6 other, 5 middle. 4 of them (9.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 18,742 students in 42 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,683. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Ann Arbor Public Schools (32), Washtenaw ISD (6), Honey Creek Community School (1), Central Academy (1), Washtenaw Technical Middle College (1), Multicultural Academy (1).
