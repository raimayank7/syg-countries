Public schools in Rochester Hills, Michigan

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Rochester Hills, Michigan: 10 elementary, 9 high, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 13,347 students in 20 of them; the median school has 527 students and the largest has 1,671. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Rochester Community School District (17), Avondale School District (3).
