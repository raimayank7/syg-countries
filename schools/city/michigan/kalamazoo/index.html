Public schools in Kalamazoo, Michigan

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Kalamazoo, Michigan: 26 elementary, 17 high, 6 middle, 2 other, 1 ungraded. 3 of them (5.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 special education schools. The file reports 17,021 students in 52 of them; the median school has 276 students and the largest has 1,767. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 10 school districts: Kalamazoo Public Schools (31), Parchment School District (6), Comstock Public Schools (5), Kalamazoo RESA (4), Paramount Charter Academy (1), Youth Advancement Academy (1), Michigan Department of Community Health (1), Kalamazoo Covenant Academy (1).
