Public schools in Detroit, Michigan

187 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 187 public schools in Detroit, Michigan: 112 elementary, 46 high, 14 other, 13 middle, 2 ungraded. 81 of them (43.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 9 alternative schools, 5 special education schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 80,789 students in 187 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 2,485. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 56 school districts: Detroit Public Schools Community District (106), Cesar Chavez Academy (4), University Preparatory Academy (PSAD) (4), Oakland International Academy (3), Old Redford Academy (3), Covenant Schools Detroit (3), University Preparatory Science and Math (PSAD) (3), Lincoln-King Adams-Young Academy (3).
