Public schools in Dearborn, Michigan

42 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 42 public schools in Dearborn, Michigan: 24 elementary, 9 high, 7 middle, 2 other. 9 of them (21.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 22,348 students in 42 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 2,092. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Dearborn City School District (33), Riverside Academy (3), Academy for Business and Technology (1), Henry Ford Academy (1), The Dearborn Academy (1), West Village Academy (1), Advanced Technology Academy (1), SER YouthBuild Learning Academy (1).
