Public schools in Burton, Michigan

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Burton, Michigan: 7 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (18.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 4,235 students in 16 of them; the median school has 238 students and the largest has 697. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Bendle Public Schools (4), Bentley Community School District (4), Atherton Community Schools (3), Burton Glen Charter Academy (1), Madison Academy (1), Flex High School of Michigan (1), Carman-Ainsworth Community Schools (1), Kearsley Community School District (1).
