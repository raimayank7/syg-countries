Public schools in Grand Rapids, Michigan

135 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 135 public schools in Grand Rapids, Michigan: 63 elementary, 39 high, 19 middle, 13 other, 1 secondary. 21 of them (15.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 15 alternative schools, 9 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 49,708 students in 135 of them; the median school has 336 students and the largest has 2,087. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 29 school districts: Grand Rapids Public Schools (51), Kentwood Public Schools (15), Forest Hills Public Schools (11), Northview Public Schools (8), Kent ISD (8), Kelloggsville Public Schools (6), East Grand Rapids Public Schools (5), Kenowa Hills Public Schools (5).
