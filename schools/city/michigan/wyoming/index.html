Public schools in Wyoming, Michigan

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Wyoming, Michigan: 10 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (5.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,834 students in 19 of them; the median school has 430 students and the largest has 1,283. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Wyoming Public Schools (7), Godfrey-Lee Public Schools (5), Grandville Public Schools (2), Vanguard Charter Academy (1), Godwin Heights Public Schools (1), Kelloggsville Public Schools (1), Vestaburg Community Schools (1), Kent ISD (1).
