Public schools in Warren, Michigan

43 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in Warren, Michigan: 22 elementary, 11 high, 7 middle, 3 other. 7 of them (16.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 19,285 students in 43 of them; the median school has 427 students and the largest has 1,343. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Warren Consolidated Schools (12), Warren Woods Public Schools (7), Fitzgerald Public Schools (6), Van Dyke Public Schools (6), Center Line Public Schools (3), Michigan Mathematics and Science Academy (2), Macomb ISD (2), Michigan Collegiate (1).
