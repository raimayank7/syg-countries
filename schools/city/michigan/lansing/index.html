Public schools in Lansing, Michigan

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Lansing, Michigan: 23 elementary, 10 high, 4 middle, 3 other, 1 secondary. 6 of them (14.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,684 students in 41 of them; the median school has 343 students and the largest has 1,090. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Lansing Public School District (26), Waverly Community Schools (6), Ingham ISD (2), Cole Academy (1), Mid-Michigan Leadership Academy (1), Windemere Park Charter Academy (1), Lansing Charter Academy (1), Blended Learning Academies Credit Recovery High School (1).
