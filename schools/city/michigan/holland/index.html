Public schools in Holland, Michigan

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Holland, Michigan: 16 elementary, 7 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 1 ungraded. 5 of them (17.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,759 students in 29 of them; the median school has 390 students and the largest has 2,185. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 8 school districts: West Ottawa Public School District (12), Holland City School District (7), Ottawa Area ISD (3), Black River Public School (2), Hamilton Community Schools (2), Vanderbilt Charter Academy (1), Eagle Crest Charter Academy (1), Lighthouse Academy (1).
