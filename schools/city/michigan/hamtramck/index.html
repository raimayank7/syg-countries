Public schools in Hamtramck, Michigan

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Hamtramck, Michigan: 9 elementary, 5 high, 1 other, 1 middle. 8 of them (50.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 6,430 students in 16 of them; the median school has 364 students and the largest has 965. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Hamtramck School District of the City of (8), Bridge Academy (2), Commonwealth Community Development Academy (1), Oakland International Academy (1), Hamtramck Academy (1), Hanley International Academy (1), Capstone Academy Charter School (SDA) (1), Caniff Liberty Academy (1).
