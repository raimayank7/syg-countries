Public schools in Jackson, Michigan

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Jackson, Michigan: 17 high, 14 elementary, 5 other, 3 middle. 4 of them (10.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 13 alternative schools, 3 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,312 students in 39 of them; the median school has 264 students and the largest has 1,027. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Jackson Public Schools (13), Michigan Department of Corrections (5), Northwest Community Schools (5), Jackson ISD (5), East Jackson Community Schools (3), Vandercook Lake Public Schools (3), Da Vinci Schools (2), Paragon Charter Academy (1).
