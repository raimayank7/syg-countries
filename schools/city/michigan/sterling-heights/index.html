Public schools in Sterling Heights, Michigan

33 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in Sterling Heights, Michigan: 20 elementary, 6 middle, 6 high, 1 other. 1 of them (3.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 17,988 students in 33 of them; the median school has 468 students and the largest has 1,951. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Utica Community Schools (21), Warren Consolidated Schools (9), Macomb ISD (2), Noor International Academy (1).
