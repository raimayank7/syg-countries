Public schools in Flint, Michigan

54 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 54 public schools in Flint, Michigan: 20 elementary, 17 high, 10 other, 6 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 12 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 11 alternative schools, 7 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,481 students in 54 of them; the median school has 209 students and the largest has 994. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 19 school districts: Flint School District of the City of (14), Carman-Ainsworth Community Schools (8), Kearsley Community School District (5), Westwood Heights Schools (5), Genesee ISD (5), Mt. Morris Consolidated Schools (3), Richfield Public School Academy (2), Northridge Academy (1).
