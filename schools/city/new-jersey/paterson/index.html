Public schools in Paterson, New Jersey

49 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in Paterson, New Jersey: 33 elementary, 9 high, 3 other, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 6 of them (12.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 27,797 students in 49 of them; the median school has 488 students and the largest has 2,087. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Paterson Public School District (43), Paterson Charter School for Science and Technology (1), Community Charter School of Paterson (1), Paterson Arts and Science Charter School (1), Philip's Academy Charter School of Paterson (1), College Achieve Paterson Charter School (1), Brilla New Jersey Charter School (1).
