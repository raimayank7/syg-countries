Public schools in Hamilton, New Jersey

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Hamilton, New Jersey: 18 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools. The file reports 12,663 students in 27 of them; the median school has 321 students and the largest has 1,454. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Hamilton Township Public School District (23), Mercer County Special Services School District (4).
