Public schools in Cherry Hill, New Jersey

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Cherry Hill, New Jersey: 13 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 10,651 students in 20 of them; the median school has 405 students and the largest has 2,093. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Cherry Hill School District (19), Office Of Education Department Of Children And Families (1).
