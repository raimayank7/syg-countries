Public schools in Atlantic City, New Jersey

11 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in Atlantic City, New Jersey: 9 elementary, 1 high, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 6,258 students in 11 of them; the median school has 538 students and the largest has 1,699. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Atlantic City School District (11).
