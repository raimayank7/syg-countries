Public schools in Newark, New Jersey

85 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 85 public schools in Newark, New Jersey: 51 elementary, 21 high, 8 other, 2 middle, 2 secondary, 1 prekindergarten. 15 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 4 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 62,531 students in 85 of them; the median school has 562 students and the largest has 6,476. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 21 school districts: Newark Public School District (64), Essex County Schools of Technology (2), Discovery Charter School (1), Marion P. Thomas Charter School (1), New Horizons Community Charter School (1), North Star Academy Charter School (1), Robert Treat Academy Charter School (1), Maria L. Varisco-Rogers Charter School (1).
