Public schools in Freehold, New Jersey

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Freehold, New Jersey: 9 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,415 students in 16 of them; the median school has 453 students and the largest has 1,407. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Freehold Township School District (8), Freehold Borough School District (3), Howell Township Public School District (2), Monmouth County Vocational School District (2), Freehold Regional High School District (1).
