Public schools in Paramus, New Jersey

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Paramus, New Jersey: 7 other, 5 high, 5 elementary, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 7 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,857 students in 19 of them; the median school has 201 students and the largest has 1,125. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Paramus Public School District (8), Bergen County Special Services School District (6), Bergen County Vocational Technical School District (3), Office Of Education Department Of Children And Families (1), Bergen Co Jdc (1).
