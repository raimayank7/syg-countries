Public schools in East Orange, New Jersey

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in East Orange, New Jersey: 15 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high. 2 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. The file reports 9,392 students in 22 of them; the median school has 328 students and the largest has 1,751. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: East Orange School District (20), East Orange Community Charter School (1), Pride Academy Charter School District (1).
