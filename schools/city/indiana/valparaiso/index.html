Public schools in Valparaiso, Indiana

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Valparaiso, Indiana: 15 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 10,756 students in 24 of them; the median school has 383 students and the largest has 2,151. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Valparaiso Community Schools (11), East Porter County School Corp (4), Union Township School Corp (4), Portage Township Schools (2), Duneland School Corporation (1), Porter County Education Services (1), Porter Township School Corp (1).
