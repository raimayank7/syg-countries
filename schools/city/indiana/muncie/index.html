Public schools in Muncie, Indiana

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Muncie, Indiana: 9 elementary, 4 other, 4 high, 3 middle. 1 of them (5.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,036 students in 20 of them; the median school has 455 students and the largest has 1,348. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Muncie Community Schools (12), Delaware Community School Corp (3), Cowan Community School Corp (2), Burris Laboratory School (1), Indiana Academy for Sci Math Hmn (1), Inspire Academy - A Sch of Inquiry (1).
