Public schools in Bloomington, Indiana

27 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in Bloomington, Indiana: 16 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 2 prekindergarten. 2 of them (7.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,928 students in 27 of them; the median school has 385 students and the largest has 1,670. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Monroe County Community Sch Corp (23), Richland-Bean Blossom C S C (2), The Bloomington Project School (1), Excel Center - Bloomington (1).
