Public schools in Indianapolis, Indiana

241 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 241 public schools in Indianapolis, Indiana: 145 elementary, 46 high, 32 middle, 14 other, 3 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 70 of them (29.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 career and technical schools, 3 special education schools. The file reports 153,328 students in 241 of them; the median school has 492 students and the largest has 5,475. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 79 school districts: Indianapolis Public Schools (48), Perry Township Schools (21), MSD Wayne Township (18), MSD Lawrence Township (17), MSD Warren Township (16), MSD Washington Township (14), MSD Pike Township (13), Franklin Township Com Sch Corp (11).
