Public schools in Gary, Indiana

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Gary, Indiana: 9 elementary, 5 other, 5 high, 3 middle. 8 of them (36.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,527 students in 22 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 1,213. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Gary Community School Corp (11), Lake Ridge New Tech Schools (3), Thea Bowman Leadership Academy (1), Charter School of the Dunes (1), 21st Century Charter Sch of Gary (1), Gary Lighthouse Charter School (1), Aspire Charter Academy (1), Gary Middle College (1).
