Public schools in Fishers, Indiana

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Fishers, Indiana: 12 elementary, 7 middle, 2 high. The file reports 20,670 students in 21 of them; the median school has 788 students and the largest has 3,664. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Hamilton Southeastern Schools (21).
