Public schools in Evansville, Indiana

41 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 41 public schools in Evansville, Indiana: 23 elementary, 7 high, 7 middle, 3 other, 1 prekindergarten. 2 of them (4.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,075 students in 41 of them; the median school has 445 students and the largest has 1,643. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Evansville Vanderburgh School Corp (39), Signature School Inc (1), Joshua Academy (1).
