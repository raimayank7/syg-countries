Public schools in Mishawaka, Indiana

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Mishawaka, Indiana: 12 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 12,866 students in 18 of them; the median school has 496 students and the largest has 3,847. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: School City of Mishawaka (10), Penn-Harris-Madison School Corp (8).
