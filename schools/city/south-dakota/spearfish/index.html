Public schools in Spearfish, South Dakota

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Spearfish, South Dakota: 3 elementary, 3 middle, 3 high. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools. The file reports 2,402 students in 9 of them; the median school has 166 students and the largest has 771. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Spearfish School District 40-2 (7), Black Hills Special Services Cooperative (2).
