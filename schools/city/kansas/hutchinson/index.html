Public schools in Hutchinson, Kansas

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Hutchinson, Kansas: 8 elementary, 4 middle, 1 high, 1 other. The file reports 5,585 students in 14 of them; the median school has 308 students and the largest has 1,283. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Hutchinson Public Schools (10), Buhler (3), Nickerson (1).
