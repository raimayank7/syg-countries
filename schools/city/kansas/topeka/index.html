Public schools in Topeka, Kansas

43 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 43 public schools in Topeka, Kansas: 25 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 2 other. 1 of them (2.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 21,697 students in 43 of them; the median school has 427 students and the largest has 1,885. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Topeka Public Schools (27), Auburn-Washburn (7), Seaman (7), Department of Corrections (1), Shawnee Heights (1).
