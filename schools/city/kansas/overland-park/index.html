Public schools in Overland Park, Kansas

47 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 47 public schools in Overland Park, Kansas: 30 elementary, 9 middle, 8 high. The file reports 29,336 students in 47 of them; the median school has 510 students and the largest has 1,618. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Blue Valley (29), Shawnee Mission Pub Sch (16), Olathe (2).
