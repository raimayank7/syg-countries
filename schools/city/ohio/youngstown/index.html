Public schools in Youngstown, Ohio

39 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in Youngstown, Ohio: 19 elementary, 12 high, 7 middle, 1 other. 10 of them (25.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 15,786 students in 39 of them; the median school has 338 students and the largest has 1,230. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Youngstown City (15), Boardman Local (6), Austintown Local Schools (4), Liberty Local (4), Youngstown Community School (1), Summit Academy-Youngstown (1), Stambaugh Charter Academy (1), Summit Academy Secondary - Youngstown (1).
