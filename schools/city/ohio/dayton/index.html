Public schools in Dayton, Ohio

84 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 84 public schools in Dayton, Ohio: 45 elementary, 22 high, 8 middle, 4 prekindergarten, 4 other, 1 secondary. 23 of them (27.4%) are charter schools. The file reports 30,339 students in 84 of them; the median school has 363 students and the largest has 1,124. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 34 school districts: Dayton City (28), Mad River Local (9), Oakwood City (5), Centerville City (4), Northridge Local (4), Jefferson Township Local (3), Kettering City School District (2), Vandalia-Butler City (2).
