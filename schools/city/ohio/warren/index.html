Public schools in Warren, Ohio

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Warren, Ohio: 11 elementary, 7 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (19.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,272 students in 21 of them; the median school has 392 students and the largest has 1,174. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Warren City (6), Howland Local (5), Champion Local (3), Lordstown Local (2), River Gate High School (1), Summit Academy Alternative LearnersWarren Middle & Secondary (1), Summit Academy Community School-Warren (1), STEAM Academy of Warren (1).
