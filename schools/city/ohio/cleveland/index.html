Public schools in Cleveland, Ohio

152 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 152 public schools in Cleveland, Ohio: 96 elementary, 37 high, 13 other, 6 middle. 53 of them (34.9%) are charter schools. The file reports 52,853 students in 152 of them; the median school has 315 students and the largest has 1,574. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 57 school districts: Cleveland Municipal (93), Mayfield City (4), Constellation Schools: Old Brooklyn Community Elementary (1), Green Inspiration Academy (1), Horizon Science Acad Cleveland (1), Invictus High School (1), Intergenerational School The (1), Constellation Schools: Westpark Community Elementary (1).
