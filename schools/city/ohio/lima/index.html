Public schools in Lima, Ohio

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Lima, Ohio: 11 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle. 2 of them (9.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 9,023 students in 22 of them; the median school has 366 students and the largest has 1,000. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Lima City (9), Bath Local (4), Shawnee Local (4), Perry Local (2), West Central Learning Academy II (1), Heir Force Community School (1), Apollo (1).
