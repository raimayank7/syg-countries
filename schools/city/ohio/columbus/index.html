Public schools in Columbus, Ohio

230 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 230 public schools in Columbus, Ohio: 132 elementary, 47 high, 31 middle, 14 other, 5 prekindergarten, 1 secondary. 75 of them (32.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 89,402 students in 230 of them; the median school has 335 students and the largest has 2,084. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 86 school districts: Columbus City Schools District (116), South-Western City (11), Worthington City (6), Hamilton Local (5), Groveport Madison Local (5), Grandview Heights Schools (3), Westerville City (3), Ohio Deaf and Blind Education Services (3).
