Public schools in Findlay, Ohio

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Findlay, Ohio: 7 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (6.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,408 students in 16 of them; the median school has 380 students and the largest has 2,050. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Findlay City (11), Liberty-Benton Local (4), Findlay Digital Academy (1).
