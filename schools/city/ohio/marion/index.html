Public schools in Marion, Ohio

19 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 19 public schools in Marion, Ohio: 9 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (15.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,377 students in 19 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 2,159. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Marion City (8), Elgin Local (3), Pleasant Local (3), TRECA Digital Academy (1), Marion Preparatory Academy (1), Buckeye Community School - Marion (1), River Valley Local (1), Tri-Rivers (1).
