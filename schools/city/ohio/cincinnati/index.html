Public schools in Cincinnati, Ohio

176 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 176 public schools in Cincinnati, Ohio: 102 elementary, 39 high, 18 middle, 10 other, 7 prekindergarten. 26 of them (14.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 99,541 students in 176 of them; the median school has 515 students and the largest has 2,500. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 44 school districts: Cincinnati Public Schools (66), Princeton City (11), Northwest Local (11), Forest Hills Local (9), Oak Hills Local (9), Winton Woods City (6), Sycamore Community City (6), Mt Healthy City (5).
