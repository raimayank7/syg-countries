Public schools in Middletown, Ohio

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in Middletown, Ohio: 10 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle. 4 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,611 students in 18 of them; the median school has 403 students and the largest has 1,649. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Middletown City (10), Madison Local (3), Summit Acdy Comm Schl for Alternative Learners of Middletown (1), Marshall High School (1), Middletown Preparatory & Fitness Academy (1), Summit Academy Secondary School - Middletown (1), Warren County Vocational School (1).
