Public schools in Mansfield, Ohio

20 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in Mansfield, Ohio: 10 elementary, 4 high, 3 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. 4 of them (20.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 8,568 students in 20 of them; the median school has 421 students and the largest has 882. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Mansfield City (8), Madison Local (6), Ontario Local (2), GOAL Digital Academy (1), Foundation Academy (1), The Richland School of Academic Arts (1), Cypress High School (1).
