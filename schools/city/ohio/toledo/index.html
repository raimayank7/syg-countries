Public schools in Toledo, Ohio

98 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 98 public schools in Toledo, Ohio: 68 elementary, 17 high, 7 other, 3 prekindergarten, 2 middle, 1 secondary. 26 of them (26.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools. The file reports 37,619 students in 98 of them; the median school has 344 students and the largest has 2,229. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 31 school districts: Toledo City (57), Washington Local (10), Sylvania Schools (2), Ottawa Hills Local (2), Autism Model School (1), Toledo School For The Arts (1), The Autism Academy Of Learning (1), Glass City Academy (1).
