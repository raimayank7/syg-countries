Public schools in Westerville, Ohio

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Westerville, Ohio: 14 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. The file reports 14,171 students in 23 of them; the median school has 481 students and the largest has 1,542. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Westerville City (21), Cornerstone Academy Community School (1), Worthington City (1).
