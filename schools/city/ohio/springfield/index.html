Public schools in Springfield, Ohio

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Springfield, Ohio: 15 elementary, 9 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (10.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 14,528 students in 28 of them; the median school has 413 students and the largest has 1,602. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 9 school districts: Springfield City School District (17), Northeastern Local (2), Northwestern Local (2), Clark-Shawnee Local (2), Cliff Park High School (1), Springfield Preparatory and Fitness Academy (1), Global Impact STEM Academy (1), Springfield-Clark County (1).
