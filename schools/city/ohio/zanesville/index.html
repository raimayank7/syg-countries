Public schools in Zanesville, Ohio

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Zanesville, Ohio: 7 elementary, 6 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 7,742 students in 17 of them; the median school has 466 students and the largest has 938. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Zanesville City (5), Maysville Local (4), West Muskingum Local (3), Foxfire High School (1), Zanesville Community High School (1), Foxfire Intermediate School (1), East Muskingum Local (1), Mid-East Career and Technology Centers (1).
