Public schools in Canton, Ohio

40 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 40 public schools in Canton, Ohio: 23 elementary, 8 middle, 7 high, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 7 of them (17.5%) are charter schools. The file reports 18,332 students in 40 of them; the median school has 329 students and the largest has 2,130. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Canton City (18), Plain Local (9), Canton Local (3), Perry Local (2), Summit Academy Community School for Alternative Learn-Canton (1), Stark High School (1), Canton Harbor High School (1), Summit Academy Secondary - Canton (1).
