Public schools in Caldwell, Idaho

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Caldwell, Idaho: 13 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 3 other. 6 of them (21.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools. The file reports 14,280 students in 28 of them; the median school has 491 students and the largest has 1,394. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 10 school districts: Caldwell District (11), Vallivue School District (8), Notus District (2), Vision Charter School Inc. (1), Heritage Community Charter School Inc. (1), Elevate Academy Inc. (1), Mosaics Public School Inc. (1), Thomas Jefferson Charter School Inc. (1).
