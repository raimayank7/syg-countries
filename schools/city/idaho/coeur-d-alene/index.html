Public schools in Coeur D'Alene, Idaho

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Coeur D'Alene, Idaho: 7 elementary, 5 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 2 of them (11.8%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 8,924 students in 17 of them; the median school has 375 students and the largest has 1,503. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Coeur D'Alene District (15), The Kootenai Bridge Academy Inc. (1), Coeur D'Alene Charter Academy Inc. (1).
