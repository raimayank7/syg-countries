Public schools in Meridian, Idaho

49 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 49 public schools in Meridian, Idaho: 20 elementary, 15 high, 7 other, 7 middle. 13 of them (26.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 6 alternative schools, 3 career and technical schools. The file reports 29,982 students in 49 of them; the median school has 477 students and the largest has 2,521. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 12 school districts: Joint School District No. 2 (36), Idaho Virtual Academy Inc. (2), Kuna Joint District (2), Compass Public Charter School Inc. (1), The Village Charter School Inc. (1), Idaho College And Career Readiness Academy Inc. (1), Gem Prep: Online Llc (1), Gem Prep: Meridian Llc (1).
