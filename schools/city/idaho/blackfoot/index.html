Public schools in Blackfoot, Idaho

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Blackfoot, Idaho: 10 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (13.0%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 7,737 students in 23 of them; the median school has 250 students and the largest has 1,243. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Blackfoot District (11), Snake River District (9), Idaho Science And Technology Charter School Inc. (1), Blackfoot Charter Community Learning Center Inc. (1), Idaho Stem Academy Inc. (1).
