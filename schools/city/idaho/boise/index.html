Public schools in Boise, Idaho

81 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 81 public schools in Boise, Idaho: 48 elementary, 16 high, 12 middle, 4 other, 1 prekindergarten. 10 of them (12.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 10 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 35,829 students in 81 of them; the median school has 354 students and the largest has 1,770. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Boise Independent District (51), Joint School District No. 2 (16), Inspire Academics Inc. (2), Isucceed Virtual High School Inc. (2), Rolling Hills Public Charter School Inc. (1), Idaho Department Of Corrections (1), Idaho Virtual Education Partners Inc. (1), The Sage International School Of Boise A Public Charter Sch (1).
