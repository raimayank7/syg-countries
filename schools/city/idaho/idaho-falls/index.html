Public schools in Idaho Falls, Idaho

45 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 45 public schools in Idaho Falls, Idaho: 23 elementary, 12 high, 5 middle, 4 other, 1 secondary. 4 of them (8.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 21,544 students in 45 of them; the median school has 448 students and the largest has 1,731. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Idaho Falls District (22), Bonneville Joint District (19), Taylor'S Crossing Public Charter School Inc. (1), American Heritage Charter School Inc. (1), Alturas International Academy Inc. (1), Alturas Preparatory Academy Inc (1).
