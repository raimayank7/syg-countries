Public schools in Newark, Delaware

29 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in Newark, Delaware: 15 elementary, 5 other, 4 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (10.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,110 students in 28 of them; the median school has 455 students and the largest has 3,047. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Christina School District (23), Red Clay Consolidated School District (2), Newark Charter School (1), Las Americas Aspira Academy (1), Academia Antonia Alonso (1), New Castle County Vocational-Technical School District (1).
