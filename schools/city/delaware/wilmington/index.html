Public schools in Wilmington, Delaware

53 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 53 public schools in Wilmington, Delaware: 27 elementary, 14 high, 5 middle, 4 other, 2 prekindergarten. 10 of them (18.9%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 31,253 students in 52 of them; the median school has 521 students and the largest has 2,192. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 14 school districts: Red Clay Consolidated School District (22), Brandywine School District (14), Christina School District (5), New Castle County Vocational-Technical School District (2), Charter School of Wilmington (1), East Side Charter School (1), Edison (Thomas A.) Charter School (1), Kuumba Academy Charter School (1).
