Greece

Greece is a country in Southern Europe. Its official name is Hellenic Republic. In the language of the place it is called Ελλάδα. The capital is Athens.

Land and position

Greece covers about 131,940 square kilometres. By area it ranks 98th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Nepal and a little larger than Nicaragua. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Albania, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Türkiye. Its centre lies at about 39° north, 22° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 10.4 million people live in Greece (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 94th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.13 percent of the world's people. On average there are 79 people to each square kilometre. About 79 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 82 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Modern Greek (1453-), English, French. A person from Greece is called Greek.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 281 billion (2025), which is about US$ 26,948 for each person (2025).

Time

Greece uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Europe/Athens). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
