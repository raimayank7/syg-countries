HS code chapters

Chapters 01 to 97 of the tariff.

The tariff is arranged in 97 chapters (chapter 77 is reserved), grouped into 21 sections from live animals to works of art. Every code starts with its chapter number: 0901 is in chapter 09, coffee, tea and spices. Pick a chapter to see its headings, or search by word on the home page. The US tariff also has chapters 98 and 99 for special provisions and temporary measures; they are not covered here.

How to use this page

A chapter is the first two digits of every tariff code, so finding the chapter is the first step in classifying goods. The table lists all chapters with the number of headings and tariff lines under each, which also shows where the tariff is detailed: chapters with many lines hold fine distinctions, while chapters for a single commodity are short. Open a chapter to see its headings, the share of lines that are duty-free and how the general rates are spread. The chapter names here are short summaries, so read the official headings before you decide a code.
