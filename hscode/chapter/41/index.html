Chapter 41: Raw hides, skins and leather

11 headings, 121 tariff lines.

Chapter 41 of the US tariff covers Raw hides, skins and leather. It has 11 headings and 121 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 22% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 5% and the median percentage rate is 2.8%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
