Chapter 39: Plastics and plastic articles

26 headings, 236 tariff lines.

Chapter 39 of the US tariff covers Plastics and plastic articles. It has 26 headings and 236 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 13% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 6.5% and the median percentage rate is 5.3%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
