Chapter 27: Mineral fuels and oils

16 headings, 76 tariff lines.

Chapter 27 of the US tariff covers Mineral fuels and oils. It has 16 headings and 76 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 54% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 7% and the median percentage rate is 7%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
