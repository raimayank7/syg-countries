Chapter 69: Ceramic products

13 headings, 82 tariff lines.

Chapter 69 of the US tariff covers Ceramic products. It has 13 headings and 82 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 27% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 28% and the median percentage rate is 8.5%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
