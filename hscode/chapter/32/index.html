Chapter 32: Dyeing extracts, paints and inks

15 headings, 107 tariff lines.

Chapter 32 of the US tariff covers Dyeing extracts, paints and inks. It has 15 headings and 107 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 19% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 6.5% and the median percentage rate is 6.5%.
