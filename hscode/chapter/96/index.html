Chapter 96: Miscellaneous manufactured articles

20 headings, 127 tariff lines.

Chapter 96 of the US tariff covers Miscellaneous manufactured articles. It has 20 headings and 127 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 20% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 32% and the median percentage rate is 5.3%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
