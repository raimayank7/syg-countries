Chapter 70: Glass and glassware

19 headings, 198 tariff lines.

Chapter 70 of the US tariff covers Glass and glassware. It has 19 headings and 198 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 27% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 38% and the median percentage rate is 6.5%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
