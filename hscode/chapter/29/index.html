Chapter 29: Organic chemicals

42 headings, 1,051 tariff lines.

Chapter 29 of the US tariff covers Organic chemicals. It has 42 headings and 1,051 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 26% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 6.5% and the median percentage rate is 5.8%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.
