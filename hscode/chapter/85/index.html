Chapter 85: Electrical machinery and electronics

48 headings, 624 tariff lines.

Chapter 85 of the US tariff covers Electrical machinery and electronics. It has 48 headings and 624 eight-digit tariff lines.

Of the lines with a general rate, 58% are duty-free; the highest percentage rate is 15% and the median percentage rate is 3.1%. The table lists the headings with their descriptions; open one to see its subheadings and rates. Chapter names here are our own short wording; the official descriptions are those of the headings.

How the general rates are spread

Among the 624 tariff lines of this chapter, 362 are listed as free, 240 carry a percentage rate of 5% or less, 15 from more than 5% up to 10%, 7 from more than 10% up to 20%, and 0 a rate above 20%. 0 have a rate that is not a simple percentage, for example an amount per unit, and 0 list no general rate of their own. The rates are those of the general column of the tariff: they are not the rate you will pay in every case, because trade agreements, special programmes and additional measures can change the duty.

Quantity units and special rates

The tariff names the unit in which each line is counted. In this chapter the units used most are No. (407 lines), kg (57 lines), W (9 lines), m2 (5 lines). 262 of the lines also list rates under special programmes in the tariff's "special" column. The headings with the most tariff lines are 8528 (70), 8501 (48), 8529 (34), so the detail of the chapter is concentrated there.
