Chapter 03, Fish and seafood.

Heading 0305 in chapter 03 (Fish and seafood) covers: Fish, dried, salted or in brine; smoked fish, whether or not cooked before or during the smoking process

The US tariff splits it into 22 subheadings and 35 eight-digit tariff lines (and 26 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 35 of the 35 tariff lines have a general rate: 25 are duty-free, 10 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 0.5% to 7.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 03.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 7.5% on 0305.20.20.00 (Fish, dried, salted or in brine; smoked fish, whether or not cooked before or during the smoking process > Liv) and the lowest is 0.5% on 0305.64.50.00 (Fish, salted but not dried or smoked and fish in brine, other than edible fish offal > Tilapias (Oreochromis s).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (28 lines). 10 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 0305.20 Livers, roes and milt of fish, dried, smoked, salted or in b; 0305.39 Other; 0305.49 Other; 0305.61 Herrings (Clupea harengus, Clupea pallasii); 0305.63 Anchovies (Engraulis spp.); 0305.64 Tilapias (Oreochromis spp.), catfish (Pangasius spp., Siluru; 0305.69 Other; 0305.31 Fish fillets, dried, salted or in brine, but not smoked and more.
