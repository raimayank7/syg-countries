HS heading 2939: Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their salts, ethers, esters a

Chapter 29, Organic chemicals.

Heading 2939 in chapter 29 (Organic chemicals) covers: Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their salts, ethers, esters and other derivatives

The US tariff splits it into 19 subheadings and 21 eight-digit tariff lines (and 6 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 21 of the 21 tariff lines have a general rate: 20 are duty-free, 1 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.5% to 6.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 29.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6.5% on 2939.80.00 (Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their salts, ethers, esters and other derivatives > Other) and the lowest is 6.5% on 2939.80.00 (Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their salts, ethers, esters and other derivatives > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (17 lines), g (1 lines). 1 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 2939.19 Other; 2939.11 Alkaloids of opium and their derivatives; salts thereof; 2939.20 Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their sal; 2939.30 Alkaloids, natural or reproduced by synthesis, and their sal; 2939.41 Alkaloids of ephedra and their derivatives; salts thereof; 2939.42 Alkaloids of ephedra and their derivatives; salts thereof; 2939.43 Alkaloids of ephedra and their derivatives; salts thereof; 2939.44 Alkaloids of ephedra and their derivatives; salts thereof and more.
