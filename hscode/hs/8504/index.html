HS heading 8504: Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rectifiers) and inducto

Chapter 85, Electrical machinery and electronics.

Heading 8504 in chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics) covers: Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rectifiers) and inductors; parts thereof

The US tariff splits it into 11 subheadings and 22 eight-digit tariff lines (and 41 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 22 of the 22 tariff lines have a general rate: 15 are duty-free, 7 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 1.6% to 6.6%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 85.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6.6% on 8504.31.40 (Having a power handling capacity less than 1 kVA) and the lowest is 1.6% on 8504.31.60.00 (Having a power handling capacity of 1 kVA).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (13 lines), kg (2 lines). 7 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8504.31 Having a power handling capacity not exceeding 1 kVA; 8504.40 Static converters; 8504.50 Other inductors; 8504.90 Parts; 8504.10 Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rec; 8504.21 Liquid dielectric transformers; 8504.22 Liquid dielectric transformers; 8504.23 Liquid dielectric transformers and more.
