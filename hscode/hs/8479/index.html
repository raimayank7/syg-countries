HS heading 8479: Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functions, not specified or

Chapter 84, Machinery and mechanical appliances.

Heading 8479 in chapter 84 (Machinery and mechanical appliances) covers: Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functions, not specified or included elsewhere in this chapter; parts thereof

The US tariff splits it into 13 subheadings and 26 eight-digit tariff lines (and 19 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 26 of the 26 tariff lines have a general rate: 20 are duty-free, 6 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2.5% to 2.8%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 84.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 2.8% on 8479.89.55.00 (Trash compactors) and the lowest is 2.5% on 8479.89.95 (Other machines and mechanical appliances > Other > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (22 lines). 6 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8479.89 Other; 8479.90 Parts; 8479.10 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio; 8479.20 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio; 8479.30 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio; 8479.40 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio; 8479.50 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio; 8479.60 Machines and mechanical appliances having individual functio and more.
