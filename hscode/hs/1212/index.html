HS heading 1212: Locust beans, seaweeds and other algae, sugar beet and sugar cane, fresh, chille

Chapter 12, Oil seeds and miscellaneous grains.

Heading 1212 in chapter 12 (Oil seeds and miscellaneous grains) covers: Locust beans, seaweeds and other algae, sugar beet and sugar cane, fresh, chilled, frozen or dried, whether or not ground; fruit stones and kernels and other vegetable products (including unroasted chicory roots of the variety Cichorium intybus sativum) of a kind used primarily for human consumption, not elsewhere specified or included

The US tariff splits it into 7 subheadings and 9 eight-digit tariff lines. 9 of the 9 tariff lines have a general rate: 6 are duty-free, 3 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 12.

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (7 lines), t (2 lines). 3 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 1212.99 Other; 1212.21 Seaweeds and other algae; 1212.29 Seaweeds and other algae; 1212.91 Other; 1212.92 Other; 1212.93 Other; 1212.94 Other.
