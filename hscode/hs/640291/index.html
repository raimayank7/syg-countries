HS code 6402.91: Covering the ankle

US tariff, 13 lines.

Subheading 6402.91 covers Covering the ankle (Other footwear with outer soles and uppers of rubber or plastics > Other footwear > Covering the ankle). It is in heading 6402: Other footwear with outer soles and uppers of rubber or plastics, chapter 64 (Footwear).

The US tariff has 13 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 32 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 13 of the 13 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 9 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6% to 48%, 4 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram). 13 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 13 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are prs. (6 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (6402.91) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 64 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 6402.91?

6402.91 is the US tariff subheading for Covering the ankle.

What is the duty rate for 6402.91?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 13 of the 13 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 9 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6% to 48%, 4 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
