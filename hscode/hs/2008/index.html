HS heading 2008: Fruit, nuts and other edible parts of plants, otherwise prepared or preserved, w

Chapter 20, Preparations of vegetables and fruit.

Heading 2008 in chapter 20 (Preparations of vegetables and fruit) covers: Fruit, nuts and other edible parts of plants, otherwise prepared or preserved, whether or not containing added sugar or other sweetening matter or spirit, not elsewhere specified or included

The US tariff splits it into 13 subheadings and 66 eight-digit tariff lines (and 39 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 66 of the 66 tariff lines have a general rate: 6 are duty-free, 39 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 0.8% to 131.8%, 21 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 20.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 131.8% on 2008.11.15.00 (Peanuts (ground-nuts) > Peanut butter and paste > Other) and the lowest is 0.8% on 2008.99.15.00 (Other > Bananas > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (50 lines). 60 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 2008.11 Peanuts (ground-nuts); 2008.19 Other, including mixtures; 2008.30 Citrus fruit; 2008.50 Apricots; 2008.70 Peaches, including nectarines; 2008.97 Mixtures; 2008.99 Other; 2008.20 Fruit, nuts and other edible parts of plants, otherwise prep and more.
