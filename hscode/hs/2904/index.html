HS heading 2904: Sulfonated, nitrated or nitrosated derivatives of hydrocarbons, whether or not h

Chapter 29, Organic chemicals.

Heading 2904 in chapter 29 (Organic chemicals) covers: Sulfonated, nitrated or nitrosated derivatives of hydrocarbons, whether or not halogenated

The US tariff splits it into 10 subheadings and 31 eight-digit tariff lines. 31 of the 31 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 30 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.7% to 5.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 29.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 5.5% on 2904.10.04.00 (2-Anthracenesulfonic acid) and the lowest is 3.7% on 2904.99.50.00 (Other > Other > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (31 lines). 30 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 2904.10 Derivatives containing only sulfo groups, their salts and et; 2904.20 Derivatives containing only nitro or only nitroso groups; 2904.99 Other; 2904.31 Perfluorooctane sulfonic acid, its salts and perfluorooctane; 2904.32 Perfluorooctane sulfonic acid, its salts and perfluorooctane; 2904.33 Perfluorooctane sulfonic acid, its salts and perfluorooctane; 2904.34 Perfluorooctane sulfonic acid, its salts and perfluorooctane; 2904.35 Perfluorooctane sulfonic acid, its salts and perfluorooctane and more.
