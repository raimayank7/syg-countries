HS code 5805.00: Hand-woven tapestries of the type Gobelins, Flanders, Aubusson, Beauvais and the like, and

US tariff, 5 lines.

Subheading 5805.00 covers Hand-woven tapestries of the type Gobelins, Flanders, Aubusson, Beauvais and the like, and needle-worked tapestries (for example, petit point, cross stitch), whether or not made up. It is in heading 5805: Hand-woven tapestries of the type Gobelins, Flanders, Aubusson, Beauvais and the like, and needle-worked tapestries (for example, petit poin, chapter 58 (Special woven fabrics, lace and embroidery).

The US tariff has 5 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 2 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 5 of the 5 tariff lines have a general rate: 5 are duty-free.

The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are m² (4 lines), kg (4 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (5805.00) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 58 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.
