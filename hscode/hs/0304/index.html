HS heading 0304: Fish fillets and other fish meat (whether or not minced), fresh, chilled or froz

Chapter 03, Fish and seafood.

Heading 0304 in chapter 03 (Fish and seafood) covers: Fish fillets and other fish meat (whether or not minced), fresh, chilled or frozen

The US tariff splits it into 48 subheadings and 64 eight-digit tariff lines (and 123 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 64 of the 64 tariff lines have a general rate: 56 are duty-free, 8 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6% to 6%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 03.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6% on 0304.91.90.00 (Other, frozen > Swordfish (Xiphias gladius) > Other) and the lowest is 6% on 0304.91.90.00 (Other, frozen > Swordfish (Xiphias gladius) > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (43 lines). 8 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 0304.71 Cod (Gadus morhua, Gadus ogac,Gadus macrocephalus); 0304.72 Haddock (Melanogrammus aeglefinus); 0304.73 Coalfish (Pollachius virens); 0304.74 Hake (Merluccius spp., Urophycis spp.); 0304.75 Alaska pollock (Theragra chalcogramma); 0304.79 Other; 0304.81 Pacific salmon (Oncorhynchus nerka, Oncorhynchus gorbuscha, ; 0304.82 Trout (Salmo trutta, Oncorhynchus mykiss, Oncorhynchus clark and more.
