HS code 8205.59: Other

US tariff, 8 lines.

Subheading 8205.59 covers Other (Handtools (including glass cutters) not elsewhere specified or included; blow torches and similar self-contained torches; vises, clamps and the like, other than accessories for and parts of machine tools or water-jet cutting machines; anvils; portable forges; hand- or pedal-operated grinding wheels with frameworks; base metal parts thereof > Other handtools (including glass cutters) and parts thereof > Other). It is in heading 8205: Handtools (including glass cutters) not elsewhere specified or included; blow torches and similar self-contained torches; vises, clamps and , chapter 82 (Tools, cutlery and spoons of base metal).

The US tariff has 8 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 5 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 8 of the 8 tariff lines have a general rate: 3 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.7% to 7.2%, 1 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram). 5 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 5 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are No. (5 lines), kg (1 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (8205.59) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 82 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 8205.59?

8205.59 is the US tariff subheading for Other.

What is the duty rate for 8205.59?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 8 of the 8 tariff lines have a general rate: 3 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.7% to 7.2%, 1 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
