HS heading 8510: Shavers, hair clippers and hair-removing appliances, with self-contained electri

Chapter 85, Electrical machinery and electronics.

Heading 8510 in chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics) covers: Shavers, hair clippers and hair-removing appliances, with self-contained electric motor; parts thereof

The US tariff splits it into 4 subheadings and 9 eight-digit tariff lines. 9 of the 9 tariff lines have a general rate: 3 are duty-free, 6 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 4% to 4.2%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 85.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 4.2% on 8510.90.55.00 (Shavers, hair clippers and hair-removing appliances, with self-contained electric motor; parts thereof > Parts) and the lowest is 4% on 8510.20.10.00 (Hair clippers to be used for agricultural or horticultural purposes).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (5 lines), kg (4 lines). 6 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8510.20 Hair clippers; 8510.90 Parts; 8510.10 Shavers, hair clippers and hair-removing appliances, with se; 8510.30 Shavers, hair clippers and hair-removing appliances, with se.
