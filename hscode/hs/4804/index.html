HS heading 4804: Uncoated kraft paper and paperboard, in rolls or sheets, other than that of head

Chapter 48, Paper and paperboard.

Heading 4804 in chapter 48 (Paper and paperboard) covers: Uncoated kraft paper and paperboard, in rolls or sheets, other than that of heading 4802 or 4803

The US tariff splits it into 12 subheadings and 18 eight-digit tariff lines (and 17 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 18 of the 18 tariff lines have a general rate: 18 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 48.

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (13 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate. The subheadings, in code order, are: 4804.31 Unbleached; 4804.39 Other; 4804.41 Unbleached; 4804.11 Kraftliner; 4804.19 Kraftliner; 4804.21 Sack kraft paper; 4804.29 Sack kraft paper; 4804.42 Other kraft paper and paperboard weighing more than 150 g/m² and more.
