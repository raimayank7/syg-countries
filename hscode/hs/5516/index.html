HS heading 5516: Woven fabrics of artificial staple fibers

Chapter 55, Man-made staple fibres.

Heading 5516 in chapter 55 (Man-made staple fibres) covers: Woven fabrics of artificial staple fibers

The US tariff splits it into 20 subheadings and 24 eight-digit tariff lines (and 100 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 24 of the 24 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 23 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 8.5% to 25%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 55.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 25% on 5516.32.05 (Containing 36 percent or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair) and the lowest is 8.5% on 5516.23.00 (Of yarns of different colors).

The quantity units asked for on entry are m² (5 lines), kg (5 lines). 23 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 5516.31 Unbleached or bleached; 5516.32 Dyed; 5516.33 Of yarns of different colors; 5516.34 Printed; 5516.11 Containing 85 percent or more by weight of artificial staple; 5516.12 Containing 85 percent or more by weight of artificial staple; 5516.13 Containing 85 percent or more by weight of artificial staple; 5516.14 Containing 85 percent or more by weight of artificial staple and more.
