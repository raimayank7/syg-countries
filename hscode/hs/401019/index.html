HS code 4010.19: Other

US tariff, 5 lines.

Subheading 4010.19 covers Other (Conveyor or transmission belts or belting, of vulcanized rubber > Conveyor belts and belting > Other). It is in heading 4010: Conveyor or transmission belts or belting, of vulcanized rubber, chapter 40 (Rubber and rubber articles).

The US tariff has 5 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading. 5 of the 5 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 5 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 1.9% to 8%. 5 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 5 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are kg (5 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (4010.19) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 40 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 4010.19?

4010.19 is the US tariff subheading for Other.

What is the duty rate for 4010.19?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 5 of the 5 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 5 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 1.9% to 8%.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
