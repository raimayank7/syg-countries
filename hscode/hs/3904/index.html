HS heading 3904: Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, in primary forms

Chapter 39, Plastics and plastic articles.

Heading 3904 in chapter 39 (Plastics and plastic articles) covers: Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, in primary forms

The US tariff splits it into 9 subheadings and 12 eight-digit tariff lines (and 2 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 12 of the 12 tariff lines have a general rate: 3 are duty-free, 9 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 5.3% to 6.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 39.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6.5% on 3904.69.50.00 (Fluoropolymers > Other > Other) and the lowest is 5.3% on 3904.30.60.00 (Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, in primary forms > Vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate co).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (11 lines). 9 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 3904.30 Vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate copolymers; 3904.69 Other; 3904.90 Other; 3904.10 Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, ; 3904.21 Other poly(vinyl chloride); 3904.22 Other poly(vinyl chloride); 3904.40 Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, ; 3904.50 Polymers of vinyl chloride or of other halogenated olefins, and more.
