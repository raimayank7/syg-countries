HS heading 0302: Fish, fresh or chilled, excluding fish fillets and other fish meat of heading 03

Chapter 03, Fish and seafood.

Heading 0302 in chapter 03 (Fish and seafood) covers: Fish, fresh or chilled, excluding fish fillets and other fish meat of heading 0304

The US tariff splits it into 45 subheadings and 59 eight-digit tariff lines (and 54 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 59 of the 59 tariff lines have a general rate: 44 are duty-free, 14 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3% to 15%, 1 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 03.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 15% on 0302.91.20.00 (Livers, roes, milt, fish fins, heads, tails, maws and other edible fish offal > Livers, roes and milt > Sturge) and the lowest is 3% on 0302.45.11.00 (Scaled (whether or not heads, viscera and/or fins have been removed, but not otherwise processed), in immediat).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (45 lines). 15 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 0302.45 Jack and horse mackerel (Trachurus spp); 0302.46 Cobia (Rachycentron canadum); 0302.54 Hake (Merluccius spp., Urophycis spp.); 0302.55 Alaska pollock (Theragra chalcogramma); 0302.56 Blue whitings (Micromesistius poutassou, Micromesistius aust; 0302.59 Other; 0302.71 Tilapias (Oreochromis spp.); 0302.72 Catfish (Pangasius spp., Silurus spp., Clarias spp., Ictalur and more.
