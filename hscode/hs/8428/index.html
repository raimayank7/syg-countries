HS heading 8428: Other lifting, handling, loading or unloading machinery (for example, elevators,

Chapter 84, Machinery and mechanical appliances.

Heading 8428 in chapter 84 (Machinery and mechanical appliances) covers: Other lifting, handling, loading or unloading machinery (for example, elevators, escalators, conveyors, teleferics)

The US tariff splits it into 10 subheadings and 10 eight-digit tariff lines (and 4 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 10 of the 10 tariff lines have a general rate: 10 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 84.

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (8 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate.
