HS heading 9101: Wrist watches, pocket watches and other watches, including stop watches, with ca

Chapter 91, Clocks and watches.

Heading 9101 in chapter 91 (Clocks and watches) covers: Wrist watches, pocket watches and other watches, including stop watches, with case of precious metal or of metal clad with precious metal

The US tariff splits it into 6 subheadings and 24 eight-digit tariff lines (and 3 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 24 of the 24 tariff lines have a general rate: 9 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.1% to 3.1%, 11 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 91.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 3.1% on 9101.21.10.00 (Of textile material or of base metal, whether or not gold- or silver-plated) and the lowest is 3.1% on 9101.21.10.00 (Of textile material or of base metal, whether or not gold- or silver-plated).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (5 lines). 15 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 9101.11 With mechanical display only; 9101.19 Other; 9101.21 With automatic winding; 9101.29 Other; 9101.91 Electrically operated; 9101.99 Other.
