HS heading 5804: Tulles and other net fabrics, not including woven, knitted or crocheted fabrics;

Chapter 58, Special woven fabrics, lace and embroidery.

Heading 5804 in chapter 58 (Special woven fabrics, lace and embroidery) covers: Tulles and other net fabrics, not including woven, knitted or crocheted fabrics; lace in the piece, in strips or in motifs, other than fabrics of headings 6002 to 6006

The US tariff splits it into 4 subheadings and 6 eight-digit tariff lines (and 7 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 6 of the 6 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 5 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 5% to 13.2%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 58.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 13.2% on 5804.30.00 (Hand-made lace) and the lowest is 5% on 5804.29.90 (Mechanically made lace > Of other textile materials > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (3 lines). 5 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes.
