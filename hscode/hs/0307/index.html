Chapter 03, Fish and seafood.

Heading 0307 in chapter 03 (Fish and seafood) covers: Molluscs, whether in shell or not, live, fresh, chilled, frozen, dried, salted or in brine; smoked molluscs, whether in shell or not, whether or not cooked before or during the smoking process

The US tariff splits it into 28 subheadings and 28 eight-digit tariff lines (and 38 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 28 of the 28 tariff lines have a general rate: 27 are duty-free, 1 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 5% to 5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 03.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 5% on 0307.60.00.00 (Snails, other than sea snails) and the lowest is 5% on 0307.60.00.00 (Snails, other than sea snails).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (16 lines). 1 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 0307.11 Oysters; 0307.12 Oysters; 0307.19 Oysters; 0307.21 Scallops and other molluscs of the family Pectinidae; 0307.22 Scallops and other molluscs of the family Pectinidae; 0307.29 Scallops and other molluscs of the family Pectinidae; 0307.31 Mussels (Mytilus spp., Perna spp.); 0307.32 Mussels (Mytilus spp., Perna spp.) and more.
