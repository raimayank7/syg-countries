HS heading 6204: Women's or girls' suits, ensembles, suit-type jackets, blazers, dresses, skirts,

Chapter 62, Woven clothing.

Heading 6204 in chapter 62 (Woven clothing) covers: Women's or girls' suits, ensembles, suit-type jackets, blazers, dresses, skirts, divided skirts, trousers, bib and brace overalls, breeches and shorts (other than swimwear)

The US tariff splits it into 25 subheadings and 86 eight-digit tariff lines (and 272 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 86 of the 86 tariff lines have a general rate: 4 are duty-free, 73 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 1% to 28.6%, 4 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 62.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 28.6% on 6204.63.11 (Other > Other > Other) and the lowest is 1% on 6204.19.40.00 (Containing 70 percent or more by weight of silk or silk waste (744)).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (28 lines), doz. (23 lines), No. (5 lines). 82 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 6204.13 Of synthetic fibers; 6204.19 Of other textile materials; 6204.22 Of cotton; 6204.29 Of other textile materials; 6204.31 Of wool or fine animal hair; 6204.32 Of cotton; 6204.33 Of synthetic fibers; 6204.39 Of other textile materials and more.
