HS code 8529.90: Other

US tariff, 31 lines.

Subheading 8529.90 covers Other (Parts suitable for use solely or principally with the apparatus of headings 8524 to 8528 > Other). It is in heading 8529: Parts suitable for use solely or principally with the apparatus of headings 8524 to 8528, chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics).

The US tariff has 31 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 14 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 31 of the 31 tariff lines have a general rate: 31 are duty-free.

The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are No. (26 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (8529.90) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 85 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 8529.90?

8529.90 is the US tariff subheading for Other.

What is the duty rate for 8529.90?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 31 of the 31 tariff lines have a general rate: 31 are duty-free.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
