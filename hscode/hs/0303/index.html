HS heading 0303: Fish, frozen, excluding fish fillets and other fish meat of heading 0304

Chapter 03, Fish and seafood.

Heading 0303 in chapter 03 (Fish and seafood) covers: Fish, frozen, excluding fish fillets and other fish meat of heading 0304

The US tariff splits it into 44 subheadings and 45 eight-digit tariff lines (and 53 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 45 of the 45 tariff lines have a general rate: 38 are duty-free, 1 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 15% to 15%, 6 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 03.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 15% on 0303.91.20.00 (Livers, roes, milt, fish fins, heads, tails, maws and other edible fish offal > Livers, roes and milt > Sturge) and the lowest is 15% on 0303.91.20.00 (Livers, roes, milt, fish fins, heads, tails, maws and other edible fish offal > Livers, roes and milt > Sturge).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (33 lines). 7 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes.
