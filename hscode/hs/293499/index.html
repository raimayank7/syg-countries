HS code 2934.99: Other

US tariff, 19 lines.

Subheading 2934.99 covers Other (Nucleic acids and their salts, whether or not chemically defined; other heterocyclic compounds > Other > Other). It is in heading 2934: Nucleic acids and their salts, whether or not chemically defined; other heterocyclic compounds, chapter 29 (Organic chemicals).

The US tariff has 19 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 4 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 19 of the 19 tariff lines have a general rate: 5 are duty-free, 14 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.7% to 6.5%. 14 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 14 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are kg (18 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (2934.99) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 29 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 2934.99?

2934.99 is the US tariff subheading for Other.

What is the duty rate for 2934.99?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 19 of the 19 tariff lines have a general rate: 5 are duty-free, 14 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.7% to 6.5%.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
