HS heading 8701: Tractors (other than tractors of heading 8709)

Chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway.

Heading 8701 in chapter 87 (Vehicles other than railway) covers: Tractors (other than tractors of heading 8709)

The US tariff splits it into 12 subheadings and 18 eight-digit tariff lines (and 27 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 18 of the 18 tariff lines have a general rate: 13 are duty-free, 5 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 4% to 4%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 87.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 4% on 8701.21.00 (With only compression-ignition internal combustion piston engine (diesel or semi-diesel)) and the lowest is 4% on 8701.21.00 (With only compression-ignition internal combustion piston engine (diesel or semi-diesel)).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (11 lines). 5 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8701.30 Track-laying tractors; 8701.91 Not exceeding 18 kW; 8701.92 Exceeding 18 kW but not exceeding 37 kW; 8701.93 Exceeding 37 kW but not exceeding 75 kW; 8701.94 Exceeding 75 kW but not exceeding 130 kW; 8701.95 Exceeding 130 kW; 8701.10 Tractors (other than tractors of heading 8709); 8701.21 Road tractors for semi-trailers and more.
