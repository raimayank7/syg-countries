HS heading 8539: Electrical filament or discharge lamps, including sealed beam lamp units and ult

Chapter 85, Electrical machinery and electronics.

Heading 8539 in chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics) covers: Electrical filament or discharge lamps, including sealed beam lamp units and ultraviolet or infrared lamps; arc lamps; light-emitting diode (LED) light sources; parts thereof

The US tariff splits it into 12 subheadings and 18 eight-digit tariff lines (and 31 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 18 of the 18 tariff lines have a general rate: 4 are duty-free, 14 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2% to 5.8%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 85.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 5.8% on 8539.22.40.00 (Christmas-tree lamps) and the lowest is 2% on 8539.10.00 (Sealed beam lamp units).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (9 lines). 14 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8539.21 Tungsten halogen; 8539.22 Other, of a power not exceeding 200 W and for a voltage exce; 8539.29 Other; 8539.39 Other; 8539.10 Electrical filament or discharge lamps, including sealed bea; 8539.31 Discharge lamps, other than ultraviolet lamps; 8539.32 Discharge lamps, other than ultraviolet lamps; 8539.41 Ultraviolet or infrared lamps; arc lamps and more.
