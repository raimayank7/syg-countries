HS heading 8462: Machine tools (including presses) for working metal by forging, hammering or die

Chapter 84, Machinery and mechanical appliances.

Heading 8462 in chapter 84 (Machinery and mechanical appliances) covers: Machine tools (including presses) for working metal by forging, hammering or die forging (excluding rolling mills); machine tools (including presses, slitting lines and cut-to-length lines) for working metal by bending, folding, straightening, flattening, shearing, punching, notching or nibbling (excluding drawbenches); presses for working metal or metal carbides, not specified above

The US tariff splits it into 20 subheadings and 26 eight-digit tariff lines (and 71 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 26 of the 26 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 26 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 4.4% to 4.4%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 84.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 4.4% on 8462.32.10 (Numerically controlled) and the lowest is 4.4% on 8462.32.10 (Numerically controlled).

The quantity units asked for on entry are not stated. 26 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8462.32 Slitting lines and cut-to-length lines; 8462.61 Hydraulic presses; 8462.62 Mechanical presses; 8462.63 Servo-presses; 8462.69 Other; 8462.90 Other; 8462.11 Hot forming machines for forging, die forging (including pre; 8462.19 Hot forming machines for forging, die forging (including pre and more.
