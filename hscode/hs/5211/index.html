HS heading 5211: Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mi

Chapter 52, Cotton.

Heading 5211 in chapter 52 (Cotton) covers: Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mixed mainly or solely with man-made fibers, weighing more than 200 g/m²

The US tariff splits it into 14 subheadings and 16 eight-digit tariff lines (and 54 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 16 of the 16 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 15 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 7.7% to 8.4%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 52.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 8.4% on 5211.20.21 (Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mixed mainly or solely with man-) and the lowest is 7.7% on 5211.11.00 (Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mixed mainly or solely with man-).

The quantity units asked for on entry are not stated. 15 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 5211.20 Bleached; 5211.11 Unbleached; 5211.12 Unbleached; 5211.19 Unbleached; 5211.31 Dyed; 5211.32 Dyed; 5211.39 Dyed; 5211.41 Of yarns of different colors and more.
