HS heading 9017: Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments (for example, draft

Chapter 90, Optical, medical and measuring instruments.

Heading 9017 in chapter 90 (Optical, medical and measuring instruments) covers: Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments (for example, drafting machines, pantographs, protractors, drawing sets, slide rules, disc calculators); instruments for measuring length, for use in the hand (for example, measuring rods and tapes, micrometers, calipers), not specified or included elsewhere in this chapter; parts and accessories thereof

The US tariff splits it into 5 subheadings and 9 eight-digit tariff lines (and 10 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 9 of the 9 tariff lines have a general rate: 2 are duty-free, 6 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.9% to 5.8%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 90.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 5.8% on 9017.30.40.00 (Micrometers and calipers) and the lowest is 3.9% on 9017.10.80.00 (Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments (for example, drafting machines, pantographs, pro).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (7 lines). 7 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 9017.10 Drafting tables and machines, whether or not automatic; 9017.20 Other drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instr; 9017.30 Micrometers, calipers and gauges; 9017.80 Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments; 9017.90 Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments.
