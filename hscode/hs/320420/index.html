HS code 3204.20: Synthetic organic products of a kind used as fluorescent brightening agents

US tariff, 3 lines.

Subheading 3204.20 covers Synthetic organic products of a kind used as fluorescent brightening agents (Synthetic organic coloring matter, whether or not chemically defined; preparations as specified in note 3 to this chapter based on synthetic organic coloring matter; synthetic organic products of a kind used as fluorescent brightening agents or as luminophores, whether or not chemically defined > Synthetic organic products of a kind used as fluorescent brightening agents). It is in heading 3204: Synthetic organic coloring matter, whether or not chemically defined; preparations as specified in note 3 to this chapter based on synthetic, chapter 32 (Dyeing extracts, paints and inks).

The US tariff has 3 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading. 3 of the 3 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 2 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.5% to 6.5%. 2 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 2 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are kg (3 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (3204.20) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 32 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 3204.20?

3204.20 is the US tariff subheading for Synthetic organic products of a kind used as fluorescent brightening agents.

What is the duty rate for 3204.20?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 3 of the 3 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 2 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.5% to 6.5%.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
