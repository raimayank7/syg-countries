HS heading 7013: Glassware of a kind used for table, kitchen, toilet, office, indoor decoration o

Chapter 70, Glass and glassware.

Heading 7013 in chapter 70 (Glass and glassware) covers: Glassware of a kind used for table, kitchen, toilet, office, indoor decoration or similar purposes (other than that of heading 7010 or 7018)

The US tariff splits it into 10 subheadings and 52 eight-digit tariff lines (and 24 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 52 of the 52 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 52 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3% to 38%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 70.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 38% on 7013.99.40.01 (Valued not over $0.30 each) and the lowest is 3% on 7013.22.50.00 (Valued over $5 each).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (40 lines). 52 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 7013.10 Of glass-ceramics; 7013.22 Of lead crystal; 7013.28 Other; 7013.33 Of lead crystal; 7013.37 Other; 7013.41 Of lead crystal; 7013.42 Of glass having a linear coefficient of expansion not exceed; 7013.49 Other and more.
