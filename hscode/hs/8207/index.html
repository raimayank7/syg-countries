HS heading 8207: Interchangeable tools for handtools, whether or not power operated, or for machi

Chapter 82, Tools, cutlery and spoons of base metal.

Heading 8207 in chapter 82 (Tools, cutlery and spoons of base metal) covers: Interchangeable tools for handtools, whether or not power operated, or for machine-tools (for example, for pressing, stamping, punching, tapping, threading, drilling, boring, broaching, milling, turning or screwdriving), including dies for drawing or extruding metal, and rock drilling or earth boring tools; base metal parts thereof

The US tariff splits it into 10 subheadings and 22 eight-digit tariff lines (and 38 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 22 of the 22 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 22 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 1.6% to 8.4%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 82.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 8.4% on 8207.50.40 (Suitable for cutting metal, and parts thereof) and the lowest is 1.6% on 8207.90.15.00 (Files and rasps, including rotary files and rasps, and parts thereof).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (9 lines), doz. (1 lines). 22 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8207.19 Other, including parts; 8207.30 Tools for pressing, stamping or punching, and parts thereof; 8207.40 Tools for tapping or threading, and parts thereof; 8207.50 Tools for drilling, other than for rock drilling, and parts ; 8207.70 Tools for milling, and parts thereof; 8207.80 Tools for turning; 8207.90 Other interchangeable tools, and parts thereof; 8207.13 Rock drilling or earth boring tools, and parts thereof and more.
