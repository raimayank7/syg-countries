HS heading 4016: Other articles of vulcanized rubber other than hard rubber

Chapter 40, Rubber and rubber articles.

Heading 4016 in chapter 40 (Rubber and rubber articles) covers: Other articles of vulcanized rubber other than hard rubber

The US tariff splits it into 7 subheadings and 16 eight-digit tariff lines (and 10 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 16 of the 16 tariff lines have a general rate: 3 are duty-free, 13 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2.5% to 4.3%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 40.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 4.3% on 4016.99.20.00 (Other > Other > Toys for pets) and the lowest is 2.5% on 4016.93.10 (Of a kind used in the automotive goods of chapter 87).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (12 lines). 13 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 4016.93 Gaskets, washers and other seals; 4016.99 Other; 4016.10 Other articles of vulcanized rubber other than hard rubber; 4016.91 Other; 4016.92 Other; 4016.94 Other; 4016.95 Other.
