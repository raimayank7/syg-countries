HS code 7304.59: Other

US tariff, 4 lines.

Subheading 7304.59 covers Other (Tubes, pipes and hollow profiles, seamless, of iron (other than cast iron) or steel > Other, of circular cross section, of other alloy steel > Other). It is in heading 7304: Tubes, pipes and hollow profiles, seamless, of iron (other than cast iron) or steel, chapter 73 (Articles of iron or steel).

The US tariff has 4 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 21 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 4 of the 4 tariff lines have a general rate: 4 are duty-free.

The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are kg (2 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (7304.59) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 73 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 7304.59?

7304.59 is the US tariff subheading for Other.

What is the duty rate for 7304.59?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 4 of the 4 tariff lines have a general rate: 4 are duty-free.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
