HS heading 1701: Cane or beet sugar and chemically pure sucrose, in solid form

Chapter 17, Sugars and sugar confectionery.

Heading 1701 in chapter 17 (Sugars and sugar confectionery) covers: Cane or beet sugar and chemically pure sucrose, in solid form

The US tariff splits it into 5 subheadings and 24 eight-digit tariff lines (and 10 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 24 of the 24 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 5 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 5.1% to 6%, 19 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 17.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6% on 1701.91.42.00 (Described in general note 15 of the tariff schedule and entered pursuant to its provisions) and the lowest is 5.1% on 1701.91.80.00 (Containing added flavoring or coloring matter > Containing added flavoring matter whether or not containing ad).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (21 lines). 24 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 1701.12 Beet sugar; 1701.13 Cane sugar specified in subheading note 2 to this chapter; 1701.14 Other cane sugar; 1701.91 Containing added flavoring or coloring matter; 1701.99 Other.
