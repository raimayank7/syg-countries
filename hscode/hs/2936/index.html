HS heading 2936: Provitamins and vitamins, natural or reproduced by synthesis (including natural

Chapter 29, Organic chemicals.

Heading 2936 in chapter 29 (Organic chemicals) covers: Provitamins and vitamins, natural or reproduced by synthesis (including natural concentrates), derivatives thereof used primarily as vitamins, and intermixtures of the foregoing, whether or not in any solvent

The US tariff splits it into 10 subheadings and 13 eight-digit tariff lines (and 8 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 13 of the 13 tariff lines have a general rate: 13 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 29.

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (10 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate. The subheadings, in code order, are: 2936.29 Other vitamins and their derivatives; 2936.21 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.22 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.23 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.24 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.25 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.26 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed; 2936.27 Vitamins and their derivatives, unmixed and more.
