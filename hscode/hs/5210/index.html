HS heading 5210: Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mi

Chapter 52, Cotton.

Heading 5210 in chapter 52 (Cotton) covers: Woven fabrics of cotton, containing less than 85 percent by weight of cotton, mixed mainly or solely with man-made fibers, weighing not more than 200 g/m²

The US tariff splits it into 11 subheadings and 40 eight-digit tariff lines (and 76 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 40 of the 40 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 40 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 7.8% to 15.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 52.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 15.5% on 5210.31.80 (Of number 69 or higher number) and the lowest is 7.8% on 5210.59.80 (Of number 69 or higher number).

The quantity units asked for on entry are m² (9 lines), kg (9 lines). 40 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 5210.11 Plain weave; 5210.19 Other fabrics; 5210.21 Plain weave; 5210.29 Other fabrics; 5210.31 Plain weave; 5210.39 Other fabrics; 5210.41 Plain weave; 5210.49 Other fabrics and more.
