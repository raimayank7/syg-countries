HS heading 8708: Parts and accessories of the motor vehicles of headings 8701 to 8705

Chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway.

Heading 8708 in chapter 87 (Vehicles other than railway) covers: Parts and accessories of the motor vehicles of headings 8701 to 8705

The US tariff splits it into 15 subheadings and 85 eight-digit tariff lines (and 57 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 85 of the 85 tariff lines have a general rate: 46 are duty-free, 39 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2.5% to 2.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 87.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 2.5% on 8708.10.30 (Parts and accessories of the motor vehicles of headings 8701 to 8705 > Bumpers and parts thereof > Bumpers) and the lowest is 2.5% on 8708.10.30 (Parts and accessories of the motor vehicles of headings 8701 to 8705 > Bumpers and parts thereof > Bumpers).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (55 lines), kg (11 lines), m2 (1 lines). 39 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8708.10 Bumpers and parts thereof; 8708.29 Other; 8708.30 Brakes and servo-brakes; parts thereof; 8708.40 Gear boxes and parts thereof; 8708.50 Drive-axles with differential, whether or not provided with ; 8708.70 Road wheels and parts and accessories thereof; 8708.80 Suspension systems and parts thereof (including shock absorb; 8708.91 Radiators and parts thereof and more.
