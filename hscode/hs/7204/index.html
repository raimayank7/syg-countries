HS heading 7204: Ferrous waste and scrap; remelting scrap ingots of iron or steel

Chapter 72, Iron and steel.

Heading 7204 in chapter 72 (Iron and steel) covers: Ferrous waste and scrap; remelting scrap ingots of iron or steel

The US tariff splits it into 7 subheadings and 7 eight-digit tariff lines (and 9 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 7 of the 7 tariff lines have a general rate: 7 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 72.

The quantity units asked for on entry are t (5 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate. The subheadings, in code order, are: 7204.10 Ferrous waste and scrap; remelting scrap ingots of iron or s; 7204.21 Waste and scrap of alloy steel; 7204.29 Waste and scrap of alloy steel; 7204.30 Ferrous waste and scrap; remelting scrap ingots of iron or s; 7204.41 Other waste and scrap; 7204.49 Other waste and scrap; 7204.50 Ferrous waste and scrap; remelting scrap ingots of iron or s.
