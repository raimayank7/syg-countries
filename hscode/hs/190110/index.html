HS code 1901.10: Preparations suitable for infants or young children, put up for retail sale

US tariff, 22 lines.

Subheading 1901.10 covers Preparations suitable for infants or young children, put up for retail sale (Malt extract; food preparations of flour, groats, meal, starch or malt extract, not containing cocoa or containing less than 40 percent by weight of cocoa calculated on a totally defatted basis, not elsewhere specified or included; food preparations of goods of headings 0401 to 0404, not containing cocoa or containing less than 5 percent by weight of cocoa calculated on a totally defatted basis, not elsewhere specified or included > Preparations suitable for infants or young children, put up for retail sale). It is in heading 1901: Malt extract; food preparations of flour, groats, meal, starch or malt extract, not containing cocoa or containing less than 40 percent by w, chapter 19 (Cereal, flour and pastry preparations).

The US tariff has 22 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading. 22 of the 22 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 15 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.4% to 17.5%, 7 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram). 22 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 22 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are kg (22 lines), kg cmsc (6 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (1901.10) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 19 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 1901.10?

1901.10 is the US tariff subheading for Preparations suitable for infants or young children, put up for retail sale.

What is the duty rate for 1901.10?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 22 of the 22 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 15 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.4% to 17.5%, 7 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
