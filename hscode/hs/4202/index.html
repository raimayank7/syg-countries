HS heading 4202: Trunks, suitcases, vanity cases, attache cases, briefcases, school satchels, spe

Chapter 42, Leather articles, bags and travel goods.

Heading 4202 in chapter 42 (Leather articles, bags and travel goods) covers: Trunks, suitcases, vanity cases, attache cases, briefcases, school satchels, spectacle cases, binocular cases, camera cases, musical instrument cases, gun cases, holsters and similar containers; traveling bags, insulated food or beverage bags, toiletry bags, knapsacks and backpacks, handbags, shopping bags, wallets, purses, map cases, cigarette cases, tobacco pouches, tool bags, sports bags, bottle cases, jewelry boxes, powder cases, cutlery cases and similar containers, of leather or of composition leather, of sheeting of plastics, of textile materials, of vulcanized fiber or of paperboard, or wholly or mainly covered with such materials or with paper

The US tariff splits it into 12 subheadings and 59 eight-digit tariff lines (and 38 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 59 of the 59 tariff lines have a general rate: 2 are duty-free, 56 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 3.3% to 20%, 1 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 42.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 20% on 4202.12.21 (Trunks, suitcases, vanity cases and similar containers) and the lowest is 3.3% on 4202.29.20.00 (Other > Of materials (other than leather, composition leather, sheeting of plastics, textile materials, vulcan).

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (46 lines), kg (32 lines). 57 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 4202.12 With outer surface of plastics or of textile materials; 4202.21 With outer surface of leather or of composition leather; 4202.22 With outer surface of sheeting of plastics or of textile mat; 4202.29 Other; 4202.31 With outer surface of leather or of composition leather; 4202.32 With outer surface of sheeting of plastics or of textile mat; 4202.39 Other; 4202.91 With outer surface of leather or of composition leather and more.
