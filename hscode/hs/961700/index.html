HS code 9617.00: Vacuum flasks and other vacuum vessels, complete; parts thereof other than glass inners

US tariff, 4 lines.

Subheading 9617.00 covers Vacuum flasks and other vacuum vessels, complete; parts thereof other than glass inners. It is in heading 9617: Vacuum flasks and other vacuum vessels, complete; parts thereof other than glass inners, chapter 96 (Miscellaneous manufactured articles).

The US tariff has 4 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading. 4 of the 4 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 6.9% to 7.2%. 4 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 4 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are No. (4 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (9617.00) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 96 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.
