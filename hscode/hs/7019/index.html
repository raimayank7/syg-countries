HS heading 7019: Glass fibers (including glass wool) and articles thereof (for example, yarn, rov

Chapter 70, Glass and glassware.

Heading 7019 in chapter 70 (Glass and glassware) covers: Glass fibers (including glass wool) and articles thereof (for example, yarn, rovings, woven fabrics)

The US tariff splits it into 18 subheadings and 59 eight-digit tariff lines (and 50 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 59 of the 59 tariff lines have a general rate: 24 are duty-free, 35 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 4.2% to 7.3%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 70.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 7.3% on 7019.61.40 (Other > Not colored > Other) and the lowest is 4.2% on 7019.13.35.00 (Slivers, rovings, yarn and chopped strands and mats thereof > Other yarn, slivers > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (37 lines), m2 (26 lines). 35 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 7019.13 Other yarn, slivers; 7019.19 Other; 7019.61 Closed woven fabrics of rovings; 7019.62 Other closed fabrics of rovings; 7019.63 Closed woven fabrics, plain weave, of yarns, not coated or l; 7019.64 Closed woven fabrics, plain weave, of yarns, coated or lamin; 7019.65 Open woven fabrics of a width not exceeding 30 cm; 7019.66 Plain weave, weighing less than 250 g/m2, of filaments measu and more.
