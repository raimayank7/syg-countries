HS heading 8529: Parts suitable for use solely or principally with the apparatus of headings 8524

Chapter 85, Electrical machinery and electronics.

Heading 8529 in chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics) covers: Parts suitable for use solely or principally with the apparatus of headings 8524 to 8528

The US tariff splits it into 2 subheadings and 34 eight-digit tariff lines (and 19 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 34 of the 34 tariff lines have a general rate: 34 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 85.

The quantity units asked for on entry are No. (27 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate. The subheadings, in code order, are: 8529.10 Antennas and antenna reflectors of all kinds; parts suitable; 8529.90 Other.
