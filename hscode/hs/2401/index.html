HS heading 2401: Unmanufactured tobacco (whether or not threshed or similarly processed); tobacco

Chapter 24, Tobacco.

Heading 2401 in chapter 24 (Tobacco) covers: Unmanufactured tobacco (whether or not threshed or similarly processed); tobacco refuse

The US tariff splits it into 3 subheadings and 37 eight-digit tariff lines (and 124 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 37 of the 37 tariff lines have a general rate: 20 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 350% to 350%, 13 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 24.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 350% on 2401.10.65 (Flue-cured, burley and other light air-cured leaf > Other > Other) and the lowest is 350% on 2401.10.65 (Flue-cured, burley and other light air-cured leaf > Other > Other).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (13 lines). 17 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 2401.10 Tobacco, not stemmed/stripped; 2401.20 Tobacco, partly or wholly stemmed/stripped; 2401.30 Tobacco refuse.
