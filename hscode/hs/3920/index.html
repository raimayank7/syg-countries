HS heading 3920: Other plates, sheets, film, foil and strip, of plastics, noncellular and not rei

Chapter 39, Plastics and plastic articles.

Heading 3920 in chapter 39 (Plastics and plastic articles) covers: Other plates, sheets, film, foil and strip, of plastics, noncellular and not reinforced, laminated, supported or similarly combined with other materials

The US tariff splits it into 19 subheadings and 28 eight-digit tariff lines (and 8 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 28 of the 28 tariff lines have a general rate: 1 are duty-free, 27 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2.9% to 6.5%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 39.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 6.5% on 3920.51.50 (Of acrylic polymers > Of poly(methyl methacrylate) > Other) and the lowest is 2.9% on 3920.73.00.00 (Of cellulose acetate).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (25 lines), m2 (1 lines). 27 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 3920.43 Containing by weight not less than 6 percent of plasticizers; 3920.51 Of poly(methyl methacrylate); 3920.59 Other; 3920.63 Of unsaturated polyesters; 3920.79 Of other cellulose derivatives; 3920.99 Of other plastics; 3920.10 Other plates, sheets, film, foil and strip, of plastics, non; 3920.20 Other plates, sheets, film, foil and strip, of plastics, non and more.
