HS heading 5801: Woven pile fabrics and chenille fabrics, other than fabrics of heading 5802 or 5

Chapter 58, Special woven fabrics, lace and embroidery.

Heading 5801 in chapter 58 (Special woven fabrics, lace and embroidery) covers: Woven pile fabrics and chenille fabrics, other than fabrics of heading 5802 or 5806

The US tariff splits it into 12 subheadings and 16 eight-digit tariff lines (and 10 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 16 of the 16 tariff lines have a general rate: 2 are duty-free, 14 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2.7% to 20.2%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 58.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 20.2% on 5801.22.90.00 (Of cotton > Cut corduroy > Other (224)) and the lowest is 2.7% on 5801.90.20 (Woven pile fabrics and chenille fabrics, other than fabrics of heading 5802 or 5806 > Of other textile materia).

The quantity units asked for on entry are m² (11 lines), kg (11 lines). 14 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 5801.22 Cut corduroy; 5801.27 Warp pile fabrics; 5801.37 Warp pile fabrics; 5801.90 Of other textile materials; 5801.10 Woven pile fabrics and chenille fabrics, other than fabrics ; 5801.21 Of cotton; 5801.23 Of cotton; 5801.26 Of cotton and more.
