Chapter 60, Knitted or crocheted fabrics.

Heading 6005 in chapter 60 (Knitted or crocheted fabrics) covers: Warp knit fabrics (including those made on galloon knitting machines), other than those of headings 6001 to 6004

The US tariff splits it into 14 subheadings and 15 eight-digit tariff lines (and 24 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 15 of the 15 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 15 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 10% to 10%.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 60.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 10% on 6005.90.10.00 (Of wool or fine animal hair (414)) and the lowest is 10% on 6005.90.10.00 (Of wool or fine animal hair (414)).

The quantity units asked for on entry are kg (7 lines). 15 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 6005.90 Other; 6005.21 Of cotton; 6005.22 Of cotton; 6005.23 Of cotton; 6005.24 Of cotton; 6005.35 Of synthetic fibers; 6005.36 Of synthetic fibers; 6005.37 Of synthetic fibers and more.
