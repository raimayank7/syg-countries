HS code 8543.70: Other machines and apparatus

US tariff, 14 lines.

Subheading 8543.70 covers Other machines and apparatus (Electrical machines and apparatus, having individual functions, not specified or included elsewhere in this chapter; parts thereof > Other machines and apparatus). It is in heading 8543: Electrical machines and apparatus, having individual functions, not specified or included elsewhere in this chapter; parts thereof, chapter 85 (Electrical machinery and electronics).

The US tariff has 14 tariff lines (eight digits) under this subheading, and 3 ten-digit statistical suffix lines. 14 of the 14 tariff lines have a general rate: 10 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2% to 2.6%. 4 lines also list special programme rates, for example free entry for goods of countries with a trade agreement.

Under "special", 4 lines list reduced or free rates for programmes and agreements. The units in which the US asks for quantities on entry are No. (13 lines). The "column 2" rate in the table is the rate for the few countries that do not receive normal trade relations; it is rarely the one that applies.

The first six digits (8543.70) are shared with the Harmonized System used by most countries, so the same six digits are used when the product is imported elsewhere, although the rate and the later digits differ from country to country. Before you use a code on a customs declaration, check it against the legal notes of chapter 85 and ask the customs authority if you are not sure.

Common questions

What is HTS code 8543.70?

8543.70 is the US tariff subheading for Other machines and apparatus.

What is the duty rate for 8543.70?

The general rate depends on the eight-digit line: 14 of the 14 tariff lines have a general rate: 10 are duty-free, 4 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 2% to 2.6%.

Is this code the same in other countries?

The first six digits are shared by most countries. Later digits and rates differ.
