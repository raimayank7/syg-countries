HS heading 6404: Footwear with outer soles of rubber, plastics, leather or composition leather an

Chapter 64, Footwear.

Heading 6404 in chapter 64 (Footwear) covers: Footwear with outer soles of rubber, plastics, leather or composition leather and uppers of textile materials

The US tariff splits it into 3 subheadings and 39 eight-digit tariff lines (and 127 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 39 of the 39 tariff lines have a general rate: 0 are duty-free, 35 have an ad valorem (percentage of value) rate from 7.5% to 48%, 4 have a specific rate (an amount per unit, such as cents per kilogram).

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 64.

Among the lines with a percentage rate, the highest is 48% on 6404.11.59.00 (Valued not over $3/pair > Other > Other) and the lowest is 7.5% on 6404.11.41 (With uppers of vegetable fibers and having outer soles with textile materials having the greatest surface area).

The quantity units asked for on entry are prs. (2 lines). 39 lines also show special rates under trade agreements or preference programmes. The subheadings, in code order, are: 6404.11 Sports footwear; tennis shoes, basketball shoes, gym shoes, ; 6404.19 Other; 6404.20 Footwear with outer soles of leather or composition leather.
