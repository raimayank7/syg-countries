HS heading 4407: Wood sawn or chipped lengthwise, sliced or peeled, whether or not planed, sanded

Chapter 44, Wood and wood articles.

Heading 4407 in chapter 44 (Wood and wood articles) covers: Wood sawn or chipped lengthwise, sliced or peeled, whether or not planed, sanded or end-jointed, of a thickness exceeding 6 mm

The US tariff splits it into 21 subheadings and 21 eight-digit tariff lines (and 63 ten-digit statistical suffixes). 21 of the 21 tariff lines have a general rate: 21 are duty-free.

Open a subheading below to see its tariff lines with the general rate, the column 2 rate and the unit of quantity. The four digits of the heading are part of the international Harmonized System, so the same heading number is used by most countries; the later digits and the rates are the US ones. This page does not decide which code your goods belong in: that depends on the exact goods and on the legal notes of chapter 44.

The quantity units asked for on entry are m3 (11 lines). None of the lines shows a special programme rate. The subheadings, in code order, are: 4407.11 Coniferous; 4407.12 Coniferous; 4407.13 Coniferous; 4407.14 Coniferous; 4407.19 Coniferous; 4407.21 Of tropical wood; 4407.22 Of tropical wood; 4407.23 Of tropical wood and more.
