Zambia

Zambia is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Zambia. The capital is Lusaka.

Land and position

Zambia covers about 752,614 square kilometres. By area it ranks 40th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Chile and a little larger than Myanmar. It shares a land border with 8 neighbours: Angola, Botswana, DR Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Tanzania, Zimbabwe. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 15° south, 30° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 21.9 million people live in Zambia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 62nd most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.27 percent of the world's people. On average there are 29 people to each square kilometre. About 46 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 67 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Bemba (Zambia), Lozi, Lunda, Luvale, Nyanja, Tonga (Zambia). A person from Zambia is called Zambian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Zambian kwacha (code ZMW, symbol ZK). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 28.9 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,318 for each person (2025).

Time

Zambia uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Lusaka). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
