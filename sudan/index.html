Sudan

Sudan is a country in Northern Africa. Its official name is Republic of the Sudan. In the language of the place it is called السودان. The capital is Khartoum.

Land and position

Sudan covers about 1,861,484 square kilometres. By area it ranks 17th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Indonesia and a little larger than Libya. It shares a land border with 7 neighbours: Central African Republic, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Libya, South Sudan, Chad. Its centre lies at about 15° north, 30° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 51.7 million people live in Sudan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 30th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.63 percent of the world's people. On average there are 28 people to each square kilometre. About 35 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 67 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, English, Nobiin. A person from Sudan is called Sudanese.

Money and economy

The currency is the Sudanese pound (code SDG, symbol PT). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 60.2 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,165 for each person (2025).

Time

Sudan uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Khartoum). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
