Chad

Chad is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Republic of Chad. In the languages of the place it is called تشاد, Tchad. The capital is N'Djamena.

Land and position

Chad covers about 1,284,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 22nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Peru and a little larger than Niger. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Central African Republic, Cameroon, Libya, Niger, Nigeria, Sudan. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 15° north, 19° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 21 million people live in Chad (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 64th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.26 percent of the world's people. On average there are 16 people to each square kilometre. About 28 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 55 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Arabic, Sara. A person from Chad is called Chadian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Central African CFA franc (code XAF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 21.5 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,022 for each person (2025).

Time

Chad uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Africa/Ndjamena). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
