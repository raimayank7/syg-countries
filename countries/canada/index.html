Land and position

Canada covers about 9,984,670 square kilometres. By area it ranks 3rd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Antarctica and a little larger than United States. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: United States. Its centre lies at about 60° north, 95° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 41.7 million people live in Canada (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 38th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.51 percent of the world's people. On average there are 4.2 people to each square kilometre. About 83 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 82 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, French, Inuktitut. A person from Canada is called Canadian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Canadian dollar (code CAD, symbol $). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 2.32 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 55,698 for each person (2025).

Time

Canada spans 23 time zone areas, from UTC-8:00 to UTC-3:30. Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
