Angola

Angola is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Republic of Angola. The capital is Luanda.

Land and position

Angola covers about 1,246,700 square kilometres. By area it ranks 24th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Niger and a little larger than Mali. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: DR Congo, Congo, Namibia, Zambia. Its centre lies at about 12° south, 18° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 39 million people live in Angola (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 39th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.48 percent of the world's people. On average there are 31 people to each square kilometre. About 71 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 65 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Portuguese. A person from Angola is called Angolan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Angolan kwanza (code AOA, symbol Kz). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 122 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,129 for each person (2025).

Time

Angola uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Africa/Luanda). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
