Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan is a country in Central Asia. Its official name is Republic of Uzbekistan. In the languages of the place it is called Узбекистан, O‘zbekiston. The capital is Tashkent.

Land and position

Uzbekistan covers about 447,400 square kilometres. By area it ranks 58th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Sweden and a little larger than Morocco. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Afghanistan, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 41° north, 64° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 37.1 million people live in Uzbekistan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 42nd most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.45 percent of the world's people. On average there are 83 people to each square kilometre. About 51 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 73 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Uzbek, Russian, Tajik. A person from Uzbekistan is called Uzbekistani.

Money and economy

The currency is the Uzbekistani soʻm (code UZS, symbol so'm). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 147 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,968 for each person (2025).

Time

Uzbekistan spans 2 time zone areas, from UTC+5:00 to UTC+5:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
