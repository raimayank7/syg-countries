Land and position

Cuba covers about 110,860 square kilometres. By area it ranks 107th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Bulgaria and a little larger than Guatemala. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 22° north, 80° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 10.9 million people live in Cuba (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 88th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.13 percent of the world's people. On average there are 99 people to each square kilometre. About 77 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 78 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, Papiamento. A person from Cuba is called Cuban.

Money and economy

The currency is the Cuban peso (code CUP, symbol $). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 107 billion (2020), which is about US$ 9,605 for each person (2020).

Time

Cuba uses one time zone, UTC-5:00 (America/Havana). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
