Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan is a country in Central Asia. Its official name is Kyrgyz Republic. In the languages of the place it is called Кыргызстан, Киргизия. The capital is Bishkek.

Land and position

Kyrgyzstan covers about 198,500 square kilometres. By area it ranks 88th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Belarus and a little larger than Senegal. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: China, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 41° north, 75° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 7.34 million people live in Kyrgyzstan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 107th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 37 people to each square kilometre. About 35 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 72 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Kirghiz, Uzbek, Russian. A person from Kyrgyzstan is called Kirghiz.

Money and economy

The currency is the Kyrgyzstani som (code KGS, symbol с). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 22.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,081 for each person (2025).

Time

Kyrgyzstan uses one time zone, UTC+6:00 (Asia/Bishkek). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
