Oman

Oman is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Sultanate of Oman. In the language of the place it is called عمان. The capital is Muscat.

Land and position

Oman covers about 309,500 square kilometres. By area it ranks 72nd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Poland and a little larger than Italy. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Yemen. Its centre lies at about 21° north, 57° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 5.49 million people live in Oman (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 121st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 18 people to each square kilometre. About 79 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 80 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, English, Baluchi, Urdu. A person from Oman is called Omani.

Money and economy

The currency is the Omani rial (code OMR, symbol ر.ع.). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 11 billion (2025), which is about US$ 19,947 for each person (2025).

Time

Oman uses one time zone, UTC+4:00 (Asia/Muscat). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
