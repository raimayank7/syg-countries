Togo

Togo is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Togolese Republic. The capital is Lomé.

Land and position

Togo covers about 56,785 square kilometres. By area it ranks 128th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Svalbard and Jan Mayen and a little larger than Croatia. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Burkina Faso, Benin, Ghana. Its centre lies at about 8° north, 1° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 8.59 million people live in Togo (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 102nd most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.1 percent of the world's people. On average there are 151 people to each square kilometre. About 44 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 63 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Ewe, Mina (Cameroon), Kabiyè, Dagbani, Hausa. A person from Togo is called Togolese.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Mali, Niger, Senegal. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 11.9 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,384 for each person (2025).

Time

Togo uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Lome). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
