Panama

Panama is a country in Central America. Its official name is Republic of Panama. In the language of the place it is called Panamá. The capital is Panama City.

Land and position

Panama covers about 78,200 square kilometres. By area it ranks 120th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Czechia and a little larger than Sierra Leone. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Colombia, Costa Rica. Its centre lies at about 9° north, 80° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 4.57 million people live in Panama (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 129th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 58 people to each square kilometre. About 66 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 80 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, English. A person from Panama is called Panamanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Panamanian balboa (code PAB, symbol B/.). Also listed as used here: United States dollar (USD). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 90.5 billion (2025), which is about US$ 19,790 for each person (2025).

Time

Panama uses one time zone, UTC-5:00 (America/Panama). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
