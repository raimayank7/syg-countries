Guinea

Guinea is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Guinea. In the language of the place it is called Guinée. The capital is Conakry.

Land and position

Guinea covers about 245,857 square kilometres. By area it ranks 80th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Ecuador and a little larger than United Kingdom. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Ivory Coast, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Sierra Leone, Senegal. Its centre lies at about 11° north, 10° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 15.1 million people live in Guinea (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 75th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.18 percent of the world's people. On average there are 61 people to each square kilometre. About 39 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 61 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French. A person from Guinea is called Guinean.

Money and economy

The currency is the Guinean franc (code GNF, symbol Fr). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 28.3 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,877 for each person (2025).

Time

Guinea uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Conakry). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
