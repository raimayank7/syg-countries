Laos

Laos is a country in South-Eastern Asia. Its official name is Lao People's Democratic Republic. In the language of the place it is called ສປປລາວ. The capital is Vientiane.

Land and position

Laos covers about 236,800 square kilometres. By area it ranks 84th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Romania and a little larger than Uganda. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: China, Cambodia, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 18° north, 105° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 7.87 million people live in Laos (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 103rd most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 33 people to each square kilometre. About 40 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 69 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Lao, French, English. A person from Laos is called Laotian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Lao kip (code LAK, symbol ₭). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 18.3 billion (2025), which is about US$ 2,325 for each person (2025).

Time

Laos uses one time zone, UTC+7:00 (Asia/Vientiane). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
