Philippines

The Philippines is a country in South-Eastern Asia. Its official name is Republic of the Philippines. In the language of the place it is called Pilipinas. The capital is Manila.

Land and position

The Philippines covers about 300,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 74th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Italy and a little larger than Burkina Faso. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 13° north, 122° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 117 million people live in the Philippines (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 14th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1.4 percent of the world's people. On average there are 389 people to each square kilometre. About 56 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 70 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Tagalog, English, Filipino, Cebuano, Iloko, Hiligaynon, Waray (Philippines), Pampanga. A person from the Philippines is called Filipino.

Money and economy

The currency is the Philippine peso (code PHP, symbol ₱). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 487 billion (2025), which is about US$ 4,171 for each person (2025).

Time

The Philippines uses one time zone, UTC+8:00 (Asia/Manila). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
