Liechtenstein

Liechtenstein is a country in Western Europe. Its official name is Principality of Liechtenstein. The capital is Vaduz.

Land and position

Liechtenstein covers about 160 square kilometres. By area it ranks 226th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Aruba and a little larger than British Virgin Islands. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Austria, Switzerland. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 47° north, 10° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 41,024 people live in Liechtenstein (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 216th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 256 people to each square kilometre. About 15 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 84 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: German. A person from Liechtenstein is called Liechtensteiner.

Money and economy

The currency is the Swiss franc (code CHF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Switzerland. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 8.91 billion (2024), which is about US$ 220,167 for each person (2024).

Time

Liechtenstein uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Europe/Vaduz). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
