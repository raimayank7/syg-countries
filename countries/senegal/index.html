Senegal

Senegal is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Senegal. In the language of the place it is called Sénégal. The capital is Dakar.

Land and position

Senegal covers about 196,190 square kilometres. By area it ranks 89th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Kyrgyzstan and a little larger than Syria. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Mauritania. Its centre lies at about 14° north, 14° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 18.9 million people live in Senegal (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 69th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.23 percent of the world's people. On average there are 96 people to each square kilometre. About 56 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 69 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Wolof, Pulaar, Mandinka. A person from Senegal is called Senegalese.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Mali, Niger, Togo. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 37 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,955 for each person (2025).

Time

Senegal uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Dakar). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
