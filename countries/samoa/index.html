Samoa

Samoa is a country in Polynesia. Its official name is Independent State of Samoa. In the language of the place it is called Sāmoa. The capital is Apia.

Land and position

Samoa covers about 2,944 square kilometres. By area it ranks 180th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than South Georgia and a little larger than Luxembourg. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 14° south, 172° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 219,306 people live in Samoa (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 188th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 74 people to each square kilometre. About 18 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 72 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Samoan, English. A person from Samoa is called Samoan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Samoan tālā (code WST, symbol T). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.29 billion (2025), which is about US$ 5,873 for each person (2025).

Time

Samoa uses one time zone, UTC+13:00 (Pacific/Apia). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
