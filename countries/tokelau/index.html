Tokelau

Tokelau is in Polynesia. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. The capital is Fakaofo.

Land and position

Tokelau covers about 10 square kilometres. By area it ranks 247th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Cocos (Keeling) Islands and a little larger than Gibraltar. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 9° south, 172° west, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 1,466 people live in Tokelau (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 241st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 147 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: Tokelau, English. A person from Tokelau is called Tokelauan.

Money and economy

The currency is the New Zealand dollar (code NZD, symbol $). It is also the main currency of Cook Islands, New Zealand, Niue, Pitcairn Islands.

Time

Tokelau uses one time zone, UTC+13:00 (Pacific/Fakaofo). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
