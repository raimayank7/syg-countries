Yemen

Yemen is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Republic of Yemen. In the language of the place it is called اليمن. The capital is Sana'a.

Land and position

Yemen covers about 527,970 square kilometres. By area it ranks 51st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than France and a little larger than Thailand. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Oman, Saudi Arabia. Its centre lies at about 15° north, 48° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 41.8 million people live in Yemen (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 37th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.51 percent of the world's people. On average there are 79 people to each square kilometre. About 37 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 69 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic. A person from Yemen is called Yemeni.

Money and economy

The currency is the Yemeni rial (code YER, symbol ﷼). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 21.6 billion (2018), which is about US$ 634 for each person (2018).

Time

Yemen uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Asia/Aden). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
