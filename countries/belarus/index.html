Belarus

Belarus is a country in Eastern Europe. Its official name is Republic of Belarus. In the languages of the place it is called Белару́сь, Беларусь. The capital is Minsk.

Land and position

Belarus covers about 207,600 square kilometres. By area it ranks 87th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Guyana and a little larger than Kyrgyzstan. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Lithuania, Latvia, Poland, Russia, Ukraine. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 53° north, 28° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 9.09 million people live in Belarus (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 100th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.11 percent of the world's people. On average there are 44 people to each square kilometre. About 79 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 74 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Belarusian, Russian. A person from Belarus is called Belarusian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Belarusian ruble (code BYN, symbol Br). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 93.4 billion (2025), which is about US$ 10,279 for each person (2025).

Time

Belarus uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Europe/Minsk). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
