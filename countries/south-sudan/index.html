South Sudan

South Sudan is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Republic of South Sudan. The capital is Juba.

Land and position

South Sudan covers about 644,329 square kilometres. By area it ranks 43rd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Afghanistan and a little larger than Somalia. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: DR Congo, Central African Republic, Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan, Uganda. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 7° north, 30° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 12.2 million people live in South Sudan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 81st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.15 percent of the world's people. On average there are 19 people to each square kilometre. About 22 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 58 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English. A person from South Sudan is called South Sudanese.

Money and economy

The currency is the South Sudanese pound (code SSP, symbol £). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 12 billion (2015), which is about US$ 1,080 for each person (2015).

Time

South Sudan uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Juba). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
