Costa Rica

Costa Rica is a country in Central America. Its official name is Republic of Costa Rica. The capital is San José.

Land and position

Costa Rica covers about 51,100 square kilometres. By area it ranks 131st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Bosnia and Herzegovina and a little larger than Slovakia. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Nicaragua, Panama. Its centre lies at about 10° north, 84° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 5.15 million people live in Costa Rica (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 127th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 101 people to each square kilometre. About 80 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 81 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, English. A person from Costa Rica is called Costa Rican.

Money and economy

The currency is the Costa Rican colón (code CRC, symbol ₡). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 103 billion (2025), which is about US$ 19,970 for each person (2025).

Time

Costa Rica uses one time zone, UTC-6:00 (America/Costa Rica). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
