French Southern and Antarctic Lands

The French Southern and Antarctic Lands is in the Antarctic region. It is not a country: it is listed here because it has its own country code. Its official name is Territory of the French Southern and Antarctic Lands. In the language of the place it is called Terres australes et antarctiques françaises. The capital is Port-aux-Français.

Land and position

The French Southern and Antarctic Lands covers about 7,829 square kilometres. By area it ranks 173rd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Puerto Rico and a little larger than Palestine. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 49° south, 69° east, in the southern hemisphere.
