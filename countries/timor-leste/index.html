Timor-Leste

Timor-Leste is a country in South-Eastern Asia. Its official name is Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste. In the language of the place it is called Timór-Leste. The capital is Dili.

Land and position

Timor-Leste covers about 15,007 square kilometres. By area it ranks 161st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Eswatini and a little larger than Montenegro. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: Indonesia. Its centre lies at about 9° south, 126° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 1.42 million people live in Timor-Leste (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 155th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 95 people to each square kilometre. About 29 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Tetum, Portuguese, Indonesian, English. A person from Timor-Leste is called East Timorese.

Money and economy

The currency is the United States dollar (code USD, symbol $). It is also the main currency of American Samoa, British Indian Ocean Territory, British Virgin Islands, Caribbean Netherlands, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guam, Marshall Islands and 7 more. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.9 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,341 for each person (2025).

Time

Timor-Leste uses one time zone, UTC+9:00 (Asia/Dili). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
