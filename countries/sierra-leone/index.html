Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Sierra Leone. The capital is Freetown.

Land and position

Sierra Leone covers about 71,740 square kilometres. By area it ranks 121st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Panama and a little larger than Ireland. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Guinea, Liberia. Its centre lies at about 8° north, 12° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 8.82 million people live in Sierra Leone (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 101st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.11 percent of the world's people. On average there are 123 people to each square kilometre. About 46 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 62 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Mende (Sierra Leone), Timne. A person from Sierra Leone is called Sierra Leonean.

Money and economy

The currency is the Sierra Leonean leone (code SLE, symbol Le). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 7.46 billion (2025), which is about US$ 846 for each person (2025).

Time

Sierra Leone uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Freetown). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
