Kosovo

Kosovo is in Southeast Europe. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code (XK is a temporary code in common use, not an official ISO code). Its official name is Republic of Kosovo. In the languages of the place it is called Kosova, Косово. The capital is Pristina.

Land and position

Kosovo covers about 10,908 square kilometres. By area it ranks 169th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Jamaica and a little larger than Lebanon. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 43° north, 21° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 1.58 million people live in Kosovo (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 154th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 145 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: Albanian, Serbian. A person from Kosovo is called Kosovar.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more.
