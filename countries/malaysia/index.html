Malaysia

Malaysia is a country in South-Eastern Asia. In the language of the place it is called مليسيا. The capital is Kuala Lumpur.

Land and position

Malaysia covers about 329,750 square kilometres. By area it ranks 67th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Finland and a little larger than Vietnam. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Brunei, Indonesia, Thailand. Its centre lies at about 2° north, 112° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 36 million people live in Malaysia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 45th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.44 percent of the world's people. On average there are 109 people to each square kilometre. About 77 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 77 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Malay (macrolanguage), English, Chinese, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Panjabi, Thai. A person from Malaysia is called Malaysian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Malaysian ringgit (code MYR, symbol RM). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 472 billion (2025), which is about US$ 13,125 for each person (2025).

Time

Malaysia spans 2 time zone areas, from UTC+8:00 to UTC+8:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
