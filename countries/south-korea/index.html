South Korea

South Korea is a country in Eastern Asia. Its official name is Republic of Korea. In the language of the place it is called 한국. The capital is Seoul.

Land and position

South Korea covers about 98,480 square kilometres. By area it ranks 110th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Iceland and a little larger than Hungary. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: North Korea. Its centre lies at about 37° north, 128° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 51.7 million people live in South Korea (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 29th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.63 percent of the world's people. On average there are 525 people to each square kilometre. About 81 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 84 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Korean, English. A person from South Korea is called South Korean.

Money and economy

The currency is the South Korean won (code KRW, symbol ₩). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.87 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 36,227 for each person (2025).

Time

South Korea uses one time zone, UTC+9:00 (Asia/Seoul). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
