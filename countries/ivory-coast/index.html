Ivory Coast

Ivory Coast is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Côte d'Ivoire. In the language of the place it is called Côte d'Ivoire. The capital is Yamoussoukro.

Land and position

Ivory Coast covers about 322,460 square kilometres. By area it ranks 70th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Norway and a little larger than Poland. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea, Liberia, Mali. Its centre lies at about 8° north, 5° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 32.7 million people live in Ivory Coast (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 50th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.4 percent of the world's people. On average there are 101 people to each square kilometre. About 55 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 62 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French. A person from Ivory Coast is called Ivorian.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Senegal, Togo. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 99.8 billion (2025), which is about US$ 3,050 for each person (2025).

Time

Ivory Coast uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Abidjan). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
