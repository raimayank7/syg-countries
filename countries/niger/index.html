Niger

Niger is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Niger. The capital is Niamey.

Land and position

Niger covers about 1,267,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 23rd among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Chad and a little larger than Angola. It shares a land border with 7 neighbours: Burkina Faso, Benin, Algeria, Libya, Mali, Nigeria, Chad. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 16° north, 8° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 27.9 million people live in Niger (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 54th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.34 percent of the world's people. On average there are 22 people to each square kilometre. About 18 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 61 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Hausa, Kanuri, Zarma. A person from Niger is called Nigerien.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Mali, Senegal, Togo. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 21.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 775 for each person (2025).

Time

Niger uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Africa/Niamey). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
