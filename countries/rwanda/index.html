Rwanda

Rwanda is a country in Eastern Africa. Its official name is Republic of Rwanda. The capital is Kigali.

Land and position

Rwanda covers about 26,338 square kilometres. By area it ranks 150th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Haiti and a little larger than North Macedonia. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Burundi, DR Congo, Tanzania, Uganda. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 2° south, 30° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 14.6 million people live in Rwanda (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 77th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.18 percent of the world's people. On average there are 553 people to each square kilometre. About 31 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 68 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Kinyarwanda, English, French, Swahili (macrolanguage). A person from Rwanda is called Rwandan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Rwandan franc (code RWF, symbol Fr). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 16.4 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,124 for each person (2025).

Time

Rwanda uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Africa/Kigali). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
