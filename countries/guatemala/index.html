Guatemala

Guatemala is a country in Central America. Its official name is Republic of Guatemala. The capital is Guatemala City.

Land and position

Guatemala covers about 108,890 square kilometres. By area it ranks 108th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Cuba and a little larger than Iceland. It shares a land border with 4 neighbours: Belize, Honduras, Mexico, El Salvador. Its centre lies at about 16° north, 90° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 18.7 million people live in Guatemala (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 70th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.23 percent of the world's people. On average there are 172 people to each square kilometre. About 56 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 73 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish. A person from Guatemala is called Guatemalan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Guatemalan quetzal (code GTQ, symbol Q). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 123 billion (2025), which is about US$ 6,598 for each person (2025).

Time

Guatemala uses one time zone, UTC-6:00 (America/Guatemala). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
