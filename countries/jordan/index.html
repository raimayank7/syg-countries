Jordan

Jordan is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. In the language of the place it is called الأردن. The capital is Amman.

Land and position

Jordan covers about 92,300 square kilometres. By area it ranks 113th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Portugal and a little larger than French Guiana. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Israel, Iraq, Palestine, Saudi Arabia, Syria. Its centre lies at about 31° north, 36° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 11.5 million people live in Jordan (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 84th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.14 percent of the world's people. On average there are 125 people to each square kilometre. About 93 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 78 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, English. A person from Jordan is called Jordanian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Jordanian dinar (code JOD, symbol د.ا). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 61.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 5,348 for each person (2025).

Time

Jordan uses one time zone, UTC+3:00 (Asia/Amman). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
