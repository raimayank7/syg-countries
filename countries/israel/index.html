Israel

Israel is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is State of Israel. In the languages of the place it is called إسرائيل, ישראל. The capital is Jerusalem.

Land and position

Israel covers about 20,770 square kilometres. By area it ranks 155th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than El Salvador and a little larger than Slovenia. It shares a land border with 5 neighbours: Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria. Its centre lies at about 31° north, 35° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 10.1 million people live in Israel (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 96th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.12 percent of the world's people. On average there are 487 people to each square kilometre. About 92 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 83 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Hebrew, Arabic, English. A person from Israel is called Israeli.

Money and economy

The currency is the Israeli new shekel (code ILS, symbol ₪). It is also the main currency of Palestine. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 611 billion (2025), which is about US$ 60,337 for each person (2025).

Time

Israel uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Asia/Jerusalem). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
