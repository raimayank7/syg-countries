Australia

Australia is a country in Australia and New Zealand. Its official name is Commonwealth of Australia. The capital is Canberra.

Land and position

Australia covers about 7,686,850 square kilometres. By area it ranks 7th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Brazil and a little larger than India. It has no land border with any other country. Its centre lies at about 27° south, 133° east, in the southern hemisphere.

People

About 27.6 million people live in Australia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 55th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.34 percent of the world's people. On average there are 3.6 people to each square kilometre. About 88 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 83 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English. A person from Australia is called Australian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Australian dollar (code AUD, symbol $). It is also the main currency of Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Kiribati, Nauru, Norfolk Island, Tuvalu. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 1.8 trillion (2025), which is about US$ 65,130 for each person (2025).

Time
