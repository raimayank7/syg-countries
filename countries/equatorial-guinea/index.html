Equatorial Guinea

Equatorial Guinea is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Republic of Equatorial Guinea. In the languages of the place it is called Guinée équatoriale, Guiné Equatorial, Guinea Ecuatorial. The capital is Ciudad de la Paz.

Land and position

Equatorial Guinea covers about 28,051 square kilometres. By area it ranks 147th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Solomon Islands and a little larger than Burundi. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Cameroon, Gabon. Its centre lies at about 2° north, 10° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 1.94 million people live in Equatorial Guinea (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 150th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 69 people to each square kilometre. About 72 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 64 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Spanish, French, Portuguese. A person from Equatorial Guinea is called Equatorial Guinean.

Money and economy

The currency is the Central African CFA franc (code XAF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, Gabon. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 12.8 billion (2025), which is about US$ 6,615 for each person (2025).

Time

Equatorial Guinea uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Africa/Malabo). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
