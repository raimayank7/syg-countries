Lebanon

Lebanon is a country in Western Asia. Its official name is Lebanese Republic. In the languages of the place it is called لبنان, Liban. The capital is Beirut.

Land and position

Lebanon covers about 10,400 square kilometres. By area it ranks 170th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Kosovo and a little larger than Cyprus. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Israel, Syria. Its centre lies at about 34° north, 36° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 5.85 million people live in Lebanon (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 116th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 562 people to each square kilometre. About 91 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 78 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Arabic, French, English, Armenian. A person from Lebanon is called Lebanese.

Money and economy

The currency is the Lebanese pound (code LBP, symbol ل.ل). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 26 billion (2024), which is about US$ 4,473 for each person (2024).

Time

Lebanon uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Asia/Beirut). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
