Gambia

The Gambia is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of the Gambia. The capital is Banjul.

Land and position

The Gambia covers about 11,300 square kilometres. By area it ranks 167th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Qatar and a little larger than Jamaica. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: Senegal. Its centre lies at about 13° north, 17° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 2.82 million people live in the Gambia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 142nd most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 250 people to each square kilometre. About 65 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 66 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, Mandinka, Gambian Wolof, Wolof, Fulah. A person from the Gambia is called Gambian.

Money and economy

The currency is the dalasi (code GMD, symbol D). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 2.59 billion (2025), which is about US$ 919 for each person (2025).

Time

The Gambia uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Banjul). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
