Estonia

Estonia is a country in Northern Europe. Its official name is Republic of Estonia. In the language of the place it is called Eesti. The capital is Tallinn.

Land and position

Estonia covers about 45,226 square kilometres. By area it ranks 134th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Dominican Republic and a little larger than Denmark. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Latvia, Russia. Its centre lies at about 59° north, 26° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 1.37 million people live in Estonia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 158th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 30 people to each square kilometre. About 71 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 79 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Estonian, Russian. A person from Estonia is called Estonian.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Finland, France and 28 more. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 47 billion (2025), which is about US$ 34,418 for each person (2025).

Time

Estonia uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Europe/Tallinn). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
