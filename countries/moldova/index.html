Moldova

Moldova is a country in Eastern Europe. Its official name is Republic of Moldova. The capital is Chișinău.

Land and position

Moldova covers about 33,843 square kilometres. By area it ranks 141st among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Taiwan and a little larger than Belgium. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Romania, Ukraine. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 47° north, 29° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 2.36 million people live in Moldova (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 146th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 70 people to each square kilometre. About 44 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 71 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Romanian, Russian, Gagauz, Turkish. A person from Moldova is called Moldovan.

Money and economy

The currency is the Moldovan leu (code MDL, symbol L). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 20.4 billion (2025), which is about US$ 8,622 for each person (2025).

Time

Moldova uses one time zone, UTC+2:00 (Europe/Chisinau). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
