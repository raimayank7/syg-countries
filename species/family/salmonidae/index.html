Family Salmonidae: 2 species in this list

The family Salmonidae belongs to the order Salmoniformes, class . This site lists 2 of its species: Oncorhynchus mykiss (Rainbow Trout), Salmo salar (Atlantic Salmon). Together they have 1,791,606 occurrence records in GBIF; the best recorded is Salmo salar (Atlantic Salmon) with 1,515,493.

A family groups related genera. Species in the same family share a recent common ancestor and usually obvious features, which is why they are often arranged together in field guides.
