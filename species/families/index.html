How to use this page

A family groups related genera, so the species of one family share a recent common ancestor and often obvious features. The table lists every family that has two or more of the species on this site, with the class it belongs to. Open a family to compare its species by the number of records in GBIF and to see how the records are spread over time and countries. A family with few species here is not a small family in nature; the list covers well-known species only, and families are listed here only when at least two of them were chosen.
