How to use this page

The groups follow the biological class, such as mammals, birds, reptiles, flowering plants or insects. A class is a broad level of classification below the phylum, so each group below holds animals or plants that share basic features. Open a group to see its species with their families and the number of records GBIF holds for each, and the families that have the most species in the list. The groups here show how this particular list is made up and do not reflect the number of species in nature, which is very different.
