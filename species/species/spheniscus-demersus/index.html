African Penguin (Spheniscus demersus)

Aves, family Spheniscidae.

Spheniscus demersus (Linnaeus, 1758) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called African Penguin in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Spheniscidae, order Sphenisciformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

The name was first used in the form Diomedea demersa Linnaeus, 1758; the species has since been placed in the genus Spheniscus.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 16,366 occurrence records of Spheniscus demersus, from 1818 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (1,901 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (15,417), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (737), material sample (189), observation (14), literature record (6).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (16,033), before 1900: 48 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 206 (1 %); 1950 to 1999: 1,633 (10 %); 2000 to 2019: 6,574 (41 %); 2020 and later: 7,572 (47 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 94 % of all records are human observations.

Relatives in this list

Other species of the family Spheniscidae: Aptenodytes forsteri (Emperor Penguin).

Common questions

What is the scientific name of African Penguin?

The accepted scientific name is Spheniscus demersus (Linnaeus, 1758).

What family does African Penguin belong to?

Spheniscus demersus belongs to the family Spheniscidae, in the order Sphenisciformes.

Where is African Penguin recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is South Africa (15,478 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
