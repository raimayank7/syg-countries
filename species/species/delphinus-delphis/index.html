Short-Beaked Common Dolphin (Delphinus delphis)

Mammalia, family Delphinidae.

Delphinus delphis Linnaeus, 1758 is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called short-beaked common dolphin in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Delphinidae, order Cetacea, class Mammalia, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Linnaeus, C. (1758). Systema Naturae per regna tria naturae, secundum classes, ordines, genera, species, cum characteribus, differentiis, synonymis, locis. <em>Editio decima, reformata [10th revised edition], vol. 1: 824 pp. Laurentius Salvius: Holmiae.</em>.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 143,559 occurrence records of Delphinus delphis, from 1800 to 2026. The year with most records is 2017 (9,218 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (131,737), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (5,975), material sample (4,032), unspecified occurrence (805), literature record (768).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (130,665), before 1900: 157 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 715 (1 %); 1950 to 1999: 20,589 (16 %); 2000 to 2019: 90,418 (69 %); 2020 and later: 18,786 (14 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 92 % of all records are human observations.

Relatives in this list

Other species of the family Delphinidae: Orcinus orca (orca), Tursiops truncatus (common bottlenose dolphin).

Common questions

What is the scientific name of short-beaked common dolphin?

The accepted scientific name is Delphinus delphis Linnaeus, 1758.

What family does short-beaked common dolphin belong to?

Delphinus delphis belongs to the family Delphinidae, in the order Cetacea.

Where is short-beaked common dolphin recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is Portugal (49,591 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
