Mute Swan (Cygnus olor)

Aves, family Anatidae.

Cygnus olor (J.F.Gmelin, 1789) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Mute Swan in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Anatidae, order Anseriformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Syst. Nat. 1 pt 2 p. 501. The name was first used in the form Anas olor J.F.Gmelin, 1789; the species has since been placed in the genus Cygnus.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 6,887,421 occurrence records of Cygnus olor, from 1575 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (663,370 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (6,876,355), observation (4,999), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (2,724), literature record (1,268), living specimen (zoo or garden) (1,161).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (6,866,798), before 1900: 295 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 754 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 377,913 (6 %); 2000 to 2019: 2,952,750 (43 %); 2020 and later: 3,535,086 (51 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 100 % of all records are human observations.
