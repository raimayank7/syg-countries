Cabbage Rose (Rosa gallica)

Magnoliopsida, family Rosaceae.

Rosa gallica L. is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Cabbage Rose in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Rosaceae, order Rosales, class Magnoliopsida, kingdom Plantae.

It was first published in L. (1753). In: Sp. Pl. 492..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 17,505 occurrence records of Rosa gallica, from 1500 to 2026. The year with most records is 2021 (422 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (10,798), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (5,757), literature record (569), observation (161), unspecified occurrence (124).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (13,213), before 1900: 2,041 (15 %); 1900 to 1949: 1,192 (9 %); 1950 to 1999: 3,206 (24 %); 2000 to 2019: 4,549 (34 %); 2020 and later: 2,225 (17 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 62 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Cabbage Rose?

The accepted scientific name is Rosa gallica L..

What family does Cabbage Rose belong to?

Rosa gallica belongs to the family Rosaceae, in the order Rosales.

Where is Cabbage Rose recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is France (4,775 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
