Burmese Python (Python bivittatus)

Squamata, family Pythonidae.

Python bivittatus Kuhl, 1820 is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Burmese Python in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Pythonidae, order not assigned, class Squamata, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Kuhl, H. Beiträge zur Zoologie und vergleichenden Anatomie..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 10,793 occurrence records of Python bivittatus, from 1853 to 2026. The year with most records is 2020 (1,420 records). The records are of these kinds: unspecified occurrence (8,151), human observation (1,422), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (590), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (564), material sample (66).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (10,546), before 1900: 6 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 12 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 43 (0 %); 2000 to 2019: 5,517 (52 %); 2020 and later: 4,968 (47 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 13 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Burmese Python?

The accepted scientific name is Python bivittatus Kuhl, 1820.

What family does Burmese Python belong to?

Python bivittatus belongs to the family Pythonidae, in the order not assigned.

Where is Burmese Python recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is United States (9,661 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
