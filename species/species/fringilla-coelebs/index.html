Common Chaffinch (Fringilla coelebs)

Aves, family Fringillidae.

Fringilla coelebs Linnaeus, 1758 is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Common Chaffinch in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Fringillidae, order Passeriformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Linnaeus, & Carolus. (1758). Systema Naturae Per Regna Tria Naturae, Secundum Classes, Ordines, Genera, Species, Cum Characteribus, Differentiis, Synonymis, Locis, 10th Edition, Vol. 1..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 10,768,048 occurrence records of Fringilla coelebs, from 1745 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (938,854 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (10,739,650), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (15,016), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (4,871), living specimen (zoo or garden) (3,634), observation (1,961).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (10,682,367), before 1900: 1,269 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 19,555 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 718,256 (7 %); 2000 to 2019: 4,821,057 (45 %); 2020 and later: 5,122,230 (48 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 100 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Common Chaffinch?

The accepted scientific name is Fringilla coelebs Linnaeus, 1758.

What family does Common Chaffinch belong to?

Fringilla coelebs belongs to the family Fringillidae, in the order Passeriformes.

Where is Common Chaffinch recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is France (1,819,192 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
