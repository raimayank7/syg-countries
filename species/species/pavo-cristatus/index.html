Indian Peafowl (Pavo cristatus)

Aves, family Phasianidae.

Pavo cristatus Linnaeus, 1758 is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Indian Peafowl in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Phasianidae, order Galliformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Linnæus, Carolus. 1758. Systema naturae per regna tria naturae, secundum classes, ordines, genera, species, cum characteribus, differentiis, synonymis, locis. Laurentii Salvii, Holmiae. Vol. Tomus I, Editio decima, reformata: i-ii, 1-824..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 620,628 occurrence records of Pavo cristatus, from 1829 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (116,814 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (618,368), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (1,321), observation (715), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (106), unspecified occurrence (90).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (619,649), before 1900: 144 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 409 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 3,999 (1 %); 2000 to 2019: 132,237 (21 %); 2020 and later: 482,860 (78 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 100 % of all records are human observations.

Relatives in this list

Other species of the family Phasianidae: Gallus gallus (Red Junglefowl), Meleagris gallopavo (Wild Turkey).

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Indian Peafowl?

The accepted scientific name is Pavo cristatus Linnaeus, 1758.

What family does Indian Peafowl belong to?

Pavo cristatus belongs to the family Phasianidae, in the order Galliformes.

Where is Indian Peafowl recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is India (474,663 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
