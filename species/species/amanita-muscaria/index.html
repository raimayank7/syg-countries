Fly Agaric (Amanita muscaria)

Agaricomycetes, family Amanitaceae.

Amanita muscaria (L.) Lam. is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Fly Agaric in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Amanitaceae, order Agaricales, class Agaricomycetes, kingdom Fungi.

It was first published in (1783). Encycl. Méth. Bot. (Paris) 1(1): 111.. The name was first used in the form Agaricus muscarius L.; the species has since been placed in the genus Amanita.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 340,411 occurrence records of Amanita muscaria, from 1811 to 2026. The year with most records is 2022 (61,650 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (332,228), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (5,266), unspecified occurrence (996), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (974), material sample (753).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (339,001), before 1900: 246 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 1,156 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 13,149 (4 %); 2000 to 2019: 65,204 (19 %); 2020 and later: 259,246 (76 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 98 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Fly Agaric?

The accepted scientific name is Amanita muscaria (L.) Lam..

What family does Fly Agaric belong to?

Amanita muscaria belongs to the family Amanitaceae, in the order Agaricales.

Where is Fly Agaric recorded most often?
