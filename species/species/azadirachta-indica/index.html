Neem (Azadirachta indica)

Magnoliopsida, family Meliaceae.

Azadirachta indica A.Juss. is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Neem in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Meliaceae, order Sapindales, class Magnoliopsida, kingdom Plantae.

It was first published in A. Juss. (1830). In: Mém. Mus. Nat. Hist. Paris 19: 220, T. 2, N. 5..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 64,472 occurrence records of Azadirachta indica, from 1678 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (9,489 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (61,584), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (2,192), unspecified occurrence (422), material sample (220), literature record (22).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (63,088), before 1900: 103 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 142 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 697 (1 %); 2000 to 2019: 20,265 (32 %); 2020 and later: 41,881 (66 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 96 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Neem?

The accepted scientific name is Azadirachta indica A.Juss..

What family does Neem belong to?

Azadirachta indica belongs to the family Meliaceae, in the order Sapindales.

Where is Neem recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is India (39,838 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
