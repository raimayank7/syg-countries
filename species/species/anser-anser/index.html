Graylag Goose (Anser anser)

Aves, family Anatidae.

Anser anser (Linnaeus, 1758) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Graylag Goose in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Anatidae, order Anseriformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Syst. Nat. ed. 10 p. 123. The name was first used in the form Anas anser Linnaeus, 1758; the species has since been placed in the genus Anser.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 5,116,836 occurrence records of Anser anser, from 1575 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (510,774 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (5,107,958), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (4,764), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (2,177), literature record (632), material sample (524).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (5,105,582), before 1900: 441 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 932 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 180,258 (4 %); 2000 to 2019: 2,305,883 (45 %); 2020 and later: 2,618,068 (51 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 100 % of all records are human observations.
