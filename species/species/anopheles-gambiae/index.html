Anopheles gambiae

Insecta, family Culicidae.

Anopheles gambiae Giles, 1900 is the accepted scientific name of a species in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Culicidae, order Diptera, class Insecta, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Giles, G.M. 1902. A handbook of the gnats or mosquitoes giving the anatomy and life history of the Culicidae together with descriptions of all species noticed up to the present date. 2nd ed. xi +[i] + 530 pp., 17 pls. J. Bale, Sons & Danielsson, London. [before 1902.05.??].

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 66,890 occurrence records of Anopheles gambiae, from 1900 to 2025. The year with most records is 2012 (7,785 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (63,310), material sample (2,116), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (1,463), unspecified occurrence (1).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (66,192), 1900 to 1949: 878 (1 %); 1950 to 1999: 5,830 (9 %); 2000 to 2019: 51,789 (78 %); 2020 and later: 7,695 (12 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 95 % of all records are human observations.

Relatives in this list

Other species of the family Culicidae: Aedes aegypti (yellow fever mosquito), Culex pipiens (Northern House Mosquito).

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Anopheles gambiae?

The accepted scientific name is Anopheles gambiae Giles, 1900.

What family does Anopheles gambiae belong to?

Anopheles gambiae belongs to the family Culicidae, in the order Diptera.

Where is Anopheles gambiae recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is Uganda (28,679 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
