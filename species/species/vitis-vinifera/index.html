Grape-Vine (Vitis vinifera)

Magnoliopsida, family Vitaceae.

Vitis vinifera L. is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Grape-vine in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Vitaceae, order Vitales, class Magnoliopsida, kingdom Plantae.

It was first published in L. (1753). In: Sp. Pl. 202..

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 163,809 occurrence records of Vitis vinifera, from 1500 to 2026. The year with most records is 2015 (16,526 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (100,530), unspecified occurrence (27,181), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (23,869), living specimen (zoo or garden) (10,872), observation (821).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (112,502), before 1900: 1,360 (1 %); 1900 to 1949: 1,602 (1 %); 1950 to 1999: 28,463 (25 %); 2000 to 2019: 68,624 (61 %); 2020 and later: 12,453 (11 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 61 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Grape-vine?

The accepted scientific name is Vitis vinifera L..

What family does Grape-vine belong to?

Vitis vinifera belongs to the family Vitaceae, in the order Vitales.

Where is Grape-vine recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is Portugal (63,904 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
