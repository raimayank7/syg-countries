Blue-Footed Booby (Sula nebouxii)

Aves, family Sulidae.

Sula nebouxii Milne-Edwards, 1882 is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Blue-footed Booby in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Sulidae, order Suliformes, class Aves, kingdom Animalia.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 64,168 occurrence records of Sula nebouxii, from 1852 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (7,563 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (63,173), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (902), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (30), unspecified occurrence (25), material sample (21).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (64,048), before 1900: 90 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 525 (1 %); 1950 to 1999: 3,128 (5 %); 2000 to 2019: 28,786 (45 %); 2020 and later: 31,519 (49 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 98 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Blue-footed Booby?

The accepted scientific name is Sula nebouxii Milne-Edwards, 1882.

What family does Blue-footed Booby belong to?

Sula nebouxii belongs to the family Sulidae, in the order Suliformes.

Where is Blue-footed Booby recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is Ecuador (37,183 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
