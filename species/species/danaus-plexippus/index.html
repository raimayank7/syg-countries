Milkweed [Butterfly] (Danaus plexippus)

Insecta, family Nymphalidae.

Danaus plexippus (Linnaeus, 1758) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Milkweed [butterfly] in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Nymphalidae, order Lepidoptera, class Insecta, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Linnaeus, C. (1758) Systema naturae per regna tria naturae, secundum classes, ordines, genera, species, cum characteribus, differentiis, synonymis, locis. Laurentii Salvii, Holmiae, 10th ed., Vol. 1, 824 pp. [in Latin] Available at https://biodiversitylibrary.org/page/726886. The name was first used in the form Papilio plexippus Linnaeus, 1758; the species has since been placed in the genus Danaus.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 842,123 occurrence records of Danaus plexippus, from 1830 to 2026. The year with most records is 2026 (83,613 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (823,653), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (14,024), unspecified occurrence (3,555), material sample (303), machine observation (camera, sensor, recording) (250).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (839,533), before 1900: 172 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 1,733 (0 %); 1950 to 1999: 304,615 (36 %); 2000 to 2019: 168,540 (20 %); 2020 and later: 364,473 (43 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 98 % of all records are human observations.
