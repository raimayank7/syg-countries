Nilgai (Boselaphus tragocamelus)

Mammalia, family Bovidae.

Boselaphus tragocamelus (Pallas, 1766) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called nilgai in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Bovidae, order Artiodactyla, class Mammalia, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Misc. Zool. p.5. The name was first used in the form Antilope tragocamelus Pallas, 1766; the species has since been placed in the genus Boselaphus.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 3,457 occurrence records of Boselaphus tragocamelus, from 1854 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (431 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (2,908), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (498), material sample (26), literature record (22), observation (3).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (3,066), before 1900: 45 (1 %); 1900 to 1949: 47 (2 %); 1950 to 1999: 80 (3 %); 2000 to 2019: 958 (31 %); 2020 and later: 1,936 (63 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 84 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of nilgai?

The accepted scientific name is Boselaphus tragocamelus (Pallas, 1766).

What family does nilgai belong to?

Boselaphus tragocamelus belongs to the family Bovidae, in the order Artiodactyla.

Where is nilgai recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is India (2,329 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
