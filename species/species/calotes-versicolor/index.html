Variable Agama (Calotes versicolor)

Squamata, family Agamidae.

Calotes versicolor (Daudin, 1802) is the accepted scientific name of the species commonly called Variable agama in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. It is a species in the family Agamidae, order not assigned, class Squamata, kingdom Animalia.

It was first published in Daudin, F. M. Histoire Naturelle, génerale et particulièredes reptiles, ouvrage faisant suite, a l’histoiure naturelle, générale et particulière composée par LECLERC DE BUFFON, et redigée par C. S. SONNINI, vol. 3.. The name was first used in the form Agama versicolor Daudin, 1802; the species has since been placed in the genus Calotes.

Where it has been recorded

GBIF.org holds 30,262 occurrence records of Calotes versicolor, from 1838 to 2026. The year with most records is 2025 (4,219 records). The records are of these kinds: human observation (24,384), preserved specimen (museum or herbarium) (5,431), material sample (403), literature record (34), unspecified occurrence (8).

Records through time

Of the records that carry a year (28,188), before 1900: 119 (0 %); 1900 to 1949: 614 (2 %); 1950 to 1999: 2,347 (8 %); 2000 to 2019: 5,259 (19 %); 2020 and later: 19,849 (70 %). Recording has grown fast in recent decades, mostly because of photo sharing and citizen-science apps, so a rise in records does not mean a rise in numbers of the species. About 81 % of all records are human observations.

Common questions

What is the scientific name of Variable agama?

The accepted scientific name is Calotes versicolor (Daudin, 1802).

What family does Variable agama belong to?

Calotes versicolor belongs to the family Agamidae, in the order not assigned.

Where is Variable agama recorded most often?

In the GBIF records the country with the most is India (9,828 records). This reflects where the species is recorded, which depends on how many observers there are.
