Magnoliopsida: 53 species

The class Magnoliopsida in this list has 53 species from 31 families. The best recorded in GBIF is Urtica dioica (Common Nettle) with 2,266,778 records. The table lists them with their families and record counts.

Reading the numbers for this group

The 53 species belong to 48 genera: Acer (2), Ficus (2), Quercus (2), Rosa (2), Solanum (2), Arabidopsis (1) and others. GBIF holds 20,523,233 occurrence records for them together. The best recorded species, Urtica dioica (Common Nettle), has 11% of them and the least recorded, Rauvolfia serpentina (ajmaline), has 0.00%, so the record counts are very uneven within the group and say more about where people look than about the species themselves. 72% of the records that carry a year were made in the year 2000 or later, and 88% are human observations. Adding up the ten countries listed for each species, the countries with the most records are United Kingdom (23%), France (21%), Germany (11%), Netherlands (9%). Record counts show where people made and published observations, so a country with many naturalists and good online systems appears often.

For 53 of the species the Backbone Taxonomy gives the place of first publication, which is shown on each species page. A first publication date or place tells you when and where the name was introduced and does not date the species.

The families with the most species here are Solanaceae (4), Fabaceae (4), Malvaceae (4), Asteraceae (3), Fagaceae (3).
