Liliopsida: 16 species

The class Liliopsida in this list has 16 species from 7 families. The best recorded in GBIF is Triticum aestivum (Bread Wheat) with 836,588 records. The table lists them with their families and record counts.

Reading the numbers for this group

The 16 species belong to 15 genera: Allium (2), Aloe (1), Bambusa (1), Cocos (1), Curcuma (1), Hordeum (1) and others. GBIF holds 2,416,026 occurrence records for them together. The best recorded species, Triticum aestivum (Bread Wheat), has 35% of them and the least recorded, Zingiber officinale (Garden Ginger), has 0.09%, so the record counts are very uneven within the group and say more about where people look than about the species themselves. 52% of the records that carry a year were made in the year 2000 or later, and 16% are human observations. Adding up the ten countries listed for each species, the countries with the most records are Mexico (16%), Afghanistan (11%), India (7%), United States (6%). Record counts show where people made and published observations, so a country with many naturalists and good online systems appears often.

For 16 of the species the Backbone Taxonomy gives the place of first publication, which is shown on each species page. A first publication date or place tells you when and where the name was introduced and does not date the species.

The families with the most species here are Poaceae (6), Amaryllidaceae (3), Arecaceae (2), Zingiberaceae (2), Asphodelaceae (1).
