Tax-exempt organizations in Alaska

5,904 organizations

The IRS file has 5,904 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Alaska, 0.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 4,766 of them (80.7%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 4,628 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $14.20 billion of income and $25.13 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Alaska?

The IRS file counts 5,904 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Alaska, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
