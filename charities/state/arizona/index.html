Tax-exempt organizations in Arizona

31,852 organizations

The IRS file has 31,852 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Arizona, 1.6% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 27,955 of them (87.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 24,261 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $130.05 billion of income and $265.32 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Arizona?

The IRS file counts 31,852 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Arizona, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
