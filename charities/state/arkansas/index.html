Tax-exempt organizations in Arkansas

16,441 organizations

The IRS file has 16,441 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Arkansas, 0.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 13,686 of them (83.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 12,446 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $33.53 billion of income and $55.92 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Arkansas?

The IRS file counts 16,441 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Arkansas, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
