Tax-exempt organizations in Florida

115,205 organizations

The IRS file has 115,205 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Florida, 5.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 104,061 of them (90.3%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 84,772 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $240.17 billion of income and $475.38 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Florida?

The IRS file counts 115,205 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Florida, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
