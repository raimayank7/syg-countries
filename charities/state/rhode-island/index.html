Tax-exempt organizations in Rhode Island

6,661 organizations

The IRS file has 6,661 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Rhode Island, 0.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 5,410 of them (81.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 5,532 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $24.35 billion of income and $49.05 billion of assets.
