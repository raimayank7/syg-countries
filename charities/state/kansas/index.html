Tax-exempt organizations in Kansas

18,575 organizations

The IRS file has 18,575 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Kansas, 0.9% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 14,801 of them (79.7%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 14,308 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $23.97 billion of income and $57.44 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Kansas?

The IRS file counts 18,575 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Kansas, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
