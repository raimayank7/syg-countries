Tax-exempt organizations in Mississippi

15,935 organizations

The IRS file has 15,935 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Mississippi, 0.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 12,928 of them (81.1%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 12,363 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $17.37 billion of income and $30.46 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Mississippi?

The IRS file counts 15,935 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Mississippi, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
