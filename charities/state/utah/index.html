Tax-exempt organizations in Utah

13,228 organizations

The IRS file has 13,228 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Utah, 0.7% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 11,482 of them (86.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 11,159 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $50.35 billion of income and $73.16 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Utah?

The IRS file counts 13,228 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Utah, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
