Tax-exempt organizations in Puerto Rico

2,604 organizations

The IRS file has 2,604 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Puerto Rico, 0.1% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 2,444 of them (93.9%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 1,802 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $6.96 billion of income and $13.96 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Puerto Rico?

The IRS file counts 2,604 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Puerto Rico, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
