Tax-exempt organizations in Kentucky

22,919 organizations

The IRS file has 22,919 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Kentucky, 1.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 18,483 of them (80.6%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 17,801 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $56.20 billion of income and $85.10 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Kentucky?

The IRS file counts 22,919 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Kentucky, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
