Tax-exempt organizations in Iowa

30,295 organizations

The IRS file has 30,295 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Iowa, 1.5% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 18,360 of them (60.6%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 25,680 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $40.74 billion of income and $79.09 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Iowa?

The IRS file counts 30,295 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Iowa, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
