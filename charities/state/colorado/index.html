Tax-exempt organizations in Colorado

36,614 organizations

The IRS file has 36,614 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Colorado, 1.9% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 31,280 of them (85.4%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 29,213 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $84.62 billion of income and $210.76 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Colorado?

The IRS file counts 36,614 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Colorado, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
