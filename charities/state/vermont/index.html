Tax-exempt organizations in Vermont

6,610 organizations

The IRS file has 6,610 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Vermont, 0.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 5,304 of them (80.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 5,844 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $13.39 billion of income and $20.21 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Vermont?
