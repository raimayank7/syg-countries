Tax-exempt organizations in Louisiana

24,166 organizations

The IRS file has 24,166 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Louisiana, 1.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 19,583 of them (81.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 17,973 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $47.04 billion of income and $80.14 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Louisiana?

The IRS file counts 24,166 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Louisiana, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
