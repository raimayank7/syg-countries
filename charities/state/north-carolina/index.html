Tax-exempt organizations in North Carolina

59,724 organizations

The IRS file has 59,724 tax-exempt organizations with an address in North Carolina, 3.0% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 52,188 of them (87.4%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 44,522 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $121.51 billion of income and $258.92 billion of assets.

How many nonprofits are there in North Carolina?
