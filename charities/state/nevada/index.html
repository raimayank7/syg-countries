Tax-exempt organizations in Nevada

13,642 organizations

The IRS file has 13,642 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Nevada, 0.7% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 11,980 of them (87.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 10,474 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $16.94 billion of income and $34.05 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Nevada?

The IRS file counts 13,642 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Nevada, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
