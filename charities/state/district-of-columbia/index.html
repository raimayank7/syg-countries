Tax-exempt organizations in District of Columbia

14,207 organizations

The IRS file has 14,207 tax-exempt organizations with an address in District of Columbia, 0.7% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 10,974 of them (77.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 11,740 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $207.02 billion of income and $188.76 billion of assets.
