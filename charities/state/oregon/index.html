Tax-exempt organizations in Oregon

27,127 organizations

The IRS file has 27,127 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Oregon, 1.4% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 22,522 of them (83.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 22,018 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $61.77 billion of income and $108.47 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Oregon?

The IRS file counts 27,127 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Oregon, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
