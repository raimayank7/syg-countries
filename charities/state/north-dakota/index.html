Tax-exempt organizations in North Dakota

6,852 organizations

The IRS file has 6,852 tax-exempt organizations with an address in North Dakota, 0.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 5,082 of them (74.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 5,263 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $12.81 billion of income and $24.76 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in North Dakota?

The IRS file counts 6,852 tax-exempt organizations with an address in North Dakota, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
