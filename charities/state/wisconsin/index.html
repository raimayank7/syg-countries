Tax-exempt organizations in Wisconsin

39,415 organizations

The IRS file has 39,415 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Wisconsin, 2.0% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 30,737 of them (78.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 32,518 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $88.33 billion of income and $140.89 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Wisconsin?

The IRS file counts 39,415 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Wisconsin, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
