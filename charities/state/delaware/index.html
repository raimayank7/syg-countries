Tax-exempt organizations in Delaware

10,467 organizations

The IRS file has 10,467 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Delaware, 0.5% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 9,311 of them (89.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 8,442 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $71.84 billion of income and $56.82 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Delaware?

The IRS file counts 10,467 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Delaware, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
