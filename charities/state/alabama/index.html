Tax-exempt organizations in Alabama

26,467 organizations

The IRS file has 26,467 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Alabama, 1.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 22,305 of them (84.3%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 20,533 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $27.83 billion of income and $73.49 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Alabama?

The IRS file counts 26,467 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Alabama, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
