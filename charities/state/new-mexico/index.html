Tax-exempt organizations in New Mexico

11,376 organizations

The IRS file has 11,376 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New Mexico, 0.6% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 9,442 of them (83.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 8,577 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $13.78 billion of income and $29.21 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in New Mexico?

The IRS file counts 11,376 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New Mexico, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
