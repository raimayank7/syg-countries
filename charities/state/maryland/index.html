Tax-exempt organizations in Maryland

44,010 organizations

The IRS file has 44,010 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Maryland, 2.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 37,559 of them (85.3%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 32,543 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $162.43 billion of income and $254.85 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Maryland?

The IRS file counts 44,010 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Maryland, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
