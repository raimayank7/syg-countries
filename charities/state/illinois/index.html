Tax-exempt organizations in Illinois

74,794 organizations

The IRS file has 74,794 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Illinois, 3.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 58,950 of them (78.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 57,585 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $304.05 billion of income and $500.49 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Illinois?

The IRS file counts 74,794 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Illinois, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
