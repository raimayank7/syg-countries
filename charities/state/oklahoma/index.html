Tax-exempt organizations in Oklahoma

22,548 organizations

The IRS file has 22,548 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Oklahoma, 1.1% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 19,047 of them (84.5%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 16,810 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $34.57 billion of income and $70.56 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Oklahoma?

The IRS file counts 22,548 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Oklahoma, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
