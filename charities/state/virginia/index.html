Tax-exempt organizations in Virginia

54,761 organizations

The IRS file has 54,761 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Virginia, 2.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 45,672 of them (83.4%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 42,814 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $167.81 billion of income and $288.82 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Virginia?

The IRS file counts 54,761 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Virginia, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
