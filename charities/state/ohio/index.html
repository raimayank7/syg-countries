Tax-exempt organizations in Ohio

77,220 organizations

The IRS file has 77,220 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Ohio, 3.9% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 61,535 of them (79.7%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 60,838 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $233.86 billion of income and $368.16 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Ohio?

The IRS file counts 77,220 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Ohio, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
