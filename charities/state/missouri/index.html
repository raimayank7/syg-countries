Tax-exempt organizations in Missouri

43,831 organizations

The IRS file has 43,831 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Missouri, 2.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 35,360 of them (80.7%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 33,409 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $159.56 billion of income and $228.06 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Missouri?

The IRS file counts 43,831 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Missouri, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
