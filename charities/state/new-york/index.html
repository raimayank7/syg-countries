Tax-exempt organizations in New York

125,167 organizations

The IRS file has 125,167 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New York, 6.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 107,389 of them (85.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 95,979 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $636.96 billion of income and $992.80 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in New York?

The IRS file counts 125,167 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New York, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
