Tax-exempt organizations in West Virginia

11,879 organizations

The IRS file has 11,879 tax-exempt organizations with an address in West Virginia, 0.6% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 9,250 of them (77.9%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 9,434 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $23.79 billion of income and $29.99 billion of assets.
