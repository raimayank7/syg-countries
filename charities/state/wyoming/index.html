Tax-exempt organizations in Wyoming

6,190 organizations

The IRS file has 6,190 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Wyoming, 0.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 4,991 of them (80.6%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 5,090 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $5.10 billion of income and $12.78 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Wyoming?

The IRS file counts 6,190 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Wyoming, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
