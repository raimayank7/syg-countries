Tax-exempt organizations in California

204,463 organizations

The IRS file has 204,463 tax-exempt organizations with an address in California, 10.4% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 179,161 of them (87.6%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 155,160 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $761.45 billion of income and $1273.93 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in California?

The IRS file counts 204,463 tax-exempt organizations with an address in California, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
