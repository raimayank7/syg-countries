Tax-exempt organizations in Texas

154,955 organizations

The IRS file has 154,955 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Texas, 7.8% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 134,559 of them (86.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 115,439 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $293.32 billion of income and $593.44 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Texas?

The IRS file counts 154,955 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Texas, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
