Tax-exempt organizations in Michigan

57,026 organizations

The IRS file has 57,026 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Michigan, 2.9% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 46,890 of them (82.2%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 43,972 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $254.02 billion of income and $366.16 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Michigan?

The IRS file counts 57,026 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Michigan, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
