Tax-exempt organizations in Massachusetts

44,212 organizations

The IRS file has 44,212 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Massachusetts, 2.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 37,533 of them (84.9%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 36,975 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $307.95 billion of income and $607.59 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Massachusetts?

The IRS file counts 44,212 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Massachusetts, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
