Tax-exempt organizations in New Jersey

54,033 organizations

The IRS file has 54,033 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New Jersey, 2.7% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 45,828 of them (84.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 41,094 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $138.34 billion of income and $222.88 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in New Jersey?

The IRS file counts 54,033 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New Jersey, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
