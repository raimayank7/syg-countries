Tax-exempt organizations in New Hampshire

9,544 organizations

The IRS file has 9,544 tax-exempt organizations with an address in New Hampshire, 0.5% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 7,676 of them (80.4%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 8,260 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $22.70 billion of income and $42.71 billion of assets.
