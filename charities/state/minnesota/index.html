Tax-exempt organizations in Minnesota

40,375 organizations

The IRS file has 40,375 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Minnesota, 2.0% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 32,404 of them (80.3%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 33,222 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $211.52 billion of income and $361.91 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Minnesota?

The IRS file counts 40,375 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Minnesota, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
