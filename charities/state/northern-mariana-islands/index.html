Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Northern Mariana Islands?

The IRS file counts 110 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Northern Mariana Islands, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
