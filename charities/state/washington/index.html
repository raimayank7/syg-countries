Tax-exempt organizations in Washington

42,965 organizations

The IRS file has 42,965 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Washington, 2.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 35,682 of them (83.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 34,370 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $376.10 billion of income and $373.30 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Washington?

The IRS file counts 42,965 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Washington, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
