Tax-exempt organizations in South Carolina

32,702 organizations

The IRS file has 32,702 tax-exempt organizations with an address in South Carolina, 1.7% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 28,818 of them (88.1%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 19,900 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $47.11 billion of income and $70.18 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in South Carolina?
