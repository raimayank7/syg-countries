Tax-exempt organizations in Georgia

63,026 organizations

The IRS file has 63,026 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Georgia, 3.2% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 56,601 of them (89.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 45,479 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $151.41 billion of income and $243.27 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Georgia?

The IRS file counts 63,026 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Georgia, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
