Tax-exempt organizations in Pennsylvania

81,001 organizations

The IRS file has 81,001 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Pennsylvania, 4.1% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 65,579 of them (81.0%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 64,866 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $343.05 billion of income and $598.78 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Pennsylvania?

The IRS file counts 81,001 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Pennsylvania, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
