Tax-exempt organizations in Maine

10,373 organizations

The IRS file has 10,373 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Maine, 0.5% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 8,179 of them (78.8%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 9,085 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $28.14 billion of income and $39.88 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Maine?

The IRS file counts 10,373 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Maine, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
