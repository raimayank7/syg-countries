Tax-exempt organizations in Hawaii

9,679 organizations

The IRS file has 9,679 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Hawaii, 0.5% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 8,456 of them (87.4%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 7,583 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $24.36 billion of income and $47.78 billion of assets.

Common questions

How many nonprofits are there in Hawaii?

The IRS file counts 9,679 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Hawaii, not counting those without recognition, such as many churches.

Is the largest organization the best one to give to?

No. Size is only reported income. Check any charity with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, your state regulator and the organization's own reports.
