Mali

Mali is a country in Western Africa. Its official name is Republic of Mali. The capital is Bamako.

Land and position

Mali covers about 1,240,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 25th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Angola and a little larger than South Africa. It shares a land border with 7 neighbours: Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Algeria, Guinea, Mauritania, Niger, Senegal. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 17° north, 4° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 25.2 million people live in Mali (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 58th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.31 percent of the world's people. On average there are 20 people to each square kilometre. About 32 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 61 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Bambara. A person from Mali is called Malian.

Money and economy

The currency is the West African CFA franc (code XOF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Niger, Senegal, Togo. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 30.1 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,193 for each person (2025).

Time

Mali uses one time zone, UTC (Africa/Bamako). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
