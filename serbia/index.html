Serbia

Serbia is a country in Southeast Europe. Its official name is Republic of Serbia. In the language of the place it is called Србија. The capital is Belgrade.

Land and position

Serbia covers about 88,361 square kilometres. By area it ranks 115th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than French Guiana and a little larger than Azerbaijan. It shares a land border with 8 neighbours: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Hungary, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Romania, Kosovo. It is landlocked, which means it has no sea coast. Its centre lies at about 44° north, 21° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 6.55 million people live in Serbia (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 110th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 74 people to each square kilometre. About 63 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 76 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Serbian, Hungarian, Bosnian, Romany. A person from Serbia is called Serbian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Serbian dinar (code RSD, symbol дин.). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 100 billion (2025), which is about US$ 15,262 for each person (2025).

Time

Serbia uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Europe/Belgrade). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
