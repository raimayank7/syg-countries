Land and position

Monaco covers about 1.95 square kilometres. By area it ranks 249th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Gibraltar and a little larger than Vatican City. It shares a land border with 1 neighbour: France. Its centre lies at about 44° north, 7° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 38,341 people live in Monaco (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 219th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 19,662 people to each square kilometre. About 100 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 86 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, English, Italian. A person from Monaco is called Monegasque.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 11.1 billion (2024), which is about US$ 288,002 for each person (2024).

Time

Monaco uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Europe/Monaco). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
