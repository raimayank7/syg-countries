Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina is a country in Southeast Europe. In the languages of the place it is called Bosna i Hercegovina, Боснa и Херцеговина. The capital is Sarajevo.

Land and position

Bosnia and Herzegovina covers about 51,129 square kilometres. By area it ranks 130th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Croatia and a little larger than Costa Rica. It shares a land border with 3 neighbours: Croatia, Montenegro, Serbia. Its centre lies at about 44° north, 18° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 3.14 million people live in Bosnia and Herzegovina (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 136th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 61 people to each square kilometre. About 53 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 78 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian. A person from Bosnia and Herzegovina is called Bosnian, Herzegovinian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Bosnia and Herzegovina convertible mark (code BAM, symbol KM). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 32.6 billion (2025), which is about US$ 10,382 for each person (2025).

Time

Bosnia and Herzegovina uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Europe/Sarajevo). Clocks are moved for daylight saving time in part of the year.
