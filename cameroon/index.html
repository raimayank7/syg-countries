Cameroon

Cameroon is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Republic of Cameroon. In the language of the place it is called Cameroun. The capital is Yaoundé.

Land and position

Cameroon covers about 475,440 square kilometres. By area it ranks 55th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Turkmenistan and a little larger than Papua New Guinea. It shares a land border with 6 neighbours: Central African Republic, Congo, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Nigeria, Chad. Its centre lies at about 6° north, 12° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 29.9 million people live in Cameroon (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 51st most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 0.36 percent of the world's people. On average there are 63 people to each square kilometre. About 56 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 64 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: English, French. A person from Cameroon is called Cameroonian.

Money and economy

The currency is the Central African CFA franc (code XAF, symbol Fr). It is also the main currency of Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon. The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 58.9 billion (2025), which is about US$ 1,972 for each person (2025).

Time

Cameroon uses one time zone, UTC+1:00 (Africa/Douala). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
