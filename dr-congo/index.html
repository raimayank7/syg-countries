DR Congo

The DR Congo is a country in Middle Africa. Its official name is Democratic Republic of the Congo. In the languages of the place it is called RD Congo, Repubilika ya Kongo Demokratiki, Republiki ya Kongó Demokratiki, Ditunga dia Kongu wa Mungalaata. The capital is Kinshasa.

Land and position

The DR Congo covers about 2,345,410 square kilometres. By area it ranks 12th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Algeria and a little larger than Greenland. It shares a land border with 9 neighbours: Angola, Burundi, Central African Republic, Congo, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia. Its centre lies at about 0° north, 25° east, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 113 million people live in the DR Congo (World Bank, 2025). That makes it the 15th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure, with about 1.4 percent of the world's people. On average there are 48 people to each square kilometre. About 45 percent of people live in towns and cities (World Bank, 2025). A baby born there can expect to live about 62 years (World Bank, 2024). Languages listed for it: French, Lingala, Kituba (Democratic Republic of Congo), Kongo, Swahili (macrolanguage), Luba-Lulua. A person from the DR Congo is called Congolese.

Money and economy

The currency is the Congolese franc (code CDF, symbol FC). The World Bank puts the size of its economy (GDP) at about US$ 91 billion (2025), which is about US$ 807 for each person (2025).

Time

The DR Congo spans 2 time zone areas, from UTC+1:00 to UTC+2:00. Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
