French Guiana

French Guiana is in South America. It is not a member state of the United Nations. It is listed here because it has its own country code. Its official name is Guiana. In the language of the place it is called Guyane française. The capital is Cayenne.

Land and position

French Guiana covers about 91,000 square kilometres. By area it ranks 114th among the 250 countries and territories on this site. It is a little smaller than Jordan and a little larger than Serbia. It shares a land border with 2 neighbours: Brazil, Suriname. Its centre lies at about 4° north, 53° west, in the northern hemisphere.

People

About 195,506 people live in French Guiana (GeoNames figure, year not stated). That makes it the 189th most populous of the 246 places on this site with a figure. On average there are 2.1 people to each square kilometre. Languages listed for it: French. A person from French Guiana is called Guianan.

Money and economy

The currency is the euro (code EUR, symbol €). It is also the main currency of Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland and 28 more.

Time

French Guiana uses one time zone, UTC-3:00 (America/Cayenne). Clocks stay the same all year: there is no daylight saving time.
